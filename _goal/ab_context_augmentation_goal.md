# Context Augmentation — Goal

The goal is to have the bot understand and use the organization's purpose, work standards, roles, and materials. Among the three augmentation dimensions of an augmented bot (AB) (context, governance, learning), the goal is to satisfy the context axis so that the bot performs on the basis of context rather than merely executing commands.

## Objectives
- Have the bot understand and use the organization's purpose, work standards, roles, and materials.
- Shift the bot's work performance from command-execution-centered to context-based performance.
- Satisfy the context axis among the three augmentation dimensions an augmented bot (AB) must possess.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when the results of the bot's work actually reflect the organization's purpose, work standards, roles, and materials.
