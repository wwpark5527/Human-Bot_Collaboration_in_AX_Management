# Governance Augmentation — Goal

The goal is to have the bot's operation take place within structures of authority, verification, approval, recording, and accountability. Following context augmentation, the goal is to satisfy the governance axis of an augmented bot (AB) so that the bot becomes not an automation tool with a weak accountability structure but an organizational-member-type AI operating within a governance structure.

## Objectives
- Have the bot's operation take place within structures of authority, verification, approval, recording, and accountability.
- Shift the bot from an automation-centered form with a weak accountability structure to a collaboration-centered form within a governance structure.
- Satisfy the governance axis among the three augmentation dimensions an augmented bot (AB) must possess.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when the bot's work results have actually been handled within structures of authority, verification, approval, recording, and accountability.
