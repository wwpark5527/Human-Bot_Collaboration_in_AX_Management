# Learning Augmentation — Goal

The goal is to have the bot continuously improve its role-performance capability by using feedback and organizational memory. Following context augmentation and governance augmentation, the goal is to satisfy the learning axis of an augmented bot (AB) so that the bot becomes a collaborative AI that produces verifiable results and improves itself.

## Objectives
- Have the bot collect feedback and reflect it in improving its role performance.
- Have the bot refer to organizational memory (past cases and records) and use it in its role performance.
- Satisfy the learning axis among the three augmentation dimensions an augmented bot (AB) must possess.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when the bot's role-performance capability has actually improved after feedback and organizational memory have been reflected.
