# Collaboration between Augmented Humans and Augmented Bots — Goal

The goal is to connect a human's purpose, judgment, and accountability with a bot's analysis, execution, and recording capabilities within common context and governance context, thereby realizing a role-based collaboration of complementation rather than replacement.

## Objectives
- Share the same context by sufficiently conveying the organization's purpose, work standards, reference materials, formats, and prohibitions (common context).
- Collaborate within an accountability structure equipped with verifiability, approvability, recordability, and accountability (governance context).
- Divide roles while building a relationship in which the human and the bot make each other stronger.
- Ensure that the result of the collaboration is not a one-off deliverable but an organizational asset reusable in the next task.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when the three conditions — sharing the same context, collaborating within an accountability structure, and dividing roles while augmenting each other — are met together, so that safer, more refined, and more meaningful results are produced and accumulate as organizational assets in which purpose, standards, materials, judgment, revision, approval, and recording are connected.
