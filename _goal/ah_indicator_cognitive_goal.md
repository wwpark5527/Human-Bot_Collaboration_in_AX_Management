# Cognitive Augmentation (Cognitive A.) — Goal

The goal is to confirm numerically whether the use of AI is actually broadening an individual member's range of thinking (cognition), and thereby to grasp objectively whether the augmented human (AH) has been realized.

## Objectives
- Grasp the change in the number of alternatives generated before and after the use of AI.
- Grasp the change in the number of variables considered before and after the use of AI.
- Confirm the change in thinking depth and cognitive speed.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when the number of alternatives generated, the number of variables considered, and thinking depth / cognitive speed are confirmed to have improved compared with before the use of AI.
