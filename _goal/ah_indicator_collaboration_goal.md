# Collaboration Augmentation (Collaboration A.) — Goal

The goal is to confirm numerically whether AI is being used naturally as a collaboration partner that shares the work, and thereby to verify whether the realization of the augmented human (AH) leads to a maturing of the human-AI division of roles.

## Objectives
- Measure the proportion of work a member can delegate to AI.
- Measure the member's level of agent usage.
- Confirm whether collaboration augmentation continues together with cognitive augmentation, decision augmentation, and learning augmentation.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when it is confirmed that roles are naturally divided with AI, that is, when the proportion of delegable work and the level of agent usage appear at a meaningful level.
