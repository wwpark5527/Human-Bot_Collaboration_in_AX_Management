# Decision Augmentation (Decision A.) — Goal

The goal is to confirm numerically whether the use of AI is raising a member's actual decision quality, and thereby to verify whether cognitive augmentation leads to an improvement in the quality of results.

## Objectives
- Grasp the change in decision accuracy before and after the use of AI.
- Measure the error-reduction rate before and after the use of AI.
- Confirm the change in the prediction success rate before and after the use of AI.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when decision accuracy, the error-reduction rate, and the prediction success rate are confirmed to have improved compared with before the use of AI.
