# Learning Augmentation (Learning A.) — Goal

The goal is to confirm numerically whether the use of AI is actually raising a member's learning speed, and thereby to verify whether the realization of the augmented human (AH) leads to a capability for continuous growth.

## Objectives
- Measure the change in learning time before and after the use of AI.
- Measure the change in the amount of knowledge acquired before and after the use of AI.
- Confirm whether learning augmentation continues together with cognitive augmentation and decision augmentation.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when learning time is confirmed to have decreased and the amount of knowledge acquired to have increased.
