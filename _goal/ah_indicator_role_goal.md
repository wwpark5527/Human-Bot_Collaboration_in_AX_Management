# Role Augmentation (Role A.) — Goal

The goal is to confirm whether a member has actually come to perform a new role through the use of AI, and thereby to make the final judgment of whether they are an augmented human (AH) on the basis of role expansion rather than an increase in productivity.

## Objectives
- Confirm the change in the member's range of roles before and after the use of AI.
- Collect concrete cases of role expansion such as researcher → designer, manager → orchestrator, and professor → AI coach.
- Combine role augmentation with cognitive, decision, learning, and collaboration augmentation and take it as the final criterion for judging AH.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when it is confirmed that the member can now perform a role they could not perform before using AI.
