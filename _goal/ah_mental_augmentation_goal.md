# Mental Augmentation — Goal

The goal is to raise a human's thinking, learning, analysis, creativity, and judgment by using AI and technology. Following physical augmentation, the goal is to satisfy the cognitive axis of the augmented human (AH) and thereby lay the foundation of judgment capability on which the subsequent role and context augmentation will be exercised.

## Objectives
- Raise thinking, learning, analysis, creativity, and judgment by using AI and technology.
- Following physical augmentation, strengthen mental capacities as well and thereby satisfy the cognitive axis of the augmented human (AH).
- Ensure that the improved mental capacities are reflected in actual work judgments.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when the capacity targeted for augmentation among thinking, learning, analysis, creativity, and judgment has actually improved through the use of AI and technology.
