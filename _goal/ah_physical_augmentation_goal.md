# Physical Augmentation — Goal

The goal is to complement and extend a human's physical limits or physical performance capability with technology. As the most basic axis among the three augmentation dimensions that make up the augmented human (AH), the goal is to lay the physical foundation on which the subsequent mental augmentation and role and context augmentation will be exercised.

## Objectives
- Complement with technology the bottlenecks in a human member's physical limits and physical performance capability.
- Substantially extend physical performance capability through technology.
- Satisfy the physical axis among the three augmentation dimensions an augmented human (AH) must possess.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when the physical limits and physical performance capability targeted for augmentation have actually been complemented and extended by technology.
