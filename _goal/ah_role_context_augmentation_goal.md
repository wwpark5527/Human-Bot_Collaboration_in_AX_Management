# Role/Context Augmentation — Goal

The goal is to develop one's role, responsibilities, and mode of contribution by collaborating with AI inside an AX organization, and thereby to reach a state in which AI can be used within the organization's purpose, standards, and accountability system. Following physical and mental augmentation, the goal is to satisfy the third axis of the augmented human (AH) and thereby hold the power to set purpose, the power to establish standards, and the power to take on judgment and accountability.

## Objectives
- Develop one's role, responsibilities, and mode of contribution by collaborating with AI inside an AX organization.
- Beyond merely improving work speed, use AI within the organization's purpose, standards, and accountability system.
- Hold the three powers: the power to set purpose, the power to establish standards, and the power to take on judgment and accountability.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when it is confirmed that the use of AI goes beyond the level of merely processing work quickly and takes place within the organization's purpose, standards, and accountability system.
