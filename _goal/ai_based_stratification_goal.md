# AI-Based Stratification (AI Class Society / AI-Based Stratification) — Goal

The goal is to recognize that AI-based stratification is not a simple loss of jobs but the accumulation of differences in access rights, utilization capability, judgment authority, ownership, and profit-sharing, and to prevent and mitigate its deepening into an AI class society.

## Objectives
- Recognize that AI-based stratification is not a simple loss of jobs but the accumulation of differences in access rights, utilization capability, judgment authority, ownership, and profit-sharing.
- Establish the need for an organization-level response (inclusive transformation AX) to prevent and mitigate the deepening into an AI class society.
- Grasp the interchangeable class/stratum notation and the seven gap types to diagnose the problem accurately.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the accumulated differences in access, capability, context, judgment authority, surveillance, ownership, and profit-sharing around AI are identified and a response direction that mitigates them (inclusive transformation AX) is prepared.
