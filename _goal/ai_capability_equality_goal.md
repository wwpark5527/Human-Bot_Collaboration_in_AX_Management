# AI Capability Equality Theory — Goal

The goal is to re-diagnose the cause of inequality in the AI era not as a gap in access to AI tools but as a gap in the capability to convey purpose, standards, and context and to verify, approve, and improve results, and to secure equality of AI-utilization capability by converting context capital, the authority system, verification standards, and the approval structure into a common production base for the whole organization.

## Objectives
- Diagnose the cause of AI-era inequality accurately as a "capability gap" rather than a "tool access gap."
- Manage AI-utilization capability at the organizational level so that it is not left to an individual's intuition or to the authority of a particular department.
- Convert context capital, the authority system, verification standards, and the approval structure into a common production base for the whole organization.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when AI-utilization capability (conveying purpose, standards, and context; verifying, approving, and improving results) has been converted from an individual privilege into a common production base shared by the whole organization.
