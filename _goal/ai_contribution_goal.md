# AI Contribution — Goal

The goal is to take as the evaluation standard, in the AI era, not mere output quantity but how efficiently AI actually narrowed the human's understanding (meaning/cognitive distance).

## Objectives
- Quantify AI's real contribution as the reduced meaning/cognitive distance divided by the AI-usage cost.
- Make clear that a large quantity of generated answers does not mean high contribution, and thereby shift the evaluation standard from output quantity to efficiency.
- Enable organizations and individuals to judge the effect of AI adoption as the reduction of understanding distance relative to cost.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the amount of reduction in meaning/cognitive distance and the AI-usage cost are each computed and their ratio (AI contribution) is produced.
