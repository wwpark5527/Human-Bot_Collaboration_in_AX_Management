# AI for ESG — Goal

The goal is to use AI as a powerful implementation tool for ESG and thereby accelerate the execution of ESG performance.

## Objectives
- Identify the areas in which AI contributes positively to ESG implementation (achieving environmental, social, and governance objectives).
- Use AI as an implementation tool to accelerate ESG performance measurement, monitoring, and reporting.
- Spread cases of ESG performance improvement through AI utilization within the organization.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when it is confirmed that AI has brought positive effects in the ESG implementation process (measurement, monitoring, reporting, improvement).
