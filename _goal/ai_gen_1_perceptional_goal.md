# AI 1.0 (Perceptional AI) — Goal

The goal is to identify accurately, on the basis of the AI generational-classification table, whether AI utilization within the organization remains at the perception/recognition (Perceptional) stage, and to judge the need for transition to a higher generation.

## Objectives
- Identify whether the level of AI utilization within the organization remains at the perception/recognition (Perceptional) stage.
- Clearly recognize the limits of the Perceptional AI stage (the absence of generative, autonomous-execution, and organization-operating functions) and judge the need for transition to the next generation (AI 2.0 and above).
- Use the AI generational-classification table (AI 1.0–4.0) as a diagnostic tool for the organization's level of AI utilization.

## Success Criteria
The goal is determined to be achieved when it can be accurately discriminated, by the table's standard, whether the organization's or system's AI utilization remains at perception, identification, and classification functions or has expanded to generative, autonomous-execution, and organization-operating functions.
