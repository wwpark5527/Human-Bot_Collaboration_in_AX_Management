# AI 2.0 (Generative AI) — Goal

The goal is to discriminate, on the basis of the AI generational-classification table, whether AI utilization within the organization has reached the generative (Generative) stage, and whether the limitations accompanying that generative ability (hallucination and security leakage) are recognized.

## Objectives
- Identify whether AI utilization within the organization has moved beyond perception and identification to reach the content-generation (Generative) stage.
- Clearly recognize the limits of generative AI (hallucination phenomena, the risk of security leakage) and thereby prevent indiscriminate trust.
- Share within the organization that the Generative AI stage has not yet reached autonomous goal-setting and execution (Agentic), and judge the need for transition to the next generation.

## Success Criteria
The goal is determined to be achieved when it can be accurately discriminated, by the table's standard, whether the organization's or system's AI utilization has reached the level of producing generated output such as sentences and images, and whether the hallucination and security-leakage limitations accompanying that output are recognized and managed.
