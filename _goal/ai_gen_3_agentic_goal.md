# AI 3.0 (Agentic AI) — Goal

The goal is to discriminate, on the basis of the AI generational-classification table, whether AI utilization within the organization has reached the autonomous-agent (Agentic) stage, and whether that autonomy still remains at the level of the individual agent (personal AX).

## Objectives
- Identify whether AI utilization within the organization has moved beyond generation (Generative) to reach the autonomous-agent (Agentic) stage that understands goals, plans, and executes.
- Judge whether the operating unit of Agentic AI remains at the individual agent and has not expanded to the whole organization.
- Diagnose the need to expand Agentic AI utilization that remains at the personal AX level to the organizational AX (AI 4.0) level.

## Success Criteria
The goal is determined to be achieved when it can be accurately discriminated, by the table's standard, whether the organization's or system's AI utilization has reached the level of producing autonomous planning and execution actions, and whether those results remain at the individual unit (personal AX) or have expanded to the organizational unit.
