# AI 4.0 (Organizational AI) — Goal

The goal is to discriminate, on the basis of the final stage of the AI generational-classification table, whether the organization's AI utilization has reached the 'true AI 4.0 level' in which it has expanded beyond the individual-agent level to the operation of the whole organization.

## Objectives
- Identify whether the operating unit of AI utilization within the organization has expanded beyond the individual agent (Agentic) to the operation of the whole organization.
- Judge whether common context and governance context have been formed and implemented so as to reach not a mere AI platform but an AI-native organizational operating system.
- Take whether the 'true AI 4.0 level' has been reached as the final determination standard for driving organizational AX.

## Success Criteria
When the organization's AI operation has been redesigned into an AI-native organizational operating system based on common context and governance context, so that organization-scale operational redesign results can be produced, it is determined that the 'true AI 4.0 level' has been reached and the goal has been achieved.
