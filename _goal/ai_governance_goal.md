# AI Governance — Goal

The goal is to put in place the norms, institutions, and operating system concerning how to control, manage, and supervise AI, so that the judgment-standard context provided by governance context actually operates within organizational operation.

## Objectives
- Clearly establish the order of AI use (prohibition rules, accountability rules, an audit system, an approval system) as norms, institutions, and an operating system.
- Actually make governance context (the judgment-standard context) operate as work procedures, roles, systems, and recording structures.
- Rather than letting control remain at surveillance and after-the-fact audit, make it not prohibition but operation, not surveillance but standardization, not after-the-fact audit but connected operation.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the order of AI use is established as norms, institutions, and an operating system, and governance context operates verifiably and accountably within work procedures, roles, systems, and recording structures.
