# AI Literacy and Affinity — Goal

The goal is to make members of an AX organization understand AI beyond mere use, down to the level of its strengths, weaknesses, hallucination, prompts, and workflow, and become people who do not fear AI and handle it well as a collaboration tool.

## Objectives
- Understand AI's strengths and weaknesses and the phenomenon of hallucination.
- Acquire an understanding of prompt use, AI workflow, and generative AI.
- Form the attitude of recognizing AI not as an object of fear but as a collaboration tool and handling it skillfully.

## Success Criteria
The goal is determined to be achieved when a member has acquired an understanding of AI's strengths, weaknesses, hallucination, prompts, and workflow, reaches the state of not fearing AI and handling it well as a collaboration tool, and thereby secures survival competency in an AX organization.
