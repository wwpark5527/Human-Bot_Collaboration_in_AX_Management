# AI Sovereignty (Sovereignty) — Goal

The goal is to take the full benefit of using powerful external LLMs while preserving the organization's data and decision sovereignty in a technically demonstrable way.

## Objectives
- Secure a technical device that fundamentally blocks the possibility of data and context flowing out through connection with external LLMs.
- Have a technical basis that can objectively prove the preservation of sovereignty, such as GP's zero-knowledge proof (ZKP) based patented technology.
- Realize simultaneously the benefit of using external AI and the preservation of organizational sovereignty.

## Success Criteria
The goal is determined to be achieved when the organization's data and decision sovereignty is preserved in a technically demonstrable way (e.g., ZKP-based patented technology) even while using external AI.
