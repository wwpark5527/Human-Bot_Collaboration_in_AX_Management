# AX (AI Transformation) — Goal

AX aims for the organization not to remain at DX-level questions of digitization and automation, but to integrate AI into the center of organizational operation (workflows, judgment systems, role structures, output systems, authority structures, accountability structures).

## Objectives
- Realize a transformation that integrates AI into the organization's judgment systems, role structures, authority structures, and accountability structures, rather than an extension of DX.
- Beyond the current mainstream purposes centered on efficiency, productivity, cost reduction, growth, and work optimization, aim for an AX that also encompasses inclusive transformation, prevention of AI stratification, benefit sharing, the right of appeal, and the human right of judgment.
- Provide the organization with a common conceptual standard by which the change in which AI intervenes in the organization's judgment, generation, execution, evaluation, and accountability structures can be explained and designed.

## Success Criteria
The AX objective is determined to have been achieved when the organization's change attempt actually answers "what can AI judge and generate in this work," "how will the roles of AI and humans be divided," "who will verify and approve AI results," and "how will the authority, responsibility, and outcome distribution caused by AI be designed."
