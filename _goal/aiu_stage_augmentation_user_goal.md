# Augmentation User — Goal

The goal is to identify whether a member has reached the Augmentation User stage, the final stage of the AI Utilization (AIU) ladder, at which AI operates as if it were part of oneself and augments memory, analysis, and creativity, and thereby to complete the AI-utilization measurement system and connect it to higher indicators such as the Augmentation Quotient (AQ).

## Objectives
- Confirm whether the member's decision quality has improved since using AI.
- Confirm whether the member's learning speed has increased since using AI.
- Confirm whether the member's productivity has increased since using AI.
- Take the Augmentation User identification result as the input for judging the final stage of AI Utilization (AIU) measurement and for calculating the Augmentation Quotient (AQ).

## Success Criteria
The Augmentation User stage is judged when it is confirmed that after using AI I have become a different person from my earlier self, that is, when the indicators of improved decision quality, increased learning speed, and increased productivity are confirmed.
