# Collaborative User — Goal

The goal is to identify whether a member has reached the Collaborative User stage on the AI Utilization (AIU) ladder, at which one repeatedly converses with AI to revise results and explore alternatives, and thereby to design the transition path to the AI Orchestrator stage.

## Objectives
- Confirm whether the member revises results through repeated conversation with AI rather than one-off questions.
- Confirm whether the member explores alternatives through AI.
- Take whether AI expands the member's thinking as the basis for the Collaborative User judgment.
- Take the Collaborative User identification result as the reference point for the AI Orchestrator transition plan.

## Success Criteria
The Collaborative User stage is judged when it is confirmed that AI expands my thinking, that is, when the indicators of the number of prompt improvements, the degree to which AI feedback is incorporated, and the number of repeated interactions with AI are confirmed.
