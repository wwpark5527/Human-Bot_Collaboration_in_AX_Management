# AI Orchestrator — Goal

The goal is to identify whether a member has reached the AI Orchestrator stage on the AI Utilization (AIU) ladder, at which one uses multiple AIs and agents and designs work processes, and thereby to design the transition path to the Augmentation User stage.

## Objectives
- Confirm whether the member uses multiple AIs and agents simultaneously.
- Confirm whether the member designs processes so that AIs do the work rather than performing the work directly.
- Measure as indicators the proportion of work handled by AI, the number of agents, and the level of automation.
- Take the AI Orchestrator identification result as the reference point for the Augmentation User transition plan.

## Success Criteria
The AI Orchestrator stage is judged when it is confirmed that one designs so that AIs do the work rather than doing the work directly, that is, when the indicators of the proportion of work handled by AI, the number of agents, and the level of automation are confirmed.
