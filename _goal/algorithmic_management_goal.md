# Algorithmic Management — Goal

The goal is to grasp transparently the influence that an algorithmic system which uses tracking data and information to organize, assign, monitor, supervise, and evaluate work exerts on workers, and to control it so that AI does not govern personnel and evaluation decisions without a manager judging directly.

## Objectives
- Clearly grasp the scope in which AI and algorithms are involved in work assignment, performance evaluation, surveillance, discipline, and reward within the organization.
- Have a system able to answer the five questions: "Who evaluates others with AI?", "Who is evaluated by AI?", "Are AI evaluation criteria explained?", "Can humans object?", "Who is the ultimate responsible party?"
- Ensure that the locus of accountability and the appeal procedure are secured even in areas where a manager does not judge directly.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the work areas to which algorithmic management is applied and their evaluation criteria, responsible parties, and appeal procedures are documented and explainable to workers.
