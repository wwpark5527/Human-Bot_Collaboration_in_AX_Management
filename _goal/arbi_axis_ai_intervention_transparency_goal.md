# AI Intervention Transparency — Goal

The goal is to evaluate whether the points and manner of AI intervention are clearly revealed in collaboration outputs and utterances.

## Objectives
- Identify the points at which AI was involved in the collaboration process.
- Confirm whether each point of intervention is explicitly revealed.
- Reveal in advance the points at which there is a risk that AI intervention is concealed.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when a judgment result on whether the points of AI intervention are exposed is produced and any existing concealment risk is explicitly revealed.
