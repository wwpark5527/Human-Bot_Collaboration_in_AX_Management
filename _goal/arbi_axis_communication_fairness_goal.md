# Communication Fairness — Goal

The goal is to confirm whether an AI-augmentation gap has turned into a power difference within the collaborative relationship.

## Objectives
- Identify differences in the level of AI utilization (the augmentation gap) among members.
- Confirm whether that gap has been converted into a relational power difference such as in voice or influence over decision-making.
- Reveal the risk of communication asymmetry in advance.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when a judgment on the fairness of the AI-augmentation gap is produced and any existing risk of communication asymmetry is explicitly revealed.
