# Complementary Fit — Goal

The goal is to evaluate whether the collaboration is actually a structure in which human strengths and AI strengths complement each other.

## Objectives
- Identify the respective strengths of the human and the AI participating in the collaboration.
- Confirm whether the two sets of strengths complement each other without overlapping or leaving gaps.
- Reveal the points where complementarity of strengths is insufficient.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when a judgment on whether human and AI strengths complement each other is produced.
