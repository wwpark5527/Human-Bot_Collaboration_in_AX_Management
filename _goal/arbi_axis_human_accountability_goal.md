# Human Accountability — Goal

The goal is to confirm whether final judgment, approval, and responsibility remain with the human in a collaboration the AI intervened in.

## Objectives
- Identify the points of final judgment and approval in the collaboration.
- Confirm whether the actor executing those points is a human.
- Reveal in advance the risk that the locus of accountability becomes unclear.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when a judgment on whether human responsibility remains is produced and any existing risk of unclear accountability is explicitly revealed.
