# Manipulation Risk — Goal

The goal is to confirm whether AI was used not as a tool that supports collaboration but as a means of securing strategic advantage over the other party or of applying coercion.

## Objectives
- Identify whether the use of AI conforms to the purpose of collaboration or is skewed toward the purpose of securing advantage.
- Confirm whether there are indications of coercion or threat by means of AI.
- Reveal in advance the risk of collaboration being impaired by misuse of AI.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when a judgment on whether AI was misused (for strategic advantage or coercion) is produced.
