# Psychological & Trust Stability — Goal

The goal is to confirm whether feelings of surveillance, alienation, decision fatigue, and burden of responsibility among the members collaborating with AI have not accumulated to an excessive level.

## Objectives
- Ascertain the level of surveillance and alienation members feel.
- Confirm whether decision fatigue and burden of responsibility caused by AI intervention have accumulated.
- Reveal in advance the risk of psychological and trust burden becoming excessive.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when a judgment on the level of psychological and trust burden on members is produced.
