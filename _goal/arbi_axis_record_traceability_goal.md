# Record & Traceability — Goal

The goal is to confirm whether the history of rationale, revisions, approvals, and materials used in the collaboration remains in a form that can be re-verified after the fact.

## Objectives
- Identify whether the history of materials created, revised, and approved during the collaboration process has been recorded.
- Confirm whether the source of the supporting material and the circumstances of its use are traceable.
- Reveal in advance the risk that re-verification becomes impossible because history is missing.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when a judgment on whether the collaboration history is recorded and re-verifiable is produced.
