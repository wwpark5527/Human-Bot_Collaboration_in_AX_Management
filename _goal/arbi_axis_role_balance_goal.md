# Role Balance — Goal

The goal is to evaluate whether the roles needed in a collaboration the AI intervened in have been assigned to fit the situation.

## Objectives
- Identify the roles the collaboration situation requires.
- Evaluate whether the actual role assignment conforms to that requirement.
- Reveal imbalance risks such as role vacancy, excess, and concentration.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when a judgment on the adequacy of role assignment, comparing the needed roles with the roles actually assigned, is produced and any existing imbalance risk is explicitly revealed.
