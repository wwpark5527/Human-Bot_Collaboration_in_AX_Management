# Utterance Agency — Goal

The goal is to make clear whether the judgments and utterances made during the collaboration are attributed to the human, to the AI, or to a joint output.

## Objectives
- Identify the individual utterances and judgments made in the collaboration.
- Distinguish the generating agent (human/AI/joint) of each utterance and judgment.
- Reveal the utterances and judgments whose agent is unclear.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when a judgment on the attribution of the agent of an utterance or judgment is produced for every object of evaluation.
