# Augmented Role Balance Index (ARBI, Augmented Role Balance Index) — Goal

The goal is to evaluate, when AI intervenes in collaboration and communication between humans, whether the division of roles between humans and AI operates healthily and accountably.

## Objectives
- Evaluate how healthily the roles of humans and AI are divided.
- Evaluate whether the roles of humans and AI complement and verify each other.
- Evaluate whether human accountability, communication fairness, and recordability are maintained in situations of AI intervention.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when role balance, utterance agency, authority boundaries, human accountability, communication fairness, and recordability are measured together and made into an index in AI-intervened human-human collaboration.
