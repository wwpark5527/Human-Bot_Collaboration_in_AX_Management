# Augmentation — Goal

The objective is to design bots not as a replacement for human labor but as an amplifier of human capability, and thereby to establish across the organization a structure of mutual learning and co-evolution in which humans and bots grow together.

## Objectives
- Apply across the organization the augmentation principle of designing bots not as a replacement for human labor but as an amplifier of human capability.
- Pursue human augmentation (physical and mental) and bot augmentation (context, value, learning) simultaneously to achieve mutual learning and co-evolution.
- Design a positive-sum rather than zero-sum structure, instill in members the perception that "AI strengthens me," and raise acceptance of organizational AX.

## Success Criteria
The objective is judged to be achieved when humans and bots perceive each other's capabilities as a relation of amplification rather than replacement, when augmented humans (AH) and augmented bots (AB) are actually formed and produce results together, and when members accept AI as a means of self-strengthening rather than as a threat.
