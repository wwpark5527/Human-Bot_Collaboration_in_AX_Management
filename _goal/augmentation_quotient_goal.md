# Augmentation Quotient (AQ) — Goal

The goal is to measure "the ability to collaborate with AI", which AI Utilization (AIU) alone does not capture, by combining it with role-centered use, level of collaboration, and level of governance compliance, thereby ascertaining a member's augmentation potential and preparing the input for calculating the higher-order indicator, the Augmented Human Index (AHI).

## Objectives
- Combine the degree of role-centered use (Role Alignment) with the member's AIU (usage) and reflect it.
- Reflect the level of human-bot collaboration (Collaboration) in the AQ calculation.
- Reflect the level of ethics/security/approval compliance (Governance) in the AQ calculation.
- Discriminate actual differences in collaboration capability even among members with the same AIU and connect this to the input for AHI measurement.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the value produced by the formula AQ = AIU x Role Alignment x Collaboration x Governance meaningfully discriminates differences in actual AI collaboration ability even among members with the same simple usage.
