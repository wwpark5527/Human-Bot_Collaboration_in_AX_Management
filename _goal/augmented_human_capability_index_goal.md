# Augmented Human Capability Index (AHCI) — Goal

The goal is to shift the object of augmented-human (AH) evaluation from a graded "person themselves" to "the capability to work with AI", and thereby to diagnose human-bot collaboration readiness from a growth- and development-centered perspective.

## Objectives
- Reflect the human augmentation degree (whether the human is moving closer to AH) in the diagnosis.
- Reflect the bot cooperation degree (whether the bot is moving closer to BH) and the human-bot alignment degree in the diagnosis.
- Reflect output fidelity (whether the results are faithful and evidence-based) and the rate of improvement (whether the relationship improves over time) in the diagnosis.
- Connect the diagnostic results naturally with the HBRM system and with education and development plans.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when, by synthesizing the five diagnostic elements (human augmentation degree, bot cooperation degree, human-bot alignment degree, output fidelity, rate of improvement), it can be confirmed whether "this human is actually being augmented while working with this bot."
