# Augmented Human Index (AHI) — Goal

The goal is to distinguish a person who merely uses AI a lot from a person who works together with AI and is truly augmented (AH), and, as the final stage of the measurement system running from AIU (usage) to AQ (potential), to determine the realization of augmentation.

## Objectives
- Reflect changes in the member's Performance in the AHI calculation.
- Reflect changes in the member's Learning speed in the AHI calculation.
- Reflect changes in the member's Influence, Innovation, and Adaptability in the AHI calculation.
- Synthesize the five elements to make the final judgment on "whether that person is actually an AH."

## Success Criteria
Success is regarded as achieved when, from the result produced by the formula AHI = f(Performance, Learning, Influence, Innovation, Adaptability), the person is judged to be an AH if increased problem-solving ability, increased learning speed, improved decision-making, and improved collaboration ability are confirmed, and is judged not to be an AH if it is confirmed that, with declining judgment and reduced creativity, they depend solely on AI.
