# AI-Centered Path — Goal

The goal is to provide a path suited to situations in AH-AH communication where processing efficiency is the top priority.

## Objectives
- Have the AI relay and structure messages so as to raise the speed of communication, and have the human provide only the purpose and the approval criteria.
- Establish a structure that secures processing efficiency (Human A ↔ AI A ↔ AI B ↔ Human B).
- Make it recognized that, in exchange for gaining efficiency, the human's original intent can change during the AI's summarizing and restructuring.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the AI relays and structures messages quickly so that processing efficiency is secured and the risk of distorting human intent is managed.
