# Human-Centered Path — Goal

The goal is to provide a path suited to situations in AH-AH communication where preserving human meaning, emotion, and trust relationships is the top priority.

## Objectives
- Keep the agent of utterance and judgment with the human, and keep the AI in the background support role of organizing, analyzing, reviewing, and drafting.
- Establish a communication structure in which human meaning and emotion and trust relationships are preserved without being damaged (AI A ↔ Human A ↔ Human B ↔ AI B).
- Identify the situations where meaning preservation is needed even at the cost of a drop in speed and level of automation, and have this path chosen.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the agent of utterance and judgment is the human and human meaning, emotion, and trust relationships are preserved.
