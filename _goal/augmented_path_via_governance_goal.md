# Via-Governance Path — Goal

The goal is to provide a path that makes accountability establishment the top priority in AH-AH communication in domains where accountability matters, such as organizations, contracts, legal, finance, public sector, healthcare, and education.

## Objectives
- Enforce that the message passes through the criteria of authority, security, verification, approval, recording, and accountability.
- Establish the structure of passing through a governance context that is essential in domains where accountability matters (Human A ↔ AI A ↔ governance context ↔ AI B ↔ Human B).
- Secure a communication structure with a clear locus of accountability even if the speed becomes slower.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the message passes all of the criteria of authority, security, verification, approval, recording, and accountability so that the locus of accountability is clear.
