# Additional Conditions of Organizational AX — Goal

The goal is to practice the additional conditions, which are not legal or social requirements but greatly raise organizational competitiveness over the mid-to-long term, and thereby lead organizational AX beyond mere survival to success.

## Objectives
- Embed an augmentation-centered philosophy throughout the organization.
- Reflect respect for diversity and complementary fit between humans and bots in organizational design.
- Raise organizational competitiveness by building a common context and a governance context.
- Secure competitive advantage by practicing the additional conditions rather than stopping at meeting the necessary conditions.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to have been achieved when an augmentation-centered philosophy, respect for diversity, complementary fit between humans and bots, the building of a common context, and the building of a governance context are actually confirmed in the operation of organizational AX and lead to enhanced mid-to-long-term competitiveness.
