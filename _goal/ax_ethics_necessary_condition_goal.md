# Ethical Necessary Conditions of Organizational AX — Goal

The goal is to protect the very existence of organizational AX by clearly identifying and enforcing the ethical necessary conditions which, if not kept in the process of realizing organizational AX, drive the whole organization to failure.

## Objectives
- Establish throughout the organization the mandatory compliance line of guaranteeing human dignity, humans' ultimate accountability, explainability, protection of personal and organizational information, prevention of bias and discrimination, safety and reliability, and building an AI governance system.
- Distinguish the necessary conditions from the additional conditions so that organizational members recognize that violating a necessary condition leads directly to the failure of organizational AX.
- Institutionalize AI governance at the level of the operating constitution of organizational AX.
- Secure consistency with the ethical standards required by nations and international bodies.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to have been achieved when it is confirmed that the seven necessary-condition items (guaranteeing human dignity, humans' ultimate accountability, explainability, protection of personal and organizational information, prevention of bias and discrimination, safety and reliability, building an AI governance system) are being kept throughout the operation of organizational AX.
