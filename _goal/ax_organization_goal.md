# AX Organization — Goal

The AX organization aims for organizational AX (the redesign process) to be completed and for the result state in which humans and bots coexist and collaborate to be reached.

## Objectives
- Complete organizational AX (the redesign process) and reach the state of an AX organization in which humans and bots coexist and collaborate.
- Implement, in actual organizational form, the target state pointed to by the title of Chapter 1 of this book — an organization in which humans and bots coexist and collaborate.
- Establish not the sporadic progress of personal AX and organizational AX, but the final result state in which the organizational operating system itself has been redesigned around AI.

## Success Criteria
The AX organization objective is determined to have been achieved when, as a result of the organization's decision-making structure, communication structure, power structure, and collaboration structure having been redesigned around AI, a state in which human members and bots (AI) coexist and collaborate together as members is actually observed.
