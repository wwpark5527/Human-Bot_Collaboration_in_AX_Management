# AI Auditor — Goal

The goal is to establish in the organization the new role that systematically verifies the results produced by AI within an AX organization.

## Objectives
- Establish procedures for verifying the accuracy and reliability of AI outputs.
- Define the scope of AI result verification (errors, hallucination, bias, etc.).
- Connect this role, which did not exist in human-centered organizations, to the existing quality management system.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when the AI result verification procedure is established and it is confirmed that the AI Auditor continuously takes charge of and operates it.
