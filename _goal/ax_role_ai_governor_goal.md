# AI Governor — Goal

The goal is to establish in the organization the new role that manages the accountability and alignment of AI within an AX organization.

## Objectives
- Establish a system that clarifies the locus of accountability for AI decisions and outputs.
- Manage AI behavior so that it is aligned with the organization's goals and ethical standards.
- Connect this role, which did not exist in human-centered organizations, to the existing governance system.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when the AI accountability and alignment management system is established and it is confirmed that the AI Governor continuously takes charge of and operates it.
