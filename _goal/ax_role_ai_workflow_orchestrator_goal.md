# AI Workflow Orchestrator — Goal

The goal is to establish in the organization the new role that systematically designs and coordinates the collaboration flow between humans and AI within an AX organization.

## Objectives
- Design the order of and connection points between human work and AI work within business processes.
- Remove bottlenecks and duplication in the human-AI collaboration flow and guarantee smooth handoff.
- Connect this role, which did not exist in human-centered organizations, to the existing process management system.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when the design of the human-AI collaboration flow is established and it is confirmed that the AI Workflow Orchestrator continuously takes charge of and operates it.
