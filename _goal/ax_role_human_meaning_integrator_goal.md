# Human Meaning Integrator — Goal

The goal is to establish in the organization the new role that connects the results produced by AI to the organization's context and meaning within an AX organization.

## Objectives
- Interpret and connect AI results in a way that fits the organization's goals, context and stakeholder situation.
- Fill the gap in meaning between AI results and organizational reality.
- Connect this role, which did not exist in human-centered organizations, to the existing decision-making system.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when an interpretation of AI results connected to the organizational context is produced and it is confirmed that the Human Meaning Integrator continuously takes charge of and operates it.
