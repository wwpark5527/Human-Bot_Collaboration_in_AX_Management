# Prompt Architect — Goal

The goal is to establish in the organization the new role that systematically designs the interaction between humans and AI within an AX organization.

## Objectives
- Establish principles for designing the interaction (prompts) through which humans convey their intent to AI accurately.
- Standardize human-AI interaction patterns by work context.
- Connect this role, which did not exist in human-centered organizations, to the existing work design system.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when a design for human-AI interaction is established and it is confirmed that the Prompt Architect continuously takes charge of and operates it.
