# Provenance Controller — Goal

The goal is to establish in the organization the new role that manages the provenance and traceability of the data AI uses and generates within an AX organization.

## Objectives
- Record the provenance of the input data used by AI and keep it verifiable.
- Secure the generation history (traceability) of the outputs generated by AI.
- Connect this role, which did not exist in human-centered organizations, to the existing data governance system.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when records of data provenance and traceability are produced and it is confirmed that the Provenance Controller continuously takes charge of and operates them.
