# Trust Manager — Goal

The goal is to establish in the organization the new role that systematically manages the trust that members and the organization have in AI within an AX organization.

## Objectives
- Measure and manage the level of members' trust in AI results and behavior.
- Adjust the risks on both sides of excessive trust (blind faith) and insufficient trust (distrust).
- Connect this role, which did not exist in human-centered organizations, to the existing risk management and governance systems.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when the result of AI trust management is produced and it is confirmed that the Trust Manager continuously takes charge of and operates it.
