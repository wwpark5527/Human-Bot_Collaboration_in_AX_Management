# Control Stress — Goal

The goal is to understand control stress, which originates from the core question 'who actually controls?', and to prevent governance collapse by clearly redefining the scope of control and decision-making authority.

## Objectives
- Identify early the situations in which the locus of control is unclear, and their causes.
- Grasp the decision-making contexts in which the core question 'who actually controls?' is raised.
- Prevent deterioration into governance collapse by clearly redefining members' scope of control.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to have been achieved when control stress is accurately identified and the decision-making authority and scope of control between humans and AI are clearly redefined so that it does not deteriorate into governance collapse.
