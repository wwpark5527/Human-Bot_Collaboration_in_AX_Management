# Identity Stress — Goal

The goal is to understand identity stress, which originates from the core question 'why am I needed?', and to prevent deterioration into a crisis of existential value by supporting members in healthily redefining their own role and value.

## Objectives
- Identify early the situations in which members cannot confirm their reason for existence.
- Grasp the context and intensity in which the core question 'why am I needed?' is raised.
- Prevent deterioration into a crisis of existential value by putting in place a support system that enables members to redefine their own role and value.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to have been achieved when identity stress is accurately identified and a response to members' demand for redefining role and value (retraining, role redesign, re-establishment of value) is carried out so that it does not deteriorate into a crisis of existential value.
