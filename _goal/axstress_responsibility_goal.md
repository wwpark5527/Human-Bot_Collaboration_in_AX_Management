# Responsibility Stress — Goal

The goal is to understand responsibility stress, which originates in the core question 'who takes responsibility?', and to prevent responsibility avoidance and declining performance by clearly redefining the scope of members' responsibility.

## Objectives
- Identify early the situations in which the attribution of responsibility for outcomes is unclear and the contexts in which they arise.
- Grasp the work situations in which the core question 'who takes responsibility?' remains unresolved.
- Prevent deterioration into responsibility avoidance and declining performance by clearly redefining the scope of members' responsibility.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when responsibility stress is accurately identified, the scope of responsibility between human and AI is clearly redefined, and it has not deteriorated into responsibility avoidance and declining performance.
