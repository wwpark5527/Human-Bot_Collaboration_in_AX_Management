# Trust Stress — Goal

The goal is to understand human-bot trust stress, which originates from the core question 'can I trust the other party?', and to prevent a collapse of human-AI trust through the design of trust-based cooperation.

## Objectives
- Identify early the situations in which humans and bots cannot trust each other, and their causes.
- Grasp the interaction contexts in which the core question 'can I trust the other party?' is raised.
- Prevent deterioration into a collapse of human-AI trust by designing trust-based cooperation.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to have been achieved when trust stress is accurately identified and a design of trust-based cooperation grounded in explainability and transparency is introduced so that it does not deteriorate into a collapse of human-AI trust.
