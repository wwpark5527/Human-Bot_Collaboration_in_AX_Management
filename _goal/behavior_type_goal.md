# Behavior Type vs Personality Type — Goal

The goal is to carry out organizational management on the basis of the direct diagnosis of the behavior types that people (and bots) actually exhibit, rather than the indirect estimation of personality types.

## Objectives
- Carry out team role (TR) diagnosis on the basis of behavior type rather than personality type.
- Accurately diagnose which behavior types an individual actually exhibits, might exhibit, or fails to exhibit.
- Continuously re-evaluate actual behavior, which varies with learned experience and effort.
- Take behavior-type-based diagnosis as a common axis that can be extended and applied not only to human members but also to bot members.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when organizational management is grounded in the direct measurement and diagnosis of behavior type rather than in the estimation of personality type, and when this distinction is consistently applied to both humans and bots in a human-bot coexistence organization (AX organization).
