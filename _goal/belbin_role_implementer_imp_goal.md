# Implementer (IMP) — Goal

The goal is to identify holders of the Implementer (IMP) team role and have them exercise the implementation function as those responsible for the establishment of AI operational processes in an AX organization.

## Objectives
- Accurately identify the Implementer (IMP) disposition (implementation) within the team.
- Reinterpret and place the Implementer role as the establishment of AI operational processes in the AX organization context.
- Distinguish the part of repetitive execution work that an AI bot can directly perform from the part a human Implementer takes on.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when holders of the Implementer (IMP) disposition are identified, when those people actually contribute to the establishment of AI operational processes in the AX organization, and when the division of execution work with AI bots is clearly designed.
