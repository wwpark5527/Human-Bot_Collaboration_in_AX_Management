# Monitor-Evaluator (ME) — Goal

The goal is to identify holders of the Monitor-Evaluator (ME) team role and have them exercise the analysis and judgment function as AI-output verifiers in an AX organization.

## Objectives
- Accurately identify the Monitor-Evaluator (ME) disposition (analysis/judgment) within the team.
- Reinterpret and place the Monitor-Evaluator role as an AI-output verifier in the AX organization context.
- Distinguish the part of analytical work that an AI bot can directly perform from the part a human Monitor-Evaluator takes on.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when holders of the Monitor-Evaluator (ME) disposition are identified, when those people actually contribute to analysis/judgment work as AI-output verifiers in the AX organization, and when the division of work with AI bots is clearly designed.
