# Resource Investigator (RI) — Goal

The goal is to identify holders of the Resource Investigator (RI) team role and have them exercise the external exploration function as those responsible for AI-ecosystem exploration in an AX organization.

## Objectives
- Accurately identify the Resource Investigator (RI) disposition (external exploration) within the team.
- Reinterpret and place the Resource Investigator role as AI-ecosystem exploration in the AX organization context.
- Distinguish the part of information-seeking work that an AI bot can directly perform from the part a human Resource Investigator takes on.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when holders of the Resource Investigator (RI) disposition are identified, when those people actually contribute to AI-ecosystem exploration in the AX organization, and when the division of exploration work with AI bots is clearly designed.
