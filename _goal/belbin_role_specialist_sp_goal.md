# Specialist (SP) — Goal

The goal is to identify holders of the Specialist (SP) team role and have them exercise the expert-knowledge function as domain + AI hybrid experts in an AX organization.

## Objectives
- Accurately identify the Specialist (SP) disposition (providing expert knowledge) within the team.
- Reinterpret and place the Specialist role as a domain + AI hybrid expert in the AX organization context.
- Secure hybrid expertise that combines expert knowledge in a specific domain with the capability to use AI.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when holders of the Specialist (SP) disposition are identified, when those people actually contribute as domain + AI hybrid experts in the AX organization, and when the combination of the domain of expertise and AI capability is clearly designed.
