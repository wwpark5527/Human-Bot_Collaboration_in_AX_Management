# Teamworker (TW) — Goal

The goal is to identify holders of the Teamworker (TW) team role and have them exercise the relational harmony function as those responsible for promoting acceptance of human-AI collaboration in an AX organization.

## Objectives
- Accurately identify the Teamworker (TW) disposition (relational harmony) within the team.
- Reinterpret and place the Teamworker role as promoting acceptance of human-AI collaboration in the AX organization context.
- Have them exercise the relational harmony function so that members emotionally accept collaboration with AI bots.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when holders of the Teamworker (TW) disposition are identified, when those people actually contribute to promoting acceptance of human-AI collaboration in the AX organization, and when members' acceptance of AI collaboration rises.
