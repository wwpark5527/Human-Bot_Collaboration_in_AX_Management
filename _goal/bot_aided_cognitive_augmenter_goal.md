# Cognitive Augmenter — Goal

The goal is to augment the human's decision-making capability in the Bot-Aided TRB structure by handling the human's cognitive burden on their behalf through information provision and coordination support.

## Objectives
- Organize the data needed for decision-making and reduce the human's information-processing burden.
- Generate alternatives and support the human's judgment.
- Perform risk simulation and schedule optimization to raise the quality of decision-making.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the results of data organization, alternative generation, risk simulation, and schedule optimization are produced and it is confirmed that the human's cognitive burden is actually eased through them.
