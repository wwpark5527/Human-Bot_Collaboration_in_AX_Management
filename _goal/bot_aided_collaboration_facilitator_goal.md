# Collaboration Facilitator — Goal

The goal is to observe and sense friction in the team collaboration process in the Bot-Aided TRB structure and to reduce it.

## Objectives
- Analyze meeting speaking time to grasp imbalances in participation.
- Detect role conflicts and flag them early.
- Detect communication bottlenecks (obstacles) so that the collaboration flow runs smoothly.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the results of meeting speaking-time analysis, role-conflict detection, and communication-bottleneck detection are produced and it is confirmed that team collaboration friction actually decreases.
