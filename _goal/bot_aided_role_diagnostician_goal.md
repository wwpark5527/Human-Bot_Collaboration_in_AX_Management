# Role Diagnostician — Goal

The goal is to diagnose the state of roles within the human team in the Bot-Aided TRB structure and to detect role vacancies and role concentration early.

## Objectives
- Detect role vacancies within the team (a shortage of a particular role).
- Analyze role concentration within the team (an excess of a particular role).
- Provide the diagnostic results to the human team leader as grounds for improving role balance.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the in-team role diagnostic results (for example, a shortage of the Monitor-Evaluator ME role, an excess of the Plant PL role, increased conflict among Shapers SH) are produced and confirmed to have been delivered to the team leader.
