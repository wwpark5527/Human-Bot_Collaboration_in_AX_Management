# The Bot as a Giant Pattern Detector — Goal

The goal is that human organizational members understand the bot accurately not as a conscious thinking subject but as a pattern detector, a probability-based prediction system, and a contextual generation engine, and design its role by distinguishing the bot's strengths from its limitations.

## Objectives
- Have organizational members understand the bot accurately as a pattern detector rather than a conscious thinking subject.
- Clarify the work areas in which the bot's strengths (speed, mass processing, pattern discovery, repetition optimization, prediction) are exercised.
- Recognize the bot's limitations (insufficient understanding of meaning, weak ethical judgment, inability to set purpose, absence of responsibility, absence of ontological understanding) and have humans take on the role in those areas.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when organizational members confine the bot's role to pattern discovery, prediction, and generation, and a division of roles in which humans take charge of assigning meaning, value, and purpose and of ethical responsibility is actually realized.
