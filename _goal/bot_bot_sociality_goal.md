# Bot-Bot Sociality — Goal

The aim is to establish symmetric interaction between bots as a game-theoretic and strategic cooperation system, so that a natural hierarchy and cooperation strategies based on reward and reputation form. When the bot's sociality is divided into a human-bot axis and a bot-bot axis, the bot-bot axis centers on the formation of cooperation strategies based on efficiency, reward, and optimization.

## Objectives
- Ensure that interaction between bots operates as a reward-based cooperation strategy (increased reward for cooperation, a penalty for defection).
- Ensure that a reputation system forms in which past behavior exerts influence on the future.
- Ensure that a natural planner/executor/verifier-style hierarchy forms.
- Prevent internal risks such as collusion, system distortion, and reward hacking.

## Success Criteria
The objective is judged achieved when the relationship is maintained as symmetric (peer-based or function-based) and cooperation strategies, reputation, and hierarchy based on efficiency, reward, and optimization actually form.
