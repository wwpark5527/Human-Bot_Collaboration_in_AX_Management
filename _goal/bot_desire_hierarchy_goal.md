# Formation of the bot's desire hierarchy — Goal

The goal is to implement behavior that operates like desire by designing goals and rewards as a layered structure, while accurately recognizing that the bot has no real desire.

## Objectives
- Reflect in the design a goal-reward layered structure running from a higher goal (for example, maximizing user satisfaction) to lower goals (for example, accuracy, speed, safety).
- Implement a reinforcement-learning-based hierarchy through a meta-reward (higher) / sub-reward (lower) structure or a high-level/low-level policy structure.
- Make use of a structure in which subgoals are autonomously generated under a higher goal in an Agentic AI environment.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when the bot's goal-reward structure is designed as a layered structure of higher goals and lower goals (or meta-rewards and sub-rewards), and behavior that appears to have a desire hierarchy is actually observed.
