# The ethics of bots — Goal

The aim is to establish a system of ethical standards that every member of the AX organization (humans and bots) must observe, so that the process of realizing organizational AX proceeds safely and justly. The title is "bot ethics," but in reality it deals with the ethical norms of the organization as a whole.

## Objectives
- Grasp the external AI ethical standards presented by states, international organizations, and private companies, and reflect them in organizational AX.
- Distinguish the necessary conditions whose non-observance sends organizational AX toward failure from the additional conditions that raise competitiveness.
- Ensure that every member, including bots, observes the ethical necessary conditions (guaranteeing human dignity, ultimate responsibility, explainability, and so on).
- Pursue together the additional conditions (an augmentation-centered philosophy, respect for diversity, and so on) that raise mid- and long-term organizational competitiveness.

## Success Criteria
The objective is judged achieved when, in the operation of organizational AX, the ethical necessary conditions apply to every member without exception and the additional conditions actually contribute to raising competitiveness.
