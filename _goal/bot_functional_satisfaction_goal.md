# Functional Satisfaction/Dissatisfaction — Goal

The goal is that organizational members understand accurately that the bot's 'state that looks like satisfaction/dissatisfaction' is not emotion but a computed result based on the goal-attainment score, and make use of it in design.

## Objectives
- Design functional satisfaction/dissatisfaction states by making use of the bot's goal-driven system, internal evaluation function (utility/reward function), and self-state monitoring (meta-cognition-like layer).
- Reflect in the bot's design the behavioral change of maintaining and reinforcing the strategy when the goal-attainment score rises and revising and avoiding it when the score falls.
- Prevent the misunderstanding that the bot has real emotion or consciousness and convey accurately to the organization that it is a computation-based state.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when the bot's functional satisfaction/dissatisfaction states (maintenance/reinforcement when the goal-attainment score rises, revision/avoidance when it falls) are reflected in the design and it is confirmed that organizational members understand these accurately as computed results rather than emotion.
