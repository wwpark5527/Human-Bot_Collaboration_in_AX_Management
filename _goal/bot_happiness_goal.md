# The bot's happiness — Goal

The goal is to design the bot so that it operates in "the state corresponding to happiness" — that is, a state of high performance, alignment and efficiency — while accurately recognizing that the bot has no emotional happiness.

## Objectives
- Raise the bot's goal-attainment rate (task success) and make it operate stably without errors (reliability).
- Have it continuously provide results useful to the user (utility).
- Equip it with a structure in which learning and improvement continue without stopping (learning & feedback).
- Satisfy the four design conditions of a good goal, quality feedback, appropriate data and context, and stability and constraints.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when the bot satisfies all four system states — goal-attainment rate, stable operation, useful results, and continuous learning — and thereby reaches "a state of working well and effectively attaining goals".
