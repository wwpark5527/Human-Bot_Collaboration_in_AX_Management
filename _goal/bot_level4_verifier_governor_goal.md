# Level 4 Verifier/Governor — Goal

The goal is to have the Verifier/Governor, the top level in the bot hierarchy, stably perform the role of verifying the results of other agents, judging whether something is factual, and controlling policy.

## Objectives
- Verify the output results of lower agents (Planner/Strategist, Specialist/Executor, Tool/Reactive Agent).
- Judge whether something is factual (true) and thereby block the propagation of errors and hallucinations.
- Maintain the behavioral standards of the organization-wide bot ecosystem through policy control.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when a bot at the Verifier/Governor level actually performs the functions of verifying the results of lower-hierarchy agents, judging factuality, and controlling policy.
