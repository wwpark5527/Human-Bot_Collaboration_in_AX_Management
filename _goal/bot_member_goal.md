# B: Bot — Goal

In the classification of AX organization member types, the goal is to clearly distinguish the non-augmented bot (B) from H·AH·AB, and to establish a foundation on which the organization can support the path by which B is converted into AB (augmented bot).

## Objectives
- Clearly define the scope of the specific-task automation role performed by B-type members (bots).
- Position B not within a 'human vs AI bot' opposition but within the four-type classification system (H/AH/AB/B).
- Establish a foundation to support the augmentation conversion path from B to AB (embedding of common and governance context).

## Success Criteria
The goal is determined to be achieved when B is clearly distinguished from and placed apart from H/AH/AB in the classification of organizational members, when the coexistence and collaboration structure between B-type bots and humans and augmented bots actually works, and when the augmentation path from B to AB is presented through the organizational AX OS.
