# Addition of TR by bots — Goal

The goal is to define and introduce into the organization the new AX roles (AI Governor, AI Auditor, Prompt Architect, AI Workflow Orchestrator, Human Meaning Integrator, Trust Manager, Provenance Controller) that did not exist in a human-centered organization and that arise as the AI bot takes on roles.

## Objectives
- Clearly define the 7 new AX roles that did not exist in a human-centered organization.
- Specify the responsibilities of each new role (accountability and alignment management, result verification, interaction design, collaboration flow design, meaning connection, trust management, provenance and traceability management).
- Incorporate the new roles into the organizational structure, distinguishing them from the existing human TR and functional role system.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when all 7 new AX roles are defined and each role is confirmed to be a new role that did not exist in a human-centered organization.
