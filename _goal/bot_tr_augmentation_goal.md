# Augmentation of TR by bots — Goal

The goal is to establish a structure in which the bot uses its own strengths (computation, memory, exploration, repetition, pattern recognition, real-time optimization) to augment (reinforce) human performance across the four TRs of Monitor-Evaluator (ME), Completer-Finisher (CF), Resource Investigator (RI), and Coordinator (CO).

## Objectives
- Define, for each of the four TRs — ME (Monitor-Evaluator), CF (Completer-Finisher), RI (Resource Investigator), CO (Coordinator) — the concrete reinforcement the bot provides.
- State the reinforcement effect of each TR (reduced intuition bias, reduced fatigue, expanded exploration scope, reduced mediation cost) in a verifiable form.
- Design the bot's intervention so that it operates as augmentation (reinforcement) of the human TR rather than as its replacement.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when, for each of the 4 TRs (ME/CF/RI/CO), the reinforcement the bot provides and the resulting effect are confirmed.
