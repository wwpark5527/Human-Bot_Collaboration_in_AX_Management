# Complementation of TR by bots — Goal

The goal is to reinterpret each Belbin TR role into an AX context and thereby establish a human-bot role structure in which the bot does not replace the human but mutually complements them with different strengths.

## Objectives
- Map each of the 9 TRs to a reinterpretation item that the bot can complement in an AX organization.
- Clearly distinguish the areas humans must take on (vision-setting, oversight, meaning-giving, ethics, integrative judgment, conflict mediation, trust-building, accountability) from the areas the bot takes on (computation, memory, exploration, repetition, pattern recognition, real-time optimization).
- Expand overall organizational performance (pie size) through a structure of complementation rather than replacement by bots.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when the AX-organization reinterpretation table for the 9 TRs is drawn up, the human-bot division of roles is designed as a structure of complementation rather than replacement, and an expansion of overall performance is observed.
