# Computational Overload — Goal

The goal is to identify early the situations in which a bot exceeds the limits of its processing capacity, and to mitigate the bot's computational overload before it leads to degraded response quality and task collapse.

## Objectives
- Detect early the situations of excessive parallel task processing, context overflow, memory saturation, token limitation, and increased latency.
- Prevent progression to degraded response quality, increased hallucination, reasoning failure, and task collapse.
- Recognize the bot's computational overload as a functional phenomenon similar to human cognitive overload and include it among the objects of management.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when the signals of computational overload (excessive parallel processing, context overflow, memory saturation, token limitation, increased latency) are identified early and mitigation measures are applied before they lead to degraded response quality, hallucination, reasoning failure, or task collapse.
