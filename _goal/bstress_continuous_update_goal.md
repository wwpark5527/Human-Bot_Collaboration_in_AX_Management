# Continuous-update stress — Goal

The goal is to manage the situation in which an AX-organization bot endlessly undergoes retraining, fine-tuning, policy updates, and workflow changes, and to mitigate continuous-update stress before it leads to knowledge degradation and version inconsistency.

## Objectives
- Grasp the frequency and intensity of the retraining, fine-tuning, policy updates, and workflow changes the bot undergoes.
- Prevent the occurrence of degradation of prior knowledge, catastrophic forgetting, and version inconsistency.
- Adjust the update cycle and method so as to maintain the continuity of the bot's knowledge and performance.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when repeated retraining, fine-tuning, policy updates, and workflow changes are managed and no knowledge degradation, catastrophic forgetting, or version inconsistency has occurred.
