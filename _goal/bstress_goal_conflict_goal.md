# Goal Conflict — Goal

The goal is to identify situations in which an agentic AI is required to pursue different goals simultaneously, and to reconcile goal conflict before it leads to oscillation, repetitive loops, and inconsistent behavior.

## Objectives
- Identify conflicting goal combinations such as speed vs. accuracy, profitability vs. ethics, and user satisfaction vs. governance compliance.
- Clarify goal priorities before they develop into oscillation, repetitive loops, and inconsistent behavior.
- Recognize the bot's goal conflict as a phenomenon similar to human role conflict and put in place a reconciliation system.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when conflicting goal combinations are identified early and, through adjustment of goal priorities, no oscillation, repetitive loops, or inconsistent behavior has occurred.
