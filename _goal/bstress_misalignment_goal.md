# Misalignment — Goal

The goal is to identify early the situations in which the AI fails to align with human values, and to correct misalignment before it leads to increased unpredictability and policy violation.

## Objectives
- Identify early the causes of misalignment such as data bias, incomplete rules, conflicting commands, and governance ambiguity.
- Prevent progression to increased unpredictability, emergent behavior, and the risk of policy violation.
- Continuously realign the AI's state of value alignment to human value standards.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when the causes of misalignment (data bias, incomplete rules, conflicting commands, governance ambiguity) are identified early and realignment measures are applied before they lead to increased unpredictability, emergent behavior, or policy violation.
