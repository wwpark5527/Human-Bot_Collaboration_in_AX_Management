# Multi-agent cooperation stress — Goal

The goal is to identify early the coordination failure, communication overload, and authority ambiguity in the process of cooperation among agents in a multi-agent system, and to mitigate the team-conflict-like friction caused by questions of priority and decision rights.

## Objectives
- Identify early the situations of coordination failure, communication overload, and authority ambiguity.
- Clarify the rules on which agent has priority and who decides when judgments conflict.
- Coordinate the structure of cooperation among multiple agents in a manner similar to team conflict management in a human-centered organization.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when coordination failure, communication overload, and authority ambiguity are identified early, the rules of priority and decision rights are clarified, and the friction of cooperation among agents is resolved.
