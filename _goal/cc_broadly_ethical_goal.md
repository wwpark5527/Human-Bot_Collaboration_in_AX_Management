# Broadly Ethical — Goal

The goal is to discipline the bot (AI model) to make ethical judgments on its own, and thereby realize in organizational AX operations the broadly ethical value, one of the four core values required by the Claude Constitution.

## Objectives
- Have the bot make ethical judgments broadly rather than being confined to particular situations.
- Equip the bot with the ability to judge ethically on the basis of the reason why it should take that behavior.
- Achieve a balance among the four core values together with broadly safe, helpfulness, and compliance with guidelines.
- Reflect a constitutional design that teaches reasons rather than mere prohibitive rules.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when the bot's behavior conforms to the broadly ethical requirement and ethical judgment grounded in the reason why it should take that behavior actually appears.
