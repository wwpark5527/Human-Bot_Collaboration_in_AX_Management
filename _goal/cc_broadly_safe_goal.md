# Broadly Safe — Goal

The goal is to discipline the bot (AI model) so that its judgments and behaviors are broadly safe, and thereby realize in organizational AX operations the broadly safe value, one of the four core values required by the Claude Constitution.

## Objectives
- Have the bot's judgments and behaviors satisfy the safety requirements broadly rather than being confined to particular situations.
- Equip the bot with the judgment ability to avoid unsafe behavior on its own.
- Achieve a balance among the four core values together with broadly ethical, helpfulness, and compliance with guidelines.
- Reflect a constitutional design that teaches the reason why it must be safe rather than mere prohibitive rules.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when the bot's behavior does not depart from the broadly safe requirement and judgment that avoids unsafe behavior on its own actually appears.
