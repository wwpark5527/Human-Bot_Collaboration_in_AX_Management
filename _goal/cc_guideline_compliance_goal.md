# Compliance With Guidelines — Goal

The goal is to discipline the bot (AI model) to follow the instructions it is given, and thereby realize in organizational AX operations compliance with guidelines, one of the four core values required by the Claude Constitution.

## Objectives
- Ensure that the bot's behavior does not depart from the guidelines presented.
- Equip it with the judgment ability to consistently produce behavior that conforms to the instructions.
- Achieve a balance among the four core values together with broadly safe, broadly ethical, and helpfulness.
- Have it understand and comply with the intent of the instructions rather than following them formally.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when the bot's behavior complies with the guidelines presented and behavior conforming to the instructions actually appears.
