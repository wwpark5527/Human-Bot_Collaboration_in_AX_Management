# Labor-union-type resistance — Goal

The goal is to understand the most traditional form of resistance, in which individual anxiety is collectivized through a labor union, and to establish a reasonable negotiation structure for the protection of human creations and for employment stability before demands for limits on automation, mandatory human employment, restrictions on the scope of AI use, and mandatory retraining escalate into a large-scale 'AX labor-rights struggle'.

## Objectives
- Identify early the signs of collective resistance to AI adoption in job families and industries where labor unions are organized.
- Grasp and respond to the four concrete demands: demanding limits on automation, mandating human employment, restricting the scope of AI use, and requiring mandatory retraining.
- Address within the labor-management negotiation structure the core claims of prohibiting the unauthorized training on human creations, restricting AI replacement, and protecting digital-reproduction rights.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when labor-union-type resistance is accurately identified in industries where labor unions are organized (for example, cases of the Hollywood writers' and actors' strike type), an agreed negotiation outcome on the scope of automation, employment stability, retraining opportunities, and protection of creations is reached, and escalation into a large-scale labor-rights struggle is prevented.
