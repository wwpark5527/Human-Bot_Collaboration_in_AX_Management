# Ontological resistance — Goal

The goal is to understand the most powerful collective resistance, which arises from the fundamental question about the meaning of human existence, "Why are humans needed when AI does it better?", and to re-establish the value of human existence through a narrative of augmentation rather than replacement.

## Objectives
- Identify whether the ground of the resistance is a question about the meaning of human existence itself rather than jobs or institutions.
- Block early the path of deterioration leading to cynicism, organizational disengagement, an anti-technology identity, human supremacism, and AI hatred.
- Understand the essence of 'defense of the meaning of human existence' and re-establish the distinctive value of humans through an augmentation narrative rather than a replacement narrative.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when the source of the resistance is accurately identified as a question about the meaning of human existence, and the value of organizational members' existence is re-established through an augmentation narrative (role redesign, expansion of human value, collaboration) without deterioration into cynicism or disengagement.
