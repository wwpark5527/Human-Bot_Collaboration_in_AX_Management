# Political-policy resistance — Goal

The goal is to understand collective resistance at the political-policy level that demands strengthening AI regulation, emphasizing data sovereignty, restricting AI use, and securing human control, and to prepare in advance principles for responding to regulation that conform to the 'human-centered AI' philosophy.

## Objectives
- Track legislative trends such as the U.S. debates over AI regulation laws (AI transparency obligations, copyright regulation, AI safety verification, deepfake regulation).
- Grasp regional regulatory frameworks such as the European EU AI Act (restrictions on high-risk AI, human-oversight obligations, explainability requirements, limits on biometric surveillance).
- Establish in advance within the organization 'human-centered AI' operating principles that conform to the demands for data sovereignty and for securing human control.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when the issues of strengthening AI regulation, data sovereignty, restricting use, and securing human control are accurately identified, and an explainability and human-oversight system conforming to major regulatory trends in the U.S., Europe, and elsewhere is established so as to respond preemptively to political-policy resistance.
