# Professional-group resistance — Goal

The goal is to understand the resistance waged by highly skilled professional groups such as the medical, education, and legal professions on the grounds that their expertise and authority of judgment are being infringed, and to mitigate professional resistance by providing clear answers to the issues of unclear allocation of responsibility, infringement of expertise, and devaluation of professional worth.

## Objectives
- Identify early the collective resistance related to AI adoption in highly skilled professional domains such as the medical, education, and legal fields.
- Grasp and respond to the three core issues of unclear allocation of responsibility, infringement of expertise, and devaluation of professional worth.
- Prepare responses tailored to the concrete concerns of each domain, such as the worsening of medical judgment authority, the degradation of task meaning and the nullification of evaluation, and legal-liability issues.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when professional-group resistance to AI adoption in the medical, education, legal, and other fields is accurately identified, and clear principles on allocation of responsibility, respect for expertise, and professional worth (AI is an assisting tool, the final judgment rests with the professional) are established so that the resistance is mitigated.
