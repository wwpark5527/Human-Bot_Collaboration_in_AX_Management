# Sociocultural resistance — Goal

The goal is to understand the two currents of cultural resistance, 'formation of an anti-AI culture' and 'distrust of the technological elite,' and to respond head-on to the human-first-ism narrative and to the sentiment of distrust toward a few big tech firms so that cultural resistance does not spread into organized resistance.

## Objectives
- Identify early the currents of anti-AI culture formation such as human-first-ism, the Slow AI movement, and the emphasis on Human-made.
- Grasp the grounds of the sentiment of distrust of the technological elite — that "a few big tech firms control human society" — namely AI monopoly, data colonialism, and algorithmic power.
- Prepare an organization-level direction of response before cultural counter-movements such as the 'AI-free art, human-written, and human-creation certification' movement spread like the organic-food movement.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when the grounds and diffusion paths of the two currents, anti-AI culture formation and distrust of the technological elite, are accurately identified, and principles of transparency and respect for creators that respond to concerns about AI monopoly, data colonialism, and algorithmic power are established so that the spread of cultural resistance is mitigated.
