# AI-in-the-loop (human-led + AI assist) — Goal

The goal is to identify the AI-in-the-loop mode of collaboration, in which the human retains the initiative while the AI assists and augments in real time, and to ensure that real-time augmentation takes place stably during the course of the work.

## Objectives
- Identify the AI-in-the-loop type, in which the initiative lies with the human and the AI comes 'inside' the loop to assist in real time.
- Design and operate a structure of real-time augmentation during the course of the work rather than intervention at the approval point (HITL).
- Deploy human leaders equipped with real-time collaboration capability at the level of a doctoral candidate.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when the human leads the work while continuously receiving the AI's real-time augmentation so that the quality and speed of performance improve, and that intervention takes place throughout the course of the work rather than at the approval point.
