# Autonomous AI — Goal

The objective is to identify the Autonomous AI collaboration mode in which AI performs work autonomously without human intervention, and to manage the reliability of results and the locus of accountability even in situations where human intervention is minimized.

## Objectives
- Identify the Autonomous AI type, in which humans are effectively absent from both execution and supervision, distinguishing it from Human-on-the-loop.
- Secure the quality and reliability of the results of AI autonomous execution even in situations where human intervention is minimized.
- Deploy AI with the full autonomous execution capability of a researcher who has become a professor, limited to the domains where it is needed.

## Success Criteria
The objective is judged to be achieved when AI performs work autonomously without prior human approval or subsequent human monitoring, when the results conform to the organization's standards, and when the locus of accountability can be traced after the fact should a problem arise.
