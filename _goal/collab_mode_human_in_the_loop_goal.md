# Human-in-the-loop (human-approval-centered) — Goal

The goal is to identify the Human-in-the-loop mode of collaboration, in which the human gives final approval inside the loop to the results proposed by the AI, and to guarantee that every deliverable passes through human approval.

## Objectives
- Identify the Human-in-the-loop type, the structure of AI proposal and human final approval.
- Design the process so that the human is positioned 'inside' the loop and every result passes through the approval procedure.
- Secure an approval point clearly distinguished from the other degrees of autonomy such as AI-in-the-loop, Human-on-the-loop, and Autonomous AI.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when the human's final approval procedure actually operates for every result proposed by the AI and no unapproved result is finalized as a deliverable.
