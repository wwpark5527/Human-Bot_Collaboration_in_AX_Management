# Human-on-the-loop (AI-autonomous + human oversight) — Goal

The goal is to identify the Human-on-the-loop mode of collaboration, in which the AI performs most of the work and the human monitors and intervenes from 'on' the loop, and to operate an after-the-fact, continuous oversight system stably.

## Objectives
- Identify the Human-on-the-loop type, in which execution has passed to the AI and the human is in the position of an overseer.
- Design an after-the-fact, continuous monitoring system rather than prior approval, so as to keep autonomous AI execution in check.
- Assign human overseers with oversight capability at the level of a postdoctoral researcher.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when the AI performs most of the work autonomously, the human maintains a position from which the results can be continuously monitored and intervened in, and intervention can be made promptly when a problem arises.
