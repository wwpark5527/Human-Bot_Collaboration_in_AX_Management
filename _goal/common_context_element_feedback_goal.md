# Feedback — Goal

The goal is to feed the experience of errors and failures back into the next common context, making the common context an updated standard rather than a fixed document.

## Objectives
- Identify and record the errors and outcomes that occur in the course of performing work.
- Actually reflect the identified errors and outcomes in the next version of the common context standard.
- Make the common context a structure that is continuously updated through organizational learning rather than a document made once and done with.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when the errors and outcomes that occurred are actually reflected in the next standard (the updated next version of the common context).
