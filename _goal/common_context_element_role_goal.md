# Role — Goal

The goal is to determine who does what, and thereby to put in place an arrangement of responsibility by role so that the subject who will interpret, verify, approve, record, and improve AI results is not unclear.

## Objectives
- Identify the human roles running from problem definer, context composer, AI execution director, result interpreter, verifier, responsibility judge, evidence manager, to improvement reflector.
- Arrange clearly what each role must confirm, interpret, verify, approve, record, and improve with respect to AI results.
- Clarify the accountability structure that lets one judge how far AI results may be used.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when 'who must confirm what' is not unclear and the subject who will interpret, verify, approve, record, and improve the result is designated.
