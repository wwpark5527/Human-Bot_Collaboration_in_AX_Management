# Source — Goal

The goal is to make it possible to confirm what materials AI relied on as grounds and whether they have been verified, thereby preventing source confusion in which unverified information is stated as if it were fact.

## Objectives
- Have AI specify the source of the materials it relied on as grounds for its answers and outputs.
- Put in place a tracking system through which the source, standards, revisions, and approval process can be confirmed after the fact.
- Prevent in advance cases of source confusion in which unverified information is presented as if it were fact.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when the source, standards, revisions, and approval process can be confirmed after the fact and the accurate source and the original-text confirmation process are built into the system.
