# AH-AH communication — Goal

The goal is to judge symmetry in communication between augmented humans by the level of augmentation power rather than by whether one holds AI, and to make contribution conflicts identifiable and adjustable.

## Objectives
- Make it possible to judge symmetry by comparing the augmentation power of both sides (A, B) across the seven elements of AI performance, quality of the common context, accessible materials, execution authority, human judgment, level of governance, and the recording/verification system.
- Make identifiable and adjustable not role vacancies but contribution conflicts (differing criteria, interpretations, verification states, and responsibility judgments).
- Make it possible to select the path suited to the situation among the three communication paths: human-centered, AI-centered, and via governance.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when symmetry is evaluated on the basis of the seven elements of augmentation power and, when a contribution conflict arises, meaning, conditions, judgment, and responsibility are coordinated through one of the three paths.
