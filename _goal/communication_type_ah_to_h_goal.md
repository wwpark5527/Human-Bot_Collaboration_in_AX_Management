# AH-H communication — Goal

The goal is to recognize explicitly the inherent asymmetry between an augmented human and a non-augmented human, and to ensure that communication does not take place with the augmentation capability hidden.

## Objectives
- Recognize explicitly the asymmetric structure between an augmented human (A: human + AI + common context) and a non-augmented human (B: human alone).
- Prescribe in advance the six fairness requirements (disclosing AI intervention, distinguishing AI-generated content from human judgment, notifying that the counterpart's utterances are recorded, limiting the scope of analysis, human re-confirmation of important agreements, and specifying the party bearing final responsibility).
- Make it possible to judge whether there is a role vacancy (a vacancy in the contributions of interpretation, verification, recording, and response).

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when all six fairness requirements are satisfied.
