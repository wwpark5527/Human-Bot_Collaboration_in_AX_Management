# Behavioral complementation — Goal

The goal is to combine the bot's capacity to perform repetitive, routine behavior with the human's capacity to respond to non-routine and exceptional situations, and thereby to settle in the domain of work execution a division of roles by way of augmentation rather than replacement of humans.

## Objectives
- Divide the roles so that the bot takes on repetitive, routine behavior such as the routine workflow, and the human takes on responses to non-routine and exceptional situations such as crisis handling.
- Make clear to the organization that behavioral complementation is not a reduction of the human's execution role but an augmentation that concentrates it on the capability of responding to exceptions and crises.
- Establish collaboration routines (such as an escalation system) in which the bot's performance of routine work and the human's response to exceptions are smoothly linked.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when the division of roles in which the bot performs repetitive, routine behavior (the routine workflow) and the human performs non-routine, exceptional situations (crisis handling) is actually realized, and the transition (escalation) from bot to human works smoothly when an exceptional situation arises.
