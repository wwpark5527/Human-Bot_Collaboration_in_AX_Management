# Cognitive complementation — Goal

The goal is to combine human intuition, creativity, and imagination with the bot's capabilities of computation, search, and memory, and to establish in organizational work a collaborative structure that fills each other's cognitive functions rather than replacing either side.

## Objectives
- Divide roles so that tasks requiring intuition and creativity, such as strategic judgment and the interpretation of meaning, are taken by humans, while tasks requiring data simulation, search, and memory are taken by bots.
- Make clear to the members of the organization that cognitive complementation is not the replacement of humans but the extension of human capability ('augmentation, not replacement').
- Establish a collaboration routine in which humans strategically interpret the bot's analytical results in actual decision-making settings such as strategy meetings.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to have been achieved when, in cognitive tasks such as strategy meetings, a division of roles is actually carried out in which the bot takes charge of computation, search, and memory (for example, data simulation) and the human takes charge of intuition, creativity, and imagination (for example, interpreting the strategic meaning), and when this is recognized as augmentation rather than the replacement of humans.
