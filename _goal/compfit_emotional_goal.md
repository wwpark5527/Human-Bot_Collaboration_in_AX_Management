# Emotional complementation — Goal

The goal is to combine the human capability of providing empathy, trust, and psychological stability with the bot's capability of immediate response and fatigue-free continuous support, and thereby to strengthen the emotional support system for members in the mode of augmentation rather than the replacement of humans.

## Objectives
- Divide roles so that support requiring empathy, such as the formation of psychological safety and trust, is taken by humans, while 24-hour immediate response and continuous feedback are taken by bots.
- Make clear to the organization that emotional complementation does not replace but extends the emotional role of the human leader.
- Establish an emotional support collaboration routine in which an AI coach and a human leader operate together (for example, always-on support + regular checks of psychological safety).

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to have been achieved when a division of roles is actually carried out in which bot-based support such as an AI coach takes charge of immediacy, freedom from fatigue, and continuity while the human leader takes charge of empathy, trust, and psychological safety, and when this is recognized as augmentation rather than the replacement of the human emotional role.
