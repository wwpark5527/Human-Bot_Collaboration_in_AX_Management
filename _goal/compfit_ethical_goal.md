# Ethical complementation — Goal

The goal is to combine the bot's rule-based consistency with the human capability of value judgment, social responsibility, and context-based adjustment, and thereby to leave the authority to judge the legitimacy of the rules themselves clearly with the human.

## Objectives
- Divide roles so that the bot takes charge of the consistent application of the given rules, while the human takes charge of judging whether the rules themselves are legitimate.
- Make the organization clearly aware of the limitation that a bot merely follows rules and cannot judge the legitimacy of the rules on its own.
- Establish a governance system that ensures the authority to judge the legitimacy of the rules always remains with the human.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to have been achieved when a structure actually operates in which the bot carries out the consistent observance of the rules, and the human ultimately takes charge of the legitimacy of the rules themselves, value judgment, social responsibility, and context-based adjustment.
