# Context Capital Access Right (Context Access Right) — Goal

The goal is to make every member, beyond formal access at the level of an AI account or a software usage license, actually access the organization's purpose, standards, materials, and approval structure (context capital) that make it possible to use AI meaningfully, and thereby to prevent the new stratification of the AI era that arises from the monopolization of context capital.

## Objectives
- Distinguish formal access such as AI accounts and usage licenses from actual access to context capital, and specify the latter as the organization's concept of access right.
- Manage context capital so that it is not monopolized by a few according to an individual's sense, language ability, information access, rank, and departmental authority.
- Convert context capital into a common production base for the whole organization, so that the ability to use AI becomes a common organizational capability rather than an individual privilege.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when members, regardless of whether they hold an AI account, can access the organization's purpose, standards, materials, and approval structure and use AI meaningfully.
