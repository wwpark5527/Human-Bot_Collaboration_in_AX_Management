# Context Capital — Goal

The goal is to build the purpose, (judgment) standards, language, materials, format, verification standards, and approval standards needed to make AI work properly into the organization's accumulated operating asset, and thereby to secure the power to direct AI as an organizational capability.

## Objectives
- Systematically accumulate the organizational context that can be given to AI (purpose, standards, language, materials, format, verification standards, and approval standards).
- Recognize and manage context capital as core organizational capital equal to money, data, models, and compute.
- Ensure that the accumulated context capital becomes the foundation of inclusive-transition AX and of the S axis of ESG (the socialization of context capital).

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when the system of purpose, standards, materials, and approval to be given to AI is documented as the organization's accumulated operating asset and AI can be directed on that basis.
