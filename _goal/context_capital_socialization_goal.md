# Socialization of Context Capital — Goal

The goal is to ensure that the ability to use AI is not monopolized by a few individuals or a particular department, and that the organization's context capital (purpose, standards, language, materials, format, verification standards, approval standards) is converted into a common production base for the whole organization.

## Objectives
- Diagnose whether context capital is concentrated in a few hands, and identify the points of monopolization.
- Convert context capital into a common production base (such as a common context) that the whole organization can access and use.
- Combined with a responsible operating system, realize the inclusive-transition management philosophy that prevents and mitigates the problem of AI stratification.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when context capital is not monopolized by particular individuals or departments but has been converted into a common production base accessible to the whole organization.
