# Context Designer (context-design-type AX talent) — Goal

The goal is to convert the organization's purpose and standards into execution conditions that AI (the first LLM) can understand so that AI works effectively within the organization, and further to lay the basis for forming the second LLM.

## Objectives
- Convert the organization's purpose and standards into conditions AI can execute, and thereby make AI work effectively within the organization.
- Accumulate context design capability and ultimately lay the basis for forming the second LLM (the organization's own judgment and operating system).
- Do not leave AI as a mere tool of use, but, as a 'context-design-type AX talent' who designs purpose, standards, verification, and feedback, lead the transformation of the organization's operating structure.
- Contribute to resolving the AI context gap, the core gap of AI-based stratification, so that context capital is not concentrated in a few hands.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when the first LLM works effectively within the organization according to its purpose and standards, and when the formation of the second LLM proceeds on that basis.
