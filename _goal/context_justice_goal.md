# Context Justice — Goal

The goal is to secure a common production base on which the whole membership can work together with AI, by treating fairness in the AI era not as narrowly confined to the problem of algorithmic bias but as a matter of procedural justice—who can access the organization's purpose, standards, materials, and verification system so as to give context to AI, verify the results, and contest the judgments.

## Objectives
- Extend the fairness problem of the AI era from the diagnosis of algorithmic bias to the problem of distributing the authority of context access, verification, and appeal.
- Manage the organization's purpose, standards, materials, and verification system so that they are not monopolized by a select few.
- Guarantee that any member can access the common production base for working together with AI.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when members actually possess the procedures and authority to give context to AI, to verify AI results, and to contest AI judgments.
