# Context Responsibility — Goal

Because AI produces generic answers without context, the goal is for AX-organization talent to provide sufficient work context so that AI works in line with the organization's purpose and standards.

## Objectives
- Clarify the work purpose, target users, organizational standards, and prohibited conditions, and convey them to AI.
- Provide AI with the usable data, the output format, the verification standards, and the performance standards.
- Put in place the contextual conditions under which AI can work in line with the organization's purpose and standards.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to have been achieved when the work purpose, target users, organizational standards, prohibited conditions, usable data, output format, verification standards, and performance standards have all been provided to AI.
