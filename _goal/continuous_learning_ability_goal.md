# Continuous Learning Ability — Goal

In an AX environment that changes at a very fast pace, the goal is to become a human being who does not settle for what one already knows and who is continuously updated by relearning faster than the pace at which technologies, tools, and ways of working evolve.

## Objectives
- Continuously detect changes in technologies, tools, and ways of working, and renew the objects of learning.
- Acknowledge that the shelf life of previously acquired expertise has shortened, and design a relearning cycle.
- Maintain a state of being continuously updated through relearning that is faster than the pace of change.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to have been achieved when one has reached a state of relearning faster than the pace of change and being continuously updated.
