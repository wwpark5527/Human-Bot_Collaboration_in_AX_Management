# Contribution — Goal

The goal is to identify the valid change that an act (work) produced. So that 'who produced what change' rather than 'who worked' can be judged, it discerns whether the state of meaning, judgment, verification, approval, record, execution, and responsibility actually changed.

## Objectives
- Confirm whether the act changed the counterpart's understanding.
- Confirm whether the act made judgment possible and whether it remained as a record.
- Confirm whether the act led to the next action and thereby judge whether it is a valid change.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when it is confirmed that, because of the act, at least one of the states of meaning, judgment, verification, approval, record, execution, and responsibility actually changed.
