# Contribution conflict — Goal

The goal is to identify in advance, and make adjustable, the contribution conflict in which, in AH-AH communication, the criteria, interpretation, verification state, and responsibility judgment diverge among participants for the same message.

## Objectives
- Explicitly recognize that the criteria, interpretation, verification state, and responsibility judgment for the same message may differ between participants (A and B).
- Stipulate that contribution conflict is the problem that becomes important in AH-AH communication, superseding the role vacancy.
- Where a contribution conflict is confirmed, prepare the grounds for adjusting it through one of the three communication paths (human-centered, AI-centered, or via governance).

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when any discrepancy in the criteria, interpretation, verification state, and responsibility judgment for the same message is explicitly identified.
