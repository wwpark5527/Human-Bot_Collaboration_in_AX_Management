# AH + AB type — Goal

The goal is to operate stably the horizontal, jointly optimizing relationship in which both the human (AH) and the bot (AB) are augmented, so that the core cooperation type of the AX organization works sustainably.

## Objectives
- Identify early the Orchestration overload, decision fatigue, concentration of responsibility, and weakened sense of reality that AH experiences.
- Identify early the Context inconsistency, Alignment stress, Autonomy ambiguity, and Continuous update overload that AB experiences.
- Apply the AI orchestration dashboard, Decision hierarchy, Role-constrained autonomy, and Explainability layer to build a common context.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to have been achieved when the level of augmentation on both the human and the bot side is maintained, the horizontal, jointly optimizing relationship is not shaken, and the stresses of AH and AB respectively are mitigated through the building of a common context.
