# AH + B type — Goal

The goal is to manage the stresses related to control, responsibility, and trust that arise when an augmented human (AH) coexists with a bot (B) that performs work independently, and to raise the probability of success at this stage by designing the role balance of human and bot (TRB).

## Objectives
- Identify early the stresses of loss of control, incapacitation of competence, concentration of responsibility, and fear of surveillance that AH experiences.
- Identify early the stresses of lack of context, governance conflict, inconsistency with human instructions, and lack of trust that B experiences.
- Clarify the final approval authority and build a governance system so as to support stably the bot's independent performance of work.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to have been achieved when AH's control and the locus of accountability become clear, B's basis of context, governance, and trust is secured, and this stage of cooperation is operated successfully in accordance with the design of the role balance of human and bot (TRB).
