# H + AH type — Goal

The goal is to manage the stress that arises from the gap in augmentation level when an ordinary human (H) and an augmented human (AH) cooperate, so that a complementary division of roles takes hold instead of comparison and ranking.

## Objectives
- Identify early the relative inferiority, alienation, and unfair evaluation that H experiences.
- Identify early the continuous high-performance pressure, AI dependency, isolation in human relationships, and identity confusion that AH experiences.
- Apply at the organizational level the emphasis on complementary roles instead of comparison, AI dependency monitoring, the establishment of a human orchestrator identity, and the ban on human-only evaluation.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to have been achieved when the gap between H and AH is reinterpreted not as a comparison of superiority but as a complementary division of roles, and when responses to their characteristic stresses (H's inferiority and alienation, AH's pressure, dependency, isolation, and identity confusion) have been applied.
