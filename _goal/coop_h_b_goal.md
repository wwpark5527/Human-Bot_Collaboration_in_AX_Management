# H + B type — Goal

The goal is to identify the different stresses that human (H) and bot (B) each experience in the vertical-relationship cooperation of human-led, bot-assisted work, and by applying the countermeasures suited to them, to make the bot function stably as a tool of augmentation rather than replacing the human.

## Objectives
- Identify early the stresses of loss of control, role erosion, imbalance of responsibility, and learning pressure that the human (H) experiences.
- Identify early the stresses of lack of Context, overload from exceptional situations, and inconsistency in human feedback that the bot (B) experiences.
- Stabilize the relationship by applying type-specific countermeasures such as the adoption of explainable AI, maintaining Human-in-the-loop, building a common context, and an exception escalation system.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to have been achieved when the human's sense of control and clarity of role are maintained, the bot's understanding of context and its capacity to handle exceptions are improved, and the H+B vertical relationship continues as a structure of augmentation rather than replacement.
