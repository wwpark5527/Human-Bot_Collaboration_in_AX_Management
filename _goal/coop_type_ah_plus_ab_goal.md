# AH + AB (a true AX organization) — Goal

The goal is to reach the AH + AB cooperation type in which both human and bot are augmented and jointly make judgments, and thereby to realize the 'human-bot coexistence and cooperation organization' that brings about human development = a true AX organization (hybrid organization).

## Objectives
- Identify whether the state in which both human and bot are augmented (AH + AB) has been reached.
- Convert the bot from a mere execution tool into a subject that jointly makes judgments with the human.
- Complete the human-bot coexistence and cooperation organization through the proper use of an organizational AX OS and the realization of HBRM.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to have been achieved when both human and bot are augmented, the bot functions not as an execution tool but as a joint-judgment subject, and the organizational AX OS and HBRM are actually in place so that the state can be called a 'true AX organization.'
