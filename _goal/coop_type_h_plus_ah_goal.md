# H + AH (augmented-human-centered cooperation) — Goal

The goal is to identify the H + AH cooperation type in which existing humans and augmented humans work together, and to mitigate the side effect of AI-competence stratification through AI democratization, building an AI collaboration culture, and role redesign.

## Objectives
- Identify the H + AH type in which the composition axis is human versus human but only some of them are augmented.
- Recognize and manage, at the organizational level, the phase in which role reorganization begins due to the stratification of AI competence.
- Mitigate the side effects of the augmentation gap through AI democratization, building an AI collaboration culture, and role redesign.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when the organization recognizes the AI-competence stratification caused by the augmentation of some humans within a human-versus-human configuration, mitigates the gap through AI democratization, building an AI collaboration culture, and role redesign, and role reorganization proceeds smoothly.
