# H + B (Tool-Type Cooperation) — Goal

The goal is to identify the H + B (tool-type cooperation) type, the early AX stage of the human-bot coexistence organization, and to establish a foundation for moving beyond the improvement of personal productivity to the next stage (H + AH).

## Objectives
- Identify the H + B type, in which the member composition is a (non-augmented) human and a bot and the bot remains in the position of a tool.
- Realize the productivity improvement of the early AX stage through the dissemination of personal productivity AI, prompt literacy education, and automation of repetitive work.
- Recognize the limits of this stage, in which responsibility is concentrated on the human, and prepare the conditions for the transition to the H + AH stage.

## Success Criteria
The goal is determined to be achieved when the organization, under a human-led + bot-assisted structure, executes the dissemination of personal productivity AI, prompt literacy education, and automation of repetitive work and achieves the improvement of personal productivity, and when it recognizes that limit (the concentration of responsibility on the human) and prepares the transition to the next stage.
