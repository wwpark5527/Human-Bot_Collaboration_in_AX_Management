# Coordinator — Goal

The goal is to connect people, AI, tools, output artifacts, schedules, and approval flows to one another. By joining the outputs produced by the shaper, implementer, validator, and operator into one unbroken workflow, it makes the whole organization actually work.

## Objectives
- Identify and connect the points of linkage between people, AI, and tools.
- Manage the flow so that output artifacts are passed on to the next stage without blockage.
- Coordinate schedules and approval flows so that they proceed without breaking off.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when people, AI, tools, output artifacts, and approval flows are connected to one another and joined into one workflow.
