# Shaper/Designer — Goal

The goal is to turn an ambiguous problem into a clear one. By defining what to build, why it should be built, and what structure is appropriate, it creates a clear starting point from which the subsequent implementation, verification, and operation roles can work.

## Objectives
- Define the problem to be solved and organize the requirements.
- Design an appropriate structure.
- Make clear the answers to what should be built and why it should be built.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when what to build, why it should be built, and what structure is appropriate have all been defined and the ambiguous problem has been converted into a clear one.
