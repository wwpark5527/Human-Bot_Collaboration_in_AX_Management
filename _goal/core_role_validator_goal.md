# Validator — Goal

The goal is to bring the output artifact to a trustworthy state. Through testing, review, and quality checking, errors, security risks, and logical flaws are found and it is judged whether the artifact is at a usable level.

## Objectives
- Test the output artifact and find errors.
- Check security risks and logical flaws through review.
- Confirm quality and judge whether it is at a usable level.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when errors, security risks, and logical flaws have all been checked, it has been judged whether the artifact is at a usable level, and the reliability of the output artifact has been secured.
