# Co-constitution of roles (Co-constitution) — Goal

The goal is to combine the different contributions of human and bot in the Human-Bot Coupled TRB structure and jointly constitute a single hybridized role.

## Objectives
- Combine the human's vision-setting with the bot's real-time strategy simulation.
- Ensure that the distinct contributions of human and bot are each hybridized within the role itself.
- Form a strategic shaper role that is impossible for a human alone.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when a single strategic shaper role combining the human's vision-setting with the bot's real-time strategy simulation is produced, and it is confirmed that the role itself has been hybridized.
