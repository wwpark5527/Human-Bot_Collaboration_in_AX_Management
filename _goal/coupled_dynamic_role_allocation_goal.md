# Dynamic role allocation (Dynamic Role Allocation) — Goal

The goal is to analyze the team's state in real time in the Human-Bot Coupled TRB structure and dynamically reallocate roles.

## Objectives
- Analyze workload in real time.
- Analyze cognitive load and stress levels in real time.
- Analyze expertise availability in real time and reallocate roles.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when real-time analysis of workload, cognitive load, stress, and expertise availability is carried out and role-reallocation results grounded in it are produced.
