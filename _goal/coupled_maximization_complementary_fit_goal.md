# Maximization of human-bot complementary fit (Maximization of Complementary Fit) — Goal

The goal is to combine human strengths and bot strengths in the Human-Bot Coupled TRB structure so as to maximize complementary fit and generate a new role capability.

## Objectives
- Reflect human strengths (empathy, value judgment, context understanding, ethics, creative meaning-making) in the role.
- Reflect bot strengths (speed, computation, memory, search, pattern analysis) in the role.
- Combine the two sets of strengths to generate a new role capability that does not exist for human or bot alone.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when human strengths and bot strengths are combined and a new role capability is generated as a result.
