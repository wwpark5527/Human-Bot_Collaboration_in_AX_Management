# Critical Thinking — Goal

The goal is to become a person who verifies AI well through verification, the search for counterexamples, logic checks, and reliability assessment, rather than relying uncritically on the highly plausible errors that AI produces.

## Objectives
- Always keep in mind the possibility of plausible errors that may be inherent in AI outputs.
- Acquire the habit of assessing the reliability of AI outputs through the search for counterexamples and logic checks.
- Establish oneself not as a person who blindly trusts AI but as a person who verifies AI.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to have been achieved when, as a person who verifies AI well rather than one who believes AI well, one has reached a state of actually being able to filter out the plausible errors in AI outputs.
