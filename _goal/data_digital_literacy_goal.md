# Data and Digital Literacy — Goal

In a work environment that has moved onto data-based, AI-based, and digital-based foundations, the goal is to secure, as a basic survival strategy, the data & digital fluency to interpret data, understand digital workflows, and verify AI outputs.

## Objectives
- Acquire the capability to accurately interpret the data encountered in work.
- Understand the structure and flow of workflows that have been converted onto digital foundations.
- Secure the ability to verify the results produced by AI.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to have been achieved when one has reached a state of being able to interpret and verify, on one's own, work that has moved onto data-based, AI-based, and digital-based foundations.
