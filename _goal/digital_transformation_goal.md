# DX (Digital Transformation) — Goal

DX aims to digitize, datafy, and cloud-migrate work and systems and to make processes efficient, thereby establishing the digital foundation on which the subsequent AX expansion can stand.

## Objectives
- Digitize, datafy, and cloud-migrate work and systems and make processes efficient, thereby establishing the digital foundation of organizational operation.
- Prepare the data, system, and process foundation that can lead to AX after digitization.
- Accumulate within the organization the answers to the DX-era questions (digitization, data storage, automation, system adoption, efficiency).

## Success Criteria
The DX objective is determined to have been achieved when the digitization, datafication, cloud migration, and process efficiency of work have actually been realized and, on that foundation, a state has been reached in which the expansion questions toward AX (AI judgment and generation, division of roles, verification and approval, authority and responsibility) can be raised.
