# AI agent — Goal

The goal is to identify, in the digital worker autonomy classification table (AI agent/Subagent/Bot), the AI agent that has the highest autonomy, and to place it appropriately in work that requires goal-oriented autonomous execution.

## Objectives
- Clarify the determination criteria of the six axes (autonomy, goal setting, role scope, decision-making, collaboration, memory) that distinguish the AI agent from the Subagent and the Bot.
- Place AI agents in work that requires establishing plans on their own and comprehensive and complex execution and decision-making.
- Support the autonomous role performance of the AI agent within a collaboration structure with humans and other agents.

## Success Criteria
The goal is determined to be achieved when the AI agent understands a goal, establishes a plan on its own, performs comprehensive and complex execution and decision-making, and the results lead to output at the level of an AI assistant or an autonomous research agent.
