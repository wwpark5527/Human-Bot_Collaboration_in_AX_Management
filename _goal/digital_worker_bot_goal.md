# Bot — Goal

The goal is to identify, in the digital worker autonomy classification table, the Bot that has the lowest autonomy, and to place it appropriately in work that requires simple and repetitive automation according to designated rules.

## Objectives
- Clarify the determination criteria of the six axes (autonomy, goal setting, role scope, decision-making, collaboration, memory) that distinguish the Bot from the AI agent and the Subagent.
- Place Bots in simple and repetitive work based on designated rules (for example a chatbot or a reservation bot).
- Clearly limit the role scope of the Bot so as to support the hierarchy and delegation structure with the higher Subagent and AI agent.

## Success Criteria
The goal is determined to be achieved when the Bot produces simple and repetitive automatic execution results according to designated rules and stably handles work at the level of a chatbot or a reservation bot.
