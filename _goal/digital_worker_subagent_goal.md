# Subagent — Goal

The goal is to identify, in the digital worker autonomy classification table, the Subagent that has intermediate autonomy, and to place it appropriately in work that requires limited and specialized execution of goals assigned by a higher agent.

## Objectives
- Clarify the determination criteria of the six axes (autonomy, goal setting, role scope, decision-making, collaboration, memory) that distinguish the Subagent from the AI agent and the Bot.
- Place Subagents in work (for example research) that requires limited and specialized execution of goals assigned by a higher agent.
- Support the specialized role performance of the Subagent within an internal agent collaboration structure.

## Success Criteria
The goal is determined to be achieved when the Subagent produces limited and specialized execution results for the goals assigned to it by a higher agent, and those results lead to output at the level of a research subagent.
