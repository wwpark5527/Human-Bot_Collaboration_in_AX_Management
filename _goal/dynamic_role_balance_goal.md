# Dynamic role balance — Goal

The goal is for the AX organization to break away from static Belbin role assignment and to establish a dynamic role balance system that switches, readjusts, and reallocates roles in real time according to the situation.

## Objectives
- Immediately activate the optimal role composition for each situation type, such as crisis, innovation, and operations.
- Build a system capable of carrying out role switching and reallocation in real time.
- Transform the static role system into a living role ecosystem.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when situationally reallocated dynamic role compositions are actually produced and it is confirmed that the organization readjusts roles in real time to changes in the situation.
