# Efficiency-centered AX — Goal

The goal is to clearly recognize the six risks brought about by an AI transformation designed solely around efficiency (reduction of human roles, widening of AI-competence gaps, abuse of algorithmic management, weakening of judgment authority, absence of the right to object, and performance concentration), and to use them as a negative example for designing inclusive-transformation AX.

## Objectives
- Identify whether the organization's AX is designed solely on the criteria of efficiency, productivity, and cost reduction.
- Check, in the organizational context, the six concrete risks brought about by efficiency-centered design.
- Compare efficiency-centered AX with inclusive-transformation AX and diagnose which performance criteria (ROI only vs. ROI + competence distribution + accountability + fairness) are being applied.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when the organization's AX approach is clearly diagnosed as either efficiency-centered or inclusive-transformation type, and, in the case where it is judged efficiency-centered, the six risks are documented.
