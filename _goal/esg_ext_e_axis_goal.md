# Extension of E (Sustainability of AI Infrastructure) — Goal

The goal is to retain the existing responsibilities for environment, energy, carbon, resources, and pollution while also managing, as the company's environmental responsibility (the E axis), the physical environmental burden induced by AI infrastructure (data centers, compute, cloud, digital operations).

## Objectives
- Explicitly include electricity use, water use, carbon emissions, semiconductors and minerals, e-waste, and local environmental impact as E-axis management items.
- Manage the environmental impact of AI infrastructure such as data centers, compute, and cloud with the same level of responsibility as existing environmental facilities.
- Identify and respond to the organizational and social implications brought about by the expansion of AI infrastructure, such as conflicts over local communities, water resources, and resource supply chains.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when the electricity, water, carbon, semiconductor, e-waste, and local environmental impacts caused by AI infrastructure (data centers, compute, cloud, digital operations) are measured and managed within the E-axis management system in the same way as existing environmental responsibilities.
