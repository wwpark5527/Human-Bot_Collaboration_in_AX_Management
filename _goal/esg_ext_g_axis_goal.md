# Extension of G (Accountability Structure of AI Power) — Goal

The goal is to build, within governance, a system of authority, verification, approval, recording, explanation, objection, accountability attribution, and improvement loops for responsibly operating the functioning and judgment of AI, while retaining the existing responsibilities for the board, control, audit, risk, and compliance.

## Objectives
- Make AI use approval, responsibility for outcomes, review and revision criteria, recording, objection, and the placement of policy and authority explicit as core governance problems of corporate operation.
- Clarify roles, authority, and responsibility, and manage the records and logs of contribution and judgment.
- Build explainability and an accountability attribution system, and operate risk management and objection procedures.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when authority, verification, approval, recording, explanation, objection, accountability attribution, and improvement loops for the functioning and judgment of AI are explicitly operated within the traditional governance system.
