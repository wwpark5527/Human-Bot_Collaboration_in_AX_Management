# Extension of S (AI Capability Equality and Human Dignity) — Goal

The goal is to guarantee, while retaining the existing responsibilities for labor, human rights, safety, diversity, and social contribution, the social capabilities enabling humans to understand, use, and control AI, to raise objections, and to participate in outcomes, rather than being beings replaced, surveilled, and excluded by AI.

## Objectives
- Address explicitly as an S-axis responsibility the influence that AI exerts on workers' roles, capabilities, evaluation, placement, education, employment stability, and the human right of judgment.
- Grant members the eight rights of AI access right, right to learn, right to use, right of judgment, right to explanation, right of appeal, right to transition, and right to benefit sharing.
- Ensure that the capability to use AI is provided equally, so that gaps in learning opportunity do not lead to gaps in the labor market.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when members can actually exercise the AI access right, right to learn, right to use, right of judgment, right to explanation, right of appeal, right to transition, and right to benefit sharing, and the social quality of an organization in which humans and AI work together is managed.
