# ESG for AI — Goal

The goal is to control, from the ESG standpoint, the negative effects and ethical problems that AI can induce, and thereby realize responsible AI (RAI) use.

## Objectives
- Identify the negative effects and ethical problems that can arise in the course of AI use.
- Prepare criteria and procedures for controlling AI use from the ESG (environmental, social, governance) standpoint.
- Build AI governance that meets the demand for responsible AI.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the negative effects and ethical problems caused by AI are identified and control criteria and procedures from the ESG standpoint are prepared, leading to responsible AI use.
