# ESG AI-era extension theory — Goal

The goal is to overcome the limitation whereby the existing ESG remains within the industrial-era responsibility system and fails to capture the problems newly emerging in the AI era (AI stratification, algorithmic management, human judgment authority, etc.), and to extend the ESG concept to the AI operating environment.

## Objectives
- Identify the points at which the existing ESG system fails to address the new problems of the AI era (stratification, algorithmic management, infringement of judgment authority, etc.).
- Make explicit to the organization the conceptual gap between the industrial-era responsibility system and the AI-era responsibility system.
- Present the grounds for shifting to an extended ESG (AI inclusive-transformation ESG) that broadens the ESG concept to the AI operating environment.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when the problems specific to the AI era that the existing ESG fails to capture are clearly identified and the necessity of shifting to an extended ESG that encompasses them is recognized at the organizational level.
