# Measurement-verification-strengthening theory — Goal

The goal is to diagnose whether the organization's ESG measurement, evaluation, and disclosure system is vulnerable to greenwashing (disguised environmentalism), and to secure the reliability of ESG data through unified criteria and strengthened verification.

## Objectives
- Diagnose the causes of ESG scores being calculated differently by each rating agency.
- Check whether the organization is disclosing only favorable data selectively and packaging itself as an eco-friendly company.
- Promote the introduction of unified evaluation and disclosure criteria and the strengthening of verification procedures.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when the causes of score discrepancies between rating agencies and any bias in data disclosure are clearly identified, and a direction for unified criteria and strengthened verification is presented.
