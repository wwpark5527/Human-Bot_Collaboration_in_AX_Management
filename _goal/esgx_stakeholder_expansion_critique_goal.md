# Stakeholder-expansion theory — Goal

The goal is to diagnose whether the organization's ESG approach is confined to company-centered single materiality (the investor-centric financial-risk perspective), and to judge the necessity of extending the ESG concept from financial materiality to impact materiality.

## Objectives
- Diagnose whether the organization's ESG evaluation and disclosure criteria are judged solely by whether something poses a risk to the company's financial condition.
- Identify the points at which non-financial issues such as climate change and human-rights issues are underestimated.
- Present to the organization a direction for stakeholder expansion in line with the regulatory trend toward impact materiality, such as the EU CSRD.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when the need for extension is explicitly identified — that the organization's ESG evaluation criteria must go beyond the financial-risk perspective to include impact on a variety of stakeholders other than investors.
