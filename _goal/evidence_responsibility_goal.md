# Evidence Responsibility — Goal

In line with the principle that in an AX organization what matters is not only the result, the goal is to leave evidence by which it can be retraced why this result arose, who reviewed it, and by what criteria it was approved.

## Objectives
- Leave on record the materials used and the instructions given to AI.
- Leave together the results AI produced and the revisions made by humans.
- Leave the verification criteria, approval records, error records, and improvements reflected in a traceable form.

## Success Criteria
The goal is determined to be achieved when the materials used, the instructions given to AI, the results AI produced, the revisions made by humans, the verification criteria, the approval records, the error records, and the improvements reflected all remain, so that the course of judgment can be retraced.
