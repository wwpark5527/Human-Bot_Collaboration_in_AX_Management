# Functional Role — Goal

The goal is to clearly identify members' formal, job-based roles (functional roles) in an AX organization and, by distinguishing them from team roles, which are informal behavior types, to secure the contrast axis for team role balance (TRB) diagnosis.

## Objectives
- Grasp, for each member, the job assignment formally granted by the organization or a superior (the functional role).
- Confirm the two conditions of whether the functional role was formally assigned and whether it is job-related.
- Clarify the baseline for distinguishing functional roles and team roles without confusion.
- Provide the list of functional roles as comparison material for team role diagnosis and TRB determination.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when every job unit in the team is confirmed as a functional role by the two conditions of formality and job-relatedness, and is clearly separated from team roles so that it can be used as the contrast reference point for TRB diagnosis.
