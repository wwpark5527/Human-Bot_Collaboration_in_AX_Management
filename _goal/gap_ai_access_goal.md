# AI Access Gap — Goal

The goal is to recognize the inequality created by whether one has access to high-performance AI and infrastructure, and to keep organizations and members from being excluded from access to the tools themselves.

## Objectives
- Recognize the inequality created by whether one has access to high-performance AI and infrastructure.
- Secure accessibility so that organizations and members are not excluded from access to the tools themselves.
- Manage the access gap so that it does not accumulate as the first cause of AI-based stratification.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the actual usability of high-performance AI and infrastructure is confirmed and the inequality of access is alleviated.
