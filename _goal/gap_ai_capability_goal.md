# AI Capability Gap — Goal

The goal is to recognize that differences in the capability to use AI lead to gaps in productivity, wages, and promotion, and to support members in acquiring the capability to convert AI into actual work performance.

## Objectives
- Recognize that differences in the capability to use AI lead to gaps in productivity, wages, and promotion.
- Support members in acquiring the capability to convert AI into actual work performance.
- Manage the capability gap so that it does not become entrenched as inequality within the organization.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when AI use is converted into actual work performance (productivity improvement) and the wage and promotion gaps caused by the capability gap are alleviated.
