# AI Context Gap — Goal

The goal is to recognize the gap in the capability to provide AI with the organization's purpose and criteria (the AI context gap) as the problem the author regards as most important from the organizational AX perspective, and to convert context capital into a common production base for the whole organization so that it is not concentrated in a few.

## Objectives
- Recognize the gap created by differences in the capability to provide AI with the organization's purpose, criteria, language, way of working, output formats, and verification criteria (the AI context gap).
- Understand that this is the gap the author regards as most important from the organizational AX perspective, and respond to it first.
- Convert context capital (purpose, criteria, language, materials, authority, verification systems) so that it is not concentrated in a few but becomes a common production base the whole organization can access and use.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the capability to provide AI with the organization's purpose and criteria is not concentrated in a few but spread across the organization, and the ownership, access, verification, and outcome attribution of context capital are carried out fairly.
