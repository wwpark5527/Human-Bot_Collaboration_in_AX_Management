# AI Right-to-Judge Gap — Goal

The goal is to clarify who holds the authority to approve, revise, or reject the results AI produces, so that humans do not fall into being mere objects of AI decisions.

## Objectives
- Clarify who holds the authority to approve, revise, or reject the results AI produces.
- Guarantee the right of judgment so that humans do not fall into being mere objects of AI decisions.
- Prevent the situation in which the AI right of judgment is concentrated in a few and the remaining majority must accept AI decisions as they are.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the authority to approve, revise, or reject AI results is clearly assigned and humans do not become the one-sided object of AI decisions.
