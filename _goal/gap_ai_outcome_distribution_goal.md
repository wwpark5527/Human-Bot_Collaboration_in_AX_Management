# AI Outcome-Distribution Gap — Goal

The goal is to grasp transparently to whom the productivity gains arising from AI adoption accrue, so that concentration at the top and exclusion at the bottom do not become entrenched.

## Objectives
- Clearly identify the scale of the productivity gains arising from AI adoption and where they accrue.
- Check the distribution structure so that the outcomes are not concentrated only in the organization's upper layer.
- Prepare sharing measures so that members of the lower layer are not excluded from outcome distribution.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when where the AI productivity gains accrue is clearly grasped, whether there is concentration at the top and exclusion at the bottom is checked, and the distribution structure is adjusted.
