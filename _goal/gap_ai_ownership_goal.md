# AI Ownership Gap — Goal

The goal is to grasp transparently the ownership structure of the revenue generated from models, data, and platforms, so that productivity profits are not concentrated only in a few actors.

## Objectives
- Clarify the owning actors of the revenue generated from the AI models, data, and platforms the organization uses.
- Grasp how the revenue from knowledge-capitalized output artifacts (structured knowledge) is distributed.
- Check the distribution structure so that productivity profits are not concentrated only in particular actors (big tech, the organization's upper layer).

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the owning actors of the revenue from models, data, and platforms are clearly grasped and whether productivity profits are concentrated is checked and managed.
