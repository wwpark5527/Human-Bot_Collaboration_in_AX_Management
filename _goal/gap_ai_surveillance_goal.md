# AI Surveillance Gap — Goal

The goal is to clarify the gap between actors who use AI as a tool to manage and monitor others and those who are managed by AI, so that algorithmic management and control are not carried out one-sidedly.

## Objectives
- Clarify the positions within the organization of who manages and monitors with AI and who is managed by AI.
- Transparently disclose the criteria and scope of algorithmic management (work assignment, evaluation, surveillance, compensation, discipline).
- Guarantee to the members who become the object of management and surveillance a channel for appeal and for demanding explanation, thereby alleviating the one-sidedness of control.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the positions of the managing and monitoring actors and of their objects, and the criteria and scope of algorithmic management, are clearly stipulated, and a channel for appeal is guaranteed on the object's side.
