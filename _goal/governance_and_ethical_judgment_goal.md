# Governance & Ethical Judgment — Goal

The goal is to become not someone who only understands technology but someone who designs trust by making AI controllable, and thereby to acquire the judgment that deals with trust, explainability, accountability, AI governance, security, and provenance, which become core as AX deepens.

## Objectives
- Acquire criteria of judgment for AI governance, explainability, accountability, security, and provenance.
- Beyond understanding technology, design AI into a controllable state.
- Design and manage trust in AI at the organizational level.

## Success Criteria
The goal is determined to be achieved when, going beyond the level of only understanding technology, one has designed trust by making AI controllable.
