# Accountability — Goal

The goal is to clearly set the person ultimately responsible for use and their criteria of judgment, and thereby make clear the locus of accountability for the use of AI results.

## Objectives
- Clearly define who bears the responsibility for the final use and judgment.
- Manage the accountability system by mapping it to ISO/IEC 42001 top-management responsibility, NIST Govern, and the provider and deployer obligations of the EU AI Act.
- Prevent the risk that arises when there is no governance context, namely "unclear accountability: it is difficult to judge how far AI results may be used."

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when who bears the responsibility for the final use and judgment is clearly determined and the locus of accountability is confirmed within the system mapped to the international standards.
