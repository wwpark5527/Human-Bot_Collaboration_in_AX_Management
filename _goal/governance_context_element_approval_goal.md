# Approval — Goal

The goal is to clearly set the necessary conditions for human review and approval, so that output artifacts requiring human approval are not used directly without review.

## Objectives
- Define the criteria for which output artifacts must go through human approval.
- Manage human intervention and the final judgment criteria by mapping them to ISO/IEC 42001 operational control procedures, NIST Manage, and EU AI Act human oversight (Article 14).
- Prevent the risk of "missing approval," in which output artifacts requiring human review are used directly.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when which output artifacts must be used only after human approval is clearly determined and human intervention and the final judgment criteria are being observed.
