# Improvement — Goal

The goal is to clearly set the way errors and failures are reflected into the next set of criteria, so that organizational learning and improvement of criteria are carried out continuously.

## Objectives
- Define where and in what way errors and feedback are reflected.
- Manage the improvement system by mapping it to ISO/IEC 42001 continual improvement, NIST Manage, and EU AI Act post-market monitoring.
- Prevent the risk of "improvement failure: errors and feedback are not reflected into the next set of criteria," which arises when there is no governance context.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when where errors and feedback are reflected is clearly judged and organizational learning and improvement of criteria are confirmed within the international standards mapping system.
