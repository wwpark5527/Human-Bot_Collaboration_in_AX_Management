# Record — Goal

The goal is to clearly set where and how the history of prompts, materials, results, revisions, and approvals is kept, thereby securing traceability and auditability.

## Objectives
- Define where and in what way the history of instructions, materials, results, revisions, and approvals is to be kept.
- Manage the record system by mapping it to ISO/IEC 42001 documentation requirements, NIST Govern/Measure, and EU AI Act automatic logging and record-keeping (Article 12).
- Prevent the risk of "record discontinuity," in which the history is scattered and tracing becomes impossible.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when where the history of instructions, materials, results, revisions, and approvals is kept is clearly judged and traceability and auditability are secured.
