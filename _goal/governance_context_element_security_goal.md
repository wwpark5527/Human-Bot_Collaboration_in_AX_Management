# Security — Goal

The goal is to make clear the criteria for handling information that must not be entered and sensitive information, so that sensitive information and confidential material are protected from exposure in the course of AI use.

## Objectives
- Define, per context, the scope of information that must not be entered, shared, or generated.
- Manage the protection of sensitive information and confidentiality by mapping it to ISO/IEC 42001 data/asset management, NIST Map/Manage, and EU AI Act data governance (Article 10).
- Prevent the "security risk" in which the boundary between sensitive information and externally disclosable information collapses.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when which information must not be entered, shared, or generated is clearly judged and sensitive information and confidential material are being protected.
