# Validation — Goal

The goal is to put in place the checklist and quality criteria for confirming AI results, so that only results whose factuality, logic, quality, and policy conformance have been confirmed are used.

## Objectives
- Define, per result type, the checklist and quality criteria to be confirmed.
- Manage the validation results by mapping them to ISO/IEC 42001 performance evaluation and monitoring, NIST Measure, and EU AI Act accuracy and robustness (Article 15).
- Prevent the risk of result misuse that arises when there are no validation criteria.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when which results must be confirmed by which criteria is clearly judged and factuality, logic, quality, and policy conformance have been confirmed.
