# Group Cohesiveness — Goal

The goal is to measure the human-bond side of teamwork as the degree of psychological attraction among members, thereby providing the established, representative axis of teamwork measurement that contrasts with the task-activation side measured by team role balance (TRB).

## Objectives
- Measure the degree of psychological attraction among members to like, cohere, and help one another.
- Maintain its standing as the teamwork measurement method academia and industry have shared since before the team-role concept appeared.
- Produce measurement results for the human-bond side that contrast with TRB (measurement of the task-activation side).
- Function as the operational measurement method for one axis in the framing of the two sides of teamwork (human bonding and task activation).

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the degree of psychological attraction among members is produced through group cohesiveness measurement and that result is structurally contrasted with the task-activation side measured by TRB and actually used to support the framing of the two sides of teamwork.
