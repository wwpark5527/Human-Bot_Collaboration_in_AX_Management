# AH1 Augmented Human — Goal

The goal is to identify whether a member has entered the AH1 stage on the ladder of human-augmentation stages, where thinking ability and learning volume have actually increased through AI use, and thereby to design the transition path to AH2 (AI orchestrator) and beyond.

## Objectives
- Confirm the change in a member's thinking ability before and after AI use.
- Confirm the change in a member's learning volume before and after AI use.
- Take the AH1 identification result as the reference point for the AH2 (AI orchestrator) transition plan.

## Success Criteria
The AH1 stage is determined when thinking and learning have increased through AI use, that is, when increased thinking ability and learning volume are confirmed.
