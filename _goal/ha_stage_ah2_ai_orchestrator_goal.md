# AH2 AI Orchestrator — Goal

The goal is to identify whether a member has reached the AH2 stage on the ladder of human-augmentation stages, where they use and direct multiple AI agents and role augmentation has actually occurred, and thereby to design the transition path to the AH3 stage (human-AI symbiotic leader), the ultimate goal of human augmentation.

## Objectives
- Confirm whether the member uses multiple AI agents simultaneously rather than a single AI.
- Confirm whether the member is at the level of directing multiple AI agents beyond simply using them.
- Take whether role expansion (role augmentation) has actually occurred as the grounds for the AH2 determination.
- Take the AH2 identification result as the reference point for the AH3 (human-AI symbiotic leader) transition plan.

## Success Criteria
The AH2 stage is determined when one uses and directs multiple AI agents, that is, when role expansion through the use/direction of multiple AI agents is confirmed.
