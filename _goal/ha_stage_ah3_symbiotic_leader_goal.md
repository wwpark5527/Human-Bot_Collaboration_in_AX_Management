# AH3 Human-AI Symbiotic Leader — Goal

The goal is to identify whether a member has reached the AH3 stage, the ultimate goal of the ladder of human-augmentation stages, in which human and AI operate as a single system, and thereby to reflect the human-AI symbiosis system in the organization's leadership structure.

## Objectives
- Confirm whether the collaboration between the member and AI operates as a single integrated system beyond the level of directing and being directed (AH2).
- Confirm whether the integrated human-AI system actually performs leadership functions (decision-making and organizational operation).
- Take the AH3 identification result as the grounds for the attainment of the ultimate goal of the organizational AX human-augmentation roadmap.

## Success Criteria
The AH3 stage is determined when human and AI operate as a single system, that is, when operation and leadership as an integrated human-AI system are confirmed.
