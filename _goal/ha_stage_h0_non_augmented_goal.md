# H0 Non-Augmented Human — Goal

The goal is to identify whether a member remains at H0 (AI barely used), the starting line of the ladder of human-augmentation stages, and thereby to secure the reference point for designing the subsequent augmentation path to H1 through AH3.

## Objectives
- Identify whether H0 applies by confirming the member's frequency of AI use and degree of AI involvement.
- Confirm whether AI involvement is virtually absent in the work of a member who remains at H0.
- Take the H0 identification result as the starting point of the transition plan to the next stage (H1 AI user).

## Success Criteria
The H0 stage is determined when AI is barely used and work performance is confirmed to have virtually no AI involvement.
