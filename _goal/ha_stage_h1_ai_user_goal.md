# H1 AI User — Goal

The goal is to identify whether a member is at the H1 stage on the ladder of human-augmentation stages, using AI at the level of asking questions and searching, and thereby to design the transition path to H2 (AI collaborator) and beyond.

## Objectives
- Confirm whether the member's manner of AI use remains at the level of questions and searches.
- Measure the degree of simply obtaining results through questions and searches.
- Take the H1 identification result as the reference point for the H2 (AI collaborator) transition plan.

## Success Criteria
The H1 stage is determined when AI use remains at the level of questions and searches and is confirmed to be simply obtaining results through questions and searches.
