# H2 AI Collaborator — Goal

The goal is to identify whether a member is at the H2 stage on the ladder of human-augmentation stages, performing joint work with AI, and thereby to design the transition path to AH1 (augmented human) and beyond.

## Objectives
- Confirm whether the member actually performs joint work with AI.
- Confirm the existence and form of the products of joint work with AI.
- Take the H2 identification result as the reference point for the AH1 (augmented human) transition plan.

## Success Criteria
The H2 stage is determined when one performs joint work with AI and its products are confirmed.
