# Essence — Goal

The goal is to clearly distinguish the fact that human stress and bot stress originate in fundamentally different domains (psychological/emotional versus functional/computational), so that the two kinds of stress are not handled in the same way.

## Objectives
- Distinguish whether the observed tension arises in the psychological/emotional domain or in the functional/computational domain.
- Manage the psychological/emotional tension of humans and the functional/computational tension of bots through different response systems.
- Make the judgment criteria within the organization clear so that responses to human stress and responses to bot stress are not confused.

## Success Criteria
The goal is determined to have been achieved when the domain in which the tension arises (psychological/emotional or functional/computational) is accurately distinguished and the response system matching it is applied.
