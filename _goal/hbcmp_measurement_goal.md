# Measurement — Goal

The goal is to observe human and bot stress with different indicator systems (emotional and behavioral indicators vs response speed, accuracy, and stability), and thereby to make the preceding judgments of essence, root cause, result, recovery mode, and risk rest on actual data.

## Objectives
- Establish a system capable of observing human stress with emotional and behavioral indicators.
- Establish a system capable of observing bot stress with the indicators of Latency, Accuracy, and Stability.
- Judge the state only with the indicators suited to each agent, without mixing the two indicator systems.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when human cases have their stress state quantified with emotional and behavioral indicators and bot cases with response speed, accuracy, and stability indicators, and those quantified results are consistent with the preceding judgments of essence, cause, result, recovery, and risk.
