# Recovery Mode — Goal

The goal is to make clear that the prescriptions for reversing human and bot stress differ from each other (rest, empathy, and motivation vs readjustment and relearning), and thereby to prevent mistaken prescriptions (for example, giving a bot rest or demanding only relearning from a human).

## Objectives
- Apply prescriptions centered on rest, empathy, and motivation to a state of human stress.
- Apply prescriptions centered on readjustment (realignment) and relearning to a state of bot stress.
- Ensure that ineffective responses are not repeated through confusion of the prescription families.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when a recovery prescription suited to the agent in a state of stress (human: rest/empathy/motivation, bot: readjustment/relearning) is applied and the state actually improves as a result.
