# Result — Goal

The goal is to identify early that the consequences appearing when human stress and bot stress are neglected differ from each other (a person's exhaustion and dissatisfaction vs a system's performance degradation and errors), and thereby to detect deterioration with indicators suited to each domain.

## Objectives
- Secure observation indicators that can catch the human-side consequences of neglect (Burnout, dissatisfaction) early.
- Secure observation indicators that can catch the bot-side consequences of neglect (performance degradation, errors) early.
- Connect the two consequence types to their respective follow-up responses (human care vs system inspection) without confusing them.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when, in a case of neglected stress, its consequence is accurately identified as belonging either to the human family (Burnout/dissatisfaction) or to the bot family (performance degradation/errors), and has been connected to the matching follow-up response.
