# Risk — Goal

The goal is to recognize in advance the worst states that can be reached when human and bot stress is neglected (human departure vs functional collapse), and thereby to intervene before a failure of recovery spreads into a risk that is difficult to reverse.

## Objectives
- Identify the human-side risk signals (depression, turnover, resistance) early.
- Identify the bot-side risk signals (hallucination, system failure) early.
- Establish an alert system so that intervention can occur at the recovery mode stage, before the risk stage is reached.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when the human or bot risk signals (human: depression/turnover/resistance, bot: hallucination/system failure) are identified at a stage prior to their actual occurrence and a preemptive intervention has been made accordingly.
