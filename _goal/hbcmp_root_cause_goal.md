# Root Cause — Goal

The goal is to pinpoint precisely that human stress and bot stress arise from different families of causes (psychological and relational vs functional and alignment-related), thereby preventing responses that do not match the cause (for example, treating a bot's misalignment with a human counseling approach).

## Objectives
- Identify whether the cause of the observed stress belongs to the anxiety, relationships, and identity family or to the overload, conflict, and misalignment family.
- Assign response resources matching the family of cause (human counseling and organizational intervention vs system readjustment and governance overhaul).
- Reduce response failures caused by misjudging the cause (a mistaken prescription).

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when the cause of a stress case is accurately classified into one of the two families and response resources matching that family have been assigned.
