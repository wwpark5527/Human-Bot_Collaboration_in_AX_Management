# Augmentation Management — Goal

The goal is to manage and support the process by which, within the collaboration flow established through collaboration-structure design, humans transition into augmented humans (AH) and bots into augmented bots (AB).

## Objectives
- Support humans (H) in growing into augmented humans (AH) through collaboration with AI.
- Support bots (B) in developing into augmented bots (AB) through the common context and the governance context.
- Develop humans and bots into a relationship that raises each other's capabilities rather than one that replaces each other.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when it is confirmed that the H→AH and B→AB transitions are actually progressing.
