# Collaboration-Structure Design — Goal

The goal is to connect the role and responsibility boundaries between humans and bots set in role design to the actual work flow, and thereby to design a structure in which humans and bots can naturally interlock and collaborate.

## Objectives
- Reflect the deliverable of the role-design stage (role and responsibility boundaries) in the actual work process.
- Design the flow of work handover, verification, and feedback between humans and bots.
- Organize the flow so that no bottleneck or conflict arises during collaboration.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when the human-bot collaboration flow has been designed including concrete steps and handover points.
