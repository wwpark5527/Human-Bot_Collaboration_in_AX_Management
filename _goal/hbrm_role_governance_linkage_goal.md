# Governance Linkage — Goal

The goal is to connect the authority, verification, approval, recording, and responsibility structures into one, so that the trust-management standards work in actual organizational operation.

## Objectives
- Clarify who holds authority in human-bot collaboration.
- Structure the procedures for verifying and approving results.
- Record the verification and approval process and its results, and clearly connect the locus of ultimate responsibility.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when the five elements of authority, verification, approval, recording, and responsibility structure are connected to one another without a break.
