# Member Definition — Goal

The goal is to define, at the HBRM level, all member types of the AX organization (H, AH, B, AB) so that they have a clear position and role within the organization.

## Objectives
- Officially recognize the four types H (human), AH (augmented human), B (bot), and AB (augmented bot) as organizational members.
- Stipulate the position each type occupies within the organization (role-centered rather than job-centered).
- Clarify the role and the scope of responsibility each type is to carry out.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when the position and role within the organization are documented and defined for all four member types H, AH, B, and AB.
