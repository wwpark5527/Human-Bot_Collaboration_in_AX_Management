# Role-Balance Management — Goal

The goal is to diagnose the role balance of the three dimensions human↔human, human↔bot, and bot↔bot, and thereby adjust the concentration, deficiency, and conflict of roles.

## Objectives
- Diagnose imbalance types such as excess of technical roles, excess of automation roles, lack of verification roles, lack of coordination roles, and unclear responsibility roles.
- Examine together the role balance of the three dimensions human↔human, human↔bot, and bot↔bot.
- Adjust the diagnosed concentration, deficiency, and conflict and return them to a balanced state.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when the excess and deficiency of roles has been diagnosed and, according to those results, adjustment measures have been taken so that balance is maintained.
