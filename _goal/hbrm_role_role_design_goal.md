# Role Design — Goal

The goal is to clearly set the role and responsibility boundaries among the four member types H, AH, B, and AB in an AX organization, so that role-centered rather than job-centered collaborative management becomes possible.

## Objectives
- Assign concrete roles to the four types (H, AH, B, AB) finalized in member definition.
- Set the responsibility boundaries between humans and bots so that the verification of results and the locus of accountability are clear.
- Convert job-centered management into role-centered collaborative management.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when the role and responsibility boundaries between humans and bots have been documented and set.
