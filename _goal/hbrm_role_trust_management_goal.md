# Trust Management — Goal

The goal is to establish collaboration standards that prevent the two extremes of overtrust and distrust in the collaboration between humans and bots.

## Objectives
- Set standards that prevent the problem of overtrust, in which a bot's results are accepted uncritically.
- Set standards that prevent the problem of excessive distrust, which impedes collaboration with bots itself.
- Establish common standards by which humans and bots can collaborate at an appropriate level of trust.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when collaboration standards preventing overtrust and distrust have been established in a document.
