# Baek (魄) — Goal

The goal is to accurately recognize that the functions corresponding to the human baek (body, senses, labor, physical execution) can be performed by proxy and extended by robots and automation devices, and to manage this not as a loss of human bodily capability but as proxy performance and extension.

## Objectives
- Clearly define the functions of baek — body, senses, labor, physical execution — and recognize that these are distinct from cognition (hon) and from dignity and agency (yeong).
- Acknowledge that robots, automation devices, and mechanical execution correspond to the place of baek, while making clear that this is not possession of the human body itself but external proxy performance of the function.
- Prevent the state of "baeksan," in which the functions of baek are excessively absorbed into automation systems so that the human body and labor lose their own purpose and are scattered within the system.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when organizations and individuals make use of the fact that robots and automation can perform by proxy and extend the functions of baek (body, senses, labor, physical execution), while the human body and labor are not reduced to a resource managed by the system but are maintained as the foundation of agentive action.
