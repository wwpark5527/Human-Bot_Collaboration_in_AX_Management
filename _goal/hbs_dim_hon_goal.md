# Hon (魂) — Goal

The goal is to recognize accurately that the cognitive functions corresponding to the human Hon (thought, memory, judgment, creation, decision-making) can be performed on the human's behalf by AI, and to manage this not as a loss of the human Hon but as delegation and extension.

## Objectives
- Clearly define the functions of Hon—thought, memory, judgment, creation, decision-making—and recognize that these are distinguished from physical execution (Baek) and from dignity and subjecthood (Yeong).
- Acknowledge that AI's analysis, reasoning, language generation, and judgment assistance correspond to the place of Hon, while making clear that this is not possession of the human Hon itself but the external performance of the function on the human's behalf.
- Prevent the state of 'honbi', in which the functions of Hon pass excessively to AI and the human loses the ability to think and judge for themselves.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when the organization and the individual make use of the fact that AI can perform and assist the functions of Hon (thought, memory, judgment, creation, decision-making) on their behalf, yet ultimately do not lose the human sovereignty of judgment by which they question, verify, and take responsibility themselves.
