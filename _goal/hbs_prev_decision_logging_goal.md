# AI's judgment process must be recorded — Goal

The goal is to leave a complete record of the grounds, data, approver, and reviewed risks of judgments in which AI was involved, thereby preventing situations in which responsibility disappears through the absence of a record.

## Objectives
- Put in place a system that records the grounds of judgment, the data used, the approver, and the risks reviewed for every important judgment in which AI was involved.
- Preserve the records in an accessible and verifiable form so that they can actually be used for after-the-fact audit and traceability of responsibility.
- Internalize into the organization's culture and procedures the principle that the absence of a record leads directly to the extinction of responsibility.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when the grounds of judgment, data, approver, and reviewed risks can be confirmed from the record at any time for important judgments in which AI was involved, and there are no cases in which the locus of accountability becomes unclear due to a missing record.
