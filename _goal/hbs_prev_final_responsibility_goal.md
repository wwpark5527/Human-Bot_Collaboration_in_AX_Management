# Ultimate responsibility must remain with humans and the organization — Goal

The goal is to build an operating structure in which, no matter how sophisticated the advice and analysis AI provides, the final right of judgment and responsibility for important decisions are fixed to humans and the organization.

## Objectives
- Explicitly reflect in the organization's decision-making system the principle that AI can advise but cannot take responsibility.
- Define the scope of important decisions and require that, within that scope, final approval by humans and the organization is always obtained.
- Put in place governance that prevents the subject of responsibility from being implicitly transferred to AI or the system.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when, even while using AI's advice and analysis in important decisions, the holder of the final right of judgment and the subject of responsibility are always clearly designated as a specific human or organization.
