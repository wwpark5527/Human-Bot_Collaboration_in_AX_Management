# Technology adoption must be designed together with governance — Goal

The goal is, when adopting AI and automation technology, not to take the securing of function alone as the objective, but to design governance—including authority, responsibility, approval, recording, auditing, and risk review—simultaneously with adoption.

## Objectives
- In AI adoption decisions, stipulate together not only "what can be done" but also "how far it may go".
- Ensure that the six elements of authority, responsibility, approval, recording, auditing, and risk review are not omitted at the adoption design stage.
- Prevent function-centered unlimited adoption and shift to an adoption in which the permitted scope is clearly stipulated.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when, for every new AI or automation adoption case, the items of authority, responsibility, approval, recording, auditing, and risk review are documented in the adoption plan, and there is no case of governance being designed retroactively after functions have been deployed.
