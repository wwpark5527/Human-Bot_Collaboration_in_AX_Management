# Humans must be reviewers of AI output, not consumers — Goal

The goal is not to consume AI-produced results uncritically, but to establish throughout the organization the human role of judge and verifier who interprets, verifies, and adjusts.

## Objectives
- Define the basic stance of the human receiving AI output as 'review' rather than 'consumption', and reflect it in the organization's norms.
- Make it a standard work procedure to go through interpretation, verification, and, when necessary, adjustment for every AI output.
- Redefine the human role from a mere user to a judge and verifier, and thereby raise together the capability and the sense of responsibility of members.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when the organization's members do not accept AI output as it is, when the behavior of interpreting, verifying, and adjusting is routinely observed, and when this process is explicitly built into work procedures.
