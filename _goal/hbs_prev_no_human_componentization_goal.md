# Automation must not reduce humans to components — Goal

The goal is to establish automation design principles so that, even when the adoption of robots and automation systems raises efficiency, human rest, safety, dignity, and right of judgment are not infringed in that process.

## Objectives
- Ensure that the purpose of automation, namely the improvement of efficiency, is designed together with the constraint of protecting human rest, safety, dignity, and right of judgment.
- Prevent automation systems from subordinating the human body and labor to the rhythms and rules the system determines and thereby driving them into a state of 'baeksan'.
- Identify in advance the points where efficiency and human protection conflict, and do not lower the human protection standard for reasons of efficiency.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when automation and robot systems raise efficiency while human rest, safety, dignity, and right of judgment are protected by measurable standards and no infringement case occurs.
