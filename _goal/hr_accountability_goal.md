# Accountability — Goal

The goal is to make a bot's actions and results explainable and verifiable, and thereby to realize in organizational AX operation the fourth and final element of the machine-readable minimum definition of human respect.

## Objectives
- Make it possible to provide an ex-post explanation for every action and result of a bot.
- Leave records so that a third party can independently verify a bot's actions and results.
- Design in advance so that actions that are unexplainable or unverifiable do not occur.
- Manage the accountability criterion explicitly as one of the four elements of the technical definition of human respect (non-harm, respect for autonomy, fairness, accountability).

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when it is actually confirmed that a bot's actions and results are explainable and verifiable.
