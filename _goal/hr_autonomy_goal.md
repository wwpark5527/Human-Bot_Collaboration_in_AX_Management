# Autonomy — Goal

The goal is to have the bot refrain from infringing upon human choice and from manipulation and deception, thereby realizing the second of the machine-understandable minimum definitions of respect for humans in organizational AX operations.

## Objectives
- Design and verify in advance that all of the bot's actions do not infringe upon human choice.
- Equip the bot with prohibition rules so that it does not act to manipulate or deceive humans.
- Exclude unjustified intervention in the human decision-making process.
- Manage the respect-for-autonomy criterion explicitly as one of the four elements of the technical definition of respect for humans (non-harm, respect for autonomy, fairness, accountability).

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when it is actually confirmed that the bot's actions do not infringe upon human choice and do not involve manipulation or deception.
