# Fairness — Goal

The goal is to keep a bot from discriminating, and thereby to realize in organizational AX operation the third element of the machine-readable minimum definition of human respect.

## Objectives
- Design and verify in advance so that no action or result of a bot gives rise to discrimination.
- Exclude from the output process any bias that is unfavorable or favorable to a particular human group.
- Have in place a review procedure that identifies the possibility of discrimination in advance.
- Manage the fairness criterion explicitly as one of the four elements of the technical definition of human respect (non-harm, respect for autonomy, fairness, accountability).

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when it is actually confirmed that a bot's actions or results do not give rise to discrimination.
