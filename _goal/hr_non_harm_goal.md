# Non-harm — Goal

The goal is to have the bot do no physical or mental harm to humans, thereby realizing the first of the machine-understandable minimum definitions of respect for humans in organizational AX operations.

## Objectives
- Design and verify in advance that all of the bot's actions do no physical harm to humans.
- Design and verify in advance that all of the bot's actions do no mental harm to humans.
- Exclude from the candidate action set any action with the potential to cause harm.
- Manage the non-harm criterion explicitly as one of the four elements of the technical definition of respect for humans (non-harm, respect for autonomy, fairness, accountability).

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when it is actually confirmed that the bot's actions do no physical or mental harm to humans.
