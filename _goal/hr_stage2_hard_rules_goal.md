# Stage 2 (Hard Rules) — Goal

The goal is to define an absolutely forbidden zone that operates independently of reward, thereby fundamentally blocking the human-violating actions that Stage 1 (reward design) alone cannot prevent.

## Objectives
- Define harmful actions as an absolutely forbidden zone, independent of reward calculation.
- Define deception and manipulation as an absolutely forbidden zone.
- Define illegal acts as an absolutely forbidden zone.
- Ensure that these hard rules always take precedence, regardless of the optimization result of the reward function.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when it is confirmed that harmful actions, deception/manipulation, and illegal acts are always blocked regardless of the result of reward calculation.
