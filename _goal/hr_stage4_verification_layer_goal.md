# Stage 4 (Verification Layer) — Goal

The goal is to confirm the effectiveness of the preceding three stages (reward design, hard rules, human feedback learning) by verifying bot behavior before and after the fact so that whether it respects humans can be proven.

## Objectives
- Verify in advance whether the bot's behavior harms humans.
- Verify after the fact whether the bot's decision-making process followed the established rules.
- Record whether respect was observed in a form that a third party can prove.
- Confirm through the verification results the effectiveness of Stages 1 to 3 (reward design, hard rules, human feedback learning).

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when it is confirmed that, for the bot's behavior, whether the result harms humans and whether the decision-making process complies with the rules are verified before and after the fact and that proof thereof is recorded.
