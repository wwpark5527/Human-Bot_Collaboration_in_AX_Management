# Cognitive Overload — Goal

The goal is to understand the cognitive overload that arises as the human, as a meta-manager, simultaneously supervises, orchestrates, and verifies multiple AI agents, data, and workflows, and to prevent the decline in performance caused by decision fatigue and attention fragmentation.

## Objectives
- Grasp the scope of the AI agents, real-time data, automated workflows, verification of AI recommendations, and governance compliance objects that the human must manage simultaneously.
- Identify early the points at which decision fatigue, attention fragmentation, and continuous monitoring burden appear.
- Redesign the cognitive burden of the meta-manager role to a manageable level.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when cognitive overload is accurately identified, the objects of simultaneous management and the monitoring burden are redesigned to a manageable level, and it has not deteriorated into decision fatigue and attention fragmentation.
