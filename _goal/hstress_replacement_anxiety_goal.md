# Replacement Anxiety — Goal

The goal is to understand replacement anxiety, which comes from the perception of the threat that one's own expertise will become inferior to AI, and to confirm and strengthen distinctively human capabilities before it develops into existential anxiety and an identity crisis.

## Objectives
- Identify early the threat-perception situations in which members worry about "whether my expertise is becoming inferior to AI".
- Grasp the signals of replacement anxiety that appear especially strongly among knowledge workers.
- Reconfirm distinctively human capabilities and roles before traditional job insecurity develops into existential anxiety and an identity crisis.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when replacement anxiety is accurately identified, the value of members' expertise and roles is reconfirmed, and it has not deteriorated into existential anxiety or an identity crisis.
