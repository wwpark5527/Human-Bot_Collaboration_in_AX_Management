# Role Ambiguity — Goal

The goal is to understand role ambiguity, in which "who is responsible for what?" becomes unclear as humans and bots work together, and to clarify the division of roles before it deteriorates into role conflict and the problem of attributing responsibility.

## Objectives
- Identify early the work situations in which the division of roles between humans and bots is unclear.
- Grasp the points where human judgment and AI recommendation conflict, and the damage to human self-esteem that occurs when the AI is more accurate.
- Prevent deterioration into role conflict and the problem of attributing responsibility by clarifying the locus of strategy, decision-making, and the ultimate responsible party.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when role ambiguity is accurately identified, the locus of roles and responsibility between humans and bots is clearly redefined, and it has not deteriorated into role conflict and the problem of attributing responsibility.
