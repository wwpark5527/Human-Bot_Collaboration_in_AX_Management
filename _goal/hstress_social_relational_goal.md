# Social and Relational Stress — Goal

The goal is to understand the social and relational stress that arises in situations where one must cooperate not only with human colleagues but also with bots, and to alleviate psychological distance and social isolation before they lead to a weakening of the need to belong.

## Objectives
- Identify early the situations in which psychological distance from bots, deterioration of human-to-human relationships, social isolation, and increased emotional labor appear.
- Grasp the points at which a lack of emotional empathy arises in customer service, AI supervision, acceptance of AI-generated feedback, and collaboration with bots.
- Establish relational support structures so that the human need to belong is not weakened.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when social and relational stress is accurately identified and, in the collaborative relationship with bots, psychological distance and social isolation are alleviated so that the human need to belong is not weakened.
