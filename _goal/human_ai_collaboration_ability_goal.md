# Human-AI Collaboration Ability — Goal

The goal is to move away from the way humans work alone and to become a collaborative human who divides roles with and collaborates with bots by judging for oneself what to entrust to AI, what humans should handle, and how to connect them.

## Objectives
- Distinguish, within work, the parts that can be delegated to AI from the parts humans must perform directly.
- Design a way to effectively connect the work of humans and AI.
- Convert from a human who works alone into a collaborative human who divides roles with bots.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when one can judge for oneself what to entrust to AI, what humans should do, and how to connect them, and has reached a state of actually dividing roles and collaborating with bots in real work.
