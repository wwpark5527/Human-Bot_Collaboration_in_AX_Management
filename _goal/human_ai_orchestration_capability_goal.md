# Human-AI Orchestration Capability — Goal

The goal is to become someone who designs the very system composed of humans and AI rather than someone who is good on their own, and to perform the coordination of multiple AI agents, the design of human and bot roles, the design of team role balance (TRB), and the design of the collaboration structure.

## Objectives
- Acquire the ability to coordinate multiple AI agents.
- Design the roles between humans and bots and align team role balance (TRB).
- Design the human-AI collaboration structure so as to raise the performance of the system as a whole.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when, beyond the level of being good at work on one's own, a human + AI system is designed and the collaboration structure and role placement are completed.
