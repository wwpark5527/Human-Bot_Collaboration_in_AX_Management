# Human-AI Orchestration (orchestration) — Goal

The goal is to create new value, organizations, markets, and order by using AI through designing, controlling, and amplifying the human-AI collaboration structure. The goal is that the leader does not remain an executor but moves to being an augmented orchestrator who integrates the capabilities of humans and AI.

## Objectives
- Orchestrate multiple AI agents.
- Design the roles of humans and bots.
- Design the collaboration structure and thereby build the human + AI system itself.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when the human-AI collaboration structure is designed, controlled, and amplified by oneself and new value, organizations, markets, and order are created — that is, when the leader functions not as an executor but as an augmented orchestrator.
