# Human Stress Related to AI — Goal

The goal is to recognize the stress humans undergo during AI adoption, such as fear of replacement, fear of surveillance, identity threat, a sense of lost competence, loss of meaning, and concern over damage to humanity, and to make it possible to diagnose the cause of AI adoption failure first from the perspective of human reaction failure rather than technology failure.

## Objectives
- Recognize the fear of replacement, fear of surveillance, identity threat, sense of lost competence, loss of meaning, and concern over damage to humanity that organizational members carry in relation to AI.
- Internalize in the organization the perspective that distinguishes the causes of AI adoption failure cases into technology failure and human reaction failure.
- Reflect in the AI adoption design measures that mitigate human stress factors in advance.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when it is confirmed that human stress factors are identified during AI adoption and that a procedure is actually operating which, upon adoption failure, diagnoses its cause first from the perspective of human reaction failure rather than technology failure.
