# Teamwork in the Human-Bond Aspect — Goal

The goal is to measure by group cohesiveness the psychological attraction among members to like one another, band together, and help one another, and to confirm that departmental teamwork and performance are maximized when it is combined with teamwork in the task-activation aspect (TRB).

## Objectives
- Measure by group cohesiveness the degree of psychological attraction among members (liking, banding together, helping).
- Grasp the state of teamwork in the supplementary fit aspect.
- Confirm the effect on the perceived performance recognized by members.
- Combine it with teamwork in the task-activation aspect to complete the full picture of departmental teamwork.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when the degree of human bonding is grasped through the measurement of group cohesiveness, when that result is confirmed to have a significant effect on members' perceived performance, and when, combined with the task-activation aspect, it is actually reflected in management as an additional condition over the medium and long term.
