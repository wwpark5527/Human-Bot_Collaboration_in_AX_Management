# HBRM (Human-Bot Role Management) — Goal

The goal is to manage the resources and roles of humans and bots in an AX organization in an integrated way under a single management principle.

## Objectives
- Confirm whether the principles of diversity management, complementary fit, human-AI collaboration, and organizational AX are applied together to the human-bot relationship.
- Check whether the existing HRM (human resource management) system can be extended to a mixed human-bot organization.
- Reveal in advance the risk that the principles of human-bot resource management are scattered individually rather than integrated.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when a judgment result is produced regarding whether the human-bot resource management principle (HBRM) of the AX organization is applied, and the level of that integration.
