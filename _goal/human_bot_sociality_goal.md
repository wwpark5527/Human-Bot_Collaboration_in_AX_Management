# Human-Bot Sociality — Goal

The aim is to establish by design the asymmetric trust and control relationship between humans and bots, so that humans can trust bots and understand their judgments. When the bot's sociality is divided into a human-bot axis and a bot-bot axis, the human-bot axis centers on the formation of human trust and the securing of safety.

## Objectives
- Form trust by enabling the bot to explain the grounds of its judgments to humans (explainability).
- Ensure that the bot's values and behavior are aligned with human values (alignment).
- Secure safety such that the bot does not harm humans.
- Design the bot so that it recognizes the human as its highest-order constraint and reward definer.

## Success Criteria
The objective is judged achieved when the relationship maintains an asymmetric master-tool or supervisor-supervised structure and explanation, transparency, and verification for the formation of trust are actually required and satisfied.
