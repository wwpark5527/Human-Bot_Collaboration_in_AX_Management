# Human Centrality — Goal

The goal is to establish throughout the organization the basic premise that all judgments about AI and the realization of AX are made on the premise of 'the survival and development of humans,' while not falling into a zero-sum human supremacism.

## Objectives
- Establish a criterion for judgment that rejects AI and AX harmful to the survival and development of humans, and make clear that the subject of that judgment is the human.
- Guard against the zero-sum thinking that 'humans always come first and bots must be sacrificed,' thereby laying the foundation on which the spirit of augmentation can be established.
- Take human centrality as the basic premise of the core-spirit system of management so that the three spirits — augmentation, respect for and use of diversity, and pursuit of complementary fit — are derived naturally.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when every decision on the introduction of AI and AX takes as its criterion whether it serves the survival and development of humans, when the subject of that judgment rests with humans, and when at the same time one does not fall into zero-sum thinking that unilaterally sacrifices bots.
