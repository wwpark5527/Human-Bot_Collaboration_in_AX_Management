# Human Illogicality — Goal

The goal is that AI/bots do not predict human behavior by logic and efficiency alone, but recognize the human characteristic of being moved by emotion, relationships, symbols, identity, and habit, and reflect it in design and operational judgment.

## Objectives
- The bot recognizes the emotional, relational, symbolic, identity-based, and habitual factors of the human stakeholders it deals with.
- The organization internalizes the criterion of treating an efficient but humanly disliked design as a failure.
- It is considered in advance that even a logically valid proposal is rejected if it does not earn trust.
- It is recognized and addressed that emotional resistance can become a core cause of organizational AX failure.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when it is confirmed that, in the design and operation of bots/AI, judgment actually takes into account not only efficiency and logic but also human emotion, relationships, symbols, identity, and habit.
