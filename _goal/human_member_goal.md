# H: Human — Goal

The goal is to design a structure of shared prosperity based on coexistence and cooperation by clearly distinguishing the non-augmented traditional human member (H) from the other three types (AH·AB·B) in the member-type classification of an AX organization.

## Objectives
- Clearly define the traditional role and scope that H-type members perform within the organization.
- Position H not within a 'human vs AI bot' opposition but within the four-type classification system (H/AH/AB/B).
- Lay the foundation for supporting, at the organizational level, the transition path from H to AH (augmented human).

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when H is clearly distinguished from AH/AB/B and placed accordingly in the classification of organizational members, and when the structure of coexistence and cooperation between H-type members and AH/AB/B-type members actually operates.
