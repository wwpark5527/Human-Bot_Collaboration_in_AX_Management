# Internalization of Respect for Humans — Goal

The goal is to internalize respect for humans, the most important element of bot education, into organizational AX operations not as moral injection but as a system design problem of ensuring that only actions which do not infringe upon humans are selected.

## Objectives
- Treat respect for humans not as the bot's moral belief but as a design problem of the action, reward, and verification structure.
- Redesign the bot's decision-making structure so that only actions that do not infringe upon humans are selected.
- Create an incentive structure in which actions that do not respect humans become disadvantageous within the system.
- Take classical duties of respect for humans and safety such as the three laws of robotics as the starting point for the internalization of respect for humans.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when the bot's action, reward, and verification structure actually operates so as to select only actions that do not infringe upon humans, and it is confirmed that respect for humans has been implemented as system design rather than as moral injection.
