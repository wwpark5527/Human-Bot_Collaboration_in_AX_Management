# Protection of Humanity (humanity) — Goal

The goal is to ensure that AI is aligned with human existence and development. When a leader of a hybrid organization judges direction and orchestration, the goal is to place the protection of humanity as the top-level criterion above any subordinate goal.

## Objectives
- Ensure that every directional judgment of the leader takes the protection of humanity as its top-level criterion.
- Continuously check whether the use of AI impairs human existence and development.
- Ensure that orchestration ability (coordination, integration, design) is exercised on top of the principle of protecting humanity.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when it is confirmed that the leader's judgment and the organization's use of AI are aligned with human existence and development — that is, when humanity has not been damaged.
