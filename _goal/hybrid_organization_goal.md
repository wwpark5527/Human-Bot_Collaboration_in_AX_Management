# True AX Organization / Hybrid Organization (AH+AB) — Goal

The goal is to reach a hybrid organization in which both humans and bots transition into a mutually augmented state (AH+AB) and form a collaborative cognition system.

## Objectives
- Identify whether both humans and bots have broken away from their existing state and transitioned into a mutually augmented state (AH, AB).
- Design and operate a collaborative cognition system in which humans and bots divide and share roles centered on roles rather than jobs and mutually augment one another.
- Complete, through the use of a proper OS for organizational AX and the realization of HBRM, a hybrid intelligent organization that continuously learns and adapts on a role basis.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when both humans and bots are augmented and, under the shared/governance context and AI governance, divide and share roles and jointly judge and learn, and when the result is accumulated not as individual performance but as the organization's operational assets.
