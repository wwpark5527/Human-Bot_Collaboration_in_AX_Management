# Inclusive / Just AI Transition ESG — Goal

The goal is to prevent and mitigate the stratification structure in which, during the AI-transition process, only some strata own, use, and direct AI while the majority are evaluated, replaced, managed, and excluded by AI, thereby building an organization in which humans' roles, income, right to judge, right to learn, and dignity are not damaged even amid the AI transition.

## Objectives
- Explicitly incorporate AI access, learning, use, verification, approval, recording, responsibility, and benefit-sharing into a company's ESG responsibility items.
- Diagnose and prevent the risk of differentiation (stratification) between the stratum that owns, uses, and directs AI and the stratum that is evaluated, replaced, managed, and excluded.
- Apply the principle of Just Transition to the AI transition so as to guarantee humans' roles, income, right to judge, right to learn, and dignity.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when AI access, learning, use, verification, approval, recording, responsibility, and benefit-sharing are explicitly incorporated into the organization's ESG responsibility system and the risk of stratification caused by AI is identified and mitigated.
