# Inclusive Transformation AX — Goal

The goal is to redesign the AI transition that proceeds on an efficiency-centered basis from the perspective of responsibility and inclusiveness, thereby preventing and mitigating AI stratification and achieving an organizational transformation in which humans' roles and rights are not damaged.

## Objectives
- Redesign the organization's AI transition so that it protects not only productivity and cost reduction but also humans' role, right to judge, right to learn, right to transition, right to appeal, and right to share in performance.
- Prevent and mitigate the AI stratification (role reduction, widening gaps, algorithmic management, weakened right of judgment, absence of appeal, concentration of outcomes) that efficiency-centered AX can bring about.
- Connect Inclusive Transformation AX to the core execution structures of ESG's S (social) and G (governance) so as to embed it in organizational operations.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when humans' role, right to judge, right to learn, right to transition, right to appeal, and right to share in performance are maintained together with documented protective devices during the AI-transition process, and efficiency and inclusiveness are secured at the same time.
