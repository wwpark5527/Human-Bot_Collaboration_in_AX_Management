# AI Accessibility — Goal

The goal is to measure the employee AI-tool access rate and the inter-departmental access gap, and thereby make it possible to confirm, from the accessibility side, whether an organization operates its AI transition responsibly. It is the first item of the 12 Inclusive Transition ESG indicators.

## Objectives
- Define the scope of the AI tools required for work, and calculate the proportion of employees who can actually access them.
- Calculate the access rate for each department to expose the gap between departments.
- Report the access rate and the gap together so that the average value does not conceal the exclusion of a particular department.
- Hand the measurement result on as grounds for measures to resolve the access gap.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when the employee AI-tool access rate and the inter-departmental access gap are calculated, and the access-rate figure and the per-department gap value are reported together.
