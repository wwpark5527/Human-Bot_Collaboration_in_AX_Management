# AI Education — Goal

The goal is to measure the AI-literacy education completion rate and job-specific reskilling hours, and thereby make it possible to confirm whether the organization is actually building in its members the capability required for AI transformation. It is the second item of the 12 Inclusive Transition ESG indicators.

## Objectives
- Define the scope of AI-literacy education and the standard for judging completion, and calculate the completion rate.
- Calculate the hours invested in job-specific reskilling at the job level.
- Report the completion rate and the reskilling hours together, thereby exposing whether education is concentrated in particular jobs.
- Hand the measurement result on as grounds for the allocation of education investment and for job-transition plans.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when the AI-literacy education completion rate and job-specific reskilling hours are calculated and the two values are reported together.
