# AI Utilization Capability — Goal

The goal is to measure AI-utilization work-improvement cases and the productivity-gain distribution, and thereby make it possible to confirm whether the access opened up and the capability built in the organization have been converted into actual work performance, and where and how much that performance has taken hold. It is the third item of the 12 Inclusive Transition ESG indicators.

## Objectives
- Define the standard for recognizing cases in which work was actually improved through the use of AI, and collect and aggregate the cases.
- Decompose the productivity gains by department and by job to calculate the **distribution** — do not stop at reporting the average value alone.
- Report the case tally and the gain distribution together, thereby exposing whether the gains are skewed toward some groups.
- Hand the measurement result on as grounds for plans to spread AI utilization and to resolve the capability gap.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when both the tally of AI-utilization work-improvement cases and the productivity-gain distribution are calculated and the two values are reported together.
