# Right to Appeal — Goal

The goal is to measure the number of requests to review AI results and the correction/remedy processing rate, and thereby make it possible to confirm whether the channel for raising objections to AI decisions actually works, and whether the objections raised lead to correction of the decision or remedy for the party concerned. It is the seventh item of the 12 Inclusive Transition ESG indicators.

## Objectives
- Identify the intake routes for review requests and tally the **number of review requests**.
- Calculate the **correction/remedy processing rate** as the proportion of requests in which the decision was actually corrected or the party concerned was remedied — mere closure or dismissal is not tallied as processing.
- Do not interpret the number of requests on its own, but read it together with the accessibility of the channel and the level of understanding of the explanations, so as to distinguish whether a low number is because there were no objections or because they could not be raised.
- Hand the measurement result on as grounds for improving the appeal procedure and the accessibility of the channel.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when the number of review requests is tallied together with the intake routes and the correction/remedy processing rate is calculated on the correction/remedy standard, and the two values are reported together.
