# Audit Record — Goal

The goal is to measure the preservation rate of the five kinds of record — prompt, data, result, correction, approval — and thereby make it possible to confirm whether the history of AI operation actually remains in a form that can be traced back later. It is the eighth item of the 12 Inclusive Transition ESG indicators.

## Objectives
- For each of the five kinds of record, define what is regarded as subject to preservation and calculate the **per-kind preservation rate**.
- Present the lowest per-kind preservation rate alongside, so that a state in which a particular kind is missing entirely is not buried in the average value.
- Confirm the connections between records and judge whether, from a single output, one can trace back to its prompt, data, correction, and approval.
- Hand the measurement result on as the basis for audit-response capability and for detecting missing approvals.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when the preservation rate of each of the five kinds of record is calculated and the per-kind minimum and whether tracing is possible are reported together.
