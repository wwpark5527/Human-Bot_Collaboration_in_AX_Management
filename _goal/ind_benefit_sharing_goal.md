# Benefit Sharing — Goal

The reason this indicator exists in an AX organization is to make the organization able to answer in figures **where** the productivity gains arising from the introduction of AI went. AI transformation almost always produces gains, but whether those gains went back to the members cannot be known unless it is measured separately. This indicator fixes their whereabouts in a single reinvestment rate.

## Objectives

- **Fix the whereabouts of the gains as a figure.** Calculate the ratio of the share reinvested through the three channels of compensation, education, and welfare relative to the AI productivity gains confirmed in the measurement period. The remainder not reinvested is precisely the share that stayed in the organization or went outside.
- **Make the denominator an object of verification.** The value most easily manipulated in this indicator is not the numerator but the denominator. If the gains are computed low, the reinvestment rate rises without effort. Therefore the objective is not to raise the reinvestment rate but to submit a reinvestment rate **whose basis of computation is disclosed**. By reporting the time series of the gains themselves alongside, an improvement achieved by shrinking the denominator is blocked.
- **Look at the three channels decomposed.** Calculate the amounts for compensation, education, and welfare separately. If only the total is seen, a high reinvestment rate can come out even when the entire amount is pushed into one channel, and that runs counter to the intent of the source's enumerating the three channels side by side.
- **Look also at who received it.** Even if the reinvestment rate is high, if that share went only to a few senior grades then the benefit has not been shared. By calculating the proportion of beneficiaries and the distribution by grade and employment type alongside, the concentration hidden behind the total amount is exposed.

## Success Criteria

This indicator is judged a success when all four of the following are satisfied in each measurement cycle.

1. The reinvestment rate is at or above the baseline the organization announced in advance.
2. **All three channels of compensation, education, and welfare exceed 0.** If even one channel is 0, it is not a success.
3. The computation method, reference point in time, and covered work for the gains (the denominator) are presented in a document, and the time series of the gains is reported alongside.
4. The proportion of beneficiaries is at or above the standard, and no concentration in a particular stratum is confirmed in the distribution by grade and employment type.

If even one of the four conditions is unsatisfied, the indicator is recorded as a failure, and the unsatisfied item and its figure are recorded alongside.
