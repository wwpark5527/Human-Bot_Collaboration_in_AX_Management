# Competence-Inferiority Type — Goal

The objective is to identify early the competence-inferiority backlash that originates in the self-deprecation of "I am inferior to AI," and to alleviate learning fatigue, digital inferiority, and especially the sense of technological alienation among middle-aged and older members, so that 'AI competence inequality' does not spread into withdrawal within the organization and intergenerational pressure.

## Objectives
- Identify early the withdrawal and self-deprecating reactions within the organization that arise from differences in the speed of AI utilization.
- Grasp and respond to the four concrete elements of learning fatigue, digital inferiority, self-deprecation arising from differences in AI usage ability, and the sense of technological alienation among middle-aged and older members.
- Alleviate the perception that "only young employees will survive" and the pressure among colleagues before they harden into the problem of AI competence inequality.

## Success Criteria
The objective is judged achieved when the competence-inferiority reaction is accurately identified among members who withdraw because of the gap in the speed of AI utilization, especially among middle-aged and older members, and when concrete responses to each of learning fatigue, digital inferiority, self-deprecation, and the sense of technological alienation (stepwise learning support, generation-friendly education design, and so on) have been implemented.
