# Control-Loss Type — Goal

The objective is to identify early the control-loss backlash that originates in the perception that "my right of judgment is shrinking," and to alleviate, through a clear answer to "who is the final judge," the distrust of members who feel that their expertise and authority are threatened in the phase where AI recommendation and automated decision-making expand.

## Objectives
- Identify early the resistance to AI recommendation and automated decision-making in job groups that hold professional rights of judgment, such as physicians, HR, and the financial sector.
- Grasp and respond to the four concrete aspects of manager resistance, defense of expert authority, algorithmic distrust, and refusal of AI recommendations.
- Clarify who the final judge is and thereby alleviate the perception that expertise and authority are being infringed.

## Success Criteria
The objective is judged achieved when the control-loss reaction is accurately identified in the phase where automated decision-making such as AI diagnostic assistance, AI hiring recommendations, and AI risk models is introduced, and when the locus of the final right of judgment and the auxiliary role of AI are clearly communicated so that expert resistance is alleviated.
