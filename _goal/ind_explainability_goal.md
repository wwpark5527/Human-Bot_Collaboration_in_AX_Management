# Explainability — Goal

The goal is to measure the AI-decision explanation-provision rate and user comprehension, and thereby make it possible to confirm whether explanations for AI decisions are actually provided and whether those explanations are understood by the people who receive them. It is the sixth item of the 12 Inclusive Transition ESG indicators.

## Objectives
- Define the scope of the AI decisions subject to the provision of explanations and calculate the **explanation-provision rate**.
- Measure with confirmation questions whether the person who received an explanation actually understood its grounds and criteria, and calculate **user comprehension**.
- Report the provision rate and comprehension together, thereby exposing the ranges in which explanations were provided but not understood.
- Hand the measurement result on as grounds for improving the form of explanations and the manner of delivery.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when both the AI-decision explanation-provision rate and user comprehension are calculated, comprehension is measured by a method of confirming understanding rather than satisfaction, and the two values are reported together.
