# Human Judgment Right — Goal

The goal is to measure the existence of a list of tasks requiring human approval and the number of approval omissions, and thereby make it possible to confirm whether the tasks a human must judge are actually prescribed, and whether that prescription is observed in operation. It is the fifth item of the Inclusive-Transition ESG 12 indicators.

## Objectives
- Confirm whether the list of tasks requiring human approval exists as a document, and record alongside the scope that list covers.
- Detect cases among the tasks falling under the list that were processed without approval, and tally the **number of approval omissions**.
- Report the scope of the list and the number of omissions together, so that the approach of eliminating omissions by narrowing the list does not appear as an achievement.
- Hand the measurement result on as grounds for revising the approval criteria and reinforcing the control points.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when the existence of the list of tasks requiring human approval and its scope are confirmed and the number of approval omissions is tallied, and the two values are reported together.
