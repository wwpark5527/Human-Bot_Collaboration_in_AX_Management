# Humanity-Defense Type — Goal

The objective is to understand the humanity-defense backlash that originates in the higher-order reaction that "what is human is disappearing," and to recognize and protect within the AX organization the unique value of human creativity, relationality, emotion, ethics, and intuition, thereby alleviating the perception that human uniqueness is being damaged.

## Objectives
- Identify early the humanity-defense reaction in job groups where human uniqueness is a core value, such as artists, professors, and the counseling and education fields.
- Grasp and respond to the four aspects of defending human creativity, emphasizing human relationality, asserting the superiority of emotion, ethics, and intuition, and the perception that "AI does not understand humans."
- Make clear that the introduction of AI complements rather than replaces the uniquely human creative, relational, and emotional values.

## Success Criteria
The objective is judged achieved when the humanity-defense reaction is accurately identified in the phase where generative AI is introduced into domains in which human uniqueness is valued, such as creation, education, and counseling, and when principles for AI utilization are designed in a direction that preserves and strengthens uniquely human values so that the backlash is alleviated.
