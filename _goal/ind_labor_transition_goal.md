# Labor Transition — Goal

The goal is to measure whether a job impact assessment was conducted before AI adoption and the transition-reassignment rate, and thereby make it possible to confirm whether the organization grasped in advance the change when jobs change through AI adoption, and whether it actually reassigned the people affected to new roles. It is the fourth item of the Inclusive-Transition ESG 12 indicators.

## Objectives
- For each AI adoption case, judge with evidence of timing whether a job impact assessment was conducted **before the point of adoption**.
- Take the people judged to be affected as the denominator and calculate the transition-reassignment rate.
- Report whether it was conducted and the reassignment rate together, thereby exposing the ranges in which assessment alone was done and did not lead to reassignment.
- Hand the measurement result on as grounds for the next AI adoption plan and for the design of transition reassignment.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when whether a job impact assessment was conducted before AI adoption is judged with evidence of timing and the transition-reassignment rate is calculated, and the two values are reported together.
