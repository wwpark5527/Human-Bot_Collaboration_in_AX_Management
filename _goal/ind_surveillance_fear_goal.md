# Surveillance-Fear Type — Goal

The objective is to identify early the surveillance-fear backlash that originates in the perception that "AI keeps watching me," and to alleviate the privacy anxiety and evaluation fear induced by the datafication, logging, and tracking of the AX organization.

## Objectives
- Identify early the anxiety reactions of members in situations where productivity-tracking AI tools, keyboard and mouse tracking, and AI meeting-analysis systems are introduced.
- Grasp and respond to the four concrete aspects of anxiety over behavior monitoring, concern about privacy infringement, fear of AI evaluation, and stress from work logs.
- Communicate transparently the purpose and scope of datafication, logging, and tracking, and thereby prevent them from being perceived as surveillance.

## Success Criteria
The objective is judged achieved when the surveillance-fear reaction is accurately identified in the phase where data-based management tools such as productivity tracking and behavior log collection are introduced, and when transparent communication about the scope and purpose of tracking and the manner of data use, together with privacy protection safeguards, has been put in place so that the anxiety is alleviated.
