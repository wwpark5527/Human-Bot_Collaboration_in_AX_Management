# Survival-Anxiety Type — Goal

The goal is to identify early the survival-anxiety-type backlash arising from the primal anxiety that "AI will replace me in the end," and to lower the uncertainty concerning restructuring, role reduction, promotion opportunities, and wages.

## Objectives
- Identify early the survival-anxiety-type reaction in repetitive knowledge-work job groups (call centers, office workers subject to document automation, developers, and so on).
- Grasp and respond to the four concrete anxiety elements of fear of restructuring, concern over role reduction, concern over reduced promotion opportunities, and anxiety over wage decline.
- Mitigate the survival-anxiety-type reaction at the individual level before it spreads into organization-level collective backlash (such as union-type backlash).

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to have been achieved when the survival-anxiety-type reaction is accurately identified in repetitive knowledge-work job groups such as call centers, office document automation, and development, and concrete responses to each of the restructuring, role-reduction, promotion, and wage anxieties (retraining, role redesign, communication on employment stability, and so on) have been implemented.
