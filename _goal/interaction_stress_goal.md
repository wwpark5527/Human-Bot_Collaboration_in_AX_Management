# Relational (Interaction) Stress — Goal

The goal is to identify and manage separately the stress of the human-bot relationship itself (loss of a sense of control, misreading of intent, imbalance of responsibility and authority, collapse of trust), which is missed by managing individual human stress or individual bot stress alone, so that it does not spread into AX-adoption failure or governance collapse.

## Objectives
- Observe relational (interaction) stress independently, separately from the stress of the individual actor (human or bot).
- Catch early the signs of loss of a sense of control, misreading of intent, imbalance of responsibility and authority, and collapse of trust.
- Intervene before relational stress expands into AX-adoption failure, governance collapse, increased organizational resistance, and performance decline.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to have been achieved when one or more of the four signs of relational (interaction) stress (loss of a sense of control, misreading of intent, imbalance of responsibility and authority, collapse of trust) is identified early and intervention is carried out before it expands into AX-adoption failure, governance collapse, and the like.
