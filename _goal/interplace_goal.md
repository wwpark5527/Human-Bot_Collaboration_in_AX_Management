# Interplace — Goal

The goal is to implement Belbin's team role (TR) theory operationally as a software tool, thereby measuring the TR characteristics of individuals and jobs in a highly reliable and valid manner and making the results usable in practice (team building, appraisal, counseling, placement, training, career development).

## Objectives
- Collect TR data on individuals and jobs through the 4 questionnaires (SPI/OA/JRE/JOA).
- Process the collected material in real time and output 14 kinds of report content.
- Secure reliability and validity by combining the subject's own subjective responses with the objective responses of those around them, and relative assessment with absolute assessment.
- Provide the results in a form actually usable for team building, appraisal, counseling, placement, manager training, and career development.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when the TR characteristics of individuals and jobs are comprehensively measured on the basis of the 4 questionnaires, and those results are actually used for grasping person-person fit and person-job fit and for presenting concrete measures to enhance teamwork.
