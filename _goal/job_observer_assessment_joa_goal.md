# Job Observer Assessment (JOA: job observer assessment) — Goal

The goal is to diagnose the results of observation by a job observer (job-observation items) and thereby secure the observation axis of job assessment, to be combined with the requirements data of the job requirement exercise (JRE).

## Objectives
- Secure job observers who can observe the target job.
- Collect responses on the basis of the job observer's observation results (job-observation items).
- Organize the collected job-observation data into a form combinable with the job requirement exercise (JRE) data.
- Secure the basis data to be used for deriving job characteristics and for staffing and selection advice.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when responses based on the results of job observation are collected completely, and those results are processed together with the JRE job-requirements data as input to job assessment and actually used for deriving job characteristics and for staffing and selection advice.
