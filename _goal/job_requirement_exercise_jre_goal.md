# Job Requirement Exercise (JRE: job requirement exercise) — Goal

The goal is to shift the assessment target from a person to a job and, by diagnosing the requirements that the job demands, to secure the requirements axis of the job assessment that will be combined with the Job Observer Assessment (JOA).

## Objectives
- Clearly define the job to be diagnosed.
- Collect responses on the basis of the requirements that the job demands (job-requirements items).
- Organize the collected job-requirements data into a form that can be combined with the Job Observer Assessment (JOA) results.
- Secure job-assessment material for judging what TRs the job should be composed of.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when responses based on the job-requirements items are collected completely, and that result is processed together with the JOA responses as input to the job assessment and is actually used in the person-job fit judgment coupled with an individual's TR characteristics.
