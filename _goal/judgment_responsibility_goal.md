# Judgment Responsibility — Goal

Following the principle that AI can generate many alternatives but the final judgment must be made by a person, the goal is to judge whether the AI result fits the organization's purpose, whether it can be used in the work, and whether further approval or review is needed.

## Objectives
- Judge the accuracy and organizational suitability of the results AI has produced.
- Judge whether the AI result may be released externally or reflected in decision-making.
- Judge whether further review or supervisor approval / legal / security review is required.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when the final judgment on whether the AI result may be used and whether approval or review is required has been made by a person.
