# Just Transition — Goal

By applying to AI transition the just-transition principle established in climate transition, the goal is that in the course of AI adoption no one is left behind and that humans' roles, income, judgment rights, learning rights, and dignity are protected.

## Objectives
- Reflect the just-transition principle explicitly in the AI transition plan so that the transition process is designed to be fair and inclusive.
- Just as a climate transition must not abandon workers and local communities, ensure that an AI transition likewise does not damage humans' roles, income, judgment rights, learning rights, and dignity.
- Present the just-transition principle clearly as the source principle of AI Inclusive-Transition ESG (Just AI Transition ESG).

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when a fair and inclusive design is achieved so that no one is left behind in the AI transition process, and humans' roles, income, judgment rights, learning rights, and dignity are guaranteed.
