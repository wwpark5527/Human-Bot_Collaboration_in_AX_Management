# action (execution) — Goal

The goal is to confirm that it becomes the organization's capability only when it moves from knowledge to action, and to have the skill defined by the runtime actually performed.

## Objectives
- Actually perform the skill following the defined SkillRuntime (input, materials, tools, prohibitions, result format, review approver, record location).
- Actually carry the knowledge-action chain from a "structure of knowing" into a "structure of execution."
- Make whether it was executed and who executed it verifiable.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when it can be confirmed whether this knowledge was actually used and who executed it.
