# context (organizational standard) — Goal

The goal is to accumulate the results that have passed through review and feedback into the organization's standards, thereby closing the knowledge-action chain and taking them as the input for the next execution.

## Objectives
- Accumulate the results that have passed through review and feedback into the organizational standard.
- Terminate the knowledge-action chain and make it an input the next execution can refer to.
- Make the next execution more refined on the basis of the accumulated execution assets.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined by whether it was reflected into the next standard, and whether the next execution becomes more refined on the basis of the accumulated execution assets.
