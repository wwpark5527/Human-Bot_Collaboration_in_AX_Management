# feedback — Goal

The goal is to return the errors and confusion revealed in review into the next standards and materials, so that the organization actually learns.

## Objectives
- Reflect recurring errors into the organizational standards.
- Turn the errors AI caused into prohibition rules.
- Organize confusion into training material so that it is reused in the next execution.

## Success Criteria
Whether recurring errors are reflected into the standards, whether AI errors are turned into prohibition rules, whether confusion is organized into training material — success is determined when this process actually happens and the organization has learned.
