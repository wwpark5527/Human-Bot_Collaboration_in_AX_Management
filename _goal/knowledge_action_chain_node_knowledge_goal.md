# knowledge — Goal

As the entry node of the knowledge-action chain, the goal is to secure a "structure of knowing" in which data, information, and evidence are organized and connected.

## Objectives
- Organize data, information, and evidence into connected knowledge so that the organization can answer "what do we know."
- Make the knowledge take on a static structure that can function as the basis for judgment.
- Recognize the limit that if one stops at this stage it is merely a structure of knowing and does not lead to execution and results, and prepare to move on to the next node (skill).

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the question "what do we know" can be answered with a structure in which data, information, and evidence are connected.
