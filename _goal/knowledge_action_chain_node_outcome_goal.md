# outcome (result) — Goal

The goal is to capture not the deliverable itself but the actual change in results that the deliverable caused.

## Objectives
- Confirm what the deliverable produced by execution (action) actually changed.
- Organize the results on the basis not of "what was produced" but of "what changed."
- Pass the actual changes in results — improved decision-making, fewer omissions, fewer customer complaints, and the like — to the next node (review).

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when not the deliverable but the change in results (what changed) has been clearly identified.
