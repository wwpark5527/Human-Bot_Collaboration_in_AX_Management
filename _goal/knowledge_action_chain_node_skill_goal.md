# skill — Goal

The goal is not to leave knowledge as a document meant to be read, but to convert it into a skill meant to be executed.

## Objectives
- Convert the organized knowledge into an executable unit (a skill).
- Distinguish "document" from "skill" so that the organization gives priority to what is meant to be executed.
- Turn documents such as policies, legal standards, and how to write minutes into execution units such as policy-based answer verification, contract risk analysis, and meeting summary and action derivation.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when that knowledge has been converted not into a document meant to be read but into an executable skill.
