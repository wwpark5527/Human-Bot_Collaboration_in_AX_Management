# Premise-Relation Measurement — Goal

The goal is to check the connection state between a higher concept and a lower concept, and thereby detect the state of speaking about an advanced concept without understanding the concept that underlies it.

## Objectives
- Check the connection state between the higher (advanced) concept and the lower (foundational) concept that is its premise.
- Discover the state where the words are advanced but the foundational concept is weak, and thereby reveal the foundational-concept deficiency.
- Confirm whether the human actually understands the premise of the advanced expression AI provides.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when it is judged whether one actually understands the foundational concept that is the premise of the advanced expression (whether one is in a state where the words are advanced but the foundational concept is weak).
