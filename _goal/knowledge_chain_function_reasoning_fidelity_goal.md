# Reasoning-Chain Fidelity Measurement — Goal

The goal is to evaluate not whether the conclusion was correct but the validity of the process (the reasoning path) leading to the result.

## Objectives
- Confirm whether the intermediate steps are valid and whether each step leads logically to the next step.
- Confirm whether the last step actually supports the final answer.
- Confirm whether each step is connected to evidence, and thereby judge the reasoning path itself rather than the result.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the intermediate steps are valid, each step leads to the next step, the last step actually supports the answer, and each step is connected to evidence.
