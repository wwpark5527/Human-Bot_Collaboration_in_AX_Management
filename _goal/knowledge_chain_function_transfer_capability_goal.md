# Transferability Measurement — Goal

The goal is to measure the ability to apply what one has learned to a new situation, that is, transferability, and thereby distinguish true understanding from familiar problem solving.

## Objectives
- Confirm whether understanding, principle, and structure are maintained even when the sentence, the example, or the domain changes.
- Confirm whether the concept is revised on one's own when a counterexample arises, and thereby judge whether there is true understanding.
- Redefine understanding not as "the ability to solve a familiar problem" but as "the ability to maintain the meaning structure even in a new situation."

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when transferability is secured: understanding is maintained even when the sentence changes, the principle is maintained even when the example changes, the structure is applied even when the domain changes, and the concept is revised even when a counterexample arises.
