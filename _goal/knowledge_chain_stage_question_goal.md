# Question — Goal

The goal is to keep a request that a human or an AI poses to the organization from ending in one-off consumption, and to make it connect onward as the first node of the circulation structure of the knowledge chain.

## Objectives
- Design the question not as the one-off starting point of "question → answer → end" but as the entry stage of a circulation/accumulation structure.
- Make the question connect naturally to the organizational context reference, governance validation, and result recording that follow.
- Break out of the one-off response structure of existing LLMs and make the question the starting point of a flow in which it accumulates into organizational knowledge.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when the question is not consumed one-off and ended, but is connected onward to context reference, validation, and recording.
