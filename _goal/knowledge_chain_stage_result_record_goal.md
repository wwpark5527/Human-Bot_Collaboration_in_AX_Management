# Result Recording — Goal

The goal is to leave the generated result and its grounds and process within the organization, turning a response from a "scattered response" into an asset that can be accumulated.

## Objectives
- Leave within the organization, as a record, the result produced in the response generation stage and the grounds and process that led to that result.
- Record the premises used along with it, so that one can later trace why it is correct, where it went wrong, and what premises were used.
- Convert the response from a one-off "scattered response" into an asset that can be accumulated and drawn on again by the organization.
- Organize the recorded result into a form that can be passed on to the next stage (re-reflection into organizational knowledge).

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when the grounds of the result are recorded so that one can later trace why it is correct, where it went wrong, and what premises were used.
