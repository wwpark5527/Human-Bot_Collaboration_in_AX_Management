# Reuse by the Next AI/Human — Goal

The goal is to have the next AI or the next human draw on the accumulated organizational knowledge again, thereby closing the chain and completing the circulation/accumulation structure of the knowledge chain.

## Objectives
- Provide the organizational knowledge updated in the re-reflection into organizational knowledge stage as input to the new task of the next AI or the next human.
- Have the next task use the accumulated organizational knowledge rather than starting over from scratch.
- Close the chain through reuse and thereby complete the circulation/accumulation structure of the knowledge chain.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when the next task takes the accumulated organizational knowledge as input rather than starting over from scratch (when consistency of results is secured).
