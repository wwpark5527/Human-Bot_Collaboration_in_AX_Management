# Korean Government (Human-Centered AI Ethics Standards) — Goal

The goal is for organizational AX to reflect the Korean government's human-centered AI ethics standards (3 basic principles and 10 core requirements), so that AI does not infringe on human autonomy and contributes to the welfare of humankind.

## Objectives
- Internalize into the operation of organizational AX the 3 basic principles: the principle of human dignity, the principle of the public good of society, and the principle of the purposiveness of technology.
- Reflect into practice the 10 core requirements: guaranteeing human rights, protecting privacy, respecting diversity, prohibition of infringement (harmlessness), fairness, safety, accountability, transparency, data management, and explainability.
- Design and operate AI so that it does not infringe on human autonomy.
- Make the use of AI contribute substantively to the welfare of humankind.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when the AI use of organizational AX actually has the system of 3 basic principles and 10 core requirements, and it is confirmed that it contributes to the welfare of humankind without infringing on human autonomy.
