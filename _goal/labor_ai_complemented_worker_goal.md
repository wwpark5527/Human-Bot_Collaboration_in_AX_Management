# AI-Complemented Worker — Goal

The goal is that a worker whose productivity and decision-making ability have improved through the use of AI does not bear only a correspondingly increased demand for performance, but receives fair treatment commensurate with the improved capability.

## Objectives
- Identify the workers and work areas whose productivity and decision-making ability improve through the use of AI.
- Manage working conditions so that improved productivity does not result only in "being demanded higher performance in less time."
- Establish a system of compensation and recognition commensurate with the increment in productivity.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when the productivity improvement of AI-complemented workers is confirmed and a commensurate improvement in compensation and working conditions is achieved.
