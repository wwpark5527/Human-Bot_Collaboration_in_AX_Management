# AI-displacement/reduction-risk worker — Goal

The goal is to identify early the workers placed at risk of automation replacement because they handle repetitive, documented work, and to prepare transition paths in preparation for the reduction or disappearance of the value of their work.

## Objectives
- Identify the repetitive, documented work that easily becomes a target for automation and the workers who handle it.
- Predict early the point at which the value of the work falls or disappears, and thereby secure time to respond.
- Prepare response paths such as retraining and job transition, and thereby mitigate the erosion of the worker's role and bargaining power.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when the work at high risk of automation and the workers in charge of it are identified, and retraining and transition paths are actually prepared and operated.
