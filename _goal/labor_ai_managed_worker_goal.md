# AI-managed worker — Goal

The goal is that workers whose work is assigned, evaluated, and monitored by algorithms understand those standards and are treated fairly.

## Objectives
- Identify the workers and work areas whose work is assigned, evaluated, and monitored by algorithms.
- Disclose the assignment, evaluation, and monitoring standards transparently so that workers can understand them.
- Prevent disadvantage or unfairness arising from the opacity of the standards.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when the assignment, evaluation, and monitoring standards of algorithmic management are transparently disclosed and workers can understand them.
