# Context-designer AX talent — Goal

The goal is to cultivate and secure within the organization talent who do not stop at merely using AI but directly design purposes, standards, verification, and feedback, thereby turning AI into part of the organization's operating structure.

## Objectives
- Identify and cultivate talent who can themselves design the purposes, judgment standards, verification standards, and feedback systems to be assigned to AI.
- Ensure that the outputs designed by such talent (common context, verification and feedback standards) are actually reflected in the organization's operating structure.
- Make it, as the most leading position among the 4 types of labor differentiation in the AI era, the transition path for workers of the other types (complemented / managed / at risk of displacement-reduction).

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when talent who directly design purposes, standards, verification, and feedback are identified, and their design outputs have been converted into the organization's operating structure, such as the common context and verification standards.
