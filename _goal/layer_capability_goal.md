# Capability layer — Goal

The goal is to identify early the capability-layer reactions of inferiority and helplessness — "Am I falling behind?" — that members experience during the AX organizational transition, and to mitigate them through learning and support.

## Objectives
- Identify whether a member's reaction originates in a threat to their relative competence position (the capability layer), and thereby distinguish it from a survival-layer reaction.
- Respond early so that inferiority and helplessness, the representative emotions of the capability layer, are not left unattended and transferred to the relationship and existence layers.
- Provide learning and retraining opportunities that reduce the competence gap versus AI and colleagues.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when a member's backlash against the AX transition is accurately diagnosed as originating in the capability-layer question "Am I falling behind?", and learning support for mitigating inferiority and helplessness has been carried out.
