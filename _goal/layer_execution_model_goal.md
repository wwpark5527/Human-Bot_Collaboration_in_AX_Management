# Execution model — Goal

The goal is to concretize the concepts theorized at the proposed-concept layer into operating procedures that can actually be run inside an organization, such as impact assessment, authority design, verification, approval, recording, performance sharing, and improvement loops.

## Objectives
- Convert the proposed concepts (theorization) into procedures executable within the organization.
- Design an operating model that includes impact assessment, authority design, verification, approval, recording, performance sharing, and improvement loops.
- Confirm whether the execution model has been concretized to a level at which it can work in actual organizational operations.

## Success Criteria
It is judged a success when the proposed concepts are concretized into procedures executable in organizational operations (from impact assessment through improvement loops) and thereby operationalized.
