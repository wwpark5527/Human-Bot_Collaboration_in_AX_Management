# Existence layer — Goal

The goal is to identify early the existence-layer reaction of identity confusion — "Is the meaning of being human diminishing?" — that members experience during the AX organizational transition, and to reaffirm for them the meaning and role that are uniquely human.

## Objectives
- Identify whether a member's reaction originates in a threat to the very meaning of human existence (the existence layer), and thereby distinguish it from survival-, capability-, and relationship-layer reactions.
- Respond early so that identity confusion, the representative emotion of the existence layer, is not left unattended and does not spread into fundamental resistance across the organization.
- Clarify what meaning and value only humans can carry even after the AX organizational transition, and thereby mitigate identity confusion.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when a member's backlash against the AX transition is accurately diagnosed as originating in the existence-layer question "Is the meaning of being human diminishing?", and measures reaffirming uniquely human meaning and roles have been carried out to mitigate identity confusion.
