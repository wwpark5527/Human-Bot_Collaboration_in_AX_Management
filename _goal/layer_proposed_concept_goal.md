# Proposed concept — Goal

Based on the problems diagnosed at the reasonable interpretation layer, the goal is to theorize the concepts the author newly puts forward, such as inclusive AI-transition ESG, context access rights, and a responsible operating system.

## Objectives
- Propose new concepts (for example, inclusive AI-transition ESG, context access rights, a responsible operating system) from the problem diagnosis (reasonable interpretation).
- Make clear that the proposed concepts come from the author's design, distinguishing them from verifiable fact and reasonable interpretation.
- Lay the theoretical foundation so that the proposed concepts can be developed into an operable form at the next layer (execution model).

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when a new concept is theorized and explicitly proposed from the problem diagnosis, and it is clearly marked as the author's design.
