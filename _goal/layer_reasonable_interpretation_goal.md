# Reasonable interpretation — Goal

From the external evidence verified at the verifiable fact layer, the goal is to diagnose problems by reasonably inferring the possibility of gaps that AI transformation may create.

## Objectives
- Infer from the confirmed facts (literature from international organizations and research institutes) the possibility that gaps in access, competence, decision authority, and performance distribution arise.
- Distinguish whether the inference is a "reasonable" interpretation grounded in facts or an ungrounded conjecture.
- Present the result of the inference clearly as a problem diagnosis and make it the foundation for the next layer (proposed concept).

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the possibility of gaps arising is reasonably inferred from the confirmed facts and that result is clearly presented as a problem diagnosis.
