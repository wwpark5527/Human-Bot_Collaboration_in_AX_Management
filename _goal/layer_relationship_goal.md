# Relationship layer — Goal

The goal is to identify early the relationship-layer reactions of alienation and defensiveness that members experience in the AX organizational transition — "Is my position in the organization weakening?" — and to preserve their networks.

## Objectives
- Identify whether a member's reaction originates from the weakening of position and relationships within the organization (the relationship layer), and distinguish it from survival-layer and capability-layer reactions.
- Respond early so that alienation and defensiveness, the representative emotions of the relationship layer, are not left unattended and do not transfer to the existence layer.
- Support members so that they can maintain their network and role position within the organization even after the AX transition.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when a member's backlash against the AX transition is accurately diagnosed as originating from the relationship-layer question "Is my position in the organization weakening?" and network-preserving measures to relieve alienation and defensiveness have been carried out.
