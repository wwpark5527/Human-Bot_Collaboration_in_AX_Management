# Survival layer — Goal

The goal is to identify early and respond to the survival-layer reactions of anxiety and fear that members experience in the AX organizational transition — "Is my job disappearing?"

## Objectives
- Identify whether a member's reaction in the organizational transition phase originates from the survival layer (the threat to the continuation of their job).
- Respond early so that anxiety and fear, the representative emotions of the survival layer, are not left unattended and do not transfer to the other layers (capability, relationship, existence).
- Prepare communication and redeployment measures that lower the uncertainty about the continuation of jobs.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when a member's backlash against the AX transition is accurately diagnosed as originating from the survival-layer question "Is my job disappearing?" and measures to relieve the corresponding anxiety and fear have been carried out.
