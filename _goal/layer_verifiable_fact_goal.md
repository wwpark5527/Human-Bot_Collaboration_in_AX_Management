# Verifiable fact — Goal

When beginning the argument for inclusive AI-transition ESG, the goal is to clearly distinguish externally verifiable facts, such as those from international organizations and research institutions, from the author's interpretations and proposals, and thereby to make the evidentiary level of the claims transparent.

## Objectives
- Identify as "verifiable fact" only what can be confirmed in the literature of international organizations and research institutions concerning AI gaps, labor transition, algorithmic management, and human-rights risks.
- Separate the evidentiary levels so that verifiable facts are not confused with the subsequent layers (reasonable interpretation, proposed concept, execution model).
- Present the external evidence clearly as the verified starting point of the inclusive AI-transition ESG argument.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when only what is confirmed in the literature of international organizations and research institutions is classified as "verifiable fact" and is not mixed with interpretive, proposed, or operational content.
