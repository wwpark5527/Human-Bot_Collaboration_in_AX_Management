# Least-preferred Role — Goal

The goal is to accurately identify roles that cannot be manifested even when both effort and support are applied, thereby confirming early the blanks that cannot be resolved by managing internal members and securing the grounds for recruitment and replacement judgments.

## Objectives
- Identify, for each individual, the roles that are not manifested even when maximum effort and others' support are applied.
- Clarify the boundary with potential/manageable roles to prevent misclassification.
- Confirm the TR blanks that cannot be resolved by managing internal members.
- Provide the grounds for abandoning the given TR or for judging the recruitment or replacement of personnel who hold that TR as a natural role.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when a least-preferred role is clearly identified by the criterion "cannot be manifested even when both effort and support are applied," and that result can be used directly in the decision to abandon internal management or to recruit or replace.
