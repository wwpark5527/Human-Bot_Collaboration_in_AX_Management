# The 1st LLM — Goal

The goal is to accurately recognize the character and limits of the external general-purpose LLM (the first LLM) the organization is already using, and thereby to judge in advance the hallucination and security risks that arise when it is used for organizational AX as it is.

## Objectives
- Identify that the external LLM the organization uses is the general-purpose 'first LLM' supplied by big tech.
- Diagnose the limits of hallucination and security leakage that come along with the ability to generate natural sentences.
- Recognize the limits of using the 'first LLM' on its own and judge the need for transformation into the 'second LLM'.

## Success Criteria
The goal is determined to have been achieved when the organization can accurately diagnose the generation ability of the first LLM and its limits (hallucination and security leakage), and judge the need for transformation into the second LLM.
