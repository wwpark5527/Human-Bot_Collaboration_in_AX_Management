# The 2nd LLM — Goal

The goal is for the organization to internalize its own discipline and context on top of the external general-purpose LLM (the first LLM) and thereby to be equipped with a controllable and reliable system of use at the level of the 'second LLM'.

## Objectives
- Recognize the paradigm shift from a text-generation focus (the first LLM) to a knowledge-control focus (the second LLM).
- Carry out the shift in operating entity from big tech-centered operation to the individual enterprise internalizing its own rule set.
- Build an enterprise-customized AX system that resolves the problems of security and hallucination and reduces electric power and token costs.

## Success Criteria
The goal is determined to have been achieved when it is judged that the organization has made the LLM learn its own discipline and context and has converted enormous generative capability into controllable performance reliability, that is, when the level of the layer 3 knowledge chain or of the sum of layers 1 through 4 is in place.
