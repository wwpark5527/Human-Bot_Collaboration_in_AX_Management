# The Third LLM — Goal

The goal is for the organization not to remain at layers 1 through 4 (the level of the 'second LLM') but to set as its ultimate goal being fully in place up to layer 5 and reaching the final completion status of the 'third LLM'.

## Objectives
- Recognize that the 'third LLM' is the topmost designation for the state in which the whole of layers 1 through 5 is fully in place.
- Distinguish that the difference between the sum of layers 1 through 4 (the 'second LLM') and the sum of layers 1 through 5 (the 'third LLM') lies in the presence or absence of layer 5.
- Confirm the detailed content of layer 5 (Chapter 8) and establish a roadmap for reaching the 'third LLM'.

## Success Criteria
The goal is determined to have been achieved when the organization can accurately distinguish the difference in status between the 'second LLM' (layers 1 through 4) and the 'third LLM' (layers 1 through 5), and can establish a roadmap toward having layer 5 fully in place.
