# Layers 1–2 (the domain of DX) — Goal

The goal is to accurately identify that layers 1–2 of the LLM architecture, as the domain of DX, remain at data and information processing, and to grasp how the formation of layer 3 (the knowledge chain) strengthens these lower layers.

## Objectives
- Identify that the organization's data and information processing system corresponds to layers 1–2 of the LLM architecture (the domain of DX).
- Recognize that layers 1–2 remain at the stage prior to knowledge-ization (data and information) and by themselves cannot make AI execute.
- Share within the organization the reverse effect by which the formation of layer 3 (the knowledge chain) qualitatively strengthens layers 1–2, and judge the need for DX-AX linkage.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when it can be accurately determined, by the standard of the architecture, whether the organization's data and information processing system remains at the stage prior to knowledge-ization (producing information) and whether it is being strengthened through the formation of layer 3.
