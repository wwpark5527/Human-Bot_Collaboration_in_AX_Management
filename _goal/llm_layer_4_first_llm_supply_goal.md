# Layer 4 (the LLM already released to the world = 'the 1st LLM') — Goal

The goal is to accurately diagnose the utilization status of layer 4 (the 1st LLM, the externally supplied LLM) that the organization has already secured, and to grasp the reason its utilization is not yet sufficient (the absence of layer 3).

## Objectives
- Identify that the external LLM (the 1st LLM) the organization has already introduced corresponds to layer 4.
- Diagnose the reality that only part of the information of layer 4 is being utilized and its cause (the absence of the layer-3 knowledge chain).
- Judge the need to shift to utilization at the level of organizational AX through linkage with layer 3, rather than the introduction of layer 4 alone.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when the organization can accurately diagnose the level of utilization of the externally supplied LLM (layer 4) and its limitation (partial utilization), and can judge the need for linkage with layer 3.
