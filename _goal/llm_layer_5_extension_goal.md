# Layer 5 (the layer added in Chapter 8) — Goal

The goal is to enable the organization not to stop at layers 1 through 4 of the LLM architecture diagram, but to recognize whether it is fully in place up to layer 5 and to set the completed status at the level of the 'third LLM' as its aim.

## Objectives
- Recognize how far, among layers 1 through 4, the organization's current use of LLMs has reached.
- Understand that layer 5 is an extension layer treated separately in Chapter 8, and recognize that having layers 1 through 4 fully in place alone is incomplete.
- Set the state in which layers 1 through 5 are fully in place (the 'third LLM') as the topmost goal status of organizational AX.

## Success Criteria
The goal is determined to have been achieved when the organization does not settle for the level of layers 1 through 4, recognizes the need to be fully in place up to layer 5, and sets the completed status of the 'third LLM' as its aim.
