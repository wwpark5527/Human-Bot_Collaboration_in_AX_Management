# Local environment — Goal

The goal is to put in place a local work context in which, within a specific task, AI can work according to the same purpose, standards, role, source, and format as humans.

## Objectives
- Identify the scope of the work that AI actually performs at the individual work unit.
- Confirm whether the purpose, standards, role, source, format, and feedback of that task are explicitly managed.
- Reveal in advance the risk that AI does not know the standards of the task because there is no common context.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the question "By what standards is this task performed?" can be answered by each of purpose, standards, role, source, format, and feedback.
