# Meaning/cognitive distance — Goal

The goal is to define meaning/cognitive distance as the combination of eight factors and to make it measurable, so that the value of AI can be evaluated not by the quantity of its answers but by how much it has reduced the distance between the current conceptual structure and the target knowledge structure.

## Objectives
- Compute the distance by combining the eight factors of conceptual structure, level of mastery, belief structure, premise relations, fidelity of the reasoning chain, reliability of evidence, transferability, and epistemic maturity.
- Make it possible to evaluate the substantive value of AI by the amount of reduction in this distance rather than by the quantity of the outputs AI has produced.
- Reflect the fact that different knowledge structures may lie hidden behind the same answer, and thereby recover the differences in level of understanding that cannot be identified from answers alone.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when all eight factors — conceptual structure, mastery, belief structure, premise relations, fidelity of the reasoning chain, evidence, transfer, and epistemic maturity — are observed and recorded, and the meaning/cognitive distance value is produced.
