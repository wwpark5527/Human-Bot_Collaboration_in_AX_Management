# Meaning design capability — Goal

The goal is for humans to create the vision, identity, and community meaning that AI cannot make, and thereby to form the meaning system and the relations among meanings that make problem framing possible.

## Objectives
- Acquire the capability to grasp the meanings, large and small, related to the organization and its work.
- Form relations among meanings of various types and weave them into a single meaning system.
- Have humans directly design the vision, identity, and community meaning that AI cannot make.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when one can grasp meaning and form relations among meanings (large and small, or of various types), and has reached the state of having created a meaning system that supports problem framing.
