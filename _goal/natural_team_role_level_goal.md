# Team/Natural Role — Goal

The goal is to accurately identify the team-role level (the natural role) that an individual manifests easily and naturally without separate help or conscious effort, and thereby to fill the reference cells of the team role balance (TRB) judgment.

## Objectives
- Identify, for each individual, the roles among the 9 team roles that are manifested easily and naturally (commonly 2–3).
- Accurately distinguish whether a role is a natural role by whether others' help or conscious effort intervenes.
- Gather the natural roles of all team members and compute whether the 9 TR cells are filled.
- Provide the natural-role data as direct input to the judgment of whether TRB is established or not.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when each individual's natural roles are accurately diagnosed by the criterion "is it manifested easily without help or effort?" and the aggregated team result can be used immediately to judge whether the 9 TR cells are filled (whether TRB is established).
