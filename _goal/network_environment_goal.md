# Network environment — Goal

The goal is to put in place, at the whole-organization unit in which multiple local environments connect to each other, a network operating context (governance context) in which authority, security, verification, approval, recording, responsibility, and improvement are managed at the organizational level.

## Objectives
- Identify the multiple local work contexts existing within the organization (customer response, education, legal affairs, marketing, development, management reporting, and so on) as a single network unit.
- Manage at the organizational level the procedures by which those local work contexts are connected, verified, approved, recorded, held accountable, and improved.
- Make it so that, even though each local environment has its own standards, they are safely connected across the whole organization.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the question "By what standards are multiple work environments connected and managed?" can be answered by each of authority, security, verification, approval, recording, responsibility, and improvement.
