# Observer assessment (OA: observer assessment) — Goal

The goal is to collect the checklist responses of people around the measured member who know them well (four or more observers), and thereby to secure the objective response axis of individual TR diagnosis that will be combined with the subjective responses of the self-perception inventory (SPI).

## Objectives
- Secure four or more observers who know the person being measured well.
- Have each observer respond to the checklist of 45 positive adjectives and 27 negative adjectives.
- Organize the observer responses into a form that can be combined with the self-perception inventory (SPI) responses.
- Secure objective data that supports the reliability and validity of the measurement instrument.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when checklist-method responses are completely collected from four or more observers, and that result is actually used, combined with the subjective responses of the SPI, to produce an individual diagnosis result mixing relative and absolute evaluation.
