# OS for organizational AX — Goal

The goal is for the organization to move beyond the level of a mere AI platform or chatbot integration tool and to build a true organizational operating system (OS for organizational AX) that integrally operates, controls, and augments the work processes of humans, AI agents, and bots.

## Objectives
- Meet the necessary conditions for being called an OS for organizational AX (operating protocols, privacy & sovereignty, AI governance, human-bot role architecture, agent orchestration, persistent organizational memory).
- Secure step by step the additional conditions that raise performance, quality, and scalability (common & governance context, knowledge chain, complementary fit engine, continuous learning, provenance & verification, organizational digital twin).
- Establish a privacy and sovereignty system that uses external LLMs without losing the organization's data sovereignty.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when the necessary conditions of the OS for organizational AX are at least satisfied, AI results accumulate as the organization's operating assets rather than as individual products, and the organization has transitioned into "an organization in which AI can work."
