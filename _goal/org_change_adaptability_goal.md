# Organizational-change adaptability — Goal

The goal is not to refuse the changes that occur rapidly at many scopes and levels in an AX organization, such as roles, authority, collaboration, and evaluation, but to adapt to those changes and remain a valuable presence in the organization rather than being marginalized.

## Objectives
- Rapidly recognize the changes occurring in the areas of roles, authority, collaboration, and evaluation.
- Acquire an attitude and methods of adapting rather than resisting change.
- Remain continuously a valuable presence within the organization through adaptation to change.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to have been achieved when, without refusing change, one has adapted to organizational change and reached a state of remaining a valuable presence in the organization without being marginalized, even amid changes occurring rapidly at many scopes and levels.
