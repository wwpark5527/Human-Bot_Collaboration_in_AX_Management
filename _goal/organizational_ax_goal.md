# Organizational AX — Goal

Organizational AX aims to carry out the process of redesigning the organizational operating system itself around AI, thereby realizing an enterprise-wide organizational transformation that does not stop at partial optimization.

## Objectives
- Redesign the organization's decision-making structure, communication structure, power structure, and collaboration structure around AI.
- Beyond partial optimization that stops at adoption in some departments, realize an enterprise-wide system transformation in which work, data, structure, authority, evaluation, culture, and governance are connected.
- Systematically make organizational redesign decisions such as the level of AI's participation in decision-making, the locus of accountability (human/AI), changes in roles between departments, data access authority, and responsibility in the event of AI error.

## Success Criteria
The objective is determined to be being achieved when organizational AX does not remain at the level of the mere adoption of AI, automation, or a pilot project (PoC), and technological innovation, organizational innovation, cultural innovation, and governance innovation are proceeding simultaneously at the enterprise-wide level.
