# Common & Governance context — Goal

The goal is to form and implement the organization's data (common context) and rules (governance context) within the OS for organizational AX, and thereby to go beyond the level of a mere AI platform and reach an AI-native organizational operating system (the level of true AI 4.0).

## Objectives
- Structure the organization's data as a common context and embed it in the OS.
- Structure the organization's rules and policies as a governance context and embed it in the OS.
- Reach the level of an AI-native organizational operating system through the formation and implementation of the two contexts.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when the common context and the governance context are actually formed and implemented, and the organization has reached an AI-native organizational operating system (the level of true AI 4.0) rather than a mere AI platform.
