# Operating protocols & control standards — Goal

The goal is to embed within the OS for organizational AX the operating protocols by which humans and AI bots can work together, so that AI does not act arbitrarily outside the organization's norms but acts within them.

## Objectives
- Establish the systems of authority & approval · role allocation · access control.
- Embed exception handling · accountability · the escalation system · human override · the audit trail into the operating protocols.
- Ensure that every AI action is logged (logging & traceability) so that it can be traced after the fact.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when all nine core elements — authority & approval, role allocation, access control, exception handling, logging, accountability, escalation, human override, and audit trail — are embedded in the operating protocols, and AI acts within the organization's norms beyond mere connection.
