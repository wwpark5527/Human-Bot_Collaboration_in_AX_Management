# Organizational digital twin(ODT) — Goal

The goal is to dynamically reproduce in digital space the organization's structure, roles, workflows, decision-making, collaboration, performance, and even culture, and thereby to simulate, predict, and optimize organizational operation in real time or near-real time.

## Objectives
- Go beyond the level of a mere organization chart or ERP and dynamically reproduce in digital space how the organization actually works.
- Secure the capability to simulate, predict, and optimize organizational operation.
- Resolve the problem of organizational complexity that human managers alone cannot understand, and the problem of organizational experiments that are hard to conduct directly in reality.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when the organization's structure, roles, workflows, decision-making, collaboration, performance, and culture are dynamically reproduced in digital space as a living virtual replica, and simulation, prediction, and optimization of organizational operation have actually become possible.
