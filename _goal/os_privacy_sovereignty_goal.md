# Privacy & sovereignty — Goal

The goal is to capture the benefits of leveraging powerful external LLMs while blocking the leakage of the organization's internal information (data and context), so that the organization does not lose its data sovereignty.

## Objectives
- Identify the paths of internal information leakage at the points of connection with external LLMs (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and the like).
- When a possibility of leakage is confirmed, prepare technical and policy measures that block that path.
- Maintain the balance that simultaneously achieves the benefits of leveraging external AI and the preservation of organizational sovereignty.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when the possibility of internal data and context leaking through the connection with external LLMs is substantially blocked, so that the benefits of leveraging external AI are maintained while the organization's sovereignty is preserved.
