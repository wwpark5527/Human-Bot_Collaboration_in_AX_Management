# Personal AX — Goal

Personal AX aims for an individual to use AI tools to improve on their own the efficiency and effectiveness of their studies and work.

## Objectives
- The individual leverages AI such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of their studies and work.
- Realize a higher level of AX at the individual level through the use of AI agents and bots.
- Recognize that personal AX does not naturally lead to organizational AX or an AX organization, and manage AX at the individual level and at the organizational level separately.

## Success Criteria
The goal of personal AX is judged to be achieved when the individual decides on their own to use AI as a tool and realizes gains in the productivity of their studies and work at a low failure cost.
