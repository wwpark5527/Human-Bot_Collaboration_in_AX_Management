# Context design — Goal

The goal is to prepare the operating condition that translates human purposes and criteria into AI's execution conditions, so that the knowledge chain is converted and developed into a world model and actually used in a real organization.

## Objectives
- Recognize and prepare for the fact that, however excellent AI may be, if it does not know human purposes and criteria its results may diverge from the organization's actual judgment.
- Explicitly translate human purposes and organizational criteria into execution conditions that AI can follow.
- Connect the technical discussion to the problems of human work, organizational responsibility, and social sustainability.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when the AI execution conditions (purpose and criteria) are made explicit and AI operates within the organization's purposes and criteria.
