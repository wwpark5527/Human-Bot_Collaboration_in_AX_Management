# World model — Goal

The goal is to secure a model structure by which AI internally represents and predicts the state of the environment, its changes, and the results of actions, and thereby to have the technical foundation (the fourth layer) of the predictive intelligence system.

## Objectives
- Enable the organization to have the simulation capability that can answer the core question "what will happen."
- Build a model that can predict the current world state, state changes, the results of actions, and the comparison of paths.
- Reduce the risks of wrong decisions, wrong resource allocation, and wrong automatic execution by experimenting with the future before execution.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when the current world state, future changes, the results of actions, the comparison of paths, and pre-execution risk can be simulated.
