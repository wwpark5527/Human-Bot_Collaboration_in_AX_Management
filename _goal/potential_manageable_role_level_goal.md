# Potential/Manageable Role — Goal

The goal is to identify the potential roles that are not exercised naturally but can be exercised through self-management and colleagues' understanding and acceptance, and thereby to secure the team's maximum potential teamwork level and the targets for promotion to natural roles.

## Objectives
- Identify, for each individual, the roles that are not exercised naturally but can be exercised through conscious effort.
- Confirm whether the two conditions — the person's own self-management effort and colleagues' understanding and acceptance — are satisfied.
- Produce the potential roles as the basis for the maximum potential teamwork level the team can reach.
- Designate the potential roles as targets to be promoted to natural roles through internal member management.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when the potential roles are accurately identified against the two conditions of "self-management + colleagues' understanding and acceptance," and can be used in calculating the team's potential teamwork level and in the management plan for promotion to natural roles.
