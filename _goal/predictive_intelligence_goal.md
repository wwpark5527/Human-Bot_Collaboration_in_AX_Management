# AI predictive intelligence — Goal

The goal is to move away from the view that understands AI only as a question-answering and generation tool, and to redefine the essence of AX as the capability to predict future states and the results of actions.

## Objectives
- Check whether the organization's use of AI remains only at question answering and the generation of documents/images/code.
- Recognize the impact that AI's predictive capability (the prediction of future states and the results of actions) will have on the organization.
- Track the trends in the development and connection of the technologies that enable predictive intelligence (the world model, the knowledge chain, context design, and the like) and prepare for the formation of a predictive intelligence system.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when the organization recognizes AI not as a generation tool but as the capability to predict future states and the results of actions.
