# Right to AI access — Goal

The goal is to guarantee that all members of the organization can substantively access the AI tools and infrastructure needed for their work, and thereby to prevent stratification according to whether one has access to AI.

## Objectives
- Provide every employee with the opportunity to access the AI tools and infrastructure needed to perform their work.
- Identify and resolve the gaps in AI access across departments and job roles.
- Manage the AI access rate as a measurement indicator and continuously check the level at which the right of access is guaranteed.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when the opportunity to access the AI tools and infrastructure needed for work is provided to all employees, and the access gaps across departments are measured and managed.
