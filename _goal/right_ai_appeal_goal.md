# Right to AI appeal — Goal

The goal is to guarantee a procedure by which a party who does not agree with an AI evaluation, recommendation, or decision can demand reconsideration, and thereby to secure a procedural remedy against AI decisions.

## Objectives
- Prepare a procedure for requesting reconsideration of AI evaluations, recommendations, and decisions.
- Operate a handling system in which reconsideration requests actually lead to correction and remedy.
- Record reconsideration requests and their handling results and manage the effectiveness of the procedure.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when a procedure to demand reconsideration of AI evaluations, recommendations, and decisions is open, and requests are actually handled as correction and remedy.
