# Right to AI benefit-sharing — Goal

The goal is to settle within the organization a distribution structure in which the productivity gains arising from the introduction of AI are not concentrated in the hands of only some owners but are reinvested in the compensation, education, and welfare of members.

## Objectives
- Identify the productivity gains arising from the introduction of AI and calculate them in a measurable form.
- Establish the distribution criteria and the reinvestment principles so that the gains do not accrue solely to capital owners.
- Structure and implement the reinvestment paths of compensation, education, welfare, and organizational capability.
- Measure and disclose the reinvestment rate regularly and verify whether the distribution is skewed.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when AI productivity gains are not concentrated in the hands of only some owners but are distributed into compensation, education, and welfare.
