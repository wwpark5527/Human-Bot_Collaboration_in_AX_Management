# Right to AI explanation — Goal

The goal is to guarantee the transparency of AI decisions by explaining the AI result and its evaluation criteria in a way the party can understand.

## Objectives
- Prepare a procedure that provides an understandable explanation for decisions in which AI results intervene.
- Disclose the AI evaluation criteria in a form the party can access and understand.
- Standardize the scope and manner of providing explanations to fit the nature of the work.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when the AI result and its evaluation criteria are explained in a way the party can understand.
