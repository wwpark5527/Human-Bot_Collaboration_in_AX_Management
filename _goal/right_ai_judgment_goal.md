# Right to AI judgment — Goal

The goal is to guarantee that in important judgments humans continue to provide the purpose, meaning, and criteria and retain the final decision-making authority, so that AI outputs do not replace human judgment.

## Objectives
- Make clear the procedure by which humans set the purpose, meaning, and criteria in important decision-making areas.
- Identify the tasks that require human approval and operate the approval procedure.
- Institutionalize the arrangement whereby the final decision-making authority over AI outputs remains with humans.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when, in important judgments, humans provide the purpose, meaning, and criteria and remain in the final decision.
