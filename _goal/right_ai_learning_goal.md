# Right to AI learning — Goal

The goal is to guarantee that all members of the organization acquire AI literacy and secure the capabilities required in the AI era through continuous reskilling and upskilling.

## Objectives
- Provide AI literacy education opportunities to all employees.
- Operate reskilling programs so that employees can respond to job changes.
- Continuously provide upskilling opportunities that deepen existing capabilities.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when AI literacy education and reskilling/upskilling opportunities are provided to employees.
