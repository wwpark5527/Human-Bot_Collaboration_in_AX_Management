# Right to AI transition — Goal

The goal is to guarantee that, when a job changes due to the introduction of AI, members can safely move to a new role through role redesign and transition training.

## Objectives
- Identify in advance the personnel whose jobs change due to the introduction of AI.
- Prepare a role redesign plan suited to the changing job.
- Support personnel in adapting to the new role through transition training and transition placement.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when role redesign and transition training are provided as a job changes due to the introduction of AI.
