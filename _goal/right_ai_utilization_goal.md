# Right to AI utilization — Goal

The goal is to guarantee that AI use within the organization does not stop at the simple replacement of repetitive work but proceeds in the direction of substantially expanding the capability of human members.

## Objectives
- Design the introduction of AI from the perspective of expanding human capability rather than replacing humans.
- Manage AI use for the purpose of replacing repetitive work separately from AI use for the purpose of expanding capability.
- Discover cases of AI use that contributed to capability expansion and spread them across the whole organization.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when AI is used for the purpose of expanding human capability rather than replacing repetitive work, and the resulting cases of work improvement are confirmed.
