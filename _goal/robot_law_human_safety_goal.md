# ① Respect for (protection of) human safety and life — Goal

The goal is to realize, in the operation of organizational AX, the most classic and fundamental principle of internalizing respect for humans, by making bots (robots) not harm humans and not leave humans in danger unattended.

## Objectives
- Design and verify in advance that every action of a bot does not harm a human.
- Put in place a duty system through which a bot can recognize and intervene for a human in danger.
- Approach respect for humans as a matter of system design rather than moral injection, and redesign the action, reward, and verification structures around humans.
- Design the system so that only actions that do not violate humans are selected.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when a bot's actions do not harm humans and intervention that does not leave a human in danger unattended is actually confirmed.
