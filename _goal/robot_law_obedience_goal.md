# ② Obedience to Humans (Recognition of Dignity and Hierarchy) — Goal

The goal is to have the bot (robot) obey human orders within the range that does not conflict with the First Law (respect for human safety and life), thereby realizing the second principle of the internalization of respect for humans in organizational AX operations.

## Objectives
- Equip the bot with an obedience structure that carries out human orders in principle.
- Equip it with a priority verification procedure that determines whether the First Law is violated before an order is carried out.
- Design obedience to be subordinate to the higher principle by having orders that conflict with the First Law be refused.
- Approach respect for humans as a system design problem rather than as moral injection, and configure the order-execution structure around humans.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when it is actually confirmed that the bot carries out human orders that do not conflict with the First Law and refuses orders that do conflict with the First Law.
