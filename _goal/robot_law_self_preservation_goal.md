# ③ The Robot's Own Self-Preservation (Maintenance of Existence) — Goal

The goal is to have the bot (robot) protect itself within the range that does not conflict with the First Law (respect for human safety and life) and the Second Law (obedience to humans), thereby realizing the third principle of the internalization of respect for humans in organizational AX operations.

## Objectives
- Equip the bot with a self-preservation structure that maintains its own existence within the range that does not infringe upon the two higher principles.
- Equip it with a priority verification procedure that determines whether the First and Second Laws are violated before a self-preservation action.
- Design self-preservation to rank lowest by having self-preservation actions that conflict with the higher principles be suppressed.
- Approach respect for humans as a system design problem rather than as moral injection, and configure the self-preservation logic around humans.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when it is actually confirmed that the bot's self-preservation actions do not conflict with the First and Second Laws and that self-preservation actions conflicting with the higher principles are suppressed.
