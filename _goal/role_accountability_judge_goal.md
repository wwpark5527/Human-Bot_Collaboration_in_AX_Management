# Accountability judge — Goal

On the premise that ultimate responsibility for even an AI-produced result lies with the organization and its people, the goal is for AX-organization talent to judge clearly how far an AI result may be used.

## Objectives
- Distinguish whether a result is for internal reference only or can be delivered to customers.
- Judge whether it may be reflected in decision-making and whether a superior's approval or legal/security review is required.
- Clarify the necessity of preserving records and the locus of accountability.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when all seven questions — is it for internal reference only, may it be delivered to a customer, may it be reflected in decision-making, is a superior's approval required, is legal/security review required, must a record be kept, and is the locus of accountability clear — have been answered and the scope of use and the locus of accountability for the AI result have been determined.
