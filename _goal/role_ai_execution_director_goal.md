# AI Execution Director — Goal

The goal is for AX talent to become not a person who uses AI like a simple search tool, but a person who designs AI work and directs it clearly.

## Objectives
- Give directions with a clear role instead of vague requests.
- Specify the input materials and the output format concretely.
- Include the constraints, verification standards, and scope of responsibility in the direction as well.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to have been achieved when all six — role, input materials, output format, constraints, verification standards, and scope of responsibility — are expressed as a clear direction, so that it becomes a concrete work direction rather than a vague request such as "organize this material for me".
