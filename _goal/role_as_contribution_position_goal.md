# Role — Goal

The goal is to fix contribution into a repeatable and accountable structure. By defining a role not as a person's name tag (title) but as a functional position that arises in the process by which meaning turns into output artifacts and responsibility, it makes people, AI, and systems each hold their own position of contribution clearly.

## Objectives
- Structure recurring patterns of contribution into functional positions (roles).
- Assign roles by the criterion of position of contribution rather than title.
- Ensure that ultimate responsibility remains with the human or the organization even if the role changes.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when contribution is structured not as a particular individual's title but as a repeatable and accountable functional position.
