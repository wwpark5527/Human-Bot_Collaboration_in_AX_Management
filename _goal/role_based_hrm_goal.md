# RBHRM (Role-Based HRM, competence + role-based) — Goal

The goal is to shift from position- and control-centered HR management to RBHRM, which is centered on role and on autonomy and commitment.

## Objectives
- Shift from management centered on CBHRM (seniority + competence-based) to management centered on RBHRM (competence + role-based).
- Shift from a control-centered approach to one centered on autonomy and commitment.
- Shift from a position-centered approach to a role-centered one.
- Make full use of RBHRM as the HRM-level foundation for realizing team role balance (TRB).

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the focus of HR management substantively moves from position, control, and seniority/competence to role and autonomy/commitment, and that shift is fully used as the basis for realizing TRB and for the performance realization of the AX organization.
