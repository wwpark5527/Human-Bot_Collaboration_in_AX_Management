# Evidence keeper — Goal

On the premise that keeping only the result makes it impossible to confirm the course of the judgment, the reviewer, and the approval criteria, the goal is to keep evidence of the AI-usage process and thereby make AX a reviewable and accountable way of operating the organization.

## Objectives
- Keep as records the materials used, the AI instructions, the AI-generated results, and the human edits.
- Keep the verification criteria, the approval status, and errors and exceptions in a traceable form.
- Keep the improvements reflected, making AX not mere automation but a reviewable and accountable way of operating.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when all eight items — the materials used, the AI instructions, the AI-generated results, the human edits, the verification criteria, the approval status, errors and exceptions, and the improvements reflected — have been kept as records.
