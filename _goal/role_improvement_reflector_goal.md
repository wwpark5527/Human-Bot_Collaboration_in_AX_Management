# Improvement reflector — Goal

On the premise that an AX organization is not completed by a single use of AI, the goal is not to let the errors, omissions, failures, and corrections that arise in the AI-usage process end as individual mistakes but to reflect them into the organization's criteria for the next task.

## Objectives
- Grasp the errors, omissions, failures, and corrections that arose in the AI-usage process.
- Analyze the causes of failure and convert them from an individual problem into a problem of the organization's criteria.
- Update prompts, context, checklists, and deliverable schemas and reflect them into the next task.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when a failure is not left to end as an individual mistake but is connected to improving the organization's criteria, so that organizational criteria reflected into the next task — updated prompts, context, checklists, deliverable schemas, and so on — have been produced.
