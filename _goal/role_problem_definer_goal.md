# Problem Definer — Goal

On the premise that AI cannot understand a problem on its own in a way that fits the organization's context, the goal is for AX talent to clearly define by itself the problem the organization actually has to solve before assigning work to AI.

## Objectives
- Define the object to be solved clearly and without ambiguity.
- Reveal how the problem connects to work and performance.
- Set the required deliverable standards together with the constraints (security, cost, time, legal conditions).
- Determine in advance the performance indicators by which success will be judged.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to have been achieved when all five questions can be answered: what is the problem, why does it matter, what result is needed, what constraints exist, and what are the success criteria.
