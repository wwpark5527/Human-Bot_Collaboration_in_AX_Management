# Result Interpreter — Goal

On the premise that an answer produced by AI cannot become the organization's judgment as it stands, the goal is for AX talent to interpret the meaning of the AI result in the organizational context and convert it into an actual organizational judgment.

## Objectives
- Check whether the AI result fits our organization's situation.
- Judge the validity of the core logic and its executability.
- Inspect whether anything is missing and whether there is any dangerous interpretation.
- Complete the interpretation in a form usable in the organization's decision-making.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to have been achieved when all six questions can be answered: does it fit our organization's situation, is the core logic valid, is it executable, is anything missing, is there any dangerous interpretation, and can it be used in decision-making.
