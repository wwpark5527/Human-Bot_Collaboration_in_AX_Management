# Role vacancy — Goal

The goal is to identify in advance the role vacancy on the non-augmented side (B) in AH-H communication, and to define it as a problem of communication fairness so as to establish the grounds for a response.

## Objectives
- Contrast the roles on the augmented human (A) side (purpose and judgment · analysis and generation · criteria · history management) with the deficits on the non-augmented human (B) side (vacancy in interpretation, verification, recording, and response) to make the points of vacancy clear.
- Explicitly define role vacancy not as mere inconvenience but as a problem of communication fairness (communication power).
- When role vacancy is confirmed, establish the grounds for responding to it rather than leaving it unattended.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the presence of a vacancy in the interpretation, verification, recording, and response contributions on side B is explicitly identified and defined as a fairness problem.
