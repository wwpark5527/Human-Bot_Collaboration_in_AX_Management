# Validator — Goal

On the premise that AI results are fast but not always accurate and that using AI without verification is dangerous automation, the goal is for AX talent, as the quality manager of AI results, to check results by established criteria.

## Objectives
- Check factuality and provenance to filter out content without grounds.
- Inspect logicality and completeness to secure the consistency of the result.
- Check suitability and security so that it matches the organization's purpose and contains no sensitive information.
- Secure accountability and traceability so as to preserve whether it may be reflected in the work and its history.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to have been achieved when all eight verification criteria — factuality, provenance, logicality, completeness, suitability, security, accountability, and traceability — have been checked and a quality-check result preventing dangerous automation has been produced.
