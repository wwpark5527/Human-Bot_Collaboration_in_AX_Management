# Self-perception inventory (SPI) — Goal

The goal is to collect the subjective self-perception of the person being measured through a survey-item response format, and thereby secure the person's own response axis of the individual TR diagnosis that will be combined with the objective responses of the observer assessment (OA).

## Objectives
- Have the person being measured respond directly to the survey items themselves.
- Collect the subjective responses by the person themselves without omission.
- Organize the collected responses into a form that can be combined with the objective responses of the observer assessment (OA).
- Secure the person's own response data for identifying an individual's TR type and level.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when the responses of the person being measured are completely collected through a survey-item response format, and that result is combined with the objective responses of the OA and actually used to produce an individual diagnostic result that mixes relative and absolute evaluation.
