# Material — Goal

The goal is to clearly specify in advance which materials will be used when a skill is executed.

## Objectives
- Specify in advance the reference scope of the materials that may be used in executing the skill.
- Prevent the skill from being executed while the scope of materials remains unclear.
- As the second of the seven definition slots of SkillRuntime, take over the finalization of the input slot and form the premise of the tool slot.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when which materials will be used has been specified.
