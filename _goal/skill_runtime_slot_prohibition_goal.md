# Prohibition — Goal

The goal is to clearly specify in advance what must not be done during skill execution.

## Objectives
- Specify in advance the restriction boundary of skill execution (the prohibition rules).
- Prevent the skill from being executed while the prohibitions remain unclear.
- As the fourth of the seven definition slots of SkillRuntime, take over the finalization of the tool slot and form the premise of the result format slot.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when what must not be done has been stated.
