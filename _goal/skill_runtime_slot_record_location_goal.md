# Record Location — Goal

The goal is to clearly specify in advance where the skill's execution process and results are to be recorded.

## Objectives
- Specify the record location in advance so that the skill's execution history is traceable.
- Prevent the skill from being executed while the record location remains unclear.
- As the last of the seven definition slots of SkillRuntime, take over the finalization of the reviewer/approver slot and complete the entire SkillRuntime structure.

## Success Criteria
Success is determined when where it is recorded has been specified.
