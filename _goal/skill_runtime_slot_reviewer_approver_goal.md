# Reviewer/Approver — Goal

The goal is to clearly specify in advance who reviews and approves the results of a skill's execution.

## Objectives
- Designate in advance the specified party that will review and approve the results of a skill's execution.
- Prevent execution results from being settled while the reviewing and approving party remains unclear.
- As the sixth of the seven definition slots of SkillRuntime, it takes over from the settlement of the result format slot and forms the premise of the record location slot.

## Success Criteria
It is judged successful when who reviews and approves has been specified.
