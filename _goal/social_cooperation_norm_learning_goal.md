# Social Cooperation Norm Learning — Goal

The goal is that AI, in order to function as an organizational member, learns the cooperation norms of a human organization moved by power, trust, tacit knowledge, politics, culture, face, and emotion, and thereby shifts beyond an intelligent system to a socially compatible system.

## Objectives
- Have the AI learn situation-specific norms about when it should speak and when it should stay silent.
- Have the AI learn its mode of explanation and the criteria for selecting whom to persuade.
- Have the AI learn to avoid expressions that organizational members may find threatening.
- Shift the AI beyond an intelligent system to a socially compatible system.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when it is confirmed that the AI observes the organization's social cooperation norms (when to speak and when to stay silent, how to explain and persuade, which expressions are threatening) in actual interaction and operates as a socially compatible system.
