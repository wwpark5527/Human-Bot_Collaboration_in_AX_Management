# Augmentation Realization — Goal

The objective is to realize augmentation in actual organizational management as the first spirit of the system of basic management philosophy and core spirits, and thereby to establish a structure in which humans and bots mutually learn, co-evolve, and produce results together.

## Objectives
- Adopt and realize, as a core spirit, the management principle of designing bots not as a replacement for human labor but as an amplifier of human capability.
- Reflect a positive-sum rather than zero-sum structure throughout management decision-making and organizational design.
- Settle augmentation as the first spirit, forming a trinity together with respect for and use of diversity and the pursuit of complementary fit.

## Success Criteria
The objective is judged to be achieved when the perspective of amplification rather than replacement is consistently applied in management principles and decision-making, and when, through the formation of augmented humans (AH) and augmented bots (AB), members come to hold the perception that "AI strengthens me" and acceptance of organizational AX rises.
