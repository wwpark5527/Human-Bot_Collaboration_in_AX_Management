# Pursuing Complementary Fit — Goal

The goal is for humans and bots to fill in each other's shortcomings on the basis of role differentiation rather than competition, and thereby pursue the wholeness that maximizes the organization's overall performance.

## Objectives
- Connect the essential strengths of humans and bots to maximize the organization's overall performance.
- Establish a management principle that asks not "who is more superior?" but "how will we complement each other?"
- Concretely design and implement the four types of complementarity: cognitive, emotional, behavioral, and ethical.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when humans and bots do not merely divide the same work between them but combine their respective strengths to maximize the organization's overall performance, and, together with augmentation and respect for diversity, form the completed body of the trinity of spirits.
