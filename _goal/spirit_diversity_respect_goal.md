# Respecting and Leveraging Diversity — Goal

The objective is to convert not only the diversity within humans but also the essential differences between humans and bots from a source of conflict into an augmentation asset, and thereby to create higher collective intelligence and a sustainable AX organization.

## Objectives
- Accurately distinguish the attributes of being different and differing, and establish an organizational culture that does not stratify differences into superior and inferior.
- Use the diversity of humans and of bots respectively (including role diversity) as an asset of mutual complementarity rather than of competition.
- Convert diversity from a 'source of conflict' into an 'augmentation asset' and make it the driving force of creative management and performance enhancement.

## Success Criteria
The objective is judged to be achieved when the differences among organizational members (both humans and bots) are respected without stratification, when those differences actually contribute to improving collective intelligence and enhancing performance, and when on that basis the next spirit, complementary fit, follows naturally.
