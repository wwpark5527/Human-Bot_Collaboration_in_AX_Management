# 1. AI Impact Assessment — Goal

The goal is to assess in advance, before introducing AI, the impact that introduction has on labor, judgment, authority, customers, and human rights, so that the AI Inclusive-Transformation ESG execution model does not remain a declaration of philosophy but works as the first step of an executable operating model.

## Objectives
- Specify the tasks and scope targeted for AI introduction and settle the object of the impact assessment.
- Identify the expected impact in advance in each of the five areas of labor, judgment, authority, customers, and human rights.
- Classify the negative impacts and risks among the identified impacts and derive directions of response.
- Hand the assessment results over as design input to the subsequent steps (building context capital, authority design, labor transition).

## Success Criteria
It is judged successful when the impact of introducing AI on labor, judgment, authority, customers, and human rights is assessed in advance and the results are documented. (ESG linkage: S / G)
