# 6. Appeal Procedure — Goal

The goal is to establish, as an organizational procedure, a structure through which explanation can be demanded, re-review requested, and correction sought regarding AI results, thereby securing the reversibility that allows even AI results that have passed approval to be reversed after the fact. It is the sixth step of the AI Inclusive-Transformation ESG execution model (9 steps).

## Objectives
- Designate the channel through which explanation of AI results can be demanded and the duty to respond.
- Define the scope of objects for which re-review can be requested and the paths of intake.
- Determine the processing method and the processing deadline for the corrections and remedies that follow from the re-review results.
- Ensure that the fact of an appeal and the results of its processing remain as records that can be confirmed at the subsequent step.

## Success Criteria
It is judged successful when a structure for requesting explanation, re-review, and correction of AI results is established, and the appeal procedure and the results of processing the ensuing re-reviews, corrections, and remedies are produced. (ESG linkage: S / G)
