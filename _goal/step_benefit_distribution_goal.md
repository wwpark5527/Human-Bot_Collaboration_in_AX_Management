# 8. Benefit Distribution — Goal

The goal is to reinvest the AI productivity gains into education, compensation, welfare, and organizational capability, and thereby fix as an organizational procedure the path by which the fruits of AI transformation return to the members rather than concentrating in a few. It is the eighth step of the AI Inclusive-Transformation ESG execution model (9 steps).

## Objectives
- Identify the productivity gains that arose from the introduction of AI and calculate their size.
- Determine the mode of distribution and the distribution ratio for each of the four reinvestment targets (education, compensation, welfare, organizational capability).
- Designate the securing of funds and the executing party so that the distribution is actually carried out.
- Calculate the reinvestment record so that it can be confirmed whether the distribution took place as designed.

## Success Criteria
It is judged successful when the AI productivity gains are reinvested into education, compensation, welfare, and organizational capability and the reinvestment record is produced. (ESG linkage: S)
