# 5. Human Approval Criteria — Goal

The goal is to define explicitly the outputs and decisions that require human intervention, and thereby fix the boundary of automation within organizational governance so that no AI output passes through without an approval procedure. It is the fifth step of the AI Inclusive-Transformation ESG execution model (9 steps).

## Objectives
- Enumerate by type the outputs and decisions in which AI is involved.
- Determine which of them necessarily require human intervention and state the grounds for that determination.
- Document the list of tasks requiring human approval and the approval criteria for each item.
- Designate the approving party and the moment of approval so that the procedure actually works.

## Success Criteria
It is judged successful when the outputs and decisions requiring human intervention are explicitly defined and the list of tasks requiring human approval and the approval criteria are documented. (ESG linkage: G)
