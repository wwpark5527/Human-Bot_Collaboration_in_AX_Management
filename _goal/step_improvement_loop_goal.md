# 9. Improvement Loop — Goal

The goal is to reflect the errors confirmed in the course of operation and the members' feedback into the next cycle's common context and governance criteria, and thereby make the AI Inclusive-Transformation ESG execution model not a procedure run once but a circulating loop. It is the last of the 9 steps.

## Objectives
- Gather in one place and categorize the errors that arose in the operation of the preceding eight steps and the feedback collected.
- Determine which element of the common context each error and item of feedback is a problem that must be fixed.
- Determine which point of the governance criteria (authority, approval, appeal, records) each error and item of feedback is a problem that must be fixed.
- Settle the determination results as an actual revision of the criteria and hand them over as the starting condition of the next cycle.

## Success Criteria
It is judged successful when the confirmed errors and feedback are reflected into the next common context and governance criteria and the updated common context and governance criteria are produced. (ESG linkage: G)
