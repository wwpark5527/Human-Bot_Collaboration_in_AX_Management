# 4. Labor Transition — Goal

The goal is to assess before introduction the impact that AI introduction has on people's jobs and to design the reskilling and role reconfiguration that follow from it, so that AI transformation results in the transition of the workforce rather than its exclusion. It is the fourth step of the AI Inclusive-Transformation ESG execution model (9 steps).

## Objectives
- Conduct a job impact assessment **before** the introduction of AI and specify the jobs and personnel that are affected.
- Design the reskilling plan needed for the affected jobs.
- Reconfigure roles in line with the changed composition of work.
- Design the transition placement into the reconfigured roles and confirm the degree of fulfillment by the transition-placement rate.

## Success Criteria
It is judged successful when a job impact assessment is conducted before the introduction of AI and reskilling and role reconfiguration are designed. (ESG linkage: S)
