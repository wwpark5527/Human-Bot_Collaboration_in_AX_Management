# Supplementary Fit — Goal

The goal is to recognize together both the positive function and the limits of supplementary fit (homogeneity-based teamwork), and to lay the foundation for the shift in awareness that expands it into respecting diversity and pursuing complementary fit.

## Objectives
- Acknowledge and maintain the positive function by which teamwork activities based on homogeneity and similarity (unity events, staff dinners, etc.) contribute to departmental and organizational performance.
- Make the organization aware of the limit that supplementary fit alone cannot respect and leverage diversity (difference).
- Share with the members the necessity of expanding the 20th-century awareness centered on supplementary fit into respecting diversity and pursuing complementary fit.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when the organization maintains the positive function of homogeneity-based teamwork while not taking it as the sole ground of organizational performance, and has expanded its awareness into respecting diversity and pursuing complementary fit.
