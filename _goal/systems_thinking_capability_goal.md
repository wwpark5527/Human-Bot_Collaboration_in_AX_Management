# Systems Thinking — Goal

The goal is to understand the AX organization as a complex system in which humans, AI agents, governance, workflow, culture, ESG, security, and context are connected, and to acquire the direction-determining capability to judge the whole integratively beyond partial optimization.

## Objectives
- Recognize the AX organization as a complex system in which humans, AI agents, governance, workflow, culture, ESG, security, and context are connected.
- Do not stay at partial optimization, but integrate value conflicts, ethics, long-term direction, organizational culture, and social ripple effects into a whole to make judgments.
- Possess judgment on AI governance, accountability, trust, and human-centeredness.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged to be achieved when one reaches a determination of direction that integrates value conflicts, ethics, long-term direction, organizational culture, and social ripple effects into a whole rather than partial optimization.
