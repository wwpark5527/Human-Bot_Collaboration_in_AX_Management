# Task-Activation Aspect of Teamwork — Goal

The goal is to measure, by team role balance (TRB), the factor of actual team performance improvement that is not explained by human bonding alone, and to confirm that a department's teamwork and actual performance are maximized when it is combined with the human bonding aspect.

## Objectives
- Measure by TRB the state in which all 9 team roles are easily and well exercised within the team.
- Grasp the state of teamwork on the complementary fit side.
- Confirm the effect on a department's actual performance as evaluated by the company.
- Combine it with the human bonding aspect of teamwork to complete the whole picture of a department's teamwork.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when the degree of task activation is grasped through TRB measurement, when that result is confirmed to have a significant effect on a department's actual performance as evaluated by the company, and when it is combined with the human bonding aspect and actually reflected in management as a short- and mid-term necessary condition.
