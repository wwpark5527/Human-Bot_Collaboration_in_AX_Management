# Team Role Balance (TRB) — Goal

The goal is to build the state in which all 9 Belbin team-role types exist within the team at the natural-role level (TRB), thereby realizing the task-activation aspect of teamwork and improving team performance.

## Objectives
- Grasp the level of team role each team member exercises (natural / latent / non-preferred).
- Confirm from the whole-team perspective whether all 9 team-role types exist at the natural-role level.
- Supplement the missing team roles and build the TRB state.
- Connect this to actual improvement of team performance through the causal chain TRB → Teamwork → Team Performance.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when all 9 team-role types are confirmed to exist within the team at the natural-role level, and when as a result the level of the task-activation aspect of teamwork and team performance actually improve.
