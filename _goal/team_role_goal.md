# Team Role (TR) — Goal

The goal is to identify the team role, the informal behavior type needed for the formation of teamwork that is not explained by the official functional role alone, and thereby to secure the atomic input unit of team role balance (TRB) diagnosis.

## Objectives
- Grasp members' official functional roles and informal team roles as distinct from each other.
- Diagnose team roles accurately on the criterion of behavior type rather than personality type.
- Distinguish, per individual, the levels of team roles that are exercised easily, that are latent, and that cannot be exercised.
- Provide the diagnostic results as the base material for team role balance (TRB) determination.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when the team roles an individual actually exercises well, holds latently, and cannot exercise are accurately diagnosed on the dimension of behavior type, and when they are clearly distinguished from functional roles and can be used for the formation of teamwork and for TRB determination.
