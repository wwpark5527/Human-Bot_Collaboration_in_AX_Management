# Communication Context — Goal

The goal is to clearly define the operating criteria needed when the augmented human relates to the outside (others, counterparts beyond the organization).

## Objectives
- Make the speaker, the scope of AI intervention, the distinction from human judgment, whether recording/analysis takes place, the scope of sharing, and whether human approval was given judgeable before the communication.
- Make it function as the core connecting concept that links the common context (internal human-AI alignment) and the governance context (whole-organization connection).
- Make it explicit within external relationships that the augmented human's utterance is not a simple individual utterance but a composite output combining human judgment and AI contribution.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when all six criteria items (speaker, AI role, distinction of judgment, notice of recording and analysis, scope of sharing, human approval) are confirmed.
