# Human-only TRB — Goal

The goal is to clearly identify and maintain, as the starting stage of the TRB evolution path, the human-centered TRB that deals only with role balance inside a human team (human ↔ human).

## Objectives
- Limit the object of role balance to a human + human team.
- Maintain and confirm the state in which no bot has entered as a role actor.
- Produce human role balance centered on a job-based organization, static roles, and individual capability.

## Success Criteria
Success is judged when the object of role balance is confirmed to be limited to a human + human team with no bot entering as a role actor, and when the human role balance output has the characteristics of a job-based organization, static roles, and individual capability.
