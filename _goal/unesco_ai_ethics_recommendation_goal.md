# UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI (2021) — Goal

The aim is for organizational AX to reference and reflect the four values and ten principles of the UNESCO Recommendation, the most internationally authoritative AI ethics norm, and thereby to have an ethical foundation conforming to international human rights law.

## Objectives
- Reflect the four values, such as respect, protection, and promotion of human rights, fundamental freedoms, and human dignity, in the operation of organizational AX.
- Apply the ten principles, such as proportionality and do no harm, safety and security, and fairness and non-discrimination, to policy and practice.
- Although not binding, put in place a system for aligning the legal system and reporting on implementation in concrete policy action areas such as education, culture, gender equality, and governance.
- Integrate the international human rights law emphasis on resolving global inequality and protecting the environment into the ethical standards of organizational AX.

## Success Criteria
The objective is judged achieved when the ethical system of organizational AX actually reflects the structure of UNESCO's four values and ten principles and has the related policy actions and implementation reporting system in place.
