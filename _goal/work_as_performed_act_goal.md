# Work — Goal

The goal is to identify the act itself of 'having done something' exactly as it is. By making clear that whether that act led to a valid change (contribution) must be judged separately, the goal is not to confuse work with contribution.

## Objectives
- Record without omission the performed act itself.
- Treat it separately from whether that act led to a change.
- Produce the basic material by which 'who worked' and 'who produced what change' can be distinguished.

## Success Criteria
The goal is judged achieved when the fact that some act was performed is clearly identified in separation from that act's result (whether there was a change).
