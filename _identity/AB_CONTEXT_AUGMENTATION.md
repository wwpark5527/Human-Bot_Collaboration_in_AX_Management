# AB_CONTEXT_AUGMENTATION — Context Augmentation

## Concept Definition
Context augmentation is the augmentation dimension of an augmented bot (AB) that understands and uses the organization's purpose, work standards, roles, and materials. An augmented bot is defined as "a collaborative AI that embeds common and governance context together with AI governance and performs its role in line with the organization's purpose and standards," and context augmentation covers the axis of understanding and using organizational context within that. It is the first of an augmented bot's three augmentation dimensions (context, governance, learning).

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the bot understands and uses the organization's purpose, work standards, roles, and materials.

## Output
Work performed in a way that reflects organizational context.
