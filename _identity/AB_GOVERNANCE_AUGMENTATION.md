# AB_GOVERNANCE_AUGMENTATION — Governance Augmentation

## Concept Definition
Governance augmentation is the augmentation dimension of an augmented bot (AB) that operates within structures of authority, verification, approval, recording, and accountability. Whereas an ordinary bot (B) operates in an automation-centered way with weak accountability structures, an augmented bot is distinguished by operating in a collaboration-centered way within a governance structure. It is the second of an augmented bot's three augmentation dimensions (context, governance, learning).

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the bot's operation takes place within structures of authority, verification, approval, recording, and accountability.

## Output
Work results that are approved and recorded within a governance structure.
