# AH_INDICATOR_COGNITIVE — Cognitive Augmentation (Cognitive A.)

## Concept Definition
Cognitive Augmentation (Cognitive A.) is the first of the five AH indicators used to measure the augmented human (AH), a measurement indicator that looks at whether AI broadens the individual's range of thinking (cognition). It emerges from the problem awareness that, once the meaning of an augmented human is defined as "the human's purpose, judgment, and accountability + AI's analysis, generation, and recording capabilities + the standard structure of common context," both the organization and its members want to confirm how much they have actually been augmented. Cognitive augmentation confirms the answer to the question "Does AI broaden my range of thinking (cognition)?" through concrete measures such as the number of alternatives generated, the number of variables considered, and thinking depth / cognitive speed.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether AI broadens my range of thinking (cognition).

## Output
Measures of the number of alternatives generated, the number of variables considered, and thinking depth / cognitive speed.
