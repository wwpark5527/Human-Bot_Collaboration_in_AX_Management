# AH_INDICATOR_COLLABORATION — Collaboration Augmentation (Collaboration A.)

## Concept Definition
Collaboration Augmentation (Collaboration A.) is the fourth of the five AH indicators, a measurement indicator that looks at whether one naturally divides roles with AI. Where cognitive augmentation, decision augmentation, and learning augmentation confirmed range of thinking, decision quality, and learning speed respectively, collaboration augmentation confirms whether a member treats AI not as a separate tool but as a collaboration partner that shares the work. It confirms the answer to the question "Do you naturally divide roles with AI?" through concrete measures such as the proportion of delegable work and the level of agent usage.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether one naturally divides roles with AI.

## Output
Measures of the proportion of delegable work and the level of agent usage.

## Evidence (original quotation)
