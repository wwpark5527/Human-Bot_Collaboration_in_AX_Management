# AH_INDICATOR_LEARNING — Learning Augmentation (Learning A.)

## Concept Definition
Learning Augmentation (Learning A.) is the third of the five AH indicators, a measurement indicator that looks at whether learning speed actually became faster through the use of AI. Where cognitive augmentation and decision augmentation confirmed the expansion of the range of thinking and the improvement of decision quality respectively, learning augmentation confirms how much the very speed at which a member acquires new knowledge and capabilities has improved through the use of AI. It confirms the answer to the question "Did learning speed become faster?" through concrete measures such as reduced learning time and an increased amount of knowledge acquired.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether learning speed became faster.

## Output
Measures of reduced learning time and increased amount of knowledge acquired.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Did learning speed become faster? — Reduced learning time, increased amount of knowledge acquired"
