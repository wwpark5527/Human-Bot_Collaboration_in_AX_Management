# AH_INDICATOR_ROLE — Role Augmentation (Role A.)

## Concept Definition
Role Augmentation (Role A.) is the fifth and last of the five AH indicators, a measurement indicator that looks at whether one has come to perform a new role because of AI. Where the four preceding indicators (cognitive, decision, learning, and collaboration augmentation) each confirmed improvement in thinking, judgment, learning, and collaboration, role augmentation is the final indicator that confirms whether those improvements actually expanded the member's role itself, and from the organizational AX perspective it becomes the final criterion for judging AH. It confirms the answer to the question "Do you perform a new role because of AI?" through concrete cases of role expansion such as researcher → researcher + designer, manager → manager + orchestrator, and professor → professor + AI coach.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether one performs a new role because of AI.

## Output
Role expansions such as researcher → researcher + designer, manager → manager + orchestrator, and professor → professor + AI coach.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Role augmentation — A new role because of AI"
