# AH_MENTAL_AUGMENTATION — Mental Augmentation

## Concept Definition
Mental augmentation is the augmentation dimension that raises thinking, learning, analysis, creativity, and judgment by using AI and technology. It is presented as the second of the three dimensions through which the augmented human (AH) is understood (physical augmentation, mental augmentation, role/context augmentation); whereas physical augmentation deals with physical performance capability, mental augmentation deals with the human's cognitive and judgment capacities. This dimension too is assumed to be already widely understood, so the book goes on to concentrate on the role/context augmentation that appears for the first time thereafter.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the object of augmentation is a mental capacity such as thinking, learning, analysis, creativity, or judgment.

## Output
Heightened thinking, learning, analysis, creativity, and judgment.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Mental augmentation — Raising thinking, learning, analysis, creativity, and judgment by using AI and technology"
