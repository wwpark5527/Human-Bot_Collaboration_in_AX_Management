# AH_PHYSICAL_AUGMENTATION — Physical Augmentation

## Concept Definition
Physical augmentation is the augmentation dimension that complements or extends physical limits or physical performance capability with technology. It is presented as the first of the three dimensions through which the augmented human (AH) is understood (physical augmentation, mental augmentation, role/context augmentation), and refers to the aspect in which technology complements and extends the human's physical bodily abilities. Together with mental augmentation, this dimension is assumed to be already widely understood, so the book goes on to concentrate on the role/context augmentation that appears for the first time thereafter.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the object of augmentation is a human's physical limits or physical performance capability.

## Output
Physical performance capability that has been complemented and extended by technology.

## Evidence (original quotation)
