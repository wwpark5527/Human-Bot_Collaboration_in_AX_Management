# AH_ROLE_CONTEXT_AUGMENTATION — Role/Context Augmentation

## Concept Definition
Role/context augmentation is the augmentation dimension in which one develops one's role, responsibilities, and mode of contribution by collaborating with AI inside an AX organization; it means a state in which AI can be used within the organization's purpose, standards, and accountability system. It is the third of the augmented human's (AH) three augmentation dimensions (physical, mental, role/context), and unlike the first two dimensions it is the one explained in detail for the first time in the book. When this augmentation is achieved, one comes to hold three powers: the power to set purpose, the power to establish standards, and the power to take on judgment and accountability.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether, beyond merely doing work faster, one uses AI within the organization's purpose, standards, and accountability system.

## Output
A developed role, responsibility, and mode of contribution, and the ensuing possession of the three powers (purpose, standards, judgment and accountability).
