# AIU_STAGE_AUGMENTATION_USER — Augmentation User

## Concept Definition
The Augmentation User is the last stage of the four-stage evolution ladder defined in the measurement of AI Utilization (AIU), an AI-usage stage in which AI operates as if it were part of oneself, augmenting one's memory, analysis, and creativity. Going beyond the AI Orchestrator (who uses multiple AIs and agents to design work processes), it is a state that has reached a level where the relationship with AI goes beyond instrumental orchestration and is integrated as part of one's own cognitive functions; a measurement that reflects the characteristics and indicators of the three prior stages (Tool User, Collaborative User, Orchestrator) up to the Augmentation User corresponds to the completion of AI-utilization measurement.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether, after using AI, I have become a different person from my earlier self.

## Output
Indicators of improved decision quality, increased learning speed, and increased productivity.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Augmentation User — Improved decision quality — After using AI, [have I become different from] my earlier self"
