# AI_CAPABILITY_EQUALITY — AI Capability Equality Theory

## Concept Definition
AI capability equality theory holds that inequality in the AI era arises not simply from gaps in access to AI tools but from gaps in the capability to convey a human's purpose, standards, and context to AI and to verify, approve, and improve the results. Therefore an organization must not leave AI-utilization capability to an individual's intuition or to the authority of a particular department; it must convert the context capital, authority system, verification standards, and approval structure that AI can use into a common production base for the whole organization.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the cause of AI-era inequality is diagnosed not as a gap in access to AI tools but as a gap in the capability to convey purpose, standards, and context and to verify, approve, and improve results, and whether this capability is being converted from an individual's intuition or a particular department's authority into a common production base for the whole organization.

## Output
The cause of the AI-utilization capability gap (inequality) is re-diagnosed from tool access to a capability gap, and a practical demand is derived that context capital, the authority system, verification standards, and the approval structure must be converted into a common production base for the whole organization. This becomes the theoretical justification for the socialization of context capital (WalkOrder 323).
