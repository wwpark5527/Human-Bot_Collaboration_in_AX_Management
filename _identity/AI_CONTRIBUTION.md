# AI_CONTRIBUTION — AI Contribution

## Concept Definition
AI contribution is an indicator that expresses AI's real value in the AI era, which matters more than mere output quantity. It is defined as the reduced meaning/cognitive distance divided by the AI-usage cost, and it measures not how many results AI produced but how efficiently it reduced the human's distance to understanding.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by computing the reduced meaning/cognitive distance (the amount of reduction in meaning/cognitive distance) and the AI-usage cost respectively, and taking their ratio.

## Output
The AI contribution value (= reduced meaning/cognitive distance / AI-usage cost).

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "AI contribution = reduced meaning/cognitive distance / AI-usage cost"
