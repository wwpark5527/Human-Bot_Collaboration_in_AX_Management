# AI_FOR_ESG — AI for ESG

## Concept Definition
This is the perspective pointing to the positive effects AI brings as a powerful implementation tool for ESG. In many cases AI brings positive effects to ESG implementation, and it expresses the first of the two perspectives on the AI-ESG relationship, namely the direction in which AI helps ESG.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether AI operates in the direction of bringing positive effects to ESG implementation.

## Output
Acceleration of ESG implementation (ESG results achieved using AI as an implementation tool).

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "The positive effect of AI as a powerful implementation tool for ESG is 'AI for ESG'"
