# AI_GEN_1_PERCEPTIONAL — AI 1.0 (Perceptional AI)

## Concept Definition
This is the first generation in the AI generational-classification table, a stage whose function is defined as 'Perceptional AI'. This table corresponds to the flow in which the information society, deepened by the arrival of the internet, developed through Web 1.0–4.0, and it shows that AI too is developing from AI 1.0 to AI 4.0 (Organizational AI); it is the first row of the AI generational-classification table (types: AI 1.0/AI 2.0/AI 3.0/AI 4.0, functions: Perceptional AI/Generative AI/Agentic AI/Organizational AI). Because it is not a standardized definition, the classification criteria may differ slightly from source to source.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether AI's function remains at perceiving, identifying, and classifying external input. If it has no generative, autonomous-execution (Agentic), or organization-operating (Organizational) function, it belongs to this generation.

## Output
It produces recognition/identification results. It cannot create new content and yields only results at the level of recognizing what is given.
