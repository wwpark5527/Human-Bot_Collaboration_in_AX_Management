# AI_GEN_2_GENERATIVE — AI 2.0 (Generative AI)

## Concept Definition
This is the second generation in the AI generational-classification table, a stage whose function is defined as 'Generative AI'. Corresponding to the development of Web 1.0–4.0, this is the second row of the AI generational-classification table showing that AI is developing from AI 1.0 to AI 4.0 (Organizational AI), and it is the stage following AI 1.0 (Perceptional AI), which remained at perception and identification. Because it is not a standardized definition, the classification criteria may differ from source to source.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether AI goes beyond perception to generate new content (sentences, etc.). However, it does not set goals and execute them on its own.

## Output
It produces generated output (sentences, images, etc.). In the text this generative ability is described as accompanied by the limitations of hallucination and security leakage.
