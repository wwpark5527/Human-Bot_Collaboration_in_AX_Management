# AI_GEN_4_ORGANIZATIONAL — AI 4.0 (Organizational AI)

## Concept Definition
This is the fourth and currently ongoing generation in the AI generational-classification table, a stage whose function is defined as 'Organizational AI'. It is the target domain of this book. Unlike AI 3.0 (Agentic AI), which remains at the individual-agent unit, this generation requires AI's operating unit to expand to the operation of the whole organization, and only when it forms and implements common context and governance context to become not a mere AI platform but an AI-native organizational operating system does it reach the 'true AI 4.0 level'.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether AI's operating unit has expanded from the individual agent to the operation of the whole organization. The text holds that the 'true AI 4.0 level' is reached only when common context and governance context are formed and implemented so as to become not a mere AI platform but an AI-native organizational operating system.

## Output
It produces organization-scale operational redesign results. Whether it is reached becomes the judgment that separates the 'true AI 4.0 level'.
