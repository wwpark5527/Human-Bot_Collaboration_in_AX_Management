# AI_GOVERNANCE — AI Governance

## Concept Definition
AI governance is the norms, institutions, and operating system concerning "how to control, manage, and supervise AI," and it is a concept at a different level from governance context, which is the context system that provides "on what basis AI should judge and act." AI governance (A) creates the order of AI use, whereas governance context (B) is the operating context AI must reference when acting within the organization. By analogy, A is like laws/a constitution — prohibition rules, accountability rules, an audit system, an approval system — while B is a collective context like social culture, custom, and situational knowledge. A is the system that actually makes B operate within real organizational operation as work procedures, roles, systems, and recording structures; its core is not prohibition but operation, not surveillance but standardization, not after-the-fact audit but connected operation.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether those norms, institutions, and operating system address the order of AI use (a system of prohibition, accountability, audit, and approval), and whether they actually make the judgment-standard context called governance context operate as work procedures, roles, systems, and recording structures.

## Output
An operational order that lets AI work verifiably and accountably within the organization — a system that implements governance context as work procedures, roles, systems, and recording structures.
