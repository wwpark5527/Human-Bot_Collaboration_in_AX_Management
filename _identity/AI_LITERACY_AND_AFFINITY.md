# AI_LITERACY_AND_AFFINITY — AI Literacy and Affinity

## Concept Definition
This is the most basic survival competency of understanding AI — not mere use, but understanding AI's strengths and weaknesses, hallucination, prompt use, AI workflow, and generative AI. It is presented as the first of the six required competencies (AI literacy and affinity, human-AI collaboration ability, continuous learning ability, data/digital literacy, critical thinking, organizational-change adaptability) put forward by the section 'A necessary condition for AX-organization talent: the competencies of humans who survive.'

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether one is a person who, rather than fearing AI, handles it well as a collaboration tool.

## Output
It produces the state of surviving in an AX organization as a person who handles AI as a collaboration tool.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "AI literacy and affinity: The most basic survival competency of the AI era is precisely AI literacy and affinity. This is not mere use but requires understanding AI's strengths and weaknesses, understanding hallucination, using prompts, understanding AI workflow, understanding generative AI, and so on. At minimum, only a person who does not fear AI and handles it well as a collaboration tool survives."
