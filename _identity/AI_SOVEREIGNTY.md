# AI_SOVEREIGNTY — AI Sovereignty (Sovereignty)

## Concept Definition
This is a core principle of the information-security necessary conditions for an organizational-AX OS, meaning that while using powerful external LLMs (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.), the organization's data and decision sovereignty is not lost. GP is a representative case that technically demonstrated this principle: with its 'perceptron-tree-based zero-knowledge proof (ZKP) method' technology it was granted EIP-7911, a new-technology patent in the blockchain field, and it also obtained a technology patent from the Korean Intellectual Property Office with the same technology.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether it has a technical basis (e.g., ZKP-based patented technology) that substantially blocks the possibility of internal information (data/context) flowing out through a connection with an external LLM.

## Output
It produces a result in which the powerful generation and reasoning ability of external AI is used while the organization's data and decision sovereignty is preserved, and this can be demonstrated with patented technology as in the GP case.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "The core of an organizational-AX OS is 'to use external AI while not losing the organization's sovereignty.'"
