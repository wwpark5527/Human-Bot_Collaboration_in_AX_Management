# AI_TRANSFORMATION — AX (AI Transformation)

## Concept Definition
AX (AI Transformation) is not a replacement term for DX (Digital Transformation) but an extended concept that became necessary after DX as AI moved into the center of organizational operation. Where DX focused on the digitization, datafication, cloud migration, and process-efficiency improvement of work and systems, AX is the transformation that integrates AI into the organization's workflows, judgment systems, role structures, output systems, authority structures, and accountability structures. Entering 2026, AX emerged as a hot topic following DX; internationally, expressions such as AI transformation, AI-driven enterprise transformation, AI operating model, and AI-enabled workflow transformation are used more broadly than the abbreviation AX. It is the master transformation paradigm around which this whole book is organized.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether an organizational change goes as far as involving AI in the organization's judgment, generation, execution, evaluation, and accountability structures. If it stops at the DX-style question "Can this work be digitized?" it is not AX; if it addresses "What can AI judge and generate in this work?", "How will the roles of AI and humans be divided?", "Who will verify and approve AI results?", and "How will authority, accountability, and profit-sharing arising from AI be designed?", it is judged to be AX.

## Output
It produces a state in which AI is integrated into the center of organizational operation and the human's role and accountability structure are newly designed. Currently, efficiency, productivity, cost reduction, growth, and work optimization are produced as the mainstream purposes, while inclusive transformation, prevention of AI stratification, profit-sharing, the right to object, and the human right of judgment remain still-insufficient areas.
