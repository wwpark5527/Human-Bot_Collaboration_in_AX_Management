# ALGORITHMIC_MANAGEMENT — Algorithmic Management

## Concept Definition
Algorithmic management refers to an algorithmic system that uses tracking data and other information to organize, assign, monitor, supervise, and evaluate work. In this structure, even without a manager judging directly, AI can influence workers' work assignment, performance evaluation, surveillance, discipline, and reward.

## Decision Criteria
Whether the organizing, assigning, monitoring, supervising, and evaluating of work is done by an algorithmic system that uses tracking data and information.

## Output
A management structure in which, even without a manager judging directly, AI influences workers' work assignment, performance evaluation, surveillance, discipline, and reward. It is the structure underlying the five questions ESG must ask in the AI era (Who evaluates others with AI? / Who is evaluated by AI? / Are AI evaluation criteria explained? / Can humans object? / Who is the ultimate responsible party?).
