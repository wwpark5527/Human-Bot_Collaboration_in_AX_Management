# ARBI — Augmented Role Balance Index (ARBI, Augmented Role Balance Index)

## Concept Definition
ARBI (Augmented Role Balance Index) is an index that, when AI intervenes in collaboration and communication between humans, evaluates how healthily the roles of humans and AI are divided, complemented, verified, and operated accountably. Unlike the existing TRB, which looks at how roles are arranged within a human team, ARBI looks at role balance in the era in which AI has entered collaboration. It is the central indicator of Chapter 6, and a single candidate confirmed as KEEP without SplitSet fragmentation.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether, in AI-intervened human-human collaboration, it measures role balance, utterance agency, authority boundaries, human accountability, communication fairness, and recordability together.

## Output
Evaluation results on the division, complementation, verification, and accountability of the roles of humans and AI (the ARBI index and its sub-measures) are produced.
