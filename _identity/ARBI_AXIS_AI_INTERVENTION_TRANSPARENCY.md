# ARBI_AXIS_AI_INTERVENTION_TRANSPARENCY — AI Intervention Transparency

## Concept Definition
AI intervention transparency is the third of the ten evaluation axes that make up ARBI (the Augmented Role Balance Index), an evaluation axis that looks at whether it is revealed where and how AI intervened. It is an element confirmed through the SplitSet fragmentation of ARBI's ten evaluation axes (S2C-0098, `ARBI_TEN_AXES`); ARBI does not judge AI use itself as good or bad but takes whether that intervention is revealed as the starting point of evaluation.

## Decision Criteria
Was it revealed where and how AI intervened?

## Output
A judgment result on whether the points of AI intervention are exposed is produced, revealing the risk of concealed AI intervention.

## Evidence (original quotation)
