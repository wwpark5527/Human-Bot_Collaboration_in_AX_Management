# ARBI_AXIS_COMMUNICATION_FAIRNESS — Communication Fairness

## Concept Definition
Communication Fairness is the seventh of the ten evaluation axes that make up ARBI (the Augmented Role-Balance Index); it is the axis that examines whether an AI-augmentation gap has turned into a power difference within the relationship. It is an element settled through the SplitSet division of ARBI's ten evaluation axes (S2C-0098, `ARBI_TEN_AXES`); where the Human Accountability axis asks "does final responsibility remain with the human," this axis asks, as the next step, "has a power difference arisen between those who wield AI better and those who do not."

## Decision Criteria
Has an AI-augmentation gap turned into a relational power difference?

## Output
It produces a judgment on the fairness of the AI-augmentation gap and exposes the risk of communication asymmetry.
