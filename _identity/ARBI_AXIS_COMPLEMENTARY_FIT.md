# ARBI_AXIS_COMPLEMENTARY_FIT — Complementary Fit

## Concept Definition
Complementary Fit is the second of the ten evaluation axes that make up ARBI (the Augmented Role-Balance Index); it is the axis that examines whether human strengths and AI strengths complement each other. It is an element settled through the SplitSet division of ARBI's ten evaluation axes (S2C-0098, `ARBI_TEN_AXES`); where the Role Balance axis asks "have the needed roles been assigned," this axis examines whether the strength relationships among those assigned roles complement one another.

## Decision Criteria
Do human strengths and AI strengths complement each other?

## Output
It produces a judgment on whether human and AI strengths complement each other.
