# ARBI_AXIS_HUMAN_ACCOUNTABILITY — Human Accountability

## Concept Definition
Human Accountability is the sixth of the ten evaluation axes that make up ARBI (the Augmented Role-Balance Index); it is the axis that examines whether final judgment, approval, and responsibility remain with the human. It is an element settled through the SplitSet division of ARBI's ten evaluation axes (S2C-0098, `ARBI_TEN_AXES`); where the Authority & Consent Boundary axis asks "was the AI's access legitimate," this axis asks, as the next step, "does final responsibility still lie with the human."

## Decision Criteria
Do final judgment, approval, and responsibility remain with the human?

## Output
It produces a judgment on whether human responsibility remains and exposes the risk of unclear accountability.
