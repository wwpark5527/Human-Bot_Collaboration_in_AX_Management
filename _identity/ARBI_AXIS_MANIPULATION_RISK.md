# ARBI_AXIS_MANIPULATION_RISK — Manipulation Risk

## Concept Definition
Manipulation Risk is the tenth and final of the ten evaluation axes that make up ARBI (the Augmented Role-Balance Index); it is the axis that examines whether AI was used not for collaboration but for strategic advantage or coercion. It is an element settled through the SplitSet division of ARBI's ten evaluation axes (S2C-0098, `ARBI_TEN_AXES`); where the Psychological & Trust Stability axis looks at the burden members feel, this axis asks about the deliberate misuse that creates that burden — that is, whether AI was used as a strategic weapon that undermines collaboration.

## Decision Criteria
Was AI used not for collaboration but for strategic advantage or coercion?

## Output
It produces a judgment on whether AI was misused (for strategic advantage or coercion).
