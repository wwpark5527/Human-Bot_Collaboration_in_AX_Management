# ARBI_AXIS_PSYCHOLOGICAL_TRUST_STABILITY — Psychological & Trust Stability

## Concept Definition
Psychological & Trust Stability is the ninth of the ten evaluation axes that make up ARBI (the Augmented Role-Balance Index); it is the axis that examines whether feelings of surveillance, alienation, decision fatigue, and burden of responsibility are excessive. It is an element settled through the SplitSet division of ARBI's ten evaluation axes (S2C-0098, `ARBI_TEN_AXES`); where the Record & Traceability axis looks at the outward re-verifiability of the collaboration history, this axis asks about the psychological burden on the members who actually live through that collaboration process.

## Decision Criteria
Are feelings of surveillance, alienation, decision fatigue, and burden of responsibility not excessive?

## Output
It produces a judgment on the level of psychological and trust burden on members.
