# ARBI_AXIS_RECORD_TRACEABILITY — Record & Traceability

## Concept Definition
Record & Traceability is the eighth of the ten evaluation axes that make up ARBI (the Augmented Role-Balance Index); it is the axis that examines whether a history of rationale, revisions, approvals, and materials used has been retained. It is an element settled through the SplitSet division of ARBI's ten evaluation axes (S2C-0098, `ARBI_TEN_AXES`); where the Communication Fairness axis looks at whether a gap transfers into power, this axis asks whether the collaboration process itself remains in a form that can be re-verified after the fact.

## Decision Criteria
Has a history of rationale, revisions, approvals, and materials used been retained?

## Output
It produces a judgment on whether the collaboration history is recorded and re-verifiable.
