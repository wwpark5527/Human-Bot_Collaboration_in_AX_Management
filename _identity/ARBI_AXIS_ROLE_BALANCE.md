# ARBI_AXIS_ROLE_BALANCE — Role Balance

## Concept Definition
Role Balance is the first of the ten evaluation axes that make up ARBI (the Augmented Role-Balance Index); it is the axis that examines whether the roles needed in a collaboration have been assigned to fit the situation. It is an element settled through the SplitSet division of ARBI's ten evaluation axes (S2C-0098, `ARBI_TEN_AXES`), and it takes as its object of evaluation a collaboration network in which multiple humans and multiple AIs participate together.

## Decision Criteria
Have the needed roles been assigned to fit the situation?

## Output
It produces a judgment on the adequacy of role assignment and exposes the risk of role imbalance.
