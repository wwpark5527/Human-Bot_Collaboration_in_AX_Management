# ARBI_AXIS_UTTERANCE_AGENCY — Utterance Agency

## Concept Definition
Utterance Agency is the fourth of the ten evaluation axes that make up ARBI (the Augmented Role-Balance Index); it is the axis that examines whether the agent of judgment and utterance is the human, the AI, or a joint output of human and AI. It is an element settled through the SplitSet division of ARBI's ten evaluation axes (S2C-0098, `ARBI_TEN_AXES`); where AI Intervention Transparency asks "was the intervention disclosed," this axis asks "whose" the utterance and judgment produced by that disclosed intervention are.

## Decision Criteria
Who is the agent of this judgment and utterance?

## Output
It produces a judgment on the attribution of the agent of an utterance or judgment.
