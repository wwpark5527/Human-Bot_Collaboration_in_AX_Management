# AUGMENTATION — Augmentation

## Concept Definition
Augmentation is not about replacing human shortcomings with bots; it is a process of mutual learning and co-evolution in which humans and bots coexist, grow each other's capabilities, and produce results together. The key is to design bots not as substitutes for human labor but as amplifiers of human capability, so that human augmentation (physical and mental) and bot augmentation (context, value, learning) happen together.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether the bot is designed as a substitute for the human or as an amplifier of human capability. The criterion is whether one pursues a positive-sum in which both sides grow, rather than a zero-sum in which one side's loss is the other's gain.

## Output
It forms an augmented human (AH) and an augmented bot (AB), and by creating the perception that "AI strengthens me" it raises receptivity toward organizational AX. Humans achieve physical and mental augmentation (cognition, decision-making, creativity, roles), while bots achieve augmentation of context, value, and learning.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Augmentation is not about replacing human shortcomings with bots; it is a process of mutual learning and co-evolution in which humans and bots coexist, grow each other's capabilities, and produce results together."

> "Because the spirit of augmentation aims not at removing humans but at extending them, only when the concept of augmentation exists does 'human-centrism' become possible."
