# AUGMENTED_PATH_AI_CENTERED — AI-Centered Path

## Concept Definition
The AI-Centered Path is an augmented-human-to-augmented-human (AH-AH) communication path in which the AI relays and structures messages while the human provides only the purpose and the approval criteria. Its structure is 'Human A ↔ AI A ↔ AI B ↔ Human B'. It is the second of the three augmented-human communication paths; the AI handles communication quickly in the middle, which is efficient, but the human's original intent can change during the AI's summarizing and restructuring.
