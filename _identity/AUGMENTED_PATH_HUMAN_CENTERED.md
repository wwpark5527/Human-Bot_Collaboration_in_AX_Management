# AUGMENTED_PATH_HUMAN_CENTERED — Human-Centered Path

## Concept Definition
The Human-Centered Path is an augmented-human-to-augmented-human (AH-AH) communication path in which the human speaks directly and judges directly, while the AI helps in the background with organizing, analyzing, reviewing, and drafting. Its structure is 'AI A ↔ Human A ↔ Human B ↔ AI B'. It is the first of the three augmented-human communication paths (human-centered, AI-centered, via-governance), and it best preserves human meaning, emotion, and trust relationships.

## Decision Criteria
Is the agent of utterance and judgment the human themselves? Are human meaning, emotion, and trust relationships preserved? In exchange, speed and level of automation are low.

## Output
Meaning preservation. 'The human-centered path is … effective for meaning preservation.'
