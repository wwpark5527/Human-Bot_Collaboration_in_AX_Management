# AXSTRESS_CONTROL — Control Stress

## Concept Definition
This is one of the four dangerous stresses that arise when it is unclear who actually holds control. It is the third type among the 'AX organizational stresses (four dangers: identity, trust, control, responsibility)'. Unlike trust stress, which is a matter of belief in the other party, control stress originates from ambiguity about the very locus of decision-making authority.

## Decision Criteria
Is the core question 'who actually controls?'

## Output
It requires members to redefine the scope of their own control, and, if left unresolved, leads to governance collapse.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "control (control: who actually controls?)"
