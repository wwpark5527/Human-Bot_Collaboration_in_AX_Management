# AXSTRESS_IDENTITY — Identity Stress

## Concept Definition
This is one of the four dangerous stresses that arise when members of an AX organization cannot confirm their reason for existence. It is the first type among the 'AX organizational stresses (four dangers: identity, trust, control, responsibility)', and it is distinguished from other ordinary stress in that it is not mere work overload but a stress tied to the members' identity itself.

## Decision Criteria
Is the core question 'why am I needed?'

## Output
It produces a demand that members redefine their own role and value, and, if left unresolved, leads to a crisis of existential value.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "identity (identity: why am I needed?)"
