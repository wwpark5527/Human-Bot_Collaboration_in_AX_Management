# AXSTRESS_RESPONSIBILITY — Responsibility Stress

## Concept Definition
This is one of the four dangerous stresses that arise when the attribution of responsibility for results is unclear. It is the fourth and final type among the 'AX organizational stresses (four dangers: identity, trust, control, responsibility)'. Where control stress concerns the locus of decision-making authority, responsibility stress originates from the problem of attributing responsibility for the results of that authority.

## Decision Criteria
Is the core question 'who takes responsibility?'

## Output
It requires members to redefine the scope of their own responsibility, and, if left unresolved, leads to responsibility avoidance and declining performance.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "It is responsibility (responsibility: who takes responsibility?) stress."
