# AXSTRESS_TRUST — Trust Stress

## Concept Definition
This is one of the four dangerous stresses that arise when one cannot be sure whether humans and bots can trust each other. It is the second type among the 'AX organizational stresses (four dangers: identity, trust, control, responsibility)'. Unlike identity stress, which is a question about members' own reason for existence, trust stress originates from the relationship (interaction) between humans and bots.

## Decision Criteria
Is the core question 'can I trust the other party?'

## Output
It requires the design of trust-based cooperation, and, if left unresolved, leads to a collapse of human-AI trust.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "trust (trust: can I trust the other party?)"
