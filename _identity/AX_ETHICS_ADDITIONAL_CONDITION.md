# AX_ETHICS_ADDITIONAL_CONDITION — Additional Conditions of Organizational AX

## Concept Definition
The additional conditions of organizational AX are ethical standards whose absence does not make AX legally or socially problematic or impossible, but which greatly raise organizational competitiveness over the mid-to-long term. Unlike the necessary conditions, they do not lead to immediate failure, yet they are the factors that let an organization not merely survive by properly executing organizational AX but become a successful company; most of what this book emphasizes overall falls into this category.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether, even when unmet, it causes no legal or social problem or AX impossibility yet still contributes to mid-to-long-term competitiveness.

## Output
Enhanced organizational competitiveness through an augmentation-centered philosophy, respect for diversity, complementary fit between humans and bots, building a common context, and building a governance context.
