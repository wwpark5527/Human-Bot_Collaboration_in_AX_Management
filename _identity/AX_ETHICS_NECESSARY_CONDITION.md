# AX_ETHICS_NECESSARY_CONDITION — Ethical Necessary Conditions of Organizational AX

## Concept Definition
The ethical necessary conditions of organizational AX are ethical standards that must be followed; if they are not, organizational AX heads toward failure. Not all principles and norms mentioned in the process of realizing organizational AX carry the same importance, and among them some are necessary conditions whose violation drives organizational AX to failure. Ethical standards mentioned by nations or international bodies mostly correspond to these necessary conditions.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether organizational AX heads toward failure when the given standard is not followed.

## Output
The mandatory compliance line: guaranteeing human dignity, humans' ultimate accountability, explainability, protection of personal and organizational information, prevention of bias and discrimination, safety and reliability, and building an AI governance system (AI governance corresponds to the operating constitution of organizational AX).

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "The standards that must be followed as ethical necessary conditions of organizational AX include guaranteeing human dignity, humans' ultimate accountability, explainability, protection of personal and organizational information, prevention of bias and discrimination, safety and reliability, and building an AI governance system."
