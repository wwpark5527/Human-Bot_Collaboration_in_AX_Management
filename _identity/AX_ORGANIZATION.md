# AX_ORGANIZATION — AX Organization

## Concept Definition
An AX organization is an organization that has become AX-transformed as a result of completing the process called organizational AX. It is the term for the case where the unit of AX application is the "organization" and the phase of change is the "result," and it is the core concept pointed to by the title of Chapter 1 of this book ("An AX Organization Where Humans and Bots Coexist and Cooperate").

## Decision Criteria
It is judged to be an AX organization when the unit of AX application is the organization and the phase of change is a completed result rather than an ongoing process. If the organizational operating system (decision-making structure, communication structure, power structure, cooperation structure) is in the "process" of being redesigned around AI, that is organizational AX; when that redesign is realized as a result and reaches a state where humans and bots coexist and cooperate, it is judged to be an AX organization.

## Output
It produces the result state of an organization in which humans and bots coexist and cooperate. As the completed result of organizational AX (the process), its output is the final state of an organization in which technological innovation, organizational innovation, cultural innovation, and governance innovation have been achieved simultaneously.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "In the end, organizational AX is the process by which an organization undergoes AX, and an AX organization means the organization that has resultantly become AX-transformed."
