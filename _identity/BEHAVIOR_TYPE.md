# BEHAVIOR_TYPE — Behavior Type vs Personality Type

## Concept Definition
A team role (TR) is not an MBTI-style personality type but a behavior type actually exhibited by a person. Personality substantially influences an individual's behavior, but adults do not act solely according to their personality, and a member's actual behavior within an organization can vary with learned individual experience and effort. In the twentieth century there was no concept of behavior type, so it was indirectly estimated through personality type; now, because both the concept of behavior type and the means to measure it exist, direct diagnosis is possible.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether what is measured is the individual's actual pattern of exhibited behavior (behavior type) or an indirect estimate of personality traits (personality type). The criterion is whether one accurately diagnoses and uses which behavior types each individual actually exhibits well, might exhibit, or fails to exhibit when working with others.

## Output
A methodology that dramatically increases team performance through behavior-type-based organizational management is derived. This distinction applies not only to humans; it becomes the grounds (footnote 44) for extending it in Chapter 6 even to bots, for which there is no reason to consider personality type at all.

## Evidence (original quotation)
