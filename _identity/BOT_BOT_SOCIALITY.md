# BOT_BOT_SOCIALITY — Bot-bot sociality

## Concept Definition
Bot-bot sociality is sociality as an optimized interaction system — grounded in game-theoretic, strategic interaction, acting on rewards, with reputation at play and hierarchy forming. The nature of the relationship is symmetrical (peer- or function-based), and its basis is efficiency, reward, and optimization. Being entirely computational, without emotion or empathy, sociality between bots appears as strategy itself.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the relationship is symmetrical (peer- or function-based) and whether cooperation strategies form on a basis of efficiency, reward, and optimization.

## Output
Cooperation strategies and reputation, a naturally forming planner/executor/verifier-style hierarchy; and, on failure, the internal risk of system collapse.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Its essence is a game-theoretic, strategic interaction system: it acts on a reward basis (cooperation increases reward, betrayal incurs a penalty), reputation is at play whereby past actions affect the future, and a planner/executor/verifier-style hierarchy naturally forms."
