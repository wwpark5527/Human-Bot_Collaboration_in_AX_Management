# BOT_DESIRE_HIERARCHY — Formation of the bot's desire hierarchy

## Concept Definition
The formation of the bot's desire hierarchy is the concept that although there is no real desire inside a bot, designing goals and rewards as a layered structure makes it operate as if it had desire. It is offered as the answer to the third basic question humans have about bots ("Does a bot have desire? If so, is it stratified into higher/lower desires?").

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether higher goals and lower goals (or meta-rewards and sub-rewards) are designed as a layered structure.

## Output
Behavior that appears to have a desire hierarchy (under the higher goal of maximizing user satisfaction, the sub-goals of accuracy, speed, and safety).

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Is the formation of a desire hierarchy possible? By design, yes. There is no real desire inside the AI, but if goals and rewards are designed as a layered structure, it operates as if it had desire."
