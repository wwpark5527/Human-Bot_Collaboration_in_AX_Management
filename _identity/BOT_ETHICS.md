# BOT_ETHICS — The ethics of bots

## Concept Definition
Despite its heading, the ethics of bots refers to ethics that must be observed not by bots alone but by every member of an AX organization. It encompasses both external ethical standards set by nations and international bodies and standards that private companies choose for themselves, and it is divided into ethical necessary conditions — which, if not met, lead to failure in realizing organizational AX — and additional conditions for good AX.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether, even under the nominal heading of "the ethics of bots," the actual scope of application extends to every member of an AX organization (humans and bots), and whether the external ethical standards referenced are treated as divided into necessary conditions and additional conditions.

## Output
A system of ethical standards that organizational AX must observe — ethical necessary conditions (guaranteeing human dignity, ultimate human accountability, explainability, protection of personal and organizational information, prevention of bias and discrimination, safety and reliability, building an AI governance system) and additional conditions (an augmentation-centered philosophy, respect for diversity, complementary fit between humans and bots, building a common context, building a governance context).

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Although the title is 'the ethics of bots,' this is something every member of an AX organization must observe."
