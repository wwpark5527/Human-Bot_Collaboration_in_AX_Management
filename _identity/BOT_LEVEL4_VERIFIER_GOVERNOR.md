# BOT_LEVEL4_VERIFIER_GOVERNOR — Level 4 Verifier/Governor

## Concept Definition
Level 4 Verifier/Governor is the role level at the top of the 4-tier hierarchy that spontaneously forms among bots. It performs the functions of verifying results, judging whether something is factual (true), and controlling policy. Following the principle that a bot with verification power stands above a generative agent in the hierarchy, "the one who verifies" occupies the top of the hierarchy.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether it verifies results, judges factuality, and controls policy.

## Output
Factual (truth) judgments and policy-control results.
