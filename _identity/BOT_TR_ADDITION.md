# BOT_TR_ADDITION — Addition of TR by bots

## Concept Definition
A mode in which, as the AI bot takes on roles, entirely new roles that did not exist in a human-centered organization come into being. It is the third mode, following complementation and augmentation of TR by bots; rather than replacement or reinforcement, it refers to the emergence of wholly new roles that did not exist in the organization.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether it is a new role that did not exist in a human-centered organization.

## Output
Produces the list of new AX roles: AI Governor, AI Auditor, Prompt Architect, AI Workflow Orchestrator, Human Meaning Integrator, Trust Manager, and Provenance Controller.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Addition of TR by bots: In an AX organization, as the AI bot takes on roles, the following new roles that did not exist in a human-centered organization sometimes come into being as additions."
