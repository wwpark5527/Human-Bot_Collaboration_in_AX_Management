# BOT_TR_AUGMENTATION — Augmentation of TR by bots

## Concept Definition
A mode in which the bot uses its own strengths to augment (reinforce) an existing human TR. It is the second mode, following complementation of TR by bots; across four TRs — Monitor-Evaluator (ME), Completer-Finisher (CF), Resource Investigator (RI), and Coordinator (CO) — it backs up human performance with concrete reinforcements.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the reinforcement the bot provides for a given TR, and the resulting effect, can be identified.

## Output
Produces the reinforcement content and effects per role. Monitor-Evaluator (ME): reduced human intuition bias; Completer-Finisher (CF): reduced human fatigue; Resource Investigator (RI): expanded scope of external exploration; Coordinator (CO): reduced organizational mediation cost.
