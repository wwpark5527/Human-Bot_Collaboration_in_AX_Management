# BOT_TR_COMPLEMENTATION — Complementation of TR by bots

## Concept Definition
A mode that translates the meaning of Belbin TR into an AX context, so that humans and bots mutually complement one another with their different strengths. The bot does not replace the human but complements them.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the bot replaces or complements the existing TR. It is complementation when the human takes on vision-setting, oversight, meaning-giving, ethics, integrative judgment, conflict mediation, trust-building, and accountability, while the bot takes on computation, memory, exploration, repetition, pattern recognition, and real-time optimization.

## Output
Produces the AX-organization reinterpretation table for the 9 TRs (Plant PL → AI-enabled innovation designer, etc.) and an expansion of the overall performance pie size.
