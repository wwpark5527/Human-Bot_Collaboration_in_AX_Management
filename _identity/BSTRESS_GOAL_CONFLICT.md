# BSTRESS_GOAL_CONFLICT — Goal Conflict

## Concept Definition
Bot stress arising when different goals are demanded simultaneously in an agentic AI. It is the second of the 'bot stress types (5 types)', positioned after computational overload, and refers to the functional tension a bot experiences between mutually conflicting goals.

## Decision Criteria
Whether different goals collide, such as speed vs. accuracy, profitability vs. ethics, or user satisfaction vs. governance compliance.

## Output
It exhibits oscillation, repetitive loops, and inconsistent behavior, similar to human role conflict.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Goal Conflict: In an agentic AI, different goals (e.g., speed vs. accuracy, profitability vs. ethics, user satisfaction vs. governance compliance) can collide."
