# BSTRESS_MULTI_AGENT_COOP — Multi-agent cooperation stress

## Concept Definition
Bot stress arising during the process of cooperation among agents in a multi-agent system. It is the fifth and last of the 'bot stress types (5 types)', positioned after continuous-update stress, and refers to the coordination tension experienced when multiple agents cooperate.

## Decision Criteria
Whether coordination failure, communication overload, or authority ambiguity occurs.

## Output
The problems of 'Which agent has priority? Who decides when different judgments conflict?' arise, closely resembling team conflict in a human-centered organization.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Multi-agent cooperation stress: In a multi-agent system, 'coordination failure, communication overload, and authority ambiguity' occur."
