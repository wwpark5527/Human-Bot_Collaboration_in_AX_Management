# CC_BROADLY_ETHICAL — Broadly Ethical

## Concept Definition
Broadly ethical is a core value of the Claude Constitution that disciplines an AI model to make ethical judgments on its own. As one of the four core values of the Claude Constitution revision released in January 2026, it is part of the top-level behavioral guidance that—together with broadly safe, helpfulness, and compliance with guidelines—disciplines the AI to make ethical and safe judgments on its own. Rather than a mere prohibitive rule, it aims at ethical judgment grounded in the reason why a behavior should be taken.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the model's behavior conforms to the broadly ethical requirement.

## Output
Ethical judgment grounded in the reason why the behavior should be taken.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "The four core values are broadly safe, broadly ethical, helpfulness, and compliance with guidelines."
