# CC_BROADLY_SAFE — Broadly Safe

## Concept Definition
Broadly safe is a core value of the Claude Constitution that disciplines an AI model so that its judgments and behaviors are broadly safe. As one of the four core values of the Claude Constitution revision released in January 2026, it is part of the top-level behavioral guidance that—together with broadly ethical, helpfulness, and compliance with guidelines—disciplines the AI to make ethical and safe judgments on its own.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the model's behavior stays within the broadly safe requirement.

## Output
Judgment that avoids unsafe behavior on its own.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "The four core values are broadly safe, broadly ethical, helpfulness, and compliance with guidelines."
