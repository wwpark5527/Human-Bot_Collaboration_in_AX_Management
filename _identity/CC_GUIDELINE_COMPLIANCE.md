# CC_GUIDELINE_COMPLIANCE — Compliance With Guidelines

## Concept Definition
Compliance with guidelines is a core value of the Claude Constitution that disciplines an AI model to follow the instructions it is given. As one of the four core values of the Claude Constitution revision released in January 2026, it is part of the top-level behavioral guidance that—together with broadly safe, broadly ethical, and helpfulness—disciplines the AI to make ethical and safe judgments on its own.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the model's behavior complies with the guidelines presented.

## Output
Behavior that conforms to the instructions.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "The four core values are broadly safe, broadly ethical, helpfulness, and compliance with guidelines."
