# CC_HELPFULNESS — Helpfulness

## Concept Definition
Helpfulness is a core value of the Claude Constitution that disciplines an AI model to produce genuinely helpful results. As one of the four core values of the Claude Constitution revision released in January 2026, it is part of the top-level behavioral guidance that—together with broadly safe, broadly ethical, and compliance with guidelines—disciplines the AI to make ethical and safe judgments on its own.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the model's response is substantively useful to the user.

## Output
Helpful assistance and responses.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "The four core values are broadly safe, broadly ethical, helpfulness, and compliance with guidelines."
