# COLLAB_MODE_AI_IN_THE_LOOP — AI-in-the-loop (human-led + AI assist)

## Concept Definition
A mode of collaboration in which the human leads and the AI augments in real time; it is the second of the four types in the human-AI collaboration typology.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the initiative lies with the human while the AI comes 'inside' the loop to assist. It is distinguished from HITL, which intervenes at the approval point, in that this mode provides real-time augmentation during the course of the work.

## Output
Produces real-time augmentation results for human-led work. By analogy to graduate researchers, it corresponds to a doctoral candidate.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Human-approval-centered         Human-led + AI assist             AI-autonomous + human oversight"
