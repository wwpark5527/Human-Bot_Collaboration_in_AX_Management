# COLLAB_MODE_AUTONOMOUS_AI — Autonomous AI (AI-autonomous execution)

## Concept Definition
A mode of collaboration in which the AI executes autonomously and human intervention is minimized; it is the last of the four types in the human-AI collaboration typology (HITL / AI-in-the-loop / HOTL / Autonomous AI) with the least human intervention.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether human intervention is minimal. It is distinguished from Human-on-the-loop in that even the human-as-overseer effectively drops out, corresponding to Sheridan's (1992) HOOTL (human-out-of-the-loop, human non-intervention).

## Output
Produces the results of AI-autonomous execution without human intervention. By analogy to graduate researchers, it corresponds to a researcher who has become a professor.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "AI-autonomous execution (minimal human intervention)"
