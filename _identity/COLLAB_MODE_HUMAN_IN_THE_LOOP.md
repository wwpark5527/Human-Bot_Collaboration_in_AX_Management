# COLLAB_MODE_HUMAN_IN_THE_LOOP — Human-in-the-loop (human-approval-centered)

## Concept Definition
A mode of collaboration in which the AI proposes and the human gives final approval; it is the first of the four types in the human-AI collaboration typology (HITL / AI-in-the-loop / HOTL / Autonomous AI) with the strongest direct human participation.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the final approval authority lies with the human. The human is 'inside' the loop, and every result must pass the human's approval.

## Output
Only results approved by the human are produced. By analogy to graduate researchers, it corresponds to a master's candidate.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Human-approval-centered         Human-led + AI assist             AI-autonomous + human oversight"
