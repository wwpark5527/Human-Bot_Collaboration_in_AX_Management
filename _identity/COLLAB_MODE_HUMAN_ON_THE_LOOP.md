# COLLAB_MODE_HUMAN_ON_THE_LOOP — Human-on-the-loop (AI-autonomous + human oversight)

## Concept Definition
A mode of collaboration in which the AI performs most of the work and the human monitors and intervenes; it is the third of the four types in the human-AI collaboration typology and represents human oversight.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether execution has passed to the AI and the human's position is that of an overseer 'on' the loop. It is distinguished from Human-in-the-loop in that oversight is after-the-fact and continuous rather than prior approval.

## Output
Produces the results the AI has performed and the human's monitoring and intervention judgments on them. By analogy to graduate researchers, it corresponds to a postdoctoral researcher.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "HOTL (human-on-the-loop) and HOOTL (human-out-of-the-loop) represent, respectively, 'direct human participation, human oversight, and human non-intervention.'"
