# COL_LABOR_UNION — Labor-union-type resistance

## Concept Definition
The most traditional form of resistance, in which individual anxiety is collectivized through a labor union. As the first of the 'collective human reaction/resistance types (5 types),' it corresponds to the first stage in which the individual human reaction/resistance types (survival-anxiety, competence-inferiority, loss-of-control, surveillance-fear, humanity-defense) develop into organized social, political, and institutional reactions.

## Decision Criteria
Whether it bears the features of demanding limits on automation, mandating human employment, restricting the scope of AI use, and requiring mandatory retraining.

## Output
Its core claims are prohibiting the unauthorized training on human creations, restricting AI replacement, and protecting digital-reproduction rights; it gave rise to the first large-scale 'AX labor-rights struggle' of the AX era.
