# COL_ONTOLOGICAL — Ontological resistance

## Concept Definition
The most powerful collective resistance, arriving at the question "Why are humans needed when AI does it better?" As the fifth and last of the 'collective human reaction/resistance types (5 types),' and unlike the preceding four types (labor-union, professional-group, sociocultural, political-policy resistance) that each start from concrete issues in the dimensions of employment, expertise, culture, and institutions, ontological resistance arises from a fundamental question about the meaning of human existence itself.

## Decision Criteria
Whether the ground of the resistance is a question about the meaning of human existence itself rather than jobs or institutions.

## Output
It can lead to cynicism, organizational disengagement, an anti-technology identity, human supremacism, and AI hatred; this is not mere technological resistance but a 'defense of the meaning of human existence.'

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Ontological resistance: the most powerful resistance, ultimately arriving at the question "Why are humans needed when AI does it better?""
