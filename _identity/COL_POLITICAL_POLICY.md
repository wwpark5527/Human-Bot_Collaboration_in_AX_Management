# COL_POLITICAL_POLICY — Political-policy resistance

## Concept Definition
Collective resistance at the political-policy level that seeks to control AI through regulation and institutions. As the fourth of the 'collective human reaction/resistance types (5 types),' it is distinguished in that, unlike sociocultural resistance which appears through cultural discourse and identity, political-policy resistance appears as demands for formal control through legislative and regulatory bodies.

## Decision Criteria
Whether it bears the features of strengthening AI regulation, emphasizing data sovereignty, restricting AI use, and securing human control.

## Output
It gives rise to the U.S. debates over AI regulation laws (AI transparency obligations, copyright regulation, AI safety verification, deepfake regulation) and the European EU AI Act (restrictions on high-risk AI, human-oversight obligations, explainability requirements, limits on biometric surveillance), which become the foundation of the 'human-centered AI' philosophy.

## Evidence (original quotation)
