# COL_SOCIOCULTURAL — Sociocultural resistance

## Concept Definition
Collective resistance at the cultural level, appearing in two sub-types: 'formation of an anti-AI culture' and 'distrust of the technological elite.' As the third of the 'collective human reaction/resistance types (5 types),' unlike labor-union-type and professional-group resistance which appear through organized subjects—the union and the profession respectively—sociocultural resistance is formed not through a specific organization but through the medium of cultural identity and distrust of the technological elite.

## Decision Criteria
Whether it is human-first-ism, the Slow AI movement, or an emphasis on Human-made, or the perception that "a few big tech firms control human society."

## Output
It gives rise to a movement of 'AI-free art, human-written, and human-creation certification,' has the potential to develop in a direction similar to the organic-food movement, and leads to critiques of AI monopoly, data colonialism, and algorithmic power.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Sociocultural resistance: here there are two sub-types, 'formation of an anti-AI culture' and 'distrust of the technological elite.'"
