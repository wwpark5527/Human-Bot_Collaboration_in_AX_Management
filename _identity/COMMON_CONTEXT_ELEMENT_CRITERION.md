# COMMON_CONTEXT_ELEMENT_CRITERION — Criterion

## Concept Definition
The criterion is the organization's standard of judgment for what counts as a good result. It is a component of the common context that makes AI share the organization's purpose, language, and standards of judgment. It is the second of the six elements (purpose, criterion, role, source, format, feedback) settled by the SplitSet fragmentation of `common context` (S2C-0103, `COMMON_CONTEXT`).

## Decision Criteria
Whether the same standard of judgment is applied to the same request every time, rather than answering by a different standard each time (preventing criterion inconsistency).

## Output
A unified standard of judgment. 'Unifying criteria: making AI share the organization's purpose, language, and standards of judgment.'
