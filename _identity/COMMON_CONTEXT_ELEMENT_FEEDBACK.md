# COMMON_CONTEXT_ELEMENT_FEEDBACK — Feedback

## Concept Definition
Feedback is the component that feeds the experience of errors and failures back into the next common context. It makes the common context an updated standard rather than a fixed document. It is the sixth and last of the six elements (purpose, criterion, role, source, format, feedback) settled by the SplitSet fragmentation of `common context` (S2C-0103, `COMMON_CONTEXT`).

## Decision Criteria
Whether the errors and outcomes that occurred are actually reflected in the next standard.

## Output
The formation of organizational learning. The updated next version of the common context.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Forming organizational learning: reflecting errors and feedback into the next common context."
