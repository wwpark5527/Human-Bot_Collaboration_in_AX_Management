# COMMON_CONTEXT_ELEMENT_ROLE — Role

## Concept Definition
Role is the component of the common context that determines who does what. It captures within the common context the arrangement of human roles running from problem definer, context composer, AI execution director, result interpreter, verifier, responsibility judge, evidence manager, to improvement reflector. It is the third of the six elements (purpose, criterion, role, source, format, feedback) settled by the SplitSet fragmentation of `common context` (S2C-0103, `COMMON_CONTEXT`).

## Decision Criteria
Whether 'who must confirm what' is not unclear. Whether the subject who will interpret, verify, approve, record, and improve the result is designated.

## Output
The arrangement of responsibility by role. Clarification of the responsibility structure that lets one judge how far AI results may be used.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "The common context turns the local environment into a local working context so that humans and AI can work according to the same purpose, criterion, role, source, and format."
