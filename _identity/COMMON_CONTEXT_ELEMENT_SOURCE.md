# COMMON_CONTEXT_ELEMENT_SOURCE — Source

## Concept Definition
Source is the component of the common context that makes it possible to confirm what materials AI relied on as grounds and whether they have been verified. It prevents source confusion in which unverified information is stated as if it were fact. It is the fourth of the six elements (purpose, criterion, role, source, format, feedback) settled by the SplitSet fragmentation of `common context` (S2C-0103, `COMMON_CONTEXT`).

## Decision Criteria
Whether the source, standards, revisions, and approval process can be confirmed after the fact. Whether the accurate source and the original-text confirmation process are built into the system.

## Output
Verifiability. Outputs whose grounds and citations can be traced.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Securing verifiability: making the source, standards, revisions, and approval process confirmable."
