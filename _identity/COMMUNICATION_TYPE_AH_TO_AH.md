# COMMUNICATION_TYPE_AH_TO_AH — AH-AH communication

## Concept Definition
Communication between an augmented human and an augmented human. With the structure 'A human + A AI + A common context ↔ B human + B AI + B common context,' it becomes an interaction between two 'human–AI composite units.'

## Decision Criteria
Symmetry is judged not by whether one holds AI, but by the level of augmentation power (AI performance, quality of the common context, accessible materials, execution authority, human judgment, level of governance, and the recording/verification system).

## Output
The coordination process of meaning, conditions, judgment, and responsibility, together with the occurrence of contribution conflicts and the three communication paths.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "The relationship between an augmented human and an augmented human has the following structure."
