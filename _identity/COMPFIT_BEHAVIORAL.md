# COMPFIT_BEHAVIORAL — Behavioral complementation

## Concept Definition
Behavioral complementation is the third of the four types of complementary fit, a complementation type in which humans have strengths in responding to non-routine and exceptional situations while bots have strengths in repetitive, routine behavior, so they divide and fill the behavioral domain. Under the spirit of complementary fit that pursues 'augmentation, not replacement,' it is embodied by assigning standardized repetitive work to bots and exceptional, non-routine responses to humans. For example, it appears as a division of roles in which the bot performs the routine workflow and the human performs crisis handling.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the object of complementation is a division of behavioral types—non-routine and exceptional situations versus repetitive, routine behavior.

## Output
It produces a division in which the bot performs the routine workflow and the human performs crisis handling.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "In the case of behavioral complementation, humans have strengths in responding to non-routine and exceptional situations while bots have strengths in repetitive, routine behavior. For example, the bot performs the routine workflow and the human performs crisis handling."
