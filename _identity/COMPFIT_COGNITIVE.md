# COMPFIT_COGNITIVE — Cognitive complementation

## Concept Definition
Cognitive complementation is one of the four types of complementary fit in an AX organization—cognitive, emotional, behavioral, and ethical complementation—a complementation type in which humans focus on intuition, creativity, and imagination while bots focus on computation, search, and memory, filling each other's cognitive functions. Under the spirit of complementary fit that pursues 'augmentation, not replacement,' it is a way of embodying the combination of 'human judgment + bot analysis.' For example, it appears as a division of roles in which, in a strategy meeting, the bot provides data simulation and the human interprets its strategic meaning.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the object of complementation is a division of cognitive functions—intuition, creativity, and imagination versus computation, search, and memory.

## Output
It produces a division in which, in a strategy meeting, the bot provides data simulation and the human interprets the strategic meaning.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "In the case of cognitive complementation, humans focus on intuition, creativity, and imagination while bots focus on computation, search, and memory; for example, in a strategy meeting the bot provides data simulation and the human focuses on interpreting the strategic meaning."
