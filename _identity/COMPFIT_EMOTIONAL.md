# COMPFIT_EMOTIONAL — Emotional complementation

## Concept Definition
Emotional complementation is the second of the four types of complementary fit, a complementation type in which humans focus on providing empathy, trust, and psychological stability while bots focus on immediate response, fatigue-free support, and continuous feedback, filling each other's emotional functions. It is a way of embodying the combination of 'human empathy + bot information processing,' and like cognitive complementation it aims at augmentation, not replacement. For example, it appears as a division of roles in which an AI coach provides 24-hour support and a human leader provides psychological safety.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the object of complementation is a division of emotional-support capabilities—empathy, trust, and psychological stability versus immediacy, freedom from fatigue, and continuity.

## Output
It produces a division in which an AI coach provides 24-hour support and a human leader provides psychological safety.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "In the case of emotional complementation, humans focus on providing empathy, trust, and psychological stability while bots focus on immediate response, fatigue-free support, and continuous feedback; for example, an AI coach provides 24-hour support and a human leader provides psychological safety."
