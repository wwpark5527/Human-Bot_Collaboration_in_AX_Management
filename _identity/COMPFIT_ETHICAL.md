# COMPFIT_ETHICAL — Ethical complementation

## Concept Definition
Ethical complementation is the fourth and last of the four types of complementary fit, a complementation type in which bots focus on rule-based consistency while humans focus on value judgment, social responsibility, and context-based adjustment, filling the ethical domain. A bot can consistently follow given rules, but it is hard for it to judge on its own whether the rules themselves are legitimate. Therefore the final authority to judge the legitimacy of the rules remains with the human, which leads to the concept of the spirit (靈) = final judgment authority that humans must keep, addressed later under 'preventing panic.'

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the object of complementation is the consistent observance of rules versus the judgment of the legitimacy of the rules themselves.

## Output
Since bots follow rules but find it hard to judge the legitimacy of the rules themselves, it produces the conclusion that judging the legitimacy of the rules remains with the human.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "In the case of ethical complementation, bots focus on rule-based consistency while humans focus on value judgment, social responsibility, and context-based adjustment. This is because a bot follows rules but finds it hard to judge the legitimacy of the rules themselves."
