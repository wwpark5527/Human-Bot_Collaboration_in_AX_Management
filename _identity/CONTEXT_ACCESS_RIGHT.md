# CONTEXT_ACCESS_RIGHT — Context Capital Access Right (Context Access Right)

## Concept Definition
The context access right is a concept revealing that stratification in the AI era arises not only from access to models but from access to context capital. Here, access right does not mean an AI account or a software usage license, but access to the organization's purpose, standards, materials, and approval structure (context capital) that make it possible to use AI meaningfully. When an organization's context capital is monopolized by a few, the ability to use AI becomes subordinate to an individual's sense, language ability, information access, rank, and departmental authority, giving rise to a new stratification.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether members can, beyond an AI account or usage license, actually access the organization's purpose, standards, materials, and approval structure (context capital) needed to use AI meaningfully.

## Output
When the context access right is monopolized by a few, an AI-use gap (stratification) arises according to an individual's sense, language ability, information access, rank, and departmental authority; when context capital is converted into a common production base for the whole organization, the ability to use AI becomes a common organizational capability rather than an individual privilege.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Therefore stratification in the AI era arises not only from access to models but from the context access right."
