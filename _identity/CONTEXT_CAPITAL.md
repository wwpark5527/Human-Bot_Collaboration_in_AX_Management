# CONTEXT_CAPITAL — Context Capital

## Concept Definition
Context capital is the accumulated operating asset of the purpose, (judgment) standards, language, materials, format, verification standards, and approval standards needed to make AI work properly. In the AI era, money, data, models, and compute are not the only capital. The organizational context that can be given to AI is also capital, and the power to direct AI comes not from mere prompt technique but from the ability to design what purpose to give, by what standards to have it judge, what materials to have it use, and what results to approve.

## Decision Criteria
Whether the purpose, (judgment) standards, language, materials, format, verification standards, and approval standards for making AI work exist as the organization's accumulated operating asset.

## Output
The source of the power to direct AI — the accumulation of the ability to design purpose, standards, materials, and approval. When it is converted into a common production base for the whole organization, the ability to use AI becomes a common organizational capability rather than an individual privilege.
