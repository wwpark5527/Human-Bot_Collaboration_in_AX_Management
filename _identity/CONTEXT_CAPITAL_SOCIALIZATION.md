# CONTEXT_CAPITAL_SOCIALIZATION — Socialization of Context Capital

## Concept Definition
The socialization of context capital means preventing the ability to use AI from being monopolized by a few individuals or a particular department, and converting the organizational context that can be given to AI (purpose, standards, language, materials, format, verification standards, approval standards) into a common production base for the whole organization. Together with the establishment of a responsible operating system, it is one of the two axes of the inclusive-transition management philosophy that prevents and mitigates the problem of AI stratification.

## Decision Criteria
Whether the ability to use AI and context capital (purpose, standards, language, materials, format, verification standards, approval standards) are not monopolized by a few individuals or a particular department, but are converted into a common production base for the whole organization.

## Output
A state in which the ability to use AI is a common organizational capability rather than an individual privilege. The realization of the inclusive-transition management philosophy that, combined with a responsible operating system, prevents and mitigates the problem of AI stratification.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "The socialization of context capital prevents the ability to use AI from being monopolized by a few individuals or a particular department, and the responsible operating system controls the organizational inequality of AI authority and responsibility."
