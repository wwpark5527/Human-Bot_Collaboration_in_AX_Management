# CONTEXT_DESIGNER — Context Designer (context-design-type AX talent)

## Concept Definition
The context designer is the person (organization) who forms the necessary conditions (purpose and standards) for the first LLM to work effectively within the organization, and further is the agent who forms the second LLM. Among the seven gaps that create AI-based stratification, the context designer is the actor (ROLE) who practically resolves the AI context gap that the author regards as most important (who can give the organization's purpose and standards to AI), and in the labor-market differentiation table it is named the 'context-design-type AX talent.' It is an entity that does not merely use AI but designs purpose, standards, verification, and feedback to convert them into the organization's operating structure.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the person forms the conditions (purpose and standards) needed for the first LLM (the organization's AI) to work effectively, and further whether they take part in forming the second LLM. Whether, in the labor-market classification, the person is a 'context-design-type AX talent'—whether they do not merely use AI but design purpose, standards, verification, and feedback to convert them into the organization's operating structure—also becomes a decision criterion.

## Output
The necessary conditions (purpose and standards) for the first LLM to work within the organization, and further the basis for forming the second LLM. A state in which AI has been converted from mere use into the organization's operating structure.
