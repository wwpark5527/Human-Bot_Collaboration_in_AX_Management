# CONTINUOUS_LEARNING_ABILITY — Continuous learning ability

## Concept Definition
In an AX environment where technologies, tools, and ways of working keep evolving, this is the ability to relearn faster than what one already knows. It is the third of the six required competencies presented in the section "Necessary conditions for AX-organization talent: the competencies of humans who survive," and it rests on the premise that whereas in the past expertise learned once lasted 10–20 years, in the AX era that shelf life has collapsed.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether one relearns faster than the pace of change and thus stays continuously updated.

## Output
Produces the maintenance of one's ability as a continuously updated human.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Continuous learning ability: The AX environment changes very fast. In the past, expertise learned once lasted 10–20 years, but in the AX era technologies, tools, and ways of working keep evolving, so one needs the ability, as a human, to relearn faster than what one already knows—that is, to stay continuously updated."
