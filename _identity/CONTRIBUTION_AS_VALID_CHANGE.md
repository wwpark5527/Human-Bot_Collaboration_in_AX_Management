# CONTRIBUTION_AS_VALID_CHANGE — Contribution

## Concept Definition
It does not mean that work was done; it is the valid change that the act produced—a valid operation that changes the state of meaning, judgment, verification, approval, record, execution, and responsibility within the communication process. It is the second of the three concepts—work, contribution, role—redefined by AI-era role theory, and it is the layer that bridges work (act) and role (structure).

## Decision Criteria
Whether the act changed the other party's understanding, enabled judgment, was left as a record, and led to the next action.

## Output
The changed state of meaning, judgment, verification, approval, record, execution, and responsibility.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Therefore, contribution is a valid operation that changes the state of meaning, judgment, verification, approval, record, execution, and responsibility within the communication process. In the AI era, 'who made what change' becomes more important than 'who did the work.'"
