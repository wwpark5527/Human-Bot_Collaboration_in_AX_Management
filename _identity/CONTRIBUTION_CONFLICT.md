# CONTRIBUTION_CONFLICT — Contribution conflict

## Concept Definition
Contribution conflict is a conflict that arises in communication between augmented human and augmented human (AH-AH) when multiple humans and AIs assign different criteria, interpretations, verification states, and responsibility judgments to the same message. For example, what AI A summarizes may differ from what AI B interprets; A's common-context criteria may differ from B's common-context criteria; A may see it as verified while B sees it as unverified; and A may see it as shareable while B sees it as a security risk.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the criteria, interpretation, verification state, and responsibility judgment differ among participants for the same message.

## Output
The identification of contribution conflict — the definition of the conflict point that becomes important in AH-AH communication, superseding the role vacancy.
