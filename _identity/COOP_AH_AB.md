# COOP_AH_AB — AH + AB type

## Concept Definition
The core cooperation type of the AX organization, in which both human and bot are augmented in a horizontal, jointly optimizing relationship. It is the last of the four cooperation types in the section "Stress by cooperation type in the AX organization," the final stage reached from H+B (vertical, tool-type) through H+AH and AH+B, and it is distinguished from the preceding three types in that both the human (AH) and the bot (AB) are augmented in a horizontal relationship.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether both human and bot are augmented, forming a horizontal, jointly optimizing relationship rather than a vertical one.

## Output
AH experiences orchestration overload, judgment fatigue, concentration of responsibility, and weakened sense of reality; AB experiences context inconsistency, alignment stress, autonomy ambiguity, and continuous update overload; and an AI orchestration dashboard, decision hierarchy, role-constrained autonomy, and explainability layer are proposed. The core solution is building a common context.
