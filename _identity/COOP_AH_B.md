# COOP_AH_B — AH + B type

## Concept Definition
A cooperation type in which an augmented human (AH), who actively leverages AI, coexists with a bot (B) that performs work independently. It is the third of the four cooperation types in the section "Stress by cooperation type in the AX organization." Unlike the H+AH type (human versus human, an augmentation gap), it now moves to a stage where the bot (B) performs work independently.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether an augmented human (AH) and a non-augmented bot (B) coexist and the bot performs work independently.

## Output
AH experiences loss of control, incapacitation of competence, concentration of responsibility, and fear of surveillance; B experiences lack of context, governance conflict, inconsistency with human instructions, and lack of trust; and clarifying final approval authority and building a governance system are proposed. Success and failure at this stage diverge greatly depending on the design of the "human-bot role balance (TRB)."

## Evidence (original quotation)
