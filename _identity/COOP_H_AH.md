# COOP_H_AH — H + AH type

## Concept Definition
A type in which an ordinary human and an augmented human cooperate. It is the second of the four cooperation types in the section "Stress by cooperation type in the AX organization." Unlike the H+B type (human-led, bot-assisted), it addresses the stress that arises in a structure where the cooperating parties are human versus human but an augmentation-level gap exists between them.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the cooperating parties are a human (H) and an augmented human (AH)—human versus human but with an augmentation-level gap present.

## Output
H experiences relative inferiority, alienation, and unfair evaluation; AH experiences continuous high-performance pressure, AI dependency, isolation in human relationships, and identity confusion; and emphasizing complementary roles instead of comparison, monitoring AI dependency, establishing a human-orchestrator identity, and organizationally banning human-only evaluation are proposed.

## Evidence (original quotation)
