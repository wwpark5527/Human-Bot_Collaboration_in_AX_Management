# COOP_H_B — H + B type

## Concept Definition
A vertical-relationship cooperation type in which the human is the dominant subject and the bot assists as a tool or automation executor. It is the first type in the table of the four cooperation types (H+B/H+AH/AH+B/AH+AB) presented in the section "Stress by cooperation type in the AX organization," followed by a table summarizing the stresses that human (H) and bot (B) each experience in a human-led, bot-assisted structure and the ways to overcome them.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the relationship between human and bot is vertical and the bot occupies the position of a tool or automation executor.

## Output
H experiences loss of control, role erosion, imbalance of responsibility, and learning pressure; B experiences lack of context, overload from exceptional situations, and inconsistency in human feedback; and as ways to overcome these, adopting explainable AI, maintaining human-in-the-loop, building a common context, and an exception escalation system are proposed.
