# COOP_TYPE_AH_PLUS_AB — AH + AB (a true AX organization)

## Concept Definition
An organization type in which both human and bot are augmented and cooperate—the most desirable of the human-bot coexistence types (H+B, H+AH, AH+B, AH+AB). Here the AB is not a mere tool but a being that is already augmented and jointly makes judgments with the human.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether both human and bot are augmented. Whether the bot is an execution tool or a joint-judgment subject is the decisive point distinguishing it from AH + B.

## Output
Produces the "human-bot coexistence and cooperation organization" that brings about human development—that is, a true AX organization (hybrid organization). The way to realize it is proper use of an organizational AX OS and the realization of HBRM.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Among the types in which human and bot coexist, the most desirable is the 'AH + AB type,' and this type is precisely the 'human-bot coexistence and cooperation organization' that will bring about human development—a 'true AX organization.'"
