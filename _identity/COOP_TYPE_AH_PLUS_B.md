# COOP_TYPE_AH_PLUS_B — AH + B (intelligent division of labor)

## Concept Definition
A structure in which an augmented human (AH) orchestrates multiple bots (B)—a cooperation type where the human formulates strategy and the bots execute. It corresponds to the third stage among the four types of human-bot coexistence organizations (H+B, H+AH, AH+B, AH+AB), referring to an intelligent division-of-labor structure in which, with only the human augmented, one directs many bots.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether, in a state where the human is augmented but the bots are not, one AH orchestrates many Bs. The key distinguishing point is whether the human-bot relationship is divided into strategy (human) and execution (bots).

## Output
Produces a strategy-execution division-of-labor structure, and requires building a multi-agent environment, human-in-the-loop design, and role-based agent design as means of realization.
