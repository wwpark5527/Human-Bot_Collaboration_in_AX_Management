# COOP_TYPE_H_PLUS_AH — H + AH (augmented-human-centered cooperation)

## Concept Definition
The second row of the cooperation-type table for human-bot coexistence organizations, referring to a structure in which existing humans and augmented humans work together. It is the type in which the stratification of AI competence occurs and role reorganization begins.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the composition axis is human versus human, but only some of them are augmented. The key distinguishing point is not the bot's status but the augmentation gap within the human side.

## Output
Produces the side effect of AI-competence stratification, and requires AI democratization, building an AI collaboration culture, and role redesign as means of realization to address it.
