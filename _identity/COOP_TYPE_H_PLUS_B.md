# COOP_TYPE_H_PLUS_B — H + B (tool-type cooperation)

## Concept Definition
The first row of the cooperation-type table for human-bot coexistence organizations, referring to the early AX stage of human-led + bot-assisted. It is the type in which the bot is a productivity-enhancement tool and responsibility is concentrated on the human.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the member composition is a (non-augmented) human and a bot, and whether the bot remains in a tool position. It is distinguished from H + AH and AH + B in that the human is not augmented.

## Output
Produces the means of realization of disseminating personal-productivity AI, prompt-literacy education, and repetitive-task automation, resulting only in improved personal productivity.
