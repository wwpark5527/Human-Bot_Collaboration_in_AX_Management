# CORE_ROLE_COORDINATOR — Coordinator

## Concept Definition
A core role that connects people, AI, tools, deliverables, schedules, and approval flows. It is the fifth and last of the five core roles of AX-organization talent. On top of the continuously operating state created by the operator, its core contribution is connecting different actors and flows so that the whole work continues without interruption.

## Decision Criteria
Whether people, AI, tools, deliverables, and approval flows are connected to one another.
