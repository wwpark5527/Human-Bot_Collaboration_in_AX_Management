# CORE_ROLE_OPERATOR — Operator

## Concept Definition
A core role that performs deployment, monitoring, and maintenance to keep deliverables operating continuously. It is the fourth of the five core roles of AX-organization talent. Its core contribution is ensuring that a deliverable whose reliability the validator secured does not stop at a one-time result but keeps operating continuously.

## Decision Criteria
Whether the deliverable does not stop at a one-time result but keeps operating continuously.

## Output
Continuity — the continuous operation of the deliverable.
