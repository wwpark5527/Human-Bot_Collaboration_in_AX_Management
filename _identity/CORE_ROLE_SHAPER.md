# CORE_ROLE_SHAPER — Shaper/Designer

## Concept Definition
A core role that performs problem definition, requirement organization, and structural design to turn an ambiguous problem into a clear one. It is the first of the five core roles that reorganize the eight roles of AX-organization talent from the "contribution" perspective. Its core contribution is making a problem clear by defining what to build, why it should be built, and what structure is appropriate.

## Decision Criteria
