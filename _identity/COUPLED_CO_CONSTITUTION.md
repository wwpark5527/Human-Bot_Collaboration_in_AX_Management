# COUPLED_CO_CONSTITUTION — Co-constitution of roles (Co-constitution)

## Concept Definition
A feature in which human and bot combine their respective, different contributions to jointly constitute a single role. It is the first of the three core features of the Human-Bot Coupled TRB (human-bot coupled TRB, a horizontal relationship) structure — co-constitution of roles, dynamic role allocation, and maximization of human-bot complementary fit.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the human's contribution and the bot's contribution combine so that the role itself is hybridized.

## Output
Produces a single strategic-shaper role that combines the human's vision-setting with the bot's real-time strategy simulation.
