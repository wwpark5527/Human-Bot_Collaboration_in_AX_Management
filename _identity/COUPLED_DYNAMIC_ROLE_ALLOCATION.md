# COUPLED_DYNAMIC_ROLE_ALLOCATION — Dynamic role allocation (Dynamic Role Allocation)

## Concept Definition
A feature in which the bot analyzes the team's state in real time and reallocates roles by redistributing them. It is the second of the three core features of the Human-Bot Coupled TRB (human-bot coupled TRB, a horizontal relationship) structure — co-constitution of roles, dynamic role allocation, and maximization of human-bot complementary fit.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether workload, cognitive load, stress, and expertise availability are analyzed in real time to reallocate roles.

## Output
Produces role-reallocation results grounded in real-time analysis.
