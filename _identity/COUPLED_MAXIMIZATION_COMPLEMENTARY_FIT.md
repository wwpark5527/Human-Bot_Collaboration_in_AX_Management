# COUPLED_MAXIMIZATION_COMPLEMENTARY_FIT — Maximization of human-bot complementary fit (Maximization of Complementary Fit)

## Concept Definition
A feature that combines human strengths and bot strengths to maximize complementary fit. The source's "기치판단" is a typo for "가치판단" (value judgment). It is the third and last of the three core features of the Human-Bot Coupled TRB (human-bot coupled TRB, a horizontal relationship) structure — co-constitution of roles, dynamic role allocation, and maximization of human-bot complementary fit.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether human strengths (empathy, value judgment, context understanding, ethics, creative meaning-making) and bot strengths (speed, computation, memory, search, pattern analysis) are combined.

## Output
Produces a new role capability.
