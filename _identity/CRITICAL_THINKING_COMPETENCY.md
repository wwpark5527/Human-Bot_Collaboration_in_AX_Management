# CRITICAL_THINKING_COMPETENCY — Critical thinking

## Concept Definition
The ability to verify, search for counterexamples, check logic, and evaluate reliability in order to counter the highly plausible errors AI produces. It is the fifth of the six required competencies presented in the section "Necessary conditions for AX-organization talent: the competencies of humans who survive," and it rests on the premise that in an AX organization, someone who verifies AI well is more important than someone who trusts AI well.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether one is a person who trusts AI well or a person who verifies AI well.

## Output
Produces a verified judgment that has filtered out the plausible errors in AI results.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Critical thinking: AI produces highly plausible errors. Therefore, the ability to verify, search for counterexamples, check logic, and evaluate reliability is essential. In an AX organization, because someone who verifies AI well matters more than someone who trusts AI well, critical thinking is needed."
