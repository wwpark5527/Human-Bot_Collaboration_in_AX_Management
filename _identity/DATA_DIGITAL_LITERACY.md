# DATA_DIGITAL_LITERACY — Data and digital literacy

## Concept Definition
Data and digital fluency, including the ability to interpret data, understand digital workflows, and verify AI results. It is the fourth of the six required competencies presented in the section "Necessary conditions for AX-organization talent: the competencies of humans who survive," a basic survival strategy required on the premise that in the AX era most work moves to a data-, AI-, and digital-based foundation.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether one can interpret and verify work that has moved to a data-based, AI-based, and digital-based foundation.

## Output
Produces the securing of data and digital literacy that functions as a basic survival strategy.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Data and digital literacy: In the AX era, most work moves to a data-based, AI-based, and digital-based foundation. Therefore, data and digital literacy (data & digital fluency)—such as the ability to interpret data, understand digital workflows, and verify AI results—becomes a basic survival strategy."
