# DYNAMIC_ROLE_BALANCE — Dynamic role balance

## Concept Definition
A concept indicating that the static Belbin model of the existing human-centered organization can, in the AI era, shift into dynamic role balance as situational role switching, real-time readjustment, and AI-based role reallocation become possible. It corresponds to the organization-design unit moving from "static roles" to "dynamic roles," and in the source's comparison table the static roles of the human-centered organization are contrasted with the dynamic roles of the AX organization.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether role switching, real-time readjustment, and AI-based role reallocation occur according to the situation (crisis, innovation, operations, etc.).

## Output
Produces situationally reallocated dynamic role compositions—such as strengthening the shaper (SH) and activating an AI risk agent in a crisis situation, strengthening the creator (PL) plus Creative AI in an innovation situation, and strengthening the implementer (IMP) plus a workflow bot in an operations situation—ultimately transforming the organization into a living role ecosystem.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "In the existing human-centered organization, the Belbin model was static. But in the AI era, 'situational role switching, real-time readjustment, and AI-based role reallocation' become possible, so it can shift into dynamic role balance."
