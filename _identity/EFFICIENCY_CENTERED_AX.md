# EFFICIENCY_CENTERED_AX — Efficiency-centered AX

## Concept Definition
Efficiency-centered AX is an AI-transformation approach that can raise a company's productivity and competitiveness, but when AI transformation is designed solely on the criterion of efficiency, risks arise such as the reduction of human roles, the widening of AI-competence gaps, algorithmic management, the weakening of judgment authority, the absence of the right to object, and performance concentration. It is the AX approach that takes only productivity, cost reduction, and speed as its core aims, in contrast to inclusive-transformation AX.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether, when AI transformation is designed solely on the criterion of efficiency, the following risks arise: reduction of human roles, widening of AI-competence gaps, abuse of algorithmic management, weakening of judgment authority, absence of the right to object, and performance concentration.

## Output
"A more efficient organization through AI." However, an organizational state that carries the risk of deepening AI stratification — an organization in which ROI, cost reduction, and increased workload become the performance criteria and the connection to ESG is limited.
