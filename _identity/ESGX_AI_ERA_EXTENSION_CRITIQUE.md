# ESGX_AI_ERA_EXTENSION_CRITIQUE — ESG AI-era extension theory

## Concept Definition
A critique type holding that although the existing ESG is strong at reflecting the responsibility system of industrial-era companies, new problems emerge in the AI era, so the ESG concept itself must be extended. It is the third of the three critique types classified by ESG extension theory, and it is precisely the position taken by this book.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the existing ESG captures the new problems that emerged in the AI era (AI stratification, algorithmic management, human judgment authority, etc.).

## Output
An extended ESG that broadens the scope of corporate responsibility to include the AI operating environment—that is, AI inclusive-transformation ESG.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "ESG AI-era extension theory: This is precisely the position of this book—that the existing ESG is not only strong at reflecting the responsibility system of industrial-era companies, but because new problems emerge in the AI era, the ESG concept must be extended."
