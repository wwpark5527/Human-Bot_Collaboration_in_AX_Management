# ESGX_MEASUREMENT_VERIFICATION_CRITIQUE — Measurement-verification-strengthening theory

## Concept Definition
A critique type that points to the prevalence of disguised environmentalism such as greenwashing and demands the strengthening of ESG measurement and verification systems. It points out that, without unified criteria, scores varied from one rating agency to another, and that ESG was abused as a means for companies to disclose only favorable data and package themselves as eco-friendly. It is the second of the three critique types classified by ESG extension theory.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether, without unified criteria, scores vary from one rating agency to another, and whether companies disclose only favorable data.

## Output
A demand for unified evaluation and disclosure criteria and strengthened verification.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Measurement-verification-strengthening theory: This criticizes the prevalence of disguised environmentalism such as greenwashing, saying that without unified criteria scores varied from one rating agency to another, and that companies abused it as a means to disclose only favorable data and package themselves as eco-friendly."
