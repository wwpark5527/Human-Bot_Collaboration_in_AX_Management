# ESGX_STAKEHOLDER_EXPANSION_CRITIQUE — Stakeholder-expansion theory

## Concept Definition
A critique type that regards the existing ESG—which remained at company-centered single materiality—as too investor-centric. It points out that the existing ESG only considered how risky climate change or human-rights issues were to a company's financial condition (its money-making) rather than the issues themselves. It is the first of the three critique types classified by ESG extension theory (stakeholder-expansion theory / measurement-verification-strengthening theory / ESG AI-era extension theory).

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the existing ESG considered only how risky climate change or human-rights issues were to a company's financial condition.

## Output
A demand for a shift from financial materiality to impact materiality (e.g., the EU CSRD).

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Stakeholder-expansion theory: This is the limitation of company-centered single materiality—the criticism that ESG is too investor-centric."
