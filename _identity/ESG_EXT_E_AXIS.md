# ESG_EXT_E_AXIS — Extension of E (Sustainability of AI Infrastructure)

## Concept Definition
An E axis extended so that, while retaining the existing responsibilities for environment, energy, carbon, resources, and pollution, it also includes the environmental responsibility of AI infrastructure, data centers, compute, cloud, and digital operations.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the physical burden that AI induces (data centers, electricity, water, semiconductors, carbon, e-waste) is managed as a corporate environmental responsibility.

## Output
Management items for electricity use, water use, carbon emissions, semiconductors and minerals, e-waste, and local environmental impact, together with their organizational and social implications.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "The environmental impact of AI must be managed on the E axis of ESG."
