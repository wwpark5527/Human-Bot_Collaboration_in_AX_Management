# ESG_EXT_G_AXIS — Extension of G (Accountability Structure of AI Power)

## Concept Definition
A G axis extended so that, while retaining the existing responsibilities for the board, control, audit, risk, and compliance, it also includes AI authority, verification, approval, recording, explanation, objection, accountability attribution, and improvement loops.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the accountability and control problems that AI induces (responsibility, verification, approval, recording, explainability, audit, risk management) are operated within governance.

## Output
An AI governance system covering the clarification of roles, authority, and responsibility; management of records and logs; approval structures and review procedures; explainability and accountability attribution; and risk management and objection procedures.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "That is, G must retain governance in the traditional sense while evolving toward including, within it, a system for responsibly operating the functioning and judgment of AI."
