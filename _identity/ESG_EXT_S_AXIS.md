# ESG_EXT_S_AXIS — Extension of S (AI Capability Equality and Human Dignity)

## Concept Definition
An S axis extended so that, while retaining the existing responsibilities for labor, human rights, safety, diversity, and social contribution, it also includes AI access rights, the right to learn, role transition, the human right to judge, and equality in the capability to use AI.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the human and social changes that AI induces (labor transition, capability gaps, algorithmic management, human rights, the human right to judge) are handled as a corporate social responsibility.

## Output
The guarantee of social capabilities enabling humans to understand, use, and control AI, to raise objections, and to participate in outcomes (granting rights to protect humans in the AI era).

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "S must address the social quality of an organization in which humans and AI work together."
