# ESG_FOR_AI — ESG for AI

## Concept Definition
A perspective that points to the negative effects AI brings, as an object of management that induces side effects from the ESG standpoint. In some cases AI produces negative effects and even ethical problems, so its use must be controlled from the ESG standpoint. It expresses the second of the two perspectives on the AI–ESG relationship, namely the direction in which ESG manages and controls AI.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether AI produces negative effects and even ethical problems such that its use must be controlled from the ESG standpoint.

## Output
Control of AI use from the ESG standpoint (leading to the demand for responsible AI).

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "From the ESG standpoint, the negative effects of AI as an object of management that induces side effects are expressed as 'ESG for AI.'"
