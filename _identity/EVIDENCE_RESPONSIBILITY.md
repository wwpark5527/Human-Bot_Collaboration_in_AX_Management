# EVIDENCE_RESPONSIBILITY — Evidence Responsibility

## Concept Definition
The responsibility that not only the result but also why this result arose, who reviewed it, and by what criteria it was approved must remain on record. It is the third and last of the three responsibilities (context, judgment, evidence) that AX-organization talent bears when working with AI.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the materials used, the instructions given to AI, the results AI produced, the revisions made by humans, the verification criteria, the approval records, the error records, and the improvements reflected are all left on record.

## Output
Produces an evidence record by which the course of judgment can be retraced.
