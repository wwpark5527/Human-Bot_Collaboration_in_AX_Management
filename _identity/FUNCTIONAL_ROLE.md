# FUNCTIONAL_ROLE — Functional Role

## Concept Definition
A functional role means a function formally assigned by the organization or a superior in a job-related way. For example, in an HR team, the recruiting officer, evaluation officer, payroll officer, and benefits officer are functional roles. This is the name Belbin gave when he newly discovered and named the team role, to contrast it with what had previously been called "role"; paired with the team role, it forms one axis of the extension of the "role" concept (functional role + team role).

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether it was formally assigned by the organization or a superior, and whether that assignment is job-related. If both conditions are met it is a functional role, distinguished from the team role, which is an informal, non-job-related role.

## Output
When the functional roles that must exist in a team are evenly in place and the team members have the ability, qualifications, experience, and motivation needed to perform them, the foundation for team performance is laid. However, the awareness that team performance is often inadequate even when functional roles are perfectly in place leads to the need for the team-role concept, an additional element required to form teamwork.

## Evidence (original quotation)
