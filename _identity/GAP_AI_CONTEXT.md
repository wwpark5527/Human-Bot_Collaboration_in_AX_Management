# GAP_AI_CONTEXT — AI Context Gap

## Concept Definition
The gap between people who can and cannot provide AI with the organization's purpose, criteria, language, way of working, output formats, and verification criteria. From the organizational-AX perspective, it is the gap the author treats as most important, and it connects to the concept of context capital.

## Decision Criteria
Who can give AI the organization's purpose and criteria.

## Output
A state in which only a few properly direct AI, and further, the problems of ownership, access, verification, and outcome attribution of context capital.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Among these (from a stance focused for now on organizational AX), the one the author regards as important is the AI context gap. Even when using the same AI, the difference between those who can and cannot provide AI with the organization's purpose, criteria, language, way of working, output formats, and verification criteria grows larger over time."
