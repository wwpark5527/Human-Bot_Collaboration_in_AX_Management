# GAP_AI_SURVEILLANCE — AI Surveillance Gap

## Concept Definition
The gap that arises between actors who use AI as a tool to manage others and those who are managed by AI. It is the fifth of the seven gaps that create AI-based stratification, and concerns who, through algorithms, stands in the position of managing and monitoring and who becomes their object.

## Decision Criteria
Who manages with AI, and who is managed by AI.

## Output
Algorithmic management and control.
