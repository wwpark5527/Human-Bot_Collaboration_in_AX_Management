# GOVERNANCE_CONTEXT_ELEMENT_ACCOUNTABILITY — Accountability

## Concept Definition
Accountability is the governance-context element that sets the person ultimately responsible for use and their criteria of judgment. It is the sixth of the seven elements (authority, security, verification, approval, record, accountability, improvement) of `governance context`(S2C-0105, `GOVERNANCE_CONTEXT`).

## Decision Criteria
Judges who bears the responsibility for the final use and judgment.

## Output
Clarification of where responsibility lies (corresponding to ISO/IEC 42001 top-management responsibility, NIST Govern, and the provider/deployer obligations of the EU AI Act).
