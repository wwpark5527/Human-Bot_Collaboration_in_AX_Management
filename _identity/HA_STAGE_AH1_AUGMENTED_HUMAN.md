# HA_STAGE_AH1_AUGMENTED_HUMAN — AH1 Augmented Human

## Concept Definition
AH1 augmented human is the fourth of the six-rung ladder of human-augmentation stages (H0–AH3), the entry stage into the augmented human where thinking and learning have increased. Going beyond H2 (AI collaborator, working jointly with AI), it is the stage where AI use actually leads to increased thinking ability and learning volume. It is the first stage to be given the name "augmented human (AH)," and serves as the gateway to the next stage, AH2 AI orchestrator (using/directing multiple AI agents).

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether thinking and learning have increased through AI use.

## Output
Increased thinking ability and learning volume.
