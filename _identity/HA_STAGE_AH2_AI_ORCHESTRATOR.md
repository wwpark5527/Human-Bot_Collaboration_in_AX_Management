# HA_STAGE_AH2_AI_ORCHESTRATOR — AH2 AI Orchestrator

## Concept Definition
AH2 AI orchestrator is the fifth of the six-rung ladder of human-augmentation stages (H0–AH3), the stage of using and directing multiple AI agents and the first stage where "role augmentation" occurs. Going beyond AH1 (augmented human, increased thinking/learning), it is the state of reaching the level of simultaneously directing and coordinating multiple AI agents beyond the use of a single AI, and corresponds to the stage just before AH3 (human-AI symbiotic leader), the ultimate goal of human augmentation.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether one uses and directs multiple AI agents.

## Output
Role expansion through the use/direction of multiple AI agents.
