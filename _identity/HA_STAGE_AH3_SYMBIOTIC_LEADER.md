# HA_STAGE_AH3_SYMBIOTIC_LEADER — AH3 Human-AI Symbiotic Leader

## Concept Definition
AH3 human-AI symbiotic leader is the last stage of the six-rung ladder of human-augmentation stages (H0–AH3), the ultimate goal stage of human augmentation in which human and AI operate as a single system. Going beyond AH2 (AI orchestrator, using/directing multiple AI agents), it is the state of reaching a level where the human-AI relationship fuses into a single integrated system beyond mere directing and being directed, and where "role augmentation" is completed so that the human functions as a leader who leads the organization in symbiosis with AI.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether human and AI operate as a single system.

## Output
Operation and leadership as an integrated human-AI system.
