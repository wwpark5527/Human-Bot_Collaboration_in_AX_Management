# HA_STAGE_H2_AI_COLLABORATOR — H2 AI Collaborator

## Concept Definition
H2 AI collaborator is the third of the six-rung ladder of human-augmentation stages (H0–AH3), the stage of performing joint work with AI. Advancing one step from H1 (AI user, question/search level), it begins to treat AI not as a mere tool but as a counterpart that performs work together, and corresponds to the turning point leading to the next stage, AH1 augmented human (increased thinking/learning).

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether one performs joint work with AI.

## Output
Products of joint work with AI.
