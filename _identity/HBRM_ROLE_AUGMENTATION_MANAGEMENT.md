# HBRM_ROLE_AUGMENTATION_MANAGEMENT — Augmentation Management

## Concept Definition
Augmentation management is the fourth of the eight roles that HBRM (Human-Bot Resource Management) performs within an AX organization. It is the function that supports the process by which, within the collaboration flow established through collaboration-structure design, humans transition into augmented humans (AH) and bots into augmented bots (AB). This connects directly to HBRM's evolutionary-expansion dimension—that is, going beyond a mere management system to design humans and bots to co-evolve. It is the management function needed for an organization to develop humans and bots into a relationship that raises each other's capabilities rather than one that replaces each other.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether the augmentation transition of humans and bots is being supported.

## Output
Progress in the H→AH and B→AB transitions.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Augmentation management                       Support the H→AH, B→AB transition"
