# HBRM_ROLE_COLLABORATION_DESIGN — Collaboration-Structure Design

## Concept Definition
Collaboration-structure design is the third of the eight roles that HBRM (Human-Bot Resource Management) performs within an AX organization. It is the function that, based on the role and responsibility boundaries between humans and bots set in the role-design stage, designs the flow of how humans and bots actually collaborate in real work. This corresponds to the stage that goes beyond deciding who takes on which role to defining how the assigned roles actually interlock and operate. Within the AX organization's role-expansion dimension, it is the collaboration-design stage that must necessarily follow role definition.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether the human-bot collaboration flow has been designed.

## Output
The designed human-bot collaboration flow.
