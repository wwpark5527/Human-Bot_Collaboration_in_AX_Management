# HBRM_ROLE_GOVERNANCE_LINKAGE — Governance Linkage

## Concept Definition
Governance linkage is the seventh of the eight roles that HBRM (Human-Bot Resource Management) performs within an AX organization. It is the function that interconnects authority, verification, approval, recording, and responsibility structures. Even when trust management establishes collaboration standards that prevent overtrust and distrust, for those standards to actually work there must be a single structure connecting who holds authority, how verification is done, who approves, how it is recorded, and who bears final responsibility. This is the core stage that answers the questions of by what criteria results are verified in an AX organization and who bears the final responsibility.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether the authority, verification, approval, recording, and responsibility structures are interconnected.

## Output
The interconnected authority, verification, approval, recording, and responsibility structures.
