# HBRM_ROLE_LEARNING_IMPROVEMENT — Learning and Improvement Management

## Concept Definition
Learning and improvement management is the eighth and final of the eight roles that HBRM (Human-Bot Resource Management) performs within an AX organization. It is the function that accumulates feedback and errors into organizational memory. HBRM's role system—which begins with member definition and continues through role design, collaboration-structure design, augmentation management, role-balance management, trust management, and governance linkage—is not complete if, even after results are verified and responsibility is connected, that experience is not built back into the organization. Learning and improvement management converts the achievements and mistakes that humans and bots create together into organizational memory, rather than letting them slip away, so that they can be reflected in the next role-balance diagnosis and HBRM operation.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether feedback and errors are accumulated into organizational memory.

## Output
Feedback and errors accumulated as organizational memory.
