# HBRM_ROLE_MEMBER_DEFINITION — Member Definition

## Concept Definition
Member definition is the first of the eight roles that HBRM (Human-Bot Resource Management) performs within an AX organization. It is the function that defines what position and role each of the four member types—H (human), AH (augmented human), B (bot), and AB (augmented bot)—occupies and takes on within the organization. This corresponds to the starting point at which HBRM extends human-centered HRM into human-bot hybrid organizational management.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether the positions and roles of the four member types (H, AH, B, AB) are defined.

## Output
The defined positions and roles of H, AH, B, and AB.
