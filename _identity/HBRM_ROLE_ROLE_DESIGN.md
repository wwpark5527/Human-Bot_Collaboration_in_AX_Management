# HBRM_ROLE_ROLE_DESIGN — Role Design

## Concept Definition
Role design is the second of the eight roles that HBRM (Human-Bot Resource Management) performs within an AX organization. It is the function that sets the boundaries of what role each of the four types—H (human), AH (augmented human), B (bot), AB (augmented bot)—finalized through member definition actually takes on within the organization and how far each is responsible. This connects directly to HBRM's role-expansion dimension—that is, the shift from job-centered management to human-bot role-based collaborative management. It corresponds to the stage that defines who takes on which role, how humans and bots collaborate, by what criteria results are verified, and who bears final responsibility.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether the role and responsibility boundaries between humans and bots are set.

## Output
The established role and responsibility boundaries.
