# HBS_DIM_BAEK — Baek (魄)

## Concept Definition
Baek (魄) is the bodily dimension of the human, responsible for the body, the senses, labor, and physical execution, in the traditional three-dimensional Yeong-Hon-Baek model. This model is a framework for understanding the human-machine relationship in the age of AI and robots through the three dimensions of Hon (魂), Baek (魄), and Yeong (靈), and it forms the foundation of the entire section on "preventing honbibaeksan (魂飛魄散, scattering of soul and spirit)." In the age of AI and robots, robots, automation devices, and mechanical execution correspond to the place of Baek. This does not mean that a robot possesses the human body itself, but that a robot externally performs on its behalf part of the physical-execution function that Baek used to carry out.

## Decision Criteria
Whether the function belongs to the body, the senses, labor, or physical execution.

## Output
In the age of AI and robots, robots, automation devices, and mechanical execution correspond to this place, and robots extend the bodily functions corresponding to the human Baek.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Baek (魄)     Body, senses, labor, physical execution             Robots, automation devices, mechanical execution"

> "AI imitates the cognitive functions corresponding to the human Hon, and robots extend the bodily functions corresponding to the human Baek."
