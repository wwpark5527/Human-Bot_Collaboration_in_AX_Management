# HBS_DIM_HON — Hon (魂)

## Concept Definition
Hon (魂) is the cognitive dimension of the human, responsible for thought, memory, judgment, creation, and decision-making, in the traditional three-dimensional Yeong-Hon-Baek model. This model is a framework for understanding the human-machine relationship in the age of AI and robots through the three dimensions of Hon (魂), Baek (魄), and Yeong (靈), and it forms the foundation of the entire section on "preventing honbibaeksan (魂飛魄散, scattering of soul and spirit)." In the age of AI and robots, AI's analysis, reasoning, language generation, and judgment assistance correspond to the place of Hon. This does not mean that AI possesses the human Hon itself, but that AI externally performs on its behalf part of the cognitive function that Hon used to carry out.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether the function belongs to thought, memory, judgment, creation, or decision-making (that is, neither physical execution nor dignity/subjecthood).

## Output
In the age of AI and robots, AI's analysis, reasoning, language generation, and judgment assistance correspond to this place, and AI imitates the cognitive functions corresponding to the human Hon.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Hon (魂)    Thought, memory, judgment, creation, decision-making          AI's analysis, reasoning, language generation, judgment assistance"

> "AI imitates the cognitive functions corresponding to the human Hon, and robots extend the bodily functions corresponding to the human Baek."
