# HBS_PREV_DECISION_LOGGING — AI's decision-making process must be recorded

## Concept Definition
This is a honbibaeksan-prevention measure stating that a record must be kept of on what grounds AI made its judgment, what data was used, who approved it, and what risks were reviewed. It is the second of the five practical guidelines in the "honbibaeksan-prevention measures" section, and it is the guideline that creates the evidentiary basis actually supporting the fixing of final responsibility to humans and the organization (the first guideline).

## Decision Criteria
Whether the grounds of judgment, the data used, the approver, and the reviewed risks are recorded.

## Output
Because responsibility also disappears when there is no record, the existence of the record yields the persistence of responsibility.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "AI's decision-making process must be recorded: it must be recorded on what grounds AI made its judgment, what data was used, who approved it, and what risks were reviewed. Where there is no record, responsibility disappears."
