# HBS_PREV_FINAL_RESPONSIBILITY — Final responsibility must remain with humans and the organization

## Concept Definition
This is a honbibaeksan-prevention measure stating that, because AI can advise but cannot bear responsibility, in important decisions humans and the organization must hold the final decision authority and responsibility. It is the first of the five concrete practical guidelines presented in the "honbibaeksan-prevention measures" section, and it concretizes the principle that Yeong (靈) cannot be outsourced into an actionable guideline at the level of organizational operation.

## Decision Criteria
Whether, in important decisions, the final decision authority and responsibility lie with humans and the organization.

## Output
The responsible party is fixed to humans and the organization and is not transferred to AI.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Final responsibility must remain with humans and the organization: AI can advise, but it cannot bear responsibility. In important decisions, humans and the organization must hold the final decision authority and responsibility."
