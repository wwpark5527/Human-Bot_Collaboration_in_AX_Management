# HBS_PREV_GOVERNANCE_BY_DESIGN — Technology adoption must be designed together with governance

## Concept Definition
This is a honbibaeksan-prevention measure stating that governance—which determines not what AI can do but how far it may go—must be designed simultaneously with AI adoption. It is the fifth and final of the five practical guidelines in the "honbibaeksan-prevention measures" section. Whereas the preceding four guidelines (final responsibility, recording the decision process, the human's reviewer role, and preventing componentization) were principles for individual decision and action situations, this guideline corresponds to a structural conclusion: that governance must be built into the technology-adoption stage itself.

## Decision Criteria
Whether governance—including authority, responsibility, approval, recording, auditing, and risk review—was included together in the adoption design.

## Output
Not a function-centered adoption, but an adoption in which the permitted scope is defined.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Technology adoption must be designed together with governance: when an organization adopts AI, it must not look only at function. More important than what AI can do is determining how far AI may go."
