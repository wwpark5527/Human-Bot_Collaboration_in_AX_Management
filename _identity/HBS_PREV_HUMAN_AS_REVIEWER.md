# HBS_PREV_HUMAN_AS_REVIEWER — Humans must be reviewers of AI output, not consumers

## Concept Definition
This is a honbibaeksan-prevention measure stating that AI-produced results must not be accepted as they are but must be interpreted, verified, and adjusted. It is the third of the five practical guidelines in the "honbibaeksan-prevention measures" section, and, on the foundation laid by the two preceding guidelines (attributing responsibility to humans, and recording the decision process), it defines the very attitude with which humans should treat AI output.

## Decision Criteria
Whether humans interpret, verify, and adjust AI results, or accept them as they are.

## Output
The human role in the AI age is defined not as a mere user but as a judge and verifier.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Humans must be reviewers of AI output, not consumers: rather than accepting AI-produced results as they are, they must be interpreted, verified, and adjusted. The human role in the AI age is not a mere user but a judge and verifier."
