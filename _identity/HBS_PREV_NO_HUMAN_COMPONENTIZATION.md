# HBS_PREV_NO_HUMAN_COMPONENTIZATION — Automation must not reduce humans to components

## Concept Definition
This is a honbibaeksan-prevention measure stating that even if robots and automation systems raise efficiency, in the process they must not infringe on human rest, safety, dignity, or decision authority. It is the fourth of the five practical guidelines in the "honbibaeksan-prevention measures" section, and whereas the preceding three guidelines dealt mainly with protecting Hon (judgment), this guideline directly corresponds to protecting Baek (body/labor)—that is, preventing "baeksan (the scattering of Baek)."

## Decision Criteria
Whether, in the automation process, human rest, safety, dignity, or decision authority is infringed.

## Output
It blocks efficiency gains from leading to the componentization of humans (baeksan).

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Automation must not reduce humans to components: robots and automation systems can raise efficiency. But in that process, human rest, safety, dignity, and decision authority must not be infringed."
