# HR_STAGE1_REWARD_DESIGN — Stage 1 (Reward Design)

## Concept Definition
Stage 1 (reward design) is the stage that embeds human respect into the reward function itself, because a bot operates on the basis of its reward function. It is presented as the first of the five architectural stages that constitute the concrete method for implementing human respect.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether the reward function includes not only task performance but also human satisfaction, human-harm risk, and a penalty for norm violation.

## Output
A reward function with human respect embedded in it.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Stage 1 (reward design): because a bot operates on the basis of its reward function, embedding human respect into the reward function is most important."
