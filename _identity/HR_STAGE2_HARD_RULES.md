# HR_STAGE2_HARD_RULES — Stage 2 (Hard Rules)

## Concept Definition
Stage 2 (hard rules) is the stage that defines the absolutely forbidden zone where "crossing the line ends it," regardless of reward. It is the second of the five architectural stages for implementing human respect, blocking, through separate absolute rules, the violations that Stage 1 (reward design) alone cannot prevent.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether an action falls into an absolutely forbidden zone such as harmful actions, deception/manipulation, or illegal acts.

## Output
An absolute prohibition line that operates independently of reward calculation.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Stage 2 (hard rules): an absolutely forbidden zone where 'crossing the line ends it' (harmful actions, deception/manipulation, illegal acts) must be defined, regardless of reward."
