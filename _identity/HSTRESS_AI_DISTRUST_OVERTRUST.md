# HSTRESS_AI_DISTRUST_OVERTRUST — Stress from distrust or overtrust of AI

## Concept Definition
This is a human stress in which the very state of tension of wavering between the two extremes of distrust and overtrust of AI becomes stress itself. It is the fourth of the "human stress types (5 types)"; whereas cognitive overload is a problem of the "amount" being managed, this stress is a problem of the "directionality of trust" in that judgment being unstable.

## Decision Criteria
Whether one wavers between the distrust of "What if the AI is wrong? What about hallucinations? What about manipulated data?" and the overtrust of "The AI recommended it, so it must be right?"

## Output
The state of tension itself becomes persistent stress, and on the overtrust side it produces weakened judgment and avoidance of responsibility.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Stress from distrust or overtrust of AI: humans come to waver between the two extremes, and this state of tension itself continually becomes stress."
