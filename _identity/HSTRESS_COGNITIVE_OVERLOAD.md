# HSTRESS_COGNITIVE_OVERLOAD — Cognitive Overload

## Concept Definition
This is the human stress that arises when a human, no longer a mere executor, becomes a meta-manager who supervises, coordinates, and verifies AI, so that the number of things to be managed simultaneously increases. It is the third of the 'Human Stress Types (5 types).' Unlike the first two types (role ambiguity and replacement anxiety), which are problems of relationship and identity, cognitive overload is a problem of the cognitive burden that comes from the growing number of things to be managed at once.

## Decision Criteria
Does the number of things to be managed simultaneously grow — multiple AI agents, real-time data, automation workflows, verification of AI recommendations, governance compliance, and the like?

## Output
It produces decision fatigue, attention fragmentation, and continuous monitoring burden.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Cognitive Overload: In an AX organization, the human is no longer a mere executor but becomes a meta-manager who supervises, coordinates, and verifies AI."
