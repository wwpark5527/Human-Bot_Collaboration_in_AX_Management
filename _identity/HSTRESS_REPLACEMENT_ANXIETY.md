# HSTRESS_REPLACEMENT_ANXIETY — Replacement Anxiety

## Concept Definition
This is the human stress that comes from experiencing the threat that one's own expertise becomes inferior to AI and that one is demoted to a supporting role. It is the second of the 'Human Stress Types (5 types).' Unlike role ambiguity, which is a problem of dividing roles, replacement anxiety stems from the perceived threat to one's own expertise and existential worth.

## Decision Criteria
Does one experience threats of the form "Is my expertise becoming inferior to AI? Am I being demoted to a supporting role? Is AI taking away my promotion opportunities?"

## Output
It can develop beyond traditional job insecurity into existential anxiety and an identity crisis, and it is experienced most strongly by knowledge workers.
