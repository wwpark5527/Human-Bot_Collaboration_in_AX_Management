# HSTRESS_ROLE_AMBIGUITY — Role Ambiguity

## Concept Definition
This is the human stress that arises when humans and bots work together and "who is responsible for what?" becomes unclear. It is the first of the 'Human Stress Types (5 types),' referring, among the psychological, social, cognitive, and identity tensions of humans, to the tension that stems from the lack of clarity in dividing roles.

## Decision Criteria
Are questions such as "Do humans do only strategy? Do bots also make decisions? Who is the final responsible party?" left unresolved?

## Output
It increases role conflict and role ambiguity, and gives rise to clashes between human judgment and AI recommendations, damage to human self-esteem when AI is more accurate, and problems of assigning responsibility upon failure.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Role Ambiguity: When humans and bots work together, "who is responsible for what?" becomes unclear."
