# HSTRESS_SOCIAL_RELATIONAL — Social and Relational Stress

## Concept Definition
This is the relational-dimension human stress that arises when, in an AX organization, one must cooperate not only with human colleagues but also with bots. It is the fifth and last of the 'Human Stress Types (5 types).' Whereas the previous four types are intrapersonal tensions of role, ability, cognition, and trust, this stress is a social-dimension tension that stems from the relationship with bots itself.

## Decision Criteria
Are there problems of psychological distance from bots, deterioration of human-to-human relationships, social isolation, and increased emotional labor?

## Output
The lack of emotional empathy in customer service, AI supervision, acceptance of AI-generated feedback, and collaboration with bots weakens the human need to belong.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Social and Relational Stress: In an AX organization, one must cooperate not only with human colleagues but also with bots."
