# HUMANITY_PROTECTION — Protection of Humanity (humanity)

## Concept Definition
This is the highest-priority top-level concept that an AX-organization leader must place first when judging essence and direction (or alignment with orchestration) — the principle that a leader of a hybrid organization must ensure AI is aligned with human existence and development. It sits after problem-definition ability and meaning-design ability, and it is the reference point a leader must hold on to before exercising orchestration ability.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether the leader's judgment and use of AI are aligned with human existence and development — that is, whether AI does not damage humanity.

## Output
A state in which AI is aligned with human existence and development — the directionality of a hybrid organization in which humanity is protected.

## Evidence (original quotation)
