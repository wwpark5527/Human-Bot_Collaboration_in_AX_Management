# HUMAN_AI_COLLABORATION_ABILITY — Human-AI Collaboration Ability

## Concept Definition
This is the ability of a human to divide roles with, or collaborate with, bots rather than working alone. It is the second of the six required competencies presented in the section 'Requirements for AX-Organization Talent: The Competencies of Surviving Humans.' Building on the first competency, AI comprehension and affinity, it addresses the practical competency of actually dividing and connecting roles with AI.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether one can decide what to entrust to AI, what humans should do, and how to connect the two.

## Output
It produces a state of having become a collaborative human rather than a human who works alone.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Human-AI Collaboration Ability: In an AX organization, humans do not work alone but divide roles with, or collaborate with, bots. What is therefore needed is the ability to decide what to entrust to AI, what humans should do, and how to connect them. In other words, one must become a collaborative human rather than a human who works alone."
