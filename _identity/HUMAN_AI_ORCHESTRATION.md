# HUMAN_AI_ORCHESTRATION — Human-AI Orchestration (orchestration)

## Concept Definition
This is one of the additional conditions for succeeding in an AX organization: the coordinating, integrating, and designing ability that plays a central role in designing, controlling, and amplifying the human-AI collaboration structure to create new value, organizations, markets, and order by leveraging AI. It is made concrete through coordinating multiple AI agents, designing the roles of humans and bots, and designing the collaboration structure. A leader who possesses it shifts from being a direct executor to an augmented orchestrator who integrates the capabilities of humans and AI.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether one personally designs, controls, and amplifies the human-AI collaboration structure to create new value, organizations, markets, and order, or instead remains a direct executor.

## Output
A designed, integrated, and coordinated human-AI collaboration structure — leadership exercised as an augmented orchestrator through coordinating multiple AI agents, designing human and bot roles, and designing the collaboration structure.
