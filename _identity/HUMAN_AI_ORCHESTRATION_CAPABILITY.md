# HUMAN_AI_ORCHESTRATION_CAPABILITY — Human-AI Orchestration Capability

## Concept Definition
This is the ability to coordinate multiple AI agents, design the roles of humans and bots, design team role balance (TRB), and design the collaboration structure. In an AX organization, what is required is not someone who is good on their own but someone who designs the very system composed of humans and AI.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether one is someone who is good on their own, or someone who designs a human + AI system.

## Output
It produces a designed human-AI collaboration structure and role placement.
