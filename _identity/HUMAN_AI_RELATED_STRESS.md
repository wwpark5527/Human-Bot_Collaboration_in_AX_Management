# HUMAN_AI_RELATED_STRESS — Human Stress Related to AI

## Concept Definition
Human stress related to AI is the stress humans carry with respect to AI, such as fear of replacement, fear of surveillance, identity threat, a sense of losing competence, loss of meaning, and worry about the erosion of humanity. Following human illogicality, it is presented as the second human characteristic a bot must understand.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether the cause of an AI-adoption failure is a technology failure or a human-reaction failure.

## Output
The diagnosis that most AI-adoption failures are human-reaction failures.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Human stress related to AI: Humans carry considerable stress with respect to AI (e.g., fear of replacement, fear of surveillance, identity threat, a sense of losing competence, loss of meaning, worry about the erosion of humanity), and in reality most AI-adoption failures are not technology failures but precisely human-reaction failures."
