# HUMAN_BOND_TEAMWORK — Teamwork in the Human-Bond Aspect

## Concept Definition
Teamwork as the psychological attraction among members to like one another, band together, and help one another. It is the existing concept itself, the one that academia and industry called teamwork until the concept of team role appeared.

## Decision Criteria
It is measured by group cohesiveness. On the fit dimension, it corresponds to supplementary fit — fitting well because members are similar to one another (line 122).

## Output
It has a significant effect on the perceived performance of their (department's) performance as recognized by members, but it does not have a significant effect on the actual performance evaluated by the company. It is therefore positioned as an additional condition to attend to over the medium and long term (lines 180-184).

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Teamwork was seen only as the human bond among members to like one another, band together, and help one another."
