# HUMAN_BOT_ROLE_MANAGEMENT — HBRM (Human-Bot Role Management)

## Concept Definition
HBRM (Human-Bot Role Management) is a management paradigm that extends and applies the principles of TR (team role) and TRB (team role balance) to the human-bot relationship in an AX organization where humans and bots coexist. It is a single candidate confirmed as KEEP without SplitSet division, and the source text, without separately expanding the acronym, presents HBRM as one core principle that integrates diversity management, complementary fit, human-AI collaboration, and organizational AX together with HBRM. Within the framework of this HBRM, Chapter 6 goes on to explain two evolutionary stages: Bot-Aided TRB (vertical relationship) and Human-Bot Coupled TRB (horizontal relationship).

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether diversity management, complementary fit, human-AI collaboration, and organizational AX are operated in an integrated way under a single management principle (HBRM) of human-bot resource management.

## Output
A judgment result is produced regarding whether the management principle (HBRM) that integrally manages human-bot resources in an AX organization is applied, and the level of that integration.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "It can be called the core principle that integrates 'diversity management, complementary fit, human-AI collaboration, organizational AX, and HBRM.'"
