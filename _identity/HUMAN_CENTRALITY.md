# HUMAN_CENTRALITY — Human Centrality

## Concept Definition
Human Centrality is the basic premise that, whether it is the technical advancement of AI or the realization of organizational AX, both presuppose 'the survival and development of humans.' It becomes the foundation from which the three core spirits (augmentation / respect for and use of diversity / pursuit of complementary fit) are derived within the core-spirit system of management.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether something harms or helps the survival and development of humans, and whether the share (subject) of that judgment rests with humans. That said, it is also judged by whether one avoids falling into zero-sum thinking of the form 'humans always come first and bots must be sacrificed.'

## Output
It establishes a criterion for judgment that rejects AI and AX harmful to the survival and development of humans, and on that basis produces the basic premise from which the three core spirits — augmentation, respect for and use of diversity, and pursuit of complementary fit — can be derived.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "AI and AX that harm the survival and development of humans are rejected, and the share (subject) of judging whether something is harmful or helpful is also the human."

> "'Human Centrality' must not mean holding zero-sum thinking of the form 'humans always come first, humans are in the position of the boss, and bots must be sacrificed for humans...'"
