# HUMAN_ILLOGICALITY — Human Illogicality

## Concept Definition
Human illogicality is the human characteristic that, while AI pursues consistency, optimization, and efficiency, humans are moved by emotion, relationship, symbol, identity, and habit. It is the first of the human characteristics a bot must understand, as presented by G. Gibbins in Welcome to your human-AI team!.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether human behavior is determined not by logic and efficiency but by emotion, relationship, symbol, identity, and habit.

## Output
The recognition that something efficient will fail if people dislike it, and something logical will be rejected if it cannot be trusted — that is, the demand for AI to understand human psychology.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Human illogicality: AI pursues consistency, optimization, and efficiency, but humans are moved by emotion, relationship, symbol, identity, and habit."
