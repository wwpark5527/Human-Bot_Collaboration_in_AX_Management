# HUMAN_MEMBER — H: Human

## Concept Definition
This is one of the four member types (H·AH·AB·B) of an AI-transformed organization (AX organization), referring to a traditional human member whose physical and mental capabilities are not augmented by AI. The member classification of an AX organization is organized along the keyword augmentation, and H corresponds to the reference point where that augmentation is not applied. This type reappears as the expression "H-centered" in the HRM/HBRM comparison table of Chapter 4 (HBRM), and HBRM's "member definition" role continues into defining the positions and roles of H·AH·B·AB.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether physical and mental capabilities have been augmented with the help of AI; if not augmented, it is judged as the H (human) type.

## Output
It produces a result that classifies AX-organization members not as a 'human vs AI bot' opposition but into the four types H/AH/AB/B, providing a classification system of shared prosperity based on coexistence and cooperation.
