# HUMAN_RESPECT_INTERNALIZATION — Internalization of Human Respect

## Concept Definition
The internalization of human respect is the most important element in bot (robot) education. It is the principle of designing the behavior, reward, and verification structure in a human-centered way so that only behaviors that do not violate humans are selected, rather than education that instills human respect as moral teaching. Taking classical human-respect and safety obligations such as the Three Laws of Robotics as a starting point, it aims to make it so that failing to respect humans becomes disadvantageous within the system.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether the bot's behavior, reward, and verification structure is designed so that only behaviors that do not violate humans are selected — that is, whether human respect is implemented as system design rather than moral instillation.

## Output
A behavior, reward, and verification structure redesigned so that only behaviors that do not violate humans are selected.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Teaching a bot to respect humans is not education but designing the system so that only behaviors that do not violate humans are selected."
