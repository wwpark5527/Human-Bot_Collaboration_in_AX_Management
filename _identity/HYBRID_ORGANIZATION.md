# HYBRID_ORGANIZATION — True AX Organization / Hybrid Organization (AH+AB)

## Concept Definition
This refers to an organization type in which both humans and bots break away from their existing state and transition into a mutually augmented state (AH + AB) to cooperate; the text calls this a 'true AX organization' or a 'hybrid organization.' Among the human-bot coexistence types (H+B, H+AH, AH+B, AH+AB), it is presented as the most desirable aim, and it is defined as a collaborative cognition system in which humans and bots, based on shared/governance context and AI governance, divide and share roles centered on roles rather than jobs, mutually augment one another, and jointly judge and learn. The author also expresses this as a hybrid intelligent organization that continuously learns and adapts on a role basis.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether both humans and bots, the members of an AX organization, have broken away from their existing state and transitioned into a mutually augmented state. Humans must be augmented to AH and bots to AB simultaneously, and whether the bot is not a mere execution tool but a subject that jointly judges with humans is the key point distinguishing this from the AH + B stage.

## Output
It produces the 'human-bot coexistence and cooperation organization,' the target point of realizing organizational AX = the true AX organization (hybrid organization). The realization method is the use of a proper OS for organizational AX and the realization of HBRM (Human Bot Resource Management), and as a result, what used to scatter as individual performance is converted into the organization's operational assets.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "The organization type in which augmented humans and bots cooperate (AH + AB) is the 'true AX organization,' and this type is called a 'hybrid organization.'"
