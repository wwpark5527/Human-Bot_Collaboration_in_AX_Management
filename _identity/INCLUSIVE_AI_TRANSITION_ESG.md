# INCLUSIVE_AI_TRANSITION_ESG — Inclusive / Just AI Transition ESG

## Concept Definition
This is an expanded management philosophy that treats AI access, learning, use, verification, approval, recording, responsibility, and benefit-sharing as a company's ESG responsibility, in order to prevent a structure in which, during the AI-transition process, only some strata own, use, and direct AI while the majority are evaluated, replaced, managed, and excluded by AI. It is a concept that extends to AI transition the principle of Just Transition used in climate transition, starting from the awareness that, just as climate transition must not abandon workers and local communities, AI transition must not abandon humans' roles, income, right to judge, right to learn, and dignity. Depending on context, the English expression is used as 'Just AI Transition ESG' (centered on just transition, labor transition, and human rights) or 'Inclusive AI Transition ESG' (centered on inclusiveness, accessibility, and capability expansion). This is the book's flagship named framework — the management frame in which, among the three types of ESG-expansion theory, this book's position (the AI-era expansion theory of ESG) is made concrete.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether AI access, learning, use, verification, approval, recording, responsibility, and benefit-sharing are explicitly treated as a company's ESG responsibility, in order to prevent differentiation (stratification) into an owning/using/directing stratum and a stratum that is evaluated, replaced, managed, and excluded during the AI-transition process.

## Output
An expanded ESG management philosophy (Inclusive AI Transition ESG) encompassing AI access, learning, use, verification, approval, recording, responsibility, and benefit-sharing.
