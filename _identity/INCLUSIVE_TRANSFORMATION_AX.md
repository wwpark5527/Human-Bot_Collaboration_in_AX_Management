# INCLUSIVE_TRANSFORMATION_AX — Inclusive Transformation AX

## Concept Definition
Inclusive Transformation AX in the AI era is an organizational-transformation framework that redesigns efficiency-centered AI transition from the perspective of responsibility and inclusiveness, preventing and mitigating AI stratification and protecting humans' role, right to judge, right to learn, right to transition, right to appeal, and right to share in performance. Without denying efficiency-centered AX, it is an organizational transformation that redesigns AX from the perspective of responsibility and inclusiveness so that humans' roles and rights are not damaged during the AI-transition process.

## Decision Criteria
Is AI transition designed to protect not only productivity but also humans' role, right to judge, right to learn, right to transition, right to appeal, and right to share in performance?

## Output
An organization that is more efficient with AI while humans' capabilities and rights are not damaged. An organizational-transformation framework that connects to the core execution structures of ESG's S (social) and G (governance).
