# IND_ACCOUNTABILITY_STRUCTURE — Accountability Structure

## Concept Definition
An indicator that checks whether a final responsible party for an AI output is designated. As the ninth item of the 12 Inclusive Transition ESG indicators, it is a basic measurement item for confirming whether an organization operates its AI transition responsibly. This indicator is not about **designing** responsibility but about measuring the **rate** at which responsible-party designation is actually carried out, reducing the claim "we operate responsibly" to a single verifiable figure when an organization makes it. The core of the measurement lies not in whether a designation exists but in the **identifiability** of the designation — an output that records only a department name or team name is indistinguishable from a state in which no one answers when an accident occurs, so it is counted as undesignated in this indicator.

## Decision Criteria
Rate of designating a final responsible party for AI outputs.

## Output
The figure for the rate of final-responsible-party designation.
