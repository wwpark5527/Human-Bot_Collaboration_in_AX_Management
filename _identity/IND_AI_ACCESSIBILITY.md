# IND_AI_ACCESSIBILITY — AI Accessibility

## Concept Definition
An indicator that looks at the degree to which employees can actually access AI tools and the variation in access between departments. As the first item of the 12 Inclusive Transition ESG indicators, it is a basic measurement item for confirming whether an organization operates its AI transition responsibly. This indicator looks at two values together — the **rate** at which employees can access the AI tools their work requires, and the **gap** showing how far that access diverges across departments. Since looking at the average access rate alone conceals the exclusion of a particular department, measuring both the total and the distribution simultaneously is a requirement of this indicator.

## Decision Criteria
Employee AI-tool access rate and inter-departmental access gap.

## Output
The access-rate figure and the inter-departmental gap value.
