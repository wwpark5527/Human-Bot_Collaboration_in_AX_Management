# IND_AI_EDUCATION — AI Education

## Concept Definition
An indicator that looks at the degree to which AI-literacy education and job-specific retraining are carried out. As the second item of the 12 Inclusive Transition ESG indicators, it is a basic measurement item for confirming whether an organization operates its AI transition responsibly. This indicator too looks at two values together — the **completion rate** at which employees have actually finished AI-literacy education, and the **reskilling hours** invested in retraining by job. Whereas the first indicator (AI accessibility) asked whether one can reach the tools, this indicator asks whether the organization is actually building the capability to handle those tools. If access is merely opened but education does not follow, the right of access remains a formality.

## Decision Criteria
AI-literacy education completion rate and job-specific reskilling hours.

## Output
The education completion-rate figure and job-specific reskilling hours.
