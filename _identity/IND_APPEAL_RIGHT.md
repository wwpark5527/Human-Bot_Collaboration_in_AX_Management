# IND_APPEAL_RIGHT — Right to Appeal

## Concept Definition
An indicator that looks at whether requests to review AI results are actually received and processed. As the seventh item of the 12 Inclusive Transition ESG indicators, it is a basic measurement item for confirming whether an organization operates its AI transition responsibly. This indicator pairs inflow with outcome — the **number of review requests** at which voices demanding review were actually received, and the **correction/remedy processing rate**, the rate at which those requests led to correction of a decision or remedy for the party concerned. The number of requests is a value that cannot by itself be judged good or bad — a small number may mean the decisions were accurate, or that there was no channel or grounds to raise them. Although the name carries the word "right," what this indicator measures is not entitlement but the operational fact of the number received and the processing rate.

## Decision Criteria
Number of requests to review AI results and correction/remedy processing rate.

## Output
Number of review requests and correction/remedy processing rate.
