# IND_COMPETENCE_INFERIORITY — Competence-Inferiority Type

## Concept Definition
An individual-resistance type that arises when one compares oneself with AI in the manner of "I am worse than AI." It is the second of the 'Individual Human Reaction/Resistance Types (5 types),' corresponding to the type in which, among the four layers of human reaction, the ability layer (perception of a capability gap relative to AI) is concretely manifested at the level of the individual member.

## Decision Criteria
Does it carry the features of learning fatigue, digital inferiority, self-deprecation from differences in AI-use ability, and a sense of technological alienation among middle-aged and older workers?

## Output
It appears as withdrawal within the organization, the perception that "only young employees survive," and pressure toward colleagues, and it connects to the problem of 'AI competence inequality.'
