# IND_CONTROL_LOSS — Control-Loss Type

## Concept Definition
An individual-resistance type in which, in the manner of "my right to judge is shrinking," one's own expertise and authority are threatened as AI recommendations and automated decision-making expand. It is the third of the 'Individual Human Reaction/Resistance Types (5 types),' corresponding to the type in which, among the four layers of human reaction, the relational layer (perception of a weakening position and authority within the organization) is concretely manifested at the level of the individual member.

## Decision Criteria
Does it carry the features of manager resistance, defense of expert authority, distrust of algorithms, and rejection of AI-made recommendations?

## Output
It appears as doctors refusing AI diagnostic assistance, HR distrust of AI hiring recommendations, and the financial sector's resistance to AI risk models, and it culminates in the problem of "who is the final judge?"

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Control-loss type: in the manner of "my right to judge is shrinking," as AI recommendations and automated decision-making expand, humans have their own expertise and authority threatened."
