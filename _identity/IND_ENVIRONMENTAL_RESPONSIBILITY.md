# IND_ENVIRONMENTAL_RESPONSIBILITY — Environmental Responsibility

## Concept Definition
An indicator that measures the environmental burden caused by operating AI infrastructure. As the eleventh and last item of the 12 Inclusive Transition ESG indicators, it is a basic measurement item for confirming whether an organization operates its AI transition responsibly. This indicator is the **only item among the 12 that measures the E axis**, and unlike the rest, which all target people and the organization (S·G), it targets physical resource consumption. It produces three values — two **absolute quantities**, power usage and carbon emissions, and one **ratio**, data-center efficiency. That absolute quantities and ratios differ in nature governs the judgment of this indicator. A state is possible in which absolute quantities rise while efficiency improves, and if that state is read as an improvement, this indicator loses its function of measuring the environmental burden.

## Decision Criteria
AI-infrastructure power usage, carbon emissions, and data-center efficiency.

## Output
Figures for power usage, carbon emissions, and data-center efficiency.
