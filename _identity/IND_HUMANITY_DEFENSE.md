# IND_HUMANITY_DEFENSE — Humanity-Defense Type

## Concept Definition
An individual-resistance type that appears as a high-order reaction of the form "what is human is disappearing." It is the fifth and last of the 'Individual Human Reaction/Resistance Types (5 types),' corresponding to the type in which, among the four layers of human reaction, the existential layer (perception of a shrinking of human meaning) is concretely manifested at the level of the individual member.

## Decision Criteria
Does it carry the features of defending human creativity, emphasizing human relationality, asserting the superiority of emotion, ethics, and intuition, and the "AI cannot understand humans" reaction?

## Output
It appears as artists' resistance to generative AI, professors' aversion to AI writing, and the emphasis on human-centeredness in the counseling and education fields, and it is a reaction to protect human uniqueness.
