# IND_HUMAN_JUDGMENT_RIGHT — Human Judgment Right

## Concept Definition
An indicator that checks whether tasks requiring human approval are defined and actually observed. As the fifth item of the Inclusive-Transition ESG 12 indicators, it is a basic measurement item for confirming whether an organization runs its AI transition responsibly. This indicator looks at both structure and execution — the **existence of a list** recording which tasks require human approval, and the **number of omissions** where approval was not carried out as the list requires. If there is no list, there is no standard by which to judge what counts as an omission; if there is only a list without counting the omissions, one cannot know whether the document is actually observed. Although the name carries the word "right," what this indicator measures is not a qualification but the operational facts of the list's existence and the number of approval omissions.

## Decision Criteria
The existence of a list of tasks requiring human approval and the number of approval omissions.

## Output
A list of tasks requiring approval and the number of approval omissions.
