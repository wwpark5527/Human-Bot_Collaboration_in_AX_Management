# IND_SURVEILLANCE_FEAR — Surveillance-Fear Type

## Concept Definition
A perception of the "AI keeps watching me" kind — an individual-resistance type that reacts to the datafication, logging, and tracking of an AX organization. As the fourth of the "Individual human reaction/resistance types (5 types)," it corresponds to the type in which member-level privacy anxiety is concretely manifested as the organization's data-based management expands.

## Decision Criteria
Whether it bears the features of behavior-monitoring anxiety, privacy-intrusion concern, fear of AI evaluation, and work-log stress.

## Output
It appears as resistance to productivity-tracking AI tools, aversion to keyboard/mouse-tracking systems, and rejection of AI meeting-analysis systems.

## Evidence (original quotation)
