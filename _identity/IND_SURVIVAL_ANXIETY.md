# IND_SURVIVAL_ANXIETY — Survival-Anxiety Type

## Concept Definition
An individual-resistance type that appears as a primal reaction of the "AI will replace me in the end" kind. As the first of the "Individual human reaction/resistance types (5 types)," it corresponds to the type in which the survival layer (threat to job continuity) among the four layers of human reaction is concretely manifested at the member level.

## Decision Criteria
Whether it bears the features of fear of restructuring, concern over role reduction, concern over reduced promotion opportunities, and anxiety over wage decline.

## Output
It appears as resistance to AI adoption in call centers, anxiety over document automation among office workers, and developers' unease with AI coding tools, and is manifested especially strongly among repetitive knowledge-work job groups.
