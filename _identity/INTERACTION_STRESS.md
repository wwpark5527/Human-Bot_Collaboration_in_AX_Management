# INTERACTION_STRESS — Relational (Interaction) Stress

## Concept Definition
A concept referring to the stress that arises from the relationship (interaction) between human and bot itself — more decisive than individual human stress or individual bot stress in human-bot collaboration. It is presented right after the "Human stress vs. bot stress comparison" table (6 items: nature, core cause, result, recovery mode, risk, measurement) is concluded, and emphasizes that managing the stress of each individual actor alone is not enough — the stress of the relationship itself must be handled separately.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether the observed tension arises not as a problem internal to the human or bot as an individual actor, but from the interaction between human and bot (sense of control, interpretation of intent, distribution of responsibility/authority, trust).

## Output
It produces the causal relationship whereby, when the stresses of a human feeling unable to control the bot, the bot misreading human intent, an imbalance between human responsibility and AI authority, and the collapse of human-AI trust grow, they lead to AX-adoption failure, governance collapse, increased organizational resistance, and performance decline.

## Evidence (original quotation)
