# INTERPLACE — Interplace

## Concept Definition
Interplace is an innovative HR and organization-management tool that was born on the basis of the research achievements of Dr. M. Belbin of the UK — praised worldwide as the godfather of team roles (TR) — and was then developed by Belbin Associates over decades of research. Software developed to identify TR types, it was released in 1988 and is currently at version 8 (Interplace 8); it is a set of software built to synthesize data related to individuals and jobs so as to be useful for team building, appraisal, counseling, placement, manager training, career development, and more.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether it is software developed by Belbin Associates to identify TR types that is actually used for TR/TRB measurement, and whether it has the 4-questionnaire system (SPI/OA/JRE/JOA) for that measurement.

## Output
It synthesizes individual and job data and provides 14 kinds of report content as printed output (in the language the client wants), operationally realizing the "measurement" pillar of TR/TRB diagnosis and application — corresponding to the measurement pillar among the HBRM 3M (method, meaning, measurement).
