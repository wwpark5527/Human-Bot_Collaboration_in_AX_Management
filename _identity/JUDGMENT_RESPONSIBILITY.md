# JUDGMENT_RESPONSIBILITY — Judgment Responsibility

## Concept Definition
Following the principle that AI can generate many alternatives but the final judgment must be made by a person, this is the responsibility of judging the organizational suitability, usability, and need for further approval of AI results. It is the second of the three responsibilities (context, judgment, evidence) that AX-organization talent bears when working with AI.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by: is this result correct, is it suitable for our organization, may it be released externally, may it be reflected in decision-making, and is further review or supervisor approval / legal / security review required.

## Output
It produces the final judgment on whether AI results may be used and whether approval/review is required.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "AI can generate many alternatives, but the final judgment must be made by a person. AX-organization talent must judge whether the AI result fits the organization's purpose, whether it can be used in the work, and whether further approval or review is needed: is this result correct, is this result suitable for our organization, may this result be released externally, may this result be reflected in decision-making, is further review needed, is supervisor approval or legal/security review needed."
