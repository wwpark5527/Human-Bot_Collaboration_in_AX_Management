# JUST_TRANSITION — Just Transition

## Concept Definition
A principle used in climate transition — the principle that the transition process must be fair and inclusive and that no one should be left behind. Applying this principle to AI transition yields the proposition that, just as a climate transition must not abandon workers and local communities, an AI transition must not abandon humans' roles, income, judgment rights, learning rights, and dignity. It is the source principle from which AI Inclusive-Transition ESG (Just AI Transition ESG) is derived.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether the transition process is designed to be fair and inclusive so that no one is left behind.

## Output
The just-transition principle extended into the AI era — the basis for the conclusion that "the extended ESG of the AI era can be organized as AI Inclusive-Transition ESG, that is, Just AI Transition ESG."

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "In climate transition, just transition is the principle that the transition process must be fair and inclusive and that no one should be left behind."
