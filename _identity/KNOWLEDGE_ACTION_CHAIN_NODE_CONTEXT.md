# KNOWLEDGE_ACTION_CHAIN_NODE_CONTEXT — context (organizational standard)

## Concept Definition
context (organizational standard) is the terminal node where results that have passed through review and feedback are accumulated back into the organization's standards (context). It closes the chain and becomes the input for the next execution. As the eighth and last node of the `knowledge-action chain` (S2C-0115, `KNOWLEDGE_ACTION_CHAIN`), it is located after the feedback node.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether it was reflected into the next standard, and whether the next execution becomes more refined on the basis of the accumulated execution assets.

## Output
The updated/accumulated organizational standard (the input to the next knowledge-action chain).

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "The knowledge-action chain is the operating chain of the AX era, in which knowledge becomes skill, skill becomes execution, execution becomes result, and the result is again accumulated into the organization's standards."
