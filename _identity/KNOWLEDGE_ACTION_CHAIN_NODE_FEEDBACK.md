# KNOWLEDGE_ACTION_CHAIN_NODE_FEEDBACK — feedback

## Concept Definition
feedback is the node that returns the errors and confusion revealed in review into the next standards and materials. As the seventh node of the `knowledge-action chain` (S2C-0115, `KNOWLEDGE_ACTION_CHAIN`), it is located after the review node and before the context node.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether recurring errors are reflected into the standards, whether AI errors are turned into prohibition rules, and whether confusion is organized into training material.

## Output
Updated prohibition rules and training material. Only with this process does the organization learn.

> "Recurring errors are reflected into the standards"
