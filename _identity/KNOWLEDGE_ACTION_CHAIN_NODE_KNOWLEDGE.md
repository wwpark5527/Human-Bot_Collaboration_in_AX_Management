# KNOWLEDGE_ACTION_CHAIN_NODE_KNOWLEDGE — knowledge

## Concept Definition
knowledge is the entry node of the knowledge-action chain. It is a "structure of knowing" in which data, information, and evidence are organized and connected, and it does not yet include execution or results. As the first node of the `knowledge-action chain` (S2C-0115, `KNOWLEDGE_ACTION_CHAIN`), it corresponds to the very structure of knowing that the knowledge chain (data→information→knowledge→connection) produces.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether one can answer "what do we know." Note, however, that if one stops at this stage, it is merely a structure of knowing.

## Output
The basis for judgment (organizational knowledge as a static structure).

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "The knowledge-action chain means the whole process in which knowledge leads to actual action, results, verification, and learning."
