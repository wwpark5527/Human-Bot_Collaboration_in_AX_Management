# KNOWLEDGE_ACTION_CHAIN_NODE_OUTCOME — outcome (result)

## Concept Definition
outcome (result) is the node of the change that execution has produced. What matters in the knowledge-action chain is not the deliverable itself but the change in results that the deliverable caused. As the fifth node of the `knowledge-action chain` (S2C-0115, `KNOWLEDGE_ACTION_CHAIN`), it is located after the action node and before the review node.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged not by "what was produced?" but by "what changed?"

## Output
The change in results (improved decision-making, fewer omissions, fewer customer complaints, etc.).

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "What matters in the knowledge-action chain is not the deliverable but the change in results."
