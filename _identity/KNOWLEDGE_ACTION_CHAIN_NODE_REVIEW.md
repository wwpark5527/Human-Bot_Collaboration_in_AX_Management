# KNOWLEDGE_ACTION_CHAIN_NODE_REVIEW — review

## Concept Definition
review is the node that examines execution results against organizational standards. It is the front end of the learning that keeps things from ending at execution. As the sixth node of the `knowledge-action chain` (S2C-0115, `KNOWLEDGE_ACTION_CHAIN`), it is located after the outcome node and before the feedback node.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether the result matches the facts, matches organizational standards, did not exceed authority, and whether a person confirmed accountability.

## Output
Only results that pass review become organizational standards.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Furthermore, the knowledge-action chain does not end at execution; there must be review and feedback for it to become learning."
