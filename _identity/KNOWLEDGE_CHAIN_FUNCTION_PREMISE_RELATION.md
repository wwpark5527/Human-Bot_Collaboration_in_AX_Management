# KNOWLEDGE_CHAIN_FUNCTION_PREMISE_RELATION — Premise-Relation Measurement

## Concept Definition
Premise-relation measurement is the function that checks the connection state between a higher concept and a lower concept. It detects the state of speaking about an advanced concept without understanding the concept that underlies it. As the second function of the `functions of the knowledge chain (4 major functions)` (S2C-0109, `KNOWLEDGE_CHAIN_FUNCTIONS`), it is located after structural-distance measurement.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether one actually understands the foundational concept that is the premise of an advanced expression (whether one is in a state where the words are advanced but the foundational concept is weak).

## Output
Detection of a foundational-concept deficiency. A judgment of "a state where the words are advanced but the foundational concept is weak."
