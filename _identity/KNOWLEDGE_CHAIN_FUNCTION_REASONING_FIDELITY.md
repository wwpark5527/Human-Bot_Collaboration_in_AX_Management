# KNOWLEDGE_CHAIN_FUNCTION_REASONING_FIDELITY — Reasoning-Chain Fidelity Measurement

## Concept Definition
Reasoning-chain fidelity measurement is the function that evaluates not whether the conclusion was correct but the validity of the process leading to the result. As the third function of the `functions of the knowledge chain (4 major functions)` (S2C-0109, `KNOWLEDGE_CHAIN_FUNCTIONS`), it is located after premise-relation measurement.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether the intermediate steps are valid, whether each step leads to the next, whether the last step actually supports the answer, and whether each step is connected to evidence.

## Output
The result of evaluating process validity (a judgment on the reasoning path, not the result).

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "③ Reasoning-chain fidelity measurement: the knowledge chain does not simply look at whether the conclusion was correct."
