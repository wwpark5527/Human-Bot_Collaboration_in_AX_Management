# KNOWLEDGE_CHAIN_FUNCTION_STRUCTURAL_DISTANCE — Structural-Distance Measurement

## Concept Definition
Structural-distance measurement is the function that shows how much the current concept structure differs from the target structure. It lets one confirm not only what one does not know but also what one knows incorrectly. It is the first function of the `functions of the knowledge chain (4 major functions)` (S2C-0109, `KNOWLEDGE_CHAIN_FUNCTIONS`).

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by the criteria that the more missing concepts there are, the greater the distance; the more incorrect connections, the greater the distance; and the more unnecessary connections, the greater the distance.

## Output
The structural-distance value between the current concept structure and the target structure (identification of missing concepts and misconceptions).
