# KNOWLEDGE_CHAIN_FUNCTION_TRANSFER_CAPABILITY — Transferability Measurement

## Concept Definition
Transferability measurement is the function that measures the ability to apply what one has learned to a new situation. It defines true understanding not as "the ability to solve a familiar problem" but as "the ability to maintain the meaning structure even in a new situation." As the fourth and last function of the `functions of the knowledge chain (4 major functions)` (S2C-0109, `KNOWLEDGE_CHAIN_FUNCTIONS`), it is located after reasoning-chain fidelity measurement.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether understanding is maintained even when the sentence changes, whether the principle is maintained even when the example changes, whether the structure is applied even when the domain changes, and whether the concept is revised even when a counterexample arises.

## Output
A judgment on the level of transferability (whether the meaning structure is maintained into a new situation).
