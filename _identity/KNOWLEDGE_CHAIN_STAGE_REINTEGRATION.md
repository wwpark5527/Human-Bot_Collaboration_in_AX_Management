# KNOWLEDGE_CHAIN_STAGE_REINTEGRATION — Re-reflection into Organizational Knowledge

## Concept Definition
Re-reflection into organizational knowledge is the stage that returns the recorded result back into the organization's knowledge and standards. It is the node that makes knowledge into a system that is connected, verified, and evolves. As the sixth stage of the `knowledge chain` (S2C-0108, `KNOWLEDGE_CHAIN`), it is located after result recording and before reuse by the next AI/human.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether the result is absorbed into organizational knowledge and changes the standard for the next judgment.

## Output
An updated organizational knowledge system.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "That is, knowledge becomes not 'scattered responses' but a system that is connected, verified, and evolves within the organization."
