# KNOWLEDGE_CHAIN_STAGE_REUSE — Reuse by the Next AI/Human

## Concept Definition
Reuse by the next AI/human is the stage in which the next AI or the next human draws on the accumulated organizational knowledge again. It closes the chain and completes the circulation/accumulation structure. As the seventh (last) stage of the `knowledge chain` (S2C-0108, `KNOWLEDGE_CHAIN`), it is located after re-reflection into organizational knowledge.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether the next task takes the accumulated organizational knowledge as input rather than starting over from scratch (whether consistency of results is secured).

## Output
Reused organizational knowledge and the consistent next response it produces.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "By contrast, a common/governance-context-based AX system has a circulation/accumulation structure of 'question → organizational context reference → governance validation → response generation → result recording → re-reflection into organizational knowledge → reuse by the next AI/human.'"
