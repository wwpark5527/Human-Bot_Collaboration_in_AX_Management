# KOREA_HUMAN_CENTERED_AI_ETHICS — Korean Government (Human-Centered AI Ethics Standards)

## Concept Definition
The Korean government's Human-Centered AI Ethics Standards are a set of standards announced by the Ministry of Science and ICT in 2020; they take human-centered artificial intelligence as their highest value and consist of 3 basic principles and 10 core requirements. They embody the intent that AI must not infringe on human autonomy and must contribute to the welfare of humankind. The 3 basic principles are the principle of human dignity, the principle of the public good of society, and the principle of the purposiveness of technology.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged by whether, under the intent that AI must not infringe on human autonomy and must contribute to the welfare of humankind, it has the system of 3 basic principles and 10 core requirements.

## Output
A national-level compliance standard consisting of the principle of human dignity, the principle of the public good of society, and the principle of the purposiveness of technology, together with the 10 core requirements ranging from guaranteeing human rights to explainability.
