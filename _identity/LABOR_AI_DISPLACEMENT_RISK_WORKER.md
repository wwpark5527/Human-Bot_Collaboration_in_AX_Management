# LABOR_AI_DISPLACEMENT_RISK_WORKER — AI-displacement/reduction-risk worker

## Concept Definition
A worker type placed at risk of having their job reduced or replaced by the automation of repetitive, documented work. It is the third of the four labor-differentiation types of the AI era, pointing to workers whose task value is at risk of erosion because the automation potential of their assigned work is high.

## Decision Criteria
Is the assigned work repetitive, documented work that is a target for automation?

## Output
The value of the work may fall or disappear.
