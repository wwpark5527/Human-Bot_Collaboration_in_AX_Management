# LABOR_AI_MANAGED_WORKER — AI-managed worker

## Concept Definition
A worker type placed in a position where their work is assigned, evaluated, and monitored by algorithms. It is the second of the four labor-differentiation types of the AI era, pointing to workers who are not the agents using AI but rather the objects managed and evaluated by it.

## Decision Criteria
Is the worker assigned, evaluated, and monitored by algorithms?

## Output
A state in which the standards of evaluation and assignment are hard to understand.
