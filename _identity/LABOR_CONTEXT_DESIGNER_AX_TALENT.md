# LABOR_CONTEXT_DESIGNER_AX_TALENT — Context-designer AX talent

## Concept Definition
A talent type who does not stop at merely using AI but turns AI into an operating structure of the organization by designing purposes, standards, verification, and feedback. It is the fourth and final of the four labor-differentiation types of the AI era; unlike the other three types (complemented / managed / displacement-reduction-risk), it holds a relatively leading position, actively assigning purposes and standards to AI and designing verification and feedback systems.

## Decision Criteria
Does the worker directly design purposes, standards, verification, and feedback?

## Output
An output that turns AI into an operating structure of the organization (common context, verification and feedback standards).
