# LAYER_CAPABILITY — Capability layer

## Concept Definition
The layer of human reaction that appears in the dimension where AX organizational transition threatens the relative position of one's own competence. It is the second of the four layers of human reaction (survival, capability, relationship, existence), arising not from the survival of the job itself but from the perceived threat to one's relative competence position versus colleagues and AI.

## Decision Criteria
Is the core question "Am I falling behind?"

## Output
It produces the representative emotions of inferiority and helplessness.
