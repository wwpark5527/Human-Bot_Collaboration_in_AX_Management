# LAYER_RELATIONSHIP — Relationship layer

## Concept Definition
The layer of human reaction that appears in the dimension where AX organizational transition weakens one's own position and relationships within the organization. It is the third of the four layers of human reaction (survival, capability, relationship, existence), arising from the perceived threat not to competence itself but to the weakening of one's network and position within the organization.

## Decision Criteria
Is the core question "Is my position in the organization weakening?"

## Output
It produces the representative emotions of alienation and defensiveness.
