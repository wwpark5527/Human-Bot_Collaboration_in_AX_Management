# LEAST_PREFERRED_ROLE_LEVEL — Least-preferred role (Least-preferred Role)

## Concept Definition
The role level that, no matter how hard we try and how much others help, simply cannot be manifested by each of us. It is called the least-preferred role and is the last of the three levels of team-role manifestation.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the role remains impossible to manifest even after applying both the person's maximum effort and others' support (the boundary criterion against the potential role).

## Output
It becomes the basis for a judgment that confirms a blank that cannot be resolved by managing internal members, so that the given TR is cleanly abandoned or converted into the recruitment/replacement of a member who holds that TR as a natural role.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "In the last case, it is a role that, no matter how hard we try and how much others help, simply cannot be manifested by each of us. This is called the least-preferred role."
