# LLM_GEN_FIRST_UNIVERSAL — The 1st LLM

## Concept Definition
A general-purpose LLM supplied externally by big tech, located at layer 4 of the LLM architecture. It accumulates and knowledge-izes almost all of humanity's information and provides it. It is concentrated on the ability to learn a huge corpus and generate natural, human-like sentences, and this layer is exactly what people commonly call an "LLM." To contrast existing LLMs with later generations, the text names this "the 1st LLM."

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether it concentrates on the ability to learn a huge corpus and generate natural, human-like sentences, and whether the operating agent is big tech.

## Output
It produces natural sentence generation, but in that process it also produces the limits of hallucination and security leakage.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "The 1st LLM concentrated on the ability to learn a huge corpus and generate natural, human-like sentences, and in that process there were the limits of hallucination and security leakage."
