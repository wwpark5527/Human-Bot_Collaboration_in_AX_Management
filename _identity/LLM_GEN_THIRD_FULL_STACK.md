# LLM_GEN_THIRD_FULL_STACK — The 3rd LLM

## Concept Definition
The name given to the sum of layers 1–5, fully equipped through layer 5, presented in footnote 4). Distinguished from calling the sum of layer 3 (knowledge chain) and layer 4 (the 1st LLM) the broad-sense "2nd LLM," the final state completed by adding layer 5 here is named "the 3rd LLM." Its detailed composition and content are not covered in Chapter 1 and are deferred to Chapter 8.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the architecture is fully equipped through layer 5. It is distinguished from the sum of layers 1–4 (the 2nd LLM) by the presence or absence of layer 5.

## Output
It produces the top-level completion status judgment of a full set of layers 1–5. In this chapter only the naming and composition criteria are presented; the content is deferred to Chapter 8.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "In the figure above there are only layers up to 4, but in Chapter 8 there is a figure with layer 5 added, and the sum of layers 1–5 fully equipped through layer 5 is being called 'the 3rd LLM.'"
