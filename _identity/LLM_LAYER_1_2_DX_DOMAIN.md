# LLM_LAYER_1_2_DX_DOMAIN — Layers 1–2 (the domain of DX)

## Concept Definition
The lowest two layers of the LLM architecture, the span the source text explicitly labels "the domain of DX." It is the layer where events and actions of the real world become data and that data turns into information. The LLM architecture distinguishes layers 1–2 (the domain of DX) from layers 1–4 (the domain of AX), and these two layers correspond to the foundation prior to layer 3 (the knowledge chain) being laid on top.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the layer's processing target stays at data and information (i.e., before knowledge-ization). DX handles it, and while it becomes the premise of AI execution, it cannot by itself make AI execute.

## Output
It produces information. Also, once layer 3 is formed, a reverse effect occurs in which the previously weak layers 1 and 2 beneath it are qualitatively strengthened.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "In this architecture, layers 1–2 are the domain of DX, and layers 1–4 are the domain of AX.5)"
