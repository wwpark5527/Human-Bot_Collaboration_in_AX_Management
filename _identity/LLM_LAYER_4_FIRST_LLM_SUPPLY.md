# LLM_LAYER_4_FIRST_LLM_SUPPLY — Layer 4 (the LLM already released to the world = 'the 1st LLM')

## Concept Definition
The fourth layer of the architecture, where what people commonly call an LLM is situated. For the sake of comparison, the text calls this "the 1st LLM." Big tech supplies it externally and it is already out in the world, but without layer 3 (the knowledge chain) backing it, it cannot be fully used for organizational AX.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether big tech supplies it externally and it is already out in the world. It is a general-purpose layer that accumulates, knowledge-izes, and provides almost all of humanity's information.

## Output
It produces and supplies external knowledge. However, since only part of its content is used in the human world, it carries the judgment that it is not yet sufficiently utilized.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "At present, layer 4 is already out in the world. Part of its content is used in the human world, but it is not yet sufficiently utilized. People call this an LLM; here, for the sake of comparison, we will call it 'the 1st LLM.'"
