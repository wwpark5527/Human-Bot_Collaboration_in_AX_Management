# LLM_LAYER_5_EXTENSION — Layer 5 (the layer added in Chapter 8)

## Concept Definition
The fifth layer of the LLM architecture, a layer that does not appear in the Chapter 1 body figure and is mentioned only in footnote 4). It is added as a separate figure in Chapter 8 and points to the top-level completed state when all of layers 1–5 are fully equipped. Layers 1–4 alone (the 1st LLM, the broad-sense 2nd LLM level) are still incomplete; the architecture is fully equipped only when layer 5 is added.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the architecture is fully equipped up to this layer. Layers 1–4 alone are incomplete, and it becomes a fully equipped state only when layer 5 is added.

## Output
It produces the top-level status judgment that the sum of layers 1–5, fully equipped through layer 5, is called "the 3rd LLM." The details are deferred to Chapter 8.
