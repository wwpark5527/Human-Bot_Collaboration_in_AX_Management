# MEANING_COGNITIVE_DISTANCE — Meaning/cognitive distance

## Concept Definition
Meaning/cognitive distance is the distance between the current conceptual structure and the target knowledge structure, and it is a core index introduced to evaluate the value of AI not by the quantity of its answers but by how much it has reduced this distance. The knowledge-chain-based meaning/cognitive distance is computed as the combined result of these eight factors: conceptual structure, level of mastery, belief structure, completeness of premise relations, fidelity of the reasoning chain, reliability of evidence, transferability, and epistemic maturity.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by combining the eight factors: differences in conceptual structure, differences in level of mastery, differences in belief structure, completeness of premise relations, fidelity of the reasoning chain, reliability of evidence, transferability, and epistemic maturity.

## Output
The knowledge-chain-based meaning/cognitive distance value (the combined result of the eight factors).
