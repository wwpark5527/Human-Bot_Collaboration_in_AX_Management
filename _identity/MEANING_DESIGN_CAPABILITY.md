# MEANING_DESIGN_CAPABILITY — Meaning design capability

## Concept Definition
The ability by which humans create the vision, identity, and community meaning that AI cannot make. It is the second of the five additional competencies presented in the section "Additional conditions for AX-organization talent: the competencies of a successful leader," presented as a competency needed to exercise the problem-framing capability well.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether one can grasp meaning and form relations among meanings (large and small, or of various types).

## Output
It produces the meaning system and the relations among meanings that make problem framing possible.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "To do problem framing well, meaning design capability is needed. AI creates efficiency, but vision, identity, community meaning, and the like are created by humans. Therefore a leader must have the ability to grasp meaning and to form relations among meanings (large and small, or of various types)."
