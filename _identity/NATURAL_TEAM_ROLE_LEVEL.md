# NATURAL_TEAM_ROLE_LEVEL — Team/natural role (Team/Natural Role)

## Concept Definition
Among the 9 team roles, the role level that is manifested easily and naturally by oneself when working together with others. It is called the team role or the natural role and is usually limited to 2–3 per person. It is the first of the three levels of team-role manifestation (natural role / team role · potential role / manageable role · least-preferred role).

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether, without others' help or the person's conscious effort, that behavior type is manifested easily and naturally when working alongside others.

## Output
It becomes the level that fills the reference cell of a TRB judgment. When the natural roles of all team members are gathered, if all 9 TR cells are filled, the TRB is established; if even one is empty, it is judged not established, and the current teamwork level score of that team is computed by the number of filled cells.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Among the 9 team roles, some roles are manifested easily and naturally by ourselves while we act together with others. These are called team roles or natural roles."
