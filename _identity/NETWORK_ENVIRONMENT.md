# NETWORK_ENVIRONMENT — Network environment

## Concept Definition
The network environment is the environment of the whole-organization unit in which multiple local environments connect to each other, taking as its central concept the connection context (governance context) by which multiple AI work environments become connected. It is the second of the two elements settled by the SplitSet division of `Local environment / Network environment` (S2C-0102, `LOCAL_AND_NETWORK_ENVIRONMENT`), and among the two environments that make up the operating structure of the AI era, it corresponds to the side where multiple local environments connect to each other.

## Decision Criteria
Can it answer "By what standards are multiple work environments connected and managed?" Are authority, security, verification, approval, recording, responsibility, and improvement being managed at the organizational level?

## Output
The operating network of the whole organization — that is, a network operating context in which local work contexts are connected, verified, approved, recorded, and improved.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "The governance context is the network operating context that makes those local work contexts get connected, verified, approved, recorded, and improved across the whole organization."
