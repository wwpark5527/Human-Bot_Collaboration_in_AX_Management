# OBSERVER_ASSESSMENT_OA — Observer assessment (OA: observer assessment)

## Concept Definition
One of the two questionnaires that evaluate a person; the second method of individual diagnosis, filled out by people around the measured member who know them well (four or more observers). Paired with the self-perception inventory (SPI), it forms the person-evaluation axis of the four questionnaires that Interplace uses.

## Decision Criteria
Distinguished by whether, unlike the self-diagnosis that has one respond to survey items, it is a checklist method in which one marks, from 45 positive adjectives and 27 negative adjectives, those relevant to the person being assessed.

## Output
Objective response data from people around the person (four or more observers). Combined with the person's own subjective responses, it becomes the basis for securing the reliability and validity of the measurement instrument (line 148).

## Evidence (original quotation)
