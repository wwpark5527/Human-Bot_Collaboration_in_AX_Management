# ORGANIZATIONAL_AX — Organizational AX

## Concept Definition
Organizational AX is the concept referring to the process by which an organization undergoes AX. It corresponds to the case where the unit of AX application is the "organization" and the phase of change is the "process," and it is paired with the AX organization as a result. Many individual AX efforts within an organization do not by themselves mean organizational AX is progressing or that an AX organization is being realized; organizational AX is the redesign of organizational operation itself around AI, and it differs from mere AI adoption or automation. Because an organization must decide to what level AI participates in decision-making, whether responsibility lies with humans or AI, and how inter-department roles change, organizational AX is not a technology project but an organizational-redesign project.

## Decision Criteria
Judged as organizational AX when the unit of AX application is the organization rather than the individual, and the phase of change is an ongoing process (a redesign project) rather than a completed result. It is not judged as organizational AX merely because individuals' individual AX efforts increase within the organization; it is judged by whether the organization's decision-making structure, communication structure, power structure, and cooperation structure themselves are being redesigned around AI, and whether work, data, structure, authority, evaluation, culture, and governance are being connected enterprise-wide rather than staying within some departments.

## Output
It produces the very process by which the organizational operating system is redesigned around AI. The output is the ongoing state of an organizational-redesign project in which technological innovation, organizational innovation, cultural innovation, and governance innovation happen at once, aiming at an enterprise-wide system transition that does not stop at partial optimization (adoption in some departments). The completed state of this process leads to the AX organization (the result concept).

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "In the end, organizational AX is the process by which an organization undergoes AX, and an AX organization means an organization that has, as a result, been AX-transformed."

> "Organizational AX is the redesign of organizational operation itself around AI, and it differs from mere AI adoption or automation."
