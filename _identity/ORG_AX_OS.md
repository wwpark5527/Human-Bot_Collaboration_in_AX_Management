# ORG_AX_OS — OS for organizational AX

## Concept Definition
An organizational operating system by which the organization's members — humans, AI agents, and bots — integrally operate, control, and augment work processes based on the use of external LLMs and internal organizational data. Just as a personal PC does not work without an OS even when connected to electricity and the internet, organizational AX also needs such an operating system. It is not a mere "AI platform" or "chatbot integration tool"; to be called an OS for organizational AX, it must meet minimal necessary conditions (operating protocols, privacy & sovereignty, AI governance, human-bot role architecture, agent orchestration, persistent organizational memory), and furthermore requires additional conditions that raise performance, quality, and scalability (common & governance context, knowledge chain, complementary fit engine, continuous learning, provenance & verification layer, organizational digital twin).

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether, beyond the level of a mere AI platform or chatbot integration tool, it meets at least the necessary conditions to be called an OS for organizational AX (operating protocols, privacy & sovereignty, AI governance, human-bot role architecture, agent orchestration, persistent organizational memory), and its level is further differentiated by the degree to which the additional conditions for performance, quality, and scalability (common & governance context, knowledge chain, complementary fit engine, continuous learning, provenance & verification, organizational digital twin) are satisfied.

## Output
It produces an organizational operating system by which humans, AI agents, and bots can integrally operate, control, and augment work processes, and, depending on the level of satisfaction of the necessary and additional conditions, it produces the transition from an organization that uses AI to "an organization in which AI can work."
