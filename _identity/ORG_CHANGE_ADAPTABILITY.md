# ORG_CHANGE_ADAPTABILITY — Organizational-change adaptability

## Concept Definition
The ability to adapt to change that occurs rapidly across many scopes and levels — role, authority, collaboration, evaluation, and so on — in an AX organization. It is the sixth and last of the six necessary competencies presented in the section "Necessary conditions for AX-organization talent: the competencies of surviving humans," a competency required on the premise that organizational AX is a huge, different-dimensional change.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether one is a person who refuses change or a person who adapts to organizational change.

## Output
It produces the state of remaining a valuable presence in the organization without being marginalized.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Organizational-change adaptability: as noted earlier, organizational AX is truly a huge and different-dimensional change. This means that in an AX organization, change occurs rapidly across many scopes and levels such as role, authority, collaboration, and evaluation. And since people who refuse change are increasingly likely to be marginalized, adaptability to organizational change becomes a necessary ability."
