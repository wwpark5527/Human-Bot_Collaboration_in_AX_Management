# OS_COMMON_GOVERNANCE_CONTEXT — Common context and governance context (Common & Governance context)

## Concept Definition
One of the six additional conditions of the OS for organizational AX; a component that forms and implements the organization's data (common context) and rules (governance context) within the OS. Even securing some of the additional conditions technically is very difficult, and forming and implementing this is regarded as a technical quantum jump in the AI field. Securing it goes beyond a mere AI platform to become an AI-native organizational operating system — that is, reaching the level of true AI 4.0.

## Decision Criteria
