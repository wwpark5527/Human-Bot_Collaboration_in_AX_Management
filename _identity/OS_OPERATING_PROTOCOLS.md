# OS_OPERATING_PROTOCOLS — Operating protocols & control standards

## Concept Definition
One of the six necessary conditions of the OS for organizational AX; a component that embeds into the OS for organizational AX the operating protocols by which humans and AI bots work together. Its core elements are nine: authority & approval, role allocation, access control, exception handling, logging & traceability, accountability, escalation system, human override, and audit trail. It must be an operating system that does not merely connect AI to the organization but makes AI act within the organization's norms.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether authority & approval, role allocation, access control, exception handling, logging & traceability, accountability, escalation system, human override, and audit trail are in place.

## Output
It produces an operating system that does not stop at merely connecting AI but makes AI act within the organization's norms.
