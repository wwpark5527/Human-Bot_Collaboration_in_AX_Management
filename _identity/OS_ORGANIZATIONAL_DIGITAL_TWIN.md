# OS_ORGANIZATIONAL_DIGITAL_TWIN — Organizational digital twin(ODT)

## Concept Definition
One of the six additional conditions of the OS for organizational AX; a living virtual replica of the organization that dynamically reproduces in digital space the organization's structure, roles, workflows, decision-making, collaboration, performance, and even culture. It is not a mere organization chart or ERP system but a system that simulates, predicts, and optimizes how the organization actually moves in real time or near-real time. It is needed because the organization has become too complex for human managers alone to understand its whole state, and because organizational experiments are hard to conduct directly in reality.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether it is a mere organization chart or ERP system, or whether it reproduces how the organization actually moves in real time or near-real time.

## Output
It produces simulation, prediction, and optimization of organizational operation. It resolves the problem that the organization has become too complex for human managers alone to understand its whole state and that organizational experiments are hard to conduct directly in reality.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "The Organizational digital twin (ODT) is 'a living virtual replica of the organization that dynamically reproduces in digital space the organization's structure, roles, workflows, decision-making, collaboration, performance, and even culture.' This is not a mere organization chart or ERP system, but a system that simulates, predicts, and optimizes how the organization actually moves in real time or near-real time. The reason the ODT is needed is that the organization has become too complex for human managers alone to understand its whole state, that in an AX organization one must not manage only humans, and further that, in such a situation, organizational experiments are hard to conduct directly in reality."
