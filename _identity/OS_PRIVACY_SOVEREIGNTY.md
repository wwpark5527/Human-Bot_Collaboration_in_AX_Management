# OS_PRIVACY_SOVEREIGNTY — Privacy & sovereignty

## Concept Definition
One of the six necessary conditions of the OS for an organizational AX, this component centers on leveraging external AI without losing the organization's sovereignty. Leveraging very powerful external LLMs (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) is beneficial in itself, but if there is any risk that internal information (data or context) leaks out through that connection, it must be blocked.

## Decision Criteria
Decided by whether the connection to an external LLM (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) could allow internal information (data or context) to leak out. If leakage is possible, it must be prevented.

## Output
Produces the result of capturing the benefits of using external LLMs while blocking any leakage of internal data and context — that is, the preservation of organizational sovereignty.
