# PERSONAL_AX — Personal AX

## Concept Definition
Personal AX refers to the process or act of an individual leveraging AI. When someone uses ChatGPT or Gemini to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of their studies or work, personal AX is already underway; going further, some realize a higher level of personal AX than others by leveraging AI agents or bots. Personal AX is a matter of tool use, whereas organizational AX is a matter of changing the organization's operating system — a systematic contrast.

## Decision Criteria
It is judged as personal AX when the unit of AX application is the individual, the individual can decide on their own, and it is a low-failure-cost, tool-centered use. If it stays at improving personal productivity and amounts to "I just use AI myself," it is personal AX; once it requires coordinating stakeholders, carries high failure cost, and demands standardization and governance centered on authority, roles, and culture, the judgment moves to organizational AX.

## Output
It yields the output of improving the efficiency and effectiveness of an individual's studies and work. The deliverable is a tool-use-level gain in personal productivity, and having many instances of personal AX within an organization does not automatically lead to organizational AX or to an AX organization.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Personal AX is a matter of tool use, whereas organizational AX is a matter of changing the organization's operating system."
