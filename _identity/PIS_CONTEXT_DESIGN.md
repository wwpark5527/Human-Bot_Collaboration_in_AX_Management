# PIS_CONTEXT_DESIGN — Context design

## Concept Definition
Context design is the operating condition required for the knowledge chain to be converted and developed into a world model and actually used in a real organization — a practical mediating structure that translates human purposes and criteria into AI's execution conditions. If the world model is AI's predictive capability, the knowledge chain is the supply chain for that predictive capability, and context design is the operating condition that makes that predictive capability work within human purposes and organizational criteria.

## Decision Criteria
Decided by whether it answers the core question 'for what and by what criteria will it be used,' and whether it provides the conditions for AI to operate within human purposes and organizational criteria.

## Output
AI execution conditions (purpose and criteria); an operating structure that connects technical discussion to human work and organizational operation.
