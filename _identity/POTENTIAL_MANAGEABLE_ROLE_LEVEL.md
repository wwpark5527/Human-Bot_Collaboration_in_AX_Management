# POTENTIAL_MANAGEABLE_ROLE_LEVEL — Potential/Manageable Role

## Concept Definition
A level of role that is not exercised easily and naturally, yet lies latent within a person, so that it can be exercised when the person consciously manages themselves and those around them understand and accept it. It is classified as a potential role or manageable role, and is the second of the three levels of team-role manifestation.

## Decision Criteria
Decided by whether the role can be exercised when two conditions are met — the person's conscious effort and self-management, and colleagues' understanding and acceptance (it is not exercised naturally, but neither is it impossible to exercise).

## Output
It is the basis for gauging the maximum potential teamwork level the team can reach, and it becomes a target for promotion — raised to the natural-role level through internal member management (colleagues' acceptance and encouragement plus the person's own effort) so that TRB is achieved after the fact.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Although some team roles are not exercised easily and naturally, they lie latent within us, so that if we consciously manage ourselves in order to exercise them and others understand and accept us, some can be exercised by ourselves. This is classified as a potential role or manageable role."
