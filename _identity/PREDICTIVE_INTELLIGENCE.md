# PREDICTIVE_INTELLIGENCE — AI predictive intelligence

## Concept Definition
AI predictive intelligence is the concept that defines AI's essential change as arising not from generative capability but from predictive capability. If AI is understood merely as a tool that answers questions, writes documents, generates images, and produces code, the essence of AX is missed. It is not yet generalized and is at a stage where only a very few big-tech firms are validating it, but the technologies that enable predictive intelligence are being developed and connected one after another by many companies, and through this the formation of an AI predictive intelligence system is underway.

## Decision Criteria
Decided by whether AI is seen only as a question-answering and generation tool, or as a capability that predicts future states and the results of actions. It also checks whether the technologies enabling predictive intelligence are in fact being developed and connected.

## Output
The definition of AX's essential paradigm shift from generative capability to predictive capability — the starting point for the subsequent discussion of the predictive intelligence stack (5-layer structure) and the predictive intelligence system.

## Evidence (original quotation)
