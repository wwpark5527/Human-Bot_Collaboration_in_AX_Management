# PROBLEM_FRAMING_CAPABILITY — Problem-framing capability

## Concept Definition
The human ability to define 'what the real problem is,' which AI cannot understand on its own. It is the first of the five additional competencies presented in the section 'Additional conditions for AX-organization talent: competencies of a successful leader,' and it rests on the premise that AI is strong at solving problems but that defining the problem itself is mainly the human's part.

## Decision Criteria
Decided by whether one can determine which problem must be solved, what the essence is, and which direction matters.

## Output
Produces a defined problem as a problem framer rather than an answer generator.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Problem-framing capability: AI is strong at solving problems, but 'what is the real problem?' is often something a human must define. Determining which problem to solve, what the essence is, and which direction matters is mainly the human's part. Therefore a successful person in an AX organization must be a problem framer rather than an answer generator."
