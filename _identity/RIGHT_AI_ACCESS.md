# RIGHT_AI_ACCESS — Right to AI access

## Concept Definition
The right guaranteeing the opportunity to access the AI tools and infrastructure needed for one's work. It is the first of the human protection rights of the AI era (the eight rights), and corresponds to the access stage among the eight rights that ensure humans have the social capability to understand, leverage, control, and challenge AI and to share in outcomes — rather than AI turning humans into beings to be replaced, surveilled, or excluded.

## Decision Criteria
Decided by whether the opportunity is given to access the AI tools and infrastructure needed to perform one's work.

## Output
Guaranteed access opportunity to AI tools for employees (measured by access rate and access gaps across departments).

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Right to AI access: the opportunity to access the AI tools and infrastructure needed for one's work"
