# RIGHT_AI_APPEAL — Right to AI appeal

## Concept Definition
The right to demand reconsideration of an AI evaluation, recommendation, or decision. It is the sixth of the human protection rights of the AI era (the eight rights), and corresponds to the stage that guarantees a procedural remedy — the party who, through the right to explanation, has understood the AI result and its evaluation criteria may demand reconsideration when they do not agree with that result.

## Decision Criteria
Decided by whether a procedure is open to demand reconsideration of an AI evaluation, recommendation, or decision.

## Output
A reconsideration request and the resulting correction or remedy handling.
