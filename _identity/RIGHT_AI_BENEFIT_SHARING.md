# RIGHT_AI_BENEFIT_SHARING — Right to AI benefit-sharing

## Concept Definition
The right concerning a reward structure that prevents AI-driven productivity gains from being concentrated in the hands of only a few owners. It is the eighth and last of the human protection rights of the AI era (the eight rights), and it is the terminus of the protection chain running from the right to access through the right to transition. Whereas the preceding seven rights address the conditions under which humans can understand, leverage, control, and challenge AI, the right to benefit-sharing addresses the distribution of the outcomes themselves. That is, it is the right ensuring that the productivity gains AI creates do not accrue solely to capital owners but flow back to members as compensation, education, and welfare.

## Decision Criteria
Decided by whether AI-driven productivity gains are distributed rather than concentrated in the hands of only a few owners.

## Output
A structure for reinvesting productivity gains into compensation, education, and welfare.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Right to AI benefit-sharing: a reward structure that prevents AI productivity gains from being concentrated in the hands of only a few owners"
