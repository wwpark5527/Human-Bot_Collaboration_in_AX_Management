# RIGHT_AI_EXPLANATION — Right to AI explanation

## Concept Definition
The right to be able to understand an AI result and its evaluation criteria. It is the fifth of the human protection rights of the AI era (the eight rights), and corresponds to the stage ensuring that the party can understand how the purpose, meaning, and criteria that humans provided through the right to judgment were actually reflected in the AI result.

## Decision Criteria
Decided by whether the AI result and its evaluation criteria are explained in a way the party can understand.

## Output
Provision of an explanation for the AI decision.

## Evidence (original quotation)
