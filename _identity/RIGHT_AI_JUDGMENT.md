# RIGHT_AI_JUDGMENT — Right to AI judgment

## Concept Definition
The right by which humans provide the purpose, meaning, and criteria in important judgments. It is the fourth of the human protection rights of the AI era (the eight rights), and corresponds to the stage that ensures that even when AI produces an output, what that output is for, what it means, and by what criteria it is evaluated are determined by humans and remain in the final decision.

## Decision Criteria
Decided by whether, in important judgments, humans provide the purpose, meaning, and criteria and remain in the final decision.

## Output
A list of tasks that require human approval and the judgment criteria humans provide.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Right to AI judgment: the right by which humans provide the purpose, meaning, and criteria in important judgments"
