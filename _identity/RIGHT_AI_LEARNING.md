# RIGHT_AI_LEARNING — Right to AI learning

## Concept Definition
The right guaranteeing opportunities for AI literacy, reskilling, and upskilling. It is the second of the human protection rights of the AI era (the eight rights), and corresponds to the stage that lets humans who have reached tools and infrastructure through the right to access build the capability to actually understand and handle them.

## Decision Criteria
Decided by whether AI literacy education and reskilling/upskilling opportunities are provided.

## Output
Provision of AI literacy education and reskilling/upskilling.

## Evidence (original quotation)
