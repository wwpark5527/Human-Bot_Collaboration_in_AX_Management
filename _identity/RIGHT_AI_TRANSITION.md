# RIGHT_AI_TRANSITION — Right to AI transition

## Concept Definition
The right to receive role redesign and transition training when a job changes due to the introduction of AI. It is the seventh of the human protection rights of the AI era (the eight rights), and corresponds to the stage that ensures humans can safely move to a new role when even the right to appeal cannot reverse the pressure of change on the job itself.

## Decision Criteria
Decided by whether role redesign and transition training are provided when a job changes due to the introduction of AI.

## Output
A role redesign plan, and transition training and transition placement.

## Evidence (original quotation)
