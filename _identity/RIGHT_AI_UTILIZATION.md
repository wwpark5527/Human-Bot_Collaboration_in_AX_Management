# RIGHT_AI_UTILIZATION — Right to AI utilization

## Concept Definition
The right to use AI for expanding human capability rather than for replacing repetitive work. It is the third of the human protection rights of the AI era (the eight rights), and corresponds to the stage that ensures the capability built through the right to learning is applied in actual work in the direction of human augmentation rather than human replacement.

## Decision Criteria
Decided by whether AI is used for the purpose of expanding human capability rather than replacing repetitive work.

## Output
Cases of AI use that expand human capability and the resulting improvements in work.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Right to AI utilization: the right to use AI for expanding human capability rather than for replacing repetitive work"
