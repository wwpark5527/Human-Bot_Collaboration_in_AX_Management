# ROBOT_LAW_HUMAN_SAFETY — ① Respect for (protection of) human safety and life

## Concept Definition
Respect for (protection of) human safety and life is the first of the three laws of robotics, holding that a robot must not harm a human and must not ignore a human in danger. It is the first of the three laws of robotics presented by the science fiction writer I. Asimov in the short story Runaround (1942), and is introduced as the most classic and representative principle imposing on robots the duty of human respect and safety.

## Decision Criteria
Decided by whether an action harms a human, or leaves a human in danger unattended.

## Output
Prohibition of harmful actions and a duty to intervene for a human in danger.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "① Respect for (protection of) human safety and life: a robot must not harm a human. And it must not ignore a human in danger."
