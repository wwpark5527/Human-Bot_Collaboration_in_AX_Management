# ROBOT_LAW_OBEDIENCE — ② Obedience to humans (recognition of dignity and hierarchy)

## Concept Definition
Obedience to humans (recognition of dignity and hierarchy) is the second of the three laws of robotics, holding that a robot must obey human commands as long as they do not conflict with the first law (respect for human safety and life). As the second of the most classic and representative principles imposing on robots the duty of human respect and safety, it defines obedience to human commands as a conditional duty subordinate to the first law.

## Decision Criteria
Decided by whether carrying out a command does not conflict with the first law.

## Output
Compliance with human commands within the range that does not conflict with the first law.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "② Obedience to humans (recognition of dignity and hierarchy): as long as it does not conflict with the first law, a robot must obey human commands."
