# ROBOT_LAW_SELF_PRESERVATION — ③ Robot's own self-preservation (maintenance of existence)

## Concept Definition
Robot's own self-preservation (maintenance of existence) is the third of the three laws of robotics, holding that a robot must protect itself as long as this does not conflict with the first law (respect for human safety and life) and the second law (obedience to humans). As the last of the most classic and representative principles imposing on robots the duty of human respect and safety, it defines self-preservation as a conditional duty subordinate to the two higher laws.

## Decision Criteria
Decided by whether a self-preservation action does not conflict with the first law and the second law.

## Output
Maintenance of its own existence within the range that does not conflict with the two higher laws.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "③ Robot's own self-preservation (maintenance of existence): as long as it does not conflict with the first law and the second law, a robot must protect itself."
