# ROLE_ACCOUNTABILITY_JUDGE — Accountability judge

## Concept Definition
Because ultimate responsibility for even an AI-produced result lies with the organization and its people, this is the role of judging how far an AI result may be used. It is the sixth of the eight roles of AX-organization talent. AX-organization talent must judge, according to the nature of a result, whether it is for internal reference only, whether it can be delivered to customers or reflected in decision-making, and whether it requires a superior's approval or legal/security review.

## Decision Criteria
Should this result be used for internal reference only, may it be delivered to a customer, may it be reflected in decision-making, does it require a superior's approval, does it require legal/security review, must a record be kept, and is responsibility clear if a problem arises.

## Output
A judgment on the scope of use and the locus of responsibility for an AI result.
