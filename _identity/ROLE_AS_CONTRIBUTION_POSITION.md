# ROLE_AS_CONTRIBUTION_POSITION — Role

## Concept Definition
It means not a title but who takes on which contribution position in the communication process, and it is the structure that makes that change repeatable and accountable. It is the third and last of the three concepts — work, contribution, and role — that the AI-era theory of roles redefines, the layer that fixes work and contribution into a repeatable and accountable structure.

## Decision Criteria
Is it a person's name tag (title), or a functional position that arises in the process by which meaning turns into deliverables and responsibility.

## Output
A functional position that makes contribution repeatable and accountable (ultimate responsibility remains with the human or the organization).

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Therefore, in the AI era, a role is not a person's name tag but a functional position that arises in the process by which meaning turns into deliverables and responsibility."
