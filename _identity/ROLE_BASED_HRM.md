# ROLE_BASED_HRM — RBHRM (Role-Based HRM, competence + role-based)

## Concept Definition
The major current of HR organization that existed in the mid-20th century was the shift from seniority-based to seniority-plus-competence-based (CBHRM, Competence-Based HRM). However, even though the shift to RBHRM (Role-Based HRM, competence + role-based) had already begun in the late 20th century, it was not being sufficiently utilized. This signifies a shift from control-centered to autonomy- and commitment-centered, and at the same time from position-centered to role-centered.

## Decision Criteria
Decided by whether the focus of HR management still rests on position, control, and seniority/competence (CBHRM), or has substantively shifted to role and to autonomy and commitment.

## Output
In AX organizations where the change from job-centered to role-centered is urgently needed, RBHRM is positioned as an HRM-level frame that is a necessary condition for realizing team-role balance (TRB) and performance, and it becomes the precursor concept leading to human-bot role management (HBRM) in Chapter 6.

## Evidence (original quotation)
