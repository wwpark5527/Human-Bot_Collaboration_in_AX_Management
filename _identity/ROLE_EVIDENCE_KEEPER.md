# ROLE_EVIDENCE_KEEPER — Evidence keeper

## Concept Definition
Because keeping only the result makes it impossible to confirm why that judgment was made, who reviewed it, and by what criteria it was approved, this is the role of keeping evidence of the AI-usage process. It is the seventh of the eight roles of AX-organization talent. AX-organization talent must keep as evidence the materials used, the AI instructions, the AI-generated results, the human edits, the verification criteria, the approval status, errors and exceptions, and the improvements reflected.

## Decision Criteria
Were the materials used, the AI instructions, the AI-generated results, the human edits, the verification criteria, the approval status, errors and exceptions, and the improvements reflected all kept.

## Output
Evidence that makes AX not mere automation but a reviewable and accountable way of operating the organization.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Evidence keeper: when working in an AX organization, if only the result is kept, it is impossible to confirm why that judgment was made, who reviewed it, and by what criteria it was approved. Therefore AX-organization talent must keep evidence of the AI-usage process (materials used, AI instructions, AI-generated results, human edits, verification criteria, approval status, errors and exceptions, improvements reflected). Only with evidence management does AX become not mere automation but a reviewable and accountable way of operating the organization."
