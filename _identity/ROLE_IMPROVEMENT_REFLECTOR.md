# ROLE_IMPROVEMENT_REFLECTOR — Improvement reflector

## Concept Definition
This is the role of an operations improver who reflects the errors, omissions, failures, and corrections that arise in the AI-usage process into the criteria for the next task. It is the eighth and last of the eight roles of AX-organization talent. An AX organization is not completed by a single use of AI, and AX talent must not let a failure end as an individual mistake but must improve the organization's criteria.

## Decision Criteria
Did the failure end as an individual mistake, or was it connected to improving the organization's criteria.

## Output
Organizational criteria — updated prompts, context, checklists, deliverable schemas, and so on — that are reflected into the next task.

That is, AX talent is an operations improver who does not let a failure end as an individual mistake but improves the organization's criteria. The flow of reflecting improvements proceeds in the following order."
