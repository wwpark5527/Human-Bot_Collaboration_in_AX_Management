# ROLE_PROBLEM_DEFINER — Problem definer

## Concept Definition
Because AI cannot understand problems on its own in the organization's context, this is the role of first defining the problem the organization must solve. It is the first of the eight roles of AX-organization talent, and the expression that encompasses all eight roles is 'collaboration coordinator.' Before agonizing over what to have AI do, AX talent must first define what problem the organization actually needs to solve.

## Decision Criteria
What is the problem (clarifying what is to be solved), why it matters (connection to work and performance), what result is needed (setting deliverable criteria), what constraints exist (checking security, cost, time, legal conditions), and what the success criteria are (setting performance indicators).

## Output
A problem definition with a clear target of resolution, along with deliverable and performance criteria.
