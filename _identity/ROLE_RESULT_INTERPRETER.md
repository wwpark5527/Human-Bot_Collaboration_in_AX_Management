# ROLE_RESULT_INTERPRETER — Result interpreter

## Concept Definition
Because an answer produced by AI cannot become the organization's judgment as-is, this is the role of a judge who interprets the meaning of that answer in the organizational context. It is the fourth of the eight roles of AX-organization talent. AI can produce an answer, but judging the meaning of that result in the organizational context is a human's role, so AX talent must become a judge who interprets the meaning of AI results.

## Decision Criteria
Does this result fit our organization's situation, is the core logic sound, is it feasible, is anything missing, are there any dangerous interpretations, and can it be used in the organization's decision-making.

## Output
An interpretation of the meaning of the AI result, converted into an organizational judgment.
