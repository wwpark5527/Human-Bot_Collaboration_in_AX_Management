# ROLE_VALIDATOR_OF_EIGHT — Validator

## Concept Definition
Because using AI without verification is dangerous automation, this is the role of checking results as a quality manager of AI results with verification criteria in hand. It is the fifth of the eight roles of AX-organization talent. AI results are fast but not always accurate, so AX talent must check results with defined verification criteria.

## Decision Criteria
Factuality (is there anything contrary to fact), sourcing (is the grounding clear), logic (does the reasoning connect from start to end), completeness (is any important content missing), suitability (does it fit the organization's purpose), security (is any sensitive information included), accountability (may it be reflected in the work), and traceability (are the judgment process and revision history kept).

## Output
A quality-check result that prevents dangerous automation.
