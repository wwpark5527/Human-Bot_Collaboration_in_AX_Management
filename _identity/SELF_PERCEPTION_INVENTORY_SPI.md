# SELF_PERCEPTION_INVENTORY_SPI — Self-perception inventory (SPI)

## Concept Definition
One of the two person-evaluating surveys among the four questionnaires used by Interplace, it is a self-assessment questionnaire that the person being measured completes by responding directly to the survey items.

## Decision Criteria
Distinguished by whether the respondent is the assessee themselves and whether subjective responses by the person themselves are collected through a survey-item response format.

## Output
The person's own response data for identifying an individual's TR type and level. Combined with the objective responses of the observer assessment (OA), it yields an individual diagnostic result that mixes relative and absolute evaluation.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "the two surveys that evaluate people — the self-perception inventory (SPI)"
