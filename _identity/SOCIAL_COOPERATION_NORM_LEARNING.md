# SOCIAL_COOPERATION_NORM_LEARNING — Social Cooperation Norm Learning

## Concept Definition
Social cooperation norm learning is the set of cooperation norms that AI must learn in order to become a member of a human organization driven by power, trust, tacit knowledge, politics, culture, saving face, and emotion. Following human irrationality and AI-related human stress, it is the third human characteristic a bot must understand.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the bot has learned when to speak and when to stay silent, how to explain and whom to persuade, and which expressions are threatening.

## Output
The transition from being merely an intelligent system to being a socially compatible system.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "In other words, AI must be not merely an intelligent system, but a socially compatible system."
