# SPIRIT_AUGMENTATION — Augmentation Realization

## Concept Definition
Augmentation realization is the first of the three core spirits of management for human-bot coexistence and prosperity (augmentation / respecting and leveraging diversity / pursuing complementary fit). Rather than replacing human shortcomings with bots, it realizes, as an organizational management principle, the process of mutual learning and co-evolution in which humans and bots coexist, grow each other's capabilities, and produce results together.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the bot is designed as a substitute for human labor or as an amplifier of human capability. The criterion is whether it pursues positive-sum rather than zero-sum.

## Output
Forming augmented humans (AH) and augmented bots (AB), and creating the perception that "AI strengthens me," thereby increasing the acceptability of the organization's AX.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Augmentation is not replacing human shortcomings with bots, but a process of mutual learning and co-evolution in which humans and bots coexist, grow each other's capabilities, and produce results together."

> "The three spirits—augmentation, respecting and leveraging diversity, and pursuing complementary fit—are mutually supporting, and in that all three must come together to form a complete whole, they resemble a trinity."
