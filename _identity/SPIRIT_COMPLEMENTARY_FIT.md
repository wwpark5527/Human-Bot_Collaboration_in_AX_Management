# SPIRIT_COMPLEMENTARY_FIT — Pursuing Complementary Fit

## Concept Definition
Pursuing complementary fit is a fit that pursues the needed wholeness by filling in each other's shortcomings. As the third spirit of the core management-spirit system, it stands in contrast to supplementary fit, which is unity through advancing similarity and homogeneity.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether it asks not "who is more superior?" but "how will we complement each other?" The criterion is whether it is based on role differentiation rather than competition.

## Output
Connecting the essential strengths of humans and bots to maximize the organization's overall performance, realized in four types of complementarity: cognitive, emotional, behavioral, and ethical.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Now complementary fit—which pursues the needed wholeness by filling in each other's shortcomings—is receiving the spotlight."

> "The AX organization does not force humans to act like bots, nor does it make bots merely imitate humans. Instead, it seeks to connect the essential strengths of each."
