# SPIRIT_DIVERSITY_RESPECT — Respecting and Leveraging Diversity

## Concept Definition
Respecting and leveraging diversity is the spirit that recognizes not only diversity within humans but also the essential differences between humans and bots, and leverages those differences—not as competition but as an asset for augmentation and mutual complementarity. It is the second spirit of the core management-spirit system.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether one can accurately distinguish the attributes of being different and diverging, does not rank differences as good or bad simply because they exist, and makes the fullest use of each one's existence and utility value.

## Output
Diversity is converted from a "source of conflict" into an "augmentation asset," creating higher collective intelligence and a sustainable AX organization, and making possible the emergence of the next spirit, complementary fit.
