# STEP_AI_CONTEXT_CAPITAL_BUILD — 2. Build AI Context Capital

## Concept Definition
The step of creating the context capital AI will use, by organizing purpose, criteria, sources, roles, formats, and verification standards. As the second execution step of the AI Inclusive-Transformation ESG execution model (9 steps), its ESG linkage is S / G. Whereas step 1, AI impact assessment, confirmed the impact of introducing AI, this step organizes and turns into an asset the organizational context that AI will actually reference, broken down by task. The items to be organized are six—purpose, criteria, sources, roles, formats, and verification standards—and the degree of fulfillment is measured by the per-task context-capital build rate and the refresh cycle.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether purpose, criteria, sources, roles, formats, and verification standards are organized for each task. (ESG linkage: S / G)

## Output
Organized context capital (measured by the per-task context-capital build rate and the refresh cycle).
