# STEP_AI_IMPACT_ASSESSMENT — 1. AI Impact Assessment

## Concept Definition
The first step of assessing AI's impact on labor, judgment, authority, customers, and human rights. As the first execution step of the AI Inclusive-Transformation ESG execution model (9 steps), its ESG linkage is S / G. This step requires confirming, before introducing AI, what impact that introduction will have on the organization and its people. That is, it acts as a gate that fixes the order so that the entire execution model begins not from "what will we introduce" but from "what does that introduction affect."

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the impact of introducing AI on labor, judgment, authority, customers, and human rights was assessed in advance. (ESG linkage: S / G)

## Output
The AI impact assessment result.
