# STEP_APPEAL_PROCEDURE — 6. Appeal Procedure

## Concept Definition
The step of establishing a structure for explanation, re-review, and correction requests regarding AI results. As the sixth execution step of the AI Inclusive-Transformation ESG execution model (9 steps), its ESG linkage is both S (Social) and G (Governance). After step 5 defines the human approval criteria and erects an approval gate, this step builds—as an organizational procedure—**a channel through which, even for results that have passed approval, the party concerned can demand an explanation, request a re-review, and seek a correction**. If there is only an approval structure and no appeal channel, AI results become decisions that cannot be reversed after the fact; therefore this step is a procedure that secures reversibility after approval.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether a structure exists to request explanation, re-review, and correction of AI results. (ESG linkage: S / G)

## Output
The appeal procedure and the results of processing re-reviews, corrections, and remedies.
