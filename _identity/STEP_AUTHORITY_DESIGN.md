# STEP_AUTHORITY_DESIGN — 3. Authority Design

## Concept Definition
The step of setting who can access which AI, data, and tools. As the third execution step of the AI Inclusive-Transformation ESG execution model (9 steps), its ESG linkage is G (Governance) alone. Once step 2 has organized the per-task context capital, this step sets, by actor, who can access that context capital and the AI, data, and tools. Explicitly designing access authority means documenting where authority resides and its boundaries, and this makes this step a pure governance item.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the actors permitted to access each AI, data, and tool are set. (ESG linkage: G)

## Output
The access-authority design.
