# STEP_BENEFIT_DISTRIBUTION — 8. Benefit Distribution

## Concept Definition
The step of reinvesting the AI productivity gains into education, compensation, welfare, and organizational capability. As the eighth execution step of the AI Inclusive-Transformation ESG execution model (9 steps), its ESG linkage is S (Social) alone. Whereas the preceding step 7 (audit record) preserved the history of AI work and made operations verifiable, this step **has the organization deliberately decide where the productivity gains thus confirmed flow to**. There are four reinvestment targets—education, compensation, welfare, and organizational capability. If the distribution of productivity gains is not designed, the profit naturally concentrates in a few; therefore this step fixes, as an execution procedure, the path by which the fruits of AI transformation return to the members.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the AI productivity gains are reinvested into education, compensation, welfare, and organizational capability. (ESG linkage: S)

## Output
The reinvestment record (measured by the compensation/education/welfare reinvestment rate).
