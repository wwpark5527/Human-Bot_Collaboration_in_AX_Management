# STEP_IMPROVEMENT_LOOP — 9. Improvement Loop

## Concept Definition
The step of reflecting errors and feedback into the next common context and governance criteria. As the ninth and final execution step of the AI Inclusive-Transformation ESG execution model (9 steps), its ESG linkage is G (Governance) alone. The errors that surface while operating the preceding eight steps, and the members' feedback, are by themselves merely one-off events. This step **feeds them back in as criteria for the next cycle, turning the 9-step model from a procedure run once into a circulating loop**. There are two reflection targets—the common context built in step 2 (purpose, criteria, sources, roles, formats, verification standards), and the governance criteria running through steps 3–7 (authority, approval, appeal, records). If they are not reflected, the same error repeats in the next cycle.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether confirmed errors and feedback are reflected into the next common context and governance criteria. (ESG linkage: G)

## Output
The updated common context and governance criteria.
