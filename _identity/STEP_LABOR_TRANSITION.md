# STEP_LABOR_TRANSITION — 4. Labor Transition

## Concept Definition
The step of designing job impact assessment, reskilling, and role reconfiguration. As the fourth execution step of the AI Inclusive-Transformation ESG execution model (9 steps), its ESG linkage is S (Social) alone. Once step 3 has designed access authority for AI, data, and tools, this step assesses **before** introduction the impact that AI introduction has on people's jobs, and designs the reskilling and role reconfiguration that follow from it. This identifier is an **execution step** of the 9-step operating model, and is a separate node at a different level from the "labor transition indicator," a measurement item of the Inclusive-Transformation ESG 12 indicators.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether a job impact assessment was conducted before AI introduction and reskilling and role reconfiguration were designed. (ESG linkage: S)

## Output
The job impact assessment result, the reskilling plan, and the reconfigured roles (measured by the transition-placement rate).
