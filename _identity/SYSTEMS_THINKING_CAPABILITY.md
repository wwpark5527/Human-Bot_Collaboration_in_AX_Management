# SYSTEMS_THINKING_CAPABILITY — Systems Thinking

## Concept Definition
Systems thinking is the way of seeing the AX organization as a complex system in which humans, AI agents, governance, workflow, culture, ESG, security, and context are connected. It is needed even by leaders of human-only organizations, but is demanded all the more in an AX organization, and a successful leader must possess the integrative judgment to see the whole rather than the parts.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether one stays at partial optimization, or integrates value conflicts, ethics, long-term direction, organizational culture, and social ripple effects into a whole to make judgments.

## Output
It produces not the generation of correct answers but the determination of direction.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Systems thinking: Systems thinking is needed in the competency of a leader of a human-only organization too, but it is needed all the more because an AX organization is a complex system in which 'humans, AI agents, governance, workflow, culture, ESG, security, and context' are connected. Furthermore, a successful leader must possess the integrative judgment to see the whole rather than the parts. AI is strong at partial optimization. But integrative judgment on value conflicts, ethics, long-term direction, organizational culture, and social ripple effects is the human's part. In an AX organization in particular, judgment on AI governance, accountability, trust, and human-centeredness is important. The core is precisely the determination of direction, not the generation of correct answers."
