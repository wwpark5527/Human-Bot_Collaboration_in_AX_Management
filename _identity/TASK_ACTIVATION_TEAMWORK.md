# TASK_ACTIVATION_TEAMWORK — Task-Activation Aspect of Teamwork

## Concept Definition
An aspect of teamwork, advocated by the author (Park Won-woo), as another factor that acts on the actual improvement of team performance and is not explained by human bonding alone. It is concretized as the state in which all 9 team roles are easily and well exercised within the team (line 112).

## Decision Criteria
Measured by TRB (Team Role Balance). This aspect, which had remained a conceptual definition, became measurable through the operational definition of Belbin's team role balance (footnote 35), and on the fit dimension it corresponds to complementary fit (line 122).

## Output
It has a significant effect on a department's actual performance as evaluated by the company, but does not have a significant effect on the members' perceived performance. It is positioned as a short- and mid-term necessary condition that directly affects actual performance (lines 180-184).

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Of course the 'human bonding' aspect is necessary, but thinking that yet another factor besides it acts on the actual improvement of team performance, he advocated the 'task-activation' aspect of teamwork, and thereafter proved that a department's teamwork, and further its actual performance, becomes highest when both of these aspects of teamwork exist together."
