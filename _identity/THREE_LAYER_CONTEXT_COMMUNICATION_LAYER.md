# THREE_LAYER_CONTEXT_COMMUNICATION_LAYER — Communication Context

## Concept Definition
The communication context is the layer of operating criteria for the augmented human's external relationships. It determines how the augmented human relates to the outside, and is the core connecting concept placed between the common context and the governance context.

## Decision Criteria
Who is the speaker, in what role did the AI intervene, is content written by the AI distinguished from human judgment, is the counterpart's utterance recorded and analyzed, how far may the message be shared, and did a human approve important decisions.

## Output
The communication design of the augmented human–other relationship (the connection to the outside).

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Communication context = the operating criteria for the augmented human's external relationships"
