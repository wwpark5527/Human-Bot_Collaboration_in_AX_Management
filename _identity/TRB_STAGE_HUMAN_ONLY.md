# TRB_STAGE_HUMAN_ONLY — Human-only TRB

## Concept Definition
The starting stage of the evolution path—a human-centered TRB that deals only with role balance among humans (human ↔ human). It is the first stage of the TRB evolution path (Human-only → Bot-aided → Human-bot coupled → Autonomous hybrid TRB).

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether the object of role balance is limited to a human + human team and no bot has entered as a role actor.

## Output
Human role balance is produced, characterized by a job-based organization, static roles, and individual capability.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "From an evolutionary standpoint, most organizations are highly likely to evolve through the stages of 'Human-only TRB → Bot-aided TRB → Human-bot coupled TRB'"
