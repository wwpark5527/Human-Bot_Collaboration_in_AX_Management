# UNESCO_AI_ETHICS_RECOMMENDATION — UNESCO's Recommendation on the Ethics of AI (2021)

## Concept Definition
UNESCO's Recommendation on the Ethics of AI is the first global AI ethics norm, adopted unanimously by 193 member states at the 2021 General Conference; based on international human rights law, it is composed of 4 values and 10 principles. The 4 values are respecting, protecting, and promoting human rights, fundamental freedoms, and human dignity; the flourishing of the environment and ecosystems; ensuring diversity and inclusiveness; and building peaceful, just, and interconnected societies. As the first comprehensive international AI-ethics norm in the UN family, it carries great symbolism and authority.

## Decision Criteria
Judged by whether, based on international human rights law, it places emphasis on resolving global inequality and protecting the environment, and is equipped with the system of 4 values and 10 principles.

## Output
An international benchmark that is non-binding but specifies concrete policy-action areas such as education, culture, gender equality, and governance, and induces countries to reform their legal systems and report on implementation.
