# WORK_AS_PERFORMED_ACT — Work

## Concept Definition
The layer that points to the act itself of "having done something," without asking whether that act produced a change. It is the first of the three concepts—work, contribution, role—that the AI-era role theory redefines, and it is the most basic layer, distinct from contribution and role.

## Decision Criteria
It considers only whether the act was performed (the AI's writing a sentence or summarizing material, in itself).

## Output
The fact of performance — a record of the act, independent of whether it led to a change.

## Evidence (original quotation)
> "Work is the act of 'having done something,' contribution is the valid change that act produced, and role is the structure that makes that change repeatable and accountable. The AI's writing a sentence is work."
