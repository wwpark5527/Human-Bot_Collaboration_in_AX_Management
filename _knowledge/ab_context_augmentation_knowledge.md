# Context Augmentation — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Context augmentation is the first of the three augmentation dimensions of an augmented bot (AB) (context augmentation, governance augmentation, learning augmentation).
- An augmented bot was defined in Chapter 1 as "a collaborative AI that embeds common and governance context together with AI governance and performs its role in line with the organization's purpose and standards."
- Whereas an ordinary bot (B) is function-centered and command-executing, an augmented bot (AB) is distinguished by being role-centered and context-based in its performance.
- When an organizational AX OS with common and governance context built in is used, the bot's context augmentation occurs naturally, without individual members having to make a separate effort to inform it.
