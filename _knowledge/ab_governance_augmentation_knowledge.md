# Governance Augmentation — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Governance augmentation is the second of the three augmentation dimensions of an augmented bot (AB) (context augmentation, governance augmentation, learning augmentation).
- Comparing an ordinary bot (B) with an augmented bot (AB), the ordinary bot is automation-centered, has a weak accountability structure, and is an individual tool, whereas the augmented bot is distinguished as collaboration-centered, operating within a governance structure, and an organizational-member-type AI.
- Whereas the ordinary bot focuses on "what to do," the augmented bot also includes "within what standards and accountability structure to do it" — therefore an augmented bot is not a simple automation tool but a collaborative AI that has organizational context.
