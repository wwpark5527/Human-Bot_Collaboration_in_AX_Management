# Collaboration between Augmented Humans and Augmented Bots — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Collaboration between augmented humans and augmented bots is one of the book's central themes, a role-based collaboration structure in which a human's purpose, judgment, and accountability are connected with a bot's analysis, execution, and recording capabilities within common context and governance context.
- The core of the collaboration is complementation, not replacement — if a human treats a bot as merely a simple tool, the bot's potential cannot be fully used, and if a human hands even their own judgment over to the bot, accountability and agency weaken.
- The three conditions for the collaboration to work properly: ① sharing the same context (common context), ② collaborating within an accountability structure (governance context), ③ dividing roles while augmenting each other.
- The augmented human gives the bot purpose and standards, and the augmented bot shows the human structure and possibility. Common context lets the two work by the same standards, and governance context makes that collaboration operate safely and accountably.
- In an organization where this collaboration takes place, purpose, standards, materials, judgment, revision, approval, and recording become connected, so the deliverable becomes an organizational asset that can be used again in the next task. This is the important change that an AX organization brings about.
