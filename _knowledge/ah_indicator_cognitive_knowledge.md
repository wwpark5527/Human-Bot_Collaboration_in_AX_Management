# Cognitive Augmentation (Cognitive A.) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Cognitive augmentation (Cognitive A.) is the first of the five AH measurement indicators, an indicator that answers the question "Does AI broaden my range of thinking (cognition)?"
- The measures are made concrete as three items: the number of alternatives generated, the number of variables considered, and thinking depth / cognitive speed.
- The five AH indicators are composed in the order of cognitive augmentation, decision augmentation, learning augmentation, collaboration augmentation, and role augmentation, and cognitive augmentation, as their starting point, deals with the expansion of thinking capacity that underlies the other four indicators (decision, learning, collaboration, role).
- Within the definition of an augmented human ("the human's purpose, judgment, and accountability + AI's analysis, generation, and recording capabilities + the standard structure of common context"), cognitive augmentation in particular measures the point at which AI's analysis and generation capabilities combine with the human's range of thinking.
