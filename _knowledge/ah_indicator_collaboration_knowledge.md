# Collaboration Augmentation (Collaboration A.) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Collaboration augmentation (Collaboration A.) is the fourth of the five AH measurement indicators, an indicator that answers the question "Do you naturally divide roles with AI?"
- The measures are made concrete as two items: the proportion of delegable work and the level of agent usage.
- In the order of the five AH indicators (cognitive augmentation → decision augmentation → learning augmentation → collaboration augmentation → role augmentation), collaboration augmentation is the fourth stage, which confirms whether improvements in the preceding three indicators (thinking, judgment, learning) lead to an actual division of work with AI, and it is the stage immediately before the last indicator, role augmentation (the final criterion).
- It is conceptually connected with HBRM's role of designing collaboration structures (building a collaboration structure in which humans and bots can work together); collaboration augmentation deals with the degree of role division with AI at the individual level, while collaboration-structure design deals with the role of designing that structure at the organizational level.
