# Decision Augmentation (Decision A.) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Decision augmentation (Decision A.) is the second of the five AH measurement indicators, an indicator that answers the question "Did decision quality improve after using AI?"
- The measures are made concrete as three items: decision accuracy, error-reduction rate, and prediction success rate.
- In the order of the five AH indicators (cognitive augmentation → decision augmentation → learning augmentation → collaboration augmentation → role augmentation), decision augmentation is the second stage, which confirms whether the range of thinking broadened by cognitive augmentation converts into the quality of actual decisions.
- Among the three powers of an augmented human (the power to set purpose, the power to establish standards, and the power to make the final judgment and bear accountability), it is the indicator directly connected in particular with "the power to make the final judgment and bear accountability."
