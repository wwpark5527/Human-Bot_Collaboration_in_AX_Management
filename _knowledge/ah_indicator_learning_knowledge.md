# Learning Augmentation (Learning A.) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Learning augmentation (Learning A.) is the third of the five AH measurement indicators, an indicator that answers the question "Did learning speed become faster?"
- The measures are made concrete as two items: reduced learning time and an increased amount of knowledge acquired.
- In the order of the five AH indicators (cognitive augmentation → decision augmentation → learning augmentation → collaboration augmentation → role augmentation), learning augmentation is the third stage, which confirms whether improvements in the preceding two indicators (range of thinking, decision quality) lead to continuous capability growth.
- It is conceptually connected with learning and improvement management (accumulating feedback and errors as organizational memory) among HBRM's eight roles; learning augmentation deals with learning speed at the individual level, while learning and improvement management deals with learning accumulation at the organizational level.
