# Role Augmentation (Role A.) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Role augmentation (Role A.) is the fifth and last of the five AH measurement indicators, an indicator that answers the question "Do you perform a new role because of AI?"
- The output is made concrete as specific cases of role expansion such as researcher → researcher + designer, manager → manager + orchestrator, and professor → professor + AI coach.
- From the organizational AX perspective, the final criterion for judging AH is "Can you now perform a role you could not perform before using AI?", that is, it is reasonable to measure whether someone is an AH by role expansion rather than by an increase in productivity.
- The stage at which 'role augmentation' has occurred corresponds, among the human augmentation stages (H0 to AH3), to AH2 (AI orchestrator) and further to AH3 (human-AI symbiotic leader), which is the final goal of human augmentation.
