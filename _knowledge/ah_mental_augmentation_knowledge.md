# Mental Augmentation — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Mental augmentation is the second of the three augmentation dimensions that make up the augmented human (AH) (physical augmentation, mental augmentation, role and context augmentation), and it is positioned immediately after physical augmentation.
- The target capacities are specified as five items: thinking, learning, analysis, creativity, and judgment.
- As with physical augmentation, mental augmentation too is assumed to be already understood by the reader, so its explanation is omitted, and the book's explanation concentrates on the role and context augmentation that appears for the first time thereafter.
