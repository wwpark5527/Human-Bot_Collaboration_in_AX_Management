# Physical Augmentation — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Physical augmentation is the first of the three augmentation dimensions that make up the augmented human (AH) (physical augmentation, mental augmentation, role and context augmentation).
- The book assumes that the reader already understands physical augmentation and mental augmentation and omits their explanation, concentrating on the explanation of role and context augmentation, which appears for the first time — that is, physical augmentation is positioned as the most antecedent and basic of the three dimensions.
- An augmented human is defined as "the human's purpose, judgment, and accountability + AI's analysis, generation, and recording capabilities + the standard structure of common context," and physical augmentation plays the role of complementing with technology the axis of physical performance capability that the human possesses.
