# Role/Context Augmentation — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Role/context augmentation is the third of the augmented human's (AH) three augmentation dimensions (physical, mental, role/context), and it is the dimension explained in detail for the first time in the book — physical and mental augmentation are omitted on the assumption that the reader already understands them.
- Role/context augmentation is especially important in an AX organization, since it means a state in which, beyond merely doing work quickly, AI can be used within the organization's purpose, standards, and accountability system.
- When role/context augmentation is achieved, one comes to hold three powers: the power to set purpose, the power to establish standards, and the power to take on judgment and accountability — this connects with the augmented human's capability of first making purpose and meaning clear before entrusting work to AI.
