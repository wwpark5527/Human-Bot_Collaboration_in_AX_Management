# AI-Based Stratification (AI Class Society / AI-Based Stratification) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- AI-based stratification (`AI_BASED_STRATIFICATION`, S2C-0133) is the central problem-concept that Chapter 8 addresses, the object to which the book's solutions (inclusive transformation AX and others) respond.
- The author uses "class" and "stratum" interchangeably (footnote 61) and also uses the aliases AI class society and AI-based stratification.
- The risk AI brings is not a simple loss of jobs but a new class structure created by the accumulation of differences in access rights, utilization capability, judgment authority, ownership, and profit-sharing.
- International organizations and research institutes already warn of the same risk under concepts such as the AI divide, the AI readiness gap, algorithmic management, labor transition, business and human rights, and responsible AI (footnote 61).
- The diagnosis that whether one holds tokens (the minimum unit of information processing) can become power beyond assets (footnote 62) is presented together with the concern that, should subscription pricing shift to usage-based charging, an "AI stratification" in which access rights and usage rights diverge according to the presence or absence of money could be realized.
- This problem is made concrete in the "seven gaps that create AI-based stratification" that follow (access, capability, context, judgment authority, surveillance, ownership, profit-sharing).
