# AI 1.0 (Perceptional AI) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The AI generational classification is organized as AI 1.0–4.0 in correspondence with the developmental flow of Web 1.0–4.0, and because it is not a standardized definition, the classification criteria may differ from source to source.
- AI 1.0 (Perceptional AI) is perception/recognition-type AI, remaining at the function of perceiving, identifying, and classifying external input.
- AI 2.0 leads to Generative AI, AI 3.0 to Agentic AI, and AI 4.0 to Organizational AI, and this book's domain is located at AI 4.0 (Organizational AI).
- Perceptional AI is distinguished from the later generations in that it has no generative, autonomous-execution, or organization-operating function.
