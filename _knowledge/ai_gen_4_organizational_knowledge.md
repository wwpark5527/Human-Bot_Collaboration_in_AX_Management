# AI 4.0 (Organizational AI) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- In the AI generational-classification table, AI 4.0 is defined as 'Organizational AI', and it is the target domain this book addresses.
- Unlike AI 3.0 (Agentic AI), which remains at the individual-agent unit, in AI 4.0 the operating unit expands to the operation of the whole organization.
- It is regarded as having reached the 'true AI 4.0 level' when common context and governance context are formed and implemented so that it becomes not a mere AI platform but an AI-native organizational operating system.
- Because it is not a standardized definition, the classification criteria may differ slightly from source to source, and the development of AI itself (discussions of AGI/ASI and the like) is treated separately in Chapter 8.
