# AI Governance — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- AI governance (A) and governance context (B) are concepts frequently confused in organizational AX, but they are concepts at different levels. A is the norms, institutions, and operating system concerning "how to control, manage, and supervise AI," while B is the context system that provides "on what basis AI should judge and act" (line 242).
- A is essentially the creation of the order of AI use, whereas B is the operating context AI must reference when acting within the organization (line 244).
- Analogy: A resembles the institutions of a state — prohibition rules, accountability rules, an audit system, an approval system, like laws/a constitution. B is a collective context, like social culture, custom, and situational knowledge, that makes AI behave in a way befitting a particular organization (lines 244-246).
- A is the system that makes B operate within real organizational operation. If B is the standard of the network environment, A is the way that standard is executed as work procedures, roles, systems, and recording structures. The core is not prohibition but operation, not surveillance but standardization, not after-the-fact audit but connected operation (line 248).
