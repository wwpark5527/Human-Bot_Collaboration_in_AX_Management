# Augmentation User — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The Augmentation User is the last stage of the four-stage evolution ladder defined in the measurement of AI Utilization (AIU) (Tool User → Collaborative User → Orchestrator → Augmentation User).
- Its characteristic is "AI operates as if it were part of oneself, and AI augments memory, analysis, and creativity," and its indicators are improved decision quality, increased learning speed, and increased productivity.
- The evaluation question is "After using AI, have I become a different person from my earlier self?", and it is the final stage after the Orchestrator (work-process design).
- A measurement is appropriate as AI-utilization measurement only if it reflects the characteristics and indicators of the three stages up to the Augmentation User (Tool User, Collaborative User, Orchestrator), and the formula for measuring AI utilization is "AI Utilization = f(Frequency + Depth + Automation + Outcome)."
