# Collaborative User — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The Collaborative User is the second stage of the four-stage evolution ladder defined in the measurement of AI Utilization (AIU) (Tool User → Collaborative User → Orchestrator → Augmentation User).
- Its characteristic is "repeated conversation with AI, revision of results, exploration of alternatives," and its indicators are the number of prompt improvements, the degree to which AI feedback is incorporated, and the number of repeated interactions with AI.
- The evaluation question is "Does AI expand my thinking?", and as the stage after the Tool User (centered on asking and copying) it leads to the next stage, the AI Orchestrator.
- The level of AI utilization means the degree of evolution in the process of moving from H0 to H1 and H2 and eventually becoming an augmented human (AH), and the Collaborative User corresponds to the interaction stage that goes beyond instrumental use.
