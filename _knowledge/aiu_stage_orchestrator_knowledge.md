# AI Orchestrator — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The AI Orchestrator is the third stage of the four-stage evolution ladder defined in the measurement of AI Utilization (AIU) (Tool User → Collaborative User → Orchestrator → Augmentation User).
- Its characteristic is "using multiple AIs, using agents, designing work processes," and its indicators are the proportion of work handled by AI, the number of agents, and the level of automation.
- The evaluation question is "Do I do the work directly, or do I design so that AIs do the work?", and as the stage after the Collaborative User (repeated conversation, revision of results) it leads to the next stage, the Augmentation User.
- The level of automation is distinguished from level 0 (humans perform 100% without AI) to level 4 (full automation, human-on-the-loop), and the AI Orchestrator is in the position of leading such automation design.
