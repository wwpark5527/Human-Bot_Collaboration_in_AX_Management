# AI Intervention Transparency — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- AI intervention transparency is `S2C-0407`, the third element generated from the SplitSet fragmentation of ARBI's ten evaluation axes (`S2C-0098`, `ARBI_TEN_AXES`).
- ARBI does not judge AI use itself as good or bad. - The core question of the AI intervention transparency axis is "Was it revealed where and how AI intervened?", and if the intervention is not revealed it leads to the risk of concealed AI intervention.
