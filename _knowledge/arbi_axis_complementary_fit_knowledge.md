# Complementary Fit — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Complementary Fit is, as `S2C-0406`, the second element generated by the SplitSet division of ARBI's ten evaluation axes (`S2C-0098`, `ARBI_TEN_AXES`).
- ARBI evaluates whether AI has made the role order of the collaboration healthy, and among those concerns the Complementary Fit axis looks at whether the respective strengths of the human and the AI operate as a structure that fills in for each other.
- ARBI (Chapter 6) contrasts with AHCI (the capability indicator of the augmented individual) treated in Chapter 4. Whereas AHCI looks at how much an individual has been augmented, ARBI looks at whether the strengths of such an augmented human are complemented fairly and responsibly when collaborating with others and with AI.
- The core question of the Complementary Fit axis is "Do human strengths and AI strengths complement each other?"
