# Manipulation Risk — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Manipulation Risk is, as `S2C-0414`, the tenth and final element generated by the SplitSet division of ARBI's ten evaluation axes (`S2C-0098`, `ARBI_TEN_AXES`).
- ARBI is an indicator that asks whether AI has made the role order of the collaboration healthy or has instead deepened role imbalance and confusion of responsibility, and this axis addresses whether that damage originated in deliberate misuse.
