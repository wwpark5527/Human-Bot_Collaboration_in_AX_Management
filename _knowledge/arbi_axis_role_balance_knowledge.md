# Role Balance — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Role Balance is, as `S2C-0405`, the first element generated by the SplitSet division of ARBI's ten evaluation axes (`S2C-0098`, `ARBI_TEN_AXES`).
- ARBI's core object of evaluation is not the relationship between one individual and one AI but a collaboration network in which multiple humans and multiple AIs participate together (for example, the case where one person analyzes material with AI and speaks in a meeting, or the case where in customer service AI drafts the reply and a human delivers it).
- ARBI does not judge the use of AI itself as good or bad. What matters is whether AI has made the role order of the collaboration healthy or has instead deepened role imbalance and confusion of responsibility.
- The core question of the Role Balance axis is "Have the needed roles been assigned to fit the situation?", and being the axis placed first among ARBI's ten axes it corresponds to the basic diagnosis of the collaboration structure.
