# Augmented Role Balance Index (ARBI, Augmented Role Balance Index) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- ARBI is `S2C-0097`, a single candidate confirmed as KEEP without SplitSet fragmentation, and it is the central indicator of Chapter 6.
- The existing TRB (Team Role Balance) looks at how roles are arranged within a human team. ARBI, by contrast, looks at role balance in the era in which AI has entered collaboration.
- The questions ARBI seeks to answer are as follows: What did the human take charge of? How far did AI intervene? Is this utterance a human's judgment, AI's generation, or a joint output of human and AI? Did AI have the authority to access the material and role in question? Do the final judgment and responsibility remain with the human? Has the AI-utilization gap not led to unfairness in communication? Can the collaboration process be recorded and verified again?
- ARBI is an index that measures together role balance, utterance agency, authority boundaries, human accountability, communication fairness, and recordability in AI-intervened human-human collaboration. It is an indicator that looks at augmented relations and collaboration, in contrast to the AHCI (an indicator of the augmented individual's capability) treated in Chapter 4.
- ARBI is likened to "a collaboration health checkup of the AI era" — just as a person's health checkup looks not only at weight but also at blood pressure, blood sugar, heart, liver, stress, and lifestyle habits, collaboration that AI has entered must also be examined along several axes, not productivity alone.
