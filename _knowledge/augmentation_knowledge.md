# Augmentation — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Augmentation is not replacement but a process of mutual learning and co-evolution, and it is the first of the core spirits of AX organization management (augmentation / respect for and use of diversity / pursuit of complementary fit).
- Humans are strong at understanding context, judging value, ethical responsibility, and forming relationships, while bots are strong at information processing, repeated execution, pattern detection, and simulation, so the design must amplify each other's strengths.
- The augmented human (AH) is a concept that goes beyond a human who uses AI well, extending to a human combined with technologies that overcome and extend physical limits (a cyborg being one such case).
- The augmented bot (AB) receives a transplant of human knowledge, values, rules, and supervisory systems, and as a core member of the AX organization takes on most of the substantive productivity.
- Only when the concept of augmentation is present can 'human centrality' hold without falling into zero-sum thinking (the notion that humans must always come first and bots must be sacrificed).

## Source Evidence Quotation
> "Augmentation is not the replacement of human shortcomings by bots, but a process of mutual learning and co-evolution in which humans and bots coexist, grow each other's capabilities, and produce results together."

> "Because the spirit of augmentation aims at human extension rather than human elimination, 'human centrality' also becomes possible only when the concept of augmentation is present."
