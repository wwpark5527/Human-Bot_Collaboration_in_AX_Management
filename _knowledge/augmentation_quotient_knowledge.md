# Augmentation Quotient (AQ) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- AQ (the Augmentation Quotient) is a concept higher than AIU (AI Utilization); it measures not usage volume but "the ability to collaborate with AI."
- Formula: AQ = AIU x Role Alignment x Collaboration x Governance — the degree of role-centered use, the level of human-bot collaboration, and the level of ethics/security/approval compliance are reflected as a product on top of AI utilization.
- AIU measures usage, AQ measures augmentation capability (potential), and AHI measures the augmentation result (realization) — by analogy with a car, AIU corresponds to how often one drives, AQ to driving skill, and AHI to the actual level of a racer.
- Even with the same AIU (for example, the same use of ChatGPT), if one person only does simple searches while another carries out problem definition, role design, prompt design, result verification, and AI collaboration, their AQ differs greatly.
- AQ is the second stage of the AIU → AQ → AHI three-stage measurement system that distinguishes "a person who uses AI a lot" from "a person who works together with AI and is truly augmented."
