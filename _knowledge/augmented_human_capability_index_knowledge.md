# Augmented Human Capability Index (AHCI) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- AHCI (the Augmented Human Capability Index) is an indicator that evaluates AH not as "the person themselves" but as "the human's capability."
- Formula: AHCI = human augmentation degree + bot cooperation degree + human-bot alignment degree + output fidelity + rate of improvement.
- Whereas AHI is centered on state, the person themselves, and gradability, AHCI is distinguished as centered on capability, performance ability, growth, and development (the source comparison table uses four axes: meaning/focus/object of evaluation/perception).
- The purpose of AHCI's evaluation is to check whether one can collaborate with AI, whether one can verify results, whether one can make responsible judgments, and whether one can use it in line with organizational standards, and these are all capability domains that connect naturally with the HBRM system.
- AHCI is not a simple score but a diagnostic structure that jointly examines whether the human is moving closer to AH, whether the bot is moving closer to BH, whether human and bot are aligning, whether the output is faithful and evidence-based, and whether the relationship improves over time, and it is measurable with the Belbin Korea tools.
