# Augmented Human Index (AHI) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- AHI (the Augmented Human Index) is the final stage of the AIU → AQ → AHI three-stage measurement system, and it judges "whether that person is actually an AH (augmented human)."
- Formula: AHI = f(Performance, Learning, Influence, Innovation, Adaptability) — a function of performance, learning speed, influence, innovativeness, and adaptability.
- AQ is augmentation potential and AHI is augmentation realization — in the car analogy, AQ corresponds to driving skill and AHI to the level of an actual racer.
- Someone who uses AI a lot but cannot make judgments and, with reduced creativity, depends on AI is merely an AI User, not an AH. Conversely, if using AI produces increased problem-solving ability, increased learning speed, improved decision-making, and improved collaboration ability, that person becomes an AH.
- It is a three-stage system in which AIU measures usage, AQ measures augmentation capability, and AHI measures the augmentation result.
