# AI-Centered Path — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The AI-Centered Path (`AUGMENTED_PATH_AI_CENTERED`, S2C-0461) is the second SplitSet child of `the three augmented-human communication paths` (S2C-0127, `AUGMENTED_COMMUNICATION_PATHS`).
- Its structure is 'Human A ↔ AI A ↔ AI B ↔ Human B', and the AI relays and structures messages.
- The human provides only the purpose and the approval criteria, and the AI handles communication quickly in the middle.
- This approach is efficient, but it carries the risk that the human's original intent can change during the AI's summarizing and restructuring.
- Among the three augmented-human communication paths, the AI-centered path is effective for 'processing efficiency' (the human-centered path is effective for meaning preservation and the via-governance path for accountability establishment).
