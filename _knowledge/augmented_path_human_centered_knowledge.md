# Human-Centered Path — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The Human-Centered Path (`AUGMENTED_PATH_HUMAN_CENTERED`, S2C-0460) is the first SplitSet child of `the three augmented-human communication paths` (S2C-0127, `AUGMENTED_COMMUNICATION_PATHS`).
- Its structure is 'AI A ↔ Human A ↔ Human B ↔ AI B', and the human speaks directly and judges directly.
- The AI performs only the auxiliary role of helping in the background with organizing, analyzing, reviewing, and drafting.
- This approach best preserves human meaning and emotion and trust relationships, but its speed and level of automation are low.
- Among the three augmented-human communication paths, the human-centered path is effective for 'meaning preservation' (the AI-centered path is effective for processing efficiency and the via-governance path for accountability establishment).
