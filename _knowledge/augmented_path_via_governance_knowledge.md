# Via-Governance Path — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The Via-Governance Path (`AUGMENTED_PATH_VIA_GOVERNANCE`, S2C-0462) is the third SplitSet child of `the three augmented-human communication paths` (S2C-0127, `AUGMENTED_COMMUNICATION_PATHS`).
- Its structure is 'Human A ↔ AI A ↔ governance context ↔ AI B ↔ Human B', and the message is not delivered directly but passes through the governance context.
- The message must pass through the criteria of authority, security, verification, approval, recording, and accountability.
- It can be slow, but it is essential in domains where accountability matters, such as organizations, contracts, legal, finance, public sector, healthcare, and education.
- Among the three augmented-human communication paths, the via-governance path is effective for 'accountability establishment' (the human-centered path is effective for meaning preservation and the AI-centered path for processing efficiency).
