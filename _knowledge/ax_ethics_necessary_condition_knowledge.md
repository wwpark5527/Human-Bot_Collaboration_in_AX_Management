# Ethical Necessary Conditions of Organizational AX — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Not all principles and norms mentioned in the process of realizing organizational AX carry the same level of importance; some are necessary conditions whose violation drives organizational AX to failure, while others have the character of additional conditions for good AX.
- The ethical necessary conditions include guaranteeing human dignity, humans' ultimate accountability, explainability, protection of personal and organizational information, prevention of bias and discrimination, safety and reliability, and building an AI governance system.
- Among these, AI governance is particularly emphasized as corresponding to the operating constitution of organizational AX.
- The ethical standards mentioned by nations or international bodies mostly correspond to these necessary conditions, while what the book emphasizes overall mostly corresponds to the additional conditions.
