# AX Organization — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- An AX organization is the result of organizational AX (the process), and it corresponds to unit of AX application = organization, phase of change = result.
- The AX organization is the core concept pointed to by the title of Chapter 1 of this book ("The AX Organization in Which Humans and Bots Coexist and Collaborate").
- Personal AX (unit = individual, process) and the AX individual (unit = individual, result) are also distinguished within the same framework, and the AX organization is the result term of that framework's organizational version.
- The book explains that the confusion between organizational AX and the AX organization has the same structure as the past situation in which the terms individual learning, organizational learning, and learning organization were mixed together.

## Source Evidence Quotation
> "In the end, organizational AX is the process of an organization undergoing AX, and an AX organization means an organization that has, as a result, been AX-ed."

> "Because terms such as personal AX, organizational AX, and AX organization are running rampant, let us pause here to sort out these terms. This confusion is the same as the case in the past (more precisely, in the early to mid-1990s) in which the terms individual learning, organizational learning, and learning organization were mixed together."
