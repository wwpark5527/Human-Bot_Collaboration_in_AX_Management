# Control Stress — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- It is the third type among the 'AX organizational stresses (four dangers: identity, trust, control, responsibility)', positioned after trust stress (a matter of relational trust), and it arises when the very locus of decision-making authority is unclear.
- The core question is 'who actually controls?', and it arises when it is unclear who substantively holds control.
- The output is that it requires members to redefine the scope of their own control, and, if left unresolved, leads to governance collapse.
- 'The feeling that humans cannot control bots' is cited as a representative example of human-bot relational stress, and if the loss of a sense of control deepens, it can lead to failure of AX adoption and increased organizational resistance. It is closely connected to the next type, responsibility stress ('who takes responsibility?').
