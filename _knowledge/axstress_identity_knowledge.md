# Identity Stress — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- It is the first type among the 'AX organizational stresses (four dangers: identity, trust, control, responsibility)', and it is stated that the most dangerous stresses in an AX organization are not mere fatigue but the four of identity, trust, control and responsibility.
- The core question is 'why am I needed?', and it arises when members cannot confirm their reason for existence.
- The output is the demand that members redefine their own role and value, and, if left unresolved, it leads to a crisis of existential value.
- AX organizational stress is not simply 'the difficulty of adapting to AI' but the question of 'how each member redefines their own role, value, control and responsibility in a new ontological organizational environment where humans and bots work together', and identity stress is the first axis of that.
