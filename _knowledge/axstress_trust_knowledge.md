# Trust Stress — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- It is the second type among the 'AX organizational stresses (four dangers: identity, trust, control, responsibility)', positioned after identity stress (the level of the individual member), and it arises at the level of the relationship (interaction) between humans and bots.
- The core question is 'can I trust the other party?', and it arises when humans and bots cannot be sure whether they can trust each other.
- The output is a demand for the design of trust-based cooperation, and, if left unresolved, it leads to a collapse of human-AI trust.
- In an AX organization, 'the stress of the relationship (interaction) between humans and bots' is more important than individual stress, and when the collapse of human-AI trust deepens together with the feeling that humans cannot control bots, bots misreading human intent, and imbalances of responsibility and authority, it leads to failure of AX adoption, governance collapse, increased organizational resistance, and declining performance.
