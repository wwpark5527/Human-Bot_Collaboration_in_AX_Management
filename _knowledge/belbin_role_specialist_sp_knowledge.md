# Specialist (SP) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The Specialist (SP) is one of Belbin's nine team role types, traditionally the team role that provides expert knowledge in a specific domain (expert knowledge).
- In the AX-reinterpretation table of chapter 6, the Specialist (SP) is reinterpreted as a domain + AI hybrid expert.
- In an AX organization, expertise in a specific domain alone is not enough, and hybrid experts who combine it with the capability to use AI are required.
- The criterion is whether the mode of contribution is providing expert knowledge.
