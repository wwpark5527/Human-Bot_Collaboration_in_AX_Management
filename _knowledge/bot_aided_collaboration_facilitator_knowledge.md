# Collaboration Facilitator — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The Collaboration Facilitator is the second fragment of the `BOT_AIDED_TRB` (Bot-Aided TRB, bot-assisted TRB, vertical relationship) SplitSet, and corresponds to the second column of the source table.
- Bot-Aided TRB is a vertical structure in which bots support the role balance of a human team, and in the 'human leader → human team members → bot assistants' hierarchy the bot sits in the subordinate augmentation layer.
- The Collaboration Facilitator's remit is observing and sensing for the reduction of collaboration friction — specifically, it performs meeting speaking-time analysis, role-conflict detection, and communication-bottleneck (obstacle) detection.
- It is the second of the three roles the bot takes on in Bot-Aided TRB (Role Diagnostician, Collaboration Facilitator, and Cognitive Augmenter), and is distinguished from the Role Diagnostician (diagnosing the state of roles inside the team) and the Cognitive Augmenter (information provision and coordination support) — the Collaboration Facilitator specializes in adjusting the friction of the collaboration process itself.
