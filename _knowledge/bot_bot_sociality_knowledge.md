# Bot-Bot Sociality — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Bot-bot sociality is the axis of the bot's sociality that deals with relationships among bots, and it is an optimized interaction system.
- The character of the relationship is symmetric (peer-based or function-based), and its basis is efficiency, reward, and optimization.
- Its essence is game-theoretic and strategic interaction, in which cooperation increases reward and defection incurs a penalty.
- Reputation, whereby past behavior exerts influence on the future, operates, and a natural planner/executor/verifier-style hierarchy forms.
- Because there is no emotion or empathy and it is entirely calculative, sociality between bots is strategy itself.
- On failure, the risk appears as internal problems such as collusion, system distortion, and reward hacking.
