# The ethics of bots — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The title is "bot ethics," but in reality it is ethics that every member of the AX organization (humans plus bots) must observe.
- External AI ethical standards are divided into those presented by states and international organizations (UNESCO, OECD, EU, IEEE, the Korean government) and those private companies have selected on their own (Claude's constitution and others).
- Not all principles and norms of organizational AX carry the same importance; they are divided into ethical necessary conditions whose non-observance leads to failure and additional conditions that raise competitiveness.
- Ethical necessary conditions: guaranteeing human dignity, the human's ultimate responsibility, explainability, protection of personal and organizational information, prevention of bias and discrimination, safety and reliability, establishment of an AI governance system (corresponding to the operating constitution of organizational AX).
- Additional conditions: an augmentation-centered philosophy, respect for diversity, complementary fit between humans and bots, establishment of a common context, establishment of a governance context.
