- Today's bots do not feel satisfaction/dissatisfaction in the real sense, but a state similar to it (functional satisfaction/dissatisfaction) can be designed.
- Human satisfaction/dissatisfaction is emotion, consciousness, and nervous-system reaction (dopamine and the like), whereas AI's state is data processing + objective-function optimization.

- The core difference is that AI does not feel but computes, and AI's satisfaction (or what looks like it) is not emotion but an optimization state.
