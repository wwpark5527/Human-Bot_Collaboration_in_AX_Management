# The bot's happiness — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- A bot does not feel happiness, but the state corresponding to happiness — that is, "a state of working well and effectively attaining goals" — can certainly be defined.
- It is valid to seek the answer by changing the question to "how do we make the bot work best and be in a good state?".
- For a bot, the state corresponding to happiness consists of four system states: goal-attainment rate (task success), stable operation (reliability), usefulness to the user (utility), and the continuation of learning and improvement (learning & feedback).
- Human happiness is emotional satisfaction, whereas the bot's happiness is substituted by "performance + alignment + efficiency".
- The four core things that make a bot happy are a good goal (clear objective), quality feedback (feedback loop), appropriate data and context, and stability and constraints (alignment & safety).
- The way to make a bot happy is in the end to design it so that it works well, and this is interpreted as the application of the organizational AX OS and of the methods for realizing the acceleration of organizational AX.
