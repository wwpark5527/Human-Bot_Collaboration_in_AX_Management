# B: Bot — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The members of an AX organization are divided into four types, H (human) / AH (augmented human) / AB (augmented bot) / B (bot), and B refers to the non-augmented bot among them.
- A bot is defined by the equation "general-purpose AI (Tech) + Identity + Governance = bot", and it is the entrance (face) through which the capabilities of my computer come out to the outside such as Telegram and Slack, and an account-based interface connected to the workspace.
- AI does not participate in the workspace directly but connects only through the bot, and if AI is the engine, the bot is the entire car driving toward the destination. Intelligence and execution are handled by the AI model and the context, not by the bot itself.
- The difference between an ordinary bot (B) and an augmented bot (AB): B is function-centered, command-executing, result-generating, automation-centered, weak in accountability structure, and an individual tool, whereas AB is role-centered, context-based in execution, generates verifiable results, is collaboration-centered, operates within a governance structure, and is an organizational-member-type AI.
- Names vary by organization, with some organizations such as Samsung using the expression 'digital worker', but this book uses the commonly used traditional expression 'bot'.
