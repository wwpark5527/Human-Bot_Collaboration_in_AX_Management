# Misalignment — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- It is the third of the 'bot stress types (5 types)', positioned after goal conflict (the second type), and it arises, on the premise that the AI must align with human values, when that alignment breaks down.
- The causes of misalignment are 'data bias, incomplete rules, conflicting commands, and governance ambiguity'.
- The result is the destabilization of the bot, which appears concretely as 'increased unpredictability, emergent behavior, and the risk of policy violation'.
- It is the third item in the order of the bot stress types (5 types) running computational overload → goal conflict → misalignment → continuous-update stress → multi-agent cooperation stress.
