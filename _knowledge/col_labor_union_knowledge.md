# Labor-union-type resistance — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- As the first of the 'collective human reaction/resistance types (5 types),' it is the most traditional form among those in which individual anxiety has been collectivized and developed into social, political, and institutional reactions.
- It is composed of four features: demanding limits on automation, mandating human employment, restricting the scope of AI use, and requiring mandatory retraining.
- The representative case is the strike by Hollywood writers and actors, in which the Writers Guild of America (WGA) and SAG-AFTRA strongly resisted the issues of script generation by generative AI and of digital reproduction.
- Its core claims are prohibiting the unauthorized training on human creations, restricting AI replacement, and protecting digital-reproduction rights, and this is recorded as one of the first large-scale 'AX labor-rights struggle' cases of the AX era.
