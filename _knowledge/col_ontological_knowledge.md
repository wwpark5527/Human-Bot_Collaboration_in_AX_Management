# Ontological resistance — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- As the fifth and last of the 'collective human reaction/resistance types (5 types),' it is the final stage reached by resistance that has deepened through labor-union-type, professional-group, sociocultural, and political-policy resistance.
- The core question is "Why are humans needed when AI does it better?", and as this question deepens it can lead to cynicism, organizational disengagement, an anti-technology identity, human supremacism, and AI hatred.
- This is not mere technological resistance but a 'defense of the meaning of human existence,' and it is defined as the most powerful resistance among the five types.
- Against this negative axis (replacement, control, distrust, exclusion, resistance, existential anxiety), the book explains an AX that is helpful to humans from the perspective of the positive axis (augmentation, collaboration, trust, coexistence, adaptation, expansion of meaning), and proposes reducing resistance with success messages (humans + AI are stronger, we augment, role redesign, expansion of human value, collaboration) instead of failure messages (replacement, automation, workforce reduction, efficiency maximization, control).
