# Professional-group resistance — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- As the second of the 'collective human reaction/resistance types (5 types),' it appears after labor-union-type resistance and is distinguished from labor-union-type resistance in that highly skilled professional groups act as the subject.
- The subject of the resistance is highly skilled professions such as the medical, education, and legal fields, and the issues at stake are unclear allocation of responsibility, infringement of expertise, and devaluation of professional worth.
- In the medical field it appears as concern over AI diagnostic systems (unclear allocation of responsibility, infringement of expertise, worsening of medical judgment authority), in the education field as teachers' and professors' resistance to generative AI (worsening of task meaning, nullification of evaluation, damage to the value of human education), and in the legal field as lawyers' concern over AI legal-drafting tools (devaluation of professional worth, legal-liability issues).
- All three domains commonly share the concern that AI makes the professional's authority of judgment and the locus of accountability ambiguous, and this can be seen as the control-loss type (an individual reaction) organized at the level of a professional group.
