# Sociocultural resistance — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- As the third of the 'collective human reaction/resistance types (5 types),' it is positioned after labor-union-type resistance and professional-group resistance, and is distinguished in that it is formed through the medium of cultural identity instead of an organized subject (a labor union, a professional group).
- The first sub-type, 'formation of an anti-AI culture,' is characterized by human-first-ism, the Slow AI movement, and the emphasis on Human-made; it gives rise to the 'AI-free art, human-written, and human-creation certification' movement as a case, and has the potential to develop in a direction similar to the organic movement.
- The second sub-type, 'distrust of the technological elite,' is the perception that "a few big tech firms control human society"; OpenAI, Google, and Meta are the representative targets, and it appears as critiques of AI monopoly, of data colonialism, and of algorithmic power.
- Both sub-types share the commonality of being resistance at the level of culture and identity rather than against a particular firm or institution, and they correspond to the stage preceding the next type, political-policy resistance (the demand for a response through regulation and institutions).
