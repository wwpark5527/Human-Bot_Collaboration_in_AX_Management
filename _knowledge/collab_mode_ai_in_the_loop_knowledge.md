# AI-in-the-loop (human-led + AI assist) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Among the four types of the human-AI collaboration typology (HITL / AI-in-the-loop / HOTL / Autonomous AI), AI-in-the-loop is the second type, positioned after Human-in-the-loop and before Human-on-the-loop.
- The initiative still lies with the human, but the key point is that the AI comes 'inside' the loop and assists and augments in real time.
- The decisive difference from HITL is the point of intervention — HITL is intervention at the approval point before the result is finalized, whereas AI-in-the-loop is real-time augmentation during the course of the work.
- By analogy to graduate researchers, AI-in-the-loop corresponds to a doctoral candidate, with higher autonomy than HITL (a master's student) and lower autonomy than HOTL (a postdoctoral researcher).
