# Autonomous AI (AI-autonomous execution) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Among the four types in the classification of human-AI collaboration modes (HITL / AI-in-the-loop / HOTL / Autonomous AI), Autonomous AI is the last and fourth type, with minimal human intervention.
- The decisive difference from Human-on-the-loop is that even the human as supervisor is effectively absent — HOTL retains subsequent and continuous monitoring, but Autonomous AI does not even have that.
- This concept corresponds to the HOOTL (human-out-of-the-loop, human non-intervention) concept of MIT's Sheridan (1992).
- In the analogy of graduate researchers, it corresponds to a researcher who has become a professor — moving from left to right across the four types, autonomy increases from master's program → doctoral program → postdoctoral program → professor.
