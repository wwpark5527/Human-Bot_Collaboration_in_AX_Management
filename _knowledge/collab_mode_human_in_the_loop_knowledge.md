# Human-in-the-loop (human-approval-centered) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Among the four types of the human-AI collaboration typology (HITL / AI-in-the-loop / HOTL / Autonomous AI), Human-in-the-loop is the first type, with the strongest direct human participation.
- This typology is separate from the classification based on the combination of members in an AX organization (H+B, H+AH, AH+B, AH+AB); it is a distinction drawn by the mode of human-AI collaboration, that is, by the degree of autonomy (Gibbins, 2025).
- Its origin is the framework of Sheridan (1992) at MIT; HITL is a structure in which 'the AI proposes and the human gives final approval', and since the human is 'inside' the loop every result must pass approval.
- By analogy to graduate researchers, HITL corresponds to a master's candidate among the four types, with autonomy rising progressively through AI-in-the-loop (doctoral candidate), HOTL (postdoctoral researcher), and Autonomous AI (professor).
