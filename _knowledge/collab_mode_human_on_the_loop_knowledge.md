# Human-on-the-loop (AI-autonomous + human oversight) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Among the four types in the human-AI collaboration typology (HITL / AI-in-the-loop / HOTL / Autonomous AI), Human-on-the-loop (HOTL) is the third type, positioned after AI-in-the-loop and before Autonomous AI.
- The core shift is that execution has passed from the human to the AI, and the human's position changes to that of an overseer 'on' the loop.
- The decisive difference from HITL is the mode of intervention — HITL is prior approval, whereas HOTL is after-the-fact, continuous monitoring.
- The origin of this concept is MIT's Sheridan (1992), and according to the footnote, HOTL (human-on-the-loop) and HOOTL (human-out-of-the-loop) represent 'human oversight' and 'human non-intervention' respectively. By analogy to graduate researchers, it corresponds to a postdoctoral researcher.

## Source Evidence Quotation
