# Purpose — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Purpose is, as `S2C-0417`, the first of the six elements (purpose, criterion, role, source, format, feedback) generated by the SplitSet fragmentation of `common context` (S2C-0103, `COMMON_CONTEXT`).
- An organization must have its own common context, and purpose is the item within it that specifies the reason the organization uses AI, one of the items that cannot be the same in every organization.
- Without a common context, AI interprets the organization as an ordinary company or institution and produces results that are not in that organization's words. When the purpose is specified, AI comes to think by the organization's standards.
