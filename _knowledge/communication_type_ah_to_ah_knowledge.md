# AH-AH communication — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- AH-AH communication (`COMMUNICATION_TYPE_AH_TO_AH`, S2C-0459) is communication between an augmented human and an augmented human, and is the second type of communication by member type (`COMMUNICATION_BY_MEMBER_TYPE`, S2C-0123).
- Since both sides have AI, the communication becomes an interaction between two 'human–AI composite units,' but merely holding AI does not automatically make the relationship fair — what matters is the level of augmentation power.
- Augmentation power is determined by seven elements: AI performance, the quality of the common context, accessible materials, execution authority, human judgment, the level of governance, and the recording/verification system.
- In communication between augmented humans, 'contribution conflict' becomes more important than 'role vacancy' — for example, what A's AI summarized and what B's AI interpreted may differ, and judgments of verified versus unverified may diverge.
- Communication takes place along three paths: the human-centered path (preserving meaning, emotion, and trust, but low in speed), the AI-centered path (efficient but carrying the risk of distorting intent), and the path via governance (slow but essential in domains where responsibility matters).
