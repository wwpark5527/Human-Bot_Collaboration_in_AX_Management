# AH-H communication — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- AH-H communication (`COMMUNICATION_TYPE_AH_TO_H`, S2C-0458) is communication between an augmented human (A human + A AI + A common context) and a non-augmented human (B human), and is the first type of communication by member type (`COMMUNICATION_BY_MEMBER_TYPE`, S2C-0123).
- This relationship is inherently asymmetric: with the help of AI, A can understand faster, speak more precisely, remember longer, compare more materials, and respond more strategically, whereas B must process the entire course within their own cognitive capacity.
- This difference is not a mere difference in productivity but a difference in communicative power.
- The problem is not the use of AI itself but that communication takes place with the augmentation capability hidden even though it exists.
- The six minimum principles for fairness: disclosing whether AI intervened, distinguishing AI-generated content from human judgment, notifying whether the counterpart's utterances are recorded, limiting the scope of analysis of the counterpart's utterances, human re-confirmation of important agreements, and specifying the party bearing final responsibility.
