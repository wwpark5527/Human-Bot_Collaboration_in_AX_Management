# Behavioral complementation — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Among the four types of complementary fit, behavioral complementation is the third type.
- In behavioral complementation, humans have strengths in responding to non-routine and exceptional situations, while bots have strengths in repetitive, routine behavior.
- For example, roles are divided in such a way that the bot performs the routine workflow (routine work) and the human performs crisis handling.
- This complementation is a concrete expression of the spirit of 'augmentation, not replacement' pursued by the AX organization: the automation of routine work does not remove humans entirely from the domain of execution but moves them into the domain of humans' distinctive strength, the response to exceptions and crises.
- Behavioral complementation too is grounded in the spirit of role differentiation rather than competition, and humans complement the limits of the bot's routine processing (its inability to respond to the non-routine).
