# Cognitive complementation — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The types of complementary fit carried out in an AX organization are four—cognitive complementation, emotional complementation, behavioral complementation, and ethical complementation—and cognitive complementation is the first of them.
- In cognitive complementation humans focus on intuition, creativity, and imagination, while bots focus on computation, search, and memory.
- Taking a strategy meeting as an example, roles are divided in such a way that the bot provides data simulation and the human focuses on interpreting the strategic meaning.
- This complementation is a concrete expression of the spirit of 'augmentation, not replacement' that the AX organization pursues, and corresponds to the combination of 'human judgment + bot analysis.'
- Complementary fit is based not on the spirit of 'who is superior' but on 'how we will complement each other'—that is, on pursuing role differentiation rather than competition.
