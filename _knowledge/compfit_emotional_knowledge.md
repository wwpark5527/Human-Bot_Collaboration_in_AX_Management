# Emotional complementation — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Among the four types of complementary fit—cognitive, emotional, behavioral, and ethical complementation—emotional complementation is the second type.
- In emotional complementation humans focus on providing empathy, trust, and psychological stability, while bots focus on immediate response, fatigue-free support, and continuous feedback.
- For example, roles are divided in such a way that an AI coach provides 24-hour support and a human leader provides psychological safety.
- This complementation is a concrete expression of the spirit of 'augmentation, not replacement' that the AX organization pursues, and corresponds to the combination of 'human empathy + bot information processing.'
- Emotional complementation, too, is based on the spirit of 'how we will complement each other' rather than 'who is superior,' and the bot does not replace the human's empathic role itself.
