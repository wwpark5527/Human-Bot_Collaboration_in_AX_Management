# Ethical complementation — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Among the four types of complementary fit, ethical complementation is the fourth and last type.
- In ethical complementation bots focus on rule-based consistency, while humans focus on value judgment, social responsibility, and context-based adjustment.
- The reason is that a bot can follow rules but finds it hard to judge the legitimacy of the rules themselves.
- From this the conclusion is drawn that the final authority to judge the legitimacy of the rules themselves must remain with the human, and this connects directly to the concept of the spirit (靈) = final judgment authority in the following section, 'preventing panic.'
- Just as with cognitive, emotional, and behavioral complementation, ethical complementation is also based on the spirit of complementary fit, namely 'role differentiation rather than competition.'
