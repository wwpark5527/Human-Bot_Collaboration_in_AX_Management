# Context Capital Access Right (Context Access Right) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The context capital access right (`CONTEXT_ACCESS_RIGHT`, S2C-0142) is the concept that follows immediately after the concept of context capital (CONTEXT_CAPITAL), and it stipulates that "stratification in the AI era arises not only from access to models but from the context access right."
- The author explicitly denies and redefines the ordinary meaning of access right (an AI account, a software usage license): "Access right in the AI era does not simply mean an AI account or a software usage license. The real access right is the right of access to the context in which AI can be used meaningfully."
- The mechanism of context capital monopolization: "When an organization's context capital is monopolized by a few, the ability to use AI becomes subordinate to an individual's sense, language ability, information access, rank, and departmental authority." That is, stratification arises not from whether one holds an AI account but from the imbalance of context access.
- The opposite case is also stated: "When context capital is converted into a common production base for the whole organization, the ability to use AI becomes a common organizational capability rather than an individual privilege." This serves as the theoretical bridge leading to the next concept, Context Justice (WalkOrder 326), and to the theory of AI capability equality (WalkOrder 327).
- The sentence "A person who has an AI account but does not know the organization's standards, materials, authority, and approval structure cannot use AI properly" shows the difference between formal access and actual access through a concrete case.
- Stage-1 structural role: this concept is classified at the "proposed concept" layer of the book (the third of the four layers verifiable fact → reasonable interpretation → proposed concept → execution model, line 350), which makes explicit that it is the author's theorization rather than verified reality.
