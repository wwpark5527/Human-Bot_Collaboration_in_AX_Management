# Socialization of Context Capital — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The socialization of context capital (`CONTEXT_CAPITAL_SOCIALIZATION`, S2C-0140) means preventing the ability to use AI from being monopolized by a few individuals or a particular department, and together with the responsible operating system (which controls the organizational inequality of AI authority and responsibility) it forms one of the two axes of the inclusive-transition management philosophy that prevents and mitigates the problem of AI stratification.
- The author states explicitly that this concept is the "core" the book emphasizes: "The core that this book includes and emphasizes is the socialization of context capital and the establishment of a responsible operating system."
- The socialization of context capital pairs directly with the concept of Context Capital defined immediately afterwards — if context capital defines "what" (the purpose, standards, language, materials, format, verification standards, and approval standards needed for AI operation), the socialization of context capital stipulates "whose it must be" (a common base for the whole organization rather than the monopoly of a few).
- As background the author diagnoses that "AI-based stratification will soon become reality and will exert a considerable influence on companies and society as a whole," and presents the socialization of context capital and the establishment of a responsible operating system as concrete remedies for this problem of stratification.
