# Context Designer (context-design-type AX talent) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The context designer (S2C-0132, `CONTEXT_DESIGNER`) is the actor (ROLE) who performs context design (`PIS_CONTEXT_DESIGN`, WalkOrder 306), and forms the necessary conditions under which the first LLM works effectively within the organization.
- Further, the context designer is also the agent who forms the second LLM — the author writes that once layers 1 to 5 of the predictive intelligence system (world model + knowledge chain + context design) are complete, the people who intend to call their sum the 'third LLM' are absorbed in it and exchange with one another 24 hours a day.
- In the labor-market differentiation table the context designer is the 'context-design-type AX talent' type, distinguished from the 'AI-complementary worker,' the 'worker managed by AI,' and the 'worker at risk of AI replacement or reduction'; such a person does not merely use AI but designs purpose, standards, verification, and feedback and converts them into the organization's operating structure.
- The agents of the context structuring stage are specified as "the organization, AX talent, and the AI operation designer," and the context designer belongs to this category of AX talent.
- The role of the context designer is directly connected to the problem of the formation and distribution of context capital, in that the context designer is the practical agent who resolves the 'AI context gap' (who can give the organization's purpose and standards to AI), the gap the author regards as the most important among the seven gaps of AI-based stratification.
