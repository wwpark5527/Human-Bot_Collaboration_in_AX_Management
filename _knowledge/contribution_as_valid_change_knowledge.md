# Contribution — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Contribution is the second of the three layers — work, contribution, role — that the role theory of the AI era redefines, and it joins work (act) and role (structure).
- Contribution does not simply mean that work was done, but is the valid change that act produced.
- Example: that the AI wrote a sentence is work, but if that sentence changed the counterpart's understanding, made judgment possible, remained as a record, and led to the next action, then it is contribution.
- Contribution is the valid operation that changes the state of meaning, judgment, verification, approval, record, execution, and responsibility in the communication process.
- In the AI era, "who produced what change" becomes more important than "who worked".
