# Contribution conflict — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Contribution conflict (`CONTRIBUTION_CONFLICT`, S2C-0126) is the problem that becomes important in communication between augmented human and augmented human (AH-AH), superseding the 'role vacancy.'
- Contribution conflict arises when multiple humans and AIs assign different criteria, interpretations, verification states, and responsibility judgments to the same message.
- Concrete aspects: what AI A summarizes and what AI B interprets may differ / A's common-context criteria and B's common-context criteria may differ / A may see it as verified while B sees it as unverified / A may see it as shareable while B sees it as a security risk.
- When a contribution conflict arises, it is adjusted through one of the human-centered path, the AI-centered path, and the path via governance.
