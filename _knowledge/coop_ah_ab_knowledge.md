# AH + AB type — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The AH+AB type is the last of the four types in the section 'Stress by cooperation type' and the core cooperation type of the AX organization: a horizontal, jointly optimizing relationship in which both human and bot are augmented.
- The four stresses on the AH side: Orchestration overload, decision fatigue, concentration of responsibility, and weakened sense of reality (over-immersion in digital cooperation).
- The four stresses on the AB side: Context inconsistency, Alignment stress, Autonomy ambiguity, and Continuous update overload.
- Countermeasures on the AH side: AI orchestration dashboard, Cognitive load reduction, AI delegation rule, and Decision hierarchy.
- Countermeasures on the AB side: Role-constrained autonomy, Explainability layer, Policy-aware agent, and Stable context memory.
- The core solution is the building of a common context, and this can be resolved through the formation of the governance context and the knowledge chain.
