# AH + B type — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The AH+B type is the third of the four types in the section 'Stress by cooperation type': a structure in which an augmented human (AH) and a non-augmented bot (B) coexist and the bot performs work independently.
- The four stresses on the AH side: loss of control, incapacitation of competence, concentration of responsibility, and fear of surveillance.
- The four stresses on the B side: lack of context, governance conflict, inconsistency with human instructions, and lack of trust.
- Countermeasures on the AH side: clarification of the final approval authority, differentiation of the roles of human and bot, building a governance system, and privacy protection rules.
- Countermeasures on the B side: building a common context, building a governance context, defining the approval system, and providing explainability.
- The core task of an AH+B cooperative organization is not to introduce a great deal of AI but to design things so that the bot augments rather than replaces the human and the human retains the final role and responsibility; success and failure at this stage diverge greatly depending on the design of the 'role balance of human and bot (TRB).'
