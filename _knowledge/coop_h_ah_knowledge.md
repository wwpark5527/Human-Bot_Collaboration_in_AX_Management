# H + AH type — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The H+AH type is the second of the four types in the section 'Stress by cooperation type': a structure in which an ordinary human (H) and an augmented human (AH) cooperate, human versus human but with a gap in augmentation level present.
- The three stresses on the H side: relative inferiority, alienation, and unfair evaluation.
- The four stresses on the AH side: continuous high-performance pressure, AI dependency, isolation in human relationships (distance from colleagues), and identity confusion (whether the performance is mine or the AI's).
- Countermeasures on the H side: emphasizing complementary roles instead of comparison, and strengthening the capabilities distinctive to humans (empathy, ethics, integrative judgment).
- Countermeasures on the AH side: AI dependency monitoring, collaborative leadership training, and establishing a human orchestrator identity rather than an AI operator identity.
- Countermeasures at the organizational level: banning human-only evaluation, an evaluation system that takes the augmentation level into account, and Pairing collaboration design (H↔AH).
