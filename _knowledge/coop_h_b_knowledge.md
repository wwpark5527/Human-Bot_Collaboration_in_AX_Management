# H + B type — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The H+B type is the first of the four cooperation types (H+B/H+AH/AH+B/AH+AB) presented in the section 'Stress by cooperation type': a vertical relationship in which the human is the dominant subject and the bot assists as a tool or automation executor.
- The four stresses on the human (H) side: loss of control, role erosion, imbalance of responsibility, and learning pressure.
- The three stresses on the bot (B) side: lack of Context, overload from exceptional situations, and inconsistency in human feedback.
- Countermeasures on the H side: adopting explainable AI, maintaining Human-in-the-loop, maintaining augmentation rather than replacement, and training in AI comprehension.
- Countermeasures on the B side: building a common context, an exception escalation system, and a feedback learning structure.
