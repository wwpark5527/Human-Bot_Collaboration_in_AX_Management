# AH + AB (a true AX organization) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Among the four cooperation types of the human-bot coexistence organization (H+B, H+AH, AH+B, AH+AB), AH + AB is the last stage and denotes the state in which both human and bot are augmented.
- The decisive point of distinction from AH + B is whether the bot is augmented — in AH + B the bot is an execution tool, whereas in AH + AB the bot (AB) is a being that is already augmented and jointly makes judgments with the human.
- It is presented as the most desirable of the human-bot coexistence types, and this type is precisely the 'human-bot coexistence and cooperation organization' that brings about human development = a 'true AX organization' (hybrid organization).
- The means of realization are the proper use of an organizational AX OS and the realization of HBRM, and this is the turning point leading to the following WalkOrder (the detailed characteristics of the hybrid organization itself).
