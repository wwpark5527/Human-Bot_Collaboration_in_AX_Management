# AH + B (intelligent division of labor) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Among the four cooperation types of human-bot coexistence organizations (H+B, H+AH, AH+B, AH+AB), AH + B is the third stage, positioned after H + AH (augmented-human-centered cooperation) and before AH + AB (a true AX organization).
- AH + B is a structure in which one augmented human (AH) orchestrates several bots (B), and the key point is that the human is augmented while the bots are not.
- The relational configuration of this type is a division of labor between strategy (human) and execution (bots), and in judgment whether the bots are augmented becomes the decisive point of distinction from AH + AB.
- The means of realization are building a multi-agent environment, human-in-the-loop design, and role-based agent design, and these are the conditions for one human to stably direct many bots.
