# H + AH (augmented-human-centered cooperation) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Among the four cooperation types of human-bot coexistence organizations (H+B, H+AH, AH+B, AH+AB), H + AH is the second stage, corresponding to the stage after H + B (tool-type cooperation).
- H + AH is a structure in which existing humans (H) and augmented humans (AH) work together, and the key point of distinction is not the bot's status but the augmentation gap within the human side.
- In this type, the stratification of AI competence occurs and role reorganization begins — the emergence of a gap between augmented humans and those who are not is the key characteristic and side effect.
- The means of realization are AI democratization, building an AI collaboration culture, and role redesign, and these are measures for responding to the side effect of stratification.
