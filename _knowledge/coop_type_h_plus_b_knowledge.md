# H + B (Tool-Type Cooperation) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Human-bot coexistence organizations are divided into four cooperation types, H+B, H+AH, AH+B, and AH+AB (organizations of humans only or bots only are excluded), and H + B is the earliest stage among them.
- H + B is a human-led + bot-assisted structure, in which the bot is a productivity-improving tool and responsibility is entirely concentrated on the human.
- Its realization methods are the dissemination of personal productivity AI, prompt literacy education, and automation of repetitive work, and as a result it stops at the improvement of personal productivity (it does not reach a fundamental transformation at the organizational level).
- It is the core characteristic of this type that it is distinguished from the H + AH and AH + B types in that the human is not augmented.

## Source Evidence Quotation
> "Whereas the existing human-centered organization is an organization of the 'H + H type', an organization in which humans and bots coexist is 'an organization of various types combining H, AH, AB, and B'. Here, organizations with only bots and no humans (B + B, B + AB, AB + AB) are cases we do not want and are therefore not objects of consideration."
