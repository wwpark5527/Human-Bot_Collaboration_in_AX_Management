# Shaper/Designer — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The shaper is the first of the five core roles of AX-organization talent. The five core roles are a compression and conversion of the eight roles of AX-organization talent from the "contribution" perspective.
- The work the shaper performs is problem definition, requirement organization, and structural design.
- The shaper's core contribution is clarity — turning an ambiguous problem into a clear one.
- Criterion of judgment: whether it defines what to build, why it should be built, and what structure is appropriate.
- Among the eight talent roles, the problem definer is mapped to the shaper as its main role, and the context composer is also mapped to the shaper as an auxiliary role.
