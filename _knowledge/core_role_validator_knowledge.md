# Validator — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The validator is the third of the five core roles of AX-organization talent.
- The work the validator performs is testing, review, and quality checking.
- The validator's core contribution is reliability — securing the reliability of the output artifact.
- Criterion of judgment: whether it finds errors, security risks, and logical flaws and judges whether the artifact is at a usable level.
- Among the eight talent roles, the result interpreter and the verifier are mapped to the validator as their main role, and the accountability judge is also mapped to the validator as an auxiliary role.
