# Dynamic role allocation (Dynamic Role Allocation) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Dynamic role allocation is the second fragment of the `HUMAN_BOT_COUPLED_TRB` (Human-Bot Coupled TRB, human-bot coupled TRB, a horizontal relationship) SplitSet.
- Human-Bot Coupled TRB is a horizontal structure in which human and bot form a TRB together as independent role subjects, and it optimizes the role balance of the whole team at the integrated human-bot level.
- The subject responsible for dynamic role allocation is the bot, which analyzes the team's state in real time and redistributes and reassigns roles.
- There are four objects of analysis: workload, cognitive load, stress, and expertise availability.
- It is the second of the three core features of the Human-Bot Coupled TRB (co-constitution of roles, dynamic role allocation, maximization of human-bot complementary fit), and is distinguished from the co-constitution of roles (formation of a hybridized role) and the maximization of complementary fit (generation of new role capability) — dynamic role allocation specializes in role reallocation grounded in real-time state analysis itself.
