# Maximization of human-bot complementary fit (Maximization of Complementary Fit) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The maximization of human-bot complementary fit is the third and last fragment of the `HUMAN_BOT_COUPLED_TRB` (Human-Bot Coupled TRB, human-bot coupled TRB, a horizontal relationship) SplitSet.
- Human-Bot Coupled TRB is a horizontal structure in which human and bot form a TRB together as independent role subjects, and it optimizes the role balance of the whole team at the integrated human-bot level.
