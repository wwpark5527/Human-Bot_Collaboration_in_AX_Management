# Critical Thinking — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Critical thinking is the fifth of the six items in "Necessary conditions for talent in an AX organization: the capabilities of the human who survives," and it is the capability at the stage following data and digital literacy.
- AI has the characteristic of producing highly plausible errors.
- The essential abilities are four: verification, the search for counterexamples, logic checks, and reliability assessment.
- In an AX organization the person who verifies AI well matters more than the person who believes AI well.
