# DX (Digital Transformation) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- DX is the transformation paradigm that was in vogue before AX, and it focuses on the digitization, datafication, cloud migration, and process efficiency of work and systems.
- The questions of the DX era are the five axes of whether digitization is possible, how data is stored, how processes are automated, how systems are adopted, and how efficiency is raised.
- DX is used in the book as the preceding concept (foil) structurally contrasted with AX, and it corresponds to layers 1–2 of the LLM layer diagram.
- The point that DX alone cannot explain the change in which AI intervenes in the organization's judgment, generation, execution, evaluation, and accountability structures becomes the grounds for the emergence of AX.
