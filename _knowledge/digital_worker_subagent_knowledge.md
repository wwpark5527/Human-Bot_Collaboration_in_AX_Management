# Subagent — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Digital workers (bots) can be subdivided according to the degree of autonomy into AI agent > Subagent > Bot (or higher-/mid-/lower-tier bot), and the Subagent is the intermediate position among them (the mid-tier bot).
- The Subagent is a specialized agent that supports a higher agent, and it is distinguished from the AI agent in that it does not set goals on its own but is assigned goals by a higher agent.
- The Subagent is distinguished from the Bot in that specialized judgment is possible, and its collaboration is limited to internal agent collaboration (collaboration with the higher agent and other subagents).
- Its role scope is limited and specialized, and its memory/context retention is partial.
- A research subagent is presented as an example.
