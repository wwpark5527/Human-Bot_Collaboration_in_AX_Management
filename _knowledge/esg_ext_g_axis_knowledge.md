# Extension of G (Accountability Structure of AI Power) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The extension of G (`ESG_EXT_G_AXIS`, S2C-0486) is the third and last of the three axes of the extension of the ESG concept in the AI era (S2C-0146, ESG_EXTENSION), and it starts from the problem awareness that "once AI substantially participates in organizational operation, a structure in which people simply judge and the system assists only as a tool is not sufficient."
- Grounds for the importance of governance: "Because who approves the use of AI, who takes responsibility for the outcome, by what criteria review and revision are carried out, whether records remain, whether objection is possible, and how policy and authority are arranged become core problems of corporate operation, the importance of governance grows."
- AI governance is understood as strengthening and concretizing the G of ESG, and seven constituent elements are enumerated: clarification of roles, authority, and responsibility; recording and log management of contribution and judgment; approval structures and review procedures; explainability and accountability attribution; risk management and objection procedures; policy-based operation and control structures; and management of intellectual property, disclosure criteria, and conflicts of interest.
- The concluding sentence, which is also the evidence: "That is, G must retain governance in the traditional sense while evolving toward including, within it, a system for responsibly operating the functioning and judgment of AI." — This reconfirms on the G axis the consistent argument of this entire chapter, that this is not dismantling but advancement (retaining the existing axes + including AI-era risks).
- The comparison table with existing ESG (lines 331-333) shows that the existing G of "board, control, audit, risk, compliance" is explicitly extended in extended ESG into "AI authority, verification, approval, recording, explanation, objection, accountability attribution, improvement loops."
- Footnote 68) specifies the related international standards: "The NIST AI Risk Management Framework presents the Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage functions for AI risk management. ISO/IEC 42001 is an international [standard] for organizations to establish, implement, maintain, and improve an AI management system."
- Stage-1/2 structure: it was originally the single candidate S1C-171 "extension of the ESG concept in the AI era (extended ESG)," but in Stage-2 it was SPLIT into the three axes E/S/G (parent S2C-0146). This knowledge file is the G-axis fragment among them (S2C-0486), and it is the last member of this SplitSet triad (E/S/G, WalkOrder 328-330). In the original text of the book, a separate subheading "**ESG execution structure**" follows immediately after the description of the extension of G, but this is outside the roster scope of this batch (325-330).
