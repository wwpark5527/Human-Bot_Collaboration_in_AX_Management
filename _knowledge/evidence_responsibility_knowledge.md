# Evidence Responsibility — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Evidence responsibility is the third and last of the three responsibilities (context, judgment, evidence) that AX-organization talent bears when working with AI.
- In an AX organization what matters is not only the result; why this result arose, who reviewed it, and by what criteria it was approved must remain on record.
- The evidence items that must remain: the materials used, the instructions given to AI, the results AI produced, the revisions made by humans, the verification criteria, the approval records, the error records, and the improvements reflected.
