# Functional Role — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- A functional role means a function formally assigned by the organization or a superior in a job-related way.
- Example: in an HR team, the recruiting officer, evaluation officer, payroll officer, and benefits officer correspond to functional roles.
- When the functional roles needed within a team are evenly in place and the team members have the ability, qualifications, experience, and motivation to perform them, the foundation for team performance is laid, but team performance is not sufficiently realized by that alone.
- The name 'functional role' itself was given by Belbin in order to contrast it with the team role he newly discovered — what had previously been called 'role' is precisely the functional role, and with the appearance of the team role the concept of 'role' was extended into functional role + team role.
- As an axis distinguished from the team role (informal, non-job-related, based on behavior type), the functional role serves as the reference point contrasted with the team role in team role balance (TRB) diagnosis.
