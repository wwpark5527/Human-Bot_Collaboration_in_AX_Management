# AI Access Gap — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The AI access gap (`GAP_AI_ACCESS`, S2C-0466) is the first of the seven gaps that create AI-based stratification (S2C-0135, `AI_STRATIFICATION_SEVEN_GAPS`), and it is the gap between actors who can actually use high-performance AI and infrastructure and those who cannot.
- The core question is "who can use high-performance AI and infrastructure," and the stratification risk is "inequality in access to the tools themselves."
- Even now there are cases where experts who make heavy use of LLMs spend more than several million won a day on subscription fees or token costs, and if the shift from subscription plans to usage-based billing occurs, the 'AI stratification' in which access rights and usage rights are divided according to the presence or absence of money may be realized.
- It is important that the seven gaps accumulate simultaneously, and the access gap corresponds to their starting point.
