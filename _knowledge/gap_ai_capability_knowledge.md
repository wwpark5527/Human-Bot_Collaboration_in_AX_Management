# AI Capability Gap — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The AI capability gap (`GAP_AI_CAPABILITY`, S2C-0467) is the second of the seven gaps that create AI-based stratification (S2C-0135, `AI_STRATIFICATION_SEVEN_GAPS`), and it arises from differences in the ability to convert AI into actual work performance.
- The core question is "who converts AI into work performance," and the stratification risk is "gaps in productivity, wages, and promotion."
- The point that distinguishes it from the AI access gap (whether one can use the tool) is that even when one can access the tool, a separate gap arises if the capability to convert it into work performance is absent.
- It is important that the seven gaps accumulate simultaneously, and the capability gap is the second accumulating factor, arising at the stage after the access gap.
