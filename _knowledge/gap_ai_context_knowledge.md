# AI Context Gap — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The AI context gap (`GAP_AI_CONTEXT`, S2C-0468) is the third of the seven gaps and the gap the author regards as most important from the organizational AX perspective.
- Definition: it is the gap between people who can and cannot provide AI with the organization's purpose, criteria, language, way of working, output formats, and verification criteria, and it connects to the concept of context capital.
- Even when using the same AI, the difference between those who can and cannot provide AI with the organization's purpose, criteria, language, way of working, output formats, and verification criteria grows larger over time.
- The means of production in the AI era do not stop at models, data, and compute but extend even to context capital such as purpose, criteria, language, materials, authority, and verification systems.
- The core of inclusive-transformation AX is not to leave context capital as the capability of a few individuals or the authority of the organization's upper layer, but to convert it into a common production base the whole organization can access and use.
- The context designer (`CONTEXT_DESIGNER`, WalkOrder 307) and context design (`PIS_CONTEXT_DESIGN`, WalkOrder 306) are precisely the concept and actor that resolve this AI context gap in practice.
