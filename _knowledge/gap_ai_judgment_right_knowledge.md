# AI Right-to-Judge Gap — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The AI right-to-judge gap (`GAP_AI_JUDGMENT_RIGHT`, S2C-0469) is the fourth of the seven gaps, and it is the gap between actors who have the authority to approve, revise, or reject the results AI produces and those who do not.
- The core question is "who can approve, revise, or reject AI results," and the stratification risk is "humans become the object of AI decisions."
- This is a problem that pairs with the AI context gap (who gives AI the purpose and criteria, `GAP_AI_CONTEXT`), and when the authority to provide context and the authority to judge the results are together concentrated in a few, stratification deepens.
- The seven gaps (access, capability, context, right to judge, surveillance, ownership, outcome distribution) accumulate simultaneously, and after the right-to-judge gap come the surveillance, ownership, and outcome-distribution gaps.
