# AI Ownership Gap — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The AI ownership gap (`GAP_AI_OWNERSHIP`, S2C-0471) is the sixth of the seven gaps, and it is the gap that arises as the owning actors of the revenue generated from models, data, and platforms become skewed.
- The core question is "who owns the revenue from models, data, and platforms," and the stratification risk is "concentration of productivity profits."
- The author connects this to the problem of knowledge capitalization: in AX, the output of the data centers that store structured knowledge is knowledge-capitalized and sold by big tech, and big tech absorbs and stores society's knowledge and sells that output in return for token fees.
- The way of sharing this knowledge capital with society may be capitalist (selling) or communist ("AI for All" sharing), and this is a matter of debate related to corporate management philosophy.
- The seven gaps (access, capability, context, right to judge, surveillance, ownership, outcome distribution) accumulate simultaneously, and after the ownership gap comes the outcome-distribution gap.
