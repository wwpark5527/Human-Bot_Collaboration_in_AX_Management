# AI Surveillance Gap — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The AI surveillance gap (`GAP_AI_SURVEILLANCE`, S2C-0470) is the fifth of the seven gaps, and it is the gap that arises between actors who use AI as a tool to manage others and those who are managed by AI.
- The core question is "who manages with AI, and who is managed by AI," and the stratification risk is "algorithmic management and control."
- The author separately explains this as "algorithmic management is the way in which software and AI perform part or all of the work of a human manager," and cites as its areas of application "work assignment, performance evaluation, surveillance, schedule management, compensation, discipline, productivity measurement." In this case the worker is at once the actor using AI and the object evaluated and managed by AI.
- It follows from the immediately preceding gap, the AI right-to-judge gap (`GAP_AI_JUDGMENT_RIGHT`, who approves, revises, or rejects AI results), and when the actor holding the right of judgment also comes to hold the surveillance and management authority, the gaps overlap.
- The seven gaps (access, capability, context, right to judge, surveillance, ownership, outcome distribution) accumulate simultaneously, and after the surveillance gap come the ownership and outcome-distribution gaps.
