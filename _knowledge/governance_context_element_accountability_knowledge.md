# Accountability — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Accountability is `S2C-0428` and is the sixth of the seven elements (authority, security, verification, approval, record, accountability, improvement) of `governance context`(S2C-0105, `GOVERNANCE_CONTEXT`).
- The seven-element table of the governance context (lines 188-209) stipulates the core question of accountability as "who bears the responsibility for the final use and judgment?" and its meaning as "clarification of where responsibility lies."
- One of the problems that arise when there is no governance context is "unclear accountability: it is difficult to judge how far AI results may be used" (line 221).
- The international-standard mapping table (lines 227-236) maps the accountability element to "top-management responsibility" in ISO/IEC 42001, "Govern" in NIST, and "provider and deployer obligations" in the EU AI Act.
