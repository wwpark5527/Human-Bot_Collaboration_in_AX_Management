# Authority — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Authority is `S2C-0423` and is the first of the seven elements (authority, security, verification, approval, record, accountability, improvement) of `governance context`(S2C-0105, `GOVERNANCE_CONTEXT`).
- The seven-element table of the governance context (lines 188-209) defines the core question of authority as "which context can access which materials and tools?" and its meaning as "management of access and execution scope."
- One of the problems that arises when there is no governance context is "authority confusion: it becomes unclear which AI can access which materials and tools" (line 218).
- The international standards mapping table (lines 227-236) maps the authority element to "role/access control" in ISO/IEC 42001, "Govern" in NIST AI RMF, and "human oversight (Article 14)" in the EU AI Act.
