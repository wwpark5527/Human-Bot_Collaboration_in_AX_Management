# Group Cohesiveness — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Before the team role (TR) concept appeared, both academia and industry understood teamwork as "the degree of psychological attraction to cohere well and help one another," and its concrete measurement mostly made use of group cohesiveness.
- Group cohesiveness is the measurement tool of the earlier perspective, which viewed teamwork solely as the human bonding among members — their liking, cohering, and willingness to help one another.
- After the author (Park Won-woo) proposed the two sides of teamwork (human bonding and task activation), group cohesiveness was positioned as the operational measurement method for the human-bond side and TRB as the operational measurement method for the task-activation side.
- In terms of the fit concept, it is the axis corresponding to supplementary fit, in which things fit well because they are similar to one another, and it stands in structural contrast to complementary fit, to which TRB corresponds.
