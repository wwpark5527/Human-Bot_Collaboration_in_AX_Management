# AH1 Augmented Human — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- AH1 augmented human is the fourth of the six-rung ladder of human-augmentation stages (H0–AH3), the entry stage into the augmented human where thinking and learning have increased.
- Its characteristic is "increased thinking/learning," and its output is confirmed as increased thinking ability and learning volume.
- On the ladder it is the stage after H2 (AI collaborator, working jointly with AI), and as the first stage to be given the name "augmented human (AH)" it leads to the next stage, AH2 AI orchestrator (using/directing multiple AI agents).
- It can be seen as the stage where improvements in cognitive augmentation and learning augmentation, among the five AH indicators (cognitive, decision, learning, collaboration, and role augmentation), accumulate and become visible, and it develops into the AH2 and AH3 stages, where role augmentation is finally confirmed.
