# Augmentation Management — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Augmentation management is the fourth of the eight HBRM roles, and it is the function that supports the H→AH and B→AB transitions in the stage following collaboration-structure design (collaboration-flow design).
- HBRM's evolutionary-expansion dimension means going beyond a mere management system to design co-evolution, and humans must grow into augmented humans (AH) through collaboration with AI while bots must develop into augmented bots (AB) through the common context and the governance context.
- The organization must design humans and bots to become a relationship that raises each other's capabilities rather than one that replaces each other, and augmentation management is the stage that manages this direction as an actual transition process.
- If augmentation management is not carried out properly, imbalance problems such as the weakening of human judgment and creativity caused by an excess of automated roles can arise in the subsequent role-balance management stage.
