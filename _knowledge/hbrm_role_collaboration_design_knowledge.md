# Collaboration-Structure Design — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Collaboration-structure design is the third of the eight HBRM roles, and it is the function that designs the human-bot collaboration flow in the stage following role design (the setting of role and responsibility boundaries).
- HBRM's role-expansion dimension is not complete by defining who takes on which role alone; it must also stipulate how humans and bots collaborate.
- If the collaboration flow is poorly designed, imbalance problems such as a shortage of coordinating roles or a shortage of verification roles can arise in the subsequent role-balance management stage.
- HBRM must be designed so that humans and bots become a relationship that raises each other's capabilities rather than one that replaces each other, and collaboration-structure design is the stage that implements this relationship as a concrete work flow.
