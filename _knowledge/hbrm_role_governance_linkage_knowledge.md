# Governance Linkage — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Governance linkage is the seventh of the eight HBRM roles, and it is the function that interconnects the authority, verification, approval, recording, and responsibility structures in the stage following trust management (the establishment of collaboration standards that prevent overtrust and distrust).
- In HBRM's role-expansion dimension the core questions are "who takes on which role, how do humans and bots collaborate, by what criteria are results verified, and who bears the final responsibility", and governance linkage is the stage that directly answers the verification and responsibility questions among these.
- The problem of "unclear responsibility roles" that appears when role balance collapses is a representative outcome that occurs when governance linkage has not been carried out properly.
- HBRM stipulates that a common and governance context must necessarily exist for the role balance and effective operation of an AX organization, and governance linkage is the stage that makes this context concrete as the five elements of authority, verification, approval, recording, and responsibility.
