# Learning and Improvement Management — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Learning and improvement management is the last, eighth role among the eight HBRM roles (member definition, role design, collaboration-structure design, augmentation management, role-balance management, trust management, governance linkage, learning and improvement management), and it is the function that accumulates feedback and errors into organizational memory.
- HBRM must be "designed not simply to place roles but to diagnose the excess and deficiency of roles and to maintain balance", and learning and improvement management is the stage that leaves behind the experience so that this diagnosis and balance maintenance can be done repeatedly.
- The five problems that appear when role balance collapses (excess of technical roles, excess of automated roles, shortage of verification roles, shortage of coordinating roles, unclear responsibility roles) can be checked for recurrence and readjusted through the organizational memory that learning and improvement management has accumulated.
- Unlike HRM, HBRM aims at "the management of the joint performance, responsibility, and learning of humans and bots", and learning and improvement management is the role that takes charge of the 'learning' axis among these.
