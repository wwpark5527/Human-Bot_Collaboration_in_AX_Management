# Role-Balance Management — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Role-balance management is the fifth of the eight HBRM roles, and it is the function that adjusts the concentration, deficiency, and conflict of roles in the stage following augmentation management (support for the H→AH and B→AB transitions).
- In HBRM the scope of role balance (team role) extends beyond the existing human↔human relationship to three balances including human↔bot and bot↔bot.
- When role balance collapses, problems arise such as an excess of technical roles (the emergence of complex systems that the field finds hard to use), an excess of automation roles (the weakening of human judgment and creativity), a lack of verification roles (the uncritical acceptance of AI results), a lack of coordination roles (expanding complexity as bots increase), and unclear responsibility roles (an unclear locus of accountability).
- HBRM must be designed not merely to place roles but to diagnose the excess and deficiency of roles and maintain balance, and for this a common and governance context is absolutely necessary.
