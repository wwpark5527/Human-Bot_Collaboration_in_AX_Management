# Role Design — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- HBRM's role-expansion dimension means the shift from job-centered management to human-bot role-based collaborative management.
- What matters in an AX organization is not the title but the role, and the core questions are who takes on which role, how humans and bots collaborate, by what criteria results are verified, and who bears the final responsibility.
- Role design is the second of the eight HBRM roles, and it is the function that sets the role and responsibility boundaries of humans and bots in the stage following member definition (the definition of the positions and roles of H/AH/B/AB).
- If role design is unclear, it can lead to problems such as unclear responsibility roles in the subsequent role-balance management stage.
