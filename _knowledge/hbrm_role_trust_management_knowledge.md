# Trust Management — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Trust management is the sixth of the eight HBRM roles, and it is the function that establishes collaboration standards preventing overtrust and distrust in the stage following role-balance management (the adjustment of role concentration, deficiency, and conflict).
- Among the problems that appear when role balance collapses, the "lack of verification roles" leads to the uncritical acceptance of AI results, that is, to overtrust, and trust management deals with this problem head-on.
- HBRM stipulates that, while not regarding humans and bots as identical persons, bots must be managed under trust and governance because they actually perform work and affect the organization's performance and risk.
- If trust management is not established, the two extreme problems can arise in which humans overtrust a bot's results and leave risks unattended or, conversely, distrust them excessively and fail to make use of the benefits of collaboration.
