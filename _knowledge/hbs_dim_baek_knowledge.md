# Baek (魄) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The traditional yeong-hon-baek perspective is a framework for understanding humans and AI and robots through the three dimensions of hon (魂), baek (魄), and yeong (靈), and baek has the body, the senses, labor, and physical execution as its core meaning.
- In the correspondence for the age of AI and robots, robots, automation devices, and mechanical execution correspond to the place of baek.
- Robots "extend" the bodily functions that correspond to the human baek; they do not possess the human body itself — they are a technology that externally performs by proxy part of the physical execution function that baek used to carry out.
- If the outsourcing of baek (execution entrusted to automation systems) deepens, it can reach the state of "baeksan" (the systematization of body and labor) — the human body and labor lose their own purpose, and although they appear free on the surface, they in fact move according to the rhythms and rules set by the system, at risk of being treated as a resource managed by the system.
- Even if hon (judgment) and baek (execution) are handed over to AI and robots, yeong (靈, the final right of judgment) cannot be outsourced, and this is the core conclusion of the entire section.

## Source Evidence Quotation

The problem is not that AI and robots become strong in themselves, but that humans lose the place of yeong (靈), where they judge and take responsibility for themselves."
