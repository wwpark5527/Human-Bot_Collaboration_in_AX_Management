# Hon (魂) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The traditional Yeong-Hon-Baek perspective is a framework for understanding humans and AI and robots through the three dimensions of Hon (魂), Baek (魄), and Yeong (靈), and Hon has thought, memory, judgment, creation, and decision-making as its core meaning among these.
- In the response of the age of AI and robots, AI's analysis, reasoning, language generation, and judgment assistance correspond to the place of Hon.
- AI 'imitates' the cognitive functions corresponding to the human Hon; it does not possess Hon itself — it is a technology that externally performs, amplifies, and stands in for part of the function that Hon used to carry out.
- If the outsourcing of Hon (judgment entrusted to AI) deepens, it can reach the state of 'honbi' (the loss of the sovereignty of judgment) — a state in which the human does not question for themselves but accepts AI's answers as they are, trusts them without verification, does not take responsibility, and hides behind the words "the AI said so."
- Even if Hon (judgment) and Baek (execution) pass to AI and robots, Yeong (靈, the final right of judgment) cannot be outsourced, and this is the core conclusion of this entire section.
