# Fairness — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- From a bot's standpoint human respect is reduced to four items, and these are the machine-readable minimum definition of human respect, of which fairness is the third element.
- Fairness is the element of not discriminating, and together with the remaining three elements (non-harm, respect for autonomy, accountability) it decomposes human respect into a form a machine can judge.
- The internalization of human respect (the higher-level principle) is not the injection of morality but the design of the system so that only actions that do not infringe on humans are selected, and fairness is one of the minimum judgeable conditions in that system design.
- The absolutely prohibited domain defined at stage 2 (mandatory rules) of the five-stage architecture for implementing human respect is a structure that can also be applied to discriminatory actions that damage fairness.
