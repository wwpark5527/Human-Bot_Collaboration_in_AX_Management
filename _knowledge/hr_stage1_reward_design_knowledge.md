# Stage 1 (Reward Design) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- A five-stage architecture is presented as the concrete method for implementing human respect, and stage 1 (reward design) is its first stage.
- Because a bot operates on the basis of its reward function, embedding human respect into the reward function itself is the most important starting point.
- A design that rewards performance alone and ignores human harm is a mistaken design, and a correct design must have the structure "reward = task performance + human satisfaction – human-harm risk – norm-violation penalty".
- This stage 1 becomes the premise for why the subsequent stage 2 (mandatory rules) separately places an absolute prohibition line that operates independently of reward — because reward design alone is not sufficient.
