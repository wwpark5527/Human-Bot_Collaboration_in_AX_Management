# Stage 2 (Hard Rules) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Among the five architectural stages for implementing human respect, Stage 2 (hard rules) is the stage following Stage 1 (reward design), and it responds to violations that reward-function design alone cannot prevent.
- Hard rules constitute an absolutely forbidden zone where "crossing the line ends it," regardless of reward, and harmful actions, deception/manipulation, and illegal acts fall within it.
- Because this absolute prohibition line must operate regardless of the result of reward calculation, it must be implemented as a separate enforcement mechanism that cannot be bypassed during reward optimization.
- If Stage 1's reward design is a soft approach that reflects human harm as a penalty factor, Stage 2's hard rules are a hard approach that excludes specific actions outright.
