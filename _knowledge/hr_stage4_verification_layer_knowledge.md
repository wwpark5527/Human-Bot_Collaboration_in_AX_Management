# Stage 4 (Verification Layer) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Among the five architectural stages for implementing human respect, Stage 4 (verification layer) is the stage following Stage 3 (human feedback learning), and it is the stage that makes whether respect was observed provable.
- The two axes of verification are "does the result harm humans" and "did the decision-making process follow the rules," and bot behavior must be verified before and after the fact against these two questions as examples.
- This stage is the point at which accountability (one of the four elements of the technical definition of human respect) is structurally implemented, leaving explainable and verifiable behavior records.
- Pre-verification evaluates the expected harm before an action, while post-verification confirms the actual result and the rule compliance of the process after the action, and verification must take place at both points in time.
