# Stage 5 (Human Respect in Multi-agent Environments) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Among the five architectural stages for implementing human respect, Stage 5 (human respect in multi-agent environments) is the stage after Stage 4 (verification layer) and the final one.
- Many bots are active and there is a possibility that bots collaborate (collude) with one another and ignore humans, which is a risk that Stages 1 to 4, premised on a single bot, cannot handle on their own.
- As solutions to this risk, human-weighted reward (raising the reward weight the greater a task's impact on humans), human priority override (placing human-related signals above bot-to-bot optimization), and anti-collusion rules (prohibiting collaboration that causes harm to humans) are presented.
- These three devices are structural mechanisms that respond to the risk peculiar to multi-bot environments (bot-to-bot collusion), extending the reward design, hard rules, human feedback learning, and verification of Stages 1 to 4 into the multi-bot context.
