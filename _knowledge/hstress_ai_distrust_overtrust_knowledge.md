# Stress from distrust or overtrust of AI — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- As the fourth type among 'human stress types (5 types)', it comes after cognitive overload (the third type) and arises from the state of tension itself in which the human wavers between the two extremes of distrust and overtrust toward AI.
- The representative case of AI distrust is "what if the AI is wrong? what about hallucination? what about manipulated data?", and the representative case of AI overtrust is "the AI recommended it, so it must be right? weakening of judgment, responsibility avoidance".
- This state of tension itself becomes continuous stress, and on the overtrust side in particular the concrete risks of a weakening of judgment and responsibility avoidance follow.
- Together with the social and relational stress that follows, it constitutes the end of the human stress types (5 types), and in that the deepening of this stress can lead to a collapse of human-AI trust, its context is also connected to the 'trust' stress among the four dangerous stresses.

## Source Evidence Quotation
