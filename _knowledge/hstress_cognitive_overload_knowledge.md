# Cognitive Overload — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- As the third type among 'human stress types (5 types)', it comes after replacement anxiety (the second type) and arises as, in an AX organization, the human becomes not a simple executor but a meta-manager who supervises, orchestrates, and verifies AI.
- The core criterion is that the objects to be managed simultaneously increase to include 'multiple AI agents, real-time data, automated workflows, verification of AI recommendations, governance compliance', and so on.
- The stress that arises at this point includes decision fatigue, attention fragmentation, and continuous monitoring burden.
- Together with the stress that comes from distrust or overtrust of AI that follows, it constitutes the latter part of the human stress types (5 types), and it is connected in that whereas cognitive overload is a problem of 'how much one supervises', AI distrust/overtrust is a problem of 'how much one trusts that supervisory judgment'.
