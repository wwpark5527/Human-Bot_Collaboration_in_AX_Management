# Replacement Anxiety — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- As the second type among 'human stress types (5 types)', it comes after role ambiguity (the first type) and arises from the experience of the threat that one's own expertise will become inferior to AI.
- The representative threat perceptions are "Is my expertise becoming inferior to AI? Am I being reduced to a supporting role? Is AI taking away promotion opportunities?".
- The core output is that it can develop beyond traditional job insecurity into existential anxiety and an identity crisis, and that it is experienced especially strongly by knowledge workers.
- Together with the Cognitive Overload that follows, it constitutes the middle part of the human stress types (5 types), and it is distinguished in that whereas role ambiguity is a problem of 'who does it', replacement anxiety is a problem of the value of existence, 'am I still needed'.
