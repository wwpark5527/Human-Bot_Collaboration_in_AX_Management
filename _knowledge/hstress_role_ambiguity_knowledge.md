# Role Ambiguity — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- As the first type among 'human stress types (5 types)', human stress is basically psychological, social, cognitive, and identity-related tension, and among these, role ambiguity originates in the unclearness of the division of roles between humans and bots.
- Examples of the determining questions are "Does the human do only strategy? Does the bot go as far as decision-making? Who is the ultimate responsible party?", and if these questions remain unresolved, role conflict and role ambiguity increase.
- In particular, conflict between human judgment and AI recommendation, damage to human self-esteem when the AI is more accurate, and the problem of attributing responsibility in case of failure are presented as representative situations that induce stress.
- Together with the Replacement Anxiety that follows, it constitutes the front part of the human stress types (5 types), and both types share the commonality of starting from the unclearness of the human-bot boundary of roles and capabilities.

## Source Evidence Quotation
> "Role Ambiguity: when humans and bots work together, "who is responsible for what?" becomes unclear."
