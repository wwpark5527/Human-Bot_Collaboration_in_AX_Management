# Human-AI Collaboration Ability — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Human-AI collaboration ability is the second of the six items in 'Requirements for AX-Organization Talent: The Competencies of Surviving Humans,' and it is the practical competency at the stage following AI comprehension and affinity.
- In an AX organization, humans do not work alone but divide roles with, or collaborate with, bots.
- The core elements of judgment are three: what to entrust to AI, what humans should do, and how to connect them.
- The aim is to become a collaborative human rather than a human who works alone.
