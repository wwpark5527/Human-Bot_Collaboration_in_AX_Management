# Human Stress Related to AI — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Humans carry considerable stress in relation to AI, such as fear of replacement, fear of surveillance, identity threat, a sense of lost competence, loss of meaning, and concern over damage to humanity.
- In reality most AI adoption failures are not technology failures but human reaction failures.
- This stress is presented as the second human characteristic a bot must understand, following human illogicality (behavior driven by emotion, relationships, symbols, identity, and habit).
- When designing AI adoption, failures can be reduced only when human psychological reactions are addressed together with technical completeness, not technical completeness alone.
