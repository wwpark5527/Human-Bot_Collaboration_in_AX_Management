# Teamwork in the Human-Bond Aspect — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Until the author (Park Won-woo) distinguished (expanded) the concept of teamwork into two aspects, academia and industry understood teamwork as human bonding alone.
- Teamwork in the human-bond aspect is the psychological attraction among members to like one another, band together, and help one another, and it is measured by group cohesiveness.
- In terms of the fit concept, it corresponds to supplementary fit, which fits well because members are similar to one another.
- This aspect is shown to have a significant effect on the perceived performance of the department as recognized by members but not to have a significant effect on the actual performance evaluated by the company, and it is therefore positioned as an additional condition to attend to over the medium and long term.
- It has been demonstrated that departmental teamwork and actual performance become highest when it is combined with teamwork in the task-activation aspect (measured by TRB).

## Source Evidence Quotation
