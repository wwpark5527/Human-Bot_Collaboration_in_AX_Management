# Human-Bot Sociality — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Human-bot sociality is the axis of the bot's sociality that deals with the relationship with humans, and it is an asymmetric trust and control relationship.
- The character of the relationship is asymmetric (master-tool or supervisor-supervised), and its basis is trust, understanding, and safety.
- From the human's standpoint, trust and understandability are central, and the human demands explainability, alignment, and safety.
- From the bot's standpoint, the human is recognized not as a partner in cooperation but as the highest-order constraint and reward definer.
- On failure, the risk appears as an external (social) problem of harm to humans.

## Source Evidence Quotation
