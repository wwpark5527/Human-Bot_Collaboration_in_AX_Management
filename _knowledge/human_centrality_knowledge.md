# Human Centrality — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Human centrality, as the basic premise of the core-spirit system of management (basic premise + 3 spirits), is the foundation that precedes the three core spirits of augmentation, respect for and use of diversity, and pursuit of complementary fit.
- This book holds that whether it is the technical advancement of AI or the realization of organizational AX, both presuppose 'the survival and development of humans,' and it rejects AI and AX harmful to them.
- The core point is that the share (subject) of judging whether something is harmful or helpful also rests with the human — both the criterion of judgment and the subject of judgment are human-centered.
- Human centrality does not mean zero-sum thinking of the form 'humans always come first, humans are in the position of the boss, and bots must be sacrificed' — this matters because only by excluding such thinking can the spirit of augmentation (mutual learning and co-evolution rather than replacement) be established.
- So that human centrality does not fall into zero-sum, the three core spirits of augmentation, respect for and use of diversity, and pursuit of complementary fit must follow.
