# Human Illogicality — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- There is a fundamental difference in that AI pursues consistency, optimization, and efficiency while humans are moved by emotion, relationships, symbols, identity, and habit.
- A system that is efficient but disliked by people fails, and even one that is logically correct is rejected if it cannot be trusted.
- Emotional resistance can become a cause of organizational AX failure.
- This characteristic is treated as the first of the human characteristics a bot must understand (human emotion, motivation, anxiety, sociality, irrationality) as presented in G. Gibbins's Welcome to your human-AI team!.
