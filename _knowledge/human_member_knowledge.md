# H: Human — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The members of an AX organization are divided not into a 'human vs AI bot' opposition but into the four types H (human)/AH (augmented human)/AB (augmented bot)/B (bot).
- Among these, H refers to the non-augmented human, that is, the traditional human member.
- The keyword of the member classification of an AX organization is augmentation, and its object is not limited to humans but applies to bots as well.
- A work from three years earlier (Hubbell, 2023) classified members as human/augmented human/non-human, but this book adds the augmented bot (AB) to complete the four-type system of H/AH/AB/B.
- This classification reappears in Chapter 4 (HBRM): if existing HRM was 'H-centered' management, HBRM expands into 'H+AH+AB+B-centered' management, and HBRM's 'member definition' role is to define the positions and roles of H, AH, B, and AB.
