# Internalization of Respect for Humans — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Among all education for bots, respect for humans is the most important of all, and the three laws of robotics are introduced as the most classical and representative content imposing the duty of respect for humans and safety on robots.
- Teaching respect for humans to a bot is not education but designing the system so that only actions that do not infringe upon humans are selected. That is, it is not a problem of moral injection but a problem of redesigning the action, reward, and verification structure around humans.
- Because making a bot understand respect for humans is incomplete, one must instead make it disadvantageous within the system not to respect humans.
- The internalization of respect for humans is subsequently made concrete in the technical definition of respect for humans (four items: non-harm, respect for autonomy, fairness, accountability) and in the five-stage architecture for implementing respect for humans (reward design, hard rules, human feedback learning, verification layer, respect for humans in a multi-agent environment).
