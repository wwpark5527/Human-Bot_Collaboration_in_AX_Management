# Protection of Humanity (humanity) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The protection of humanity is the top-level concept an AX-organization leader must think about when judging essence and direction (or alignment with orchestration).
- A leader of a hybrid organization must ensure that AI is aligned with human existence and development.
- This principle sits immediately after problem-definition ability and meaning-design ability, and immediately before the explanation of human-AI orchestration ability — it is the criterion that must be established first, before coordination, integration, and design ability are exercised.
- In a footnote, this is linked to the core concept of S. Russell's Human Compatible (2019): the problem awareness that AI becomes dangerous if it misunderstands human goals (= AX fails), and that the core of organizational AX is not to use AI a great deal but to align it with human purposes and organizational purposes.
