# True AX Organization / Hybrid Organization (AH+AB) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- A hybrid organization (a true AX organization) is an organization in which both humans and bots have broken away from their existing state and transitioned into a mutually augmented state, and those who have actually experienced it call this a 'hypered state' or a 'third state,' while some call it 'hybrid minds.'
- This is the organizational form reached when the AH+AB type among the human-bot coexistence and cooperation types (WalkOrder 32, `COOP_TYPE_AH_PLUS_AB`) is actually realized, and it stands in an inseparable relation with that cooperation type.
- HRM (Human Resource Management) took only humans as its object, but in a hybrid organization where the organizational members are expanded to H+AH+AB+B, the management system must be converted to HBRM (Human Bot Resource Management). HBRM is a move from efficiency-centered management to co-evolution-centered management.
- A hybrid organization is a hybrid intelligent organization that cooperates on a role basis and continuously learns and adapts, and the essence of that cooperation is not simple automation but a collaborative cognition system centered on roles rather than jobs, based on the shared/governance context and AI governance.
- Compared with the existing organization (H+H) and the early stage of AI introduction (H+B), the decisive difference of an AX organization (AH+AB) is that the accountability structure connects to clear verification and final approval, and that individual performance is converted into the organization's operational assets.
