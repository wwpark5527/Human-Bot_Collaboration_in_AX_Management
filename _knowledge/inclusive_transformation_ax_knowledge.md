# Inclusive Transformation AX — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Inclusive Transformation AX (`INCLUSIVE_TRANSFORMATION_AX`, S2C-0138) is an organizational-transformation framework that redesigns the efficiency-centered AI transition of the AI era from the perspective of responsibility and inclusiveness, preventing and mitigating AI stratification and protecting humans' role, right to judge, right to learn, right to transition, right to appeal, and right to share in performance.
- The author diagnoses that the current mainstream of global AX "is unfolding centered on efficiency, productivity, cost reduction, automation, and operational optimization," and that this "is necessary in terms of corporate competitiveness but is not sufficient." He therefore argues that "AX must be expanded from an efficiency-centered transition into Inclusive Transformation AX."
- Inclusive Transformation AX does not deny efficiency-centered AX — the relation is defined by the contrast that "efficiency-centered AX makes 'an organization more efficient with AI.' Inclusive Transformation AX makes 'an organization that is more efficient with AI while humans' capabilities and rights are not damaged.'"
- The six things Inclusive Transformation AX seeks to protect are normative and operational protective principles specified in a footnote: role, right to judge, right to learn, right to transition, right to appeal, and right to share in performance (footnote 63) — these are not legal rights but "normative and operational protective principles that must be guaranteed in the operation of organizations in the AI era."
- Inclusive Transformation AX is presented in this book as the realization method of the expansion of the ESG concept (footnote 64) — in the ESG connection item, efficiency-centered AX is "limited," whereas Inclusive Transformation AX is positioned as "the core execution structure of S and G."
