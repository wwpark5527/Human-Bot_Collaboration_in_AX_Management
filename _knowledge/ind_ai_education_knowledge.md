# AI Education — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- "AI education" (`IND_AI_EDUCATION`, S2C-0514) is the **second indicator** constituting the "12 Inclusive Transition ESG indicators" (`INCLUSIVE_TRANSITION_ESG_12_INDICATORS`, S2C-0158, class INDEX). Definition: "an indicator that looks at the degree to which AI-literacy education and job-specific retraining are carried out." The criteria are "AI-literacy education completion rate and job-specific reskilling hours," and the output is "the education completion-rate figure and job-specific reskilling hours."
- The higher context of the 12 indicators as a whole is source line 531 — "Furthermore, AI inclusive-transition ESG must be measurable. The following indicators (provisionally called the 12 Inclusive Transition ESG indicators) are basic items for confirming whether an organization operates its AI transition responsibly." This indicator is placed at line 534 as the second item of that list, following the immediately preceding item, AI accessibility (line 533).
- **This indicator too looks at two values together** — the education "completion rate" and job-specific "reskilling hours". The grounds are that the source text wrote "AI-literacy education completion rate" and "job-specific reskilling hours" side by side within one item. The completion rate indicates how many people went through it, and the reskilling hours indicate how deeply the investment was made. If one looks only at the completion rate, the figure can be filled with formal attendance; if one looks only at the hours, investment concentrated on a few can be mistaken for the achievement of the whole organization.
- **The ordering logic with respect to the first indicator.** If AI accessibility (line 533) asked whether one can reach the tools, this indicator asks whether the organization is actually building the capability to handle those tools. If access alone is opened and education does not follow, access remains a formality, so the very order of the list, education coming after accessibility, carries meaning.
- What this indicator measures corresponds to the second right in the rights layer. The second item of the 8 rights is "right of AI learning: AI literacy, reskilling, upskilling" (line 488), and this indicator confirms in figures how far that right of learning is actually guaranteed. The right prescribes what must be guaranteed (`RIGHT_AI_LEARNING`, WalkOrder 343), and the indicator counts how far it has been realized (this identity). Whereas the rights item in the source text enumerates the three of literacy, reskilling, and upskilling, the measured values of this indicator are the two of literacy completion rate and reskilling hours, so whether to aggregate upskilling separately must be decided by the organization at the measurement design stage.
- There is a corresponding point in the execution layer as well. "4. Labor transition" of the 9-step execution model (line 523, `STEP_LABOR_TRANSITION`, WalkOrder 353) is the step that performs "design job impact assessment, reskilling, role reconstruction". That is, what **executes** reskilling is step 4, and what **measures** how far that execution was carried out is this indicator. The two nodes belong to different layers, and the practical use of this indicator is to check whether reskilling hours were actually invested in the jobs judged to be heavily affected at step 4.
- The problem this indicator seeks to expose is itself already conceptualized in the preceding section. The second gap item in the discussion of AI-based stratification is `GAP_AI_CAPABILITY` (AI capability gap, WalkOrder 310), and the distribution of job-specific reskilling hours in this indicator becomes the tool for confirming whether that gap is narrowing within the organization.
- In the Stage-1 structure, S1C-183 was classified as class **INDEX** (a value transferred as is from the Stage-1 C0 row), and in Stage-2 it was SPLIT and divided into individual indicator fragments. This candidate is the **second fragment** of that.
- **Note the placement of the source list.** The list of 12 indicators is not continuous. The indicators continue at lines 533-539, then at line 541 a paragraph concerning the EU AI Act, UNESCO, and the OECD is inserted, and the remaining indicators resume at lines 543-548. This identity (line 534) lies before that interruption point and is unaffected, but when quoting the latter part of the list the line numbers must be confirmed individually.
