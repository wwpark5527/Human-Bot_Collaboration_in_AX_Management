# AI Utilization Capability — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- "AI utilization capability" (`IND_AI_UTILIZATION_CAPABILITY`, S2C-0515) is the **third indicator** constituting the "12 Inclusive Transition ESG indicators" (`INCLUSIVE_TRANSITION_ESG_12_INDICATORS`, S2C-0158, class INDEX). Definition: "an indicator that looks at the degree to which AI has been converted into actual work improvement and productivity gains, and the distribution thereof." The criteria are "AI-utilization work-improvement cases and productivity-gain distribution," and the output is "tally of work-improvement cases and productivity-gain distribution."
- The higher context of the 12 indicators as a whole is source line 531 — "Furthermore, AI inclusive-transition ESG must be measurable. The following indicators (provisionally called the 12 Inclusive Transition ESG indicators) are basic items for confirming whether an organization operates its AI transition responsibly." This indicator is placed at line 535 as the third item of that list.
- **This indicator too looks at two values together** — "work-improvement cases" and "productivity-gain distribution". The grounds are that the source text wrote the two side by side within one item. The cases leave concrete evidence of what AI utilization actually changed in the work, and the distribution exposes where within the organization that change took hold. If one looks only at the cases, it amounts to displaying a few successful instances; if one looks only at the gain figures, whose gains they are disappears.
- **The word 'distribution' is the core of this indicator.** The source text wrote not "productivity-gain rate" or "productivity-gain average" but "productivity-gain **distribution**". A company-wide average gain rate creates the illusion that gains concentrated in some departments or jobs are the performance of the whole organization. Demanding the distribution means that this indicator is designed to measure not only the size of the gains but also **whether the gains are skewed**, and this is the point that makes this indicator an inclusive-transition indicator rather than a simple productivity indicator.
- **The ordering logic with respect to the preceding two indicators.** AI accessibility (line 533) asked whether one can reach the tools, and AI education (line 534) asked whether the capability to handle those tools was built. This indicator asks whether the access and education thus secured have been **converted into actual performance**. If there is a range where access and education are in place yet utilization performance does not appear, that is a signal that the problem lies not in access or education but on the side of work design, authority, or tool fit, so reading the three indicators side by side is the practical use of this indicator.
- **It must be distinguished from the rights layer.** The third item of the 8 rights is "right of AI utilization: the right to use AI for the extension of human capability rather than for the replacement of repetitive work" (line 489, `RIGHT_AI_UTILIZATION`, WalkOrder 344). The right is an entitlement prescribing **for what purpose AI must be usable**, and this indicator is a measurement item that **counts** how much work improvement and productivity gain that utilization actually led to and how it is distributed. The two nodes overlap in name but differ in layer. This identity does not use the language of rights ("the right to ~", "guarantees") but only the language of measurement (number of cases, gain values, distribution, measurement cycle, time series).
- The problem this indicator seeks to expose is itself already conceptualized in the preceding section. The capability gap item in the discussion of AI-based stratification is `GAP_AI_CAPABILITY` (AI capability gap, WalkOrder 310), and the productivity-gain distribution of this indicator becomes the tool for confirming whether that gap is narrowing or widening within the organization. If gains appear concentrated in a particular group, that is at once a performance and a signal of a widening gap.
- In the Stage-1 structure, S1C-183 was classified as class **INDEX** (a value transferred as is from the Stage-1 C0 row), and in Stage-2 it was SPLIT and divided into individual indicator fragments. This candidate is the **third fragment** of that.
- **Note the placement of the source list.** The list of 12 indicators is not continuous. The indicators continue at lines 533-539, then at line 541 a paragraph concerning the EU AI Act, UNESCO, and the OECD is inserted, and the remaining indicators resume at lines 543-548. This identity (line 535) lies before that interruption point and is unaffected, but when quoting the latter part of the list the line numbers must be confirmed individually.
