# Competence-Inferiority Type — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- It is the second type among the 'individual human reaction and backlash types (5 types)', a reaction that appears after the survival-anxiety type, and it arises from directly comparing one's own competence with that of AI.
- The competence-inferiority type is the concretization, at the level of the individual member, of the competence layer (core question 'Am I inferior to AI?') in the model of the 4 layers of human reaction (survival, competence, relationship, existence).
- It consists of the four characteristics of learning fatigue, digital inferiority, self-deprecation arising from differences in AI usage ability, and the sense of technological alienation among middle-aged and older members, and the representative cases are withdrawal within the organization caused by differences in the speed of generative AI utilization, the perception that "only young employees will survive," and pressure felt toward colleagues who submit AI-based reports in meetings.
- Such reactions do not stop at individual emotional reactions but connect to the problem of 'AI competence inequality', and can exert a structural impact on the organization as gaps between generations and between job groups.
