# Control-Loss Type — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- It is the third type among the 'individual human reaction and backlash types (5 types)', appears after the competence-inferiority type, and the expansion of AI recommendation and automated decision-making is its direct trigger.
- The control-loss type is the concretization, at the level of the individual member, of the relationship layer (core question 'Is my position in the organization being weakened?') in the model of the 4 layers of human reaction (survival, competence, relationship, existence).
- It consists of the four characteristics of manager resistance, defense of expert authority, algorithmic distrust, and refusal of recommendations made by AI, and the representative cases are physicians' refusal of AI diagnostic assistance, HR's distrust of AI hiring recommendations, and backlash against AI risk models in the financial sector.
- This reaction is not a simple rejection of technology but comes down to the problem of the locus of authority and responsibility, "who is the final judge?", and it is especially conspicuous in job groups that hold expertise and authority.
