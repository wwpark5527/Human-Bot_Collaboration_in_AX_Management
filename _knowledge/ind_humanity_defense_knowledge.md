# Humanity-Defense Type — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- It is the fifth and last type among the 'individual human reaction and backlash types (5 types)', and it is the most higher-order reaction, appearing after the survival-anxiety type, the competence-inferiority type, the control-loss type, and the surveillance-fear type.
- The humanity-defense type is the concretization, at the level of the individual member, of the existence layer (core question 'Is the meaning of the human being diminished?', representative emotion identity confusion) in the model of the 4 layers of human reaction (survival, competence, relationship, existence).
- It consists of the four characteristics of defending human creativity, emphasizing human relationality, asserting the superiority of emotion, ethics, and intuition, and the reaction that "AI does not understand humans," and the representative cases are artists' backlash against generative AI, professors' antipathy toward AI writing, and the emphasis on human centrality in the counseling and education fields.
- This reaction is a reaction protecting human uniqueness that goes beyond practical anxiety about jobs, competence, and authority, and when individual anxiety becomes collectivized it can develop into collective reaction and backlash types such as the labor-union type of backlash.
