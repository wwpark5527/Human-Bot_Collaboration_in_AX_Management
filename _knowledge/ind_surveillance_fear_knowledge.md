# Surveillance-Fear Type — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- It is the fourth type among the 'individual human reaction and backlash types (5 types)', appears after the control-loss type, and the datafication, logging, and tracking that accompany the AX organization are its direct trigger.
- As the organization comes to systematically collect and analyze members' behavior and performance data, the perception at the level of the individual member of being continuously observed appears as the surveillance-fear type.
- It consists of the four characteristics of anxiety over behavior monitoring, concern about privacy infringement, fear of AI evaluation, and stress from work logs, and the representative cases are backlash against productivity-tracking AI tools, antipathy toward keyboard and mouse tracking systems, and rejection of AI meeting-analysis systems.
- Whereas the other individual reaction types are anxieties about jobs, competence, and authority, the surveillance-fear type is a reaction that arises on the separate axis of privacy and continuous observation.
