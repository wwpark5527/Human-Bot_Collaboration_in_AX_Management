# Survival-Anxiety Type — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- As the first type among the 'Individual human reaction/resistance types (5 types)', it is the most primal reaction among the five types of survival-anxiety type, competence-inferiority type, control-loss type, surveillance-fear type, and humanity-defense type.
- The survival-anxiety type is the concretization at the level of the individual member of the survival layer (core question 'Will my job disappear?', representative emotion anxiety and fear) of the model of the four layers of human reaction (survival, ability, relationship, existence).
- It is composed of the four features of fear of restructuring, concern over role reduction, concern over reduced promotion opportunities, and anxiety over wage decline, and its representative cases are backlash against the introduction of AI in call centers, office workers' anxiety over generative-AI-based document automation, and developers' unease with AI coding tools.
- It appears especially strongly in repetitive knowledge-work job groups, because the more repetitive and standardized the work, the more intuitively the possibility of AI replacement is perceived. When individual anxiety becomes collective, it can develop into social, political, and institutional reactions such as union-type backlash.
