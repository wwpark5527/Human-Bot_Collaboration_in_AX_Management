# Interplace — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Interplace is an HR and organization-management software tool that was born on the basis of the research achievements of Dr. M. Belbin and was developed by Belbin Associates over decades. It was released in 1988, and version 8 (Interplace 8) is currently in general use.
- The Korean edition has been provided by Belbin Korea from Interplace 5 in 1998 through to the current Interplace 8.
- It is a set of software built to synthesize data related to individuals and jobs so as to be used for team building, appraisal, counseling, placement, manager training, career development, and more, and more than 40% of the UK's top 100 companies use it on a standing basis.
- It uses 4 kinds of questionnaire: two questionnaires that assess people (the self-perception inventory SPI, the observer assessment OA) and two questionnaires that assess jobs (the job requirement exercise JRE, the job observer assessment JOA).
- It processes the collected data in real time and outputs 14 kinds of report content in the language the client wants.
- Since the subject's own subjective responses are combined with the objective responses of those around them (4 or more observers), and the diagnostic process mixes relative assessment with absolute assessment, it is known to have the highest reliability and validity as a measurement instrument.
- Responses are transmitted by email to a UK server, and several minutes after the responses are completed the results are delivered by e-mail (or via the client company's person in charge).
