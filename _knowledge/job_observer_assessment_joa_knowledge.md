# Job Observer Assessment (JOA: job observer assessment) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- JOA is one of the two questionnaires that assess jobs (JRE, JOA) among the 4 questionnaires (SPI/OA/JRE/JOA) that Interplace uses.
- It is the same as JRE in that the assessment target is a job, but it is distinguished in that the basis of response is not the job requirements (JRE) but the results of observation by a job observer (job-observation items).
- The JOA data is processed together with the job-requirements (JRE) data as input to job assessment, and becomes the basis for deriving job characteristics and for staffing and selection advice.
- The results of SPI+OA (person assessment) and JRE+JOA (job assessment) are entered into Interplace together and are processed and output in real time as 14 kinds of report.
