# Job Requirement Exercise (JRE: job requirement exercise) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The JRE is one of the two questionnaires that assess a job (JRE, JOA) among the 4 questionnaires Interplace uses (SPI/OA/JRE/JOA).
- It is fundamentally distinguished from the SPI and OA (person assessment) in that the assessment target is not a person but a job.
- The basis for responses is the requirements that the job demands (job-requirements items), and in this respect it is also distinguished from the JOA, which is based on the results of observing the job.
- JRE data is processed as job-assessment material for what TRs the job should be composed of, and coupled with an individual's TR characteristics it becomes the grounds for the person-job fit judgment.
