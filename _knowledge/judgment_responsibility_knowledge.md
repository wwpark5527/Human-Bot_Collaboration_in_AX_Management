# Judgment Responsibility — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Judgment responsibility is the second of the three responsibilities (context, judgment, evidence) that AX-organization talent bears when working with AI.
- The premise is the principle that AI can generate many alternatives, but the final judgment must be made by a person.
- AX-organization talent must judge whether the AI result fits the organization's purpose, whether it can be used in the work, and whether further approval or review is needed.
- Judgment items: accuracy of the result, organizational suitability, releasability to the outside, admissibility for reflection in decision-making, need for further review, need for supervisor approval / legal / security review.

## Source Evidence Quotation
> "AI can generate many alternatives, but the final judgment must be made by a person. AX-organization talent must judge whether the AI result fits the organization's purpose, whether it can be used in the work, and whether further approval or review is needed: is this result correct / is this result suitable for our organization / may this result be released externally / may this result be reflected in decision-making / is further review needed / is supervisor approval or legal/security review needed."
