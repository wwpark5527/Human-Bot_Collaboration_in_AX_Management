# knowledge — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- knowledge is `S2C-0440`, the first, entry node among the 8 nodes of the `knowledge-action chain` (S2C-0115, `KNOWLEDGE_ACTION_CHAIN`) (knowledge→skill→runtime→action→outcome→review→feedback→context).
- The knowledge chain is the flow in which data becomes information, information is organized into knowledge, and that knowledge is connected to other knowledge. The 'knowledge' node of the knowledge-action chain corresponds to the product of this knowledge chain.
- Knowledge alone is not enough: it connects data and creates the basis for judgment, but it is merely a "structure of knowing" that does not yet include execution and results.
- The next node is `skill` (KNOWLEDGE_ACTION_CHAIN_NODE_SKILL), the stage that turns knowledge into an executable form.
