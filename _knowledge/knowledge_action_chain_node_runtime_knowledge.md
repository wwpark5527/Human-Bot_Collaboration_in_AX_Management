# runtime (SkillRuntime) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- runtime (SkillRuntime) is `S2C-0442`, the third of the 8 nodes of the `knowledge-action chain` (S2C-0115, `KNOWLEDGE_ACTION_CHAIN`). It is located after `skill` (KNOWLEDGE_ACTION_CHAIN_NODE_SKILL) and before `action (execution)` (KNOWLEDGE_ACTION_CHAIN_NODE_ACTION).
- The knowledge-action chain turns knowledge into a skill, and turns that skill into an actual execution structure. What matters here is the SkillRuntime.
- The SkillRuntime defines seven things: what is the input, what materials will be used, what tools will be used, what must not be done, what is the result format, who reviews and approves, where it is recorded.
- Only with this structure does a skill work in real tasks. If it is not defined, the skill still remains an abstract concept.
