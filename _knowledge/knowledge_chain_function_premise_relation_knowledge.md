# Premise-Relation Measurement — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Premise-relation measurement is `S2C-0437`, the second function of the `functions of the knowledge chain (4 major functions)` (S2C-0109, `KNOWLEDGE_CHAIN_FUNCTIONS`). It is located after structural-distance measurement and before reasoning-chain fidelity measurement.
- As the function that checks the connection state between a higher concept and a lower concept, it detects the state of speaking about an advanced concept without understanding the concept that underlies it.
- It is particularly important in the AI era, because AI easily provides advanced expressions, but whether the human actually understands the premise of that expression is a separate matter.
- The criterion is whether one actually understands the foundational concept that is the premise of the advanced expression (whether one is in a state where the words are advanced but the foundational concept is weak).
