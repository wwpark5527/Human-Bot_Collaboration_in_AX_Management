# Reasoning-Chain Fidelity Measurement — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Reasoning-chain fidelity measurement is `S2C-0438`, the third function of the `functions of the knowledge chain (4 major functions)` (S2C-0109, `KNOWLEDGE_CHAIN_FUNCTIONS`). It is located after premise-relation measurement and before transferability measurement.
- It is the function that evaluates not whether the conclusion was correct but the validity of the process leading to the result.
- There are four criteria: are the intermediate steps valid, does each step lead to the next step, does the last step actually support the answer, is each step connected to evidence.
- The output is the result of evaluating process validity, and it is a judgment on the reasoning path itself rather than on the result.
