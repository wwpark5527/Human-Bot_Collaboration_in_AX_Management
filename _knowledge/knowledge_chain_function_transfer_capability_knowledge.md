# Transferability Measurement — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Transferability measurement is `S2C-0439`, the fourth and last function of the `functions of the knowledge chain (4 major functions)` (S2C-0109, `KNOWLEDGE_CHAIN_FUNCTIONS`). It is located after reasoning-chain fidelity measurement, and the next identity is `meaning-cognitive distance` (MEANING_COGNITIVE_DISTANCE).
- Transfer is the ability to apply what one has learned to a new situation. In the AI era transfer is central, because true understanding is not the ability to solve a familiar problem but the ability to maintain the meaning structure even in a new situation.
- High transferability means four things: understanding is maintained even when the sentence changes, the principle is maintained even when the example changes, the structure is applied even when the domain changes, and the concept is revised even when a counterexample arises.
- Transferability is one of the eight elements that make up the knowledge-chain-based meaning-cognitive distance (conceptual structure, mastery, belief structure, premise relation, reasoning-chain fidelity, reliability of grounds, transferability, epistemic maturity).
