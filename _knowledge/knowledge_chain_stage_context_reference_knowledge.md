# Organizational Context Reference — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Organizational context reference is `S2C-0431`, the second stage of the circulation structure of the `knowledge chain` (S2C-0108, `KNOWLEDGE_CHAIN`).
- It is the stage that interprets a question against the organization's common context (purpose, standards, roles, sources, format), and it is the node that brings in the "organization's history, intent, norms, and memory" that existing LLMs lack.
- One of the problems of existing LLMs is that "there are per-user differences in interpretation" (line 254). Organizational context reference resolves this problem by referencing the organization's standards.
- The criterion is whether the answer was produced by referencing the organization's standards, without being swayed by per-user differences in interpretation.
