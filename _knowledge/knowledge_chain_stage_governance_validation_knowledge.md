# Governance Validation — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Governance validation is `S2C-0432`, the third stage of the circulation structure of the `knowledge chain` (S2C-0108, `KNOWLEDGE_CHAIN`).
- It is the stage that confirms whether the referenced context and the response to be generated pass the organization's authority, security, verification, and approval standards; it is located after organizational context reference and before response generation.
- One of the problems of existing LLMs is that "verification/accountability tracing is difficult" (line 254). Governance validation is the stage for resolving this defect.
- The criterion is whether verification and accountability tracing are possible, and this is the point at which four of the seven elements of the governance context treated earlier — authority, security, verification, and approval — are actually applied.
