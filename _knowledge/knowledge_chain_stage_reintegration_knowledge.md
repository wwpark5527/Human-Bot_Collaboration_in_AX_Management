# Re-reflection into Organizational Knowledge — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Re-reflection into organizational knowledge is `S2C-0434`, the sixth stage of the circulation structure of the `knowledge chain` (S2C-0108, `KNOWLEDGE_CHAIN`).
- It is located after result recording and before reuse by the next AI/human, and it is the stage that returns the recorded result back into the organization's knowledge and standards.
- Through this stage, knowledge becomes not "scattered responses" but a system that is connected, verified, and evolves within the organization (line 256).
- The criterion is whether the result is absorbed into organizational knowledge and changes the standard for the next judgment.
- The output is an updated organizational knowledge system, and this becomes the input of the next stage, reuse by the next AI/human.
