# Result Recording — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Result recording is `S2C-0433`, the fifth stage of the circulation structure of the `knowledge chain` (S2C-0108, `KNOWLEDGE_CHAIN`).
- It is located after response generation and before re-reflection into organizational knowledge, and it is the stage that leaves the generated result and its grounds and process within the organization.
- Existing LLMs had the defect that "verification/accountability tracing is difficult" (line 254). Result recording is the stage for resolving this defect, and it creates a traceable record in which grounds, process, and version remain.
- The criterion is whether the grounds of the result are recorded so that one can later trace why it is correct, where it went wrong, and what premises were used.
- The output is a traceable record (grounds, process, version), and this becomes the input of the next stage, re-reflection into organizational knowledge.

## Source Evidence Quotation
> "By contrast, a common/governance-context-based AX system has a circulation/accumulation structure of 'question → organizational context reference → governance validation → response generation → result recording → re-reflection into organizational knowledge → reuse by the next AI/human.'"

> "The problem of existing LLMs is that they answer anew for each question, that the organization's history, intent, norms, and memory are lacking, that there are per-user differences in interpretation, that consistency of results is lacking, and that verification/accountability tracing is difficult — there is a response but the continuity (chain) of organizational knowledge is weak."
