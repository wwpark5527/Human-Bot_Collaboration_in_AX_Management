# Korean Government (Human-Centered AI Ethics Standards) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- They are standards announced by the Ministry of Science and ICT in 2020, taking "human-centered artificial intelligence" as their highest value.
- The intent is that AI must not infringe on human autonomy and must contribute to the welfare of humankind.
- The 3 basic principles: the principle of human dignity, the principle of the public good of society, and the principle of the purposiveness of technology.
- The 10 core requirements: guaranteeing human rights, protecting privacy, respecting diversity, prohibition of infringement (harmlessness), fairness, safety, accountability, transparency, data management, and explainability.
- Besides the four types of AI ethics standards authoritatively referenced worldwide (UNESCO, OECD, EU, IEEE), they are treated together as Korea's national-level standard.
