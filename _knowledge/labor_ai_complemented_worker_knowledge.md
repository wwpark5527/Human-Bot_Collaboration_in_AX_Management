# AI-Complemented Worker — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The AI-complemented worker (`LABOR_AI_COMPLEMENTED_WORKER`, S2C-0473) is the first of the 4 types of labor differentiation in the AI era (AI-complemented / AI-managed / at risk of AI displacement-reduction / context-designing).
- The criterion is "whether productivity and decision-making ability improve through the use of AI," and the change in the organization/labor field is "being demanded higher performance in less time."
- As background to the change in the labor market, the author cites the IMF (2024) analysis that "about 60% of jobs in advanced economies may be affected by AI," and the ILO (2025) analysis that "generative AI is more likely to transform jobs at the task level than to replace entire occupations at once."
- It stands in contrast to the remaining three types (AI-managed worker, AI-displacement/reduction-risk worker, context-designer AX talent); it belongs to the side whose capability improves by actively using AI, but it has the two-sidedness that the demand for performance rises just as much.
- The author emphasizes: "The core is not only the number of jobs. What matters more is how the worker's role, authority, mode of evaluation, and bargaining power change."
