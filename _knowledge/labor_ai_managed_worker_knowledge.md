# AI-managed worker — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The AI-managed worker (`LABOR_AI_MANAGED_WORKER`, S2C-0474) is the second of the four labor-differentiation types of the AI era: a worker placed in a position where their work is assigned, evaluated, and monitored by algorithms.
- The criterion is "whether the worker is assigned, evaluated, and monitored by algorithms," and the change in the organization/labor field is "a state in which the standards of evaluation and assignment are hard to understand."
- This connects directly to the concept of algorithmic management treated earlier: "algorithmic management is a mode in which software and AI perform some or all of the work of a human manager," and its areas of application are "work assignment, performance evaluation, monitoring, schedule management, compensation, discipline, productivity measurement" and so on; in this case the worker is at once an agent using AI and an object evaluated and managed by AI.
- It stands in contrast to the preceding AI-complemented worker (the side whose capability improves by using AI), and this type can be seen as a case in which the AI surveillance gap (`GAP_AI_SURVEILLANCE`, "who manages with AI and who is managed by AI") is concretely realized in the labor market.
- It is the second position among the four types (complemented / managed / at risk of displacement-reduction / context-designing), and it leads on to the next type, the AI-displacement/reduction-risk worker.
