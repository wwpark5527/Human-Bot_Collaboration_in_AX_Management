# Context-designer AX talent — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The context-designer AX talent (`LABOR_CONTEXT_DESIGNER_AX_TALENT`, S2C-0476) is the fourth and last of the four labor-differentiation types of the AI era: a talent type who does not stop at merely using AI but turns AI into an operating structure of the organization by designing purposes, standards, verification, and feedback.
- The criterion is "whether the worker directly designs purposes, standards, verification, and feedback," and the output is "an output that turns AI into an operating structure of the organization (common context, verification and feedback standards)."
- Unlike the preceding three of the four types (AI-complemented worker, AI-managed worker, AI-displacement/reduction-risk worker), who are in the position of using AI or of being evaluated and managed by AI, this type stands in the active position of assigning purposes and standards to AI.
- Surveying the change in the labor market as a whole, the author emphasizes that "the core is not only the number of jobs; what matters more is how the worker's role, authority, mode of evaluation, and bargaining power change," and the context-designer AX talent holds the most advantageous position in this change of role and authority — the role of the agent who manages and designs AI.
- This type connects directly to the inclusive-transformation AX and the concept of context capital developed subsequently — context capital is defined as "the accumulated operating asset of the purposes, (judgment) standards, language, materials, formats, verification standards, and approval standards needed to make AI work properly," and the context-designer AX talent is precisely the agent who designs and accumulates this context capital.
