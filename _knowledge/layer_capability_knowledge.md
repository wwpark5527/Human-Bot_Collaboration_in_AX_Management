# Capability layer — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- It is the second of the four layers of human reaction (survival, capability, relationship, existence); where the survival layer is a threat to the continuation of the job, the capability layer originates in a threat to the relative position of one's competence.
- The capability layer is defined by the core question "Am I falling behind?" and the representative emotions "inferiority, helplessness," and this is a dimension of reaction distinct from the survival layer ("Is my job disappearing?" / anxiety and fear).
- Among the individual types of human reaction and backlash, the "competence-inferiority type" ("I am worse than AI") corresponds to the concrete manifestation of this capability layer, appearing as learning fatigue, digital inferiority, and the sense of technological alienation among middle-aged and older members.
- Because AX organizational transition means a restructuring of human roles, identity, power, relationships, and existential value, the reaction of the capability layer must likewise be understood not as a simple learning burden but as a composite reaction at the psychological and social dimensions.
