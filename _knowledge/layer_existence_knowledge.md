# Existence layer — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- It is the fourth and most fundamental of the four layers of human reaction (survival, capability, relationship, existence); where the relationship layer is the weakening of one's position and network within the organization, the existence layer originates in a threat to the very meaning of human existence.
- The existence layer is defined by the core question "Is the meaning of being human diminishing?" and the representative emotion "identity confusion," and it is a distinct and the deepest dimension of reaction, separate from the relationship layer ("Is my position in the organization weakening?" / alienation and defensiveness).
- Among the individual types of human reaction and backlash, the "humanity-defense type" ("what is human is disappearing") corresponds to the concrete manifestation of the existence layer, appearing as a reaction protecting human uniqueness.
- AX organizational transition is not a simple introduction of technology but a restructuring of human roles, identity, power, relationships, and existential value; and because AI bots, unlike the machines of the industrial revolution, penetrate even human cognition, judgment, creativity, and authority, backlash against AX is far more psychological and philosophical — this is the fundamental background against which the existence layer appears.
