# Relationship layer — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- It is the third of the four layers of human reaction (survival, capability, relationship, existence); whereas the capability layer is a threat to one's relative position in competence, the relationship layer originates from the threat of weakening one's position and network within the organization itself.
- The relationship layer is defined by the core question "Is my position in the organization weakening?" and by the representative emotions "alienation, defensiveness," and it is a separate dimension of reaction distinct from the capability layer ("Am I falling behind?" / inferiority, helplessness).
- Among the individual types of human reaction and backlash, the "control-loss type" ("my right of judgment is shrinking") is a manifestation adjacent to the relationship layer, in which a defensive attitude appears out of the perception that one's expertise and authority are threatened.
- Because AX organizational transition means a restructuring of human roles, identity, power, relationships, and existential value, relationship-layer reactions must be understood not as simple interpersonal problems but as reactions to the restructuring of power and role structures within the organization.
