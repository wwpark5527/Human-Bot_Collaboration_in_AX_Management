# Least-preferred Role — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The least-preferred role is the last of the three levels of team-role manifestation; it is a role that, no matter how hard we try and how much others help, simply cannot be manifested.
- Boundary of judgment: a potential/manageable role is manifested once the conditions of self-management effort plus colleagues' understanding and acceptance are in place, whereas the least-preferred role is distinguished in that it cannot be manifested even when all of those conditions are applied.
- Once confirmed as a least-preferred role, it is established as a blank that cannot be resolved by managing internal members (the person's own effort, colleagues' acceptance and encouragement).
- This judgment becomes the grounds for the practical decision to cleanly abandon the given TR or to convert it into the recruitment or replacement of a member who holds that TR as a natural role.

## Source Evidence Quotation
> "In the last case, it is a role that, no matter how hard we try and how much others help, simply cannot be manifested by each of us. This is called the least-preferred role."
