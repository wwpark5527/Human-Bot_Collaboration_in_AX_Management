# The First LLM — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The first LLM is a general-purpose LLM supplied externally by big tech, is located at layer 4 of the LLM architecture diagram, and is the object people ordinarily call an 'LLM'.
- It is the product of concentrating on the ability to learn a huge corpus and generate sentences as natural as a human's, and in that process the limits of hallucination and security leakage arose along with it.
- It accumulates and turns into knowledge almost all information of human society and provides it, but if the organization's internal data has not been turned into knowledge (layer 3), it remains at the level of use by individual employees.
- On the paradigm side, the first LLM is the generation that concentrates on 'text generation', and this becomes the starting point contrasted with the second LLM, which then shifts to 'knowledge control'.
