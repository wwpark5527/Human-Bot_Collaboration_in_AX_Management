# Layers 1–2 (the domain of DX) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- In the LLM architecture, layers 1–2 are "the domain of DX," and layers 1–4 are "the domain of AX."
- Events and actions of the real world become data, and DX plays the role of turning that data into information — this is the processing scope of layers 1–2.
- The turning of information into the knowledge the organization needs takes place at layer 3, and layers 1–2 themselves remain at the stage prior to knowledge-ization.
- Even if DX within the organization is insufficient, when organizational AX is pursued correctly there is a reverse effect in which layers 1 and 2 are qualitatively strengthened in the course of forming layer 3.
