# Layer 4 (the LLM already released to the world = 'the 1st LLM') — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Layer 4 of the LLM architecture is the layer where what people commonly call an "LLM" is situated, and the text, for the sake of comparison, calls it "the 1st LLM."
- Layer 4 is supplied externally by big tech and is already out in the world, but only part of its content is used in the human world, so it is not yet sufficiently utilized.
- The externally supplied (1st) LLM accumulates, knowledge-izes, and provides almost all of the information of human society, but if the organization's internal data and information have not been knowledge-ized (layer 3), employees are doing no more than continuing to use only external knowledge.
- If one connects only to layer 4 without layer 3, it generates only enormous costs for individuals' use of the external LLM (token costs, electricity costs), the formation of synergy within the organization is institutionally blocked, and the risk of information leakage also grows.
