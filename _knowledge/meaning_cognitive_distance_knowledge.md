# Meaning/cognitive distance — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Meaning/cognitive distance is `S2C-0110`, the central object of the identifiability that the knowledge chain restores. It is located immediately after the functions of the knowledge chain (the four functions, S2C-0109), and the next identifier is `AI contribution` (AI_CONTRIBUTION).
- The outputs AI produces and the knowledge a human actually possesses are not the same. That AI has produced an excellent answer does not mean that human understanding has deepened, so the value of AI must be evaluated not by how many answers it has produced but by how much it has reduced the human's meaning/cognitive distance.
- An evaluation that looks only at answers cannot distinguish different knowledge structures. Even when they give the same answer, one person may have answered from deep understanding and another from memorization. The additional observation structure for solving this problem is the knowledge chain.
- The knowledge-chain-based meaning/cognitive distance is the combined result of eight factors: differences in conceptual structure, differences in level of mastery, differences in belief structure, completeness of premise relations, fidelity of the reasoning chain, reliability of evidence, transferability, and epistemic maturity.
