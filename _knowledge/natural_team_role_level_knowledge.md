# Team/Natural Role — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The team/natural role is the first of the three levels of team-role manifestation (natural role, potential role, least-preferred role); it is a role that is manifested easily and naturally by oneself when acting together with others.
- The natural roles per individual commonly amount to no more than 2–3 — no one manifests all 9 team roles well at the same time.
- The criterion of judgment is whether it is manifested "without others' help or the person's own conscious effort" — in this respect it is distinguished from the potential/manageable role, which is manifested only when effort, self-management, and others' understanding are present.
- Team role balance (TRB) is judged by whether, when the natural roles of all team members are gathered, all 9 TR cells are filled: if even one is empty it is not established, if all are filled it is established, and the number of filled cells itself becomes that team's current teamwork level score.
