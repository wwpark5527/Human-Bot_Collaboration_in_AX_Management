# Observer assessment (OA: observer assessment) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The OA is, among the four questionnaires that Interplace uses (SPI/OA/JRE/JOA), the second method of individual diagnosis among the two questionnaires that evaluate a person (SPI, OA).
- The respondents are not the person being measured but the people around them who know them well, and four or more observers respond — in this respect it is distinguished from the self-perception inventory (SPI), to which the person themselves responds.
- Unlike the SPI, which has one respond to survey items, the response method is a checklist method in which one marks, from 45 positive adjectives and 27 negative adjectives, the items relevant to the person being assessed.
- The OA responses (objective) are combined with the SPI responses (subjective) and go through a diagnostic process mixing relative and absolute evaluation, and as a result it is known to have the highest reliability and validity as a measurement instrument.

## Source Evidence Quotation
> "The observer assessment (OA), the second method of individual diagnosis, is filled out by people around the measured member who know them well."
