# OS for organizational AX — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The OS for organizational AX is not a mere "AI platform" or "chatbot integration tool" but an organizational operating system by which the organization's members — humans, AI agents, and bots — integrally operate, control, and augment work processes based on the use of external LLMs and internal organizational data.
- Just as a personal PC does not work without an OS such as Windows even when connected to electricity and the internet, such an operating system is essential to organizational AX as well.
- The necessary conditions consist of operating protocols (operating protocols & control standards), privacy & sovereignty, AI governance, human-bot role architecture, agent orchestration capability, and persistent organizational memory.
- The additional conditions consist of common & governance context, knowledge chain, complementary fit engine, continuous learning & adaptation, provenance & verification layer, and organizational digital twin (ODT), and once these are in place the organization reaches the level of an AI-native organizational operating system, that is, true AI 4.0.
- The core of privacy and sovereignty is "to use external AI without losing the organization's sovereignty," and after the OS for organizational AX is introduced, AI results accumulate as the organization's operating assets rather than as individual products.
