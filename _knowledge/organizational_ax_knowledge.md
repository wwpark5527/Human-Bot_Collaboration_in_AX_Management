# Organizational AX — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Organizational AX is the "process" of an organization undergoing AX, and it is distinguished from the AX organization, which is the result.
- Personal AX becoming more widespread within the organization and organizational AX being under way are separate matters; organizational AX is the redesign of organizational operation itself around AI.
- Organizational AX is not a technology project but an organizational redesign project, and it demands a variety of decisions such as the level of AI's participation in decision-making, the locus of accountability, roles between departments, data access authority, and responsibility for errors.
- It cannot succeed through partial optimization (adoption in some departments); it must be an enterprise-wide system transformation in which work data, structure, authority, evaluation, culture, and governance are connected.

## Source Evidence Quotation
> "In the end, organizational AX is the process of an organization undergoing AX, and an AX organization means an organization that has, as a result, been AX-ed."

> "The organization must make a variety of decisions (e.g., to what level can AI participate in decision-making? Is responsibility the human's or the AI's? How do roles between departments change? What about data access authority? Who is responsible when an AI error occurs?). Therefore organizational AX is not a technology project but an organizational redesign project."

> "It cannot succeed through partial optimization: AI is effective even when introduced in only some departments, but a true organizational AX requires that work data, structure, authority, evaluation, culture, and governance be connected. That is, organizational AX is a matter of enterprise-wide system transformation."
