# Operating protocols & control standards — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- As one of the necessary conditions, the OS for organizational AX must embed operating protocols (operating protocols & control standards).
- The core elements of the operating protocols are nine: authority & approval, role allocation, access control, exception handling, logging & traceability, accountability, the escalation system, human override, and the audit trail.
- The purpose is not to stop at merely connecting AI to the organization but to make AI act within the organization's norms.
- This component appears in the source text as the first of the six necessary conditions among the necessary and additional conditions of the OS for organizational AX (S2C-0013, `ORG_AX_OS_CONDITIONS`).

## Source Evidence Quotation
> "Operating protocols: the OS for organizational AX must embed the operating protocols by which humans and AI bots work together. The core elements are 'authority & approval, role allocation, access control, exception handling, logging & traceability, accountability, escalation system, human override, audit trail' — that is, it must be an operating system that does not merely connect AI but makes AI act within the organization's norms."
