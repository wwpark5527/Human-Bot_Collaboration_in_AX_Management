# Organizational digital twin(ODT) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The ODT is one of the additional conditions of the OS for organizational AX, a living virtual replica of the organization that dynamically reproduces in digital space the organization's structure, roles, workflows, decision-making, collaboration, performance, and even culture.
- Unlike a mere organization chart or ERP system, it simulates, predicts, and optimizes how the organization actually moves in real time or near-real time.
- The reason the ODT is needed is that the organization has become too complex for human managers alone to understand its whole state, that in an AX organization one must not manage only humans, and that organizational experiments are hard to conduct directly in reality.
- This component appears in the source text as the last of the six additional conditions among the necessary and additional conditions of the OS for organizational AX (S2C-0013, `ORG_AX_OS_CONDITIONS`) (the fourth promoted split element).

## Source Evidence Quotation

This is not a mere organization chart or ERP system, but a system that simulates, predicts, and optimizes how the organization actually moves in real time or near-real time. The reason the ODT is needed is that the organization has become too complex for human managers alone to understand its whole state, that in an AX organization one must not manage only humans, and further that, in such a situation, organizational experiments are hard to conduct directly in reality."
