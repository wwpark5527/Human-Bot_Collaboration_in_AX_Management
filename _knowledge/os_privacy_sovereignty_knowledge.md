# Privacy & sovereignty — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- As one of the necessary conditions, the OS for organizational AX must embed privacy and sovereignty.
- The core principle is "to leverage external AI without losing the organization's sovereignty."
- Leveraging very powerful external LLMs (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and the like) is itself beneficial, but if there is a risk that internal information (data and context) flows out through that connection, it must be blocked.
