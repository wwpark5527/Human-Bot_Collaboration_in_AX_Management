# Personal AX — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Personal AX is the process or act of an individual leveraging AI, and it begins with raising the efficiency of one's studies and work with ChatGPT/Gemini and the like.
- A higher level of personal AX can be realized through the use of AI agents and bots.
- Personal AX is a matter of tool use, whereas organizational AX differs in character as a matter of changing the organization's operating system.
- Personal AX and organizational AX are contrasted along the axes of improving personal productivity vs changing the organization's operating system, the individual being able to decide on their own vs requiring the coordination of stakeholders, low vs high failure cost, tool-centered vs authority-, role-, and culture-centered, and quickly realizable vs requiring standardization and governance.
- The fact that there is much personal AX within an organization does not mean that the organization is pursuing organizational AX and that an AX organization is realized.
