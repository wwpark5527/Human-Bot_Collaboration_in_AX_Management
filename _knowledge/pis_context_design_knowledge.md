# Context design — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Context design (`PIS_CONTEXT_DESIGN`, S2C-0465) is a SplitSet child of `the AI predictive intelligence system / predictive intelligence stack` (S2C-0129, `PREDICTIVE_INTELLIGENCE_SYSTEM`), and it is the operating condition for the knowledge chain to be converted and developed into a world model and used in a real organization.
- However excellent AI may be, if it does not know human purposes and criteria, its results may diverge from the organization's actual judgment.
- Context design provides the conditions under which AI does not remain a tool that merely produces fast answers but can work according to human purposes and criteria.
- If the world model is AI's predictive capability, the knowledge chain is the supply chain for that predictive capability, and context design is the operating condition that makes that predictive capability work within human purposes and organizational criteria.
- The context designer is the person (or organization) who forms the necessary conditions for the first LLM to work effectively within the organization, and further is the agent who forms the second LLM.
