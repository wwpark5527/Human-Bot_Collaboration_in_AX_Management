# Potential/Manageable Role — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The potential/manageable role is the second of the three levels of team-role manifestation, a role that is not exercised easily and naturally yet lies latent within us.
- There are two conditions for its exercise: (1) the person's own conscious effort to manage themselves, and (2) the surrounding colleagues understanding and accepting that behavior. When both conditions are met, it can be exercised.
- The difference from the natural role is the presence or absence of "naturalness," and the difference from the least-preferred role is the presence or absence of the very possibility of exercise — the potential role is exercised when there is effort and acceptance, whereas the least-preferred role is not exercised however much one tries.
- The potential role is the basis that tells us the maximum potential teamwork level the team can reach, and it becomes a target for promotion, raised to the natural-role level through internal member management that combines colleagues' acceptance and encouragement with the person's own effort, so that TRB is achieved after the fact.

## Source Evidence Quotation
> "Although some team roles are not exercised easily and naturally, they lie latent within us, so that if we consciously manage ourselves in order to exercise them and others understand and accept us, some can be exercised by ourselves. This is classified as a potential role or manageable role."
