# ① Respect for (protection of) human safety and life — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The three laws of robotics are principles presented by the science fiction writer I. Asimov in the short story Runaround (1942), and they are the most classic and representative content imposing on robots the duty of human respect and safety.
- The first law (respect for and protection of human safety and life) is the principle that a robot must not harm a human and must not ignore a human in danger.
- The second law (obedience to humans) is that a robot must obey human orders as long as this does not conflict with the first law, and the third law (self-preservation) is that a robot must protect itself as long as this does not conflict with the first and second laws.
- Teaching a bot respect for humans is not education but a matter of designing the system so that only actions that do not violate humans are selected, and it is a matter of redesigning the action, reward, and verification structures around humans rather than injecting morality.
