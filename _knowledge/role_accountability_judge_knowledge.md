# Accountability judge — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The accountability judge is the sixth of the eight roles of AX-organization talent.
- Because ultimate responsibility for even an AI-produced result lies with the organization and its people, AX-organization talent must judge how far an AI result may be used.
- Criteria of judgment: is it for internal reference only, may it be delivered to a customer, may it be reflected in decision-making, is a superior's approval required, is legal/security review required, must a record be kept, and is the locus of accountability clear if a problem arises.
