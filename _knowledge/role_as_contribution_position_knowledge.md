# Role — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Role is the last of the three layers — work, contribution, role — that the role theory of the AI era redefines, and it is the structure that makes contribution repeatable and accountable.
- The role of the AI era is not a title. One person can take on several roles, AI can perform some roles, and a system can take on the recording role.
- However, ultimate responsibility remains with the human or the organization — even if roles are functionally distributed, responsibility is not distributed along with them.
- Judgment criterion: is it a person's name tag (title), or is it a functional position that arises in the process by which meaning turns into output artifacts and responsibility.
- Belbin's role theory, explained in Part 3, likewise explains patterns of contribution rather than tasks, and stands on the same axis as this concept of 'role'.
