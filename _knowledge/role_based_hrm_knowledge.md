# RBHRM (Role-Based HRM, competence + role-based) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The major current of HR organization in the mid-20th century was the shift from seniority-based to seniority-plus-competence-based (CBHRM, Competence-Based HRM).
- The shift to RBHRM (Role-Based HRM, competence + role-based) had already begun in the late 20th century, but it was not being sufficiently utilized.
- This shift proceeds together with a shift from control-centered to autonomy- and commitment-centered, and at the same time from position-centered to role-centered.
- In AX organizations where the change from job-centered to role-centered is urgently needed, the shift to RBHRM is a necessary condition for performance realization.
