# Evidence keeper — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The evidence keeper is the seventh of the eight roles of AX-organization talent.
- Because keeping only the result makes it impossible to confirm why that judgment was made, who reviewed it, and by what criteria it was approved, evidence of the AI-usage process must be kept.
- Evidence that must be kept: the materials used, the AI instructions, the AI-generated results, the human edits, the verification criteria, the approval status, errors and exceptions, and the improvements reflected.
- Only with evidence management does AX become not mere automation but a reviewable and accountable way of operating the organization.
