# Improvement reflector — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The improvement reflector is the eighth and last of the eight roles of AX-organization talent.
- An AX organization is not completed by a single use of AI, and the errors, omissions, failures, and corrections that arise in the AI-usage process must be reflected into the criteria for the next task.
- AX talent is an operations improver who does not let a failure end as an individual mistake but improves the organization's criteria.
- The flow of reflecting improvements: AI usage → result review → discovery of errors and omissions → cause analysis → prompt correction → context reinforcement → checklist update → deliverable schema improvement → reflection into the next task.
