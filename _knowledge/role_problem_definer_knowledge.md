# Problem Definer — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The problem definer is the first of the eight roles of AX organization talent, and the expression that encompasses all eight roles is 'collaboration coordinator'.
- Because AI cannot understand a problem on its own in a way that fits the organization's context, AX talent must define the problem the organization has to solve before deciding what to have AI do.
- The five questions needed for problem definition: what is the problem (clarifying the object to be solved), why does it matter (connection to work and performance), what result is needed (setting deliverable standards), what constraints exist (security, cost, time, legal conditions), what are the success criteria (setting performance indicators).
