# Role vacancy — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Role vacancy (`ROLE_VACANCY`, S2C-0124) is pointed to as the greatest problem in communication between an augmented human and a non-augmented human (AH-H).
- Side A (human + AI + common context) is equipped with four roles: purpose and judgment (human), analysis and generation (AI), criteria (common context), and history management (recording system).
- Side B (the human alone) must perform "understanding, interpretation, verification, memory, response, defense, and approval" alone, without such roles.
- Role vacancy is the state in which the interpretation, verification, recording, and response roles are empty on the side that has no AI, and it is defined not as mere inconvenience but as a problem of communication fairness.

## Source Evidence Quotation
> "When an augmented human and a human communicate, the greatest problem is 'role vacancy'. Role vacancy refers to the state in which one side has no AI and no common context, so that the interpretation, verification, recording, and response contributions are empty."
>
> "Side A = human + AI + common context; Side B = human"
>
> "But side B does not have those roles. B must perform 'understanding, interpretation, verification, memory, response, defense, and approval' alone. This is role vacancy — that is, the state in which the interpretation, verification, recording, and response roles are empty on the side that has no AI. Role vacancy is not a mere inconvenience. It is a problem of communication fairness."
