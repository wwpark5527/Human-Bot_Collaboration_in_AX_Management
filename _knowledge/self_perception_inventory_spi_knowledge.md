# Self-perception inventory (SPI) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The SPI is one of the two surveys that evaluate people (SPI, OA) among the four questionnaires (SPI/OA/JRE/JOA) used by Interplace.
- The respondent is the person being measured themselves, who writes the responses by responding directly to the survey items — in this respect it is distinguished from the observer assessment (OA), which people around them complete in a checklist format.
- The SPI responses are subjective responses by the person themselves, and combined with the objective responses of the OA (four or more observers) they go through a diagnostic process that mixes relative and absolute evaluation.
- The SPI+OA results (person evaluation) are entered into Interplace together with the JRE+JOA results (job evaluation) and are processed and output in real time as 14 kinds of reports.
