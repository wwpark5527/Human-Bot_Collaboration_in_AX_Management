# Tool — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Tool is `S2C-0450`, the third of the seven slots defined by `SkillRuntime` (S2C-0117, `SKILL_RUNTIME`). It is positioned after material (`SKILL_RUNTIME_SLOT_MATERIAL`) and before prohibition (`SKILL_RUNTIME_SLOT_PROHIBITION`).
- The knowledge-action chain turns knowledge into skills and turns those skills into actual execution structures. The third item that SkillRuntime defines is the tool.
- The seven items that SkillRuntime as a whole defines: input, material, tool, prohibition, result format, reviewer/approver, record location. Only with this structure does a skill work in actual work.
- If the scope of tools is not specified in advance, the skill will use arbitrary means of execution — as the third slot, after material and before prohibition, it settles the scope of means to be used in execution.
