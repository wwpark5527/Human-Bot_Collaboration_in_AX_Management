# Pursuing Complementary Fit — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Pursuing complementary fit is the third spirit of the core management-spirit system (the basic premise + the three spirits), and it is the last spirit that forms the trinity together with augmentation and respect for and use of diversity.
- In the twentieth century, unity through advancing similarity and homogeneity — that is, supplementary fit — was regarded as the driving force of teamwork and performance improvement, but because of transportation, communication, and globalization, complementary fit, which fills in each other's shortcomings, is now receiving the spotlight.
- The AX organization does not force humans to act like bots, nor does it make bots merely imitate humans; instead it connects the essential strengths of each — it pursues "augmentation, not replacement."
- There are four types of complementary fit: cognitive complementarity (intuition and creativity vs. calculation, search, and memory), emotional complementarity (empathy and trust vs. immediate response and continuous feedback), behavioral complementarity (unstructured and exceptional response vs. repetitive and standardized action), and ethical complementarity (value judgment and social responsibility vs. rule-based consistency).
- Because bots follow rules but find it difficult to judge the legitimacy of the rules themselves, in the ethical domain human value judgment must necessarily complement them.
