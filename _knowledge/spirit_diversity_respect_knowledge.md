# Respecting and Leveraging Diversity — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Respect for and use of diversity is the second spirit of the system of core management spirits (basic premise + 3 spirits); it comes after augmentation and leads to complementary fit.
- Twentieth-century management saw supplementary fit (homogeneity and unity) as the driving force of teamwork, but now the understanding must be extended toward respecting and using diversity (difference).
- In traditional organizations diversity was perceived as a 'source of conflict' (increased communication and coordination costs), but in the AX organization it is converted into an 'augmentation asset' (the driving force of creative management and performance enhancement).
- The object of diversity is not confined to the interior of humanity but includes the essential differences between humans and bots, and it is especially important to use those differences from the perspective of role diversity.
- Only when diversity is respected and regarded as an object of mutual augmentation can the next spirit, 'complementary fit', arise.

## Source Evidence Quotation
> "Respect for and use of diversity is the spirit of recognizing not only the diversity within humans but also the essential differences between humans and bots, and of using those differences as assets of augmentation and mutual complementarity rather than of competition, thereby creating higher collective intelligence and a sustainable AX organization."
