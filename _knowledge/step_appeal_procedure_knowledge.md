The core verb of this step is "establish a structure." That is, it is not about responding to individual objections case by case, but about erecting in advance, as an organizational procedure, the channel through which objections can be raised at all. Without a channel, AI results are fixed immediately upon approval and cannot be reversed.

That is, appeal is a guarantee of social capability (S) for the individual and a control structure (G) for the organization.
- In the order of steps, this step is placed after step 5, human approval criteria, and before step 7, audit record. After the approval gate is erected (step 5), a channel for re-review is opened even for results that have passed that approval (step 6), and the history of those requests and their processing is preserved (step 7). The sequential logic is approval → appeal → record, and since it is impossible to confirm after the fact whether processing took place if no appeal history remains, the connection to step 7 is essential.
