# Supplementary Fit — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Supplementary fit is the perspective that sees feeling mutual similarity and homogeneity as one aspect of teamwork that helps improve departmental and organizational performance.
- Twentieth-century management often saw diversity as an obstacle to realizing performance, and there was a strong tendency to see unity through advancing similarity and homogeneity as the driving force of teamwork and performance improvement — what was emphasized then was supplementary fit.
- Unity events, "one-heart" rallies, and staff dinners are representative practices for forming homogeneity, and such activities are not in themselves bad; they remain necessary and important.
- As the development of transportation and communication and globalization increased exchange and joining with different people, complementary fit, which pursues the needed wholeness by filling in each other's shortcomings, came to receive the spotlight.
- Supplementary fit is not a concept to be discarded entirely but one axis that must be expanded and complemented by an awareness that respects and leverages diversity (being different).
