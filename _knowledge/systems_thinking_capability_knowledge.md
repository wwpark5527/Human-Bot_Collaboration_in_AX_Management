# Systems Thinking — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Systems thinking is the third capability among the five items of "additional conditions for AX organization talent: the competencies of a successful leader."
- Systems thinking is needed by leaders of human-only organizations too, but it is needed all the more because an AX organization is a complex system in which humans, AI agents, governance, workflow, culture, ESG, security, and context are connected.
- AI is strong at partial optimization, but the integrative judgment on value conflicts, ethics, long-term direction, organizational culture, and social ripple effects is the human's part.
- In an AX organization, judgment on AI governance, accountability, trust, and human-centeredness is important, and the core is not the generation of correct answers but the determination of direction.
