# Task-Activation Aspect of Teamwork — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The author (Park Won-woo), holding that actual team performance improvement is not fully explained by the human bonding aspect alone, advocated the task-activation aspect of teamwork as yet another factor.
- This aspect is concretized as the state in which all 9 team roles are easily and well exercised within the team, and it became measurable through the operational definition of Belbin's team role balance (TRB).
- In the concept of fit it corresponds to complementary fit, which stands in structural contrast to the human bonding aspect that corresponds to supplementary fit.
- This aspect was found to have a significant effect on a department's actual performance as evaluated by the company, but not to have a significant effect on the perceived performance recognized by the members, and so it is positioned as a short- and mid-term necessary condition that directly affects actual performance.
- It was proved that a department's teamwork, and further its actual performance, becomes highest when both the human bonding aspect and the task-activation aspect exist.

## Source Evidence Quotation
> "Of course the 'human bonding' aspect is necessary, but thinking that yet another factor besides it acts on the actual improvement of team performance, he advocated the 'task-activation' aspect of teamwork, and thereafter proved that a department's teamwork, and further its actual performance, becomes highest when both of these aspects of teamwork exist together."
