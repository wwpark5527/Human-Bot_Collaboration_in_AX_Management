# Team Role Balance (TRB) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Team role balance (TRB) is the state in which all 9 team-role types exist within one team at the natural-role level, and Belbin gave it its name.
- The 9 team roles are classified, according to an individual's level of exercise, into 3 stages: natural roles (exercised easily and naturally) / latent and manageable roles (exercisable with effort and with the help of others) / non-preferred roles (not exercisable no matter how hard one tries).
- TRB → Teamwork → Team Performance: the causal relation that when team role balance is achieved the task-activation aspect of teamwork rises, and when teamwork rises team performance improves, has been proven by empirical studies regardless of country, industry, or organization size.
- Regardless of the number of team members, when the "TRB" state is reached in which all 9 team roles exist in the natural-role state, the level of teamwork, and further the degree of team performance, changes.
- TRB theory not only tells the current level of teamwork and its potential highest possible level, but also tells the concrete methods of individual and team management that close the gap between the current level and the possible level and achieve performance.

## Source Evidence Quotation
> "All 9 team-role types must exist within one team, and moreover must exist at the natural-role level. Belbin says that in that case team role balance (TRB) has been achieved."
