# Communication Context — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- The communication context (`THREE_LAYER_CONTEXT_COMMUNICATION_LAYER`, S2C-0456) is the second of the three layers that make up the common-communication-governance three-layer context structure (`THREE_LAYER_CONTEXT_ARCHITECTURE`, S2C-0121).
- If the common context takes charge of the internal alignment of human and AI, the communication context determines how the augmented human relates to the outside.
- The communication context deals with six questions: who is the speaker, in what role did the AI intervene, is content written by the AI distinguished from human judgment, is the counterpart's utterance recorded and analyzed, how far may the message be shared, and did a human approve important decisions.
- "Context aligns the inside, communication connects with the outside, and governance makes that connection accountable" — in this three-layer structure the communication context is the core connecting concept placed between the common context and the governance context.

That is, context aligns the inside, communication connects with the outside, and governance makes that connection accountable."
