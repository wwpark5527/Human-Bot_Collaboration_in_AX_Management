# Human-only TRB — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Human-only TRB is the first fragment of the `TRB_EVOLUTION_PATH` (TRB evolution path, Human-only → Bot-aided → Human-bot coupled → Autonomous hybrid TRB) SplitSet.
- It is stated that most organizations are highly likely to evolve through the stages of "Human-only TRB → Bot-aided TRB → Human-bot coupled TRB," and that thereafter they will advance toward the type of "Autonomous hybrid TRB."
- The Human-only TRB stage is the stage in which the object of role balance is limited to a human + human team and no bot has entered as a role actor, and it is characterized by a job-based organization, static roles, and individual capability.
- This stage serves as the baseline that becomes the starting point of the later stages Bot-aided TRB (a vertical relationship in which bots participate as assistants, defined separately in detail in Chapters 5 and 6 — `BOT_AIDED_TRB`) and Human-bot coupled TRB (a horizontal relationship in which bots participate as joint role actors — `HUMAN_BOT_COUPLED_TRB`).
- It is stated that the evolution is at the level of "AI assistance" in the early period, but in the long term is highly likely to develop in the direction in which humans and bots form a role ecosystem together.
