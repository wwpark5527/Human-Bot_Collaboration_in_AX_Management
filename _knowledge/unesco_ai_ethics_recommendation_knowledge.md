# UNESCO's Recommendation on the Ethics of AI (2021) — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- It is the first global AI ethics norm, adopted unanimously by 193 member states at the 2021 General Conference.
- It is grounded in international human rights law, and its emphasis lies on resolving global inequality and protecting the environment.
- The four values: respect, protection, and promotion of human rights, fundamental freedoms, and human dignity; the flourishing of the environment and ecosystems; ensuring diversity and inclusiveness; and building peaceful, just, and interconnected societies.
- The ten principles: proportionality and do no harm, safety and security, fairness and non-discrimination, sustainability, right to privacy and data protection, human oversight and determination, transparency and explainability, responsibility and accountability, awareness and literacy, and multi-stakeholder governance collaboration.
- Although not binding, it specifies concrete policy action areas such as education, culture, gender equality, and governance, and it serves as an international benchmark that induces each member state's government to align its legal system and report the state of implementation to UNESCO.
- It is one of the most widely cited standards by the criteria of global representativeness and reflection in national policy, and it carries the symbolic weight and authority of being the first comprehensive international norm on AI ethics in the UN system.
