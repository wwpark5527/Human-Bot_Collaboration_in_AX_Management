# Work — Knowledge

## Core Knowledge
- Work is the most basic of the three layers — work, contribution, role — that the role theory of the AI era redefines.
- Work is the act itself of 'having done something', and it does not ask whether that act produced a change.
- Contribution is the valid change that act produced, and role is the structure that makes that change repeatable and accountable — work is the layer that is the premise of these two.
- Example: that the AI wrote a sentence, that the AI summarized material, is in itself 'work'. Only when it is separately confirmed whether it changed the counterpart's understanding and made judgment possible does it become 'contribution'.
- The most important change in communication in the AI era is the separation of role and contribution, and work is the starting point of that separation.
