# Collaboration Augmentation (Collaboration A.) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect the work list of the member being measured and calculate the proportion of that work which can be delegated to AI (an agent).
2. Measure the level of agent usage, that is, whether the member uses the agent at the level of simple invocation or at the level of dividing roles and entrusting them.
3. Review the proportion of delegable work and the level of agent usage together.
4. Combine the two measures and judge whether collaboration augmentation has occurred.

## Criteria
If the proportion of delegable work and the level of agent usage appear at a meaningful level and it is confirmed that roles are naturally divided with AI, it is judged PASS (collaboration augmentation confirmed); otherwise it is judged FAIL (collaboration augmentation not confirmed).
