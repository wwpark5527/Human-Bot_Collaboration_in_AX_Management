# Decision Augmentation (Decision A.) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Record the accuracy, error rate, and prediction success rate of decisions the member being measured made without using AI (the baseline).
2. Record the accuracy, error rate, and prediction success rate when the same member makes the same type of decision using AI.
3. Compare decision accuracy, error-reduction rate, and prediction success rate between the two conditions.
4. Combine the three measures and judge whether decision augmentation has occurred.

## Criteria
If decision accuracy, the error-reduction rate, and the prediction success rate have improved after the use of AI, it is judged PASS (decision augmentation confirmed); if they have not improved, it is judged FAIL (decision augmentation not confirmed).
