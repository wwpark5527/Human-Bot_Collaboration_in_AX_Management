# Learning Augmentation (Learning A.) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Record the learning time it takes the member being measured to acquire new knowledge and capabilities without using AI (the baseline).
2. Record the learning time it takes the same member to acquire the same type of knowledge and capabilities using AI.
3. Compare the amount of knowledge acquired over the same period under the two conditions.
4. Combine the reduction in learning time and the increase in the amount of knowledge acquired and judge whether learning augmentation has occurred.

## Criteria
If learning time has decreased and the amount of knowledge acquired has increased after the use of AI, it is judged PASS (learning augmentation confirmed); otherwise it is judged FAIL (learning augmentation not confirmed).
