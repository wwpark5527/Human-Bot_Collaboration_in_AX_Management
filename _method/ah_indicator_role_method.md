# Role Augmentation (Role A.) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Record the range of roles the member performed before the use of AI (the baseline).
2. Record the roles the member has newly come to perform after the use of AI (for example, researcher → designer, manager → orchestrator, professor → AI coach).
3. Compare the two ranges of roles and confirm whether a new role has actually been added.
4. On the basis of the confirmed role expansion, judge "Do you perform a new role because of AI?", and take this as the final criterion for whether the member is an AH.

## Criteria
If after the use of AI the member has actually come to perform a role they could not perform before, it is judged PASS (role augmentation confirmed, the final AH judgment satisfied); otherwise it is judged FAIL (role augmentation not confirmed).
