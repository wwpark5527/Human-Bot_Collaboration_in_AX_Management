# Role/Context Augmentation — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Grasp the current level of one's role, responsibilities, and mode of contribution.
2. Redesign the role and mode of collaboration so that AI is used within the organization's purpose, standards, and accountability system.
3. Confirm whether the power to set purpose, the power to establish standards, and the power to take on judgment and accountability are actually being exercised.
4. Apply the developed role and mode of contribution to the work and re-confirm.

## Criteria
If it is confirmed that the use of AI goes beyond the level of mere speed improvement and takes place within the organization's purpose, standards, and accountability system, it is judged PASS (role/context augmentation confirmed); otherwise it is judged FAIL (role/context augmentation not confirmed).
