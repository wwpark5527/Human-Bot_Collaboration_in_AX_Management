# AI-Based Stratification (AI Class Society / AI-Based Stratification) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Investigate the differences in access to AI models, data, compute, platforms, organizational context, utilization capability, and decision authority.
2. Discriminate whether the access differences accumulate and result in ownership, direction, verification, and use by a few versus evaluation, control, replacement, and exclusion of the many.
3. Identify which of the seven gap types (access, capability, context, judgment authority, surveillance, ownership, profit-sharing) is especially pronounced in the organization.
4. Connect the diagnostic result to the design of inclusive transformation AX.

## Criteria
If the accumulation of access differences leads to a social structure of ownership, direction, verification, and use (a few) versus evaluation, control, replacement, and exclusion (the many), it is determined that the risk of AI-based stratification exists (PASS); otherwise it is determined that the risk is not established (FAIL).
