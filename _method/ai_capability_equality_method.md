# AI Capability Equality Theory — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Observe the gaps in AI-utilization performance within the organization and diagnose them by distinguishing whether their cause is a tool access gap or a capability gap.
2. Evaluate, by individual and by department, the capability to convey purpose, standards, and context and the capability to verify, approve, and improve results.
3. Identify structures in which AI-utilization capability depends on an individual's intuition or on a particular department's authority.
4. Convert context capital, the authority system, verification standards, and the approval structure into a common production base accessible to the whole organization.
5. Re-measure after the conversion whether the AI-utilization capability gap between members has narrowed.

## Criteria
If the cause of the AI-utilization capability gap is accurately diagnosed as capability (conveying purpose, standards, and context; verifying, approving, and improving) rather than tool access, and context capital, the authority system, verification standards, and the approval structure have been converted into a common production base for the whole organization, it is determined PASS; if they are still left to an individual's intuition or to a particular department's authority, it is determined FAIL.
