# AI Contribution — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Measure the meaning/cognitive distance before AI intervention.
2. Measure the meaning/cognitive distance after AI intervention.
3. Calculate the difference between the two values to obtain the reduced meaning/cognitive distance.
4. Tally the AI-usage cost expended on the relevant period and task.
5. Divide the reduced meaning/cognitive distance by the AI-usage cost to produce the AI contribution.

## Criteria
If the meaning/cognitive distance before and after AI intervention is both measured and the AI-usage cost is tallied so that the ratio (AI contribution) is produced, it is determined PASS; if even one is missing, it is determined FAIL.
