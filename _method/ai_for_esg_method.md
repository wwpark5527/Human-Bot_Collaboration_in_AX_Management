# AI for ESG — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Organize the organization's ESG implementation process (data collection, measurement, monitoring, reporting) stage by stage.
2. Identify at each stage the points where applying AI can raise efficiency and accuracy.
3. Introduce AI-based tools to automate ESG data collection, analysis, and monitoring.
4. Comparatively measure ESG implementation performance (speed, accuracy, scope) before and after applying AI.
5. Document and spread the cases in which positive effects were confirmed.

## Criteria
If measurement confirms that the speed, accuracy, and scope of the ESG implementation process have improved through the application of AI, it is determined PASS; if improvement is not confirmed or the negative effects are greater, it is determined FAIL (in that case, review again from the ESG_FOR_AI perspective).
