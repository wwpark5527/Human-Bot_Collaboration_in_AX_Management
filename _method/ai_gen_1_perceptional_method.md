# AI 1.0 (Perceptional AI) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the target AI function perceives, identifies, and classifies external input.
2. Check the presence or absence of generative (content generation), autonomous-execution (agentic behavior), and organization-operating (Organizational) functions.
3. If all three functions are absent, classify it as Perceptional AI (AI 1.0); if even one is present, reclassify it into a higher generation (AI 2.0/3.0/4.0).

## Criteria
When the target AI produces only perception, identification, and classification results and no generative, autonomous-execution, or organization-operating function is confirmed, the AI 1.0 (Perceptional AI) determination is regarded as complete.
