# AI 2.0 (Generative AI) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the target AI function generates new content such as sentences and images.
2. Check whether it sets goals on its own and plans and executes them (Agentic).
3. If it has a generative function but no autonomous execution, classify it as Generative AI (AI 2.0); if autonomous execution is also confirmed, reclassify it into a higher generation (AI 3.0/4.0).
4. Separately check and record the hallucination phenomena and security-leakage risk of the generated output.

## Criteria
When the target AI produces generated output (sentences, images, etc.) but does not set goals and execute them on its own, the AI 2.0 (Generative AI) determination is regarded as complete.
