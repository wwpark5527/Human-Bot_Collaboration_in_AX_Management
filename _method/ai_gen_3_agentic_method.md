# AI 3.0 (Agentic AI) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the target AI understands goals and sets up plans and executes them on its own.
2. Check whether the operating unit of that autonomous execution is the individual agent (the individual) or the whole organization.
3. If autonomous execution is confirmed and the operating unit remains at the individual agent, classify it as Agentic AI (AI 3.0).
4. If the operating unit has expanded to the operation of the whole organization, reclassify it into the higher generation (AI 4.0).

## Criteria
When the target AI operates as an autonomous agent that understands goals, plans, and executes, but its unit remains at the individual agent, the AI 3.0 (Agentic AI) determination is regarded as complete.
