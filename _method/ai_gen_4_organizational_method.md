# AI 4.0 (Organizational AI) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the operating unit of the target AI utilization is the individual agent or the operation of the whole organization.
2. Check whether common context and governance context have been formed and implemented within the organization.
3. Discriminate whether it remains at the level of a mere AI platform or has reached the level of an AI-native organizational operating system.
4. If it has expanded to the operation of the whole organization and both contexts have been implemented, determine it to be the 'true AI 4.0 level'.

## Criteria
When AI's operating unit has expanded to the operation of the whole organization and an AI-native organizational operating system based on common and governance context has been implemented, the AI 4.0 (Organizational AI) determination is regarded as complete.
