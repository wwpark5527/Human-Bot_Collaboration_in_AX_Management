# AI Governance — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Identify the prohibition rules, accountability rules, audit system, and approval system concerning how the organization will control, manage, and supervise AI.
2. Organize these as AI governance (the system of control, A) in the form of norms, institutions, and an operating system.
3. Distinguish the level from governance context (the judgment-standard context, B), making clear that B is the judgment standard of the network environment and A is the system that executes that standard.
4. Translate B into work procedures, roles, systems, and recording structures and reflect it within A.
5. Check whether the mode of control does not remain at prohibition, surveillance, and after-the-fact audit but is realized as operation, standardization, and connected operation.

## Criteria
If the order of AI use is established as norms, institutions, and an operating system, and governance context operates verifiably and accountably within work procedures, roles, systems, and recording structures, it is determined PASS; if control stops at prohibition, surveillance, and after-the-fact audit and is not connected to the judgment-standard context, it is determined FAIL.
