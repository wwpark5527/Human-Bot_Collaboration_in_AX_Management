# AI Literacy and Affinity — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the subject understands AI beyond the level of mere use, distinguishing its strengths and weaknesses.
2. Confirm whether the subject recognizes the phenomenon of hallucination and has the habit of verifying AI output.
3. Check the subject's prompt-use ability and level of understanding of AI workflow and generative AI.
4. Confirm whether the attitude of not fearing AI and handling it as a collaboration tool has been formed.
5. If the above four items are insufficient, supplement them through learning and practice.

## Criteria
If the subject has an understanding of AI's strengths, weaknesses, hallucination, prompts, and workflow and does not fear AI but handles it well as a collaboration tool, it is determined PASS (AI literacy and affinity secured). If even one of the above items is not satisfied, it is determined FAIL (further learning required).
