# AI Sovereignty (Sovereignty) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Grasp the list of external LLMs the organization is using or plans to use and their connection methods.
2. Check at each connection point the possibility that organizational sovereignty is damaged through the outflow of data and context.
3. Confirm whether a method that can technically demonstrate the preservation of sovereignty (e.g., ZKP-based patented technology) is applied.
4. Comprehensively determine whether the benefit of using external AI and the preservation of organizational sovereignty hold simultaneously.

## Criteria
It is determined PASS when the organization's data and decision sovereignty is preserved in a technically demonstrable way even while using external AI.
