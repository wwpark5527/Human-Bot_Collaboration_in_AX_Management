# AX (AI Transformation) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Diagnose whether the organization's current change attempt remains at DX-era questions (centered on digitization, automation, and system adoption) or has expanded to AX-era questions (AI judgment and generation, division of roles, verification and approval, authority, responsibility, and outcome distribution).
2. Using the table of AX's objective position (category / objective position) as the standard, organize the organization's AX expressions and purposes in line with global and Asian practice.
3. Register the areas lacking in the current mainstream purposes (efficiency, productivity, cost reduction, growth, work optimization) — inclusive transformation, prevention of AI stratification, benefit sharing, the right of appeal, the human right of judgment — as supplementary tasks.
4. Redesign human roles and responsibilities at each point where AI is integrated into the center of organizational operation (workflows, judgment systems, role structures, output systems, authority structures, accountability structures).

## Criteria
Execution of the method is determined to be complete when steps 1 through 4 have all been carried out and the organization's change attempt has reached the level of substantively intervening in AI's judgment, generation, execution, evaluation, and accountability structures.
