# AI Orchestrator — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Tally the number of AIs and agents the member uses simultaneously.
2. Measure the proportion of work handled by AI against total work.
3. Judge the level of automation in work performance (partial automation / semi-automation / full automation).
4. Combine the three indicators and evaluate "Do I do the work directly, or do I design so that AIs do the work?"
5. On the basis of the evaluation result, judge whether the member falls into the AI Orchestrator stage.

## Criteria
If the proportion of work handled by AI and the level of automation are high and the member is in the position of designing work processes, it is judged PASS (falls into the AI Orchestrator stage); if the share of work the member still performs directly is high, it is judged FAIL (does not fall into the AI Orchestrator stage, re-evaluation at the Collaborative User stage is required).
