# Algorithmic Management — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Identify the points at which algorithms and AI are involved in the areas of work assignment, performance evaluation, surveillance, schedule management, reward, discipline, and productivity measurement within the organization.
2. Specify for each point who holds AI evaluation authority (who evaluates others with AI).
3. Specify for each point who is subject to AI evaluation (who is evaluated by AI).
4. Check whether the AI evaluation criteria can be explained and, where necessary, write explanatory materials.
5. Design and announce a procedure by which humans can object to AI evaluation.
6. Designate and document the ultimate responsible party for each point.

## Criteria
If the points of algorithmic management application are identified and documents and procedures able to answer all five questions (control of authority / workers' rights / transparency / remedy procedure / accountability structure) are in place, it is determined PASS; if even one cannot be answered or is left unattended, it is determined FAIL.
