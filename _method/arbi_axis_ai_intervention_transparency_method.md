# AI Intervention Transparency — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Secure the collaboration outputs and the history of utterances.
2. Identify the stages in which AI was involved (data analysis, drafting, verification, recording, and so on).
3. Confirm whether AI intervention at each stage is specified.
4. Mark unspecified points of intervention as concealment risks.
5. Determine whether AI intervention transparency holds.

## Criteria
If all points of AI intervention are explicitly revealed, it is determined PASS; if even one is not revealed and there is a concealment risk, it is determined FAIL.
