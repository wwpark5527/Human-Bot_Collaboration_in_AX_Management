# Communication Fairness — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Survey the level of AI augmentation of the human and AH (augmented human) members participating in the collaboration.
2. Observe each member's voice and influence over decision-making in meetings, document work, and the like.
3. Compare whether voice and influence are skewed toward the members with a higher level of augmentation.
4. If a skew is confirmed, mark it as a point of conversion into a power difference.
5. Determine whether the AI-augmentation gap is fair and record the risk of communication asymmetry.

## Criteria
If the AI-augmentation gap has not turned into a relational power difference, it is judged PASS; if a skew in voice and influence is confirmed, it is judged FAIL.
