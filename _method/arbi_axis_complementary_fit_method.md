# Complementary Fit — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the strengths the human exercised in the collaboration under evaluation.
2. Confirm the strengths the AI exercised in the same collaboration.
3. Compare the two lists of strengths to identify areas of overlap and areas of gap.
4. Determine whether the strengths form a structure that complements one another without overlap.

## Criteria
If human strengths and AI strengths complement each other without overlap or gap, it is judged PASS; if the strengths overlap or a gap on either side is left unfilled, it is judged FAIL.
