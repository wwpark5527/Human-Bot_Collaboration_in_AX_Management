# Human Accountability — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Specify the final judgment and approval stages in the collaboration process.
2. Confirm the actor (human/AI) executing those stages.
3. Verify whether the human bears final approval and responsibility.
4. Identify the stages where the attribution of responsibility is unclear.
5. Determine whether human responsibility remains and record the risk of unclearness.

## Criteria
If final judgment, approval, and responsibility clearly remain with the human, it is judged PASS; if they do not or are unclear, it is judged FAIL.
