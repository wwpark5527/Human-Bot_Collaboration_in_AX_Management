# Manipulation Risk — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect the instances of AI use that occurred during the collaboration process.
2. Classify the purpose of use in each instance as facilitating collaboration or as securing advantage and applying coercion.
3. Compare whether there are indications of coercion or threat exploiting information asymmetry.
4. Separately mark the instances suspected of misuse.
5. Determine whether AI was misused (for strategic advantage or coercion) and record the result.

## Criteria
If AI was not used for strategic advantage or coercion rather than for collaboration, it is judged PASS; if indications of misuse are confirmed, it is judged FAIL.
