# Psychological & Trust Stability — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Survey the degree of felt surveillance and alienation among the members collaborating with AI.
2. Confirm the signs of accumulated decision fatigue in situations of repeated judgment about AI intervention.
3. Compare whether the burden of responsibility is concentrated on particular members.
4. Judge comprehensively whether the psychological and trust burden is at an excessive level.
5. Determine the level of psychological and trust burden on members and record the result.

## Criteria
If feelings of surveillance, alienation, decision fatigue, and burden of responsibility are not excessive, it is judged PASS; if even one of them is confirmed to be at an excessive level, it is judged FAIL.
