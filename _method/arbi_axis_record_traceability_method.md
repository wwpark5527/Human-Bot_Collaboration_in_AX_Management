# Record & Traceability — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect the supporting materials, revision histories, and approval records used during the collaboration process.
2. Confirm the time of creation, the actor, and the rationale for each material, revision, and approval item.
3. Compare whether the source of the materials used is traceable.
4. Identify the items whose history is missing or whose rationale is unclear.
5. Determine whether the collaboration history is recorded and re-verifiable.

## Criteria
If the history of rationale, revisions, approvals, and materials used all remains, it is judged PASS; if even one of them is missing or untraceable, it is judged FAIL.
