# Role Balance — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Specify the collaboration scene under evaluation.
2. Enumerate the roles needed for that collaboration to hold together.
3. Confirm the roles actually assigned to the human and to the AI.
4. Compare the needed roles against the actual assignment to identify vacancy, excess, and concentration.
5. Determine the adequacy of role assignment and record the imbalance risks.

## Criteria
If the needed roles are assigned to fit the situation, it is judged PASS; if even one instance of role vacancy, excess, or concentration is found, it is judged FAIL (imbalance risk present).
