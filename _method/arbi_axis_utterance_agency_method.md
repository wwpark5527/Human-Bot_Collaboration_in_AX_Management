# Utterance Agency — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Separate the individual utterance and judgment units from the collaboration records.
2. Trace how each unit was generated (who produced the draft and who finalized it).
3. Classify each as human alone, AI alone, or joint output.
4. Mark the items whose classification is unclear.
5. Produce a judgment on the attribution of the agent of the utterance.

## Criteria
If the agent of every utterance and judgment is clearly attributed, it is judged PASS; if there is even one item whose attribution is unclear, it is judged FAIL.
