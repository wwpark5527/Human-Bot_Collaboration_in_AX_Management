# Augmented Role Balance Index (ARBI, Augmented Role Balance Index) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect cases of AI-intervened human-human collaboration and communication.
2. Distinguish for each utterance and output whether it is a human's judgment, AI's generation, or a joint output.
3. Confirm the scope of authority over the materials and roles AI accessed.
4. Confirm whether the final judgment and responsibility remain with the human.
5. Measure role balance, utterance agency, authority boundaries, human accountability, communication fairness, and recordability respectively.
6. Aggregate the measurements to produce the ARBI index.

## Criteria
If role balance, utterance agency, authority boundaries, human accountability, communication fairness, and recordability are all measured and aggregated into an index, it is determined PASS; if only some are measured or they are not aggregated, it is determined FAIL.
