# Augmentation — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. First determine whether the purpose of introducing bots is to replace human labor or to amplify human capability.
2. If a replacement purpose is confirmed, convert the design from a zero-sum to a positive-sum structure.
3. Identify the human augmentation domains (physical/mental) and the bot augmentation domains (context/value/learning) respectively and establish an augmentation plan.
4. Confirm the results of forming augmented humans (AH) and augmented bots (AB), and check whether the perception that "AI strengthens me" has formed among members.
5. Measure whether positive-sum outcomes (growth of both sides) actually appear, and make the final determination of whether augmentation has occurred.

## Criteria
When bots are designed as an amplifier of human capability and positive-sum outcomes in which both humans and bots grow are confirmed, a PASS determination of augmentation is made. If the purpose is replacement, or if the structure is zero-sum in which one side gains at the other's sacrifice, a FAIL determination is made.
