# Augmented Human Capability Index (AHCI) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Diagnose the human augmentation degree (whether the human is moving closer to AH).
2. Diagnose the bot cooperation degree (whether the bot is moving closer to BH).
3. Diagnose the human-bot alignment degree (whether human and bot are aligning).
4. Diagnose output fidelity (whether the results are faithful and evidence-based).
5. Diagnose the rate of improvement (whether the relationship improves over time).
6. Sum the five elements to produce AHCI.
7. Use the produced result as the input to the HBRM capability diagnosis and to education and development plans.

## Criteria
If all five elements are diagnosed and it is confirmed that "this human is actually being augmented while working with this bot", it is judged PASS; if a missing element or a shortfall in alignment degree or fidelity is confirmed, it is judged FAIL (further development needed).
