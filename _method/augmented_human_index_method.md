# Augmented Human Index (AHI) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Measure the change in Performance before and after AI use.
2. Measure the change in Learning (learning speed).
3. Measure the change in Influence.
4. Measure the change in Innovation.
5. Measure the change in Adaptability.
6. Synthesize the five elements as a function to compute AHI = f(Performance, Learning, Influence, Innovation, Adaptability).
7. Make the final judgment on "whether that person is actually an AH" from the produced AHI result.

## Criteria
If increased problem-solving ability, increased learning speed, improved decision-making, and improved collaboration ability are confirmed together, it is judged PASS (judged to be an AH); if it is confirmed that, with declining judgment and reduced creativity, the person depends solely on AI, it is judged FAIL (merely an AI User, not an AH).
