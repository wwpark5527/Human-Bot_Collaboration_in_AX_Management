# Human-Centered Path — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the communication in question is a situation where meaning, emotion, and trust matter.
2. Set the path as a structure in which the human speaks and judges directly (AI A ↔ Human A ↔ Human B ↔ AI B).
3. Limit the AI's role to organizing, analyzing, reviewing, and drafting.
4. Confirm whether meaning, emotion, and trust are preserved even at the cost of reduced speed.

## Criteria
If the agent of utterance and judgment is the human and meaning, emotion, and trust relationships are preserved, it is judged PASS (the human-centered path is suitable); if the AI takes over utterance and judgment or the relationship is damaged, it is judged FAIL.
