# Additional Conditions of Organizational AX — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Make the five items — augmentation-centered philosophy, respect for diversity, complementary fit between humans and bots, building a common context, and building a governance context — concrete in a way that fits the organization's situation.
2. Confirm that no legal or social problem or AX impossibility arises even when each item is unmet, while analyzing the possibility of a loss of competitiveness if it is left unattended.
3. Establish an execution plan that reflects the five items in the organization's strategy and design principles.
4. Periodically measure whether the execution results raise organizational competitiveness over the mid-to-long term.
5. Allocate resources by coordinating priorities with the necessary conditions.

## Criteria
If the five additional conditions are reflected in organizational operations and the effect of enhanced mid-to-long-term competitiveness is confirmed, it is judged PASS (additional conditions met); if they are not reflected or the effect is not confirmed, it is judged FAIL (additional conditions unmet). However, it is recorded together with the judgment that being unmet does not immediately mean a legal or social problem or AX impossibility.
