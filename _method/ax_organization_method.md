# AX Organization — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Convert the progress indicators of the organizational AX redesign process (the redesign items for the decision-making structure, communication structure, power structure, and collaboration structure) into result-based criteria and determine whether they are complete.
2. Finalize the organizational structure and role allocation in which human members and bots (AI) collaborate together.
3. Establish and operate the governance (authority, responsibility, and verification systems) for maintaining the AX organization state.

## Criteria
Execution of the method is determined to be complete when the redesign of the organizational operating system is confirmed as a result rather than a process, and the human-bot coexistence and collaboration structure is actually in operation.
