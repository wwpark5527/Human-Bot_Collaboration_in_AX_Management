# AI Auditor — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Grasp the types of AI outputs (analysis results, generated content, decision proposals, etc.).
2. Establish verification criteria by type (accuracy, reliability, presence of hallucination, presence of bias).
3. Check AI outputs against the verification criteria and record the results.
4. If errors, hallucination or bias are found, connect to a rework or reporting procedure.

## Criteria
If a verification procedure for AI results is established and verification is actually performed and recorded, it is judged PASS; if the verification procedure or the record of its performance is not confirmed, it is judged FAIL.
