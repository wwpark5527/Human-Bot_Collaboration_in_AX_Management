# AI Governor — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Grasp the AI decision points and the scope of outputs within the organization.
2. Designate the locus of accountability for each AI decision and output (who bears final approval and the duty to explain).
3. Establish a procedure for checking whether AI behavior and outputs are aligned with the organization's goals and ethical standards.
4. Operate the accountability and alignment management system and continuously update it.

## Criteria
If both the locus of accountability for AI and the alignment management procedure are confirmed, it is judged PASS; if even one of the two is not confirmed, it is judged FAIL.
