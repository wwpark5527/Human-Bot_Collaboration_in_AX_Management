# AI Workflow Orchestrator — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Decompose the target business process into human-performed segments and AI-performed segments.
2. Define the execution order of each segment and the handoff points between humans and AI.
3. Design the format of information transfer (input/output) at the handoff points.
4. Run the collaboration flow while monitoring and improving bottlenecks, duplication and delays.

## Criteria
If a design of the human-AI collaboration flow is established and applied to and operated in the actual process, it is judged PASS; if there is no design or its operation is not confirmed, it is judged FAIL.
