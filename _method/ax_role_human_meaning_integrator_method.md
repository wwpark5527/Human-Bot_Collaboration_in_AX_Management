# Human Meaning Integrator — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect the outputs produced by AI (analyses, recommendations, predictions, etc.).
2. Confirm the organization's current goals, strategy and stakeholder situation.
3. Interpret the meaning and implications by placing the AI results into the organizational context.
4. Deliver the interpretation to decision-makers and the relevant departments and reflect their feedback.

## Criteria
If an interpretation of AI results connected to the organizational context is produced and actually reflected in the decision-making process, it is judged PASS; if there is no interpretation or the AI results are passed on as they are without context, it is judged FAIL.
