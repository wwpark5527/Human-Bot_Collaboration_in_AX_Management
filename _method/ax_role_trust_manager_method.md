# Trust Manager — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect members' expressions of trust in AI outputs (acceptance rate, appeals, requests for re-verification, etc.).
2. Analyze the level of trust and identify the ranges of deviation toward overconfidence or distrust.
3. Grasp the causes of the deviation (lack of explanation, past experience of errors, etc.).
4. Adjust the level of trust into an appropriate range by providing explanations, guiding verification procedures, and so on.

## Criteria
If the result of AI trust management is produced and measures to adjust the trust deviation are actually implemented, it is judged PASS; if the level of trust is left unattended and unmanaged, it is judged FAIL.
