# Control Stress — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the core question in the expression of stress is 'who actually controls?'.
2. Distinguish whether the cause of the stress is not a matter of trust but the ambiguity of the locus of control.
3. Confirm whether a demand for redefining the scope of control appears.
4. If the above conditions are confirmed, judge it to be control stress and warn of the risk that, if left unresolved, it leads to governance collapse.

## Criteria
If the core question is 'who actually controls?' and the cause of the stress lies in the ambiguity of the locus of control, it is judged PASS as control stress. If the cause corresponds to the other three dangers (identity, trust, responsibility), it is judged FAIL (reclassified as another type).
