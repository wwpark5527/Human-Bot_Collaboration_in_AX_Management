# Identity Stress — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the core question in the member's expression of stress is 'why am I needed?'.
2. Distinguish whether the cause of the stress is not workload but uncertainty about the reason for existence and the value of the role.
3. Confirm whether a demand for redefining role and value appears.
4. If the above conditions are confirmed, judge it to be identity stress and warn of the risk that, if left unresolved, it leads to a crisis of existential value.

## Criteria
If the core question is 'why am I needed?' and the cause of the stress is uncertainty about the reason for existence and the value of the role, it is judged PASS as identity stress. If the cause is workload or corresponds to the other three dangers (trust, control, responsibility), it is judged FAIL (reclassified as another type).
