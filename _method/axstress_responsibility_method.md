# Responsibility Stress — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the core question in the expression of stress is 'who takes responsibility?'.
2. Distinguish whether the cause of the stress is not the problem of the locus of control but the unclearness of outcome attribution itself.
3. Confirm whether a demand for redefining the scope of responsibility appears.
4. When the above conditions are confirmed, judge it as responsibility stress and warn of the risk that, if left unresolved, it leads to responsibility avoidance and declining performance.

## Criteria
If the core question is 'who takes responsibility?' and the cause of the stress lies in the unclearness of outcome attribution, judge it PASS as responsibility stress. If the cause corresponds to the other three dangers (identity, trust, control), judge it FAIL (reclassify as another type).
