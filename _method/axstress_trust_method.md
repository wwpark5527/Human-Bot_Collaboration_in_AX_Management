# Trust Stress — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the core question in the expression of stress is 'can I trust the other party?'.
2. Distinguish whether the cause of the stress is not the individual member's reason for existence but the human-bot interaction relationship.
3. Confirm whether a demand for the design of trust-based cooperation appears.
4. If the above conditions are confirmed, judge it to be trust stress and warn of the risk that, if left unresolved, it leads to a collapse of human-AI trust.

## Criteria
If the core question is 'can I trust the other party?' and the cause of the stress lies in the human-bot interaction relationship, it is judged PASS as trust stress. If the cause corresponds to the other three dangers (identity, control, responsibility), it is judged FAIL (reclassified as another type).
