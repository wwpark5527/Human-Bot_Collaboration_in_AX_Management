# Coordinator (CO) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Grasp, through TR diagnosis (Interplace and the like), the degree to which each individual exhibits the Coordinator disposition.
2. Evaluate the contribution to the coordination of members and tasks against the criteria.
3. Map those personnel to the human-AI workflow orchestrator role in the context of an AX organization.

## Criteria
If the mode of contribution is the coordination of members and tasks, it is judged PASS (Coordinator role). If it shows the characteristics of another team role such as creative ideation, analysis and judgment, or execution, it is judged FAIL.
