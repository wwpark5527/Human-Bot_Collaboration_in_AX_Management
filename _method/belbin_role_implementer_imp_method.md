# Implementer (IMP) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Use TR diagnostics (Interplace and the like) to grasp the degree to which each individual exercises the Implementer disposition.
2. Evaluate the contribution of implementation (actual execution) against the criteria.
3. In the AX organization context, map that person to the role of establishing AI operational processes, and design the division of work with the repetitive execution tasks an AI bot can perform.

## Criteria
If the mode of contribution is implementation (actual execution), it is judged PASS (Implementer role). If it shows the characteristics of other team roles such as creative ideation, analysis and judgment, or coordination, it is judged FAIL.
