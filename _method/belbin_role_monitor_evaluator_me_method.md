# Monitor-Evaluator (ME) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Use TR diagnostics (Interplace and the like) to grasp the degree to which each individual exercises the Monitor-Evaluator disposition.
2. Evaluate the contribution of cool-headed analysis and judgment against the criteria.
3. In the AX organization context, map that person to the role of AI-output verifier, and design the division of work with the analytical tasks an AI bot can perform.

## Criteria
If the mode of contribution is cool-headed analysis and judgment, it is judged PASS (Monitor-Evaluator role). If it shows the characteristics of other team roles such as creative ideation, coordination, or execution, it is judged FAIL.
