# Plant (PL) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Use TR diagnostics (Interplace and the like) to grasp the degree to which each individual exercises the Plant disposition.
2. Evaluate the contribution of producing creative ideas against the criteria.
3. In the AX organization context, map that person to the role of AI-enabled innovation designer.

## Criteria
If the mode of contribution is producing new conceptions and ideas, it is judged PASS (Plant role). If it shows the characteristics of other team roles such as analysis/judgment, coordination, or execution, it is judged FAIL.
