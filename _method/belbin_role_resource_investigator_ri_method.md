# Resource Investigator (RI) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Use TR diagnostics (Interplace and the like) to grasp the degree to which each individual exercises the Resource Investigator disposition.
2. Evaluate the contribution of external exploration against the criteria.
3. In the AX organization context, map that person to the role of AI-ecosystem exploration, and design the division of work with the information-seeking tasks an AI bot can perform.

## Criteria
If the mode of contribution is external exploration, it is judged PASS (Resource Investigator role). If it shows the characteristics of other team roles such as error removal and completion or relational harmony, it is judged FAIL.
