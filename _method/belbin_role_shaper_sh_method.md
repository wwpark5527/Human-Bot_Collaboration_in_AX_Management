# Shaper (SH) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Use TR diagnostics (Interplace and the like) to grasp the degree to which each individual exercises the Shaper disposition.
2. Evaluate the contribution of exercising driving force against the criteria.
3. In the AX organization context, map that person to the role of AX change driver and deploy them into the stalled stretches of the transformation.

## Criteria
If the mode of contribution is exercising driving force, it is judged PASS (Shaper role). If it shows the characteristics of other team roles such as relational harmony or the provision of expert knowledge, it is judged FAIL.
