# Specialist (SP) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Use TR diagnostics (Interplace and the like) to grasp the degree to which each individual exercises the Specialist disposition.
2. Evaluate the contribution of providing expert knowledge against the criteria.
3. In the AX organization context, map that person to the role of domain + AI hybrid expert and design a plan for combining the domain of expertise with AI capability.

## Criteria
If the mode of contribution is providing expert knowledge, it is judged PASS (Specialist role). If it shows the characteristics of other team roles such as driving force or external exploration, it is judged FAIL.
