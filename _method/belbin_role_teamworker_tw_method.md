# Teamworker (TW) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Use TR diagnostics (Interplace and the like) to grasp the degree to which each individual exercises the Teamworker disposition.
2. Evaluate the contribution of relational harmony and atmosphere-building against the criteria.
3. In the AX organization context, map that person to the role of promoting acceptance of human-AI collaboration, and design activities that help members accept it emotionally.

## Criteria
If the mode of contribution is relational harmony and atmosphere-building, it is judged PASS (Teamworker role). If it shows the characteristics of other team roles such as external exploration or driving force, it is judged FAIL.
