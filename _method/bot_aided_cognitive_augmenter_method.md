# Cognitive Augmenter — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect and organize the raw data needed for the team's decision-making.
2. Generate multiple decision alternatives on the basis of the organized data.
3. Simulate the risks for each alternative.
4. Optimize the schedule by reflecting the simulation results.
5. Provide the results of organization, generation, simulation, and optimization to the human decision-maker and support coordination.

## Criteria
If the results of data organization, alternative generation, risk simulation, and schedule optimization are all produced and provided to the human decision-maker, it is judged PASS; otherwise FAIL.
