# Collaboration Facilitator — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect and analyze speaking-time data from meetings and collaboration sessions.
2. When an imbalance in participation is sensed, record the relevant information.
3. Detect signals of conflict between roles (duplicate instructions, contradictory decisions, and the like).
4. Detect communication bottlenecks (response delays, information breaks, and the like).
5. Share the sensing and detection results with the team and support coordination.

## Criteria
If the results of meeting speaking-time analysis, role-conflict detection, and communication-bottleneck detection are produced and shared with the team, it is judged PASS; if observing and sensing do not take place, it is judged FAIL.
