# Role Diagnostician — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect the team members' work performance data and Belbin role classification information.
2. Analyze whether a particular role (for example, the Monitor-Evaluator ME) is lacking.
3. Analyze whether a particular role (for example, the Plant PL) is in excess.
4. Detect patterns of increased conflict between roles (for example, conflict among Shapers SH).
5. Report the diagnostic results to the human team leader.

## Criteria
If the diagnostic results for in-team role vacancy, role concentration, and role conflict are produced and delivered to the team leader, it is judged PASS; if the diagnosis does not take place, it is judged FAIL.
