# Level 3 Planner/Strategist — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the goal (or policy constraints) given from above.
2. Formulate the strategy for achieving the goal.
3. Decompose the strategy into executable sub-tasks (subgoals).
4. Allocate the decomposed sub-tasks to the appropriate Level 2 / Level 1 agents.
5. Re-check the executability and completeness of the decomposed tasks.

## Criteria
If the function of setting the goal and decomposing it into executable sub-tasks is actually performed, it is judged PASS (the Planner/Strategist role is performed); if the goal is unclear or is passed down unchanged without decomposition, it is judged FAIL (the role is not performed).
