# B: Bot — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the entity of the target member is a human or a bot.
2. If the entity is a bot, confirm whether common & governance context and AI governance are embedded.
3. If they are not embedded, classify it as the B (bot) type; if they are embedded, reclassify it as AB (augmented bot).
4. Record the role and work scope of the B-type bot (specific-task automation) in contrast with the AB/AH/H types.

## Criteria
It is determined to be the B type when the entity is a bot and common & governance context and AI governance are in a non-embedded state. If embedding is confirmed, it is reclassified as AB.
