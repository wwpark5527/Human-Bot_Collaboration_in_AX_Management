# Complementation of TR by bots — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the traditional meaning of each of the 9 Belbin TRs.
2. Map each TR onto the AX-organization reinterpretation table (9 reinterpretation items).
3. In each TR, distinguish the elements the bot can perform (those related to computation, memory, exploration, repetition, pattern recognition, real-time optimization) from the elements the human must retain (those related to vision-setting, oversight, meaning-giving, ethics, integrative judgment, conflict mediation, trust-building, accountability).
4. Distribute roles as a structure of complementation rather than replacement, and confirm the effect of expanding overall performance (pie size).

## Criteria
It is judged FAIL if the bot's intervention replaces the existing TR, and PASS (complementation mode) if the human and the bot mutually complement one another with different strengths.
