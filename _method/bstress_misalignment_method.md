# Misalignment — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether one or more of bias, incompleteness, conflict, or ambiguity exists in the bot's data, rules, commands, and governance system.
2. Confirm whether increased unpredictability, emergent behavior, or the risk of policy violation is observed in the bot's behavior.
3. If the above conditions are confirmed, judge it misalignment and apply realignment measures per cause (data/rules/commands/governance).
4. After the realignment measures, re-confirm whether predictability and policy compliance are restored.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS as misalignment when one or more of data bias, incomplete rules, conflicting commands, or governance ambiguity is confirmed and one or more of increased unpredictability, emergent behavior, or policy violation accompanies it. If the cause falls under another bot stress type such as conflict among goals or limits of computational resources, it is judged FAIL (reclassified into another type).
