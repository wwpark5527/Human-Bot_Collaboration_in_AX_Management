# Multi-agent cooperation stress — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the stress originates not from a single bot but from the process of cooperation among agents within a multi-agent system.
2. Confirm whether one or more of coordination failure, communication overload, or authority ambiguity is observed.
3. Check whether the problem of 'Which agent has priority? Who decides when judgments conflict?' is confirmed.
4. If the above conditions are confirmed, judge it multi-agent cooperation stress and establish rules of priority and decision rights.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS as multi-agent cooperation stress when one or more of coordination failure, communication overload, or authority ambiguity among agents is confirmed and the problem of unclear priority/decision rights accompanies it. If the cause falls under another bot stress type such as a single bot's limits of computational resources or value misalignment, it is judged FAIL (reclassified into another type).
