# Labor-union-type resistance — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect the official AI-related demands and statements of labor unions or similar collective organizations in the target industry and job family.
2. Confirm whether demands for limits on automation or demands for mandating human employment appear.
3. Confirm whether demands for restricting the scope of AI use, or for mandatory retraining, appear.
4. When one or more of the four features is confirmed, judge it to be labor-union-type resistance.

## Criteria
When one or more of demanding limits on automation, mandating human employment, restricting the scope of AI use, and requiring mandatory retraining appears, a PASS judgment of labor-union-type resistance is made. When all four features are absent, a FAIL judgment (reclassification as another type) is made.
