# Ontological resistance — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the ground of the resistance — distinguish whether it is a concrete issue such as jobs or institutions, or a question about the meaning of human existence itself.
2. Confirm whether the core question "Why are humans needed when AI does it better?" is raised explicitly or implicitly.
3. Confirm signs of deterioration such as cynicism, organizational disengagement, an anti-technology identity, human supremacism, and AI hatred.
4. When the ground is a question about the meaning of human existence, judge it to be ontological resistance and immediately begin a response based on the augmentation narrative.

## Criteria
When the ground of the resistance is a question about the meaning of human existence itself rather than jobs or institutions, a PASS judgment of ontological resistance is made. When the ground remains at concrete issues (employment, expertise, culture, institutions), a FAIL judgment (reclassification as another type) is made.
