# Political-policy resistance — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the resistance appears as a demand for control through formal institutions such as legislative and regulatory bodies.
2. Confirm whether strengthening AI regulation or emphasizing data sovereignty is raised as an issue.
3. Confirm whether restricting AI use or securing human control is raised as an issue.
4. When one or more of the above features is confirmed, judge it to be political-policy resistance.

## Criteria
When one or more of the features of strengthening AI regulation, emphasizing data sovereignty, restricting AI use, and securing human control is confirmed, a PASS judgment of political-policy resistance is made. When all are absent, a FAIL judgment (reclassification as another type) is made.
