# Professional-group resistance — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the subject of the resistance is a highly skilled professional group such as the medical, education, or legal field.
2. Confirm whether unclear allocation of responsibility is raised as an issue.
3. Confirm whether infringement of expertise or devaluation of professional worth is raised as an issue.
4. When the subject of the resistance is a highly skilled profession and one or more of the three issues is confirmed, judge it to be professional-group resistance.

## Criteria
When the subject of the resistance is a highly skilled profession such as the medical, education, or legal field and one or more of unclear allocation of responsibility, infringement of expertise, and devaluation of professional worth is at issue, a PASS judgment of professional-group resistance is made. When the subject is not a highly skilled profession or all three issues are absent, a FAIL judgment (reclassification as another type) is made.
