# Sociocultural resistance — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the resistance appears through the medium of cultural identity or distrust of the technological elite rather than a particular organization (a labor union, a professional association).
2. Confirm whether there are signs of 'formation of an anti-AI culture' such as human-first-ism, the Slow AI movement, and the emphasis on Human-made.
3. Confirm whether there is the perception that "a few big tech firms control human society," that is, critiques of AI monopoly, data colonialism, and algorithmic power.
4. When one or more of the two sub-types (formation of an anti-AI culture, distrust of the technological elite) is confirmed, judge it to be sociocultural resistance.

## Criteria
When there are signs of human-first-ism, the Slow AI movement, or the emphasis on Human-made, or when the perception that "a few big tech firms control human society" (critiques of AI monopoly, data colonialism, and algorithmic power) is confirmed, a PASS judgment of sociocultural resistance is made. When both sub-types are absent, a FAIL judgment (reclassification as another type) is made.
