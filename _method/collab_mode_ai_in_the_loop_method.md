# AI-in-the-loop (human-led + AI assist) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the initiative for the work lies with the human.
2. Confirm whether the AI's intervention takes place in real time during the course of the work rather than at a prior approval point.
3. Confirm whether the AI comes 'inside' the loop and assists and augments the human's work in real time.
4. If all the above conditions are satisfied, judge it the AI-in-the-loop type.
5. Produce and record the real-time augmentation results for the human-led work.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS as AI-in-the-loop when the human retains the initiative while the AI assists in real time during the course of the work. If the AI's intervention is confined to the approval point, it is reclassified as Human-in-the-loop; if the performing agent passes to the AI, it is reclassified as Human-on-the-loop.
