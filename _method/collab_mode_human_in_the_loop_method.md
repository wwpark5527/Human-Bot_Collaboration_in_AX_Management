# Human-in-the-loop (human-approval-centered) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether a stage exists at which the AI proposes a result.
2. Confirm whether that proposal passes through a stage of human review before it is finalized as a deliverable.
3. Confirm whether the final approval authority lies with the human rather than the AI.
4. If all the above conditions are satisfied, judge it the Human-in-the-loop type.
5. Operate the process so that only results approved by the human are finalized as deliverables.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS as Human-in-the-loop when a result can be finalized only after the human's final approval following the AI's proposal. If the human's intervention is at the level of real-time assistance, it is reclassified as AI-in-the-loop; if it is at the level of post hoc oversight, it is reclassified as Human-on-the-loop.
