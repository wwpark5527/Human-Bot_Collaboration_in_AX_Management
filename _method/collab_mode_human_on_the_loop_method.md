# Human-on-the-loop (AI-autonomous + human oversight) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether execution of the work has passed to the AI rather than the human.
2. Confirm whether human intervention takes the form of after-the-fact, continuous monitoring rather than prior approval.
3. Confirm whether the human is in the position of an overseer 'on' the loop, that is, whether the structure is one of monitoring the whole rather than approving each step.
4. When all of the above conditions are met, judge it to be the Human-on-the-loop type.
5. Record and produce together the results the AI has performed and the human's monitoring and intervention judgments.

## Criteria
When execution rests with the AI and the human is in the position of an after-the-fact, continuous overseer, a PASS judgment of Human-on-the-loop is made. If human intervention is at the level of real-time assistance, it is reclassified as AI-in-the-loop; if there is effectively no human intervention, it is reclassified as Autonomous AI.
