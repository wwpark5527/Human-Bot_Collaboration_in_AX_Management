# Role — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Decompose the AI workflow stage by stage, from problem definition to improvement reflection.
2. Assign to each stage the applicable role among problem definer, context composer, AI execution director, result interpreter, verifier, responsibility judge, evidence manager, and improvement reflector.
3. Identify the stages to which no role has been assigned and mark them as gaps.
4. Clarify, by role, the accountability structure for how far AI results may be used.
5. Verify that 'who must confirm what' is not unclear at any stage.

## Criteria
A PASS judgment is made when the subjects who will interpret, verify, approve, record, and improve the result are all designated; a FAIL judgment is made when the subject for even one of them is unclear.
