# AH-AH communication — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Compare and evaluate the augmentation power of both sides across the seven elements of AI performance, quality of the common context, accessible materials, execution authority, human judgment, level of governance, and the recording/verification system.
2. Inspect whether there is a contribution conflict (a mismatch in criteria, interpretation, verification state, or responsibility judgment).
3. Select the path suited to the situation (human-centered / AI-centered / via governance) and coordinate meaning, conditions, judgment, and responsibility.

## Criteria
A PASS judgment is made when symmetry is evaluated by the seven elements of augmentation power and, where a contribution conflict has arisen, it is coordinated through one of the three paths; otherwise, a FAIL judgment is made.
