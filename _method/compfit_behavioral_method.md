# Behavioral complementation — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Classify whether the target work is repetitive and routine or non-routine and exceptional.
2. Assign repetitive, routine behavior (the routine workflow) to the bot and have it performed automatically.
3. Monitor whether exceptional or crisis situations that fall outside the predefined scope arise during the bot's performance.
4. When an exceptional or crisis situation (crisis handling) arises, escalate it to the human to be responded to.
5. Inspect whether the result leads not to excluding humans from execution but to an augmentation that concentrates them on the capability of responding to exceptions.

## Criteria
A PASS judgment is made when the bot takes charge of repetitive, routine behavior and the human takes charge of responses to non-routine and exceptional situations, and escalation works smoothly when an exception arises. A FAIL judgment is made when the bot handles even exceptional situations at its own discretion or when there is no escalation path.
