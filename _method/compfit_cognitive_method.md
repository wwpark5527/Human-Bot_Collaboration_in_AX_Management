# Cognitive complementation — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the target task requires cognitive judgment (for example, a strategy meeting).
2. Decompose the task into computation, search, and memory elements and into intuition, creativity, and imagination elements.
3. Assign the computation, search, and memory elements (for example, data simulation) to the bot and have it perform them.
4. The human receives the bot's output and interprets the strategic meaning by exercising intuition, creativity, and imagination.
5. Check whether the result leads to the augmentation of human capability rather than the replacement of humans.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the bot takes charge of computation, search, and memory, the human takes charge of interpretation and judgment based on intuition, creativity, and imagination, and their combination results in the augmentation rather than the replacement of human capability. It is judged FAIL when the bot exclusively handles judgment and interpretation as well, or when the human judges without the bot's computational results.
