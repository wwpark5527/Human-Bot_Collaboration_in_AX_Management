# Emotional complementation — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the target task requires emotional support (for example, managing members' stress).
2. Decompose the task into immediate-response, fatigue-free, and continuity elements and into empathy, trust, and psychological stability elements.
3. Assign the immediate-response, fatigue-free, and continuity elements (for example, 24-hour always-on support) to the bot (AI coach) and have it perform them.
4. The human leader directly takes charge of the empathy, trust, and psychological stability elements (for example, the formation of psychological safety).
5. Check whether the combination of the two forms of support leads to the augmentation rather than the replacement of the human emotional role.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the bot takes charge of immediacy, freedom from fatigue, and continuity, the human takes charge of providing empathy, trust, and psychological stability, and their combination results in the augmentation rather than the replacement of the human emotional role. It is judged FAIL when the bot exclusively handles the provision of psychological safety as well, or when human support is replaced by bot responses alone.
