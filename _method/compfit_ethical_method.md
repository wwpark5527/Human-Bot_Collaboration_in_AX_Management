# Ethical complementation — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the target work involves ethical judgment.
2. Decompose the work into elements of the consistent application of rules and elements of judging the legitimacy of the rules.
3. Assign the consistent application of rules (for example, determination against fixed criteria) to the bot and have it perform them.
4. The human directly judges the legitimacy of the rules themselves, value judgment, social responsibility, and context-based adjustment.
5. Confirm whether the authority for final approval and correction of the bot's rule-application results lies with the human.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the bot takes charge of the consistent application of the rules and the human ultimately takes charge of judging the legitimacy of the rules themselves. It is judged FAIL when the bot decides the legitimacy of the rules on its own, or when rules are automatically confirmed without the human's final approval.
