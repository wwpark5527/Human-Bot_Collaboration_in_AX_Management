# Context Capital Access Right (Context Access Right) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Ascertain, in the target organization or work unit, the current state of members' holding of AI accounts and usage licenses (formal access).
2. Separately ascertain, in the same unit, the current state of members' access to purpose, standards, materials, and approval structure (actual access, the context capital access right).
3. Compare the two states and identify the members who have an AI account but cannot access context capital (the group with formal access only).
4. Diagnose the causes of context capital monopolization (sense, language ability, information access, rank, departmental authority, and so on).
5. Execute the disclosure, standardization, and distribution procedures for converting the monopolized context capital into a common production base for the whole organization.
6. Re-examine whether, after the conversion, the ability to use AI has moved from an individual privilege to a common organizational capability.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when members, regardless of holding an AI account, actually access the purpose, standards, materials, and approval structure and use AI meaningfully, and FAIL when they have an AI account but cannot access context capital and therefore cannot use AI properly.
