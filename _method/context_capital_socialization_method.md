# Socialization of Context Capital — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Survey the current holdings of the context needed for AI use within the organization (purpose, standards, language, materials, format, verification standards, approval standards).
2. Identify the points at which context capital is monopolized by a few individuals or a particular department.
3. Establish a plan to convert the monopolized context capital into a form accessible to the whole organization, such as a common context.
4. Grant members access rights to the converted context capital and provide training in its use.
5. In connection with the responsible operating system, maintain the authority, verification, and record systems for the socialized context capital.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when context capital has been converted into a common production base accessible to the whole organization and access rights have been granted, and FAIL when a state of monopolization by a few individuals or departments is maintained.
