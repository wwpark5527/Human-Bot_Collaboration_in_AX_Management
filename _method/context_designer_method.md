# Context Designer (context-design-type AX talent) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect and organize the organization's purpose, standards, language, ways of working, output formats, and verification criteria.
2. Explicitly convert what has been collected into conditions that AI (the first LLM) can execute.
3. Verify AI execution results against the organization's standards and reflect the feedback.
4. Through repeated context design activity, integrate the accumulated organizational context as an asset for forming the second LLM.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the first LLM works effectively within the organization according to its purpose and standards, and FAIL when the necessary conditions are not formed and the AI results diverge from the organization's judgment.
