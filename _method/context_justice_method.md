# Context Justice — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Survey the current distribution of access authority over the organization's purpose, standards, materials, and verification system, and diagnose whether it is monopolized by a few.
2. If monopolization is confirmed, design the procedure by which members can give context (purpose, standards, materials) to AI.
3. Establish a member verification procedure and verification criteria for AI output results.
4. Establish an appeal channel and a review process for AI judgments.
5. Regularly examine whether the three authorities—the right to give context, the right to verify, and the right of appeal—are actually exercised, and redesign the parts that fall short.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when members actually exercise the procedures and authority to give context to AI, to verify AI results, and to contest AI judgments, and FAIL when even one of these three authorities is monopolized by a select few so that members cannot exercise it.
