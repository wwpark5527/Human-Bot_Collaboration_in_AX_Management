# Continuous Learning Ability — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Check the extent of change between the point in time that the currently held knowledge and skills take as their reference and the present point in time.
2. Compare one's own relearning speed against the pace of change in technologies, tools, and ways of working.
3. If the relearning speed falls short of the pace of change, re-establish and execute the learning plan.
4. Check whether the relearning has actually been reflected in how work is carried out.

## Criteria
If one is relearning faster than the pace of change and is continuously updated, it is judged PASS (a state of continuous learning is maintained). If the relearning speed falls short of the pace of change and knowledge is stagnant, it is judged FAIL (the relearning plan needs to be re-established).
