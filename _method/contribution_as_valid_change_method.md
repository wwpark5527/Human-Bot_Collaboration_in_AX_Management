# Contribution — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the performed act (work).
2. Inspect whether that act changed the counterpart's understanding.
3. Inspect whether that act made judgment, verification, and approval possible.
4. Inspect whether that act remained as a record and whether it led to the next action.
5. If at least one of the above conditions is confirmed, judge it a valid change and record the changed state (meaning / judgment / verification / approval / record / execution / responsibility).

## Criteria
If because of the act at least one of the states of meaning, judgment, verification, approval, record, execution, and responsibility actually changed it is judged PASS (valid change — contribution), and if there is only the act with no state change it is judged FAIL (stopped at work, not contribution).
