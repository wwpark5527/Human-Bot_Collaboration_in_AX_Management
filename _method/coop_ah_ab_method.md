# AH + AB type — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether, in the cooperative relationship, both human and bot are augmented and whether it is a horizontal, jointly optimizing relationship.
2. Examine the AH side for signs of Orchestration overload, decision fatigue, concentration of responsibility, and weakened sense of reality.
3. Examine the AB side for signs of Context inconsistency, Alignment stress, Autonomy ambiguity, and Continuous update overload.
4. If signs on the AH side are confirmed, apply the AI orchestration dashboard, Cognitive load reduction, AI delegation rule, and Decision hierarchy.
5. If signs on the AB side are confirmed, apply Role-constrained autonomy, Explainability layer, Policy-aware agent, and Stable context memory.
6. Finally examine whether a common context has been built through the formation of the governance context and the knowledge chain.

## Criteria
When it is confirmed that both human and bot are augmented and form a horizontal, jointly optimizing relationship, it is judged PASS as the AH+AB type and the above response procedure is applied. If either side is not augmented or the relationship is vertical, it is judged FAIL (reclassification into another cooperation type required).
