# AH + B type — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the cooperative composition is the coexistence of an augmented human (AH) and a non-augmented bot (B), and whether the bot performs work independently.
2. Examine the AH side for signs of loss of control, incapacitation of competence, concentration of responsibility, and fear of surveillance.
3. Examine the B side for signs of lack of context, governance conflict, inconsistency with human instructions, and lack of trust.
4. If signs on the AH side are confirmed, apply clarification of the final approval authority, role differentiation, building a governance system, and privacy protection rules.
5. If signs on the B side are confirmed, apply building a common context, building a governance context, defining the approval system, and providing explainability.
6. Re-examine the success or failure of this stage according to the design criteria for the role balance of human and bot (TRB).

## Criteria
When it is confirmed that an augmented human (AH) and a non-augmented bot (B) coexist and the bot performs work independently, it is judged PASS as the AH+B type and the above response procedure is applied. If the bot does not perform work independently or the composition of the parties differs, it is judged FAIL (reclassification into another cooperation type required).
