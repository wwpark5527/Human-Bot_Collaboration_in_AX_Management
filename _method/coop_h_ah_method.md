# H + AH type — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the cooperating parties are a human (H) and an augmented human (AH), and whether a gap in augmentation level exists between them.
2. Examine the H side for signs of relative inferiority, alienation, and unfair evaluation.
3. Examine the AH side for signs of continuous high-performance pressure, AI dependency, isolation in human relationships, and identity confusion.
4. If signs on the H side are confirmed, apply the emphasis on complementary roles instead of comparison and the strengthening of the capabilities distinctive to humans.
5. If signs on the AH side are confirmed, apply AI dependency monitoring, collaborative leadership training, and the establishment of a human orchestrator identity.
6. At the organizational level, review the ban on human-only evaluation, an evaluation system that takes the augmentation level into account, and Pairing collaboration design.

## Criteria
When the cooperating parties are a human (H) and an augmented human (AH) and a gap in augmentation level is confirmed, it is judged PASS as the H+AH type and the above response procedure is applied. If one of the parties is a bot or no gap is confirmed, it is judged FAIL (reclassification into another cooperation type required).
