# H + B type — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the structure of initiative in the cooperative relationship and determine whether it is the vertical relationship of human-led, bot-assisted work.
2. Examine the human (H) side for signs of loss of control, role erosion, imbalance of responsibility, and learning pressure.
3. Examine the bot (B) side for signs of lack of Context, overload from exceptional situations, and inconsistency in human feedback.
4. If signs on the H side are confirmed, apply the adoption of explainable AI, maintaining Human-in-the-loop, maintaining augmentation instead of replacement, and training in AI comprehension.
5. If signs on the B side are confirmed, apply building a common context, an exception escalation system, and a feedback learning structure.

## Criteria
When it is confirmed that the relationship between human and bot is vertical and the bot occupies the position of a tool or automation executor, it is judged PASS as the H+B type and the above response procedure is applied. If the relationship is horizontal or the bot performs work independently, it is judged FAIL (reclassification into another cooperation type required).
