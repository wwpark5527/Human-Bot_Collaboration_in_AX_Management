# AH + AB (a true AX organization) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the humans among the organization's members are in an augmented (AH) state.
2. Confirm whether the bots among the organization's members are in an augmented (AB) state — look at whether the common and governance contexts and AI governance are built in, rather than its being a mere automation tool.
3. If both human and bot are augmented, determine it to be the AH + AB type.
4. Confirm whether the bot is an execution tool or a joint-judgment subject, and thereby finally distinguish it from AH + B.
5. Examine and carry out the proper use of an organizational AX OS and the realization of HBRM.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS as the AH + AB type when both human and bot are augmented. If the bot is not augmented, it is reclassified as AH + B.
