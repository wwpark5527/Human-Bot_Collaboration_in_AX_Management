# H + AH (augmented-human-centered cooperation) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the organization's member composition is human versus human (H and AH) and whether bots are not the key axis of distinction.
2. Confirm whether some of the members are physically and mentally augmented (AH).
3. Confirm whether the remaining portion is not augmented (H).
4. If all the above conditions are satisfied, judge it as the H + AH type, and check whether the stratification of AI competence has occurred.
5. Carry out AI democratization, building an AI collaboration culture, and role redesign.

## Criteria
When the composition axis is human versus human and only some of them are augmented, a PASS judgment is made as the H + AH type. If bots are the key axis of distinction it is reclassified as H+B or AH+B, and if both humans and bots are augmented it is reclassified as AH+AB.
