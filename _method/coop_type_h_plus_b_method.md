# H + B (Tool-Type Cooperation) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether both non-augmented humans (H) and bots (B) exist in the organization's member composition.
2. Confirm whether the bot remains in the position of a tool (a means of improving productivity) and is not an entity that replaces the human or judges jointly with the human.
3. If both of the above conditions are satisfied, determine it to be the H + B (tool-type cooperation) type.
4. Apply the realization methods of this type by executing the dissemination of personal productivity AI, prompt literacy education, and automation of repetitive work.

## Criteria
It is determined PASS as the H + B type when the member composition is a non-augmented human and a bot, the bot remains in the position of a tool, and responsibility is concentrated on the human. If the human is augmented it is reclassified as H + AH, and if the bot goes beyond a tool and becomes an object of orchestration it is reclassified as AH + B.
