# Co-constitution of roles (Co-constitution) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. The human performs the vision-setting (direction and goals) contribution.
2. The bot performs the real-time strategy simulation contribution.
3. Integrate the two contributions by way of combination rather than mutual replacement.
4. Confirm whether the result of the integration has been formed into a single hybridized role (a strategic shaper role).
5. Reflect the formed role in the team's role system.

## Criteria
If the human's vision-setting and the bot's real-time strategy simulation combine and a strategic shaper role is produced it is judged PASS, and if the two contributions remain separate without combining it is judged FAIL.
