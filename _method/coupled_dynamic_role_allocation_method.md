# Dynamic role allocation (Dynamic Role Allocation) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect workload data per team member in real time.
2. Analyze the cognitive load and stress signals of each team member in real time.
3. Confirm the expertise availability of each team member.
4. Synthesize the four analysis results and produce a role-reallocation plan.
5. Apply the reallocation plan to the team.

## Criteria
If workload, cognitive load, stress, and expertise availability are all analyzed in real time and role-reallocation results grounded in them are produced it is judged PASS, and if roles are fixed without analysis it is judged FAIL.
