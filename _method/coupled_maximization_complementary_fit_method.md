# Maximization of human-bot complementary fit (Maximization of Complementary Fit) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the human strengths (empathy, value judgment, context understanding, ethics, creative meaning-making).
2. Confirm the bot strengths (speed, computation, memory, search, pattern analysis).
3. Find the points at which the two sets of strengths combine by way of complementarity rather than mutual replacement.
4. Define a new role capability through the combination.
5. Reflect the generated role capability in the team's role system.

## Criteria
If human strengths and bot strengths are combined and a new role capability is generated it is judged PASS, and if the two sets of strengths are not combined and remain a mere parallel arrangement it is judged FAIL.
