# Critical Thinking — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Do not accept the results produced by AI immediately, but designate them as objects of verification.
2. Search for whether counterexamples to those results exist.
3. Check the logical development of the results step by step and confirm whether there are leaps or contradictions.
4. Synthesize the results of verification, counterexample search, and logic checking to assess reliability and decide whether to adopt them.

## Criteria
If verification, counterexample search, and logic checking have actually been performed on the AI outputs and plausible errors have been filtered out, it is judged PASS (a person who verifies AI well). If the AI outputs were accepted as they were without verification, it is judged FAIL (a person who believes AI well, re-checking needed).
