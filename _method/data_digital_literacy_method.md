# Data and Digital Literacy — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Identify the data handled in the target work and confirm whether its meaning can be interpreted.
2. Check whether the stages of the digital workflow to which that work has moved are understood.
3. Confirm whether the results produced by AI can be verified by collating them against the original data and logic.
4. If there is a weak area among interpretation, understanding, and verification, supplement it with learning and re-check.

## Criteria
If one can interpret and verify, on one's own, work that has moved onto data-based, AI-based, and digital-based foundations, it is judged PASS (data and digital literacy secured). If interpretation or verification is impossible, it is judged FAIL (supplementary learning needed).
