# DX (Digital Transformation) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Identify the areas of the organization's work and systems that have not been digitized.
2. Design the method of storing and cloud-migrating data and raise the level of datafication.
3. Select repetitive processes as targets for automation and automate them.
4. Raise efficiency through the adoption of new systems, and measure the results.

## Criteria
Execution of the method is determined to be complete when steps 1 through 4 have been carried out and the digitization, datafication, cloud migration, and process efficiency of work and systems have been achieved to a measurable level.
