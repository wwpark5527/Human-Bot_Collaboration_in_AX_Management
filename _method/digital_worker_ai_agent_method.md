# AI agent — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the autonomy level of the target digital worker.
2. Confirm whether it can set goals on its own (even partially).
3. Confirm in turn whether the role scope is comprehensive and complex, whether decision-making is possible, whether it collaborates with humans and other agents, and whether memory/context retention is strong.
4. If the top value is confirmed on all six axes, determine it to be an AI agent.

## Criteria
It is determined PASS as an AI agent when all six axes are satisfied: autonomy high, goal setting partially possible, role scope comprehensive and complex, decision-making possible, collaboration possible, and memory/context retention strong. If even one falls short, it is reclassified as a Subagent or a Bot.
