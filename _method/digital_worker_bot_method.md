# Bot — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the autonomy level of the target digital worker (high/intermediate/low).
2. Confirm whether goal setting is almost absent and whether the role scope is simple and repetitive.
3. Confirm whether decision-making is rule-based centered, whether collaboration is limited, and whether memory/context retention is weak.
4. If the lowest value is confirmed on all six axes, determine it to be a Bot.

## Criteria
It is determined PASS as a Bot when all six axes are satisfied: autonomy low, goal setting almost none, role scope simple and repetitive, decision-making rule-based centered, collaboration limited, and memory/context retention weak. If even one exceeds this, it is reclassified as a Subagent or an AI agent.
