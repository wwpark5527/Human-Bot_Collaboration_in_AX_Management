# Subagent — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the autonomy level of the target digital worker (high/intermediate/low).
2. Confirm whether it sets goals on its own, is assigned them by a higher agent, or has almost no goal setting.
3. Confirm whether the role scope is limited and specialized, whether decision-making is possible in a limited way, whether collaboration is limited to internal agent collaboration, and whether memory/context retention is partial.
4. If the combination of intermediate autonomy + goals assigned by a higher agent + limited and specialized role scope is confirmed, determine it to be a Subagent.

## Criteria
It is determined PASS as a Subagent when autonomy is intermediate, goals are assigned by a higher agent, the role scope is limited and specialized, decision-making is possible in a limited way, collaboration is internal agent collaboration, and memory/context retention is partial. If it sets goals on its own it is reclassified as an AI agent, and if it is rule-based without specialized judgment it is reclassified as a Bot.
