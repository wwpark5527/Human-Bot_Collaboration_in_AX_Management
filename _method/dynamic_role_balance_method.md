# Dynamic role balance — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Continuously monitor the organization's situation and detect situation types such as crisis, innovation, and operations.
2. Predefine, for each situation type, the combination of human roles (for example, shaper SH, creator PL, implementer IMP) and AI support elements (AI risk agent, Creative AI, Workflow bot) that corresponds to it.
3. When a situation is detected, activate and reallocate the corresponding role combination in real time.
4. Readjust the role assignment when the situation ends or changes.

## Criteria
If the role combination is reallocated in real time in response to changes in the situation and the results are recorded it is judged PASS, and if roles remain fixed even when the situation changes it is judged FAIL.
