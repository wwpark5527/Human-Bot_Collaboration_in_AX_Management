# Efficiency-centered AX — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect the organization's AX goals and KPIs and determine whether they are centered on productivity, cost reduction, and speed.
2. Check each of the six items: reduction of human roles, widening of AI-competence gaps, abuse of algorithmic management, weakening of judgment authority, absence of the right to object, and performance concentration.
3. Apply the comparison table of efficiency-centered AX and inclusive-transformation AX (core aim/position of the human/organizational concern/performance criteria/principal risk/connection to ESG) to diagnose the organization's current position.
4. On the basis of the diagnostic results, derive the necessity and priorities of redesign toward inclusive-transformation AX.

## Criteria
If all six risk items are identified and documented and a diagnosis against inclusive-transformation AX is carried out it is judged PASS, and if AX continues on efficiency indicators alone without the risks being identified it is judged FAIL.
