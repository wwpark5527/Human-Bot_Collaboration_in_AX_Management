# Extension of E (Sustainability of AI Infrastructure) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Measure the electricity consumption of AI training, inference, and data-center operations and analyze the burden on the power grid.
2. Measure the water use arising from data-center cooling and operations and its impact on local water resources.
3. Calculate carbon emissions on the basis of electricity use and the energy mix, and establish a plan for the transition to renewable energy.
4. Assess the resource risks of AI chip production and of the supply chains for key minerals.
5. Manage the volume of e-waste generated according to the replacement cycles of servers, chips, and equipment.
6. Assess the impact that data-center siting, power grids, cooling water, and land use have on the local environment and local communities.
7. Integrate the measurement results of the above six items into the existing E-axis (environment, energy, carbon, resources, pollution) reporting system.

## Criteria
If all six items — electricity, water, carbon, semiconductors/minerals, e-waste, and local environmental impact — are measured and managed as the company's environmental responsibility (the E axis) it is judged PASS, and if one or more items are omitted or not integrated into the management system it is judged FAIL.
