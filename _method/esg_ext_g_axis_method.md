# Extension of G (Accountability Structure of AI Power) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Clearly define the system of roles, authority, and responsibility that approves the use of AI.
2. Build a log management system that records the contribution and judgment of AI.
3. Prepare approval structures and review procedures for AI output artifacts.
4. Secure the explainability of AI judgment and establish criteria for accountability attribution.
5. Prepare an AI-related risk management system and objection procedures.
6. Establish a policy-based control structure for AI operation.
7. Define principles for managing AI-related intellectual property, disclosure criteria, and conflicts of interest, and integrate all of the above into the existing governance system (board, control, audit, risk, compliance).

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when roles, authority, and responsibility; records and logs; approval and review; explainability and accountability attribution; risk management and objection; and policy control structures are all operated within the governance system, and FAIL when one or more are missing so that the functioning and judgment of AI cannot be operated responsibly.
