# Extension of S (AI Capability Equality and Human Dignity) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Investigate how AI changes workers' roles (replacement / complementation / redeployment / upskilling).
2. Prepare explainability and appeal procedures for AI outcomes.
3. Where AI is used for evaluation and placement, prepare an algorithmic management system.
4. Check whether education, retraining, and accessibility for the capability to use AI are provided equally to all members.
5. Check whether AI reinforces discrimination through data bias.
6. Confirm whether human oversight and responsibility (the right of judgment) are maintained in the final judgment.
7. Institutionalize the eight rights of AI access right, right to learn, right to use, right of judgment, right to explanation, right of appeal, right to transition, and right to benefit sharing.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the eight rights (access right, right to learn, right to use, right of judgment, right to explanation, right of appeal, right to transition, right to benefit sharing) are actually guaranteed to members and the social quality of an organization in which humans and AI work together is managed, and FAIL when one or more rights are not guaranteed or AI is used only in a way that replaces, surveils, and excludes humans.
