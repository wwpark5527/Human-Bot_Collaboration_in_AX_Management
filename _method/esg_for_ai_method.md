# ESG for AI — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Check the current state of AI use within the organization and investigate the points where negative effects (misuse, bias, infringement, etc.) can occur.
2. Assess whether AI use induces ethical problems (human rights, the right of judgment, fairness, etc.).
3. Establish control criteria for approving, verifying, and auditing AI use from the ESG (environmental, social, governance) standpoint.
4. Review related frameworks such as the RAI evaluation model and judge their applicability.
5. Integrate the AI control criteria into the organization's ESG governance system and operate them.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the negative effects and ethical risks caused by AI are identified and control criteria and procedures from the ESG standpoint are explicitly established, and FAIL when there is no risk identification or no establishment of control criteria.
