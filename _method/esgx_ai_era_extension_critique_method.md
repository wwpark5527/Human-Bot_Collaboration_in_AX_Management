# ESG AI-era extension theory — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Organize the scope of responsibility reflected by the organization's current ESG system (environment, society, governance; industrial-era criteria).
2. List the problems newly emerging within the organization due to the introduction of AI (AI stratification, algorithmic management, infringement of human judgment authority, etc.).
3. Judge one by one whether each new problem is dealt with within the existing ESG framework.
4. Redefine the problems that are not dealt with into an extended ESG scope that includes the AI operating environment.
5. Organize the grounds for adopting an extended ESG (AI inclusive-transformation ESG) and present them to the organization.

## Criteria
If it is confirmed that the new problems of the AI era are omitted from the existing ESG framework and the grounds for shifting to an extended ESG are explicitly presented it is judged PASS, and if the new problems are judged to be sufficiently dealt with within the existing framework it is judged FAIL.
