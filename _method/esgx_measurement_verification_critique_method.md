# Measurement-verification-strengthening theory — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect the score calculation criteria and results of the multiple ESG rating agencies to which the organization is exposed.
2. Analyze the causes of score differences between rating agencies from the perspective of the non-unification of criteria.
3. Check whether the organization's ESG data disclosure items are selective (centered on favorable indicators).
4. Review candidate unified evaluation and disclosure criteria and judge their applicability.
5. Design measures for strengthening data verification procedures, such as third-party verification.

## Criteria
If the causes of score discrepancies between rating agencies and bias in data disclosure are clearly identified and a direction for unified criteria and strengthened verification is presented it is judged PASS, and if there is no clarification of causes or presentation of direction it is judged FAIL.
