# Stakeholder-expansion theory — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect the materiality judgment method used in the organization's current ESG evaluation and disclosure criteria.
2. Analyze whether non-financial issues such as climate change and human-rights issues are evaluated only after being converted into financial risk.
3. Compare the impact materiality concept required by the EU CSRD with the current criteria and identify the gaps.
4. Derive an improvement direction that broadens the scope of evaluation from an investor focus to a variety of stakeholders.
5. Organize and report the gap between financial materiality and impact materiality and the direction of stakeholder expansion.

## Criteria
If whether a shift from financial materiality to impact materiality is needed and the direction of stakeholder expansion are explicitly identified it is judged PASS, and if it still remains only within the company financial-risk perspective it is judged FAIL.
