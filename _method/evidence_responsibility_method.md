# Evidence Responsibility — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Record the materials used in the AI work and the instructions given to AI.
2. Preserve the output artifacts AI produced exactly as they are.
3. Record the revisions made by humans separately from the output artifacts.
4. Leave the verification criteria and approval records.
5. Organize and store the error records and the improvements reflected in a traceable form.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS (evidence responsibility fulfilled) when the materials used, the instructions given to AI, the results AI produced, the revisions made by humans, the verification criteria, the approval records, the error records, and the improvements reflected all remain, so that the course of judgment can be retraced. If even one is missing, it is judged FAIL (evidence records need supplementing).
