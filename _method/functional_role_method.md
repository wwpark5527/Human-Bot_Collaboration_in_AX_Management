# Functional Role — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the list of formal jobs within the team (recruiting officer, evaluation officer, payroll officer, etc.).
2. Confirm whether each job holder was formally assigned that role by the organization or a superior.
3. Determine whether that role is directly job-related.
4. When both conditions of formality and job-relatedness are met, confirm it as a functional role.
5. Register the confirmed functional roles in contrast with team roles, which are informal, non-job-related behavior types.
6. Deliver the list of functional roles as the contrast axis for team role balance (TRB) diagnosis.

## Criteria
Was it formally assigned by the organization or a superior + is it job-related — when both conditions are met it is PASS (determined to be a functional role), and when even one is unmet it is FAIL (reclassified as a team role or another role).
