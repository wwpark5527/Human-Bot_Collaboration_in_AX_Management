# AI Access Gap — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Investigate the current accessibility of high-performance AI models and infrastructure (compute, platforms).
2. Analyze the structure of access costs (subscription fees, token costs) and identify the actors who are excluded.
3. Design support measures for resolving the inequality of access (budget, shared infrastructure, etc.).
4. Verify the results of securing accessibility.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when high-performance AI and infrastructure are actually accessible, and FAIL (an access gap exists) when access is blocked by cost, authority, and the like.
