# AI Context Gap — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Explicitly document the organization's purpose, criteria, language, way of working, output formats, and verification criteria.
2. Expand across the organization the personnel and systems (context designers) able to provide these to AI.
3. Check the structure of ownership, access, verification, and outcome attribution of context capital.
4. Convert context capital into a common production base accessible to the whole organization and verify the results.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the capability to provide the organization's purpose and criteria to AI is distributed across the organization, and FAIL (an AI context gap exists) when it is concentrated in a few.
