# AI Right-to-Judge Gap — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Grasp the scope and influence of the results AI produces.
2. Explicitly assign the authority to approve, revise, or reject the results.
3. Check whether humans without authority remain only as objects of AI decisions.
4. Record and verify the results of exercising the right of judgment (the history of approvals/revisions/rejections).

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the authority to approve, revise, or reject AI results is clearly assigned and exercised, and FAIL when humans remain, without authority, only as objects of AI decisions.
