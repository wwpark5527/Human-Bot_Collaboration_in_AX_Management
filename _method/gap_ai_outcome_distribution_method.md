# AI Outcome-Distribution Gap — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Measure the productivity indicators before and after AI adoption (work throughput, output quality, time required, etc.).
2. Trace which stratum within the organization the productivity gains accrue to (through compensation, promotion, budget allocation, etc.).
3. Assess whether there is concentration at the top and exclusion at the bottom according to the determination criteria.
4. When a skew is confirmed, establish and apply benefit sharing and redistribution measures.
5. Periodically re-check changes in the distribution structure.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the accrual of the AI productivity gains is grasped transparently and redistribution measures are prepared in case of a skew, and FAIL when the accrual is opaque or concentration at the top and exclusion at the bottom are left unaddressed.
