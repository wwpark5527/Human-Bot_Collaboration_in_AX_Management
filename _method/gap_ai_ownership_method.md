# AI Ownership Gap — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. List the owning and operating actors of the AI models, data, and platforms the organization uses.
2. Grasp the revenue flows arising from each actor (subscription fees, token fees, knowledge-capitalization profits).
3. Analyze whether the revenue ownership structure is skewed toward particular actors (big tech, the organization's upper layer).
4. When a skew is confirmed, establish and apply profit sharing and redistribution measures.
5. Periodically re-check changes in the distribution structure.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the ownership structure of the revenue from models, data, and platforms is grasped and redistribution measures are prepared in case of a skew, and FAIL when the ownership structure is opaque or the skew is left unaddressed.
