# AI Surveillance Gap — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Identify respectively the actors who use AI as a management and surveillance tool and the members who become their object.
2. List the areas to which algorithmic management is applied (work assignment, evaluation, surveillance, scheduling, compensation, discipline, productivity measurement).
3. Disclose to the members under management the determination criteria and data sources for each area.
4. Prepare and operate a procedure by which those under management can appeal the surveillance and evaluation results.
5. Periodically check and record the degree of authority asymmetry between the managing actors and their objects.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the areas and criteria of applied management and surveillance are disclosed and the appeal procedure is actually operated, and FAIL when the criteria are undisclosed or the objects are only surveilled and controlled one-sidedly.
