# Governance & Ethical Judgment — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Check the current state of trust, explainability, accountability, security, and provenance in the areas of AI use.
2. Identify the deficient parts for each item and establish governance criteria.
3. Verify whether AI is in a controllable state beyond the level of being technically understood.
4. On the basis of controllability, design trust and convey it to the organization.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS (governance and ethical judgment secured) when, not remaining at the level of technical understanding, one has designed trust by making AI controllable. When one remains only at technical understanding, it is judged FAIL (the governance system needs redesign).
