# Accountability — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Identify the scope in which the results generated by AI are used in actual work.
2. For each scope of use, designate the person responsible for the final judgment and use.
3. Define the criteria of judgment the responsible person must follow (on what grounds final use is approved or rejected).
4. Confirm whether the defined accountability system meets the requirements of ISO/IEC 42001 top-management responsibility, NIST Govern, and the provider and deployer obligations of the EU AI Act.
5. Register the accountability system as the accountability component of the governance context and apply it to actual operation.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when who bears the responsibility for the final use and judgment is clearly defined, and FAIL when the locus of accountability is unclear so that it cannot be judged how far AI results may be used.
