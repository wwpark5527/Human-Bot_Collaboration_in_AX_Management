# Approval — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Identify the types of output artifacts AI produces.
2. For each type, define whether human approval is required and, if so, who approves and by what criteria.
3. Confirm whether the defined approval procedure meets the requirements of ISO/IEC 42001 operational control procedures, NIST Manage, and EU AI Act human oversight (Article 14).
4. Register the approval procedure as the approval component of the governance context and apply it to actual operation.
5. Check whether there are cases in which output artifacts are used without human approval (missing approval).

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when which output artifacts must be used only after human approval is clearly defined and is observed in actual operation, and FAIL when missing approval occurs.
