# Record — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Identify the history items (prompts, materials, results, revisions, approvals) that arise in the course of AI operation.
2. Define, per item, the location (log, repository, document) and the way in which the record is to be kept.
3. Confirm whether the defined record system satisfies the requirements of ISO/IEC 42001 documentation requirements, NIST Govern/Measure, and EU AI Act automatic logging and record-keeping (Article 12).
4. Register the record system as the record component of the governance context and apply it in actual operation.
5. Check whether the history is not scattered and whether tracing and auditing are possible.

## Criteria
PASS is determined when where the history of instructions, materials, results, revisions, and approvals is kept is clearly defined and traceability and auditability are secured; FAIL is determined when the history is scattered and tracing is impossible.
