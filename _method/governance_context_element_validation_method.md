# Validation — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Identify the types of results AI produces.
2. Organize the items to be confirmed per type (factuality, logic, quality, policy conformance) and create a checklist.
3. Confirm whether the checklist satisfies the requirements of ISO/IEC 42001 performance evaluation and monitoring, NIST Measure, and EU AI Act accuracy and robustness (Article 15).
4. Register the checklist as the validation component of the governance context and apply it to actual results.
5. Check that no result is used without having gone through validation.

## Criteria
PASS is determined when which results must be confirmed by which criteria is clearly defined and factuality, logic, quality, and policy conformance are confirmed; FAIL is determined when results are used without validation.
