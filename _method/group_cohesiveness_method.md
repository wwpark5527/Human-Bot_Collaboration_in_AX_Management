# Group Cohesiveness — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Fix the group or team to be measured.
2. Apply a measurement tool that asks about the degree of psychological attraction among members to like, cohere, and help one another.
3. Collect the responses and produce them as the teamwork indicator for the human-bond side.
4. Place it alongside the task-activation side indicator measured by TRB and contrast them.
5. Combine the two indicators to judge the position of that group or team within the framing of the two sides of teamwork.

## Criteria
PASS (valid group cohesiveness measurement) is decided by whether the measurement takes as its object the degree of psychological attraction among members (liking, cohering, helping). FAIL is determined when the measurement takes as its object the degree to which the nine team roles are exercised or task activation (confusion with TRB).
