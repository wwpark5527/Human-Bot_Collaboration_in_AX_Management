# AH1 Augmented Human — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Record the level of a member's thinking ability and learning volume before AI use (the baseline).
2. Record the level of the member's thinking ability and learning volume after AI use.
3. Compare the two levels and confirm whether thinking ability and learning volume have actually increased.
4. Determine whether the AH1 stage applies on the grounds of the confirmed increase.

## Criteria
PASS (the AH1 stage applies) is determined when thinking ability and learning volume have both increased after AI use; FAIL (the AH1 stage does not apply, and reassessment at the H2 stage is required) is determined when no increase is confirmed.
