# AH2 AI Orchestrator — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect the list of AI agents the member is using and the frequency of use of each agent.
2. Distinguish, for each AI agent, whether the member is a mere user or a director who allocates and coordinates work.
3. Compare the scope of roles the member can perform before and after the use/direction of AI agents.
4. Determine whether the AH2 stage applies on the grounds of whether roles have actually expanded through directing multiple AI agents.

## Criteria
PASS (the AH2 stage applies) is determined when the member uses and directs multiple AI agents and the resulting role expansion is confirmed; FAIL (the AH2 stage does not apply, and reassessment at the AH1 stage is required) is determined when the member stops at the use of a single AI or no role expansion is confirmed.
