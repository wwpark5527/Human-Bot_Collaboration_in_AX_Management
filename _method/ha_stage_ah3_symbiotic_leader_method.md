# AH3 Human-AI Symbiotic Leader — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Observe whether the mode of collaboration between the member and AI stops at individual direction (the AH2 level) or operates as a single integrated system.
2. Collect cases in which that integrated system performs leadership functions in the organization's actual decision-making and operation.
3. Confirm, on the grounds of the observations and cases, whether human and AI operate as a single system.
4. Determine whether the AH3 stage applies on the grounds of the confirmation result.

## Criteria
PASS (the AH3 stage applies) is determined when human and AI operate as a single system and even perform leadership functions; FAIL (the AH3 stage does not apply, and reassessment at the AH2 stage is required) is determined when they remain at the level of individual direction.
