# H0 Non-Augmented Human — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether AI is used, from the work performance records of the member to be measured.
2. Confirm whether, even where AI has been used, its frequency and degree of involvement are at a virtually absent level.
3. Calculate the proportion of work overall in which there is no AI involvement.
4. Determine whether the H0 stage applies on the grounds of the calculation result.

## Criteria
PASS (the H0 stage applies) is determined when AI is barely used and there is virtually no AI involvement in work performance; FAIL (the H0 stage does not apply, and reassessment at a higher stage is required) is determined when AI use is confirmed.
