# H1 AI User — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the type of use (questions and searches versus joint work and the like) from the AI use records of the member to be measured.
2. Distinguish whether the results obtained from AI are at the level of simply obtaining results or at a collaborative level with role division.
3. Synthesize the type of use and the form of result utilization and judge whether it is at the question/search level.
4. Determine whether the H1 stage applies on the grounds of the judgment result.

## Criteria
PASS (the H1 stage applies) is determined when AI use remains at the level of questions and searches and stops at simply obtaining results; FAIL (the H1 stage does not apply, and reassessment at a higher stage is required) is determined when use beyond that, such as joint work, is confirmed.
