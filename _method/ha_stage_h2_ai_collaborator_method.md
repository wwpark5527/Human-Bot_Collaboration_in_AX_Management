# H2 AI Collaborator — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the mode of interaction with AI from the work performance records of the member to be measured.
2. Confirm whether that mode goes beyond the level of simple questions and searches into the form of joint work (role division, joint production of deliverables, and so on).
3. Collect the deliverables actually produced through joint work with AI.
4. Determine whether the H2 stage applies on the grounds of the confirmed mode of joint work and its deliverables.

## Criteria
PASS (the H2 stage applies) is determined when the member performs joint work with AI and its products are confirmed; FAIL (the H2 stage does not apply, and reassessment at a lower stage is required) is determined when the member remains at the level of questions and searches.
