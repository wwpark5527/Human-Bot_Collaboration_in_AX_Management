# Essence — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the subject of the observed state of tension is a human or a bot.
2. Confirm whether that tension originates in the psychological/emotional domain, such as anxiety, relationships, or identity.
3. Confirm whether that tension originates in the functional/computational domain, such as overload, misalignment, or goal conflict.
4. Apply the human-stress (psychological/emotional) or bot-stress (functional/computational) response system according to the determination result.

## Criteria
When the source of the tension is confirmed to be the psychological/emotional domain, PASS is determined as the essence of human stress; when it is confirmed to be the functional/computational domain, PASS is determined as the essence of bot stress. When neither domain is confirmed or they are mixed so that distinction is impossible, FAIL (reclassification required) is determined.
