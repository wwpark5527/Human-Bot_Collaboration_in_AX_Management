# Measurement — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the agent under observation is a human or a bot.
2. If human, measure emotional and behavioral indicators (changes in affect, changes in behavioral patterns, and so on).
3. If a bot, measure the indicators of Latency, Accuracy, and Stability.
4. On the basis of the measured indicators, re-confirm the validity of the judgments of essence, root cause, result, recovery mode, and risk.

## Criteria
If emotional and behavioral indicators are actually measured for a human agent, it is judged PASS (human stress measurement complete), and if response speed, accuracy, and stability indicators are actually measured for a bot agent, it is judged PASS (bot stress measurement complete). If the indicators cannot be collected, it is judged FAIL (measurement impossible, re-inspection of the observation system required).
