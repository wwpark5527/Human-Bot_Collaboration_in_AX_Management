# Recovery Method — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the agent in a state of stress requiring recovery is a human or a bot.
2. If human, select the prescription suited to the situation among rest, empathy, and motivation.
3. If a bot, select the prescription suited to the situation between readjustment (realignment) and relearning.
4. Apply the selected prescription and confirm whether the stress indicators improve.

## Criteria
If the human's stress indicators improve after applying a rest, empathy, or motivation prescription, it is judged PASS (human stress recovery), and if the bot's stress indicators improve after applying a readjustment or relearning prescription, it is judged PASS (bot stress recovery). If the indicators do not improve even after the prescription is applied, it is judged FAIL (review of the prescription required).
