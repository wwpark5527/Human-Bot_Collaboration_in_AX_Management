# Root Cause — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the agent showing signs of stress is a human or a bot.
2. If human, confirm the grounds for which of anxiety, relationships, or identity is the cause.
3. If a bot, confirm the grounds for which of Overload, Conflict, or Misalignment is the cause.
4. Record the confirmed family of cause as the value of the root cause axis, and connect the corresponding prescription system to it.

## Criteria
If the cause is confirmed as one of anxiety, relationships, or identity, it is judged PASS as a human stress cause, and if it is confirmed as one of overload, conflict, or misalignment, it is judged PASS as a bot stress cause. If the cause is not confirmed or the two families are intermixed, it is judged FAIL (reinvestigation of the cause required).
