# meaning — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Define the meaning of HBRM by reviewing the role HBRM performs in the AX organization and its scope of management.
2. Define the meaning of the augmented human (AH) with reference to the three dimensions of augmentation (physical, mental, and role and context augmentation).
3. Define the meaning of the augmented bot (AB) with reference to how the augmented bot develops through the common and governance contexts.
4. Integrate the three definitions into a single meaning perspective and settle the preparation for moving on to the measurement discussion.

## Criteria
PASS (the meaning perspective holds) is determined when the content being treated defines the definition and role of the subject (HBRM, the augmented human, the augmented bot); FAIL (it belongs to another M) is determined when it concerns methods of execution or numerical indicators.
