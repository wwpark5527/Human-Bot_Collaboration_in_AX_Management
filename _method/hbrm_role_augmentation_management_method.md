# Augmentation Management — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Find the points where augmentation of humans and bots is needed, using the deliverable of the collaboration-structure design stage (the collaboration flow diagram).
2. Design learning and collaboration opportunities through which humans (H) can grow into AH through collaboration with AI.
3. Establish a plan for developing bots (B) into AB by applying the common context and the governance context.
4. Periodically review and record the progress of the H→AH and B→AB transitions.
5. Pass the progress record on as input to the subsequent role-balance management stage.

## Criteria
If the record confirms that the H→AH and B→AB transitions are progressing as planned, it is judged PASS (augmentation management in progress/complete); if there is no transition-support plan or no progress is confirmed, it is judged FAIL (augmentation management insufficient).
