# Collaboration-Structure Design — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Check the deliverables of the role-design stage (the role assignment table by type, the responsibility-boundary rules).
2. Arrange, along a time sequence, the order in which humans and bots are involved for each unit of work.
3. Identify the work-handover points between humans and bots, and decide the verification method for each point.
4. Review and adjust the points in the collaboration flow where bottlenecks or conflicts may occur.
5. Document the completed collaboration flow and pass it on as input to the augmentation management stage.

## Criteria
If the human-bot collaboration flow has been designed in a document including handover points and verification methods, it is judged PASS (collaboration-structure design complete); if the flow is not defined or the handover and verification points are unclear, it is judged FAIL (collaboration-structure design incomplete).
