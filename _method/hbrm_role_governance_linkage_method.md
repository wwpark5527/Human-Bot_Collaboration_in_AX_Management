# Governance Linkage — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Check the deliverable of the trust-management stage (the collaboration standards that prevent overtrust and distrust).
2. Designate the authority holder who will execute the collaboration standards (who decides).
3. Decide the procedure for verifying results and the verifying party (who verifies).
4. Decide the procedure for approving verified results and the approving party (who approves).
5. Decide the method of recording the entire authority, verification, and approval process, and specify the locus of ultimate responsibility, thereby connecting the five elements into a single structure.

## Criteria
If all five elements of authority, verification, approval, recording, and responsibility structure are defined and connected to one another without a break, it is judged PASS (governance linkage complete); if one or more elements are missing or the connection is broken, it is judged FAIL (governance linkage incomplete).
