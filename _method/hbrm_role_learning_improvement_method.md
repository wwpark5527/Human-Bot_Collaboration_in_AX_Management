# Learning and Improvement Management — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Check the deliverable of the governance linkage stage (the connected authority, verification, approval, recording, and responsibility structures).
2. Collect, from both the human and the bot side, the feedback (what went well) and the errors (what went wrong) that arose in the actual operation process.
3. Accumulate the collected feedback and errors as records (organizational memory) that the organization can reuse.
4. Re-diagnose by mapping the accumulated organizational memory onto the five problem types of role-balance imbalance.
5. Feed the re-diagnosis results back into the member definition and role design stages of the next HBRM operating cycle.

## Criteria
If feedback and errors are accumulated into organizational memory, it is judged PASS (learning and improvement management complete); if they are not accumulated and are lost, it is judged FAIL (learning and improvement management incomplete).
