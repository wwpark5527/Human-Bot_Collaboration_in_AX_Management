# Member Definition — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Review the organization's current scope of members and confirm whether, besides humans, bots are actually performing work.
2. Classify members into the four types H (non-augmented human), AH (augmented human), B (ordinary bot), and AB (augmented bot).
3. Decide the position each type will occupy within the organization (on a role basis, not a job basis).
4. Stipulate and document, at a first level, the role and responsibility boundaries of each type.
5. Connect the definition results to the subsequent HBRM role-design stage (the setting of roles and responsibility boundaries).

## Criteria
If the position and role within the organization are explicitly defined for all four member types H, AH, B, and AB, it is judged PASS (member definition complete); if one or more types are undefined or their position and role are unclear, it is judged FAIL (member definition incomplete).
