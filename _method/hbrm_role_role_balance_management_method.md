# Role-Balance Management — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Survey the current state of role allocation in the three dimensions human↔human, human↔bot, and bot↔bot.
2. Check whether any of the five imbalance types applies: excess of technical roles, excess of automation roles, lack of verification roles, lack of coordination roles, and unclear responsibility roles.
3. Establish concrete adjustment measures for each discovered concentration, deficiency, and conflict (for example, adding a verification step when verification roles are lacking, or designating a responsible party when responsibility roles are unclear).
4. Execute the adjustment measures and document the results.
5. Pass the adjustment results on as input to the subsequent trust-management stage.

## Criteria
If the role balance of the three dimensions has been diagnosed and adjustment measures for the discovered imbalances are confirmed in a document, it is judged PASS (role-balance management complete); if no diagnosis has been made or the imbalance is left unattended, it is judged FAIL (role-balance management insufficient).
