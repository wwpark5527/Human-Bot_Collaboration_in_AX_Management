# Role Design — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Check the deliverable of the member-definition stage (the definition of the positions and roles of H/AH/B/AB).
2. List the concrete roles each type will carry out within the organization.
3. Set the responsibility boundaries between humans and bots for each role (for example, who executes and who verifies and finally approves).
4. Document the established role and responsibility boundaries.
5. Pass the documented role and responsibility boundaries on as input to the collaboration-structure design stage.

## Criteria
If the role and responsibility boundaries between humans and bots are explicitly stated in a document, it is judged PASS (role design complete); if the boundaries are unclear or not documented, it is judged FAIL (role design incomplete).
