# Trust Management — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Identify the human-bot collaboration points from the deliverable of the role-balance management stage (the record of adjusted role concentration, deficiency, and conflict).
2. Check the risk of overtrust in a bot's results (uncritical acceptance) at each collaboration point.
3. Check the risk of excessive distrust in a bot's results (impeded collaboration) at each collaboration point.
4. Establish together a verification procedure to prevent overtrust and a procedure for presenting grounds for trust to mitigate distrust.
5. Document the established collaboration standards and pass them on as input to the governance linkage stage.

## Criteria
If both the overtrust-prevention standard and the distrust-mitigation standard have been established in a document, it is judged PASS (trust management complete); if even one of the two has not been established, it is judged FAIL (trust management incomplete).
