# Baek (魄) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Check whether the target function belongs to the body, the senses, labor, or physical execution, and thereby determine whether it is the domain of baek (if it is cognitive judgment, classify it as hon; if it is dignity or agency, classify it as yeong).
2. Within the domain of baek, identify the parts that can be performed by proxy through robots, automation devices, and mechanical execution.
3. For the functions of baek delegated to automation devices, insert procedures that ensure human rest, safety, dignity, and right of judgment are not violated.
4. Examine whether the human body and labor are merely subordinated to the rhythms and rules set by the system or are maintained as the foundation of agentive action, and thereby determine whether "baeksan" is present.

## Criteria
Even if the functions of baek are performed by proxy and extended by robots and automation, it is judged PASS when human rest, safety, dignity, and right of judgment are protected. It is judged FAIL when the human body and labor are treated as a resource managed by the system and lose the initiative (baeksan).
