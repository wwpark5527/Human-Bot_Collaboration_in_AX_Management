# Hon (魂) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the target function belongs to thought, memory, judgment, creation, or decision-making and thereby determine whether it is the domain of Hon (classify it as Baek if it is physical execution, and as Yeong if it is dignity and subjecthood).
2. Identify the part of the domain of Hon that can be performed on the human's behalf by AI's analysis, reasoning, language generation, and judgment assistance.
3. Insert a procedure by which the human questions and verifies for themselves the functions of Hon delegated to AI.
4. Check whether the human merely accepts AI's output as it is or verifies and takes responsibility for themselves, and thereby judge whether 'honbi' is present.

## Criteria
If, even when the functions of Hon are assisted by AI, the procedure by which the human questions, verifies, and takes responsibility for themselves is maintained, it is judged PASS. If the human accepts AI's judgment as it is without verification and evades responsibility (honbi), it is judged FAIL.
