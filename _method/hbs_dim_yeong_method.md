# Yeong (靈) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Check whether the target decision belongs to dignity, morality, responsibility, or agency, and thereby determine whether it is the domain of yeong (if it is calculation or reasoning, classify it as hon; if it is physical execution, classify it as baek, but make the final determination by whether the function is outsourceable).
2. For decisions determined to be in the domain of yeong, do not delegate them to AI or robots and enforce a procedure of final human approval.
3. Insert a procedure in which humans ask themselves whether to accept, reject, revise, or take responsibility for the answers AI has presented or the actions robots have carried out.
4. Examine whether there are signs of humans accepting without questioning, trusting without verifying, or evading responsibility, and thereby determine whether yeong has been lost.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the final right of judgment and responsibility lie with humans in important decisions and humans do not give up the last question. It is judged FAIL (loss of yeong) when the right of judgment has in effect been transferred to AI or the system and humans give up the last question.
