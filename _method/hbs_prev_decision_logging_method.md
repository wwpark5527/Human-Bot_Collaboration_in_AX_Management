# AI's judgment process must be recorded — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Check whether AI was involved in the target judgment.
2. If it was involved, check whether the grounds of judgment are recorded.
3. Check whether the data used in the judgment, the approver, and the risks reviewed are each recorded.
4. If even one of the four items (grounds, data, approver, risks) is missing, judge the record incomplete and require it to be supplemented.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the grounds of AI's judgment, the data used, the approver, and the risks reviewed are all recorded. It is judged FAIL when even one record is missing, which means that confirming the locus of accountability becomes impossible.
