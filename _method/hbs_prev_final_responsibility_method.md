# Final responsibility must remain with humans and the organization — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Determine whether the decision under review qualifies as an "important decision" (a decision that has a significant impact on the organization and its stakeholders).
2. Check whether AI's advice, analysis, or recommendations were involved in that decision.
3. Regardless of whether AI was involved, check whether the final approval and signature were made by a designated human or organizational unit.
4. Examine whether the locus of accountability is attributed to humans and the organization in documents and records, and whether it has not been implicitly shifted to AI or the system.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the final right of judgment and responsibility are explicitly attributed to humans and the organization in important decisions. It is judged FAIL when the subject of responsibility is unclear or has in substance been transferred to AI or the system.
