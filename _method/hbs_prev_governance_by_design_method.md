# Technology adoption must be designed together with governance — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Define the functional scope of the AI or automation system scheduled for adoption.
2. Confirm whether rules on authority (who approves and executes what) for that system are included in the adoption design.
3. Confirm whether rules on responsibility (the party responsible when a problem occurs) are included.
4. Confirm whether approval (the approval procedure before adoption and change), recording (recording the grounds of judgment, the data, and the approver), auditing (the ex-post review procedure), and risk review (the prior risk assessment) are each included, and if even one of the six elements is missing, judge the governance to be deficient.

## Criteria
If all six elements of authority, responsibility, approval, recording, auditing, and risk review are included in the adoption design, it is judged PASS. If even one is missing, it is judged FAIL (a function-centered adoption with deficient governance).
