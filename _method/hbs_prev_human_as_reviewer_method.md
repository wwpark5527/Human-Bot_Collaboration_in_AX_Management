# Humans must be reviewers of AI output, not consumers — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Identify the points in the target work where AI output arises.
2. Confirm whether there is a step in which the human interprets that output (grasps what it means).
3. Confirm whether there is a step in which the human verifies it (checks the grounds and the accuracy).
4. Confirm whether there is a step in which the human adjusts it when necessary (corrects and supplements it), and make an overall judgment on whether all three steps are present.

## Criteria
If all three steps of interpretation, verification, and adjustment are present, it is judged PASS (reviewer). If AI output is adopted as it is without the three steps, it is judged FAIL (consumer).
