# Automation must not reduce humans to components — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Grasp the way in which the target automation or robot system raises efficiency (platform labor, surveillance systems, production algorithms, data-based deployment, and so on).
2. Confirm whether that system infringes on human rest time.
3. Confirm whether that system infringes on human safety.
4. Confirm whether that system infringes on human dignity and right of judgment, and if even one of the four items is infringed, judge it a risk of componentization (baeksan).

## Criteria
If automation raises efficiency while human rest, safety, dignity, and right of judgment are all protected, it is judged PASS. If even one of the four items is infringed, it is judged FAIL (componentization of the human, baeksan).
