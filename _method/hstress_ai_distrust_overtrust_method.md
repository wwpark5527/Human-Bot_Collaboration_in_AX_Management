# Stress from AI Distrust or Overtrust — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether distrust signals of the kind "what if the AI is wrong? what about hallucination? what about manipulated data?" appear in the expression of stress.
2. Confirm whether overtrust signals of the kind "the AI recommended it, so it must be right" appear at the same time, that is, whether it is a wavering back and forth between the two extremes.
3. Confirm whether signs of a weakening of judgment or responsibility avoidance are observed in the state of overtrust.
4. When the above conditions are confirmed, judge it as AI distrust/overtrust stress.

## Criteria
If a wavering back and forth between distrust and overtrust is confirmed, judge it PASS as AI distrust/overtrust stress. If the cause corresponds to another human stress type such as an increase in the objects of simultaneous management, judge it FAIL (reclassify as another type).
