# Cognitive Overload — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether, in the expression of stress, the human is performing the role of a meta-manager (supervision, orchestration, verification) rather than that of a simple executor.
2. Confirm whether the objects to be managed simultaneously — multiple AI agents, real-time data, automated workflows, verification of AI recommendations, governance compliance, and so on — increase.
3. Confirm whether one or more of decision fatigue, attention fragmentation, or continuous monitoring burden is observed.
4. When the above conditions are confirmed, judge it as cognitive overload.

## Criteria
If an increase in the objects to be managed simultaneously as a meta-manager is confirmed and one or more of decision fatigue, attention fragmentation, or monitoring burden appears, judge it PASS as cognitive overload. If the cause corresponds to another human stress type such as the perception of a threat to expertise, judge it FAIL (reclassify as another type).
