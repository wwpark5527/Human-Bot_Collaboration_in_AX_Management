# Replacement Anxiety — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether threat perceptions of the kind "is my expertise becoming inferior to AI / am I being reduced to a supporting role / am I losing promotion opportunities" appear in the expression of stress.
2. Distinguish whether the cause is not ambiguity in the division of roles but the perception of a threat to one's expertise and value of existence.
3. Confirm the context that affects the intensity of the threat perception, such as whether the person is a knowledge worker.
4. When the above conditions are confirmed, judge it as replacement anxiety and warn of the possibility of development into existential anxiety and an identity crisis.

## Criteria
If the perception of a threat regarding a sense of inferiority in expertise, reduction to a supporting role, or loss of promotion opportunities is confirmed, judge it PASS as replacement anxiety. If the cause corresponds to another human stress type such as unclearness in the division of roles, judge it FAIL (reclassify as another type).
