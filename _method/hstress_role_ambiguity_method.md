# Role Ambiguity — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the question "who is responsible for what?" between the human and the bot is raised in the expression of stress.
2. Confirm whether the locus of strategy, decision-making, and the ultimate responsible party is unclear, that is, whether it is a problem of the boundary of the division of roles.
3. Confirm whether one or more of the following is observed: conflict between human judgment and AI recommendation, damage to self-esteem when the AI is superior, and the problem of attributing responsibility in case of failure.
4. When the above conditions are confirmed, judge it as role ambiguity stress.

## Criteria
If unclearness in the division of roles regarding "who is responsible for what?" is confirmed and one or more of judgment-recommendation conflict, damage to self-esteem, or the problem of attributing responsibility appears, judge it PASS as role ambiguity. If the cause corresponds to another human stress type such as a sense of inferiority in expertise or cognitive burden, judge it FAIL (reclassify as another type).
