# Social and Relational Stress — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the expression of stress is a tension originating in the collaborative relationship with bots rather than with human colleagues.
2. Confirm whether one or more of psychological distance from bots, deterioration of human-to-human relationships, social isolation, and increased emotional labor is observed.
3. Confirm whether there are situations of a lack of emotional empathy in customer service, AI supervision, acceptance of AI-generated feedback, and collaboration with bots.
4. If the above conditions are confirmed, judge it to be social and relational stress and warn of the risk of a weakened need to belong.

## Criteria
If one or more of psychological distance, relationship deterioration, social isolation, and increased emotional labor is confirmed in the collaborative relationship with bots, it is judged PASS as social and relational stress. If the cause falls under another human stress type, such as shaken trust or cognitive burden, it is judged FAIL (reclassified as another type).
