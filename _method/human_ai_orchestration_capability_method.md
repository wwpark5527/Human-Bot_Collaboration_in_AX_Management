# Human-AI Orchestration Capability — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Identify the multiple AI agents in operation within the organization or required by it.
2. Design the mode of coordination among AI agents and a plan for the division of human-bot roles.
3. Adjust the role placement of humans and bots according to the team role balance (TRB) criteria.
4. Verify the designed collaboration structure from the perspective of the organization as a whole.

## Criteria
If, rather than stopping at being good on one's own, a human + AI system is designed and the collaboration structure and role placement are completed, it is judged PASS (orchestration capability secured). If one remains at the level of individual competency alone, it is judged FAIL (rework of the system design is needed).
