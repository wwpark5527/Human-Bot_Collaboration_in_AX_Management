# Human-AI Orchestration (orchestration) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Identify the AI agents that need to be orchestrated and their roles.
2. Design the division of roles between humans and bots (team role balance).
3. Design the collaboration structure (including the common context, decision criteria, and governance structure).
4. Do not remain an executor, but orchestrate the whole from the standpoint of integrating the capabilities of humans and AI.
5. Inspect whether the designed structure leads to the creation of new value, organizations, markets, and order.

## Criteria
If the human-AI collaboration structure is designed, controlled, and amplified and the leader functions not as an executor but as an augmented orchestrator it is judged PASS (orchestration capability exercised), and if it remains at individual execution it is judged FAIL (executor level).
