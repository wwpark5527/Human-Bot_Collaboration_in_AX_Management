# Teamwork in the Human-Bond Aspect — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Fix the department/team to be measured.
2. Collect the degree of psychological attraction among members with a group cohesiveness measurement instrument.
3. Compute the results as the teamwork indicator for the human-bond aspect.
4. Analyze the correlation with members' perceived performance data.
5. Combine with the results of the task-activation aspect (TRB) to produce a comprehensive diagnosis of departmental teamwork.

## Criteria
Whether the result is PASS (a valid diagnosis of teamwork in the human-bond aspect) is decided by whether the measurement targets group cohesiveness (psychological attraction among members) and corresponds to the supplementary fit aspect. If the measurement targets the degree to which the nine team roles are exercised or complementary fit (confusion with the task-activation aspect), it is judged FAIL.
