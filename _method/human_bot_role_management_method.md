# HBRM (Human-Bot Role Management) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect the organization's systems and practices related to diversity management, complementary fit, human-AI collaboration, and organizational AX.
2. Check whether the existing HRM (human resource management) system also encompasses bots (agents of automation).
3. Compare whether each practice is connected under a single human-bot resource management principle (HBRM).
4. Identify the gaps where human-bot resource management is scattered across individual practices.
5. Judge whether HBRM is applied and the level of its integration.

## Criteria
If diversity management, complementary fit, human-AI collaboration, and organizational AX are integrated into a single HBRM principle, it is judged PASS; if they are scattered as individual practices or bot resources are omitted from the existing HRM system, it is judged FAIL.
