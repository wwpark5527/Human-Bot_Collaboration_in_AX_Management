# Human Centrality — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Evaluate whether the object of an AI- or AX-related decision harms or helps the survival and development of humans.
2. Confirm whether the subject of that judgment actually rests with humans (that the AI does not judge on its own).
3. If it is judged to be harmful, reject the application of that AI or AX.
4. Check whether the zero-sum thinking that 'humans always come first and bots must be sacrificed' has intervened in the judgment process.
5. Once it is confirmed not to be zero-sum thinking, design follow-up measures so that it leads on to the three core spirits of augmentation, respect for and use of diversity, and pursuit of complementary fit.

## Criteria
When both the judgment of the impact on the survival and development of humans and the subject of that judgment rest with humans, and one does not fall into zero-sum thinking, it is judged PASS as human centrality. If the subject of judgment is not human or zero-sum thinking (the unilateral sacrifice of bots) is confirmed, it is judged FAIL.
