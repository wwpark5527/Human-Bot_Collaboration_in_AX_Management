# Human Illogicality — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Investigate the emotional, relational, and symbolic reaction patterns of the human stakeholder groups the bot deals with.
2. Add human acceptability criteria to the evaluation procedure so that proposed designs and policies are not evaluated by efficiency/logic criteria alone.
3. Identify in advance the points at risk of rejection due to lack of trust and prepare mitigation measures.
4. Establish and execute an explanation and communication plan for changes where emotional resistance is expected.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS (human illogicality reflected) when design and operation reflect not only efficiency/logic but also human emotion, relationships, symbols, identity, and habit, and FAIL (human illogicality not reflected) when judgment is made by efficiency/logic criteria alone and fails to earn trust and acceptance.
