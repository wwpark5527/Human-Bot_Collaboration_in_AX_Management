# H: Human — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the target organizational member has been augmented with the help of AI in terms of physical and mental capabilities.
2. If not augmented, distinguish whether the entity is a human or a bot.
3. If the entity is a human and is not augmented, classify it as the H (human) type.
4. Record the role and scope of work of H-type members in contrast with the AH/AB/B types.

## Criteria
It is judged to be the H type when the entity is a human whose physical and mental capabilities have not been augmented by AI. If augmentation is confirmed, it is reclassified as AH; if the entity is a bot, it is reclassified as B or AB.
