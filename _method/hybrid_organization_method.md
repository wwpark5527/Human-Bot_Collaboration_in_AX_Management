# True AX Organization / Hybrid Organization (AH+AB) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm in the organizational member table whether the humans fall into the augmented (AH) column.
2. Confirm whether the bots fall into the augmented (AB) column — the key point is whether the common context, the governance context, and AI governance are embedded.
3. Confirm whether humans and bots divide and share roles centered on roles rather than jobs and mutually augment one another.
4. If all the above conditions are satisfied, judge it to be a hybrid organization (a true AX organization).
5. Accelerate the transition by executing the use of a proper OS for organizational AX and the realization of HBRM.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS as a hybrid organization when both humans and bots have transitioned into a mutually augmented state and form a role-centered collaborative cognition system based on the shared/governance context. If the bots have not been augmented, it is reclassified as having remained at the AH+B stage.
