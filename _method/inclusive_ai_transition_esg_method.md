# Inclusive / Just AI Transition ESG — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Identify within the organization the stratum that owns, uses, and directs AI and the stratum that is evaluated, replaced, managed, and excluded by AI, and diagnose the risk of stratification.
2. Design a system that equitably guarantees the right of access to AI and the right to learn to all organizational members.
3. Design the procedures of AI use, verification, and approval so that humans' right of judgment is reflected in them.
4. Build a system of recording and attribution of responsibility for AI-related activities.
5. Establish a benefit-sharing system that shares the outcomes of AI use with organizational members.
6. Distinguish verified reality from the author's interpretation and design by separating the four layers of verifiable fact (external grounds) - reasonable interpretation (problem diagnosis) - proposed concept (theorization) - execution model (operationalization).
7. Integrate the whole of the above into the company's ESG responsibility system.

## Criteria
If AI access, learning, use, verification, approval, recording, responsibility, and benefit-sharing are all explicitly incorporated into the ESG responsibility system and the risk of stratification is mitigated, it is judged PASS; if one or more is omitted and the differentiation of strata is left unattended, it is judged FAIL.
