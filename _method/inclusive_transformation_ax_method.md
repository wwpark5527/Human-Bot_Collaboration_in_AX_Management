# Inclusive Transformation AX — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Diagnose whether the organization's current AX is designed solely around efficiency, productivity, and cost reduction.
2. Check the level of protection of each of humans' role, right to judge, right to learn, right to transition, right to appeal, and right to share in performance.
3. Evaluate whether the risks of efficiency-centered AX (role reduction, widening gaps, algorithmic management, weakened right of judgment, absence of appeal, concentration of outcomes) actually appear in the organization.
4. Redesign the AX transition plan so as to reflect the perspective of responsibility and inclusiveness.
5. Connect the redesigned framework to the S·G execution structures of ESG (the common context, the governance context, and so on) and reflect it in organizational operations.

## Criteria
If protective devices for the six rights (role, right to judge, right to learn, right to transition, right to appeal, right to share in performance) are included in the AI transition plan and it is connected to the ESG S·G structures, it is judged PASS; if it is designed solely by efficiency and productivity indicators and the six rights are not reflected, it is judged FAIL.
