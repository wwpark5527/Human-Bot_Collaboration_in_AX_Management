# AI Education — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Fix the scope of AI-literacy education and the standard for judging completion — state explicitly which courses fall under it and what the conditions for recognizing completion are (finishing the course, passing the assessment, submitting a practical deliverable, and so on).
2. Calculate the AI-literacy education completion rate — compute the proportion of people who completed it relative to the target population according to the standard in 1.
3. Calculate job-specific reskilling hours — aggregate the hours invested in retraining at the job level. Decide in advance whether to distinguish reskilling (for the purpose of job transition) from upskilling (deepening the current job), and apply this consistently.
4. Confirm the distribution across jobs — see whether reskilling hours are skewed toward particular jobs, and check whether they were actually invested in the jobs judged to be heavily affected at step 4, labor transition.
5. Present the completion rate and the reskilling hours bound together as one reporting unit.
6. Fix a measurement cycle and accumulate a time series on the same standard to confirm the trend of education investment.

## Criteria
With the completion judgment standard stated explicitly, it is PASS when **both the AI-literacy education completion rate and job-specific reskilling hours** are calculated, the two values are reported together, and a time series on the same standard is accumulated. It is judged FAIL if the completion judgment standard is unclear so that the figures are not reproducible, or if only the completion rate is calculated and job-specific reskilling hours are not produced. The latter cannot filter out the filling of the figure with formal attendance alone and therefore runs counter to the purpose of this indicator.
