# AI Utilization Capability — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Fix the standard for recognizing work-improvement cases — state explicitly the three conditions of comparability of before and after the improvement, identifiability of the AI contribution, and reproducibility, and do not tally as a case any instance in which even one of them is not confirmed.
2. Fix the collection routes — fix where and how cases will be received (departmental submission, tool logs, regular interviews), and mark non-submitting departments separately in order to reduce submission bias.
3. Tally the AI-utilization work-improvement cases — record the department and job together with each case so that the distribution calculation in step 4 becomes possible.
4. Fix the measurement unit of productivity gain — decide in advance whether the gain will be expressed in hours saved, throughput, rework rate, or a quality indicator, and apply it identically throughout the measurement cycle.
5. Calculate the productivity-gain distribution — decompose the gain values by department and by job, and present together the gap between the upper and lower ranges and the ranges with no gain at all. Reporting the company-wide average value alone does not satisfy this step.
6. Check against the preceding two indicators — confirm whether utilization performance appears in the groups where AI accessibility and AI education were secured, and record separately as its own item the range in which access and education exist but performance does not.
7. Present the case tally and the gain distribution bound together as one reporting unit.
8. Fix a measurement cycle and accumulate a time series on the same standard to confirm the trend of the spread of utilization performance.

## Criteria
With the case recognition standard and the gain measurement unit stated explicitly, it is PASS when **both the tally of AI-utilization work-improvement cases and the productivity-gain distribution** are calculated, the two values are reported together, and a time series on the same standard is accumulated. It is judged FAIL if the recognition standard is unclear so that the case tally is not reproducible, or if the productivity gain is reported **only as a single company-wide average value** and the distribution by department and by job is not produced. The latter makes a state in which gains are concentrated in a part look like the performance of the whole organization and therefore runs directly counter to the purpose of this indicator, which specifies 'distribution'.
