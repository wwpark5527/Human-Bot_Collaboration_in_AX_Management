# Benefit Sharing — Method

The measurement cycle is **half-yearly or annual**, aligned with the organization's accounting cycle. Since the execution of compensation, education, and welfare is fixed on accounting units, quarterly measurement would mix in unconfirmed amounts and be inaccurate. There are two comparison targets: the immediately preceding identical cycle and the same period of the previous year.

## Steps

**Step 1 — Compute the denominator and fix its basis in a document.**
Convert the AI productivity gains confirmed in the measurement period into an amount of money. The following three must be stated explicitly.
- Computation method: what was counted as a gain (conversion of hours saved into labor cost, increase in throughput, cost reduction, and so on).
- Reference point in time: what it was compared against (the same period before introduction, the same period of the previous year, a non-adopting control group, and so on).
- Covered work: how far the work included extends.
A reinvestment rate calculated without this document is invalidated without proceeding to the subsequent steps.

**Step 2 — Aggregate the numerator divided into three channels.**
Fix the compensation paid, the education invested, and the welfare executed **each separately**, then sum them. Unify and state explicitly the basis of execution (accrual basis / cash basis).

**Step 3 — Separate out reinvestment outside the channels.**
Exclude from the numerator reinvestment amounts not attributed to members, such as strengthening organizational capability, facilities, and retained earnings, and leave the amount as a separate item. Also show the remainder left after subtracting the three-channel reinvestment and the out-of-channel reinvestment from the gains, so that the whole whereabouts is exposed.

**Step 4 — Calculate the reinvestment rate.**
Reinvestment rate = (compensation + education + welfare) ÷ gains × 100. This is the value this indicator must produce. Produce the composition ratio by channel as well.

**Step 5 — Calculate the distribution of benefit.**
Produce the number of people who actually received reinvestment, the proportion of beneficiaries relative to all members, and the distribution of amounts by grade and by employment type. State explicitly whether non-regular, contract, and dispatched personnel are included, and if they were excluded, record the reason for exclusion and the number of such personnel.

**Step 6 — Attach the denominator time series.**
Place side by side the values of the gains themselves for the immediately preceding cycle and the same period of the previous year.

**Step 7 — Compare and judge.**
Check the reinvestment rate, the composition ratio by channel, and the distribution of benefit against the immediately preceding cycle and the same period of the previous year, and decide PASS/FAIL by the criteria below.

**Step 8 — Record.**
Leave the reinvestment rate, the amounts and composition ratios by channel, the denominator amount and the document of its computation basis, the denominator time series, the out-of-channel reinvestment amount, the table of benefit distribution, and the judgment result and its grounds.

## Criteria

Only when **all** four of the following conditions are satisfied is it judged PASS.

- **(a) The reinvestment rate is at or above the baseline.** The baseline must have been announced before the measurement, and cannot be adjusted after the fact to fit the result.
- **(b) All three channels of compensation, education, and welfare exceed 0.** If even one channel is 0, it is FAIL. This holds even if the total exceeds the baseline.
- **(c) The basis of computation of the denominator is presented in a document, and the time series of the gains is reported alongside.** A ratio placed on top of a denominator without a basis is not an object of judgment.
- **(d) The proportion of beneficiaries is at or above the standard, and no concentration in a particular stratum is confirmed in the distribution by grade and employment type.**

The following are explicit FAIL conditions.

- **A reinvestment rate submitted without a basis of computation for the denominator** — judgment is not deferred but recorded as FAIL. This is the most common form of failure in this indicator.
- **A case in which the reinvestment rate rose but the gains (the denominator) decreased** — since the possibility of a rise due to a shrinking denominator cannot be excluded, it is recorded as FAIL unless it can be shown that the absolute amount of the numerator increased as well.
- **A result submitting only the total without decomposition by channel** — since (b) cannot be judged, it is FAIL.
- **A result submitting only the total amount without the distribution of benefit** — since (d) cannot be judged, it is FAIL. The total amount conceals concentration at the top.
- **A reinvestment rate calculated by including out-of-channel reinvestment such as organizational capability in the numerator** — since this violates the definition, the figure itself is invalidated. The definition of this indicator covers only the share that flowed back to members.
