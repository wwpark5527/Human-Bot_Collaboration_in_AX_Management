# Competence-Inferiority Type — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect the reactions and remarks related to AI utilization of the target member or job group.
2. Check whether learning fatigue or digital inferiority appears in the reaction.
3. Check whether self-deprecation arising from differences in AI usage ability, or the sense of technological alienation characteristic of middle-aged and older members, appears.
4. If one or more of the four characteristics is confirmed, determine it to be the competence-inferiority type.

## Criteria
If one or more of learning fatigue, digital inferiority, self-deprecation arising from differences in AI usage ability, and the sense of technological alienation among middle-aged and older members appears, it is judged PASS (competence-inferiority type). If all four characteristics are absent, it is judged FAIL (reclassified as another type).
