# Control-Loss Type — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect the reactions of the target member or job group related to AI recommendation and automated decision-making.
2. Check whether manager resistance or defense of expert authority appears in the reaction.
3. Check whether algorithmic distrust, or refusal of recommendations made by AI, appears.
4. If one or more of the four characteristics is confirmed, determine it to be the control-loss type.

## Criteria
If one or more of manager resistance, defense of expert authority, algorithmic distrust, and refusal of recommendations made by AI appears, it is judged PASS (control-loss type). If all four characteristics are absent, it is judged FAIL (reclassified as another type).
