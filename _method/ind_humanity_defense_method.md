# Humanity-Defense Type — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect the reactions of the target member or job group (artists, professors, counseling and education practitioners, and so on) related to generative AI.
2. Check whether defense of human creativity or emphasis on human relationality appears in the reaction.
3. Check whether the assertion of the superiority of emotion, ethics, and intuition, or the reaction that "AI does not understand humans," appears.
4. If one or more of the four characteristics is confirmed, determine it to be the humanity-defense type.

## Criteria
If one or more of defending human creativity, emphasizing human relationality, asserting the superiority of emotion, ethics, and intuition, and the reaction that "AI does not understand humans" appears, it is judged PASS (humanity-defense type). If all four characteristics are absent, it is judged FAIL (reclassified as another type).
