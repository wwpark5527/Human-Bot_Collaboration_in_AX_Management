# Labor Transition — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Fix the unit of an "AI adoption case" and the definition of the point of adoption — state explicitly what will be counted as one adoption case and whether the point of adoption is taken as the contract date, the release date, or the date actual use began, and apply this identically throughout the measurement cycle.
2. Secure the list of adoption cases — enumerate without omission all adoption cases within the measurement period. Since a case missing from the list makes the judgment of whether an assessment was conducted impossible in the first place, confirm the completeness of the list first.
3. For each adoption case, confirm the existence of a job impact assessment and its **date of writing** — check against source materials whether the writing date of the assessment document precedes the point of adoption defined in step 1.
4. Judge **whether a job impact assessment was conducted before AI adoption** — tally as conducted only the cases satisfying the timing condition. An assessment written after adoption is judged as not conducted, and the reason is recorded explicitly as after-the-fact writing.
5. Present whether it was conducted in aggregate form — report alongside the number of cases conducted in advance relative to all adoption cases.
6. Fix the denominator of the transition-reassignment rate — take as the denominator the people judged in the impact assessment to have their jobs substantively changed. Do not use the whole workforce as the denominator.
7. Calculate the **transition-reassignment rate** — compute the proportion of the denominator population whose reassignment to a new role was completed, and mark as separate items each of those with reassignment in progress, those with reassignment incomplete, and those who departed, so that they do not quietly drop out of the denominator.
8. Present whether it was conducted and the reassignment rate bound together as one reporting unit.
9. Fix a measurement cycle and accumulate a time series on the same standard to confirm the trend of transition fulfilment relative to the scale of adoption.

## Criteria
With the unit of adoption cases and the definition of the point of adoption stated explicitly, it is PASS when **whether a job impact assessment was conducted before AI adoption is judged with evidence of timing and the transition-reassignment rate is calculated**, the two values are reported together, and a time series on the same standard is accumulated. It is judged FAIL if any one of the following applies — (a) the case in which the writing date of the impact assessment was not confirmed, so that conduct in advance and writing after the fact are not distinguished, (b) the case in which the denominator of the transition-reassignment rate was taken as the whole workforce, so that a reassignment rate on the basis of the people judged to be affected is not produced, (c) the case in which only whether it was conducted is reported and the transition-reassignment rate is not produced. (a) nullifies the source text's timing condition "before adoption", (b) makes the reassignment rate look higher than it actually is, and (c) conceals the state in which assessment alone was done and did not lead to reassignment, and each therefore runs counter to the purpose of this indicator.
