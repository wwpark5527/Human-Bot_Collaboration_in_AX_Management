# Surveillance-Fear Type — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect the reactions of the target member or job group related to data-based management tools (productivity tracking, behavior logs, and so on).
2. Check whether anxiety over behavior monitoring or concern about privacy infringement appears in the reaction.
3. Check whether fear of AI evaluation, or stress caused by work logs, appears.
4. If one or more of the four characteristics is confirmed, determine it to be the surveillance-fear type.

## Criteria
If one or more of anxiety over behavior monitoring, concern about privacy infringement, fear of AI evaluation, and stress from work logs appears, it is judged PASS (surveillance-fear type). If all four characteristics are absent, it is judged FAIL (reclassified as another type).
