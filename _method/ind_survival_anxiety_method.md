# Survival-Anxiety Type — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect the reactions of the target members or job group (call center, office workers, developers, and so on) concerning AI adoption.
2. Confirm whether fear of restructuring appears in the reactions.
3. Confirm whether concern over role reduction, concern over reduced promotion opportunities, and anxiety over wage decline appear.
4. If one or more of the four features are confirmed and the case falls within a repetitive knowledge-work job group, judge it as the survival-anxiety type.

## Criteria
If one or more of fear of restructuring, concern over role reduction, concern over reduced promotion opportunities, and anxiety over wage decline appear, judge PASS as the survival-anxiety type. If all four features are absent, judge FAIL (reclassify as another type).
