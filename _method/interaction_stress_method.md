# Relational (Interaction) Stress — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Separately from the inspection of individual human stress or individual bot stress, observe the phases of human-bot interaction.
2. Confirm whether there is a feeling on the human side of being unable to control the bot.
3. Confirm whether there are cases on the bot side of misinterpreting human intent.
4. Confirm whether there is an imbalance between human responsibility and AI authority.
5. Confirm whether human-AI trust is collapsing, and if one or more are confirmed, trigger intervention at the governance and organizational level.

## Criteria
If one or more of loss of a sense of control, misreading of intent, imbalance of responsibility and authority, and collapse of trust are confirmed, judge PASS as relational (interaction) stress (intervention required). If none of the four signs is confirmed, judge FAIL (relational stress not confirmed; re-inspect on the individual-stress axis).
