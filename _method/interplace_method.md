# Interplace — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Fix the individuals and jobs to be measured.
2. Distribute the self-perception inventory (SPI) to the measurement subject themselves and collect the responses.
3. Distribute the observer assessment (OA) to 4 or more observers and collect the responses.
4. Collect job-related data through the job requirement exercise (JRE) and the job observer assessment (JOA).
5. Enter and transmit the collected data to the Interplace system (the UK server).
6. The system processes the data in real time and generates 14 kinds of report.
7. Receive the results by e-mail (or via the client company's person in charge) and use them in practice.

## Criteria
If both the subject's own responses (SPI) and the observer responses (OA) are collected and, after a processing course mixing relative and absolute assessment, 14 kinds of report are output, judge PASS (a valid TR/TRB measurement is complete); if the response sample is insufficient (fewer than 4 observers, and so on), judge FAIL (re-collection required).
