# Job Observer Assessment (JOA: job observer assessment) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Fix the job to be diagnosed (the same job unit as JRE).
2. Select job observers who can observe that job.
3. Proceed so that responses are given on the basis of the observation results (job-observation items) (on the basis of observation results, not a description of requirements).
4. Collect the responses and organize them into the Interplace input format.
5. Combine the JOA data with the job requirement exercise (JRE) data and pass it on as input to job assessment.

## Criteria
Whether it is PASS (a valid JOA response) is decided by whether the response was written on the basis of the results of observation by a job observer. If the response was written in the manner of describing job requirements (confused with JRE), judge FAIL.
