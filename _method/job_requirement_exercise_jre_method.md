# Job Requirement Exercise (JRE: job requirement exercise) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Fix the job to be diagnosed.
2. Distribute the JRE to respondents who are well versed in that job.
3. Have them respond on the basis of the job-requirements items (the assessment basis is the job's requirements, not a person's characteristics).
4. Collect the responses and organize them into the Interplace input format.
5. Combine the JRE data with the Job Observer Assessment (JOA) data and hand it over as job-assessment material.

## Criteria
PASS (a valid JRE response) is decided by whether the response was written about a job rather than a person, on the basis of the requirements that the job demands. If the response targets a person's characteristics (confused with the SPI/OA) or is based on the results of observing the job (confused with the JOA), it is judged FAIL.
