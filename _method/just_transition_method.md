# Just Transition — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the just-transition principle of the climate transition field (fairness and inclusiveness of the transition process, no one left behind).
2. Review the organization's AI transition plan and identify the points where humans' roles, income, judgment rights, learning rights, and dignity risk being damaged.
3. Apply the just-transition principle to the identified risk points and design mitigation measures.
4. Check whether the just-transition principle is continuously reflected within the AI Inclusive-Transition ESG (Just AI Transition ESG) frame.

## Criteria
If the points in the AI transition plan that damage humans' roles, income, judgment rights, learning rights, and dignity are identified and mitigation measures are prepared, it is judged PASS; if they are left unattended, FAIL.
