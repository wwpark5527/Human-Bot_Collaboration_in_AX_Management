# context (organizational standard) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the updated prohibition rules and training materials that came out of the 'feedback' node.
2. Accumulate these into the organization's standards (the materials the next execution will refer to).
3. Confirm whether the accumulation was actually reflected into the next standard.
4. Close the chain while confirming whether the next execution becomes more refined on the basis of the accumulated execution assets.

## Criteria
Whether it was reflected into the next standard, and whether the next execution becomes more refined on the basis of the accumulated execution assets — if both conditions are confirmed it is judged PASS, and if they are not confirmed, FAIL.
