# outcome (result) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the actual action and deliverable that came out of the 'action' node.
2. Distinguish them so that the deliverable itself is not mistaken for the result.
3. Find what that deliverable made different (improved decision-making, fewer omissions, fewer customer complaints, and the like).
4. Settle the result on the basis not of "what was produced" but of "what changed."
5. Pass the settled change in results to the next node (review).

## Criteria
It is judged not by "what was produced?" but by "what changed?" If there is only a deliverable and no change in results is identified, it is judged FAIL.
