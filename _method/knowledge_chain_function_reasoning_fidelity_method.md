# Reasoning-Chain Fidelity Measurement — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the conclusion to be evaluated and the intermediate reasoning steps leading to it.
2. Check whether each intermediate step is valid.
3. Confirm whether each step leads to the next step.
4. Confirm whether the last step actually supports the final answer.
5. Confirm whether each step is connected to evidence, and produce the result of the process-validity evaluation.

## Criteria
If all four items — validity of the intermediate steps, connection between steps, the last step's support of the answer, and connection to evidence — are confirmed, it is judged PASS; if even one is not confirmed, FAIL.
