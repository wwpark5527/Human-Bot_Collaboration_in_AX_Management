# Structural-Distance Measurement — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Grasp the current concept structure.
2. Grasp the target concept structure.
3. Compare the two structures and confirm the respective numbers of missing concepts, incorrect connections, and unnecessary connections.
4. Calculate the structural-distance value in a way where the more of these three elements there are, the greater the distance.
5. Identify missing concepts and misconceptions from the calculated structural distance.

## Criteria
If missing concepts and misconceptions are identified and the structural-distance value is produced, it is judged PASS; if what one does not know and what one knows incorrectly cannot be distinguished, FAIL.
