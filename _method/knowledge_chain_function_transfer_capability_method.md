# Transferability Measurement — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the original sentence, example, and domain that the person being assessed learned.
2. Present the sentence in changed form and confirm whether understanding is maintained.
3. Present the example in changed form and confirm whether the principle is maintained.
4. Present the domain in changed form and confirm whether the same structure is applied.
5. Present a counterexample and confirm whether the concept is revised on one's own, and thereby judge the level of transferability.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when all four items are confirmed — understanding maintained when the sentence changes, principle maintained when the example changes, structure applied when the domain changes, and concept revised when a counterexample is presented — and FAIL if even one is not confirmed.
