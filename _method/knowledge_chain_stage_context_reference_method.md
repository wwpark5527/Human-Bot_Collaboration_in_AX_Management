# Organizational Context Reference — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the request unit passed on from the question stage.
2. Identify, among the organization's common context (purpose, standards, roles, sources, format), the items related to that request.
3. Interpret the request by referencing the organization's history, intent, norms, and memory.
4. Confirm whether the interpretation result is grounded in the organization's standards rather than depending on a particular user's personal interpretation.
5. Pass the interpretation context in which organizational standards are reflected on to the next stage (governance validation).

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the answer is produced by referencing the organization's standards without being swayed by per-user differences in interpretation, and FAIL when it depends on personal interpretation and is swayed independently of the organization's standards.
