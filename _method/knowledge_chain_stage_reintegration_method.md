# Re-reflection into Organizational Knowledge — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the traceable record (grounds, process, version) passed on from the result recording stage.
2. Compare that record against the organization's existing knowledge and standards and connect it to them.
3. Verify the connected knowledge and update (evolve) it if necessary.
4. Reflect the updated knowledge into the organizational knowledge system so that it becomes the standard for the next judgment.
5. Pass the updated organizational knowledge system on to the next stage (reuse by the next AI/human).

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the result is absorbed into organizational knowledge and changes the standard for the next judgment, and FAIL when it is not absorbed and remains a scattered response.
