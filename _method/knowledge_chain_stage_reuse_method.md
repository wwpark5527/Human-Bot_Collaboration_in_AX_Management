# Reuse by the Next AI/Human — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the organizational knowledge system updated in the re-reflection into organizational knowledge stage.
2. Provide this organizational knowledge as input when the next AI or the next human begins a new question/task.
3. Check whether the next task uses the accumulated knowledge rather than starting over from scratch.
4. Confirm whether the chain has been closed by reuse and the circulation/accumulation structure has been completed.
5. Confirm whether the reused knowledge makes the next response maintain consistency with what came before.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the next task takes the accumulated organizational knowledge as input and consistency of results is secured, and FAIL when it starts anew from scratch each time and there is no consistency.
