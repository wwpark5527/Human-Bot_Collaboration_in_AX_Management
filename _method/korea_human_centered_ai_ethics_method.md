# Korean Government (Human-Centered AI Ethics Standards) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Stipulate the 3 basic principles (human dignity, the public good of society, the purposiveness of technology) in the organizational AX policy document.
2. Turn the 10 core requirements into a practical checklist item by item (guaranteeing human rights, protecting privacy, respecting diversity, harmlessness, fairness, safety, accountability, transparency, data management, explainability).
3. Review whether human autonomy is infringed at the design and operation stages of the AI system.
4. Periodically evaluate the contribution of AI use to the welfare of humankind.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS (Korean AI ethics standards satisfied) when, under the intent that AI must not infringe on human autonomy and must contribute to the welfare of humankind, the system of 3 basic principles and 10 core requirements is actually in place, and FAIL (Korean AI ethics standards not satisfied) otherwise.
