# AI-Complemented Worker — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Identify the work and the workers whose productivity and decision-making ability improve through the use of AI.
2. Compare and measure the performance-demand standards (processing time, output volume) before and after the introduction of AI.
3. Check whether the increase in performance demand causes an excessive burden on the worker (risk of burnout and so on).
4. Prepare and apply a plan for improving compensation and working conditions in proportion to the increment in productivity.
5. Re-examine the worker's burden and satisfaction after the improvement plan has been applied.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when improvement in productivity and decision-making ability is confirmed and a commensurate improvement in compensation and treatment is achieved, and FAIL when only the demand for performance increases with no improvement in treatment.
