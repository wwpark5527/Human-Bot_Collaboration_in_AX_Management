# AI-displacement/reduction-risk worker — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Identify the repetitive, documented work within the organization and assess its automation potential.
2. Grasp the workers who handle that work and the share it takes in their duties.
3. Assess the urgency and the expected timing of the risk that the value of the work is reduced or disappears.
4. Establish and apply response paths such as retraining, job transition, and transition into a context-designing role.
5. Periodically check the progress and the effect of the transition paths.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the work at risk of automation and the persons in charge are identified and retraining and transition paths are prepared and operated, and FAIL when the risk is left unattended and workers are exposed to the reduction of the value of their work without any response.
