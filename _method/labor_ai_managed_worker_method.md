# AI-managed worker — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Identify the workers and work areas whose work is assigned, evaluated, and monitored by algorithms.
2. Document the judgment standards and the data sources of the assignment, evaluation, and monitoring algorithms being applied.
3. Disclose the standards to workers in an explainable form.
4. Confirm whether workers actually understand the standards, and if there is a gap in understanding, supplement it.
5. When opaque or unfair standards are found, improve them and re-announce them.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the assignment, evaluation, and monitoring standards are disclosed and workers can understand them, and FAIL when the standards remain opaque and a state in which workers find them hard to understand persists.
