# Context-designer AX talent — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Clearly define the purpose to be assigned to AI in the target work area.
2. Design the (judgment) standards by which the suitability of AI outputs will be judged.
3. Design the procedure and the verification standards by which AI results will be verified.
4. Design a system for collecting and reflecting feedback on the results of AI operation.
5. Document the design outputs of purpose, standards, verification, and feedback as the organization's operating structure, such as the common context, and reflect them there.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when purposes, standards, verification, and feedback are directly designed and their outputs are reflected in the organization's operating structure as common context and verification standards, and FAIL when the worker stops at merely using AI and does not contribute to transforming the organizational structure.
