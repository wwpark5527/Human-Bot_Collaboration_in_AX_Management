# Capability layer — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect the AX-transition-related statements and behaviors of the member or job group under observation.
2. Confirm whether that reaction converges on the question "Am I falling behind?"
3. Confirm whether emotional signs of inferiority and helplessness appear in the reaction, and when both conditions are met together, judge it a capability-layer reaction.
4. Once judged to be the capability layer, carry out learning and coaching support to close the competence gap.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS as the capability layer when the core question is "Am I falling behind?" and the representative emotions appear as inferiority and helplessness. When the question or the emotion belongs to another layer (survival, relationship, existence), it is judged FAIL (reclassified into another layer).
