# Existence layer — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect the AX-transition-related statements and behaviors of the member or job group under observation.
2. Confirm whether that reaction converges on the question "Is the meaning of being human diminishing?"
3. Confirm whether emotional signs of identity confusion appear in the reaction, and when both conditions are met together, judge it an existence-layer reaction.
4. Once judged to be the existence layer, carry out measures reaffirming uniquely human value and roles.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS as the existence layer when the core question is "Is the meaning of being human diminishing?" and the representative emotion appears as identity confusion. When the question or the emotion belongs to another layer (survival, capability, relationship), it is judged FAIL (reclassified into another layer).
