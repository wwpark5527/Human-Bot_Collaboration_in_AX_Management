# Reasonable interpretation — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Take as input the external evidence organized at the verifiable fact layer.
2. Examine in which of the areas — access, competence, decision authority, performance distribution — those facts may induce gaps.
3. Confirm whether the possibility of gaps arising can be reasonably inferred from the facts — exclude ungrounded leaps.
4. Organize the inferred problem into the "reasonable interpretation" layer (problem diagnosis).

## Criteria
It is judged PASS if the possibility of gaps arising is reasonably inferred from the confirmed facts, and FAIL if it is a conjecture that leaps without factual grounds.
