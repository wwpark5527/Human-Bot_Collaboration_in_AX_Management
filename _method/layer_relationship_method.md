# Relationship layer — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect the AX-transition-related statements and behaviors of the member or job group under observation.
2. Confirm whether that reaction converges on the question "Is my position in the organization weakening?"
3. Confirm whether emotional signs of alienation and defensiveness appear in the reaction, and when both conditions are met together, judge it a relationship-layer reaction.
4. If it is judged to be the relationship layer, carry out measures to rebuild the network and reconfirm the role.

## Criteria
If the core question is "Is my position in the organization weakening?" and the representative emotions appear as alienation and defensiveness, it is judged PASS as the relationship layer. If the question or the emotion belongs to another layer (survival, capability, existence), it is judged FAIL (reclassified into another layer).
