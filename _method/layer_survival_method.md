# Survival layer — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect the AX-transition-related statements and behaviors of the member or job group under observation.
2. Confirm whether that reaction converges on the question "Is my job disappearing?"
3. Confirm whether emotional signs of anxiety and fear appear in the reaction, and when both conditions are met together, judge it a survival-layer reaction.
4. If it is judged to be the survival layer, execute the sharing of information related to job continuation and the retraining and redeployment response.

## Criteria
If the core question is "Is my job disappearing?" and the representative emotions appear as anxiety and fear, it is judged PASS as the survival layer. If the question or the emotion belongs to another layer (capability, relationship, existence), it is judged FAIL (reclassified into another layer).
