# Verifiable fact — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect the literature of international organizations and research institutions concerning AI gaps, labor transition, algorithmic management, and human-rights risks.
2. Distinguish whether the collected literature is externally verifiable fact or the interpretation or claim of a particular author or institution.
3. Classify and organize only the verifiable facts into the "verifiable fact" layer.
4. Provide the classified facts as the grounds for the subsequent stages (reasonable interpretation / proposed concept / execution model), while not confusing the facts themselves with interpretations and proposals.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS if the content is confirmed in the literature of international organizations and research institutions, and FAIL if unconfirmed assumptions, interpretations, or claims are mixed in.
