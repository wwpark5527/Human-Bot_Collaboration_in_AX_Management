# Least-preferred Role — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Identify the team-role candidates that have not been classified as natural roles or potential roles.
2. Test whether there is any possibility of manifestation when the person applies maximum effort.
3. Confirm whether there is no possibility of manifestation even when others' (colleagues') support is additionally applied.
4. If manifestation is impossible even when both conditions are applied, confirm it as a least-preferred role.
5. Register the given TR as a blank that cannot be resolved by managing internal members.
6. Propose either abandoning the TR or recruiting or replacing personnel who hold that TR as a natural role.

## Criteria
If manifestation is impossible even when both the person's maximum effort and others' support are applied, PASS (judged a least-preferred role); if a possibility of manifestation is confirmed once the conditions are applied, FAIL (reclassified as a potential/manageable role).
