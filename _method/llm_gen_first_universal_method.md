# The First LLM — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the target LLM is a general-purpose model supplied externally by big tech.
2. Check whether it is concentrated on the ability to generate natural sentences on the basis of huge corpus learning.
3. When the conditions are confirmed, classify it as the 'first LLM' (layer 4 of the architecture diagram).
4. Diagnose, together, whether hallucination occurs and the risk of security leakage (external storage of queries and data).

## Criteria
The determination of the 'first LLM' is regarded as complete when the conditions of external supply by big tech and concentration on natural sentence generation are confirmed, and the limits of hallucination and security leakage are recorded along with them.
