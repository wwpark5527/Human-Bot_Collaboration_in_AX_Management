# The Third LLM — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the target system has layers 1 through 4 fully in place (whether it has reached the level of the second LLM).
2. Check, on the basis of Chapter 8, whether layer 5 has additionally been made fully in place.
3. When layer 5 is confirmed as well, determine it to be the 'third LLM' (layers 1 through 5 fully in place); otherwise keep it at the stage of the 'second LLM' (layers 1 through 4).

## Criteria
It is determined by whether the architecture diagram is fully in place up to layer 5. It is distinguished from the sum of layers 1 through 4 (the second LLM) by the presence or absence of layer 5.
