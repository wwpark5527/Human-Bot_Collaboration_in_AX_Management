# Layers 1–2 (the domain of DX) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Distinguish whether the output handled by the target processing system is data, information, or knowledge.
2. If the output remains at the level of data and information (prior to knowledge-ization), classify it as layers 1–2 (the domain of DX).
3. If it has been converted to the level of knowledge, reclassify it as layer 3 or above.
4. Confirm whether the formation of layer 3 is under way, and together check the resulting effect of strengthening the quality of layers 1–2.

## Criteria
When the processing target remains at data and information and by itself cannot make AI execute, the judgment of layers 1–2 (the domain of DX) is regarded as complete.
