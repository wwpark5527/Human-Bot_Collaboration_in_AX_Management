# Layer 3 (knowledge chain based on common & governance context) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether information inside the organization is converted into knowledge by passing through a knowledge chain based on common context and governance context.
2. Inspect whether that knowledge chain is implemented inside the organizational AX OS.
3. If the knowledge chain has been formed, judge layer 3 to be secured; if it is insufficient, warn of the risk that connecting to layer 4 (an external LLM) alone will remain at the level of personal AX.
4. Evaluate whether synergy arises upon connection with layer 4, together with the level of knowledge-chain formation.

## Criteria
When a knowledge chain based on common and governance context has been formed inside the organization and information is converted into knowledge, the judgment that layer 3 is secured is regarded as complete.
