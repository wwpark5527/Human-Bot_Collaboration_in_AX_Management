# Layer 4 (the LLM already released to the world = 'the 1st LLM') — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the target LLM is a general-purpose LLM that big tech supplies externally and that is already out in the world.
2. Inspect whether that LLM corresponds to a general-purpose layer that accumulates, knowledge-izes, and provides the information of human society.
3. If the conditions are confirmed, classify it as layer 4 ("the 1st LLM").
4. Depending on whether it is linked with layer 3 (the knowledge chain), also diagnose the actual level of utilization (partial utilization vs. creation of synergy).

## Criteria
When the target LLM is confirmed to be a general-purpose LLM externally supplied by big tech and its level of utilization (whether it is linked with layer 3) is recorded together with it, the judgment of layer 4 ("the 1st LLM") is regarded as complete.
