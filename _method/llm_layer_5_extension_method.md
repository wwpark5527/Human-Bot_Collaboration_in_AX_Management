# Layer 5 (the layer added in Chapter 8) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm whether the target organization/system already has layers 1 through 4 (the level of the first LLM/the second LLM) in place.
2. Check whether layer 5 applies by comparing against the constituent requirements of layer 5 presented in Chapter 8.
3. Determine the case of having only layers 1 through 4 as an 'incomplete' state, and the case in which layer 5 has been added as a 'fully in place' state (the sum of layers 1 through 5 = the third LLM).
4. Upon a determination of being fully in place, move on to the detailed criteria of Chapter 8 and confirm the detailed composition.

## Criteria
The determination is regarded as complete when both whether layers 1 through 4 are satisfied and whether layer 5 is satisfied have been confirmed, and the result is explicitly recorded as 'incomplete' or 'fully in place (the third LLM)'.
