# Meaning/cognitive distance — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Observe the current conceptual structure of the person being evaluated through the knowledge chain (concept nodes, premise relations, reasoning steps, use of evidence, handling of counterexamples, self-correction, performance of transfer, change over time).
2. Define the target knowledge structure.
3. Measure each of the eight factors: conceptual structure, mastery, belief structure, premise relations, fidelity of the reasoning chain, evidence, transfer, and epistemic maturity.
4. Combine the eight factors and produce the meaning/cognitive distance value.
5. Repeat this procedure before and after AI intervention, and compare and compute the amount of distance reduction.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS if all eight factors (conceptual structure, mastery, belief structure, premise relations, fidelity of the reasoning chain, evidence, transfer, epistemic maturity) are measured and the distance value is produced, and FAIL if even one of them is not measured.
