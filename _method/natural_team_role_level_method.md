# Team/Natural Role — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Observe and measure the behavior types an individual actually shows when working alongside others within the team (for example, an Interplace diagnosis).
2. Confirm whether each behavior type comes out easily and naturally without help or conscious effort.
3. Classify the behavior types that meet the condition as that individual's natural roles (team roles).
4. Tally the number of natural roles per individual (commonly 2–3).
5. Gather the natural roles of all team members by the 9 TR types.
6. Confirm whether all 9 TR cells are filled and compute whether TRB is established and the teamwork level score.

## Criteria
PASS (judged a natural role) is decided by whether it is manifested easily and naturally without help or conscious effort. If it is not manifested naturally it is FAIL, and in that case whether it is a potential/manageable role or a least-preferred role must be judged separately.
