# Observer assessment (OA: observer assessment) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm four or more observers who know the person being measured well.
2. Distribute the OA checklist (45 positive adjectives and 27 negative adjectives) to each observer.
3. Proceed so that the observers mark the items relevant to the person being assessed (a checklist method, not a response to survey items).
4. Collect the responses of four or more observers and organize them into the Interplace input format.
5. Combine the OA (objective) and SPI (subjective) responses and pass them to the mixed relative-and-absolute evaluation processing.

## Criteria
PASS (a valid OA response) is decided by whether the response was filled out by four or more observers who know the person being measured well, in the checklist method. If there are fewer than four observers, or if it was filled out in the method where the person themselves responds to the items (the SPI method), it is judged FAIL (cannot be recognized as an OA, confused with the SPI).
