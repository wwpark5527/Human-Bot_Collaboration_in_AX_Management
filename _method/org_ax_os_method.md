# OS for organizational AX — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Distinguish whether the target system is at the level of a mere AI platform/chatbot integration tool or is an integral operating, control, and augmentation system.
2. Check item by item whether the six necessary conditions (operating protocols, privacy & sovereignty, AI governance, human-bot role architecture, agent orchestration, persistent organizational memory) are satisfied.
3. Check the level of satisfaction of the six additional conditions (common & governance context, knowledge chain, complementary fit engine, continuous learning & adaptation, provenance & verification, organizational digital twin).
4. Combine the satisfaction of the necessary conditions and the level of satisfaction of the additional conditions to determine whether the system is an OS for organizational AX and at what maturity.

## Criteria
Whether a system is an OS for organizational AX is determined by whether it goes beyond the level of a mere AI platform or chatbot integration tool and at least meets the necessary conditions. If it also meets the additional conditions, it is judged to be at the level of an AI-native organizational operating system (AI 4.0).
