# Organizational Change Adaptability — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Grasp the changes that have occurred in the areas of roles, authority, collaboration, and evaluation.
2. Check one's own attitude, whether one is reacting to those changes with refusal or trying to adapt.
3. Establish and execute concrete courses of action for adapting to the change.
4. After execution, check whether one remains a valuable presence within the organization and has not been marginalized.

## Criteria
If one has actually adapted to changes occurring rapidly at many scopes and levels and remains a valuable presence in the organization, it is judged PASS (adapted to organizational change). If one refuses change and signs of marginalization appear, it is judged FAIL (the adaptation plan needs to be re-established).
