# Organizational AX — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Survey the state of personal AX use within the organization, but separately diagnose the state of redesign of the organizational operating system so that it is not mistaken for organizational AX being under way.
2. Decide at the organizational level the level of AI's participation in decision-making, the locus of accountability (human/AI), changes in roles between departments, data access authority, and responsibility in the event of AI error.
3. Establish a plan that does not confine the scope of adoption to some departments but connects work data, structure, authority, evaluation, culture, and governance across the whole enterprise.
4. Execute the redesign of the decision-making structure, communication structure, power structure, and collaboration structure, and check whether it has spread enterprise-wide.

## Criteria
Execution of the method is determined to be complete when steps 1 through 4 have been carried out, organizational AX has spread enterprise-wide rather than remaining at the level of a pilot project (PoC), and technological innovation, organizational innovation, cultural innovation, and governance innovation are proceeding simultaneously.
