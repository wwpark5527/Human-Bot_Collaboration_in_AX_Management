# Common & Governance context — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. First confirm whether the target OS for organizational AX addresses the stage of the additional conditions.
2. Check whether the organization's data has been formed and implemented in the form of a common context.
3. Check whether the organization's rules have been formed and implemented in the form of a governance context.
4. Combine the levels of formation and implementation of the two contexts to determine whether an AI-native organizational operating system (AI 4.0) has been reached.

## Criteria
PASS (an AI-native organizational operating system, the level of true AI 4.0) is judged when both the common context and the governance context have been formed and implemented and the level of a mere AI platform has been surpassed.
