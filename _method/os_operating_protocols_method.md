# Operating protocols & control standards — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. First confirm whether operating protocols are embedded in the target OS for organizational AX.
2. Check whether the three items of authority & approval · role allocation · access control are designed.
3. Check whether the three items of exception handling · logging · accountability are designed.
4. Check whether the three items of the escalation system · human override · the audit trail are designed, and make the final judgment on the basis of whether all nine items are satisfied.

## Criteria
PASS is judged when all nine core elements (authority & approval, role allocation, access control, exception handling, logging, accountability, escalation, human override, audit trail) are in place and make AI act within the organization's norms.
