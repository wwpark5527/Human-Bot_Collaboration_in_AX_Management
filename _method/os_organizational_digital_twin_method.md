# Organizational digital twin(ODT) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Distinguish whether the target system is at the level of a mere organization chart or ERP system, or is a system that dynamically reproduces the organization's actual operation.
2. Check whether the organization's structure, roles, workflows, decision-making, collaboration, performance, and culture data are reflected in digital space.
3. Check whether real-time or near-real-time simulation, prediction, and optimization functions are working.
4. Confirm whether organizational experiments can be safely conducted on the ODT, and make the final judgment.

## Criteria
PASS (an ODT) is judged when, beyond the level of a mere organization chart or ERP system, the organization's actual way of working can be reproduced, simulated, predicted, and optimized in real time or near-real time.
