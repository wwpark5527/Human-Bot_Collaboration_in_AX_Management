# Privacy & sovereignty — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the list of external LLMs (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and the like) to which the organization connects.
2. Check whether internal data and context can leak out along each external LLM connection path.
3. Apply blocking measures to the paths where a possibility of leakage has been confirmed.
4. Finally confirm whether the benefits of leveraging external AI and the preservation of organizational sovereignty have been achieved at the same time.

## Criteria
PASS is judged when the possibility of internal information leaking through the connection with external LLMs has been identified and blocked and the organization's sovereignty is preserved.
