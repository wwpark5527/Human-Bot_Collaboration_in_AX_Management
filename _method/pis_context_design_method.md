# Context design — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Explicitly organize the organization's purposes and criteria.
2. Convert them into conditions that AI can execute (purpose and criteria).
3. Mediate so that the deliverables of the knowledge chain are delivered to the world model according to these conditions.
4. Review whether the results of AI execution stay within the organization's purposes and criteria.

## Criteria
PASS is judged when the AI execution conditions (purpose and criteria) are made explicit and the results of AI execution operate within the organization's purposes and criteria; FAIL is judged when the conditions are absent or the results depart from the criteria.
