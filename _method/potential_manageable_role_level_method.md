# Potential/Manageable Role — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Identify the candidate team roles that the individual does not exercise naturally (the items that do not belong to the natural roles in the diagnostic results).
2. Assess whether there is a possibility of exercise if the person applies conscious effort and self-management.
3. Confirm whether the surrounding colleagues are in a position to understand and accept that behavior type.
4. When both conditions (self-management effort + colleagues' understanding and acceptance) hold, confirm it as a potential/manageable role.
5. Reflect the confirmed potential roles in the calculation of the team's maximum potential teamwork level.
6. Establish an intervention plan that combines colleagues' acceptance and encouragement with the person's own effort, and pursue promotion to a natural role.

## Criteria
PASS (judged a potential/manageable role) when the role can be exercised once the two conditions of self-management effort and colleagues' understanding and acceptance are both met; FAIL (reclassified as a least-preferred role) when it cannot be exercised even though both conditions are met.
