# AI predictive intelligence — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Check whether the current use of AI remains at question answering and the generation of documents, images, and code.
2. Confirm whether AI is used for the prediction of future states and the results of actions.
3. Check whether the technologies that enable predictive intelligence (the world model, the knowledge chain, context design) have been introduced.
4. Shift the direction of AI use from generation-centered to prediction-centered.

## Criteria
PASS (predictive intelligence recognized) is judged when the use of AI also includes the prediction of future states and the results of actions, and FAIL (generation-centered, not yet shifted) when it remains only at question answering and generation.
