# Right to AI access — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Define, for each job role, the AI tools and infrastructure needed to perform the work.
2. Survey the employees' current status of access to AI tools and infrastructure.
3. Calculate the access rate by department and job role and identify the areas with large access gaps.
4. Implement measures that expand access to AI tools and infrastructure in the departments and job roles where access gaps have been confirmed.
5. Re-measure the access rate and the access gaps across departments regularly and confirm whether there has been improvement.

## Criteria
PASS is judged when the opportunity to access the AI tools and infrastructure needed to perform work is provided to all employees and the access gaps across departments are continuously managed; FAIL is judged when the access gaps are left unattended.
