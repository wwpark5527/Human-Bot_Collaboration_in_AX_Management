# Right to AI appeal — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Identify, among the tasks in which AI evaluation, recommendation, or decision intervenes, those that may become subject to appeal.
2. Design the intake channel and reception procedure for reconsideration requests.
3. Establish the procedure and criteria for reviewing the reconsideration requests received.
4. Implement correction and remedy measures according to the results of the review.
5. Record the reconsideration requests and their handling results and check the effectiveness of the procedure regularly.

## Criteria
PASS is judged when a procedure to demand reconsideration of AI evaluations, recommendations, and decisions is open and requests actually lead to correction and remedy; FAIL is judged when there is no reconsideration procedure or it exists only in form.
