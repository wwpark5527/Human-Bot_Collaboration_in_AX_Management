# Right to AI benefit-sharing — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Compare productivity indicators such as workload, processing time, and deliverable quality before and after the introduction of AI, and calculate the productivity gains attributable to AI.
2. Analyze the accrual structure of where the calculated gains currently accrue (capital owners, labor-cost savings, reinvestment, and the like).
3. Establish the distribution criteria for the gains — specify the proportions for each item of compensation, education, welfare, and reinvestment in organizational capability.
4. Reflect the distribution criteria in the compensation system and in the education and welfare budget allocation, and execute the actual reinvestment.
5. Measure the reinvestment rate regularly and check its distribution across departments and job groups, and correct the distribution criteria when skew is confirmed.

## Criteria
PASS when the AI productivity gains are calculated, their distribution criteria are specified, they are reinvested into compensation, education, and welfare, and the reinvestment rate is measured and verified. FAIL is judged when the gains are not calculated, or when, even if calculated, they are concentrated in the hands of only some owners and not distributed to members.
