# Right to AI explanation — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Identify the tasks in which the AI result directly affects the party.
2. Document the AI evaluation criteria for each task and organize them in a form that even a non-specialist can understand.
3. Design a standard procedure for providing the party with an explanation of the AI decision.
4. Operate the explanation-provision procedure and record the history of provision.
5. Confirm the party's degree of understanding and improve the manner of explanation.

## Criteria
PASS is judged when the AI result and its evaluation criteria are explained in a way the party can understand; FAIL is judged when no explanation is provided or it is provided in an incomprehensible form.
