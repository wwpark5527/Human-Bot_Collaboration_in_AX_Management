# Right to AI judgment — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Survey the whole range of tasks in which AI outputs intervene and identify the tasks that require important judgment.
2. Define, for each identified task, the purpose, meaning, and criteria that humans must provide.
3. Design the approval procedure so that no final decision is made without human approval.
4. Operate the approval procedure and record the judgment criteria that humans provided.
5. Check the status of approval implementation regularly and confirm that the human final decision-making authority is maintained.

## Criteria
PASS is judged when, in important judgments, humans provide the purpose, meaning, and criteria and retain the final decision-making authority; FAIL is judged when AI outputs lead to final decisions without human approval.
