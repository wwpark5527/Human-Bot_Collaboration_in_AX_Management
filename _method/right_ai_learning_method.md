# Right to AI learning — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Define the required level of AI literacy for each job and diagnose the gap against current capability.
2. Design a basic AI literacy education curriculum.
3. Identify personnel whose jobs are expected to change and provide reskilling programs.
4. Provide upskilling programs for personnel already using AI.
5. Regularly measure course completion rates and capability improvement to improve the programs.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when AI literacy education and reskilling/upskilling opportunities are continuously provided to employees and the capability gap narrows, and FAIL when education opportunities are not provided or the gap is left unaddressed.
