# Right to AI transition — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Review the AI introduction plan and identify in advance the personnel and areas where job change is expected.
2. Establish a role redesign plan suited to the changing job.
3. Design the transition training program required for the role redesign.
4. Provide the transition training and carry out transition placement into the redesigned role.
5. Check the state of adaptation after transition placement and provide additional support when necessary.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when role redesign and transition training are provided and transition placement is carried out as a job changes due to the introduction of AI, and FAIL when the job change is left unaddressed and takes place without transition support.
