# Right to AI utilization — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Survey the current modes of AI use by department and job and classify them as repetitive-work-replacing or capability-expanding.
2. Select capability-expanding use cases and measure their work improvement effects.
3. Identify areas skewed toward the replacement of repetitive work and establish plans to redesign them into capability-expanding forms.
4. Apply the redesign plan and re-measure the results.
5. Share and spread capability-expanding use cases across the whole organization.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when AI is used for the purpose of expanding human capability rather than replacing repetitive work and the resulting work improvement is confirmed, and FAIL when AI use stays confined to the replacement of repetitive work.
