# Accountability judge — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Judge whether the result will be used for internal reference only.
2. Judge whether it may be delivered to a customer.
3. Judge whether it may be reflected in decision-making.
4. Confirm whether a superior's approval is required.
5. Confirm whether legal/security review is required.
6. Decide whether a record must be kept.
7. Confirm whether the locus of accountability is clear if a problem arises.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS (determination of scope of use and locus of accountability complete) when all seven items — is it for internal reference only, may it be delivered to a customer, may it be reflected in decision-making, is a superior's approval required, is legal/security review required, must a record be kept, and is the locus of accountability clear — have been determined. If even one is unclear, it is judged FAIL (accountability judgment needs supplementation).
