# Role — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Collect the patterns of contribution that are repeatedly confirmed.
2. Define those patterns of contribution as functional positions rather than titles.
3. Judge which of people, AI, and systems can take on that functional position.
4. Even if the role is distributed across several actors, state the locus of ultimate accountability as the human or the organization.

## Criteria
If the position of contribution is defined as a functional position rather than a particular individual's title and the locus of ultimate accountability is clear it is judged PASS (role structure secured), and if it is fixed to a title or the locus of accountability is unclear it is judged FAIL (role not structured).
