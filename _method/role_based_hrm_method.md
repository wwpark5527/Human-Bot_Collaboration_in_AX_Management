# RBHRM (Role-Based HRM, competence + role-based) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Diagnose where current HR management stands among position, control, and CBHRM.
2. Design role definitions and management elements based on autonomy and commitment.
3. Establish an RBHRM transition plan and introduce it in stages.
4. Verify the effect of the transition in connection with the TRB measurement system (Interplace and the like).

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the focus of HR management has shifted to role and autonomy/commitment. It is judged FAIL when it still remains at position, control, and seniority/competence (CBHRM).
