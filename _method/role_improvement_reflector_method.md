# Improvement reflector — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Review the results of the AI usage.
2. Discover errors and omissions.
3. Analyze the causes.
4. Correct the prompt.
5. Reinforce the context.
6. Update the checklist.
7. Improve the deliverable schema.
8. Reflect it into the next task.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS (improvement reflection complete) when a failure is not left to end as an individual mistake but is connected to improving the organization's criteria, and FAIL (improvement reflection insufficient) when the failure is handled only as an individual problem and is not reflected into the organization's criteria.
