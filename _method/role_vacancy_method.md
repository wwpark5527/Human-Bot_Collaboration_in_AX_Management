# Role vacancy — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Divide the communication participants into A (augmented: human + AI + common context) and B (non-augmented: the human alone).
2. Confirm whether the four roles on side A (purpose and judgment · analysis and generation · criteria · history management) are in place.
3. Check whether the interpretation, verification, recording, and response roles are lacking on side B.
4. When a deficiency is confirmed, judge it as role vacancy and record it as a fairness problem.

## Criteria
If, due to the absence of AI and common context on side B, one or more of interpretation, verification, recording, and response is empty, role vacancy is judged PASS (role vacancy determined to exist); if all four roles are also filled on side B, it is judged FAIL (no role vacancy).
