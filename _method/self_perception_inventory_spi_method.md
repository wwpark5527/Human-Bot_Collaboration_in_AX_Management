# Self-perception inventory (SPI) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the person being measured and distribute the SPI questionnaire.
2. Proceed so that the person responds directly to the survey items (the person's own responses, without the intervention of others).
3. Collect the responses and organize them into the Interplace input format.
4. Prepare for the collection of observer assessment (OA) responses on the same person and their combination.
5. Combine the SPI (subjective) and OA (objective) responses and pass them on for mixed relative/absolute evaluation processing.

## Criteria
Whether it is PASS (a valid SPI response) is decided by whether the response was written directly by the person being measured through a survey-item response format. If someone other than the person responded, or it was written in a checklist format (the OA format), it is judged FAIL (cannot be recognized as an SPI, confused with an OA).
