# Realization of Augmentation — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Check whether management decisions (introduction of bots/AI, organizational design) are replacement-oriented or amplification-oriented.
2. If judged to be replacement-oriented, apply the management principle so that they are redesigned into a positive-sum structure.
3. Confirm the current state of forming augmented humans (AH) and augmented bots (AB) and supplement the deficient domains.
4. Check whether the realization of augmentation operates together with respect for and use of diversity and the pursuit of complementary fit — check the balance of the trinity of spirits.
5. Confirm whether the perception that "AI strengthens me" has formed among members, and make the final determination of whether the spirit of augmentation has been realized.

## Criteria
When management decisions follow a positive-sum structure, augmented humans (AH) and augmented bots (AB) are actually formed, and they operate together with the other two spirits (respect for diversity, complementary fit), a PASS determination of the realization of augmentation is made. If a zero-sum structure persists or it is disconnected from the other spirits, a FAIL determination is made.
