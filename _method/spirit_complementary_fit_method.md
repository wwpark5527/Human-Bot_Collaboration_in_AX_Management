# Pursuing Complementary Fit — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Check whether the same work is merely being divided between humans and bots, or whether their respective strengths are being combined.
2. If the question being asked is "who is more superior?", shift the question to "how will we complement each other?"
3. Map the strengths of humans and bots in each of the four domains — cognitive, emotional, behavioral, and ethical — and differentiate the roles.
4. In domains that require ethical judgment, always combine the bot's rule-based consistency with the human's value judgment and social responsibility.
5. Measure whether the organization's overall performance has been maximized as a result of role differentiation, and judge whether complementary fit has been realized.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS as pursuing complementary fit when, on the basis of role differentiation rather than competition, the essential strengths of humans and bots are connected and the organization's overall performance is maximized. It is judged FAIL if it is a mere division of the same work or a structure of competition over superiority.
