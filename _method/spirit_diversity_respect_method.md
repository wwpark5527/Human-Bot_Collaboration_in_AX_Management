# Respecting and Leveraging Diversity — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Extend the object of diversity within the organization to include both the differences within humans (generation, background, etc.) and the essential differences between humans and bots, and list them.
2. Check whether those differences are being stratified into superior and inferior or into good and bad.
3. Design a role-diversity arrangement that makes the fullest use of the existence and utility value of each member (human and bot).
4. Find the areas where diversity is being treated as a factor of conflict and coordination cost, and carry out interventions that reinterpret and convert it into an augmentation asset.
5. Measure whether collective intelligence and organizational sustainability have improved as a result of using diversity.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS as respecting and leveraging diversity when the attributes of difference are accurately distinguished without stratification and the utility value of each member is made the fullest use of, leading to improved collective intelligence and performance. It is judged FAIL if differences are stratified into superior and inferior or treated only as a factor of conflict.
