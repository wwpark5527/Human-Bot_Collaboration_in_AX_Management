# 1. AI Impact Assessment — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Specify the tasks, organizations, and scope targeted for AI introduction and settle the boundary of the impact assessment.
2. Assess the labor impact — confirm job changes, replacement risk, and workload redistribution before introduction.
3. Assess the judgment impact — distinguish which judgments are transferred to AI and which judgments remain with humans.
4. Assess the authority impact — confirm how access, approval, and decision authority move.
5. Assess the customer impact — confirm the customer touchpoints and the consequences for customers.
6. Assess the human-rights impact — confirm the risks of surveillance, discrimination, and exclusion.
7. Synthesize the results of the five areas, document them as the AI impact assessment result, and hand them over as design input to the subsequent steps (2. build context capital, 3. authority design, 4. labor transition, 5. human approval criteria).

## Criteria
PASS if, **before** the introduction of AI, the impacts in all five areas of labor, judgment, authority, customers, and human rights are assessed and the results are documented and handed over to the subsequent steps. It is judged FAIL if assessment is made only after the fact following introduction, or if some of the five areas are omitted. (ESG linkage: S / G)
