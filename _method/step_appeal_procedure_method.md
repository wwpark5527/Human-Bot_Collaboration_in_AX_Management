# 6. Appeal Procedure — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Take the output of step 5, human approval criteria (the list of tasks requiring human approval and the approval criteria), as input, and settle the scope of AI results that are subject to appeal.
2. Design the path for explanation requests — designate the channel through which the party concerned can demand an explanation of the grounds and judgment criteria of the AI result, the responding party, and the response deadline.
3. Design the path for re-review requests — define the method and requirements of intake, the party performing the re-review (including whether it is separated from the original judge), and the processing deadline.
4. Determine the method of processing corrections and remedies — state how the result is corrected when the objection is upheld and how the resulting disadvantage is restored.
5. Designate the points at which the history of intake, processing, and closure of appeals remains, and connect them to the subsequent step, audit record.
6. Put in place protective provisions so that no disadvantage arises on the ground that an appeal was raised, and give notice of the entire procedure in a form the party concerned can know.

## Criteria
PASS if a structure for requesting each of the three — **explanation, re-review, and correction** — of AI results is established, the intake path, the processing party, and the processing deadline are designated, and the processing results remain as history. It is judged FAIL if a channel is missing for even one of the three requests, or if there is a channel but no processing party or deadline is set, so that a request can lapse without a response.
