# 7. Audit Record — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Define the five kinds of preservation objects — prompts, materials, results, corrections, approval history. For each kind, state at the item level what must be included in the record.
2. Designate the point at which the record of each kind is generated and the method of collection. Design it so that records remain automatically within the workflow rather than depending on manual entry by the person in charge.
3. Set the output-artifact identifier and the linking rules so that the five kinds of records for a single output artifact are bound into a single history.
4. Determine the retention period, storage location, access rights, and method of tamper prevention.
5. Incorporate the approval history of step 5, human approval criteria, and the intake and processing history of step 6, appeal procedure, into this record system, so that the preceding steps become verifiable after the fact.
6. Determine the method of calculating the record preservation rate and calculate it periodically to find the gaps where records are missing.

## Criteria
PASS if **all five kinds** — prompts, materials, results, corrections, and approval history — are preserved, can be reconstructed as the history of a single output artifact, and the retention period, access rights, and tamper prevention are determined. It is judged FAIL if even one of the five kinds has no collection point, or if records remain but are not linked at the output-artifact level so that history reconstruction is impossible.
