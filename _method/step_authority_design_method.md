# 3. Authority Design — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. List separately the AI resources (models, agents, services), data resources, and tools the organization operates.
2. Classify the sensitivity and scope of impact of the data and tools to create the basis for differentiating the strength of control.
3. Referring to the per-task context capital built in step 2, map which tasks and roles require each resource.
4. Set, for each resource, the actors that can access it (roles, jobs, organizational units) — complete the cross-table of the resource axis and the actor axis.
5. Document the results of the setting as the access-authority design so that the locus and boundaries of authority can be confirmed.
6. Determine the procedures for granting and revoking authority, and connect them to operations so that authority is updated upon personnel transfers and job changes.

## Criteria
PASS if the accessing actors are set for all three kinds of resources — AI, data, and tools — the results are documented as the access-authority design, and the granting and revoking procedures are connected to operations. It is judged FAIL if only some of the resource kinds are set, or if, even when set, they are not documented so that the locus of authority cannot be confirmed. (ESG linkage: G)
