# 8. Benefit Distribution — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Take the AI work history secured by step 7, audit record, as input, identify the productivity gains originating from the introduction of AI, and settle the method of calculation.
2. Make the four reinvestment targets concrete — education (literacy and reskilling), compensation (reflection in member compensation), welfare (improvement of working conditions and welfare), organizational capability (accumulation of context capital, tools, and infrastructure).
3. Determine the mode of distribution and the distribution ratio for each target and record the grounds for them.
4. Designate the method of securing funds, the executing party, and the execution cycle so that the distribution is actually carried out.
5. Put in place a procedure for checking whether the distribution results are skewed toward particular job groups or strata, and adjust the distribution ratios if skew is confirmed.
6. Calculate the reinvestment record and connect it so that the basis for the calculation is secured from the record system of step 7. Hand the calculation results and the remaining problems over to step 9, the improvement loop.

## Criteria
PASS if the AI productivity gains are identified and calculated, the mode and ratio of distribution are determined for **all four targets — education, compensation, welfare, and organizational capability** — and the funds, executing party, and cycle are designated so that the reinvestment record is produced. It is judged FAIL if the distribution is arbitrary for lack of a basis for calculating the gains, or if only some of the four targets are dealt with, or if only a distribution policy is declared and, without funds or an executing party, nothing is carried out.
