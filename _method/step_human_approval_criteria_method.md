# 5. Human Approval Criteria — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Take the roles and division of work reconfigured in step 4, labor transition, as input, and enumerate by type the outputs and decisions in which AI is involved.
2. Determine for each item whether human intervention is needed — apply as determination criteria the size of the impact, whether it can be reversed, external legal effect, and relevance to human rights and safety.
3. Settle the items determined to require human intervention as the list of tasks requiring human approval, and record the grounds for the determination alongside them.
4. Write the approval criteria for each item — state what the approver checks and on what grounds the approver approves or rejects.
5. Designate the approving party and the moment of approval for each item (immediately after production, before external dispatch, etc.).
6. Determine the points at which approval history remains so that omissions of approval can be detected, and connect them to the subsequent step, audit record.

## Criteria
PASS if the outputs and decisions requiring human intervention are **explicitly** enumerated and the approval criteria, approving party, and moment of approval for each item are documented. It is judged FAIL if whether a human intervenes is left to discretionary judgment at the moment a matter arises, or if a list exists but there are no approval criteria so that it is not known what must be checked.
