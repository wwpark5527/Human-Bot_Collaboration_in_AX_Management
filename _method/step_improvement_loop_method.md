# 9. Improvement Loop — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Settle the collection paths for errors and feedback — take as input the correction history of step 7, audit record, the re-review and remedy processing results of step 6, appeal procedure, the approval omissions of step 5, and the skew-checking results of step 8, benefit distribution.
2. Categorize the collected items — distinguish whether each is a one-off mistake, a defect in the criteria themselves, or a problem in the design of authority and procedures. Different types have different points of reflection.
3. Determine the portion to be reflected into the common context — designate what among purpose, criteria, sources, roles, formats, and verification standards must be fixed so that it does not recur (the return line back to step 2, building context capital).
4. Determine the portion to be reflected into the governance criteria — designate what must be fixed among authority design (step 3), human approval criteria (step 5), appeal procedure (step 6), and audit record (step 7).
5. Settle the determination results as a revision of the criteria — determine the revising party, the revision cycle, and the way the revision history is recorded.
6. Hand the updated criteria over as **the starting condition of the next cycle**. Leave the items decided not to be reflected together with the reasons so that they can be re-examined in the next cycle.

## Criteria
PASS if it is determined, for **both the common context and the governance criteria**, whether the confirmed errors and feedback are to be reflected, and the reflected portion is settled as an actual revision of the criteria that becomes the starting condition of the next cycle. It is judged FAIL if errors and feedback are merely collected and it is not determined which criteria are to be fixed, or if the determination is made but there is no revising party so that the criteria are not actually updated. Settling individual matters as they arise and leaving the criteria untouched is not fulfillment of this step. (ESG linkage: G)
