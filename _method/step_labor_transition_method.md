# 4. Labor Transition — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Conduct a job impact assessment at a point **before** the introduction of AI — identify, job by job, which tasks are transferred to AI and which tasks remain with people.
2. Specify the affected jobs and the personnel concerned, and record the size of the impact (the proportion of tasks transferred, the nature of the remaining tasks).
3. Calculate the capability gap for each job and design a reskilling plan — include the targets, content, duration, and completion standards.
4. Bundle the tasks remaining after transfer to AI with the newly arising tasks, reconfigure the roles, and revise the job descriptions.
5. Design a transition placement plan into the reconfigured roles.
6. Calculate the transition-placement rate to confirm whether the design led to actual placement, and design supplementary measures for the personnel not yet transitioned.

## Criteria
PASS if a job impact assessment is conducted **before** the introduction of AI and, on the basis of its results, a reskilling plan and role reconfiguration are designed. It is judged FAIL if the impact assessment is postponed until after introduction, or if only the impact assessment is conducted and it does not lead to the design of reskilling and role reconfiguration.
