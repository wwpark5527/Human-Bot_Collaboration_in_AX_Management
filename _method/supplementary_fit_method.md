# Supplementary Fit — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Check whether the teamwork and unity activities within the organization are based on homogeneity and similarity.
2. Examine whether those activities actually contribute to departmental and organizational performance.
3. Diagnose whether those activities are working as the sole ground of performance and thereby blocking respect for diversity (difference).
4. If a limit is found, design follow-up measures that expand awareness into respecting diversity and pursuing complementary fit.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the positive function of homogeneity-based activities is maintained while it does not exclude respecting diversity and pursuing complementary fit. It is judged FAIL if supplementary fit becomes entrenched as the sole ground of performance and diversity is excluded.
