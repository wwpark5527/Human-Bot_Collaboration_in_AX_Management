# Systems Thinking — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. List the elements that make up the AX organization: humans, AI agents, governance, workflow, culture, ESG, security, and context.
2. Grasp the connections among the elements and draw them as a complex system structure.
3. Check whether the matter under review stays at the level of partial optimization, and whether the alternative proposed by AI is merely partially optimal.
4. Integrate value conflicts, ethics, long-term direction, organizational culture, and social ripple effects into a whole to make a judgment.
5. Determine the direction on the basis of the integrative judgment (determination of direction, not generation of correct answers).

## Criteria
If one does not stay at partial optimization but integrates value conflicts, ethics, long-term direction, organizational culture, and social ripple effects into a whole and determines the direction, it is judged PASS (systems thinking secured). If one stays at partial optimization, it is judged FAIL (systems thinking needs re-examination).
