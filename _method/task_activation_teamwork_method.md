# Task-Activation Aspect of Teamwork — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Fix the department/team to be measured.
2. Obtain each individual member's TR analysis result through Interplace.
3. Gather the TR analysis results of all team members and compute team role balance (TRB).
4. Organize the TRB result as the indicator of the task-activation aspect of teamwork.
5. Combine it with the result of the human bonding aspect (group cohesiveness) to produce a comprehensive diagnosis of the department's teamwork.

## Criteria
Whether the measurement targets the degree to which the 9 team roles are exercised within the team (TRB) and corresponds to the complementary fit side decides PASS (valid diagnosis of the task-activation aspect of teamwork). If the measurement targets mental attraction among members or supplementary fit (confusion with the human bonding aspect), it is judged FAIL.
