# Team Role Balance (TRB) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Diagnose each team member's level of exercise of the 9 team roles (natural / latent / non-preferred).
2. Map, from the whole-team perspective, whether all 9 team-role types exist at the natural-role level.
3. Identify the team roles that are missing or that remain at the non-preferred level.
4. Supplement the missing roles through reassignment of team members, coaching on role exercise, or recruitment.
5. After the supplementation, re-confirm whether TRB has been achieved and measure the level of teamwork.
6. Confirm the correlation between the level of teamwork and team performance indicators, and thereby verify the causal chain TRB → Teamwork → Team Performance.

## Criteria
If all 9 team-role types exist within the team at the natural-role level, it is judged PASS (TRB established); if even one is missing or falls short of the natural-role level, it is judged FAIL (TRB not established, supplementation needed).
