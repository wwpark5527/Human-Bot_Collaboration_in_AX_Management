# Team Role (TR) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the member's official functional role (job-based).
2. Observe the behavior the member informally exercises within the team.
3. Classify the observed behavior on the criterion of behavior type (team role) rather than personality type.
4. Distinguish, per individual, the team roles that are exercised easily and naturally, the latent team roles that can be exercised with effort, and the team roles that cannot be exercised.
5. Contrast the functional role with the team role to confirm that they are separate dimensions.
6. Feed the diagnostic results into the team-level team role balance (TRB) determination.

## Criteria
If the diagnosed role is confirmed to be an informal behavior type with no job relation (non-job-related) and is clearly distinguished from the functional role, it is judged PASS (determined to be a team role); if it is not distinguished from the official role assigned by the job, it is judged FAIL (confusion with the functional role, re-diagnosis needed).
