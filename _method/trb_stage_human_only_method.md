# Human-only TRB — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Confirm the list of the team's role actors.
2. Check whether the role actors are all human members and whether any bot is included.
3. Limit the scope of the role balance analysis to a human + human team.
4. Produce human role balance on the basis of a job-based organization, static roles, and individual capability.

## Criteria
If the object of role balance is limited to a human + human team, no bot has entered as a role actor, and the human role balance output has the characteristics of a job-based organization, static roles, and individual capability, it is judged PASS; if a bot is confirmed as a role actor, it is judged FAIL (reclassified into the next stage, Bot-aided TRB).
