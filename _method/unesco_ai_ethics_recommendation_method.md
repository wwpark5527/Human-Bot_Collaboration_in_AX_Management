# UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI (2021) — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Compare the four values and ten principles of the original UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI against the existing ethical system of organizational AX.
2. Reflect the four values (human rights, freedoms, and dignity; the environment and ecosystems; diversity and inclusiveness; peaceful, just, and interconnected societies) in organizational policy documents.
3. Map the ten principles, from proportionality and do no harm through multi-stakeholder governance collaboration, item by item onto the organization's practical guidelines.
4. Establish concrete policy actions in the areas of education, culture, gender equality, and governance, and draw up a plan for aligning the legal system.
5. Periodically check the state of implementation and operate a reporting system.

## Criteria
If, grounded in international human rights law, the emphasis is placed on resolving global inequality and protecting the environment and the system of four values and ten principles is actually in place, it is judged PASS (reflection of the UNESCO Recommendation confirmed); otherwise it is judged FAIL (UNESCO Recommendation not reflected).
