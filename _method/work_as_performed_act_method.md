# Work — Method

## Method / Procedure
1. Specify the performed act (e.g. writing a sentence, summarizing material, reviewing).
2. Confirm only whether that act actually took place.
3. Do not judge whether the result of the act produced a change, and pass it on to a separate stage (contribution judgment).
4. Leave the fact of the act's performance as a record.

## Criteria
If the act was actually performed it is judged PASS (fact of performance confirmed), and if the act itself did not take place it is judged FAIL (not performed). Whether the result of the act led to a change is not the object of this judgment.
