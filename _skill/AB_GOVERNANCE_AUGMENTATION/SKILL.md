# Governance Augmentation — Skill

## Purpose
Have the bot's operation take place within structures of authority, verification, approval, recording, and accountability, thereby satisfying the governance axis among the three augmentation dimensions an augmented bot (AB) must possess.

## Input
- The scope of work the bot will perform
- The organization's system of authority, verification, approval, recording, and accountability structures

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Define the authority required for the bot's scope of work.
2. Design and apply verification and approval procedures for the work results.
3. Leave the work process and results as records.
4. Confirm that a structure able to trace accountability when a problem arises is operating.

## Output
Work results that are approved and recorded within a governance structure.

## Criteria
If it is confirmed that the bot's operation took place within structures of authority, verification, approval, recording, and accountability, it is judged PASS (governance augmentation confirmed); otherwise it is judged FAIL (governance augmentation not confirmed).
