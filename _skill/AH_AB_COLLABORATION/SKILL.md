# Collaboration between Augmented Humans and Augmented Bots — Skill

## Purpose
Connect a human's purpose, judgment, and accountability with a bot's analysis, execution, and recording capabilities safely and accountably, creating more refined and meaningful results and organizational assets reusable in the next task.

## Input
- The organization's purpose / work standards / reference materials / formats / prohibitions
- Governance rules (access authority / approval procedures / recording rules)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Form the common context (convey purpose, work standards, reference materials, formats, and prohibitions).
2. Build the governance context (define access authority, approval, recording, and accountability rules).
3. The human provides the bot with purpose and standards.
4. The bot performs analysis, execution, and recording and presents the results to the human.
5. Verify the results and connect purpose, standards, materials, judgment, revision, approval, and recording, accumulating them as organizational assets.

## Output
A common context document, a governance context system, and role-based collaboration deliverables (reusable organizational assets).

## Criteria
If all three conditions — sharing the same context, collaborating within an accountability structure, and dividing roles while augmenting each other — are confirmed, it is judged PASS (collaboration established); if even one is lacking, it is judged FAIL (risk of mere tool-use or an accountability vacuum).
