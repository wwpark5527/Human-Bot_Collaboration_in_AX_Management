# Learning Augmentation (Learning A.) — Skill

## Purpose
Measure whether the use of AI actually raises a member's learning speed, and thereby confirm the third indicator of whether the augmented human (AH) has been realized.

## Input
- Learning records without the use of AI (the baseline)
- Learning records with the use of AI

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Record the learning time in the state without AI use.
2. Record the learning time in the state with AI use.
3. Compare the amount of knowledge acquired over the same period in the two states.
4. Combine the reduction in learning time and the increase in the amount of knowledge acquired and judge whether learning augmentation has occurred.

## Output
A comparison table of learning time, a comparison table of the amount of knowledge acquired, and the learning augmentation judgment result.

## Criteria
If learning time has decreased and the amount of knowledge acquired has increased after the use of AI, it is judged PASS (learning augmentation confirmed); otherwise it is judged FAIL (learning augmentation not confirmed).
