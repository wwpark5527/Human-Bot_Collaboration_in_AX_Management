# Role Augmentation (Role A.) — Skill

## Purpose
Measure whether, through the use of AI, a member has actually come to perform a new role they could not perform before, and thereby make the final judgment of whether they are an augmented human (AH).

## Input
- Records of the range of roles before the use of AI
- Records of the range of roles after the use of AI

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Record the range of roles before the use of AI.
2. Record the range of roles after the use of AI.
3. Compare the two ranges and confirm whether role expansion has occurred.
4. On the basis of the confirmed role expansion, judge whether role augmentation (the final AH judgment) holds.

## Output
A comparison table of the range of roles, a list of role expansion cases, and the role augmentation (final AH) judgment result.

## Criteria
If the member can now perform a role they could not perform before, it is judged PASS (role augmentation confirmed); otherwise it is judged FAIL (role augmentation not confirmed).
