# Mental Augmentation — Skill

## Purpose
Raise a human's thinking, learning, analysis, creativity, and judgment by using AI and technology, thereby satisfying the mental axis among the three augmentation dimensions an augmented human (AH) must possess.

## Input
- The current state of the mental capacity to be improved (thinking / learning / analysis / creativity / judgment)
- Applicable ways of using AI and technology

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm the mental capacity targeted for improvement (thinking, learning, analysis, creativity, judgment).
2. Apply a method of using AI and technology that raises that capacity.
3. Measure and compare the change in the capacity level after application.
4. Re-confirm whether the improved capacity is reflected in actual judgment and work performance.

## Output
Heightened thinking, learning, analysis, creativity, and judgment.

## Criteria
If it is confirmed that the target capacity among thinking, learning, analysis, creativity, and judgment has actually been raised through the use of AI and technology, it is judged PASS (mental augmentation confirmed); otherwise it is judged FAIL (mental augmentation not confirmed).
