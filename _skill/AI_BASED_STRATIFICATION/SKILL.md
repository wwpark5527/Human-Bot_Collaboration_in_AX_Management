# AI-Based Stratification (AI Class Society / AI-Based Stratification) — Skill

## Purpose
Diagnose the risk of AI-based stratification and produce input for the design of inclusive transformation AX.

## Input
- Materials on the organization's current state of AI access, utilization, and decision authority

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Investigate the differences in access to AI models, data, compute, platforms, organizational context, utilization capability, and decision authority.
2. Discriminate whether the access differences accumulate and result in ownership, direction, verification, and use by a few versus evaluation, control, replacement, and exclusion of the many.
3. Identify which of the seven gap types (access, capability, context, judgment authority, surveillance, ownership, profit-sharing) is especially pronounced.
4. Connect the diagnostic result to the design of inclusive transformation AX.

## Output
AI-based stratification diagnostic result (risk level by gap type).

## Criteria
If the accumulation of access differences leads to a structure of ownership, direction, verification, and use (a few) / evaluation, control, replacement, and exclusion (the many), it is determined PASS (risk confirmed); otherwise FAIL.
