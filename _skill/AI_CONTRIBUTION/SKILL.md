# AI Contribution — Skill

## Purpose
According to the formula AI contribution = reduced meaning/cognitive distance / AI-usage cost, determine how efficiently AI intervention actually narrowed the human's understanding distance.

## Input
- Measured value of meaning/cognitive distance before AI intervention
- Measured value of meaning/cognitive distance after AI intervention
- The AI-usage cost of the relevant period and task

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Measure the meaning/cognitive distance before and after AI intervention respectively.
2. Calculate the difference between the two values (the reduced meaning/cognitive distance).
3. Tally the AI-usage cost.
4. Divide the reduced meaning/cognitive distance by the AI-usage cost to produce the AI contribution.

## Output
AI contribution value (= reduced meaning/cognitive distance / AI-usage cost).

## Criteria
If the meaning/cognitive distance before and after AI intervention and the AI-usage cost are all secured so that the ratio is produced, it is determined PASS; if even one is missing, it is determined FAIL.
