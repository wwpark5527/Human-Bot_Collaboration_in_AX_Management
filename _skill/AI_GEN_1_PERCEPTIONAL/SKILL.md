# AI 1.0 (Perceptional AI) — Skill

## Purpose
Discriminate, on the basis of the AI generational-classification table, whether the target AI is at the perception/recognition-type (Perceptional AI) stage.

## Input
- List of the actual operations of the target AI system/function
- AI generational-classification table (function definitions for AI 1.0–4.0)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the target AI perceives, identifies, and classifies external input.
2. Check the presence or absence of generative, autonomous-execution, and organization-operating functions.
3. If all three functions are absent, classify it as AI 1.0 (Perceptional AI).
4. If even one is present, reclassify it into the corresponding higher generation (AI 2.0/3.0/4.0).

## Output
AI generation discrimination result, diagnostic report on the need for transition to a higher generation.

## Criteria
If the discrimination result accurately conforms to the table's standard (perception/identification/classification only vs. generative/autonomous-execution/organization-operating), it is determined PASS.
