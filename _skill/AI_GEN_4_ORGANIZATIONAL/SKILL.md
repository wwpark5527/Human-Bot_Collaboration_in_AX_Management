# AI 4.0 (Organizational AI) — Skill

## Purpose
Discriminate, on the basis of the AI generational-classification table, whether the target organization's AI utilization has reached the organizational-type (Organizational AI) stage, that is, the 'true AI 4.0 level'.

## Input
- The target organization's AI operating system (operating unit, current state of context implementation)
- AI generational-classification table (function definitions for AI 1.0–4.0)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the operating unit of AI utilization is the individual agent (Agentic) or the operation of the whole organization.
2. Check whether common context and governance context have been formed and implemented.
3. Distinguish whether it is at the level of a mere AI platform or at the level of an AI-native organizational operating system.
4. If both expansion to the whole organization and the implementation of both contexts are confirmed, determine it to be the 'true AI 4.0 level'.
5. If even one is insufficient, determine it as not reached and specify the deficient area.

## Output
Determination result on whether AI 4.0 (organizational type) has been reached, diagnostic report on organization-scale operational redesign.

## Criteria
If the discrimination result accurately conforms to the table's standard (expansion of the operating unit to the whole organization + implementation of common and governance context), it is determined PASS.
