# AI Governance — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether AI governance (the system of control) is making governance context (the judgment-standard context) operate verifiably and accountably within organizational operation.

## Input
- List of prohibition rules, accountability rules, audit systems, and approval systems concerning AI use
- Current state of governance context translated into work procedures, roles, systems, and recording structures

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the order of AI use (prohibition, accountability, audit, approval systems) is organized as norms, institutions, and an operating system.
2. Confirm whether AI governance (A) and governance context (B) are distinguished as different levels.
3. Confirm whether B is actually translated into and reflected as work procedures, roles, systems, and recording structures.
4. Determine whether the mode of control remains at prohibition, surveillance, and after-the-fact audit or is realized as operation, standardization, and connected operation.

## Output
Determination result on the operational order that lets AI work verifiably and accountably within the organization.

## Criteria
If governance context actually operates within work procedures, roles, systems, and recording structures, it is determined PASS; if control remains only at prohibition, surveillance, and after-the-fact audit, it is determined FAIL.
