# AI Literacy and Affinity — Skill

## Purpose
Discriminate the subject's level of AI literacy and affinity, identify the deficient items (understanding of strengths and weaknesses, understanding of hallucination, prompt use, understanding of AI workflow, attitude toward AI as a collaboration tool), and provide a direction for supplementation.

## Input
- The subject's history of AI utilization and self-diagnosis result of level of understanding
- Cases of verifying AI output and writing prompts

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the subject understands AI's strengths and weaknesses with discrimination.
2. Confirm whether the subject recognizes the phenomenon of hallucination and verifies output.
3. Check the subject's prompt-use ability and level of understanding of AI workflow and generative AI.
4. Confirm the attitude of not fearing AI and handling it as a collaboration tool.
5. Propose supplementation through learning and practice for the insufficient items.

## Output
Determination result of the level of AI literacy and affinity and a list of items requiring supplementation.

## Criteria
If the subject has all of the understanding of AI's strengths, weaknesses, hallucination, prompts, and workflow and the attitude toward AI as a collaboration tool, it is determined PASS (AI literacy and affinity secured); if even one is insufficient, it is determined FAIL (further learning required).
