# AX (AI Transformation) — Skill

## Purpose
Diagnose and define the organization's change efforts from the perspective of AX (AI Transformation), and design the integration scope proper to AX as distinguished from DX (workflows, judgment systems, role structures, output systems, authority structures, accountability structures).

## Input
- The organization's current change attempt/strategy documents
- AX-era question checklist (5 axes: work, data, roles, verification, authority)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Apply the change attempt to the table of DX-era questions and AX-era questions to identify which axis it remains at.
2. In light of AX's objective position (global/Asian expressions, conceptual origin, mainstream purposes, lacking areas), mark the organization's current position.
3. Derive supplementary tasks for the lacking areas (inclusive transformation, prevention of AI stratification, benefit sharing, the right of appeal, the human right of judgment).
4. Draw up a redesign plan for human roles and responsibilities at each AI integration point (workflows, judgment systems, role structures, output systems, authority structures, accountability structures).

## Output
AX position definition document, results of the AX-era question checklist, list of supplementary tasks for lacking areas, role and responsibility redesign plan.

## Criteria
It is determined PASS when the deliverables go beyond DX-level questions and explicitly address AI's intervention in judgment, generation, execution, evaluation, and accountability structures.
