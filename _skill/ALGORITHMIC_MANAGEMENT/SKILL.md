# Algorithmic Management — Skill

## Purpose
Grasp the scope of application of an algorithmic system that uses tracking data and information to organize, assign, monitor, supervise, and evaluate work, and secure control of authority, transparency, remedy procedures, and the accountability structure.

## Input
- The current state of the organization's systems related to work assignment, performance evaluation, surveillance, reward, and discipline, and whether AI and algorithms are used

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Identify the points of algorithm and AI involvement in the areas of work assignment, performance evaluation, surveillance, schedule management, reward, discipline, and productivity measurement.
2. Specify who holds AI evaluation authority at each point (G: control of authority).
3. Specify who is subject to AI evaluation at each point (S: workers' rights).
4. Check whether the AI evaluation criteria can be explained (G: transparency).
5. Design the human appeal procedure (S/G: remedy procedure).
6. Designate the ultimate responsible party for each point (G: accountability structure).

## Output
List of the points of algorithmic management application, document answering the five questions (G/S mapping), appeal procedure document, document designating the responsible parties.

## Criteria
If documents and procedures able to answer all five questions (control of authority / workers' rights / transparency / remedy procedure / accountability structure) are in place, it is determined PASS; if even one is left unattended, it is determined FAIL.
