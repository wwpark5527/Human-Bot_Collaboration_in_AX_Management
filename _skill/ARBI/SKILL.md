# Augmented Role Balance Index (ARBI, Augmented Role Balance Index) — Skill

## Purpose
Measure together and index role balance, utterance agency, authority boundaries, human accountability, communication fairness, and recordability in AI-intervened human-human collaboration.

## Input
- Records of collaboration and communication in which AI intervened
- Agency distinction information for each utterance and output (human/AI/joint)
- Information on AI's authority to access materials and roles

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Collect cases of AI-intervened collaboration.
2. Distinguish the agency of utterances and outputs (human/AI/joint).
3. Confirm the scope of AI's access authority.
4. Confirm whether the final judgment and responsibility are attributed to the human.
5. Measure role balance, utterance agency, authority boundaries, human accountability, communication fairness, and recordability.
6. Aggregate the measurements to produce the ARBI index.

## Output
The ARBI index aggregating role balance, utterance agency, authority boundaries, human accountability, communication fairness, and recordability.

## Criteria
If all six elements are measured and aggregated into a single index, it is determined PASS; otherwise it is determined FAIL.
