# Communication Fairness — Skill

## Purpose
It compares each member's level of AI augmentation against their voice and influence over decision-making, determines whether the AI-augmentation gap has been converted into a relational power difference, and reveals the risk of communication asymmetry.

## Input
- Information on the level of AI augmentation (tool accessibility and proficiency of use) of each participating member
- Records of voice and influence over decision-making in meetings, document work, and the like

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Survey each member's level of AI augmentation.
2. Observe each member's voice and influence over decision-making.
3. Compare the correlation (skew) between the level of augmentation and voice and influence.
4. Determine whether the AI-augmentation gap is fair and record the risk of communication asymmetry.

## Output
A judgment on the fairness of the AI-augmentation gap and a list of communication asymmetry risks.

## Criteria
If the AI-augmentation gap has not turned into a relational power difference, it is judged PASS; if a skew in voice and influence is confirmed, it is judged FAIL.
