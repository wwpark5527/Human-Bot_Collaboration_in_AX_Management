# Complementary Fit — Skill

## Purpose
It compares the respective strengths of the human and the AI participating in the collaboration and determines whether the strengths complement each other without overlap or gap.

## Input
- Records of the collaboration under evaluation
- Information on the strengths the human exercised
- Information on the strengths the AI exercised

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm the strengths the human exercised.
2. Confirm the strengths the AI exercised.
3. Compare the two sets of strengths to identify areas of overlap and gap.
4. Determine whether the strengths are complementary.

## Output
A judgment on whether human and AI strengths complement each other.

## Criteria
If the strengths complement each other without overlap or gap, it is judged PASS; otherwise it is judged FAIL.
