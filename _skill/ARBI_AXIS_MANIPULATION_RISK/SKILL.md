# Manipulation Risk — Skill

## Purpose
It classifies the instances of AI use by purpose, checks the indications of securing strategic advantage and of coercion, and determines whether AI was misused.

## Input
- Records of the instances of AI use during the collaboration process
- Material on indications related to information asymmetry and coercion

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Collect the instances of AI use.
2. Classify the purpose of use as facilitating collaboration vs securing advantage and applying coercion.
3. Check the indications of coercion and threat.
4. Determine whether AI was misused (for strategic advantage or coercion).

## Output
A judgment on whether AI was misused (for strategic advantage or coercion).

## Criteria
If AI was not used for strategic advantage or coercion rather than for collaboration, it is judged PASS; if indications of misuse are confirmed, it is judged FAIL.
