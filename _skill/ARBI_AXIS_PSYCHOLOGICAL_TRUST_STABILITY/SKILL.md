# Psychological & Trust Stability — Skill

## Purpose
It surveys members' felt surveillance and alienation, the accumulation of decision fatigue, and any concentration of the burden of responsibility, and determines the level of psychological and trust burden.

## Input
- Survey material on felt surveillance and alienation per member
- Records of decision fatigue and burden of responsibility in judgment situations involving AI intervention

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Survey the felt level of surveillance and alienation per member.
2. Confirm whether decision fatigue has accumulated.
3. Check whether the burden of responsibility is concentrated.
4. Determine the level of psychological and trust burden.

## Output
A judgment on the level of psychological and trust burden on members.

## Criteria
If feelings of surveillance, alienation, decision fatigue, and burden of responsibility are not excessive, it is judged PASS; if even one of them is confirmed to be at an excessive level, it is judged FAIL.
