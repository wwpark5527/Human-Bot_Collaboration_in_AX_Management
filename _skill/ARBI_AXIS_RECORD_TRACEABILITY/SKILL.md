# Record & Traceability — Skill

## Purpose
It collects the history of materials created, revised, and approved during the collaboration process and verifies traceability, thereby determining whether the collaboration history is recorded and re-verifiable.

## Input
- A list of the supporting materials created during the collaboration process
- Revision histories, approval records, and the list of materials used

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Collect the supporting materials, revision histories, approval records, and materials used.
2. Confirm the time of creation, the actor, and the rationale for each item.
3. Identify the items whose history is missing or untraceable.
4. Determine whether the collaboration history is recorded and re-verifiable.

## Output
A judgment on whether the collaboration history is recorded and re-verifiable.

## Criteria
If the history of rationale, revisions, approvals, and materials used all remains, it is judged PASS; if even one of them is missing or untraceable, it is judged FAIL.
