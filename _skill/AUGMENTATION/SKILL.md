# Augmentation — Skill

## Purpose
Identify whether the use of AI and bots within the organization follows the augmentation principle — mutual learning and co-evolution in which humans and bots coexist and grow each other's capabilities — and distinguish it from replacement-oriented design.

## Input
- The design purpose of introducing bots/AI (replacement vs amplification)
- The current state of the human augmentation domains (physical/mental) and the bot augmentation domains (context/value/learning)
- Whether it is zero-sum (one side gains at the other's loss) or positive-sum (both sides grow)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the purpose of introducing bots is to replace human labor or to amplify human capability.
2. Determine whether the structure is zero-sum or positive-sum.
3. Confirm whether the elements of human augmentation (physical, mental) and bot augmentation (context, value, learning) are included in the design.
4. When the conditions are satisfied, determine it to be augmentation, and aim at forming augmented humans (AH) and augmented bots (AB).

## Output
Determination result of whether augmentation obtains, plan for forming augmented humans (AH) and augmented bots (AB).

## Criteria
When bots are designed as an amplifier of human capability and positive-sum outcomes are confirmed, a PASS determination of augmentation is made.
