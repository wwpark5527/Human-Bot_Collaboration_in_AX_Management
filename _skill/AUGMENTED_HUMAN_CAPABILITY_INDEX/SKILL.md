# Augmented Human Capability Index (AHCI) — Skill

## Purpose
It produces AHCI from the five elements human augmentation degree, bot cooperation degree, human-bot alignment degree, output fidelity, and rate of improvement, and connects it to the HBRM capability diagnosis and to education and development plans.

## Input
- Diagnostic data on the human augmentation degree
- Diagnostic data on the bot cooperation degree and the human-bot alignment degree
- Diagnostic data on output fidelity and the rate of improvement

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Diagnose the human augmentation degree.
2. Diagnose the bot cooperation degree.
3. Diagnose the human-bot alignment degree.
4. Diagnose output fidelity.
5. Diagnose the rate of improvement and sum the five elements to produce AHCI.

## Output
The AHCI diagnostic result and an HBRM-linked capability diagnosis report.

## Criteria
If all five elements are diagnosed and it is confirmed that "this human is actually being augmented while working with this bot", it is judged PASS; if an element is missing or alignment degree or fidelity falls short, it is judged FAIL (further development needed).
