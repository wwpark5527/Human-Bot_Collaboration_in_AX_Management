# Human-Centered Path — Skill

## Purpose
It constructs the human-centered path in AH-AH communication situations where meaning preservation matters more than speed and automation and determines whether it is suitable.

## Input
- The communication situation in question (message exchange between augmented humans A and B)
- Whether meaning preservation is the priority

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the preservation of meaning, emotion, and trust is the priority.
2. Set the structure in which the human speaks and judges directly (AI A ↔ Human A ↔ Human B ↔ AI B).
3. Limit the AI to the roles of organizing, analyzing, reviewing, and drafting.
4. Confirm whether the result is effective for meaning preservation.

## Output
The construction of a meaning-preserving AH-AH communication path (the human-centered path).

## Criteria
If the agent of utterance and judgment is the human and meaning, emotion, and trust relationships are preserved, it is judged PASS; otherwise it is judged FAIL.
