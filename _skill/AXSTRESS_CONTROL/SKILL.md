# Control Stress — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether the stress of a member of an AX organization corresponds to control stress among the four dangerous stresses, and provide the grounds for a response that redefines the scope of control.

## Input
- Records of stress expressions and statements observed in decision-making situations
- Observed context related to control (authority imbalance, expansion of automated decision-making, etc.)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the core question in the expression of stress is 'who actually controls?'.
2. Distinguish whether the cause is not a matter of trust but the ambiguity of the locus of control.
3. Confirm whether there are signs of a demand for redefining the scope of control.
4. Synthesize the confirmed characteristics and judge whether it is control stress.

## Output
The result of the judgment as to whether the target situation corresponds to control stress, and the priority for redefining the scope of control.

## Criteria
If the core question is 'who actually controls?' and the cause lies in the ambiguity of the locus of control, it is judged PASS (control stress); otherwise it is judged FAIL (another type).
