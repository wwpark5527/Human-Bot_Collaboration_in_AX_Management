# Identity Stress — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether the stress of a member of an AX organization corresponds to identity stress among the four dangerous stresses, and provide the grounds for a response that supports the redefinition of role and value.

## Input
- Records of the member's stress expressions and statements
- Observed context of changes in work and role (AI adoption, role restructuring, etc.)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the core question in the expression of stress is 'why am I needed?'.
2. Confirm whether the cause is not workload but uncertainty about the reason for existence and the value of the role.
3. Confirm whether there are signs of a demand for redefining role and value.
4. Synthesize the confirmed characteristics and judge whether it is identity stress.

## Output
The result of the judgment as to whether the target member corresponds to identity stress, and the priority for supporting the redefinition of role and value.

## Criteria
If the core question is 'why am I needed?' and the cause is uncertainty about the reason for existence and the value of the role, it is judged PASS (identity stress); otherwise it is judged FAIL (another type).
