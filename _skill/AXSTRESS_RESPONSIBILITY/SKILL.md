# Responsibility Stress — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether a stress of an AX organization member corresponds to responsibility stress among the four dangerous stresses, and provide the grounds for a response that redefines the scope of responsibility.

## Input
- Records of stress expressions and utterances observed in situations where an outcome has occurred
- Observed context related to the attribution of responsibility (performance evaluation, identification of the cause of failure, etc.)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the core question in the expression of stress is 'who takes responsibility?'.
2. Distinguish whether the cause is not the problem of the locus of control but the unclearness of outcome attribution itself.
3. Confirm whether there are signs of a demand for redefining the scope of responsibility.
4. Synthesize the confirmed characteristics and judge whether it is responsibility stress.

## Output
The judgment result of whether the target situation corresponds to responsibility stress, and the priority for redefining the scope of responsibility.

## Criteria
If the core question is 'who takes responsibility?' and the cause lies in the unclearness of outcome attribution, judge PASS (responsibility stress); otherwise judge FAIL (another type).
