# Trust Stress — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether the stress of a member of an AX organization corresponds to trust stress among the four dangerous stresses, and provide the grounds for a response that designs trust-based cooperation.

## Input
- Records of stress expressions and statements observed in human-bot interaction
- Observed context related to trust (loss of a sense of control, misreading of intent, authority imbalance, etc.)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the core question in the expression of stress is 'can I trust the other party?'.
2. Distinguish whether the cause is the human-bot interaction relationship or a problem at the level of the individual member.
3. Confirm whether there are signs of a demand for the design of trust-based cooperation.
4. Synthesize the confirmed characteristics and judge whether it is trust stress.

## Output
The result of the judgment as to whether the target interaction corresponds to trust stress, and the priority for the design of trust-based cooperation.

## Criteria
If the core question is 'can I trust the other party?' and the cause lies in the human-bot interaction relationship, it is judged PASS (trust stress); otherwise it is judged FAIL (another type).
