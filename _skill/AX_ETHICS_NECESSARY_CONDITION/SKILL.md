# Ethical Necessary Conditions of Organizational AX — Skill

## Purpose
Protect the existence of organizational AX by identifying and fulfilling the ethical necessary conditions which, if not kept, drive organizational AX to failure.

## Input
- Documents on the operating status and policies of organizational AX
- The seven necessary-condition standards (guaranteeing human dignity, humans' ultimate accountability, explainability, protection of personal and organizational information, prevention of bias and discrimination, safety and reliability, building an AI governance system)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Make the seven items — guaranteeing human dignity, humans' ultimate accountability, explainability, protection of personal and organizational information, prevention of bias and discrimination, safety and reliability, and building an AI governance system — concrete in a way that fits the organization's situation.
2. For each item, analyze the concrete path (legal, social and operational risk) by which organizational AX heads toward failure when it is not complied with.
3. Establish and document the AI governance system at the level of the operating constitution of organizational AX.
4. Build a system that regularly checks compliance with the seven necessary conditions.
5. Set the priority of resource allocation by distinguishing the necessary conditions from the additional conditions.

## Output
The mandatory compliance line of guaranteeing human dignity, humans' ultimate accountability, explainability, protection of personal and organizational information, prevention of bias and discrimination, safety and reliability, and building an AI governance system (AI governance corresponds to the operating constitution of organizational AX).

## Criteria
If all seven necessary conditions are fulfilled and no risk of organizational AX failure due to non-compliance is found, it is judged PASS (ethical necessary conditions met); if even one is not complied with and a risk of failure is confirmed, it is judged FAIL (violation of the ethical necessary conditions).
