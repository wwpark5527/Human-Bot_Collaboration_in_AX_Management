# AX Organization — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether the organization has reached the state of an AX organization (the result), and design the human-bot coexistence and collaboration operating system.

## Input
- Materials on the progress of the organizational AX redesign
- Materials on organizational structure, roles, and responsibilities

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Check whether each organizational AX redesign item is complete.
2. Finalize the human-bot collaboration structure, role, and accountability system.
3. Establish the governance for maintaining the AX organization state.

## Output
AX organization completion determination report, human-bot collaboration structure diagram, governance system.

## Criteria
It is determined PASS when the redesign of the organizational operating system is confirmed as a result and human-bot coexistence and collaboration is actually in operation.
