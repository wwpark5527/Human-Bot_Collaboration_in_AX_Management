# AI Governor — Skill

## Purpose
Establish the AI governance of an AX organization by clarifying the locus of AI accountability and managing AI behavior so that it is aligned with the organization's goals and ethical standards.

## Input
- A list of the AI decision points and outputs within the organization
- Documents of the organization's goals and ethical standards

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Grasp the AI decision points and the scope of outputs.
2. Designate the locus of accountability for each AI decision and output.
3. Establish a procedure for checking whether AI behavior and outputs are aligned.
4. Operate the accountability and alignment management system and continuously update it.

## Output
A document defining the locus of AI accountability, and an AI accountability and alignment management system.

## Criteria
If both the locus of accountability for AI and the alignment management procedure are confirmed, it is judged PASS; otherwise it is judged FAIL.
