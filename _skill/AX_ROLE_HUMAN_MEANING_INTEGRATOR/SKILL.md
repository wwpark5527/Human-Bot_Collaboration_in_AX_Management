# Human Meaning Integrator — Skill

## Purpose
Fill the gap in meaning between AI results and organizational reality, and provide decision-making with an interpretation connected to the organizational context.

## Input
- The analysis, recommendation and prediction outputs produced by AI
- Information on the organization's current goals, strategy and stakeholder situation

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Collect the outputs produced by AI.
2. Confirm the organization's goals, strategy and stakeholder context.
3. Interpret the meaning and implications by placing the AI results into that context.
4. Deliver the interpretation to decision-makers and the relevant departments and reflect their feedback.

## Output
An interpretation of AI results connected to the organizational context.

## Criteria
If an interpretation of AI results connected to the organizational context is produced and reflected in decision-making, it is judged PASS; otherwise it is judged FAIL.
