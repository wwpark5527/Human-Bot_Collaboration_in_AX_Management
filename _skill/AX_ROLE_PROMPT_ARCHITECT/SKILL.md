# Prompt Architect — Skill

## Purpose
Design standards for human-AI interaction by type of work so as to raise the accuracy of AI use and reduce rework.

## Input
- The status of AI use by type of work and recurring cases of interaction failure
- The organization's guidelines for work design and standardization

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Identify, by type of work, the intent, context and constraints that must be conveyed to AI.
2. Design the identified elements into a structured interaction format.
3. Document the designed patterns as standards and templates.
4. Verify the results of application and improve the design.

## Output
A design for human-AI interaction (prompt standards/templates by type of work), and records of the verification of interaction effects.

## Criteria
If a design for human-AI interaction is established and the effect of its actual application is confirmed, it is judged PASS; otherwise it is judged FAIL.
