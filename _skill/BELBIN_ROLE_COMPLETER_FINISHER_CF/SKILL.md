# Completer-Finisher (CF) — Skill

## Purpose
By accurately identifying those who hold the Completer-Finisher (CF) team role and deploying them as those in charge of AI hallucination auditing in an AX organization, secure the completeness and reliability of AI outputs.

## Input
- TR (Interplace) diagnostic data for each member
- The history of errors and hallucination occurring in AI outputs

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Grasp, through TR diagnosis, the degree to which each individual exhibits the Completer-Finisher disposition.
2. Evaluate the contribution of error elimination and completion against the criteria.
3. Map them to the AI hallucination auditing role in the context of an AX organization, and design the error checking procedure.

## Output
Results of the Completer-Finisher (CF) role diagnosis, a plan for assigning AI hallucination auditing tasks, and a design of the error checking procedure.

## Criteria
If the mode of contribution is error elimination and completion, it is judged PASS; otherwise it is judged FAIL.
