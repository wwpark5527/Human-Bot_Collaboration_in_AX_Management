# Coordinator (CO) — Skill

## Purpose
By accurately identifying those who hold the Coordinator (CO) team role and deploying them as the human-AI workflow orchestrator of an AX organization, secure the function of coordinating the flow of work between members and AI bots.

## Input
- TR (Interplace) diagnostic data for each member
- Material on the current state of the human-AI collaboration workflow

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Grasp, through TR diagnosis, the degree to which each individual exhibits the Coordinator disposition.
2. Evaluate the contribution to the coordination of members and tasks against the criteria.
3. Map and deploy them to the human-AI workflow orchestrator role in the context of an AX organization.

## Output
Results of the Coordinator (CO) role diagnosis, and a plan for assigning human-AI workflow orchestrator tasks.

## Criteria
If the mode of contribution is the coordination of members and tasks, it is judged PASS; otherwise it is judged FAIL.
