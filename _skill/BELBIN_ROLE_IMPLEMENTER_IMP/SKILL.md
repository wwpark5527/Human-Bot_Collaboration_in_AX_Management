# Implementer (IMP) — Skill

## Purpose
By accurately identifying holders of the Implementer (IMP) team role and placing them in charge of the establishment of AI operational processes in the AX organization, secure a human-AI division-of-work system for the implementation function.

## Input
- TR (Interplace) diagnostic data for each member
- A list of repetitive execution tasks that AI bots are performing or could perform

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Use TR diagnostics to grasp the degree to which each individual exercises the Implementer disposition.
2. Evaluate the contribution of implementation (actual execution) against the criteria.
3. In the AX organization context, map the person to the role of establishing AI operational processes and design the division of work with AI bots.

## Output
Implementer (IMP) role diagnostic results, a task assignment plan for the establishment of AI operational processes, and the human-AI division-of-work design.

## Criteria
If the mode of contribution is implementation (actual execution), it is judged PASS; otherwise FAIL.
