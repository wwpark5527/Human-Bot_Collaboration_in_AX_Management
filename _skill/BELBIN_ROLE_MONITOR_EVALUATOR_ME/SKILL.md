# Monitor-Evaluator (ME) — Skill

## Purpose
By accurately identifying holders of the Monitor-Evaluator (ME) team role and placing them as AI-output verifiers in the AX organization, secure a human-AI division-of-work system for the analysis/judgment function.

## Input
- TR (Interplace) diagnostic data for each member
- A list of analytical tasks that AI bots are performing or could perform

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Use TR diagnostics to grasp the degree to which each individual exercises the Monitor-Evaluator disposition.
2. Evaluate the contribution of cool-headed analysis and judgment against the criteria.
3. In the AX organization context, map the person to the role of AI-output verifier and design the division of work with AI bots.

## Output
Monitor-Evaluator (ME) role diagnostic results, an AI-output verification task assignment plan, and the human-AI division-of-work design.

## Criteria
If the mode of contribution is cool-headed analysis and judgment, it is judged PASS; otherwise FAIL.
