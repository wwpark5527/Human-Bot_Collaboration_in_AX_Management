# Plant (PL) — Skill

## Purpose
By accurately identifying holders of the Plant (PL) team role and placing them as AI-enabled innovation designers in the AX organization, secure the function of producing creative ideas within the team.

## Input
- TR (Interplace) diagnostic data for each member
- A list of tasks within the team that require the production of creative ideas

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Use TR diagnostics to grasp the degree to which each individual exercises the Plant disposition.
2. Evaluate the contribution of producing creative ideas against the criteria.
3. In the AX organization context, map and place the person in the role of AI-enabled innovation designer.

## Output
Plant (PL) role diagnostic results and a task assignment plan for AI-enabled innovation design.

## Criteria
If the mode of contribution is producing new conceptions and ideas, it is judged PASS; otherwise FAIL.
