# Resource Investigator (RI) — Skill

## Purpose
By accurately identifying holders of the Resource Investigator (RI) team role and placing them in charge of AI-ecosystem exploration in the AX organization, secure a human-AI division-of-work system for the external exploration function.

## Input
- TR (Interplace) diagnostic data for each member
- A list of information-seeking tasks that AI bots are performing or could perform

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Use TR diagnostics to grasp the degree to which each individual exercises the Resource Investigator disposition.
2. Evaluate the contribution of external exploration against the criteria.
3. In the AX organization context, map the person to the role of AI-ecosystem exploration and design the division of work with AI bots.

## Output
Resource Investigator (RI) role diagnostic results, an AI-ecosystem exploration task assignment plan, and the human-AI division-of-work design.

## Criteria
If the mode of contribution is external exploration, it is judged PASS; otherwise FAIL.
