# Shaper (SH) — Skill

## Purpose
By accurately identifying holders of the Shaper (SH) team role and placing them as change drivers of the AX organization, secure the driving force of the AX transformation.

## Input
- TR (Interplace) diagnostic data for each member
- The current state of the stalled and resistant stretches of the AX transformation process

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Use TR diagnostics to grasp the degree to which each individual exercises the Shaper disposition.
2. Evaluate the contribution of exercising driving force against the criteria.
3. In the AX organization context, map the person to the role of AX change driver and deploy them into the stalled stretches of the transformation.

## Output
Shaper (SH) role diagnostic results, an AX change driver task assignment plan, and a transformation acceleration plan.

## Criteria
If the mode of contribution is exercising driving force, it is judged PASS; otherwise FAIL.
