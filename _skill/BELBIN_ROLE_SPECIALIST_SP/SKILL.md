# Specialist (SP) — Skill

## Purpose
By accurately identifying holders of the Specialist (SP) team role and placing them as domain + AI hybrid experts of the AX organization, secure expertise that combines a domain of expertise with the capability to use AI.

## Input
- TR (Interplace) diagnostic data for each member
- The current state of each member's domain expertise and capability to use AI tools

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Use TR diagnostics to grasp the degree to which each individual exercises the Specialist disposition.
2. Evaluate the contribution of providing expert knowledge against the criteria.
3. In the AX organization context, map the person to the role of domain + AI hybrid expert and design the expertise combination plan.

## Output
Specialist (SP) role diagnostic results, a domain + AI hybrid expert task assignment plan, and an expertise combination plan.

## Criteria
If the mode of contribution is providing expert knowledge, it is judged PASS; otherwise FAIL.
