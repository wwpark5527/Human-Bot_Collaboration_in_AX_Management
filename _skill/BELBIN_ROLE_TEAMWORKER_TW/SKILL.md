# Teamworker (TW) — Skill

## Purpose
By accurately identifying holders of the Teamworker (TW) team role and placing them in charge of promoting acceptance of human-AI collaboration in the AX organization, raise members' emotional acceptance of AI collaboration.

## Input
- TR (Interplace) diagnostic data for each member
- Data on the current state of members' acceptance of and resistance to collaborating with AI bots

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Use TR diagnostics to grasp the degree to which each individual exercises the Teamworker disposition.
2. Evaluate the contribution of relational harmony and atmosphere-building against the criteria.
3. In the AX organization context, map the person to the role of promoting acceptance of human-AI collaboration and design activities that promote emotional acceptance.

## Output
Teamworker (TW) role diagnostic results, a task assignment plan for promoting acceptance of human-AI collaboration, and the activity design.

## Criteria
If the mode of contribution is relational harmony and atmosphere-building, it is judged PASS; otherwise FAIL.
