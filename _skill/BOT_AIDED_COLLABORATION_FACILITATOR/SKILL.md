# Collaboration Facilitator — Skill

## Purpose
Observe and sense the friction arising in the team collaboration process and adjust it so that the collaboration flow runs smoothly.

## Input
- Speaking records from meetings and collaboration sessions
- The team's role assignment and decision-making history

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Collect and analyze meeting speaking-time data.
2. Detect signals of conflict between roles.
3. Detect communication bottlenecks (obstacles).
4. Share the sensing results with the team and support coordination.

## Output
The results of meeting speaking-time analysis, role-conflict detection, and communication-bottleneck (obstacle) detection.

## Criteria
If the results of meeting speaking-time analysis, role-conflict detection, and communication-bottleneck detection are produced and shared with the team, it is judged PASS; otherwise FAIL.
