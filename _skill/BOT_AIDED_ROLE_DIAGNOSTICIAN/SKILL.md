# Role Diagnostician — Skill

## Purpose
Diagnose the state of roles within the human team so as to detect role vacancies and role concentration early and support the team leader's improvement of role balance.

## Input
- Work performance data for each team member
- Belbin role classification information for each team member

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Collect the team members' work performance data and role classification information.
2. Analyze the shortage of a particular role (for example, a shortage of the Monitor-Evaluator ME).
3. Analyze the excess of a particular role (for example, an excess of the Plant PL).
4. Detect increased conflict between roles (for example, conflict among Shapers SH).
5. Report the diagnostic results to the team leader.

## Output
In-team role diagnostic results.

## Criteria
If the diagnostic results for role vacancy, role concentration, and role conflict are produced and delivered to the team leader, it is judged PASS; otherwise FAIL.
