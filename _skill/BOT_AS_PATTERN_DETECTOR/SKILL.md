# The Bot as a Giant Pattern Detector — Skill

## Purpose
Human organizational members understand the bot accurately not as a conscious thinking subject but as a pattern detector, a probability-based prediction system, and a contextual generation engine, and design its role by distinguishing the bot's strengths from its limitations.

## Input
- The list of tasks the bot is performing or is scheduled to perform
- Bot outputs (pattern, prediction, and generation results)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Classify the list of tasks the bot is performing or is scheduled to perform into pattern discovery, prediction, and generation functions and meaning, value, and purpose assignment functions.
2. Design the tasks that fall under pattern discovery, prediction, and generation functions so as to make the fullest use of the bot's strengths (speed, mass processing, repetition optimization).
3. Exclude tasks that require the assignment of meaning, value, and purpose, ethical judgment, or a locus of accountability from those the bot performs alone, and design the procedure so that they pass through human review and decision.
4. Prepare verification procedures (temporal precedence, control of third variables) so that the bot's pattern and correlation findings are not mistaken for causation.

## Output
Bot work function classification table, strength and limitation mapping, human-bot role division design.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS (understanding of the bot as a pattern detector reflected) when the bot's work is concentrated in its strength areas and human involvement is confirmed in its limitation areas, and FAIL (understanding of the pattern detector not reflected) otherwise.
