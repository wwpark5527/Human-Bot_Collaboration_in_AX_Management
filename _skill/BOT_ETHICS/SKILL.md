# The ethics of bots — Skill

## Purpose
Establish a system of ethical standards that every member of the AX organization (humans and bots) must observe, so that the process of realizing organizational AX proceeds safely and justly.

## Input
- Literature on external AI ethical standards such as UNESCO, OECD, the EU, IEEE, the Korean government, and Claude's constitution
- The operating status of organizational AX and the list of members (humans plus bots)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Collect and compare external AI ethical standards such as UNESCO, OECD, the EU AI Act, IEEE, the Korean government, and Claude's constitution.
2. Divide the principles and norms to be applied to organizational AX into ethical necessary conditions and additional conditions.
3. Apply the necessary conditions (guaranteeing human dignity, ultimate responsibility, explainability, information protection, prevention of bias and discrimination, safety and reliability, AI governance) to every member, including bots, without exception.
4. Pursue the additional conditions (an augmentation-centered philosophy, respect for diversity, complementary fit, establishment of a common and governance context) in stages from the standpoint of raising organizational competitiveness.
5. Periodically check whether the necessary conditions have been violated and the degree to which the additional conditions have been implemented.

## Output
A system of ethical standards that organizational AX must observe — the implementation results of the ethical necessary conditions and additional conditions.

## Criteria
If the ethical necessary conditions apply to every member, including bots, without exception, it is judged PASS (necessary conditions satisfied); if even one is violated, it is judged FAIL (necessary condition violated — risk of organizational AX failure).
