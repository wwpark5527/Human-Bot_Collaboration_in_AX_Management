# Complementation of TR by bots — Skill

## Purpose
For each of the 9 Belbin TRs, reinterpret the elements the bot can complement in an AX organization, and design the human-bot division of roles as a structure of mutual complementation rather than replacement, thereby expanding overall organizational performance (pie size).

## Input
- The 9 Belbin TR types and the TR diagnostic results of each team member
- The current state of bot/AI use within the organization and the list of repetitive, computational, and exploratory work

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm the traditional meaning of each of the 9 TRs.
2. Map each TR onto the AX-organization reinterpretation table.
3. Distinguish the elements the bot will perform from the elements the human will retain.
4. Distribute roles as a structure of complementation rather than replacement, and confirm the effect of expanded performance.

## Output
The AX-organization reinterpretation table for the 9 TRs, and a document of the principles for complementary human-bot role distribution.

## Criteria
It is judged FAIL if the bot's intervention replaces the existing TR, and PASS if the human and the bot mutually complement one another with different strengths.
