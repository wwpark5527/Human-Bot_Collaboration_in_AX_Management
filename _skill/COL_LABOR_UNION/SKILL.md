# Labor-union-type resistance — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether collective resistance corresponds to organized demands through a labor union, that is, to labor-union-type resistance, and provide the grounds for a labor-management negotiation response.

## Input
- The official demands, statements, and negotiation records of labor unions or similar collective organizations in the target industry and job family
- Observed signs of demands (related to limits on automation, mandatory employment, scope of use, and retraining)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the target reaction comes from an organized collective actor such as a labor union.
2. Confirm signs of demands for limits on automation or for mandating human employment.
3. Confirm signs of demands for restricting the scope of AI use, or for mandatory retraining.
4. Synthesize the confirmed features and judge whether it is labor-union-type resistance.

## Output
The judgment result on whether the target reaction corresponds to labor-union-type resistance, and the priority order of the labor-management negotiation agenda.

## Criteria
When one or more of demanding limits on automation, mandating human employment, restricting the scope of AI use, and requiring mandatory retraining is confirmed, a PASS judgment (labor-union-type resistance) is made; otherwise, a FAIL judgment (another type) is made.
