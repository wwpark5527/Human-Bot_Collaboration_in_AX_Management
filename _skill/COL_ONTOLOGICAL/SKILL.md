# Ontological resistance — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether collective resistance corresponds to ontological resistance, which seeks to defend the meaning of human existence itself, and provide the grounds for a response based on the augmentation narrative.

## Input
- A record of the ground and language of the observed resistance (concrete issues vs a question about the meaning of existence)
- Observation records of signs of deterioration such as cynicism, disengagement, and an anti-technology identity

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Distinguish whether the ground of the resistance is a concrete issue such as jobs or institutions, or a question about the meaning of human existence.
2. Confirm whether the question "Why are humans needed when AI does it better?" is raised.
3. Confirm signs of cynicism, organizational disengagement, an anti-technology identity, human supremacism, and AI hatred.
4. Synthesize the confirmed grounds and judge whether it is ontological resistance.

## Output
The judgment result on whether the target reaction corresponds to ontological resistance, and the priority order of responses based on the augmentation narrative.

## Criteria
When the ground of the resistance is a question about the meaning of human existence itself, a PASS judgment (ontological resistance) is made; when it remains at concrete issues (employment, expertise, culture, institutions), a FAIL judgment (another type) is made.
