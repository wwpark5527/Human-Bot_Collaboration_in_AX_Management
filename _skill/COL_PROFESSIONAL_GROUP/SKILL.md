# Professional-group resistance — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether collective resistance corresponds to professional-group resistance, arising from highly skilled professional groups' concern over the infringement of their expertise and authority of judgment, and provide the grounds for a response that establishes the professional's final authority of judgment.

## Input
- Records of statements, declarations, and concerns related to AI adoption in the target professional domain (the medical, education, legal fields, etc.)
- Observed signs of issues (related to allocation of responsibility, infringement of expertise, and professional worth)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the subject of the resistance is a highly skilled professional group such as the medical, education, or legal field.
2. Confirm whether the issue of unclear allocation of responsibility is raised.
3. Confirm whether the issue of infringement of expertise, or of devaluation of professional worth, is raised.
4. Synthesize the confirmed features and judge whether it is professional-group resistance.

## Output
The judgment result on whether the target reaction corresponds to professional-group resistance, and the priority order of the response establishing the professional's final authority of judgment.

## Criteria
When the subject of the resistance is a highly skilled profession and one or more of unclear allocation of responsibility, infringement of expertise, and devaluation of professional worth is confirmed, a PASS judgment (professional-group resistance) is made; otherwise, a FAIL judgment (another type) is made.
