# Criterion — Skill

## Purpose
Inspect the organization's standard of judgment for what counts as a good result, and judge whether there is criterion inconsistency in which a different standard is applied to the same request each time.

## Input
- Materials on standards of judgment by area of work
- Records of the ways results are judged by department and by person in charge

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Collect the standards of judgment by area of work.
2. Compare the differences in standards of judgment among departments and persons in charge.
3. Consolidate them into a standard of judgment unified across the whole organization.
4. Judge whether the same standard is applied to the same request.

## Output
The judgment result on the unified standard of judgment.

## Criteria
A PASS judgment is made when the same standard of judgment is applied to the same request rather than answering by a different standard each time; a FAIL judgment is made when criterion inconsistency is found.
