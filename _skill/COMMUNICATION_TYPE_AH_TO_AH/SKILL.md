# AH-AH communication — Skill

## Purpose
Judge the symmetry according to the level of augmentation power in communication between augmented human A and augmented human B, and select an appropriate coordination path when a contribution conflict arises.

## Input
- The target communication situation (an exchange of messages between augmented human A and augmented human B)
- Information on the augmentation power of both sides (AI performance, quality of the common context, accessible materials, execution authority, human judgment, level of governance, recording/verification system)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Compare and evaluate the augmentation power of both sides across the seven elements.
2. Inspect whether there is a contribution conflict (a mismatch in criteria, interpretation, verification state, or responsibility judgment).
3. Select the path suited to the situation (human-centered / AI-centered / via governance).
4. Coordinate meaning, conditions, judgment, and responsibility through the selected path.

## Output
The coordination process of meaning, conditions, judgment, and responsibility, together with the occurrence of contribution conflicts and the three communication paths.

## Criteria
A PASS judgment is made when symmetry is evaluated by the seven elements of augmentation power and the contribution conflict is coordinated through one of the three paths; otherwise, a FAIL judgment is made.
