# Context Capital Access Right (Context Access Right) — Skill

## Purpose
Diagnose, by distinguishing formal access at the level of AI accounts and software usage licenses from actual access to purpose, standards, materials, and approval structure (the context capital access right), and prevent the stratification of the AI era caused by the monopolization of context capital.

## Input
- The AI account and usage license status of the target organization or work unit, the current state of documentation of purpose, standards, materials, and approval structure (where it exists), and information on members' rank, department, and information access

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Ascertain the current state of members' holding of AI accounts and usage licenses (formal access).
2. Ascertain the current state of members' access to purpose, standards, materials, and approval structure (actual access).
3. Identify the members who have an AI account but cannot access context capital.
4. Diagnose the causes of context capital monopolization (sense, language ability, information access, rank, departmental authority).
5. Execute the disclosure, standardization, and distribution procedures that convert the monopolized context capital into a common production base for the whole organization.
6. Re-examine whether the ability to use AI has spread organizationally after the conversion.

## Output
A comparison table of formal and actual access, a diagnostic report on the causes of context capital monopolization, disclosed and standardized context capital materials, and a diffusion re-examination report.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when members, regardless of holding an AI account, actually access context capital and use AI meaningfully, and FAIL when they cannot access it and therefore cannot use AI properly.
