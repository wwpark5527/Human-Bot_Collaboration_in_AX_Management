# Socialization of Context Capital — Skill

## Purpose
Convert context capital into a common production base for the whole organization so that the ability to use AI is not monopolized by a few individuals or a particular department.

## Input
- The current state of the purpose, standards, materials, and approval systems related to AI use within the organization, and the current context holdings by department and by individual

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Survey the current holdings of the context needed for AI use.
2. Identify the points at which context capital is monopolized by a few individuals or a particular department.
3. Establish a plan to convert the monopolized context capital into the form of a common context.
4. Grant members access rights to the converted context capital and train them.
5. In connection with the responsible operating system, continuously manage the socialized context capital.

## Output
A diagnostic report on the points of context capital monopolization, a plan for conversion into a common context, and records of the granting of access rights and of training.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when context capital is converted into a common production base accessible to the whole organization, and FAIL when the state of monopolization is maintained.
