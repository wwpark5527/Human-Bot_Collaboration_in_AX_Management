# Context Designer (context-design-type AX talent) — Skill

## Purpose
As a context designer, convert the organization's purpose and standards into AI execution conditions and verify the results.

## Input
- Materials on the organization's purpose, standards, language, ways of working, output formats, and verification criteria

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Collect and organize the organization's purpose, standards, language, ways of working, output formats, and verification criteria.
2. Convert what has been collected into conditions that AI (the first LLM) can execute.
3. Verify AI execution results against the organization's standards and reflect the feedback.
4. Integrate the accumulated organizational context as an asset for forming the second LLM.

## Output
The necessary conditions for the first LLM to work (purpose and standards), and the foundational assets for forming the second LLM.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when AI works within the organization's purpose and standards, and FAIL when it departs from them.
