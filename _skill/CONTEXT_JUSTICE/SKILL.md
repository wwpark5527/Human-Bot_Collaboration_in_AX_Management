# Context Justice — Skill

## Purpose
So that access to the organization's purpose, standards, materials, and verification system is not monopolized by a select few, design and operate the procedures by which members can give context to AI, verify the results, and contest the judgments.

## Input
- The current state of access authority over the organization's purpose, standards, materials, and verification system, the existing procedures for verifying AI results and for appeal (where they exist), and the target work domain

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Diagnose whether the access authority over purpose, standards, materials, and the verification system is concentrated in a few.
2. Design the procedure by which members can give context to AI.
3. Establish the procedure and criteria by which members can verify AI output results.
4. Establish an appeal channel and a review process for AI judgments.
5. Examine the actual exercise of the right to give context, the right to verify, and the right of appeal, and redesign the parts that fall short.

## Output
A diagnostic report on the monopolization of access authority, a context-giving procedure document, a results verification procedure document, an appeal process document, and an authority-exercise review report.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when members can actually exercise the right to give context, the right to verify, and the right of appeal, and FAIL when these are monopolized by a select few so that they cannot be exercised.
