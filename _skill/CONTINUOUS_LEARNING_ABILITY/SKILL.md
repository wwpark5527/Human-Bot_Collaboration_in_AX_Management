# Continuous Learning Ability — Skill

## Purpose
It supports maintaining one's state as a human being who is continuously updated, by tracking changes in technologies, tools, and ways of working and relearning faster than the pace of that change.

## Input
- A list of the expertise, skills, and tool knowledge currently held, with the time of acquisition
- Recently observed changes in technologies, tools, and ways of working

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Check the extent of change between the time of acquisition of the held knowledge and skills and the present point in time.
2. Compare relearning speed against the pace of change to identify areas requiring relearning.
3. Establish and execute a relearning plan for the identified areas.
4. Periodically check whether the results of relearning have been reflected in how work is actually carried out.

## Output
The judgment result of relearning speed against the pace of change, and the continuously updated state of knowledge and skills.

## Criteria
If one is relearning faster than the pace of change and is continuously updated, it is judged PASS (a state of continuous learning is maintained); if the relearning speed falls short of the pace of change, it is judged FAIL (the relearning plan needs to be re-established).
