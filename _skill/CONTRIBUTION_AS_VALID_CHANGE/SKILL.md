# Contribution — Skill

## Purpose
So that 'who produced what change' rather than 'who worked' can be judged, it identifies and records the valid change that a performed act produced.

## Input
- A record of an act (work) whose fact of performance has been confirmed

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm the performed act (work).
2. Inspect whether that act changed the counterpart's understanding.
3. Inspect whether that act made judgment, verification, and approval possible.
4. Inspect whether that act remained as a record and whether it led to the next action.
5. If at least one of the above conditions is confirmed, judge it a valid change and record the changed state.

## Output
The changed state of meaning, judgment, verification, approval, record, execution, and responsibility.

## Criteria
If because of the act at least one of the states of meaning, judgment, verification, approval, record, execution, and responsibility actually changed it is judged PASS (valid change — contribution), and if there is only the act with no state change it is judged FAIL (stopped at work).
