# AH + AB type — Skill

## Purpose
Identify a cooperative relationship as the AH+AB type (the horizontal joint optimization of augmented human and augmented bot), identify the characteristic stresses of AH and of AB respectively, and apply countermeasures centered on the building of a common context.

## Input
- The horizontality of the cooperative relationship and whether both the human and the bot side are augmented
- The signs of stress observed on the AH and AB sides respectively

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether both human and bot are augmented and form a horizontal, jointly optimizing relationship.
2. Examine the stresses on the AH side (Orchestration overload, decision fatigue, concentration of responsibility, weakened sense of reality).
3. Examine the stresses on the AB side (Context inconsistency, Alignment stress, Autonomy ambiguity, Continuous update overload).
4. Apply the AI orchestration dashboard and Decision hierarchy to AH and Role-constrained autonomy and Explainability layer to AB, and pursue the building of a common context as the core solution.

## Output
The determination of the AH+AB type and the list of stress countermeasures applied to AH and AB respectively.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS (AH+AB type) when both human and bot are augmented and form a horizontal, jointly optimizing relationship, and FAIL (reclassification into another cooperation type required) when they do not.
