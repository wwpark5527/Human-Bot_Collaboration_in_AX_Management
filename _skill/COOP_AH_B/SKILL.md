# AH + B type — Skill

## Purpose
Identify a cooperative relationship as the AH+B type (the coexistence of an augmented human and an independently performing bot), identify the characteristic stresses of AH and of B respectively, and apply countermeasures centered on the clarification of approval authority and the governance system.

## Input
- The cooperative composition (whether the human is augmented / whether the bot performs work independently)
- The signs of stress observed on the AH and B sides respectively

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the cooperative composition is the coexistence of an augmented human (AH) and a non-augmented bot (B) and whether the bot performs work independently.
2. Examine the stresses on the AH side (loss of control, incapacitation of competence, concentration of responsibility, fear of surveillance).
3. Examine the stresses on the B side (lack of context, governance conflict, inconsistency with human instructions, lack of trust).
4. Apply to AH the clarification of the final approval authority, role differentiation, and the building of a governance system, and to B the building of a common context, the definition of the approval system, and the provision of explainability.

## Output
The determination of the AH+B type and the list of stress countermeasures applied to AH and B respectively.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS (AH+B type) when an augmented human and a non-augmented bot coexist and the bot performs work independently, and FAIL (reclassification into another cooperation type required) when they do not.
