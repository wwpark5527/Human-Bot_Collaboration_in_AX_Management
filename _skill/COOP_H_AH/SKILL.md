# H + AH type — Skill

## Purpose
Identify a cooperative relationship as the H+AH type (the cooperation of an ordinary human and an augmented human, with an augmentation gap present), identify the characteristic stresses of H and of AH respectively, and apply countermeasures at the individual and organizational levels.

## Input
- The composition of the cooperating parties (human / augmented human / bot) and information on the gap in augmentation level
- The signs of stress observed on the H and AH sides respectively

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the cooperating parties are a human (H) and an augmented human (AH) and whether a gap in augmentation level exists.
2. Examine the stresses on the H side (relative inferiority, alienation, unfair evaluation).
3. Examine the stresses on the AH side (continuous high-performance pressure, AI dependency, isolation in human relationships, identity confusion).
4. Apply to H the emphasis on complementary roles and the strengthening of the capabilities distinctive to humans, and to AH AI dependency monitoring, collaborative leadership training, and the establishment of a human orchestrator identity, while at the organizational level also banning human-only evaluation.

## Output
The determination of the H+AH type and the list of stress countermeasures applied to H, to AH, and at the organizational level.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS (H+AH type) when the cooperating parties are a human and an augmented human and an augmentation gap is confirmed, and FAIL (reclassification into another cooperation type required) when they are not.
