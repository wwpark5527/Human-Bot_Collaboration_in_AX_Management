# H + B type — Skill

## Purpose
Identify a cooperative relationship as the H+B type (human-led, with the bot as a tool or automation executor), identify the characteristic stresses of the human (H) and the bot (B) respectively, and apply the appropriate countermeasures.

## Input
- The structure of initiative in the cooperative relationship (who is the subject and who assists)
- The signs of stress observed on the human (H) and bot (B) sides respectively

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the cooperative relationship is the vertical relationship of human-led plus bot-assisted work.
2. Examine the stresses on the human (H) side (loss of control, role erosion, imbalance of responsibility, learning pressure).
3. Examine the stresses on the bot (B) side (lack of Context, overload from exceptional situations, inconsistency in human feedback).
4. Apply to the H side the adoption of explainable AI, maintaining Human-in-the-loop, maintaining augmentation, and training in AI comprehension, and to the B side building a common context, an exception escalation system, and a feedback learning structure.

## Output
The determination of the H+B type and the list of stress countermeasures applied to H and B respectively.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS (H+B type) when the relationship is vertical and the bot occupies the position of a tool or automation executor, and FAIL (reclassification into another cooperation type required) when it does not.
