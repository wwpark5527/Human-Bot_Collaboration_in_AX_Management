# AH + AB (a true AX organization) — Skill

## Purpose
Identify whether the organization's cooperative structure is the AH + AB type, in which both human and bot are augmented and jointly make judgments, and distinguish it from H+B/H+AH/AH+B.

## Input
- The combination of the organization's members (including whether the human and the bot are augmented)
- Whether the bot is an execution tool or a joint-judgment subject
- The state of application of the organizational AX OS and HBRM

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the human is in an augmented (AH) state.
2. Confirm whether the bot is in an augmented (AB) state.
3. Confirm whether the bot is not a mere execution tool but a subject that jointly makes judgments with the human.
4. If all the conditions are met, determine it to be AH + AB, and examine the state of use of the organizational AX OS and of the realization of HBRM.

## Output
The determination of the cooperation type (H+B | H+AH | AH+B | AH+AB), whether the AH + AB type (a true AX organization) has been reached, and the results of the review of the joint-judgment system.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS as AH + AB when both human and bot are augmented and function as joint-judgment subjects.
