# Implementer — Skill

## Purpose
Convert the problems and structures defined by the shaper into actually working deliverables, thereby realizing ideas and designs.

## Input
- The problem definition and design structure defined by the shaper
- The materials and environment needed for implementation

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm the design.
2. Write the code.
3. Connect the features.
4. Automate repetitive work.
5. Confirm whether the deliverable actually works.

## Output
Realization — a working deliverable.

## Criteria
If ideas and designs have been converted into actually working deliverables it is judged PASS (realization achieved), and if they do not work or are incomplete it is judged FAIL (implementation not completed).
