# Co-constitution of roles (Co-constitution) — Skill

## Purpose
Combine the different contributions of human and bot to form a single hybridized strategic shaper role.

## Input
- Materials for the human's vision-setting (direction and goals)
- The results of the bot's real-time strategy simulation

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Collect the human's vision-setting contribution.
2. Collect the bot's real-time strategy simulation contribution.
3. Combine the two contributions.
4. Form the result of the combination into a single hybridized role.

## Output
A strategic shaper role combining the human's vision-setting with the bot's real-time strategy simulation.

## Criteria
If a combined strategic shaper role is produced and the role itself has been hybridized it is judged PASS, and otherwise FAIL.
