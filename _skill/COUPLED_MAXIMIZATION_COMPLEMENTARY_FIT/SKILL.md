# Maximization of human-bot complementary fit (Maximization of Complementary Fit) — Skill

## Purpose
Combine the respective strengths of human and bot to maximize complementary fit and generate a new role capability.

## Input
- Information on human strengths (empathy, value judgment, context understanding, ethics, creative meaning-making)
- Information on bot strengths (speed, computation, memory, search, pattern analysis)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm the human strengths.
2. Confirm the bot strengths.
3. Find the points of combination of the two sets of strengths.
4. Define a new role capability through the combination.

## Output
The new role capability generated by the combination of human strengths and bot strengths.

## Criteria
If human strengths and bot strengths are combined and a new role capability is generated it is judged PASS, and otherwise FAIL.
