# Critical Thinking — Skill

## Purpose
It supports filtering out plausible but wrong errors by performing verification, counterexample search, logic checking, and reliability assessment on the results produced by AI.

## Input
- Outputs produced by AI
- Original data or grounds for judgment that can be collated against them

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Designate the AI outputs as objects requiring verification.
2. Search for counterexamples to the results.
3. Check the logical development of the results and confirm leaps or contradictions.
4. Synthesize verification, counterexample search, and logic checking to assess reliability and decide whether to adopt them.

## Output
Records of verification, counterexample search, and logic checking on the AI outputs, together with the reliability assessment result.

## Criteria
If plausible errors have been filtered out through verification, counterexample search, and logic checking, it is judged PASS (a person who verifies AI well); if the outputs were accepted as they were without verification, it is judged FAIL (re-checking needed).
