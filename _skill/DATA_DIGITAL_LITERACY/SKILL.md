# Data and Digital Literacy — Skill

## Purpose
By combining the ability to interpret data, to understand digital workflows, and to verify AI outputs, it supports the transition to a state in which one can handle, on one's own, work that has moved onto data, AI, and digital foundations.

## Input
- Data arising in the target work and information about its digital workflow
- Outputs produced by AI

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Identify the data of the target work and interpret its meaning.
2. Grasp the stages and tools of the digital workflow of that work.
3. Verify the AI outputs by collating them against the original data and logic.
4. Fill, through supplementary learning, the weak areas revealed by the results of interpretation, understanding, and verification.

## Output
The results of data interpretation, the level of understanding of the digital workflow, and the verification judgment on AI outputs.

## Criteria
If one can interpret and verify, on one's own, work that has moved onto data-based, AI-based, and digital-based foundations, it is judged PASS (data and digital literacy secured); if interpretation or verification is impossible, it is judged FAIL (supplementary learning needed).
