# Efficiency-centered AX — Skill

## Purpose
Diagnose the six risks brought about by an AI transformation designed solely on the criterion of efficiency, and evaluate the organization's current position relative to inclusive-transformation AX.

## Input
- The organization's AX goal and KPI documents, the status of AI use, and materials on the performance evaluation and compensation system

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the organization's AX goals and KPIs are centered on productivity, cost reduction, and speed.
2. Check whether there is a reduction of human roles.
3. Check whether AI-competence gaps are widening.
4. Check whether algorithmic management is being abused.
5. Check whether there is a weakening of judgment authority.
6. Check whether there is an absence of the right to object.
7. Check whether there is performance concentration.
8. Apply the efficiency-centered AX vs. inclusive-transformation AX comparison table to diagnose the organization's current position.

## Output
An efficiency-centered AX risk diagnostic checklist (6 items), and the results of applying the efficiency-centered AX vs. inclusive-transformation AX comparison table.

## Criteria
If all six risk items are identified and documented it is judged PASS, and if the risks are left unidentified it is judged FAIL.
