# ESG AI-era extension theory — Skill

## Purpose
Diagnose the limitation whereby the existing ESG is strong on the industrial-era responsibility system while failing to capture the new problems of the AI era, and present the grounds and direction for extending the ESG concept to the AI operating environment (the shift to AI inclusive-transformation ESG).

## Input
- The organization's current ESG system documents, the status of AI adoption, and a list of the new issues within the organization that result from it (stratification, algorithmic management, infringement of judgment authority, etc.)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Organize the scope of responsibility reflected by the current ESG system.
2. List the problems newly emerging due to the introduction of AI.
3. Judge whether each new problem is dealt with within the existing ESG framework.
4. Redefine the problems that are not dealt with into an extended ESG scope.
5. Organize the grounds for adopting an extended ESG.

## Output
A gap analysis report between the existing ESG and the problems of the AI era, and a grounds document for adopting an extended ESG (AI inclusive-transformation ESG).

## Criteria
If the omission of the new problems of the AI era is confirmed and the grounds for the shift to an extended ESG are explicitly presented it is judged PASS, and otherwise FAIL.
