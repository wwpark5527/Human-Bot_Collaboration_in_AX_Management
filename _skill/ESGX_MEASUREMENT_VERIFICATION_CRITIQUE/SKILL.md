# Measurement-verification-strengthening theory — Skill

## Purpose
Examine the organization's ESG measurement and verification system in order to prevent disguised environmentalism such as greenwashing, and prepare unified evaluation and disclosure criteria and measures for strengthened verification.

## Input
- Score and criteria materials from the multiple ESG rating agencies to which the organization is exposed, the organization's ESG data disclosure items, and the status of verification (third-party verification and the like)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Collect the score calculation criteria and results of multiple rating agencies.
2. Analyze the causes of score differences between rating agencies.
3. Check whether the organization's data disclosure items are selective.
4. Review candidate unified evaluation and disclosure criteria.
5. Design measures for strengthening verification procedures (third-party verification and the like).

## Output
An analysis report on score discrepancies between rating agencies, a checklist for bias in data disclosure, and a plan for unified criteria and strengthened verification.

## Criteria
If the causes of score discrepancies between rating agencies and bias in data disclosure are clearly identified and a direction for unified criteria and strengthened verification is presented it is judged PASS, and otherwise FAIL.
