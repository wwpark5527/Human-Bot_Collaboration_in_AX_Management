# Stakeholder-expansion theory — Skill

## Purpose
Diagnose whether the organization's ESG approach remains at company-centered single materiality (the investor-centric financial-risk perspective), and present a direction for stakeholder expansion in line with the current shift toward impact materiality, such as the EU CSRD.

## Input
- The organization's current ESG evaluation and disclosure criteria documents, its materiality judgment method, and related regulatory trends (the EU CSRD and the like, for reference)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Collect the materiality judgment method of the current ESG evaluation and disclosure criteria.
2. Analyze whether non-financial issues such as climate change and human-rights issues are evaluated only after being converted into financial risk.
3. Compare with the EU CSRD's impact materiality concept and identify the gaps.
4. Derive an improvement direction that expands the scope of evaluation from an investor focus to a variety of stakeholders.
5. Organize the gap analysis and the direction of expansion into a document.

## Output
A gap analysis report on financial versus impact materiality in the ESG evaluation criteria, and a proposal for the direction of stakeholder expansion.

## Criteria
If whether a shift from financial materiality to impact materiality is needed and the direction of stakeholder expansion are explicitly identified it is judged PASS, and if it remains only within the company financial-risk perspective it is judged FAIL.
