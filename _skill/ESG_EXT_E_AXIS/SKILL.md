# Extension of E (Sustainability of AI Infrastructure) — Skill

## Purpose
While retaining the existing responsibilities for environment, energy, carbon, resources, and pollution, extend the ESG E axis to also manage the physical environmental burden induced by AI infrastructure (electricity, water, carbon, semiconductors and minerals, e-waste, local environment).

## Input
- The operating status of AI data centers/compute/cloud, electricity and water use data, the replacement history of server and chip equipment, and data-center siting information

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Measure the electricity consumption of AI training, inference, and data-center operations.
2. Measure the water use arising from data-center cooling and operations and its impact on local water resources.
3. Calculate carbon emissions and check the share of renewable energy used.
4. Assess the risks of the semiconductor and mineral supply chains.
5. Manage the volume of e-waste generated and the equipment replacement cycles.
6. Assess the local environmental and local community impact of data-center siting.
7. Integrate the above results into the existing E-axis management and reporting system.

## Output
Management data by item for electricity/water/carbon/semiconductors and minerals/e-waste/local environmental impact, and an integrated E-axis report.

## Criteria
If all six management items are measured and managed as the company's environmental responsibility it is judged PASS, and if they are omitted or not integrated into the management system it is judged FAIL.
