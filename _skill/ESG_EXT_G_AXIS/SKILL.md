# Extension of G (Accountability Structure of AI Power) — Skill

## Purpose
While retaining the existing responsibilities for the board, control, audit, risk, and compliance, build an AI governance system (authority, verification, approval, recording, explanation, objection, accountability attribution, improvement loops) for responsibly operating the functioning and judgment of AI.

## Input
- The organization's existing governance system documents, the state of AI adoption, the AI use approval/recording system (if any), and related international standards (NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, etc., for reference)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Clarify the system of roles, authority, and responsibility that approves the use of AI.
2. Build a recording and log management system for the contribution and judgment of AI.
3. Prepare approval structures and review procedures for AI output artifacts.
4. Secure the explainability of AI judgment and establish criteria for accountability attribution.
5. Prepare AI-related risk management and objection procedures.
6. Establish a policy-based control structure for AI operation.
7. Define principles for managing intellectual property, disclosure criteria, and conflicts of interest, and integrate them into the existing governance system.

## Output
A document clarifying roles/authority/responsibility, a recording and log management system, approval and review procedure documents, explainability and accountability attribution criteria, risk management and objection procedures, a policy control structure, and an integrated AI governance system.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when all seven elements of AI governance (roles, authority, and responsibility; records and logs; approval and review; explainability and accountability attribution; risk management and objection; policy control; intellectual property/conflicts of interest) are in place, and FAIL when one or more are missing.
