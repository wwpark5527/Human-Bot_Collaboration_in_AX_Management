# Extension of S (AI Capability Equality and Human Dignity) — Skill

## Purpose
While retaining the existing responsibilities for labor, human rights, safety, diversity, and social contribution, guarantee the social capabilities enabling humans not to be replaced, surveilled, and excluded by AI but to understand, use, and control AI, to raise objections, and to participate in outcomes.

## Input
- The current state of changes in workers' roles following AI adoption, the existing evaluation/placement system, the current state of education and retraining programs, and data on members' AI accessibility

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Investigate how AI changes workers' roles among replacement / complementation / redeployment / upskilling.
2. Prepare explainability and appeal procedures for AI outcomes.
3. Where AI is used for evaluation and placement, prepare an algorithmic management system.
4. Check whether education, retraining, and accessibility for the capability to use AI are provided equally.
5. Check whether discrimination is reinforced by AI's data bias.
6. Confirm whether the human final right of judgment is maintained.
7. Institutionalize the eight rights (access right, right to learn, right to use, right of judgment, right to explanation, right of appeal, right to transition, right to benefit sharing).

## Output
A diagnosis of changes in workers' roles, explainability/appeal procedures, an algorithmic management system, the results of the capability equality check, a bias check report, and a document institutionalizing the eight rights.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the eight rights are actually guaranteed to members, and FAIL when one or more are not guaranteed or AI is used only in a way that replaces, surveils, and excludes humans.
