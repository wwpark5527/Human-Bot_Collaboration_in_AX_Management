# ESG for AI — Skill

## Purpose
Manage and control from the ESG standpoint the side effects and ethical problems that AI can induce, and thereby build a responsible AI (RAI) use system.

## Input
- Documents on the organization's current state of AI use, a list of possible side effects and ethical risks, and RAI-related reference models (the RAI evaluation model, etc.)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Check the current state of AI use and investigate the points where negative effects can occur.
2. Assess the ethical problems that AI use induces.
3. Establish AI control criteria from the ESG standpoint (approval, verification, audit).
4. Review the applicability of related frameworks such as the RAI evaluation model.
5. Integrate the control criteria into the organization's ESG governance system.

## Output
An investigation report on AI side effects and ethical risks, AI control criteria and procedure documents from the ESG standpoint, and an RAI application plan.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when AI side effects and ethical risks are identified and control criteria and procedures from the ESG standpoint are explicitly established, and FAIL otherwise.
