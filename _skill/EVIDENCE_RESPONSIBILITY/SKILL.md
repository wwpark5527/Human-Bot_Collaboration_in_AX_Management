# Evidence Responsibility — Skill

## Purpose
Support leaving as evidence the materials used, the instructions to AI, the AI results, the human revisions, the verification criteria, the approval records, the error records, and the improvements reflected, so that in an AX organization not only the result but the course of judgment can be retraced.

## Input
- The materials used in the AI work and the instructions delivered to AI
- The AI output artifacts and the history of human revisions and reviews

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Record the materials used and the instructions given to AI.
2. Preserve the results AI produced and the revisions made by humans separately.
3. Leave the verification criteria and approval records.
4. Organize the error records and the improvements reflected in a traceable form.

## Output
An evidence record by which the course of judgment can be retraced.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS (evidence responsibility fulfilled) when the materials used, the instructions given to AI, the results AI produced, the revisions made by humans, the verification criteria, the approval records, the error records, and the improvements reflected all remain, and FAIL (evidence records need supplementing) when even one is missing.
