# Functional Role — Skill

## Purpose
Confirm functional roles by the two conditions of formality and job-relatedness, clearly distinguish them from team roles, which are informal behavior types, and provide the contrast reference for team role balance (TRB) diagnosis.

## Input
- The list of formal jobs within the team (for example, recruiting officer, evaluation officer, payroll officer, benefits officer)
- Information on the assigning party (organization/superior) of each job and on its job-relatedness

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm the formal job units within the team.
2. Confirm whether each job was formally assigned by the organization or a superior.
3. Determine whether each role is directly job-related.
4. Confirm the roles that meet both conditions as functional roles and list them.
5. Compare the confirmed list of functional roles with the list of team roles and register it as material for TRB diagnosis.

## Output
A list of functional roles (job assignments) by team member, a functional role–team role comparison table, and base material for TRB diagnosis.

## Criteria
Was it formally assigned by the organization or a superior + is it job-related — when both conditions are met it is PASS (determined to be a functional role), and when even one is unmet it is FAIL (reclassified as a team role or another role).
