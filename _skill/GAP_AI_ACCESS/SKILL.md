# AI Access Gap — Skill

## Purpose
Diagnose and alleviate the inequality in access to the tools themselves (the AI access gap).

## Input
- The current state of access to AI models and infrastructure, and materials on the cost structure

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Investigate the current accessibility of high-performance AI models and infrastructure.
2. Analyze the structure of access costs (subscription fees, token costs) and identify the actors who are excluded.
3. Design support measures for resolving the inequality of access.
4. Verify the results of securing accessibility.

## Output
The diagnostic results on the access gap and measures for securing accessibility.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when access is actually possible, and FAIL when access is blocked.
