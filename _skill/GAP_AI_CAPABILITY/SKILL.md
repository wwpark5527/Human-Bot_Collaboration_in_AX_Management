# AI Capability Gap — Skill

## Purpose
Diagnose the AI capability gap and prepare measures for capability improvement.

## Input
- Materials on each member's AI use and work performance

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Measure members' capability to use AI (the ability to convert it into actual work performance).
2. Analyze the correlation between the level of capability and the outcomes in productivity, wages, and promotion.
3. Design and implement training and support programs for resolving the capability gap.
4. Re-measure and verify whether conversion into work performance occurs after the training.

## Output
The diagnostic results on the AI capability gap and an education system for capability improvement.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when AI use is converted into work performance, and FAIL when it is not converted.
