# AI Right-to-Judge Gap — Skill

## Purpose
Diagnose the AI right-to-judge gap and prepare a system for distributing the authority to approve, revise, and reject.

## Input
- The results AI produces and the current state of the authority to approve, revise, and reject

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Grasp the scope and influence of the results AI produces.
2. Explicitly assign the authority to approve, revise, or reject the results.
3. Check whether humans without authority remain only as objects of AI decisions.
4. Record and verify the results of exercising the right of judgment.

## Output
Rules and procedures for distributing the AI right of judgment.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the right of judgment is clearly assigned and exercised, and FAIL when humans remain only as objects of decisions.
