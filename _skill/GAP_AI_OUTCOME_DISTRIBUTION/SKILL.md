# AI Outcome-Distribution Gap — Skill

## Purpose
Diagnose the AI outcome-distribution gap and prepare a distribution structure so that the productivity gains are not concentrated in the upper layer and the lower layer is not excluded.

## Input
- Productivity indicators before and after AI adoption, and the current state of compensation, promotion, and budget allocation

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Measure the change in productivity before and after AI adoption.
2. Trace which stratum within the organization the productivity gains accrue to.
3. Determine whether there is concentration at the top and exclusion at the bottom.
4. When a skew is confirmed, establish benefit sharing and redistribution measures.
5. Periodically re-check changes in the distribution structure.

## Output
An analysis of where the AI productivity gains accrue, and benefit sharing and redistribution measures.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the accrual is grasped transparently and redistribution measures are prepared in case of a skew, and FAIL when it is opaque or the skew is left unaddressed.
