# AI Ownership Gap — Skill

## Purpose
Diagnose the AI ownership gap and make transparent the distribution structure of the revenue from models, data, and platforms.

## Input
- The current state of ownership and revenue of the AI models, data, and platforms the organization uses

## Procedure (Steps)
1. List the owning and operating actors of the AI models, data, and platforms.
2. Grasp the revenue flows arising from each actor.
3. Analyze whether the revenue ownership structure is skewed.
4. When a skew is confirmed, establish profit sharing and redistribution measures.
5. Periodically re-check changes in the distribution structure.

## Output
An analysis of the revenue structure of AI models, data, and platforms, and profit distribution measures.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the revenue ownership structure is grasped and redistribution measures are prepared in case of a skew, and FAIL when it is opaque or the skew is left unaddressed.
