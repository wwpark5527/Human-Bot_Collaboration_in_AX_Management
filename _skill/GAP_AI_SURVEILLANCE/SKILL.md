# AI Surveillance Gap — Skill

## Purpose
Diagnose the AI surveillance gap and make the criteria transparent so that algorithmic management and control are not carried out one-sidedly.

## Input
- The current state of the organization's AI-based work assignment, evaluation, surveillance, compensation, and discipline

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Identify the actors who use AI as a management and surveillance tool and the members who become their object.
2. List the areas to which algorithmic management is applied (work assignment, evaluation, surveillance, scheduling, compensation, discipline, productivity measurement).
3. Disclose to the members under management the determination criteria and data sources for each area.
4. Prepare and operate a procedure by which those under management can appeal.
5. Periodically check the authority asymmetry between the managing actors and their objects.

## Output
A document on the scope of applied AI management and surveillance, and appeal procedures.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the areas and criteria of application are disclosed and the appeal procedure is operated, and FAIL when the criteria are undisclosed or there is only one-sided control.
