# Governance & Ethical Judgment — Skill

## Purpose
Support governance judgment that, beyond understanding technology, makes AI controllable and secures trust, explainability, accountability, security, and provenance.

## Input
- The current state of AI use and the related governance policies
- Issues or objects of review related to trust, explainability, accountability, security, and provenance

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Check the current state of trust, explainability, accountability, security, and provenance.
2. Establish governance criteria for the deficient items.
3. Verify whether AI is in a controllable state.
4. On the basis of the verification results, design trust and reflect it in the organization.

## Output
Trust designed by making AI controllable.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS (governance and ethical judgment secured) when, going beyond the level of only understanding technology, one has designed trust by making AI controllable, and FAIL (the governance system needs redesign) when one remains only at technical understanding.
