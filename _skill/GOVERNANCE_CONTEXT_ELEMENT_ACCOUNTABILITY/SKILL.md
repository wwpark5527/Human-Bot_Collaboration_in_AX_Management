# Accountability — Skill

## Purpose
Check the person ultimately responsible for use and the criteria of judgment, and determine whether the locus of accountability is being clarified in correspondence with the international standards (ISO/IEC 42001, NIST, EU AI Act).

## Input
- A list of the actual scopes of use of AI results
- The definition of the ultimately responsible person and the criteria of judgment for each scope of use

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Identify the scope in which the results generated by AI are used in actual work.
2. Confirm whether a person responsible for the final judgment and use is designated for each scope of use.
3. Confirm whether the accountability system corresponds to the requirements of ISO/IEC 42001, NIST, and the EU AI Act.
4. Determine whether the locus of accountability is clear enough to judge how far AI results may be used (whether accountability is unclear).

## Output
The determination result on the clarification of where responsibility lies (corresponding to ISO/IEC 42001 top-management responsibility, NIST Govern, and the provider and deployer obligations of the EU AI Act).

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when who bears the responsibility for the final use and judgment is clearly determined, and FAIL when there is unclear accountability.
