# Improvement — Skill

## Purpose
Check the location and manner in which errors and feedback are reflected, and determine whether organizational learning and improvement of criteria are taking place in correspondence with international standards (ISO/IEC 42001, NIST, EU AI Act).

## Input
- List of error, failure, and feedback cases that have arisen in the course of AI operation
- Definition of the criteria and procedures for reflecting errors and feedback

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Identify cases of errors and feedback that have arisen in the course of AI operation.
2. Confirm into which criteria (policies, prompts, verification checklists, and so on) errors and feedback are reflected.
3. Confirm whether the improvement system corresponds to the requirements of ISO/IEC 42001, NIST, and the EU AI Act.
4. Determine whether errors and feedback are actually reflected into the next set of criteria (whether improvement failure exists).

## Output
Determination result for organizational learning and improvement of criteria (corresponding to ISO/IEC 42001 continual improvement, NIST Manage, and EU AI Act post-market monitoring).

## Criteria
PASS is determined when where errors and feedback are reflected is clearly judged; FAIL is determined when there is improvement failure.
