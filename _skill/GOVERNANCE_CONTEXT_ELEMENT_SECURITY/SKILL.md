# Security — Skill

## Purpose
Check the criteria for handling information that must not be entered and sensitive information, and determine whether sensitive information and confidential material are being protected in correspondence with international standards (ISO/IEC 42001, NIST, EU AI Act).

## Input
- Current state of information entered into, shared with, and generated by AI per context
- Criteria defining sensitive information and confidentiality

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Identify, per context, the information entered into, shared with, and generated by AI.
2. Classify the items that fall under sensitive information and confidentiality.
3. Confirm whether the criteria prohibiting entry, sharing, and generation correspond to the requirements of ISO/IEC 42001, NIST, and the EU AI Act.
4. Determine whether a security risk (collapse of the boundary between sensitive information and externally disclosable information) exists.

## Output
Determination result for protection of sensitive information and confidentiality (corresponding to ISO/IEC 42001 data/asset management, NIST Map/Manage, and EU AI Act data governance Article 10).

## Criteria
PASS is determined when which information must not be entered, shared, or generated is clearly judged; FAIL is determined when there is a security risk.
