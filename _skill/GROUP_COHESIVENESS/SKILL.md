# Group Cohesiveness — Skill

## Purpose
Measure the human-bond side of teamwork accurately as the degree of psychological attraction among members, and thereby provide an indicator that stands in structural contrast to the task-activation side measured by TRB.

## Input
- Roster of the group or team to be measured
- Group cohesiveness measurement tool (survey or scale)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Fix the group or team to be measured.
2. Apply the measurement tool that asks about the degree of psychological attraction among members.
3. Collect the responses and produce them as the teamwork indicator for the human-bond side.
4. Place it alongside the task-activation side indicator measured by TRB.
5. Combine the two indicators to judge the position of that group or team within the framing of the two sides of teamwork.

## Output
Group cohesiveness measurement value (degree of psychological attraction among members), teamwork indicator for the human-bond side, and comparison material for contrast with TRB.

## Criteria
PASS (valid group cohesiveness measurement) is determined when the measurement takes as its object the degree of psychological attraction among members; FAIL (confusion with TRB) is determined when it takes as its object the degree to which the nine team roles are exercised or task activation.
