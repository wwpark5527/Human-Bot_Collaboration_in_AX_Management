# AH1 Augmented Human — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether a member has entered the AH1 stage on the ladder of human-augmentation stages, where thinking and learning have increased through AI use, and thereby secure the reference point for designing the transition path to AH2 (AI orchestrator) and beyond.

## Input
- Record of thinking ability and learning volume before AI use (the baseline)
- Record of thinking ability and learning volume after AI use

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Record thinking ability and learning volume before AI use.
2. Record thinking ability and learning volume after AI use.
3. Compare the two records and confirm whether an increase has occurred.
4. Determine whether the AH1 stage applies on the basis of the confirmed increase.

## Output
Measurement table of change in thinking ability, measurement table of change in learning volume, and AH1 stage determination result.

## Criteria
PASS (the AH1 stage applies) is determined when thinking ability and learning volume have both increased; FAIL (the AH1 stage does not apply) is determined otherwise.
