# AH2 AI Orchestrator — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether a member has reached the AH2 stage on the ladder of human-augmentation stages, where they use and direct multiple AI agents and role augmentation has occurred, and thereby secure the reference point for designing the AH3 (human-AI symbiotic leader) transition path.

## Input
- List of AI agents the member is using and the frequency of use
- Record of the member's role scope before and after the use/direction of AI agents

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Collect the list of AI agents in use and the frequency of use.
2. Distinguish, for each AI agent, whether it is mere use or direction (allocation and coordination).
3. Compare the role scope before and after the use/direction of AI agents.
4. Determine whether the AH2 stage applies on the basis of whether role expansion has occurred.

## Output
Record of the AI agent usage list and the manner of direction, role expansion confirmation table, and AH2 stage determination result.

## Criteria
PASS (the AH2 stage applies) is determined when the member uses and directs multiple AI agents and role expansion is confirmed; FAIL (the AH2 stage does not apply) is determined otherwise.
