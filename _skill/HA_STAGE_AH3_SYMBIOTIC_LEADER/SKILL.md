# AH3 Human-AI Symbiotic Leader — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether a member has reached the AH3 stage, the ultimate goal of the ladder of human-augmentation stages, in which human and AI operate as a single system, and thereby confirm whether the organizational AX human-augmentation roadmap has been completed.

## Input
- Observation record of the member-AI collaboration mode (individual direction versus integrated system)
- Cases of the integrated human-AI system performing decision-making and operational leadership

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Observe whether the collaboration mode is individual direction (AH2) or an integrated system.
2. Collect cases of the integrated system performing leadership functions.
3. Confirm, on the grounds of the observations and cases, whether it operates as a single system.
4. Determine whether the AH3 stage applies on the basis of the confirmation result.

## Output
Observation record of integrated human-AI operation, confirmation table of leadership function performance, and AH3 stage determination result.

## Criteria
PASS (the AH3 stage applies) is determined when human and AI operate as a single system and perform leadership; FAIL (the AH3 stage does not apply) is determined otherwise.
