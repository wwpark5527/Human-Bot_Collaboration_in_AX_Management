# H0 Non-Augmented Human — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether a member is at H0 (AI barely used), the starting line of the ladder of human-augmentation stages, and thereby secure the reference point for designing the subsequent augmentation path.

## Input
- The member's work performance records
- Records of whether and how often AI is used

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether AI is used, from the work performance records.
2. Confirm the frequency of AI use and the degree of involvement.
3. Calculate the proportion of work in which there is no AI involvement.
4. Determine whether the H0 stage applies on the basis of the calculation result.

## Output
AI use frequency confirmation table, table of the proportion of work without AI involvement, and H0 stage determination result.

## Criteria
PASS (the H0 stage applies) is determined when AI is barely used and AI involvement is virtually absent; FAIL (the H0 stage does not apply) is determined otherwise.
