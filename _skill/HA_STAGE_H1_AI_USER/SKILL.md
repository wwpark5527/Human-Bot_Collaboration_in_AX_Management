# H1 AI User — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether a member is at the H1 stage on the ladder of human-augmentation stages, using AI at the level of questions and searches, and thereby secure the reference point for designing the transition path to H2 (AI collaborator) and beyond.

## Input
- The member's AI use records
- Records of the type of AI use and the utilization of results

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm the type of use (questions and searches versus joint work) from the AI use records.
2. Confirm the form of result utilization (whether it is simply obtaining results).
3. Review the type of use and the form of result utilization together.
4. Determine whether the H1 stage applies on the basis of the synthesized result.

## Output
AI use manner confirmation table, table of the form of utilization of question and search results, and H1 stage determination result.

## Criteria
PASS (the H1 stage applies) is determined when AI use remains at the level of questions and searches; FAIL (the H1 stage does not apply) is determined when use beyond that is confirmed.
