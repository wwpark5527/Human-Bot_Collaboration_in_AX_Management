# H2 AI Collaborator — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether a member is at the H2 stage on the ladder of human-augmentation stages, performing joint work with AI, and thereby secure the reference point for designing the transition path to AH1 (augmented human) and beyond.

## Input
- The member's work performance records
- Records of the mode of interaction with AI

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm the mode of interaction with AI.
2. Confirm whether that mode takes the form of joint work.
3. Collect the products of joint work with AI.
4. Determine whether the H2 stage applies on the basis of what has been confirmed.

## Output
Confirmation table of the mode of joint work with AI, list of the products of joint work with AI, and H2 stage determination result.

## Criteria
PASS (the H2 stage applies) is determined when the member performs joint work with AI and its products are confirmed; FAIL (the H2 stage does not apply) is determined otherwise.
