# Essence — Skill

## Purpose
Discriminate, according to the "essence" axis of the human stress vs bot stress comparison structure, whether an observed state of tension belongs to the psychological/emotional domain or the functional/computational domain, and provide the grounds for selecting the appropriate response system.

## Input
- Information on the subject of the observed state of tension (human/bot)
- The manner in which the tension is expressed (emotional and behavioral indicators or performance and error indicators)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the subject of the tension is a human or a bot.
2. If a human, confirm whether that tension originates in the psychological/emotional domain (anxiety, relationships, identity, and so on).
3. If a bot, confirm whether that tension originates in the functional/computational domain (overload, misalignment, goal conflict, and so on).
4. Classify the essence as psychological/emotional tension or functional/computational tension according to the confirmed domain.

## Output
Classification result of the essence of the target state of tension (psychological/emotional tension or functional/computational tension).

## Criteria
PASS (the essence of human stress) is determined when the source of the tension is the psychological/emotional domain, PASS (the essence of bot stress) when it is the functional/computational domain, and FAIL (reclassification required) when the two domains are mixed so that distinction is impossible.
