# meaning — Skill

## Purpose
Clearly define the meaning and role of HBRM and the meaning of the augmented human (AH) and augmented bot (AB), and thereby settle the second axis of the HBRM 3M structure.

## Input
- Descriptions concerning the definition and role of HBRM
- Descriptions concerning the meaning of the augmented human (AH) and augmented bot (AB)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Define the meaning and role of HBRM.
2. Define the meaning of the augmented human (AH).
3. Define the meaning of the augmented bot (AB).
4. Integrate the three definitions into the meaning perspective and move on to the measurement discussion.

## Output
Definition statement of the meaning and role of HBRM, definition statement of the meaning of the augmented human (AH), and definition statement of the meaning of the augmented bot (AB).

## Criteria
PASS (the meaning perspective holds) is determined when the content being treated defines the definition and role of the subject; FAIL (it belongs to another M) is determined when it concerns methods of execution or numerical indicators.
