# method — Skill

## Purpose
Organize into a single perspective the method by which the augmentation of humans and bots is actually accomplished, and thereby settle the first axis of the HBRM 3M structure.

## Input
- The human and bot augmentation descriptions of the whole of Chapter 3
- The augmentation-related descriptions of the first part of Chapter 4 (HBRM's eight roles)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm the augmentation-related descriptions of the whole of Chapter 3.
2. Confirm the augmentation-related descriptions per HBRM role in the first part of Chapter 4.
3. Integrate the two sets of descriptions under the method question of "how to augment humans and bots."
4. Settle the integrated result as method and move on to the meaning and measurement discussions.

## Output
List of the human and bot augmentation methods of Chapter 3, list of the HBRM role-based augmentation methods of the first part of Chapter 4, and the integrated organizing document of the method perspective.

## Criteria
PASS (the method perspective holds) is determined when the content being treated concerns how to augment humans and bots; FAIL (it belongs to another M) is determined when it concerns the definition and role of the subject or measurement indicators.
