# Augmentation Management — Skill

## Purpose
Support the process by which, within the collaboration flow established through collaboration-structure design, humans transition into augmented humans (AH) and bots into augmented bots (AB).

## Input
- The deliverable of the collaboration-structure design stage (the collaboration flow diagram)
- The current augmentation-level status of human and bot members

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Find the points where augmentation of humans and bots is needed within the collaboration flow.
2. Design learning and collaboration opportunities that will support the AH transition of humans (H).
3. Establish an AB transition plan by applying the common context and the governance context to bots (B).
4. Periodically review and record the progress of the H→AH and B→AB transitions.
5. Pass the progress record on to the role-balance management stage.

## Output
Human augmentation (AH) support plan, bot augmentation (AB) support plan, augmentation transition progress record.

## Criteria
If the record confirms that the H→AH and B→AB transitions are progressing as planned, it is judged PASS (augmentation management in progress/complete); if there is no transition-support plan or no progress is confirmed, it is judged FAIL (augmentation management insufficient).
