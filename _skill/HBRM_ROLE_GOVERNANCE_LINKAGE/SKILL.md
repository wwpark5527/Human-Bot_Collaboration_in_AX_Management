# Governance Linkage — Skill

## Purpose
Connect the five elements of authority, verification, approval, recording, and responsibility structure to one another, thereby completing a governance system in which the trust-management standards actually work.

## Input
- The deliverable of the trust-management stage (the collaboration standards that prevent overtrust and distrust)
- An overview of the organization's decision-making and approval system

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Check the deliverable of the trust-management stage.
2. Designate the authority holder who will execute the collaboration standards.
3. Decide the result-verification procedure and the verifying party.
4. Decide the approval procedure for verified results and the approving party.
5. Decide the method of recording the authority, verification, and approval process and specify the locus of ultimate responsibility, thereby connecting the five elements.

## Output
Authority-holder designation table, verification and approval procedure definition document, recording and responsibility structure linkage document.

## Criteria
If all five elements of authority, verification, approval, recording, and responsibility structure are defined and connected to one another without a break, it is judged PASS (governance linkage complete); if one or more elements are missing or the connection is broken, it is judged FAIL (governance linkage incomplete).
