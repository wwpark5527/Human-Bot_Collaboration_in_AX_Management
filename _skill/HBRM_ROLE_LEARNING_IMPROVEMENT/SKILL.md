# Learning and Improvement Management — Skill

## Purpose
Accumulate the feedback and errors that arose in the collaboration process of humans and bots into organizational memory, so that HBRM's role system improves itself.

## Input
- The deliverable of the governance linkage stage (the authority, verification, approval, recording, and responsibility structures)
- The raw feedback and error data that arose in the operation process

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Check the deliverable of the governance linkage stage.
2. Collect the feedback and errors that arose on both the human and the bot side.
3. Record and accumulate the collected content in the form of organizational memory.
4. Re-diagnose by mapping the accumulated memory onto the five types of role-balance imbalance.
5. Reflect the re-diagnosis results in the next HBRM operating cycle.

## Output
Feedback and error collection record, organizational memory repository, role-balance re-diagnosis reflection results.

## Criteria
If feedback and errors are accumulated into organizational memory, it is judged PASS (learning and improvement management complete); if they are not accumulated, it is judged FAIL (learning and improvement management incomplete).
