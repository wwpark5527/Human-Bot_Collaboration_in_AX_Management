# Role-Balance Management — Skill

## Purpose
Diagnose the role balance of the three dimensions human↔human, human↔bot, and bot↔bot, and adjust the concentration, deficiency, and conflict of roles.

## Input
- The deliverable of the augmentation management stage (the H→AH, B→AB transition progress record)
- The organization's current status of role allocation

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Survey the current state of role allocation in the three dimensions (human-human/human-bot/bot-bot).
2. Check the five imbalance types: excess of technical roles, excess of automation roles, lack of verification roles, lack of coordination roles, and unclear responsibility roles.
3. Establish adjustment measures for the discovered concentration, deficiency, and conflict.
4. Execute the adjustment measures and document the results.
5. Pass the adjustment results on to the trust-management stage.

## Output
Role-balance diagnosis table for the three dimensions, adjustment measure proposals by imbalance type, role-balance management document.

## Criteria
If the role balance of the three dimensions has been diagnosed and adjustment measures for the discovered imbalances are confirmed in a document, it is judged PASS (role-balance management complete); if no diagnosis has been made or the imbalance is left unattended, it is judged FAIL (role-balance management insufficient).
