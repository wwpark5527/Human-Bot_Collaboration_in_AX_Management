# Trust Management — Skill

## Purpose
Establish collaboration standards that prevent both overtrust (uncritical acceptance) and distrust (impeded collaboration) in the collaboration between humans and bots.

## Input
- The deliverable of the role-balance management stage (the record of adjusted role concentration, deficiency, and conflict)
- The list of human-bot collaboration points

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Check the human-bot collaboration points.
2. Check the risk of overtrust in a bot's results at each point.
3. Check the risk of excessive distrust in a bot's results at each point.
4. Establish together an overtrust-prevention verification procedure and a distrust-mitigating procedure for presenting grounds for trust.
5. Document the established collaboration standards and pass them on to the governance linkage stage.

## Output
Overtrust-prevention standard (result verification procedure), distrust-mitigation standard (procedure for presenting grounds for trust), trust management collaboration standards document.

## Criteria
If both the overtrust-prevention standard and the distrust-mitigation standard have been established in a document, it is judged PASS (trust management complete); if even one of the two has not been established, it is judged FAIL (trust management incomplete).
