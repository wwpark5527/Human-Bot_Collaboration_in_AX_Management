# Baek (魄) — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether, while making use of robots, automation devices, and mechanical execution in the domain of baek (body, senses, labor, physical execution), human rest, safety, dignity, and right of judgment are not violated and the body and labor have not become subordinated to the system (whether baeksan is present).

## Input
- Whether the target work belongs to the domain of baek (body, physical execution)
- The execution content that robots and automation devices perform by proxy
- Whether procedures for protecting human rest, safety, dignity, and right of judgment exist

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Check whether the target function is in the domain of baek, belonging to the body, the senses, labor, or physical execution.
2. Grasp to what extent robots and automation devices are performing that function by proxy and extending it.
3. Check whether there are procedures ensuring that human rest, safety, dignity, and right of judgment are not violated in the course of automation.
4. Determine whether there are signs (baeksan) that the human body and labor are subordinated only to the rhythms and rules set by the system.

## Output
The status of automated proxy performance in the domain of baek (body, physical execution) and the determination result on whether baeksan (the systematization of body and labor) is present.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when automation is used while human rest, safety, dignity, and right of judgment are protected, and FAIL (baeksan) when the human body and labor become subordinated to the system and are treated as a resource.
