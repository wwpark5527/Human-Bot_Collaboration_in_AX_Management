# Hon (魂) — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether, while making use of AI's analysis, reasoning, language generation, and judgment assistance in the domain of Hon (thought, memory, judgment, creation, decision-making), the human has not lost the sovereignty of judgment by which they question, verify, and take responsibility for themselves (whether honbi is present).

## Input
- Whether the target work belongs to the domain of Hon (cognition)
- The content of the analysis, reasoning, language generation, and judgment assistance provided by AI
- Whether a human verification and responsibility procedure exists

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the target function is the domain of Hon, belonging to thought, memory, judgment, creation, or decision-making.
2. Grasp to what degree AI is performing and assisting that function on the human's behalf.
3. Confirm whether there is a procedure by which the human questions and verifies AI's output for themselves.
4. Judge whether there are signs (honbi) of accepting AI's judgment as it is without verification or of evading responsibility.

## Output
The status of AI's performance on the human's behalf in the domain of Hon (cognitive functions) and the result of judging whether honbi (the loss of the sovereignty of judgment) is present.

## Criteria
If AI assistance is used while the human procedure of questioning, verification, and responsibility is maintained, it is judged PASS; if AI's judgment is accepted as it is without verification, it is judged FAIL (honbi).
