# Ultimate responsibility must remain with humans and the organization — Skill

## Purpose
In accordance with the principle that AI can advise but cannot take responsibility, determine whether the holder of the final right of judgment and the subject of responsibility in important decisions are clearly designated as a human or an organization.

## Input
- The importance and nature of the decision under review
- The record of AI's advice, analysis, and recommendations involved in that decision
- The final approval and signature records and the documents on the locus of accountability

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Determine whether the target decision qualifies as an "important decision."
2. Check to what extent AI's advice and analysis were involved in the decision process.
3. Check whether the final approval and signature were made by a designated human or organizational unit.
4. Determine whether the locus of accountability is attributed to humans and the organization and has not been implicitly shifted to AI or the system.

## Output
The result of confirming the holder of the final right of judgment and the subject of responsibility in important decisions, and the determination of whether responsibility has been transferred.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the final right of judgment and responsibility are explicitly attributed to humans and the organization, and FAIL when the subject of responsibility is unclear or has been transferred to AI or the system.
