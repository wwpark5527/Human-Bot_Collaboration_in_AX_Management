# Technology adoption must be designed together with governance — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether an AI or automation technology adoption plan does not stop at securing function but also designs governance including authority, responsibility, approval, recording, auditing, and risk review.

## Input
- The adoption plan and functional scope of the target AI or automation system
- The design documents for each of the items authority, responsibility, approval, recording, auditing, and risk review, or the fact of their absence

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Grasp the functional scope of the system scheduled for adoption.
2. Confirm whether rules on authority and responsibility are included in the adoption design.
3. Confirm whether the approval and recording procedures are included.
4. Confirm whether the auditing and risk review procedures are included, and make an overall judgment.

## Output
The result of judging whether the six governance elements (authority, responsibility, approval, recording, auditing, risk review) are included in the AI or automation adoption plan.

## Criteria
If all six elements are included, it is judged PASS; if even one is missing, it is judged FAIL (deficient governance).
