# Automation must not reduce humans to components — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether the efficiency improvement of robot and automation systems is infringing on human rest, safety, dignity, and right of judgment and thereby degrading the human into a component of the system (baeksan).

## Input
- The target automation or robot system and its way of raising efficiency
- Impact data on human rest, safety, dignity, and right of judgment, or the record of infringement reports

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Grasp in what way the target automation system raises efficiency.
2. Confirm whether human rest time is infringed.
3. Confirm whether human safety is infringed.
4. Confirm whether human dignity and right of judgment are infringed, and make an overall judgment.

## Output
The way in which the automation system raises efficiency and the result of judging whether human rest, safety, dignity, and right of judgment are infringed.

## Criteria
If rest, safety, dignity, and right of judgment are all protected, it is judged PASS; if even one is infringed, it is judged FAIL (componentization, baeksan).
