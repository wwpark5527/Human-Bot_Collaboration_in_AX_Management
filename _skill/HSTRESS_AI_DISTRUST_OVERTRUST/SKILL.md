# Stress from AI Distrust or Overtrust — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether a stress of an AX organization member corresponds to AI distrust/overtrust stress among the human stress types (5 types), and provide the grounds for a response that establishes an evidence-based level of trust.

## Input
- Records of AI-related trust/distrust expressions observed in members' utterances and behavior
- Observed context related to judgment and responsibility avoidance (whether AI recommendations are accepted, whether verification procedures are omitted, etc.)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether AI distrust signals ("what if it is wrong? what about hallucination? what about manipulated data?") appear in the expression of stress.
2. Confirm whether AI overtrust signals ("it recommended it, so it must be right") appear at the same time so that there is a movement back and forth between the two extremes.
3. Confirm whether signs of a weakening of judgment or responsibility avoidance are observed in the state of overtrust.
4. Synthesize the confirmed characteristics and judge whether it is AI distrust/overtrust stress.

## Output
The judgment result of whether the target situation corresponds to AI distrust/overtrust stress, and the priority for re-establishing the level of trust.

## Criteria
If a wavering between distrust and overtrust is confirmed, judge PASS (AI distrust/overtrust stress); otherwise judge FAIL (another human stress type).
