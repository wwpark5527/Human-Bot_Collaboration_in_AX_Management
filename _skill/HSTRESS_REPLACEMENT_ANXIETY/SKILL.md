# Replacement Anxiety — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether a stress of an AX organization member corresponds to replacement anxiety among the human stress types (5 types), and provide the grounds for a response that reconfirms distinctively human capabilities.

## Input
- Records of stress expressions observed in members' utterances and behavior
- Observed context related to threats to expertise (promotion competition, evaluation criteria, whether the person is a knowledge worker, etc.)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the perception of a threat regarding a sense of inferiority in expertise, reduction to a supporting role, or loss of promotion opportunities appears in the expression of stress.
2. Distinguish whether the cause is not ambiguity in the division of roles but the perception of a threat to one's expertise and value of existence.
3. Confirm the context that affects the intensity of the threat perception, such as whether the person is a knowledge worker.
4. Synthesize the confirmed characteristics and judge whether it is replacement anxiety.

## Output
The judgment result of whether the target situation corresponds to replacement anxiety, and the priority for reconfirming distinctively human capabilities.

## Criteria
If the perception of a threat regarding a sense of inferiority in expertise, reduction to a supporting role, or loss of promotion opportunities is confirmed, judge PASS (replacement anxiety); otherwise judge FAIL (another human stress type).
