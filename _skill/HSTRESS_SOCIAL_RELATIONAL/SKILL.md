# Social and Relational Stress — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether the stress of a member of an AX organization falls under social and relational stress among the human stress types (5 types), and provide the grounds for responses of relational support and empathy supplementation.

## Input
- Records of stress expressions and remarks observed in human-bot collaboration situations
- The observed relational context (customer service, AI supervision, acceptance of AI-generated feedback, collaboration frequency, and so on)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the expression of stress originated in the collaborative relationship with bots.
2. Confirm whether there are signs of psychological distance from bots, deterioration of human-to-human relationships, social isolation, and increased emotional labor.
3. Confirm whether there is a lack of emotional empathy in customer service, AI supervision, acceptance of AI-generated feedback, and collaboration situations.
4. Synthesize the confirmed characteristics and judge whether it is social and relational stress.

## Output
The judgment result as to whether the target situation falls under social and relational stress, and the priorities for relational support and empathy supplementation.

## Criteria
If one or more of psychological distance, relationship deterioration, social isolation, and increased emotional labor is confirmed in the collaborative relationship with bots, it is judged PASS (social and relational stress); otherwise it is judged FAIL (another human stress type).
