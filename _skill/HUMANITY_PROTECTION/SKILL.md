# Protection of Humanity (humanity) — Skill

## Purpose
Apply the top-level principle of ensuring that AI is aligned with human existence and development when a leader of a hybrid organization judges direction and orchestration.

## Input
- The directional judgment the leader intends to make, or an orchestration design proposal
- The AI utilization plan or its current status

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Identify the object of the leader's directional judgment (or orchestration design).
2. Check whether that judgment aligns AI with human existence and development.
3. If there are elements that are not aligned, identify them and propose a direction for adjustment.
4. Reconfirm whether the adjusted judgment satisfies the criterion of protecting humanity.

## Output
A state in which AI is aligned with human existence and development — the directionality of a hybrid organization in which humanity is protected.

## Criteria
If the leader's judgment and the use of AI are aligned with human existence and development, it is judged PASS (humanity is protected); otherwise it is judged FAIL (risk of damage to humanity).
