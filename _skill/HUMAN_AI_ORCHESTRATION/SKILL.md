# Human-AI Orchestration — Skill

## Purpose
By designing, controlling, and amplifying the human-AI collaboration structure, it exercises the orchestrating, integrating, and designing capability that creates new value, organizations, markets, and order by using AI.

## Input
- The current state of the multiple AI agents and human members that need to be orchestrated
- Information on the not-yet-designed collaboration structure and team role balance (TRB)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Identify the AI agents that need to be orchestrated and their roles.
2. Design the division of roles between humans and bots (team role balance).
3. Design the collaboration structure (including the common context, decision criteria, and governance structure).
4. Do not remain an executor, but orchestrate the whole from the standpoint of integrating the capabilities of humans and AI.
5. Inspect whether the designed structure leads to the creation of new value, organizations, markets, and order.

## Output
A designed, integrated, and orchestrated human-AI collaboration structure — leadership exercised as an augmented orchestrator through orchestrating multiple AI agents, designing human and bot roles, and designing the collaboration structure.

## Criteria
If the human-AI collaboration structure is designed, controlled, and amplified and the leader functions as an augmented orchestrator it is judged PASS (orchestration capability exercised), and if it remains at individual execution it is judged FAIL (executor level).
