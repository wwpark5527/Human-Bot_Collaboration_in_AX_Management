# Human-AI Orchestration Capability — Skill

## Purpose
Support the ability to design a human + AI system through coordinating multiple AI agents, designing human and bot roles, designing team role balance (TRB), and designing the collaboration structure.

## Input
- A list of AI agents within the organization and the current status of role division
- Information on the competencies and roles of human members

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Identify the multiple AI agents within the organization and design the mode of coordination.
2. Clearly design the roles of humans and bots.
3. Apply team role balance (TRB) and adjust the role placement.
4. Design the collaboration structure and review it as a whole.

## Output
A designed human-AI collaboration structure and role placement.

## Criteria
If, beyond the level of being good on one's own, a human + AI system is designed and the collaboration structure and role placement are completed, it is judged PASS (orchestration capability secured); if one remains at the level of individual competency alone, it is judged FAIL (rework of the system design is needed).
