# Human Stress Related to AI — Skill

## Purpose
Recognize the stress humans undergo during AI adoption, and make it possible to diagnose the cause of AI adoption failure first from the perspective of human reaction failure rather than technology failure.

## Input
- Information on the organizational members targeted for AI adoption
- Past AI adoption failure cases (where they exist)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Survey the organizational members targeted for AI adoption for fear of replacement, fear of surveillance, identity threat, sense of lost competence, loss of meaning, and concern over damage to humanity.
2. Collect past AI adoption failure cases and classify their causes into technology failure and human reaction failure.
3. Reflect mitigation measures (communication, participation, role redesign, and so on) for each identified stress factor in the design.
4. After adoption, monitor human reactions periodically to check whether stress recurs and adjust measures when necessary.

## Output
Human stress factor diagnosis results, failure cause classification (technology vs. human reaction), stress mitigation measures.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS (human stress reflected) when the possibility of human reaction failure is examined first in analyzing the causes of AI adoption failure and stress mitigation measures are reflected, and FAIL (human stress not reflected) otherwise.
