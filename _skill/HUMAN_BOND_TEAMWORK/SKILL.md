# Teamwork in the Human-Bond Aspect — Skill

## Purpose
Accurately diagnose the human-bond aspect of teamwork through the measurement of group cohesiveness, thereby grasping its effect on members' perceived performance and providing one axis of the comprehensive teamwork diagnosis to be combined with the task-activation aspect.

## Input
- The roster of the department/team to be measured
- A group cohesiveness measurement instrument
- Members' perceived performance data (for combined analysis)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Fix the department/team to be measured.
2. Collect the degree of psychological attraction among members with a group cohesiveness measurement instrument.
3. Compute the results as the teamwork indicator for the human-bond aspect.
4. Analyze the correlation with members' perceived performance data.
5. Combine with the results of the task-activation aspect (TRB) to produce a comprehensive diagnosis of departmental teamwork.

## Output
Human-bond measurement values based on group cohesiveness, analytical material on the effect on perceived performance, and comparative material for combination with TRB.

## Criteria
If the measurement targets group cohesiveness (psychological attraction among members) and corresponds to the supplementary fit aspect, it is judged PASS (a valid diagnosis); if it targets the degree to which the nine team roles are exercised or complementary fit, it is judged FAIL (confusion with the task-activation aspect).
