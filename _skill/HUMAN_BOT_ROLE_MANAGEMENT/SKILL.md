# HBRM (Human-Bot Role Management) — Skill

## Purpose
Review the organization's practices of diversity management, complementary fit, human-AI collaboration, and organizational AX together with the existing HRM system, and judge whether the human-bot resource management principle (HBRM) is applied and the level of its integration.

## Input
- Materials on the organization's systems and practices related to diversity management, complementary fit, human-AI collaboration, and organizational AX
- Documents of the existing HRM (human resource management) system

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Collect the practices of diversity management, complementary fit, human-AI collaboration, and organizational AX.
2. Check whether the existing HRM system also encompasses bot resources.
3. Compare whether each practice is integrated under a single HBRM principle.
4. Judge whether HBRM is applied and the level of its integration.

## Output
The judgment result regarding whether the human-bot resource management principle (HBRM) of the AX organization is applied and the level of its integration.

## Criteria
If diversity management, complementary fit, human-AI collaboration, and organizational AX are integrated into a single HBRM principle, it is judged PASS; if they are scattered as individual practices or bot resources are omitted, it is judged FAIL.
