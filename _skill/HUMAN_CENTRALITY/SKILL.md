# Human Centrality — Skill

## Purpose
Identify whether an AI- or AX-related decision presupposes 'the survival and development of humans' and whether the subject of that judgment rests with humans, and determine whether it has fallen into zero-sum thinking of the 'humans first, bots sacrificed' form.

## Input
- The object of the AI/AX application or decision and its impact
- The subject of judgment (human vs. the AI judging by itself)
- Whether the logic of judgment is zero-sum (premised on sacrificing bots) or positive-sum (oriented toward augmentation)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Evaluate whether the object harms or helps the survival and development of humans.
2. Confirm whether the subject of that judgment rests with humans.
3. If it is judged to be harmful, reject it; otherwise, approve it.
4. Confirm that the logic of judgment contains no zero-sum thinking of the 'humans first, bots sacrificed' form.

## Output
The judgment result on whether human centrality is satisfied, and the rejection/approval decision with its grounds.

## Criteria
When the subject of judgment is human, the evaluation is made by the criterion of human survival and development, and there is no zero-sum thinking, it is judged PASS.
