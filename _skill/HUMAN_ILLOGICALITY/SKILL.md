# Human Illogicality — Skill

## Purpose
AI/bots do not predict human behavior by logic and efficiency alone, but recognize the human characteristic of being moved by emotion, relationships, symbols, identity, and habit, and reflect it in design and operational judgment.

## Input
- Information on the human stakeholder groups the bot deals with
- The design proposals and policy proposals under review

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Investigate the emotional, relational, and symbolic reaction patterns of the human stakeholder groups the bot deals with.
2. Add human acceptability criteria to the evaluation procedure so that proposed designs and policies are not evaluated by efficiency/logic criteria alone.
3. Identify in advance the points at risk of rejection due to lack of trust and prepare mitigation measures.
4. Establish and execute an explanation and communication plan for changes where emotional resistance is expected.

## Output
Human illogicality factor checklist, acceptability and trust evaluation results, emotional resistance management measures.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS (human illogicality reflected) when design and operation reflect not only efficiency/logic but also human emotion, relationships, symbols, identity, and habit, and FAIL (human illogicality not reflected) otherwise.
