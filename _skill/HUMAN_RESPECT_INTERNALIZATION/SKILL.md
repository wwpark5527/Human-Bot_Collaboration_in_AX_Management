# Internalization of Respect for Humans — Skill

## Purpose
Internalize respect for humans, the most important element of bot education, not as moral injection but as a system design problem of ensuring that only actions which do not infringe upon humans are selected.

## Input
- The bot's candidate actions and scenarios
- Classical criteria for the duty of respect for humans and safety, such as the three laws of robotics

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Review classical duties of respect for humans and safety such as the three laws of robotics and take them as the starting point of internalization.
2. Exclude the approach of injecting respect for humans as a moral teaching, and redefine it as a system design problem.
3. Identify, among the bot's candidate actions, those that infringe upon humans, and redesign the action, reward, and verification structure so that such actions are not selected.
4. Reflect in the design an incentive mechanism that makes actions not respecting humans disadvantageous within the system.
5. Verify whether the redesigned structure actually selects only actions that do not infringe upon humans.

## Output
An action, reward, and verification structure redesigned so that only actions that do not infringe upon humans are selected.

## Criteria
If the bot's action, reward, and verification structure operates so as to select only actions that do not infringe upon humans, it is judged PASS (the internalization of respect for humans is satisfied); if it relies solely on moral injection or an action infringing upon humans is selected, it is judged FAIL (internalization not satisfied).
