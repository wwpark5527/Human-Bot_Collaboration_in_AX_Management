# True AX Organization / Hybrid Organization (AH+AB) — Skill

## Purpose
Identify whether an organization corresponds to a hybrid organization (AH+AB, a true AX organization), and review the conditions for reaching it (the use of the organizational AX OS and the realization of HBRM).

## Input
- The augmentation status of humans/bots
- Whether the shared/governance context and AI governance are embedded in the bots
- The structure of role-centered division and sharing as against jobs
- The current status of the application of the organizational AX OS and HBRM

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the humans have been augmented into AH.
2. Confirm whether the bots have been augmented into AB (the shared/governance context + AI governance embedded).
3. Confirm whether humans and bots divide and share on a role basis rather than a job basis and jointly judge and learn.
4. If all conditions are satisfied, judge it to be a hybrid organization and review the state of application of the organizational AX OS and HBRM.

## Output
The hybrid organization judgment result, a list of transition gaps, and recommendations for applying the organizational AX OS and HBRM.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS as a hybrid organization when both humans and bots are mutually augmented and operate as a role-based collaborative cognition system.
