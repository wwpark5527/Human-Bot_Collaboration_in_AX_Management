# Inclusive / Just AI Transition ESG — Skill

## Purpose
Prevent and mitigate the stratification (the owning, using, and directing stratum versus the evaluated, replaced, managed, and excluded stratum) that can arise during the AI-transition process, and build in the organization an expanded ESG management philosophy (Inclusive AI Transition ESG) that protects humans' roles, income, right to judge, right to learn, and dignity.

## Input
- The organization's current status of AI introduction, documents of the existing ESG system, and materials grasping the stratified structure of AI use (owning, using, directing vs. the objects of evaluation and replacement)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Identify the stratum that owns, uses, and directs AI and the stratum that is the object of evaluation, replacement, management, and exclusion, and diagnose the risk of stratification.
2. Design a system that equitably guarantees the right of access to AI and the right to learn to organizational members.
3. Reflect humans' right of judgment in the procedures of AI use, verification, and approval.
4. Build a system of AI-related recording and attribution of responsibility.
5. Establish a benefit-sharing system for the outcomes of AI use.
6. Distinguish the level of grounds by the four layers of verifiable fact / reasonable interpretation / proposed concept / execution model.
7. Integrate the whole into the company's ESG responsibility system.

## Output
A system guaranteeing the right of AI access and the right to learn, a procedural manual for AI use, verification, and approval, criteria for AI recording and attribution of responsibility, a benefit-sharing system, and an integration proposal for Inclusive AI Transition ESG.

## Criteria
If AI access, learning, use, verification, approval, recording, responsibility, and benefit-sharing are all incorporated into the ESG responsibility system and the risk of stratification is mitigated, it is judged PASS; if one or more is omitted, it is judged FAIL.
