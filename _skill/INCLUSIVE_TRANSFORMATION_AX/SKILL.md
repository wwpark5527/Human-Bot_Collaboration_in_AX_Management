# Inclusive Transformation AX — Skill

## Purpose
Redesign the efficiency-centered AI transition from the perspective of responsibility and inclusiveness so as to prevent and mitigate AI stratification, and realize an organizational transformation in which humans' roles and rights are not damaged.

## Input
- The organization's current AX/AI transition plan, policies related to human roles and authority, and materials on the current ESG status

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Diagnose whether the current AX is designed solely around efficiency, productivity, and cost reduction.
2. Check the level of protection of humans' role, right to judge, right to learn, right to transition, right to appeal, and right to share in performance.
3. Evaluate whether the six risks of efficiency-centered AX appear in the organization.
4. Redesign the AX transition plan so as to reflect the perspective of responsibility and inclusiveness.
5. Connect the redesigned framework to the ESG S·G execution structures.

## Output
An AX efficiency-inclusiveness diagnostic report, a checklist for the protection of the six rights, a redesigned Inclusive Transformation AX roadmap, and a document on the ESG-linked execution structure.

## Criteria
If protective devices for the six rights are included in the AI transition plan and it is connected to the ESG S·G structures, it is judged PASS; if it is designed solely by efficiency indicators and the six rights are not reflected, it is judged FAIL.
