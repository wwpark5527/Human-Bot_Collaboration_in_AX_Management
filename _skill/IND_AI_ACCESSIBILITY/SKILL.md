# AI Accessibility — Skill

## Purpose
By calculating the employee AI-tool access rate and the inter-departmental access gap, make it possible to confirm from the accessibility side whether an organization operates its AI transition responsibly. It measures the first of the 12 Inclusive Transition ESG indicators.

## Input
- The list of AI tools and infrastructure required for work, the status of account and authority issuance per tool, the employee roster and department composition, and the measurement results of the previous cycle (for time-series comparison)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Fix the scope of the tools to be measured and the conditions for judging "accessible".
2. Calculate the company-wide employee AI-tool access rate.
3. Calculate the per-department access rate for each department.
4. Calculate the inter-departmental gap (the highest-lowest difference and the distribution).
5. Present the access rate and the gap bound together as one reporting unit.
6. Fix a measurement cycle and accumulate a time series on the same standard.

## Output
The access-rate figure and the per-department gap value — the scope measured and the judgment conditions, the company-wide access rate, the per-department access rates, the inter-departmental gap value and distribution, the time-series record.

## Criteria
With the access judgment standard stated explicitly, it is judged PASS when both the access rate and the per-department gap are calculated and reported together, and FAIL if the standard is unclear so that the figures are not reproducible, or if only the average access rate is calculated and the gap is not produced.
