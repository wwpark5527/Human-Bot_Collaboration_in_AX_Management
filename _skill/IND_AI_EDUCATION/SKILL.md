# AI Education — Skill

## Purpose
By calculating the AI-literacy education completion rate and job-specific reskilling hours, make it possible to confirm whether the organization is actually building in its members the capability required for AI transformation. It measures the second of the 12 Inclusive Transition ESG indicators.

## Input
- The list of AI-literacy education courses and the attendance and completion records, the job-specific retraining programs and the records of hours invested, the employee roster and job composition, the results of the job impact assessment of step 4 labor transition, and the measurement results of the previous cycle (for time-series comparison)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Fix the education scope and the completion judgment standard.
2. Calculate the AI-literacy education completion rate.
3. Aggregate job-specific reskilling hours (fixing in advance whether reskilling and upskilling are distinguished).
4. Confirm the distribution across jobs and check it against the jobs affected by labor transition.
5. Present the completion rate and the reskilling hours bound together as one reporting unit.
6. Fix a measurement cycle and accumulate a time series on the same standard.

## Output
The education completion-rate figure and job-specific reskilling hours — the education scope and the completion judgment standard, the company-wide completion rate, job-specific reskilling hours, the distribution across jobs and the result of checking it against the affected jobs, the time-series record.

## Criteria
With the completion judgment standard stated explicitly, it is judged PASS when both the completion rate and job-specific reskilling hours are calculated and reported together, and FAIL if the standard is unclear so that the figures are not reproducible, or if only the completion rate is calculated and the reskilling hours are not produced.
