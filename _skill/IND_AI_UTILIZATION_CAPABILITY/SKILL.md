# AI Utilization Capability — Skill

## Purpose
By calculating AI-utilization work-improvement cases and the productivity-gain distribution, make it possible to confirm whether the access opened up and the capability built in the organization have been converted into actual work performance, and where within the organization that performance took hold. It measures the third of the 12 Inclusive Transition ESG indicators.

## Input
- AI tool usage logs and per-department adoption status, the submission records of work-improvement cases, productivity-related operational data such as work processing time, throughput, and rework rate, the employee roster and department and job composition, the most recent measurement results of the AI accessibility and AI education indicators, and the measurement results of the previous cycle (for time-series comparison)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Fix the standard for recognizing work-improvement cases (comparability, identifiability of the AI contribution, reproducibility).
2. Fix the case collection routes and mark non-submitting departments.
3. Tally the AI-utilization work-improvement cases together with department and job.
4. Fix the measurement unit of productivity gain.
5. Calculate the productivity-gain distribution by department and by job (reporting the company-wide average alone is prohibited).
6. Check against the AI accessibility and AI education indicators and mark the range in which access and education exist but performance does not.
7. Present the case tally and the gain distribution bound together as one reporting unit.
8. Fix a measurement cycle and accumulate a time series on the same standard.

## Output
The tally of work-improvement cases and the productivity-gain distribution — the case recognition standard, the list and number of cases, the gain measurement unit, the gain distribution by department and by job and the ranges with no gain, the result of checking against the accessibility and education indicators, the time-series record.

## Criteria
With the case recognition standard and the gain measurement unit stated explicitly, it is judged PASS when both the case tally and the productivity-gain distribution are calculated and reported together, and FAIL if the recognition standard is unclear so that the tally is not reproducible, or if the productivity gain is reported only as a single company-wide average value and the distribution by department and by job is not produced.
