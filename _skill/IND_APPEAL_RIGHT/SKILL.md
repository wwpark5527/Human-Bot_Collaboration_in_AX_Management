# Right to Appeal — Skill

## Purpose
By calculating the number of requests to review AI results and the correction/remedy processing rate, make it possible to confirm whether the channel for raising objections to AI decisions actually works and whether the objections raised lead to correction of the decision or remedy for the party concerned. It measures the seventh of the 12 Inclusive Transition ESG indicators.

## Input
- The records and intake register of the appeal desk, whether oral and informal submissions are recorded, the list of AI decision cases and decision types, the results of review processing and the notification records, the time taken to process, the user-understanding measurement results of the explainability indicator, and the measurement results of the previous cycle (for time-series comparison)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Identify the intake routes for review requests and state explicitly the routes that are not recorded.
2. Fix the recognized scope of requests.
3. Tally the number of requests to review AI results by decision type and by department and mark the ranges with no requests.
4. Record alongside the grounds for interpreting the number of requests (existence and accessibility of the channel, understanding of explanations).
5. Classify the processing results into decision corrected, party concerned remedied, dismissed, and unprocessed.
6. Calculate the correction/remedy processing rate (the numerator is corrections plus remedies, excluding dismissals and mere closures).
7. Record the time taken to process from receipt to notification of the result.
8. Present the number of requests and the processing rate bound together as one reporting unit.
9. Fix a measurement cycle and accumulate a time series on the same standard.

## Output
The number of review requests and the correction/remedy processing rate — the intake routes and whether they are recorded, the recognized scope of requests, the number of requests by type and by department and the ranges with no requests, the grounds for interpretation, the classification of processing results, the correction/remedy processing rate, the time taken to process, the time-series record.

## Criteria
With the intake routes and the recognized scope stated explicitly, it is judged PASS when both the number of requests and the correction/remedy processing rate are calculated, the numerator of the processing rate is limited to corrections and remedies, and the grounds for interpretation are attached to the count and reported together; it is judged FAIL if dismissals or closures were included in the numerator, if a low count was reported as good without grounds for interpretation, or if only the count was tallied and the processing rate was not produced.
