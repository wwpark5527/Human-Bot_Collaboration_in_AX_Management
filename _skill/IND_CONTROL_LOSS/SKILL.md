# Control-Loss Type — Skill

## Purpose
It determines whether a member's backlash against AI adoption corresponds to the control-loss type originating in the perception of a weakening of the right of judgment, expertise, and authority, and provides the grounds for a response that clarifies the locus of the final right of judgment.

## Input
- Records of remarks and behavior of the target member or job group (physicians, HR, the financial sector, and so on) related to AI recommendation and automated decision-making
- Observed signs of resistance (related to managers, expert authority, algorithms, and recommendations)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Check whether the target reaction appears in a professional situation where AI recommendation and automated decision-making are expanding.
2. Check for signs of manager resistance or defense of expert authority.
3. Check for signs of algorithmic distrust, or of refusal of AI recommendations.
4. Synthesize the confirmed characteristics and determine whether it is the control-loss type.

## Output
The determination result of whether the target reaction corresponds to the control-loss type, and the priority of responses that clarify the locus of the final right of judgment.

## Criteria
If one or more of manager resistance, defense of expert authority, algorithmic distrust, and refusal of recommendations made by AI is confirmed, it is judged PASS (control-loss type); otherwise it is judged FAIL (another type).
