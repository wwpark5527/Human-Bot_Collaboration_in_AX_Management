# Humanity-Defense Type — Skill

## Purpose
It determines whether a member's backlash against AI adoption corresponds to the humanity-defense type originating in the protection of human uniqueness, and provides the grounds for a response that preserves the uniquely human domain.

## Input
- Records of remarks and behavior of the target member or job group (artists, professors, counseling and education practitioners, and so on) related to generative AI
- Observed signs of reaction (related to creativity, relationality, emotion, ethics, and intuition, and the perception of non-understanding)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Check whether the target reaction appears in a domain where human uniqueness is a core value, such as creation, education, and counseling.
2. Check for signs of defending human creativity or emphasizing human relationality.
3. Check for signs of asserting the superiority of emotion, ethics, and intuition, or of the reaction that "AI does not understand humans."
4. Synthesize the confirmed characteristics and determine whether it is the humanity-defense type.

## Output
The determination result of whether the target reaction corresponds to the humanity-defense type, and the priority of responses that preserve the uniquely human domain.

## Criteria
If one or more of defending human creativity, emphasizing human relationality, asserting the superiority of emotion, ethics, and intuition, and the reaction that "AI does not understand humans" is confirmed, it is judged PASS (humanity-defense type); otherwise it is judged FAIL (another type).
