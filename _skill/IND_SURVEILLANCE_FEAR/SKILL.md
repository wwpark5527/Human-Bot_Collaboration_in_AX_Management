# Surveillance-Fear Type — Skill

## Purpose
It determines whether a member's backlash against AI adoption corresponds to the surveillance-fear type originating in anxiety about continuous observation and data collection, and provides the grounds for responses of transparency disclosure and privacy protection.

## Input
- Records of remarks and behavior of the target member or job group related to data-based management tools
- Observed signs of anxiety (related to monitoring, privacy, AI evaluation, and work logs)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Check whether the target reaction appears in a situation where datafication, logging, and tracking tools such as productivity tracking and behavior log collection are introduced.
2. Check for signs of anxiety over behavior monitoring or concern about privacy infringement.
3. Check for signs of fear of AI evaluation, or of stress from work logs.
4. Synthesize the confirmed characteristics and determine whether it is the surveillance-fear type.

## Output
The determination result of whether the target reaction corresponds to the surveillance-fear type, and the priority of responses of transparency disclosure and privacy protection.

## Criteria
If one or more of anxiety over behavior monitoring, concern about privacy infringement, fear of AI evaluation, and stress from work logs is confirmed, it is judged PASS (surveillance-fear type); otherwise it is judged FAIL (another type).
