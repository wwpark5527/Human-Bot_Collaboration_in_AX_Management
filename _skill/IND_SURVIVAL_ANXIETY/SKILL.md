# Survival-Anxiety Type — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether a member's backlash against AI adoption corresponds to primal anxiety over job replacement, that is, the survival-anxiety type, and provide grounds for an employment-stability response.

## Input
- Records of statements and behavior concerning AI adoption by the target members or job group (call center, office workers, developers, and so on)
- Observed signs of anxiety (relating to restructuring, role reduction, promotion, and wages)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the target reaction appears in a repetitive knowledge-work job group.
2. Confirm signs of fear of restructuring.
3. Confirm signs of anxiety over role reduction, reduced promotion opportunities, and wage decline.
4. Synthesize the confirmed features and judge whether it is the survival-anxiety type.

## Output
The judgment result of whether the target reaction corresponds to the survival-anxiety type, and the priority of the employment-stability response.

## Criteria
If one or more of fear of restructuring, concern over role reduction, concern over reduced promotion opportunities, and anxiety over wage decline are confirmed, judge PASS (survival-anxiety type); otherwise judge FAIL (another type).
