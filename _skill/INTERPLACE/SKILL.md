# Interplace — Skill

## Purpose
Measure the TR characteristics of individuals and jobs with high reliability and validity by combining the subject's own responses (subjective) with observer responses (objective), and relative assessment with absolute assessment, and provide the results in a form usable for practical decision-making.

## Input
- The list of individuals to be measured and observers (4 or more)
- Information on the target jobs

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Distribute the self-perception inventory (SPI) to the measurement subject themselves and collect the responses.
2. Distribute the observer assessment (OA) to 4 or more observers and collect the responses.
3. Collect job data with the job requirement exercise (JRE) and the job observer assessment (JOA).
4. Enter and transmit the collected data to the Interplace system.
5. The system processes the data in real time and generates 14 kinds of report.
6. Receive the results and use them for team building, appraisal, counseling, placement, manager training, and career development.

## Output
Per-individual TR type and level diagnostic reports (14 kinds), analysis results for person-person fit and person-job fit, and practical materials.

## Criteria
If both the subject's own responses and the observer responses (4 or more) are collected and, after processing mixing relative and absolute assessment, 14 kinds of report are output, judge PASS (valid measurement complete); if the response sample is insufficient, judge FAIL (re-collection required).
