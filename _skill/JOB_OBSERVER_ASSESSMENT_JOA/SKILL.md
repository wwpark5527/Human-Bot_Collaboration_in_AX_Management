# Job Observer Assessment (JOA: job observer assessment) — Skill

## Purpose
Collect the job observer's observation results accurately, and thereby provide the job-observation data to be used for deriving job characteristics and for staffing and selection advice.

## Input
- The definition of the job to be diagnosed
- The JOA questionnaire sheet
- Job observers who can observe that job

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Fix the job to be diagnosed.
2. Select job observers who can observe that job.
3. Proceed so that responses are given on the basis of the observation results (job-observation items).
4. Collect the responses and organize them into the Interplace input format.
5. Combine the JOA data with the JRE data and pass it on as input to job assessment.

## Output
The raw JOA response data by job, the job-observation data, and the input data for combination with JRE (job assessment material).

## Criteria
If the response was written on the basis of the results of job observation, judge PASS (a valid JOA response); if it was written in the manner of describing job requirements, judge FAIL (cannot be recognized as JOA).
