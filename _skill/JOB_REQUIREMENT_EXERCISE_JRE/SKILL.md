# Job Requirement Exercise (JRE: job requirement exercise) — Skill

## Purpose
Taking a job rather than a person as the assessment target, it accurately diagnoses the requirements that the job demands and provides the job-requirements data to be used in the person-job fit judgment.

## Input
- Definition of the job to be diagnosed
- The JRE questionnaire items
- Respondents who are well versed in that job

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Fix the job to be diagnosed.
2. Distribute the JRE to respondents who are well versed in that job.
3. Have them respond on the basis of the job-requirements items.
4. Collect the responses and organize them into the Interplace input format.
5. Combine the JRE data with the JOA data and hand it over as job-assessment material.

## Output
Raw JRE response data per job, job-requirements data, and input data for combination with the JOA (job-assessment material).

## Criteria
If the response was written on the basis of the job-requirements items it is judged PASS (a valid JRE response); if it targets a person's characteristics or is based on the results of observing the job it is judged FAIL (not admissible as a JRE).
