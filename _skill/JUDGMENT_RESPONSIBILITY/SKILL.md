# Judgment Responsibility — Skill

## Purpose
Following the principle that AI can generate many alternatives but the final judgment must be made by a person, it supports a person in making the final judgment on the organizational suitability and usability of AI results.

## Input
- The result artifact AI generated
- Information on the organization's purpose, work standards, and approval and review procedures

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Review whether the AI result is correct and whether it is suitable for the organization.
2. Judge whether the result may be released externally or reflected in decision-making.
3. Check whether further review or supervisor approval / legal / security review is required.
4. Initiate the further procedures when needed and settle the final decision on use.

## Output
The final judgment on whether the AI result may be used and whether approval or review is required.

## Criteria
If the final judgment on the result's accuracy, organizational suitability, releasability to the outside, admissibility for reflection in decision-making, and need for further approval has been made by a person, it is judged PASS (judgment responsibility fulfilled); if the AI result is used as is without judgment, it is judged FAIL (judgment procedure omitted).
