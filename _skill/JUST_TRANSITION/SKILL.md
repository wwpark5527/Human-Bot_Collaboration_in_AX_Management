# Just Transition — Skill

## Purpose
By applying to AI transition the just-transition principle that the transition process must be fair and inclusive and that no one should be left behind, it reflects in execution the source principle of AI Inclusive-Transition ESG, which protects humans' roles, income, judgment rights, learning rights, and dignity.

## Input
- The organization's AI transition plan and the list of affected personnel and roles

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm the just-transition principle (fairness, inclusiveness, no one left behind).
2. Identify the points in the AI transition plan where humans' roles, income, judgment rights, learning rights, and dignity risk being damaged.
3. Apply the just-transition principle to the identified risks and design mitigation measures.
4. Check continuously within the AI Inclusive-Transition ESG frame.

## Output
The proposition applying the just-transition principle to AI transition, and the supporting grounds document.

## Criteria
If the risk points that damage humans' roles, income, judgment rights, learning rights, and dignity are identified and mitigation measures are prepared, it is judged PASS; if they are left unattended, FAIL.
