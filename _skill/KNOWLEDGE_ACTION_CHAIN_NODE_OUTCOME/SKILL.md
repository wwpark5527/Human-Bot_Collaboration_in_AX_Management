# outcome (result) — Skill

## Purpose
It determines whether the 'outcome' node of the knowledge-action chain accurately captured the change in results rather than the deliverable.

## Input
- The actual action and deliverable that came out of the 'action' node

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Distinguish the deliverable itself from the change the deliverable caused.
2. Find the result on the basis not of "what was produced" but of "what changed."
3. Organize it into concrete changes in results such as improved decision-making, fewer omissions, and fewer customer complaints.
4. Pass the organized change in results to the next node (review).

## Output
The change in results (improved decision-making, fewer omissions, fewer customer complaints, and the like).

## Criteria
It is judged not by "what was produced?" but by "what changed?" If there is only a deliverable and no change in results is identified, it is judged FAIL.
