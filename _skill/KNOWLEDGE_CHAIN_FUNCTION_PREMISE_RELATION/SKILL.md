# Premise-Relation Measurement — Skill

## Purpose
It determines whether the premise-relation measurement function of the knowledge chain confirms that the foundational concept which is the premise of the advanced expression is actually understood, and thereby discovers the foundational-concept deficiency.

## Input
- The advanced concept (expression) to be checked
- The lower concept that underlies it

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Identify the advanced concept (expression) and the lower concept that underlies it.
2. Check the connection state of the higher concept and the lower concept.
3. Detect whether there is a state of speaking about the advanced concept without having understood the foundational concept.
4. Judge the deficiency that is found as "a state where the words are advanced but the foundational concept is weak."

## Output
Detection of a foundational-concept deficiency. A judgment of "a state where the words are advanced but the foundational concept is weak."

## Criteria
If whether there is a foundational-concept deficiency is actually judged, it is PASS; if it cannot be judged, FAIL.
