# Transferability Measurement — Skill

## Purpose
Judge whether the transferability measurement function of the knowledge chain actually measures "the ability to maintain the meaning structure even in a new situation" rather than "the ability to solve a familiar problem."

## Input
- The sentence, example, and domain originally learned by the person being assessed
- A set of changed sentences, examples, domains, and counterexamples

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm the originally learned sentence, example, and domain.
2. Present the sentence, example, and domain each in changed form and confirm whether understanding, principle, and structure are maintained.
3. Present a counterexample and confirm whether the concept is revised on one's own.
4. Synthesize the check results of the four items and judge the level of transferability.

## Output
A judgment on the level of transferability (whether the meaning structure is maintained into a new situation).

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when understanding maintained when the sentence changes, principle maintained when the example changes, structure applied when the domain changes, and concept revised when a counterexample is presented are all confirmed, and FAIL if even one is not confirmed.
