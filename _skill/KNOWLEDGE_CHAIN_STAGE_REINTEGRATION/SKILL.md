# Re-reflection into Organizational Knowledge — Skill

## Purpose
Judge whether the re-reflection into organizational knowledge stage of the knowledge chain absorbs the recorded result into the organizational knowledge system, so that knowledge becomes not "scattered responses" but a system that is connected, verified, and evolves, and actually updates the standard for the next judgment.

## Input
- The traceable record (grounds, process, version) passed on from the result recording stage
- The organization's existing knowledge and standards

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Compare the recorded result against the organization's existing knowledge and standards.
2. Connect the recorded result to organizational knowledge and verify it.
3. Update (evolve) the organizational knowledge system with the verified result.
4. Pass the updated knowledge system on to the next stage (reuse by the next AI/human).

## Output
An updated organizational knowledge system.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the result is absorbed into organizational knowledge and changes the standard for the next judgment, and FAIL when it is not absorbed.
