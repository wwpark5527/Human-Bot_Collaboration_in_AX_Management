# Reuse by the Next AI/Human — Skill

## Purpose
Judge whether the reuse by the next AI/human stage of the knowledge chain actually takes the accumulated organizational knowledge as input and, without starting over from scratch, produces a consistent next response that completes the circulation/accumulation structure.

## Input
- The organizational knowledge system updated in the re-reflection into organizational knowledge stage
- The new question/task of the next AI or the next human

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm the updated organizational knowledge system.
2. Provide this organizational knowledge as input to the new task of the next AI/human.
3. Check whether the task actually uses the accumulated knowledge and does not start over from scratch.
4. Confirm whether the circulation/accumulation structure has been completed through reuse and whether the next response maintains consistency.

## Output
Reused organizational knowledge and the consistent next response it produces.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the next task takes the accumulated organizational knowledge as input and consistency of results is secured, and FAIL otherwise.
