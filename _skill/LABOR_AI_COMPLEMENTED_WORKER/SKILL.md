# AI-Complemented Worker — Skill

## Purpose
Confirm the productivity improvement of AI-complemented workers and diagnose whether a commensurate improvement in treatment is achieved.

## Input
- Work performance indicators before and after the introduction of AI, and the current state of compensation and working conditions

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Identify the work and the workers whose productivity and decision-making ability improve through the use of AI.
2. Compare and measure the change in performance-demand standards before and after the introduction of AI.
3. Check whether the increase in performance demand leads to an excessive burden.
4. Prepare a plan for improving compensation and working conditions in proportion to the increment in productivity.
5. Re-examine the burden and satisfaction after the improvement plan has been applied.

## Output
Measurement data on the AI complementation effect, and a plan for improving compensation and treatment.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when productivity improvement is confirmed and improvement in treatment is achieved, and FAIL when only the demand for performance increases with no improvement in treatment.
