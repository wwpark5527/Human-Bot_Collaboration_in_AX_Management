# AI-displacement/reduction-risk worker — Skill

## Purpose
Identify the work at high risk of automation and the workers in charge of it, and prepare retraining and job-transition paths so as to respond to the erosion of the value of their work.

## Input
- The organization's list of work, the degree of repetitiveness and documentation of each item of work, and the current state of the personnel in charge

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Identify the repetitive, documented work and assess its automation potential.
2. Grasp the workers who handle that work and the share it takes in their duties.
3. Assess the urgency and the expected timing of the risk that the value of the work is reduced or disappears.
4. Establish paths for retraining, job transition, and transition into a context-designing role.
5. Periodically check the progress and the effect of the transition paths.

## Output
A list of the work and personnel at risk of automation, an assessment report on the urgency of the risk, and a plan for retraining and transition paths.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the work at risk and the persons in charge are identified and transition paths are prepared and operated, and FAIL when the risk is left unattended.
