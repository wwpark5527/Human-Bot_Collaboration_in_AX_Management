# AI-managed worker — Skill

## Purpose
Identify the workers who are the objects of algorithmic management (assignment, evaluation, monitoring) and disclose the standards transparently so as to secure understandability.

## Input
- The current state of the organization's algorithm-based work assignment, evaluation, and monitoring

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Identify the workers and work areas whose work is assigned, evaluated, and monitored by algorithms.
2. Document the judgment standards and the data sources of the algorithms being applied.
3. Disclose the standards to workers in an explainable form.
4. Confirm the workers' level of understanding and supplement any gap in understanding.
5. Improve opaque or unfair standards and re-announce them.

## Output
A document of the algorithmic assignment, evaluation, and monitoring standards, and a record of the explainability check.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the standards are disclosed and workers can understand them, and FAIL when a state in which the standards are opaque persists.
