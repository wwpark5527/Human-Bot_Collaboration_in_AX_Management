# Context-designer AX talent — Skill

## Purpose
Directly design the purposes, judgment standards, verification standards, and feedback systems to be assigned to AI, and thereby turn AI into part of the organization's operating structure.

## Input
- The target work area, the current state of existing AI use, and documents related to the organization's purposes and standards (where they exist)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Define the purpose to be assigned to AI in the target work area.
2. Design the (judgment) standards by which the suitability of AI outputs will be judged.
3. Design the procedure and the verification standards by which AI results will be verified.
4. Design a system for collecting and reflecting feedback on the results of AI operation.
5. Document the design outputs as the organization's operating structure, such as the common context, and reflect them there.

## Output
A definition document of AI purposes and judgment standards, a document of verification and feedback standards, and the organizational operating structure reflected as the common context.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when purposes, standards, verification, and feedback are directly designed and their outputs are reflected in the organization's operating structure, and FAIL when the worker stops at merely using AI.
