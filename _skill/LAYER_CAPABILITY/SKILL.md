# Capability layer — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether a member's reaction to the AX organizational transition originates in the capability layer, where the relative position of their competence is threatened, and provide the grounds for a learning and capability support response.

## Input
- Records of the AX-transition-related statements and behaviors of the member or job group concerned
- Observed emotional signs (inferiority, helplessness, and so on)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Grasp which question the reaction converges on (job, competence, relationship, or existence).
2. Confirm whether the question corresponds to "Am I falling behind?"
3. Confirm whether the emotional signs correspond to inferiority and helplessness.
4. Synthesize the two conditions and judge whether it is the capability layer.

## Output
The judgment result on whether the reaction belongs to the capability layer, and the priority of learning support.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS (capability layer) when the core question is the relative position of competence and the emotions are inferiority and helplessness, and FAIL (another layer) otherwise.
