# Existence layer — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether a member's reaction to the AX organizational transition originates in the existence layer, where the very meaning of human existence is threatened, and provide the grounds for a response reaffirming uniquely human value.

## Input
- Records of the AX-transition-related statements and behaviors of the member or job group concerned
- Observed emotional signs (identity confusion and so on)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Grasp which question the reaction converges on (job, competence, relationship, or existence).
2. Confirm whether the question corresponds to "Is the meaning of being human diminishing?"
3. Confirm whether the emotional signs correspond to identity confusion.
4. Synthesize the two conditions and judge whether it is the existence layer.

## Output
The judgment result on whether the reaction belongs to the existence layer, and the priority of the identity reaffirmation response.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS (existence layer) when the core question is the meaning of human existence and the emotion is identity confusion, and FAIL (another layer) otherwise.
