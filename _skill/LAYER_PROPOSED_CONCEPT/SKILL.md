# Proposed concept — Skill

## Purpose
On the basis of the problem diagnosed at the reasonable interpretation layer (the possibility that a gap arises), theorize concepts as the author's new design and produce the proposed concept layer (theorization).

## Input
- The problem diagnosis of the reasonable interpretation layer (the diagnostic report on the possibility that a gap arises)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Review the content of the problem diagnosis.
2. Design a new concept that responds to that problem.
3. Confirm that the designed concept is the author's new proposal (distinguished from existing verified facts).
4. Produce the theorized concept.

## Output
A list of newly proposed concepts based on the problem diagnosis and a theorization document (the proposed concept layer).

## Criteria
It is judged PASS if a new concept is explicitly theorized from the problem diagnosis, and FAIL if it stops at restating existing facts without a new concept.
