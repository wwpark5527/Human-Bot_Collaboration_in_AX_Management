# Reasonable interpretation — Skill

## Purpose
On the basis of the external evidence verified at the verifiable fact layer, reasonably infer the possibility that gaps in access, competence, decision authority, and performance distribution arise, and produce a problem diagnosis.

## Input
- The external evidence of the verifiable fact layer (material organized from literature of international organizations and research institutes)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Review the confirmed external evidence.
2. Infer, grounded in the facts, the possibility that gaps arise in the areas of access, competence, decision authority, and performance distribution.
3. Verify that the inference is derived from the facts without any leap.
4. Organize the result of the inference into a problem diagnosis.

## Output
A diagnostic report on the possibility of gaps arising, based on the confirmed facts (the reasonable interpretation layer, problem diagnosis).

## Criteria
It is judged PASS if the possibility of gaps arising is reasonably inferred from the confirmed facts, and FAIL if it leaps without grounds.
