# Relationship layer — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether a member's reaction to AX organizational transition originates from the relationship layer, where their position and network within the organization are threatened, and provide the grounds for a network-rebuilding response.

## Input
- Records of the target member's or job group's AX-transition-related statements and behaviors
- Observed emotional signs (alienation, defensiveness, and so on)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Grasp which question the target reaction converges on (job, competence, relationship, or existence).
2. Confirm whether the question corresponds to "Is my position in the organization weakening?"
3. Confirm whether the emotional signs correspond to alienation and defensiveness.
4. Combine the two conditions and judge whether it is the relationship layer.

## Output
The judgment result on whether the target reaction belongs to the relationship layer, and the priority for network rebuilding.

## Criteria
If the core question is the weakening of one's position within the organization and the emotions are alienation and defensiveness, it is judged PASS (relationship layer); otherwise FAIL (another layer).
