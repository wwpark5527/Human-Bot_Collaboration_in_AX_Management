# Survival layer — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether a member's reaction to AX organizational transition originates from the survival layer, where the continuation of their job is threatened, and provide the grounds for a response that relieves anxiety and fear.

## Input
- Records of the target member's or job group's AX-transition-related statements and behaviors
- Observed emotional signs (anxiety, fear, and so on)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Grasp which question the target reaction converges on (job, competence, relationship, or existence).
2. Confirm whether the question corresponds to "Is my job disappearing?"
3. Confirm whether the emotional signs correspond to anxiety and fear.
4. Combine the two conditions and judge whether it is the survival layer.

## Output
The judgment result on whether the target reaction belongs to the survival layer, and the priority of the response.

## Criteria
If the core question is job continuation and the emotions are anxiety and fear, it is judged PASS (survival layer); otherwise FAIL (another layer).
