# Verifiable fact — Skill

## Purpose
On the grounds of the literature of international organizations and research institutions concerning AI gaps, labor transition, algorithmic management, and human-rights risks, select only externally verifiable facts and organize them as the starting point (external evidence) of the inclusive AI-transition ESG argument.

## Input
- Literature of international organizations and research institutions related to AI gaps, labor transition, algorithmic management, and human-rights risks

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Collect the relevant literature of international organizations and research institutions.
2. Determine whether the content of the literature is externally verifiable fact.
3. Exclude unverified interpretations and claims, and leave only the facts.
4. Produce the organized facts as the "verifiable fact" layer (external evidence).

## Output
A list of external evidence based on the literature of international organizations and research institutions (the verifiable fact layer).

## Criteria
It is judged PASS if the content is confirmed in the literature of international organizations and research institutions, and FAIL if unverified interpretations or claims are mixed in.
