# Least-preferred Role — Skill

## Purpose
Clarify the boundary with the potential role so as to judge the least-preferred role accurately, and provide the grounds for the decision to abandon, recruit, or replace with respect to TR blanks that cannot be resolved by managing internal members.

## Input
- A list of team-role candidates not classified as natural roles or potential roles
- The results of applying the individual's maximum effort and the results of applying colleagues' support

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm the candidates that have not been classified as natural roles or potential roles.
2. Test whether the role is manifested even when the person applies maximum effort.
3. Confirm whether manifestation is impossible even when others' support is additionally applied.
4. If it remains impossible even when both conditions are applied, confirm it as a least-preferred role.
5. Register it as a TR blank that cannot be resolved by managing internal members.
6. Propose either abandoning the TR or recruiting or replacing personnel who hold that TR as a natural role.

## Output
A list of least-preferred roles per individual, a list of TR blanks that cannot be resolved by internal management, and supporting material for the decision to abandon a TR or to recruit or replace.

## Criteria
If manifestation is impossible even when both the person's maximum effort and others' support are applied, PASS (least-preferred role); if a possibility of manifestation is confirmed, FAIL (reclassified as a potential/manageable role).
