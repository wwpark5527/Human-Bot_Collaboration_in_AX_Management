# Layer 4 (the LLM already released to the world = 'the 1st LLM') — Skill

## Purpose
By the standard of the LLM architecture, determine whether the target LLM corresponds to layer 4 ("the 1st LLM") and what its level of utilization is.

## Input
- The status of the external LLM introduced by the target organization
- Whether layer 3 (the knowledge chain) has been formed
- The LLM architecture (the layer 1–4/5 structure)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the target LLM is a general-purpose LLM externally supplied by big tech.
2. Inspect whether it has the characteristics of a general-purpose layer that accumulates, knowledge-izes, and provides the information of human society.
3. If the conditions are confirmed, classify it as layer 4 ("the 1st LLM").
4. Confirm whether it is linked with layer 3 (the knowledge chain) and diagnose whether it remains at partial utilization or produces synergy.

## Output
The result of determining layer 4 ("the 1st LLM"), and a diagnostic report on the level of utilization based on linkage with layer 3.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the determination result accurately conforms to the standard of the source (externally supplied by big tech, already out in the world) and the level of utilization is recorded together with it.
