# Meaning/cognitive distance — Skill

## Purpose
Judge whether the meaning/cognitive distance — produced not from the quantity of answers but from the combination of conceptual structure, mastery, belief structure, premise relations, fidelity of the reasoning chain, evidence, transfer, and epistemic maturity — has actually decreased before and after AI intervention.

## Input
- The current conceptual structure of the person being evaluated (the results of knowledge-chain observation)
- The target knowledge structure

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Observe and record the current conceptual structure through the knowledge chain.
2. Compare it with the target knowledge structure.
3. Measure the eight factors (conceptual structure, mastery, belief structure, premise relations, fidelity of the reasoning chain, evidence, transfer, epistemic maturity).
4. Combine the eight factors, produce the meaning/cognitive distance value, and compare the values before and after AI intervention.

## Output
The knowledge-chain-based meaning/cognitive distance value (the combined result of the eight factors) and the amount of distance reduction before and after AI intervention.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS if all eight factors are measured and the distance value is produced, and FAIL if even one of them is not measured.
