# Team/Natural Role — Skill

## Purpose
Accurately diagnose each individual's natural roles (the team roles manifested easily and naturally) and provide direct input for whether the team's 9 TR cells are filled and for the judgment that TRB is established.

## Input
- Observation/diagnosis data of the behavior types an individual actually shows within the team (for example, Interplace results)
- The reference table of the 9 team-role types

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Observe the behavior types an individual manifests when working alongside others.
2. Confirm whether they are manifested easily and naturally without help or conscious effort.
3. When the condition is met, classify that behavior type as a natural role.
4. Confirm the list of natural roles per individual (commonly 2–3).
5. Gather the natural roles of all team members by the 9 TR types and calculate the status of filled cells.
6. Compute whether TRB is established or not by whether all 9 cells are filled.

## Output
A list of natural roles per individual, a status table of the 9 TR cells filled at the team level, the judgment of whether TRB is established or not, and the teamwork level score.

## Criteria
PASS (natural role) is decided by whether it is manifested easily and naturally without help or conscious effort. If it is not manifested naturally it is FAIL and is reclassified as a potential/manageable role or a least-preferred role.
