# Observer assessment (OA: observer assessment) — Skill

## Purpose
Accurately collect, in the checklist method, the objective responses based on the observation of people around the person being measured who know them well, and provide the observer response data for diagnosing an individual's TR types and levels.

## Input
- A roster of observers who know the person being measured well (four or more)
- The OA checklist (45 positive adjectives and 27 negative adjectives)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm four or more observers who know the person being measured well.
2. Distribute the OA checklist to each observer.
3. Proceed so that the observers mark the items relevant to the person being assessed (a checklist method).
4. Collect the responses of four or more observers and organize them into the Interplace input format.
5. Combine the OA (objective) and SPI (subjective) responses and pass them to the mixed relative-and-absolute evaluation processing.

## Output
Raw checklist data per observer (four or more), aggregated observer response data, and input data for combination with the SPI.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS (a valid OA response) if the response was filled out by four or more observers in the checklist method, and FAIL (cannot be recognized as an OA) if there are fewer than four observers or if it is the method where the person themselves responds to the items.
