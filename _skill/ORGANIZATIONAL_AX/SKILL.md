# Organizational AX — Skill

## Purpose
Diagnose the level of progress of organizational AX (the process of an organization undergoing AX), and design an enterprise-wide organizational redesign plan that goes beyond partial optimization.

## Input
- Materials on the organization's state of personal AX / organizational AX
- Organizational redesign decision checklist (level of AI's participation in decision-making, locus of accountability, roles between departments, data access authority, responsibility for errors)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Diagnose, in distinction from each other, the state of the spread of personal AX and the state of progress of organizational AX (redesign of the organizational operating system).
2. Decide the level of AI's participation in decision-making, the locus of accountability, roles between departments, data access authority, and responsibility for errors.
3. Check whether partial optimization (adoption in some departments) is occurring, and establish a plan for the enterprise-wide connection of work, data, structure, authority, evaluation, culture, and governance.
4. Execute the redesign plan for the decision-making structure, communication structure, power structure, and collaboration structure.

## Output
Personal AX / organizational AX diagnosis report, list of organizational redesign decisions, enterprise-wide connection roadmap, redesign plan for the organizational operating system.

## Criteria
It is determined PASS when the deliverables specify an enterprise-wide redesign in which work, data, structure, authority, evaluation, culture, and governance are connected, rather than adoption confined to some departments.
