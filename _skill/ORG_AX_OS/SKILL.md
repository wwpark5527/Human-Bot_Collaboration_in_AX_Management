# OS for organizational AX — Skill

## Purpose
Discern whether the target system meets the necessary conditions for being called an OS for organizational AX, and further whether it is at the level of an AI-native organizational operating system that also meets the additional conditions.

## Input
- The satisfaction status of the six necessary conditions of the target system
- The satisfaction status of the six additional conditions of the target system
- The status of the organization's verification, approval, and record system for AI results

## Procedure (Steps)
1. First rule out that the target system is at the level of a mere AI platform/chatbot integration tool.
2. Check whether the six necessary conditions are satisfied.
3. Check the level of satisfaction of the six additional conditions.
4. Judge the system as an "OS for organizational AX" when the necessary conditions are satisfied, and at the level of an "AI-native organizational operating system (AI 4.0)" when the additional conditions are also satisfied.

## Output
The result of discerning the OS for organizational AX (not satisfied | necessary conditions satisfied | additional conditions also satisfied), and a maturity diagnosis report.

## Criteria
PASS is judged when the discernment result exactly conforms to the source criteria (the distinction between necessary and additional conditions, and the differentiation from a mere AI platform).
