# Common & Governance context — Skill

## Purpose
Discern whether the OS for organizational AX has actually formed and implemented the additional conditions of the common context and the governance context, and thereby reached the level of an AI-native organizational operating system (true AI 4.0) beyond a mere AI platform.

## Input
- The status of the structuring of the organization's data as a common context
- The status of the structuring of the organization's rules and policies as a governance context

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the organization's data has been formed and implemented as a common context.
2. Confirm whether the organization's rules have been formed and implemented as a governance context.
3. Check the actual implementation difficulty and completeness of the two contexts.
4. When satisfied, judge that an AI-native organizational operating system (the AI 4.0 level) has been reached.

## Output
The judgment on the formation and implementation of the common and governance contexts (not satisfied | formation and implementation complete = the AI 4.0 level reached).

## Criteria
PASS is judged when both the common context and the governance context have actually been formed and implemented and the level of a mere AI platform has been surpassed.
