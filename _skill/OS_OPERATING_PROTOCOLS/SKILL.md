# Operating protocols & control standards — Skill

## Purpose
Discern whether the OS for organizational AX goes beyond merely connecting AI and has the operating protocols that make AI act within the organization's norms.

## Input
- The design status of the target OS's authority & approval / role allocation / access control
- The design status of exception handling / logging / accountability
- The design status of escalation / human override / audit trail

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm the systems of authority & approval · role allocation · access control.
2. Confirm the systems of exception handling · logging & traceability · accountability.
3. Confirm the systems of the escalation system · human override · the audit trail.
4. Combine the satisfaction of the nine elements to determine whether the operating protocols are embedded.

## Output
A table of the satisfaction status of the operating protocols (satisfied/not satisfied for each of the nine elements), and the final judgment (embedded | deficient).

## Criteria
PASS is judged when all nine elements are confirmed and the structure in which AI acts within the organization's norms is demonstrated.
