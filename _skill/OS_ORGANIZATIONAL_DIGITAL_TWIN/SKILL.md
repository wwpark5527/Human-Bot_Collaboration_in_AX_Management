# Organizational digital twin(ODT) — Skill

## Purpose
Discern whether the target system has reached the ODT level, at which the organization's actual way of working is dynamically reproduced in digital space so that simulation, prediction, and optimization are possible.

## Input
- The status of the digital reflection of the organization's structure, roles, workflows, decision-making, collaboration, performance, and culture data
- The implementation status of the simulation, prediction, and optimization functions

## Procedure (Steps)
1. First rule out that the target system remains at the level of a mere organization chart or ERP system.
2. Check whether the data of the organization's seven dimensions (structure, roles, workflows, decision-making, collaboration, performance, culture) are reflected digitally.
3. Check whether the real-time and near-real-time simulation, prediction, and optimization functions are working.
4. Confirm whether organizational experiments can be conducted in digital space rather than in reality, and make the final judgment on whether it is an ODT.

## Output
The result of discerning the ODT (not satisfied | the ODT level reached), and a report on the possible scope of organizational simulation, prediction, and optimization.

## Criteria
PASS is judged when the organization's actual way of working is dynamically reproduced in digital space so that simulation, prediction, and optimization are possible.
