# Personal AX — Skill

## Purpose
Diagnose the level of AI use at the individual level (personal AX) and design a tool-centered plan for improving efficiency and effectiveness.

## Input
- The individual's current status of AI tool use
- The list of study and work tasks

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Identify the available AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, AI agents, bots).
2. Select the areas of study and work whose efficiency and effectiveness are to be raised with AI.
3. Apply, starting from low-failure-cost attempts that the individual can decide on their own.
4. Advance the level of personal AX through the use of AI agents/bots.

## Output
A list of personal AI usage scenarios, and a self-diagnosis checklist for the level of personal AX.

## Criteria
PASS is judged when the efficiency and effectiveness of the individual's studies and work have actually improved through the use of AI tools.
