# Context design — Skill

## Purpose
Perform and judge the context design that translates human purposes and organizational criteria into AI execution conditions.

## Input
- Materials on the organization's purposes and criteria
- The deliverables of the knowledge chain

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Organize the organization's purposes and criteria.
2. Translate the purposes and criteria into AI execution conditions.
3. Mediate the deliverables of the knowledge chain to the world model according to these conditions.
4. Review whether the results of AI execution operate within the purposes and criteria.

## Output
AI execution conditions (purpose and criteria), and an operating structure that connects technical discussion to human work and organizational operation.

## Criteria
PASS is judged when the results of AI execution operate within the organization's purposes and criteria, and FAIL when they depart from them.
