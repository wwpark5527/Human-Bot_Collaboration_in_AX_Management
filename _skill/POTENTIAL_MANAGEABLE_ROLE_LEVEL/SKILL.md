# Potential/Manageable Role — Skill

## Purpose
Discern the potential roles by the two conditions of self-management effort and colleagues' understanding and acceptance, and thereby support the calculation of the team's potential teamwork level and the designation of targets for promotion to natural roles.

## Input
- The team-role diagnostic results for each individual (the items not classified as natural roles)
- Information on the possibility of the person's own self-management effort and on the conditions for colleagues' understanding and acceptance

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm the candidate team roles not classified as natural roles.
2. Assess whether there is a possibility of exercise through the person's conscious effort and self-management.
3. Confirm whether the conditions are such that colleagues can understand and accept that behavior.
4. When both conditions hold, confirm it as a potential/manageable role.
5. Reflect the confirmed potential roles in the calculation of the team's maximum potential teamwork level.
6. Combine colleagues' acceptance and encouragement with the person's own effort to establish a plan for promotion to natural roles.

## Output
A list of potential/manageable roles for each individual, materials for calculating the maximum potential teamwork level at the team level, and the candidates and plan for promotion to natural roles.

## Criteria
PASS (a potential/manageable role) when the role can be exercised once the two conditions of self-management effort and colleagues' understanding and acceptance are both met; FAIL (reclassified as a least-preferred role) when it cannot be exercised even though both conditions are met.
