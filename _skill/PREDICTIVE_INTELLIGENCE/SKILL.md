# AI predictive intelligence — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether the use of AI remains generation-centered or has shifted to being centered on predictive intelligence (the prediction of future states and the results of actions), and support that shift.

## Input
- Materials on the organization's status of AI use

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether the current use of AI remains at question answering and the generation of documents, images, and code.
2. Check whether AI is used for the prediction of future states and the results of actions.
3. Confirm whether the technologies that enable predictive intelligence (the world model, the knowledge chain, context design) have been introduced.
4. Determine whether the shift from generation-centered to prediction-centered has taken place.

## Output
The definition of AX's essential paradigm shift moving from generative capability to predictive capability.

## Criteria
PASS is judged when AI is used for the prediction of future states and the results of actions, and FAIL when it remains only at question answering and generation.
