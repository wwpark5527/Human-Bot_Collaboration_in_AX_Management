# Right to AI access — Skill

## Purpose
Guarantee to all members of the organization the opportunity to access the AI tools and infrastructure needed for their work, and thereby reflect in practice the first of the eight human protection rights of the AI era (the right to AI access).

## Input
- The list of AI tools and infrastructure needed for each job role, and data on the employees' status of AI access

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Define, for each job role, the AI tools and infrastructure needed for the work.
2. Survey the employees' current status of access.
3. Calculate the access rate and the access gaps across departments.
4. Implement measures that expand access opportunities in the departments and job roles with large gaps.
5. Re-measure the access rate and the gaps regularly.

## Output
A measurement report on the AI tool and infrastructure access rate and the access gaps across departments, and a plan of measures to expand access opportunities.

## Criteria
PASS is judged when the opportunity to access AI tools and infrastructure is guaranteed to all employees and the access gaps across departments are continuously managed; FAIL is judged when the gaps are left unattended.
