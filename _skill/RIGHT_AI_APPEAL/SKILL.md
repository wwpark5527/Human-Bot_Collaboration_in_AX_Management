# Right to AI appeal — Skill

## Purpose
Guarantee a procedure by which reconsideration of AI evaluations, recommendations, and decisions can be demanded, and thereby reflect in practice the sixth of the eight human protection rights of the AI era (the right to AI appeal).

## Input
- The list of tasks in which AI evaluation, recommendation, or decision intervenes, and the status of existing appeal and remedy procedures

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Identify the AI evaluation, recommendation, and decision tasks that may become subject to appeal.
2. Design the intake channel and reception procedure for reconsideration requests.
3. Establish the procedure and criteria for reviewing the requests received.
4. Implement correction and remedy measures according to the results of the review.
5. Record the requests and their handling results and check the effectiveness of the procedure.

## Output
A document of the reconsideration request procedure, records of reconsideration requests and their handling, and the results of correction and remedy measures.

## Criteria
PASS is judged when a procedure to demand reconsideration is open and requests actually lead to correction and remedy; FAIL is judged when the procedure is absent or merely formal.
