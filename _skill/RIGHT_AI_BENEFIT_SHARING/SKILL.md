# Right to AI benefit-sharing — Skill

## Purpose
Design the distribution structure for AI productivity gains and verify the reinvestment rate, and thereby reflect in the organization's operation the eighth of the eight human protection rights of the AI era (the right to AI benefit-sharing).

## Input
- Productivity indicators before and after the introduction of AI, the status of labor costs and the compensation system, the allocation records of the education and welfare budget, and the workforce composition by department and job group

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Compare the productivity indicators before and after the introduction of AI and calculate the productivity gains attributable to AI.
2. Analyze the current accrual structure of the gains and confirm whether they are concentrated among owners.
3. Establish the distribution criteria for each item of reinvestment in compensation, education, welfare, and organizational capability.
4. Reflect the distribution criteria in the compensation system and the budget allocation, and execute the reinvestment.
5. Measure the reinvestment rate and check its distribution across departments and job groups, and correct any skew.

## Output
The results of calculating the productivity gains, an analysis of the accrual structure, the distribution criteria and reinvestment principles, the execution records of reinvestment, and a measurement report on the reinvestment rate.

## Criteria
PASS is judged when the gains are calculated, reinvested into compensation, education, and welfare according to the distribution criteria, and the reinvestment rate is measured; FAIL is judged when the gains are not calculated or are concentrated in the hands of only some owners.
