# Right to AI explanation — Skill

## Purpose
Provide explanations so that the party can understand the AI results and evaluation criteria, and thereby reflect in practice the fifth of the eight human protection rights of the AI era (the right to AI explanation).

## Input
- The list of tasks in which AI results intervene, and information on the AI evaluation criteria for each task

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Identify the tasks in which the AI result affects the party.
2. Document the AI evaluation criteria for each task in an understandable form.
3. Design the procedure for providing an explanation of the AI decision.
4. Operate the explanation-provision procedure and record its history.
5. Confirm the party's degree of understanding and improve the manner of explanation.

## Output
An explanatory document of the AI results and evaluation criteria, the explanation-provision procedure, and records of the history of explanation provision.

## Criteria
PASS is judged when the AI result and its evaluation criteria are explained in a way the party can understand, and FAIL when no explanation is provided.
