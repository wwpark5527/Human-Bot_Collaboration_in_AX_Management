# Right to AI judgment — Skill

## Purpose
Guarantee that in important judgments humans provide the purpose, meaning, and criteria and remain in the final decision, and thereby reflect in practice the fourth of the eight human protection rights of the AI era (the right to AI judgment).

## Input
- The list of tasks in which AI outputs intervene, and the importance and required level of judgment for each task

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Identify, among the tasks in which AI outputs intervene, the important judgment tasks that require human approval.
2. Define, for each task, the purpose, meaning, and criteria that humans must provide.
3. Design the human approval procedure and establish the system for retaining the final decision-making authority.
4. Operate the approval procedure and record the judgment criteria.
5. Check the status of approval implementation regularly.

## Output
A list of tasks that require human approval, a document of the judgment criteria for each task, and records of the operation of the approval procedure.

## Criteria
PASS is judged when, in important judgments, humans provide the purpose, meaning, and criteria and retain the final decision-making authority; FAIL is judged when a final decision is made without human approval.
