# Right to AI learning — Skill

## Purpose
By guaranteeing AI literacy education and reskilling/upskilling opportunities to organizational members, the second right among the eight human protection rights of the AI era (the right to AI learning) is reflected in execution.

## Input
- Required AI literacy level per job, current capability data of employees, job change forecast information

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Diagnose the required AI literacy level per job and the current capability gap.
2. Design and provide a basic AI literacy education curriculum.
3. Provide reskilling programs to personnel whose jobs are expected to change.
4. Provide upskilling programs to personnel already using AI.
5. Measure course completion rates and capability improvement and improve the programs.

## Output
AI literacy education courses, results of operating reskilling/upskilling programs, and a measurement report on the capability gap and completion rates.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when AI literacy education and reskilling/upskilling opportunities are continuously provided and the capability gap narrows, and FAIL when education opportunities are left unaddressed.
