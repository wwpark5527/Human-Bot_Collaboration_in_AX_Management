# Right to AI transition — Skill

## Purpose
By providing role redesign and transition training when a job changes due to the introduction of AI, the seventh right among the eight human protection rights of the AI era (the right to AI transition) is reflected in execution.

## Input
- AI introduction plan, impact analysis per job, list of target personnel

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Review the AI introduction plan and identify the personnel and areas where job change is expected.
2. Establish a role redesign plan suited to the changing job.
3. Design and provide a transition training program.
4. Carry out transition placement into the redesigned role.
5. Check the state of adaptation after transition placement and provide additional support.

## Output
A list of personnel expected to undergo job change, a role redesign plan, and the transition training program and transition placement results.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when role redesign and transition training are provided and transition placement is carried out, and FAIL when the job change is left unaddressed without transition support.
