# Right to AI utilization — Skill

## Purpose
By making AI use serve the expansion of human capability rather than the replacement of repetitive work, the third right among the eight human protection rights of the AI era (the right to AI utilization) is reflected in execution.

## Input
- Status of AI use by department and job, work improvement effect data

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Classify the current modes of AI use as repetitive-work-replacing or capability-expanding.
2. Select capability-expanding use cases and measure their work improvement effects.
3. Redesign areas skewed toward the replacement of repetitive work into capability-expanding forms.
4. Apply the redesign plan and re-measure the results.
5. Share and spread capability-expanding use cases across the whole organization.

## Output
Classification results for the modes of AI use, a report on capability expansion cases and work improvement effects, and a redesign plan for the modes of use.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when AI is used for the purpose of expanding human capability rather than replacing repetitive work and work improvement is confirmed, and FAIL when it stays confined to the replacement of repetitive work.
