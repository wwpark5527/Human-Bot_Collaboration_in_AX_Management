# Accountability judge — Skill

## Purpose
On the premise that ultimate responsibility for AI results lies with the organization and its people, this supports AX talent in judging clearly how far an AI result may be used.

## Input
- The AI output whose scope of use must be judged
- The work context in which that output will be used (internal/customer/decision-making)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Judge whether the result will be used for internal reference only.
2. Judge whether it may be delivered to a customer.
3. Judge whether it may be reflected in decision-making.
4. Confirm whether a superior's approval is required.
5. Confirm whether legal/security review is required.
6. Decide whether a record must be kept.
7. Confirm whether the locus of accountability is clear if a problem arises.

## Output
A judgment on the scope of use and the locus of accountability for the AI result.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS (determination of scope of use and locus of accountability complete) when all seven items — is it for internal reference only, may it be delivered to a customer, may it be reflected in decision-making, is a superior's approval required, is legal/security review required, must a record be kept, and is the locus of accountability clear — have been determined, and FAIL (accountability judgment needs supplementation) if even one is unclear.
