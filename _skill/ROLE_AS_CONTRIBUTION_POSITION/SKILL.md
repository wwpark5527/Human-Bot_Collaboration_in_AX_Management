# Role — Skill

## Purpose
By fixing contribution into a repeatable and accountable structure, it makes a role not a person's name tag but a functional position that arises in the process by which meaning turns into output artifacts and responsibility.

## Input
- Records of repeatedly confirmed contribution (valid change)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Collect the patterns of contribution that are repeatedly confirmed.
2. Define those patterns of contribution as functional positions rather than titles.
3. Judge which of people, AI, and systems can take on that functional position.
4. Even if the role is distributed across several actors, state the locus of ultimate accountability as the human or the organization.

## Output
A functional position that makes contribution repeatable and accountable (ultimate responsibility remains with the human or the organization).

## Criteria
If the position of contribution is defined as a functional position rather than a title and the locus of ultimate accountability is clear it is judged PASS (role structure secured), and otherwise it is judged FAIL (role not structured).
