# RBHRM (Role-Based HRM, competence + role-based) — Skill

## Purpose
By shifting HR management that remains at CBHRM (seniority + competence-based) to RBHRM (competence + role-based), the necessary condition for performance realization is satisfied in AX organizations where the change from job-centered to role-centered is urgently needed.

## Input
- Current state of the HR management system (position/job descriptions, evaluation and reward criteria, and the like)
- Diagnostic material on the organization's control/autonomy culture

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Diagnose where current HR management stands among position, control, and CBHRM.
2. Design role definitions and management elements based on autonomy and commitment.
3. Establish an RBHRM transition plan and introduce it in stages.
4. Verify the effect of the transition in connection with the TRB measurement system.

## Output
RBHRM transition diagnosis and roadmap, a draft role-centered HR management system, and a TRB linkage plan.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when the focus of HR management has shifted to role and autonomy/commitment, and FAIL when it still remains at position, control, and seniority/competence.
