# Evidence keeper — Skill

## Purpose
By systematically keeping evidence of the AI-usage process, this supports AX in becoming not mere automation but a reviewable and accountable way of operating the organization.

## Input
- The materials used in the AI usage and the instructions given to the AI
- The AI-generated results and the human edits

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Record the materials used.
2. Record the AI instructions.
3. Record the AI-generated results.
4. Record the human edits.
5. Record the verification criteria.
6. Record the approval status.
7. Record errors and exceptions.
8. Record the improvements reflected.

## Output
Evidence that makes AX not mere automation but a reviewable and accountable way of operating the organization.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS (evidence management complete) when all eight items — the materials used, the AI instructions, the AI-generated results, the human edits, the verification criteria, the approval status, errors and exceptions, and the improvements reflected — have been kept, and FAIL (evidence needs supplementation) if even one is missing.
