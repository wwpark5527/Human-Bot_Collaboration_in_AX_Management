# Improvement reflector — Skill

## Purpose
On the premise that an AX organization is not completed by a single use of AI, this supports connecting failures in the AI-usage process to improvement of the organization's criteria.

## Input
- The results of the AI usage and the records of their review
- The errors, omissions, failures, and corrections discovered

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Review the results of the AI usage.
2. Discover errors and omissions.
3. Analyze the causes.
4. Correct the prompt.
5. Reinforce the context.
6. Update the checklist.
7. Improve the deliverable schema.
8. Reflect it into the next task.

## Output
Organizational criteria reflected into the next task, such as updated prompts, context, checklists, and deliverable schemas.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS (improvement reflection complete) when a failure is not left to end as an individual mistake but is connected to improving the organization's criteria, and FAIL (improvement reflection insufficient) when it is not reflected into the organization's criteria.
