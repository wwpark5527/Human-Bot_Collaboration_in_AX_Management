# Social Cooperation Norm Learning — Skill

## Purpose
So that AI can function as an organizational member, it learns the cooperation norms of the human organization and shifts beyond an intelligent system to a socially compatible system.

## Input
- Information on the organization's power, trust, tacit knowledge, politics, culture, face, and emotion factors
- The AI's existing patterns of speech, explanation, and persuasion

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Investigate the organization's power structure, trust relationships, tacit knowledge, political dynamics, culture, face, and emotional factors.
2. On the basis of the investigation results, establish norms that distinguish the situations in which the AI should speak from those in which it should stay silent.
3. Establish the mode of explanation (what and how to explain) and the criteria for selecting whom to persuade.
4. Identify expressions that organizational members may find threatening and reflect their avoidance in the AI's response norms.
5. Reflect the established norms in the AI's learning and operational data and verify them in actual interaction.

## Output
Organizational cooperation norm definition document, situation-specific speech/silence criteria, explanation and persuasion guide, list of threatening expressions to avoid.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS (social cooperation norm learning complete) when the AI actually observes the norms of situation-specific speech/silence, explanation/persuasion, and expression avoidance, and FAIL (social cooperation norms not learned) otherwise.
