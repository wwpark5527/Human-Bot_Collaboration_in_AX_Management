# Augmentation Realization — Skill

## Purpose
Determine whether, within the system of core management spirits (basic premise + 3 spirits), augmentation is being realized as a principle of amplification rather than replacement, and confirm whether it operates as a trinity together with respect for and use of diversity and the pursuit of complementary fit.

## Input
- Whether management decisions (introduction of bots/AI, organizational design) are replacement-oriented or amplification-oriented
- The current state of forming augmented humans (AH) and augmented bots (AB)
- The state of linkage with the other two core spirits (respect for and use of diversity, pursuit of complementary fit)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm whether management decisions have a zero-sum (replacement) structure or a positive-sum (amplification) structure.
2. Confirm whether augmented humans (AH) and augmented bots (AB) have been formed.
3. Confirm whether the perception that "AI strengthens me" has formed among members.
4. Confirm whether it operates as a trinity together with respect for and use of diversity and the pursuit of complementary fit.

## Output
Determination result of whether augmentation has been realized, report on the current state of forming augmented humans (AH) and augmented bots (AB).

## Criteria
When a positive-sum structure is confirmed, AH/AB are formed, and they operate together with the other two spirits, a PASS determination of the realization of augmentation is made.
