# Pursuing Complementary Fit — Skill

## Purpose
Identify whether the role design of humans and bots is based on role differentiation that fills in each other's shortcomings rather than on competition and comparison of superiority, and determine the four types of complementarity: cognitive, emotional, behavioral, and ethical.

## Input
- The mode of human-bot role division (mere division vs. combination of strengths)
- The current state of the human-bot strength mapping for each of the four types (cognitive/emotional/behavioral/ethical)
- Whether there is "competition over superiority" or "role differentiation"

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Check whether the role design is a mere division of the same work or a role differentiation that connects strengths.
2. Check whether the criterion is not "who is more superior?" but "how will we complement each other?"
3. Check whether the four types of complementarity — cognitive, emotional, behavioral, and ethical — are reflected in the design.
4. If the conditions are met, judge it as pursuing complementary fit.

## Output
The judgment result on whether complementary fit has been realized, and the role-differentiation design for each of the four major complementarity types.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when strengths are connected on the basis of role differentiation and the organization's overall performance is maximized.
