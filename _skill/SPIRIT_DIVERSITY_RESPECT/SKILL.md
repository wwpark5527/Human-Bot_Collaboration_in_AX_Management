# Respecting and Leveraging Diversity — Skill

## Purpose
Identify whether the diversity within humans and the essential differences between humans and bots are respected without stratification and are being used as assets of augmentation and mutual complementarity rather than of competition.

## Input
- A list of the objects of diversity within the organization (differences within humans + human-bot differences)
- Whether differences are stratified (whether judgments of superiority and inferiority exist)
- Whether diversity is treated as a factor of conflict or as an augmentation asset

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Check whether the object of diversity is not confined to the interior of humanity but also includes the essential differences between humans and bots.
2. Check whether differences are being stratified into good and bad.
3. Check whether the existence and utility value of each member are being made the fullest use of (role diversity).
4. Check whether diversity is treated not as a factor of conflict but as an augmentation asset.

## Output
The judgment result on whether respecting and leveraging diversity has been realized, and measures for leveraging role diversity.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS when differences are not stratified but used as an augmentation asset so that collective intelligence and performance improve.
