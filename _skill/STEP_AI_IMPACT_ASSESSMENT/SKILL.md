# 1. AI Impact Assessment — Skill

## Purpose
Assess the impact in the five areas of labor, judgment, authority, customers, and human rights before AI is introduced, thereby performing the first step of the AI Inclusive-Transformation ESG execution model (9 steps) and generating the design input for the subsequent steps.

## Input
- The AI introduction plan (target tasks, organizations, and scope), current job descriptions, the current authority and approval system, customer touchpoint processes, and internal standards related to human rights and labor affairs

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Specify the tasks, organizations, and scope targeted for AI introduction and settle the boundary of the impact assessment.
2. Assess the labor impact — job changes, replacement risk, workload redistribution.
3. Assess the judgment impact — the distinction between the judgments transferred to AI and the judgments remaining with humans.
4. Assess the authority impact — the movement of access, approval, and decision authority.
5. Assess the customer impact — the customer touchpoints and the consequences for customers.
6. Assess the human-rights impact — the risks of surveillance, discrimination, and exclusion.
7. Synthesize and document the results and hand them over to the subsequent steps (steps 2-5).

## Output
The AI impact assessment result — the scope definition document, the impact analysis for each of the 5 areas, the risk classification and directions of response, and the items handed over to the subsequent steps.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS if all five areas are assessed and documented before the introduction of AI and handed over to the subsequent steps, and FAIL if it stops at an after-the-fact assessment or an area is omitted. (ESG linkage: S / G)
