# 6. Appeal Procedure — Skill

## Purpose
Establish, as an organizational procedure, a structure for explanation, re-review, and correction requests regarding AI results, thereby securing reversibility even for AI results that have passed approval. It performs the sixth step of the AI Inclusive-Transformation ESG execution model (9 steps). The ESG linkage is S (Social) and G (Governance).

## Input
- The output of step 5, human approval criteria (the list of tasks requiring human approval and the approval criteria), the list of types of impact AI results have on the parties concerned, the current grievance-handling and objection system, and internal standards related to personal data and record retention

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Settle the scope of the AI results (evaluations, recommendations, decisions, output artifacts) that are subject to appeal.
2. Designate the channel for explanation requests together with the responding party and the deadline.
3. Define the intake path, requirements, performing party, and processing deadline for re-review requests.
4. Determine the method of correction and the method of remedy for when an objection is upheld.
5. Determine the points at which history from intake to closure is recorded, and connect them to the audit record step.
6. Put in place provisions protecting the party who raises an appeal, and give notice of the procedure to the parties concerned.

## Output
The appeal procedure and the results of processing re-reviews, corrections, and remedies — the scope subject to appeal, the channels for explanation and re-review requests with their parties and deadlines, the method of processing corrections and remedies, the points at which history is recorded, and the provisions protecting the party who raises an appeal.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS if all three request channels — explanation, re-review, and correction — are established and the processing parties, deadlines, and history recording points are designated, and FAIL if even one channel is missing or a request can lapse without a response. (ESG linkage: S / G)
