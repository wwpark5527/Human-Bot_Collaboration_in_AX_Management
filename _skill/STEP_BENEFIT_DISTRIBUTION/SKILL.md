# 8. Benefit Distribution — Skill

## Purpose
Reinvest the AI productivity gains into education, compensation, welfare, and organizational capability, thereby fixing as an organizational procedure the path by which the fruits of AI transformation return to the members. It performs the eighth step of the AI Inclusive-Transformation ESG execution model (9 steps). The ESG linkage is S (Social).

## Input
- The output of step 7, audit record (the preserved AI work history), work-performance data from before and after the introduction of AI, the current compensation, education, and welfare systems and budget structure, and the composition of the workforce by job group and stratum

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Identify the productivity gains originating from the introduction of AI and settle the method of calculation.
2. Make the four reinvestment targets (education, compensation, welfare, organizational capability) concrete.
3. Determine the mode and ratio of distribution for each target and record the grounds.
4. Designate the funds, the executing party, and the execution cycle.
5. Check the distribution results for skew by job group and stratum and adjust the ratios if necessary.
6. Calculate the reinvestment record and hand the results and the remaining problems over to the improvement loop step.

## Output
The reinvestment record — the method of calculating the productivity gains, the mode and ratio of distribution for the four targets, the funds, executing party, and cycle, the skew-checking procedure, and the execution results.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS if the gains are calculated, the mode and ratio of distribution are determined for all four targets, and the funds and executing party are designated so that a record is produced; it is judged FAIL if there is no basis for calculation, or only some targets are dealt with, or only a policy is declared without an executing party. (ESG linkage: S)
