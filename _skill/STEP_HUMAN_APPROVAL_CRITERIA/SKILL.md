# 5. Human Approval Criteria — Skill

## Purpose
Define explicitly the outputs and decisions that require human intervention and establish the approval criteria for each item, thereby performing the fifth step of the AI Inclusive-Transformation ESG execution model (9 steps). The ESG linkage is G (Governance).

## Input
- The output of step 4, labor transition (the reconfigured roles and division of work), the list of AI-involved tasks and outputs, the current approval and sign-off system, and internal standards related to external legal effect, human rights, and safety

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Enumerate by type the outputs and decisions in which AI is involved.
2. Determine for each item whether human intervention is needed (size of impact, reversibility, external legal effect, relevance to human rights and safety).
3. Settle the list of tasks requiring human approval and record the grounds for the determination.
4. Write the approval criteria for each item (what is checked and on what basis it is approved or rejected).
5. Designate the approving party and the moment of approval.
6. Determine the points at which approval history is recorded and connect them to the audit record step.

## Output
The list of tasks requiring human approval and the approval criteria — the list of types of outputs and decisions, the determination results on whether intervention is needed together with their grounds, the approval criteria for each item, and the table designating the approving party and the moment of approval.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS if the items requiring human intervention are explicitly enumerated and the approval criteria, party, and moment are documented; it is judged FAIL if it is left to discretionary judgment or there are no approval criteria. (ESG linkage: G)
