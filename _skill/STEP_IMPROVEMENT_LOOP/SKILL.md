# 9. Improvement Loop — Skill

## Purpose
Reflect errors and feedback into the next common context and governance criteria, thereby making the AI Inclusive-Transformation ESG execution model (9 steps) not a procedure run once but a circulating loop. It performs the last of the 9 steps. The ESG linkage is G (Governance).

## Input
- Step 7, audit record (the correction history), step 6, appeal procedure (the re-review and remedy processing results), step 5, human approval criteria (the details of approval omissions), step 8, benefit distribution (the skew-checking results), the current common context produced as the output of step 2, and the current governance criteria spanning steps 3 to 7

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Settle the collection paths for errors and feedback and gather the input.
2. Categorize the items (one-off mistake / defect in the criteria / problem in the design of authority and procedures).
3. Determine the portion to be reflected into the common context (purpose, criteria, sources, roles, formats, verification standards).
4. Determine the portion to be reflected into the governance criteria (authority, approval, appeal, records).
5. Determine the revising party, the revision cycle, and the way the history is recorded, and settle the revision of the criteria.
6. Hand the updated criteria over as the starting condition of the next cycle, and leave the items not reflected together with the reasons.

## Output
The updated common context and governance criteria — the collection paths and the categorization results, the portion reflected into the common context, the portion reflected into the governance criteria, the revising party, cycle, and history, and the items not reflected together with their reasons.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS if it is determined for both the common context and the governance criteria whether reflection is to take place and the reflected portion is settled as a revision of the criteria that becomes the starting condition of the next cycle; it is judged FAIL if items are merely collected and the points of reflection are not determined, or if there is no revising party so that the criteria are not updated. (ESG linkage: G)
