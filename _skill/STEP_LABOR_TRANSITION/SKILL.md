# 4. Labor Transition — Skill

## Purpose
Conduct a job impact assessment before the introduction of AI and design reskilling and role reconfiguration, thereby performing the fourth step of the AI Inclusive-Transformation ESG execution model (9 steps). The ESG linkage is S (Social).

## Input
- The AI introduction plan and its timing, the results of step 1, AI impact assessment, the current job descriptions and task lists, the workforce status, and the education and training operating system

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Conduct a job impact assessment at a point before the introduction of AI (identify transfer at the task level).
2. Specify the affected jobs and personnel and record the size of the impact.
3. Calculate the capability gap for each job and design a reskilling plan.
4. Bundle the remaining tasks and the new tasks, reconfigure the roles, and revise the job descriptions.
5. Design a transition placement plan into the reconfigured roles.
6. Calculate the transition-placement rate and design supplementary measures for the personnel not yet transitioned.

## Output
The job impact assessment result, the reskilling plan, the reconfigured roles (the revised job descriptions), and the transition placement plan together with the transition-placement rate measurement result.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS if a job impact assessment is conducted before the introduction of AI and reskilling and role reconfiguration are designed; it is judged FAIL if the impact assessment is postponed until after introduction or does not lead to design. (ESG linkage: S)
