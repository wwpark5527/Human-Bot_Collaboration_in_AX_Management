# Supplementary Fit — Skill

## Purpose
While confirming the positive function of homogeneity-based teamwork activities, determine whether it has become entrenched as the sole ground of performance and thereby impairs respect for diversity and the pursuit of complementary fit.

## Input
- The content of the teamwork and unity activities (e.g., unity events, staff dinners)
- The way those activities contribute to organizational performance
- Whether there are follow-up measures related to respecting diversity and pursuing complementary fit

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Check whether the activities are based on homogeneity and similarity.
2. Examine whether they contribute to departmental and organizational performance.
3. Diagnose whether those activities lead to an entrenched state that blocks respect for diversity.
4. If necessary, recommend follow-up measures for expanding awareness into respecting diversity and pursuing complementary fit.

## Output
The judgment result on the degree of reliance on supplementary fit and the recommendation for expanding respect for diversity.

## Criteria
It is judged PASS if the positive function of homogeneity-based activities is maintained and there is no exclusion of diversity, and FAIL if diversity is excluded through the entrenchment of supplementary fit.
