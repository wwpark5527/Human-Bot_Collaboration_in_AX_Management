# Systems Thinking — Skill

## Purpose
It supports recognizing the AX organization as a complex system and exercising the integrative judgment that does not stay at partial optimization, so as to make a determination of direction rather than a generation of correct answers.

## Input
- Information related to the AX organization's constituent elements (humans, AI agents, governance, workflow, culture, ESG, security, context)
- The partially optimized alternative proposed by AI, or a matter requiring judgment

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Identify the AX organization's constituent elements and grasp the connections among them.
2. Check whether the object under review stays at the level of partial optimization.
3. Integrate value conflicts, ethics, long-term direction, organizational culture, and social ripple effects from the perspective of the whole.
4. Determine the direction on the basis of the integrative judgment.

## Output
A determination of direction grounded in integrative judgment that goes beyond partial optimization.

## Criteria
If value conflicts, ethics, long-term direction, organizational culture, and social ripple effects are integratively judged as a whole and the direction is determined, it is judged PASS (systems thinking secured); if one stays at partial optimization, it is judged FAIL (systems thinking needs re-examination).
