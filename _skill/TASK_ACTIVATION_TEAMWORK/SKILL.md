# Task-Activation Aspect of Teamwork — Skill

## Purpose
It diagnoses the task-activation aspect of teamwork precisely through team role balance (TRB) measurement, thereby grasping the effect on a department's actual performance as evaluated by the company and providing one axis of the comprehensive teamwork diagnosis to be combined with the human bonding aspect.

## Input
- Roster of the department/team to be measured
- Each individual member's Interplace TR analysis result
- The department's actual performance data (for combined analysis)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Fix the department/team to be measured.
2. Obtain each individual member's TR analysis result through Interplace.
3. Gather the TR analysis results of all team members and compute TRB.
4. Organize the TRB result as the indicator of the task-activation aspect of teamwork.
5. Combine it with the result of the human bonding aspect (group cohesiveness) to produce a comprehensive diagnosis of the department's teamwork.

## Output
TRB-based task-activation measurement values, analysis material on the effect on actual performance, and comparison material for combination with group cohesiveness.

## Criteria
If the measurement targets the degree to which the 9 team roles are exercised within the team and corresponds to the complementary fit side, it is judged PASS (valid diagnosis); if it targets mental attraction among members or supplementary fit, it is judged FAIL (confusion with the human bonding aspect).
