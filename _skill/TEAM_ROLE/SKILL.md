# Team Role (TR) — Skill

## Purpose
It diagnoses accurately, on the criterion of behavior type, the team role that is distinguished from the functional role, and thereby provides the atomic input unit for the formation of teamwork and for team role balance (TRB) determination.

## Input
- Per-member functional role (job) information
- Behavioral data of members observed within the team

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Confirm the member's official functional role.
2. Observe the behavior informally exercised within the team.
3. Classify the observed behavior on the criterion of behavior type (team role).
4. Distinguish, per individual, the natural / latent / non-preferred team role levels.
5. Contrast it with the functional role and feed the diagnostic results into the TRB determination.

## Output
A per-individual functional role versus team role comparison table, and behavior-type-based team role diagnostic results (natural / latent / non-preferred distinction).

## Criteria
If the diagnosed role is confirmed to be an informal behavior type with no job relation and is clearly distinguished from the functional role, it is judged PASS (determined to be a team role); if it is not distinguished, it is judged FAIL (confusion with the functional role, re-diagnosis needed).
