# Team Role Balance (TRB) — Skill

## Purpose
It diagnoses and builds TRB (team role balance) to form the task-activation aspect of teamwork, and improves team performance through the causal chain TRB → Teamwork → Team Performance.

## Input
- Per-member diagnostic data on the level of exercise of the 9 team roles (natural role / latent role / non-preferred role)
- Team composition information

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Diagnose each team member's level of exercise of the 9 team roles.
2. Map, from the whole-team perspective, whether all 9 team roles exist at the natural-role level.
3. Identify the team roles that are missing or that remain at the non-preferred level.
4. Supplement the missing roles through reassignment, coaching, or recruitment of team members.
5. Re-confirm whether TRB has been achieved and verify its linkage with teamwork and team performance.

## Output
The team-level determination result on whether TRB is satisfied, and the teamwork and team performance linkage report.

## Criteria
If all 9 team-role types exist at the natural-role level, it is judged PASS (TRB established); if even one is missing or falls short of the natural-role level, it is judged FAIL (TRB not established, supplementation needed).
