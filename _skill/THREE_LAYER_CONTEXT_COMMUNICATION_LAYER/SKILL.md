# Communication Context — Skill

## Purpose
It determines whether the communication context is in place as the operating criteria for the augmented human-outside relationship.

## Input
- The target communication situation (message exchange between the augmented human and an external counterpart)

## Procedure (Steps)
1. Identify the speaker and the AI's intervening role.
2. Distinguish and mark content written by the AI from the human's judgment.
3. Give notice of whether the counterpart's utterance is recorded and analyzed.
4. Set the scope within which the message may be shared.
5. Confirm human approval for important decisions.

## Output
The communication design of the augmented human-other relationship (the connection to the outside).

## Criteria
If all six criteria items (speaker, AI role, distinction of judgment, notice of recording and analysis, scope of sharing, human approval) are confirmed it is judged PASS; if even one is not confirmed it is judged FAIL.
