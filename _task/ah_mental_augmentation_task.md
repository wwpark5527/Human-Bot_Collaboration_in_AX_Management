# Mental Augmentation — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify the mental capacity (thinking, learning, analysis, creativity, judgment) to be improved by using AI and technology.
2. Design the way of using AI and technology that raises that capacity.
3. Apply the designed way to actual work.
4. Compare and confirm the change in capacity before and after application.

## Deliverables
- A record identifying the mental capacity targeted for improvement
- A record of the application of AI and technology
- Results of the capacity improvement confirmation
