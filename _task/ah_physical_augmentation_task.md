# Physical Augmentation — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify the human member's physical limits or the bottlenecks in their physical performance capability.
2. Grasp the technologies (tools, equipment, systems) that can complement and extend them.
3. Apply the identified technologies to the actual work environment.
4. Confirm the change in physical performance capability before and after application.

## Deliverables
- A record identifying physical limits and bottlenecks
- A list of the complementing and extending technologies applied
- Results of the physical performance capability improvement confirmation
