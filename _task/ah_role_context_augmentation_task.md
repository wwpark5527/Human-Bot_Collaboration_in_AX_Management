# Role/Context Augmentation — Task

## Tasks
1. Confirm how the current role, responsibilities, and mode of contribution differ from those before the use of AI.
2. Design and apply a way of using AI within the organization's purpose, standards, and accountability system.
3. Check each of the power to set purpose, the power to establish standards, and the power to take on judgment and accountability.
4. Reflect the developed role and mode of contribution in the organization's work.

## Deliverables
- A record of changes in role, responsibilities, and mode of contribution
- A record of the application aligning the use of AI with the organization's purpose, standards, and accountability system
- Results of checking the three powers (purpose, standards, judgment and accountability)
