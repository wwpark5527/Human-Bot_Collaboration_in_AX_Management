# AI Capability Equality Theory — Task

## Tasks
1. Diagnose whether the cause of the AI-utilization capability gap within the organization is a tool access problem or a problem of the capability to convey purpose, standards, and context and to verify, approve, and improve.
2. Investigate whether AI-utilization capability depends on an individual's intuition or on a particular department's authority.
3. Convert context capital (purpose, standards, materials) into a common production base accessible to the whole organization.
4. Standardize the AI-related authority system, verification standards, and approval structure and share them across the whole organization.
5. Measure after the conversion whether the AI-utilization capability gap between members has narrowed.

## Deliverables
- Diagnostic report on the cause of the capability gap, context capital conversion plan, standardized authority system/verification standards/approval structure documents, capability gap measurement report.
