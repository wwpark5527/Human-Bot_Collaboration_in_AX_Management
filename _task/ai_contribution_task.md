# AI Contribution — Task

## Tasks
1. Measure the meaning/cognitive distance before and after AI intervention respectively.
2. Compute the "reduced meaning/cognitive distance" as the difference between the two values.
3. Tally the AI-usage cost used for the relevant period and task.
4. Divide the reduced meaning/cognitive distance by the AI-usage cost to produce the AI contribution.
5. Report the produced AI contribution separately from mere output-quantity indicators.

## Deliverables
- Measured values of meaning/cognitive distance before and after AI intervention
- Reduced meaning/cognitive distance
- AI-usage cost tally
- AI contribution value (= reduced meaning/cognitive distance / AI-usage cost)
