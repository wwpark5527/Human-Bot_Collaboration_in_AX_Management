# AI Orchestrator — Task

## Tasks
1. Grasp the number of AIs and agents the member uses simultaneously.
2. Measure the proportion of work handled by AI.
3. Measure the level of automation (partial automation, semi-automation, full automation, and so on).
4. Take "Do I do the work directly, or do I design so that AIs do the work?" as the evaluation question and make the judgment.
5. Register members judged to be AI Orchestrators as subjects for transition to the Augmentation User stage.

## Deliverables
- A record table of the number of AIs and agents used and the proportion of work
- An indicator of the level of automation
- The AI Orchestrator stage judgment result
