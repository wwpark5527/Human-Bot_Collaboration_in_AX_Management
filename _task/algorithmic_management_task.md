# Algorithmic Management — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify the points at which algorithms and AI are involved in work assignment, performance evaluation, surveillance, schedule management, reward, discipline, and productivity measurement within the organization.
2. Specify at each point "who evaluates others with AI" (G: control of authority).
3. Specify "who is evaluated by AI" (S: workers' rights).
4. Check "are AI evaluation criteria explained" (G: transparency) and prepare explanatory materials.
5. Prepare an appeal procedure that answers "can humans object" (S/G: remedy procedure).
6. Designate and document "who is the ultimate responsible party" (G: accountability structure).

## Deliverables
- List of the points of algorithmic management application, document answering the five questions (G/S mapping), appeal procedure document, document designating the responsible parties.
