# Authority & Consent Boundary — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify the material the AI accessed during the collaboration and the role it performed.
2. Confirm the scope of authority and consent granted in advance for that material and role.
3. Compare the actual scope of access and performance against the approved scope of authority and consent.
4. Produce a judgment on whether the authority/consent boundary was complied with.

## Deliverables
- A list of the material accessed and the roles performed by the AI
- A record of the scope of authority and consent
- A judgment on compliance with the authority/consent boundary
