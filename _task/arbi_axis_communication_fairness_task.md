# Communication Fairness — Task

## Tasks
1. Ascertain each participating member's level of AI augmentation (tool accessibility and proficiency of use).
2. Observe whether differences in the level of augmentation lead to differences in voice or influence in meetings and decision-making.
3. Separately mark the points that have been converted into a power difference.
4. Produce a judgment on the fairness of the AI-augmentation gap and the risk of communication asymmetry.

## Deliverables
- A list of each member's level of AI augmentation
- Observation results on differences in voice and influence relative to the augmentation gap
- A fairness judgment and a list of communication asymmetry risks
