# Complementary Fit — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify the strengths of the human participating in the collaboration (experience, contextual judgment, acceptance of responsibility, and so on).
2. Identify the strengths of the AI participating in the collaboration (analysis speed, data throughput, generative capability, and so on).
3. Compare whether the two sets of strengths overlap, whether there are empty areas, and whether they complement each other.
4. Produce a judgment on whether human and AI strengths complement each other.

## Deliverables
- A list of human strengths
- A list of AI strengths
- An analysis of the complementary relationship of strengths and the resulting judgment
