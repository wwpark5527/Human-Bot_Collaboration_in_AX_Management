# Record & Traceability — Task

## Tasks
1. Collect the supporting materials created during the collaboration process, the revision histories, the approval records, and the list of materials used.
2. Confirm when, by whom, and on what rationale each item was created and approved.
3. Separately mark the items whose history is missing or untraceable.
4. Produce a judgment on whether the collaboration history is recorded and re-verifiable.

## Deliverables
- A list of the history of rationale, revisions, approvals, and materials used
- A list of items with missing history or that are untraceable
- A judgment on whether the record is re-verifiable
