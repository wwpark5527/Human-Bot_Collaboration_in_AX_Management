# Role Balance — Task

## Tasks
1. Define the list of roles required in the collaboration scene under evaluation (for example, material analysis, speaking, verification, recording, and so on).
2. Confirm the roles actually assigned to the human and to the AI in the actual collaboration.
3. Compare the vacancy, excess, and concentration between the required roles and the actual assignment.
4. Produce a judgment on the adequacy of role assignment and the imbalance risks.

## Deliverables
- A list of the roles the collaboration requires
- A mapping table of the actual role assignment
- A judgment on the adequacy of role assignment and a list of imbalance risks
