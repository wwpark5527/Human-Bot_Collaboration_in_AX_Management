# Utterance Agency — Task

## Tasks
1. List the individual utterance and judgment units from the collaboration records.
2. Distinguish whether each utterance and judgment corresponds to human alone, AI alone, or joint output.
3. Separately mark the items whose distinction of agent is unclear.
4. Produce a judgment on the attribution of the agent of the utterance or judgment.

## Deliverables
- A list of utterances and judgments
- The results of distinguishing the agent (human/AI/joint)
- A judgment on the attribution of the agent of the utterance or judgment
