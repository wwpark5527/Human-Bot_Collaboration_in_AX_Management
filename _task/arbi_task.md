# Augmented Role Balance Index (ARBI, Augmented Role Balance Index) — Task

## Tasks
1. Collect cases of AI-intervened human-human collaboration.
2. Distinguish in each case the part the human took charge of and the part in which AI intervened.
3. Measure role balance, utterance agency, authority boundaries, human accountability, communication fairness, and recordability.
4. Aggregate the measurement results to produce the ARBI index.

## Deliverables
- List of cases of AI-intervened human-human collaboration
- Measurement results for role balance, utterance agency, authority boundaries, human accountability, communication fairness, and recordability
- ARBI index
