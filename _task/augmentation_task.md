# Augmentation — Task

## Tasks
1. Check whether the introduction of bots is designed as an amplifier of human capability rather than as a simple replacement of human labor.
2. Identify and support the human augmentation domains (physical augmentation — overcoming congenital and acquired disability, overcoming aging, enhancing bodily capability; mental augmentation — augmentation of cognitive ability, decision-making power, creativity, and role).
3. Design the bot augmentation domains (context augmentation, value augmentation, learning augmentation) so that bots receive a transplant of human knowledge, values, rules, and supervisory systems.
4. Communicate so that the perception that "AI strengthens me" forms through positive-sum structural design, and raise acceptance of organizational AX.

## Deliverables
- Plan for forming augmented humans (AH) and augmented bots (AB)
- Roadmap for augmenting human and bot capability, document of positive-sum design principles
