# Human-Centered Path — Task

## Tasks
1. Judge whether the preservation of meaning, emotion, and trust is the top-priority criterion in the communication situation.
2. Construct the structure in which the human (A) speaks directly and judges directly (AI A ↔ Human A ↔ Human B ↔ AI B).
3. Limit the AI to background support roles such as organizing, analyzing, reviewing, and drafting.
4. Accept the drop in speed and level of automation and confirm whether meaning, emotion, and trust relationships between humans are preserved.
