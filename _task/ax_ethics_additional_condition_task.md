# Additional Conditions of Organizational AX — Task

## Tasks
1. Check whether an augmentation-centered philosophy is reflected in the organization's strategy and design principles.
2. Reflect respect for diversity and complementary fit between humans and bots in the organizational structure and the allocation of work.
3. Evaluate and supplement the level at which the common context and the governance context are built.
4. Measure the effect that fulfilling the additional conditions has on mid-to-long-term organizational competitiveness.

## Deliverables
- Results of the check on the reflection of an augmentation-centered philosophy
- Results of the reflection of diversity and complementary fit
- Results of the evaluation of the building of the common and governance contexts
- A report measuring the contribution to mid-to-long-term competitiveness
