# Ethical Necessary Conditions of Organizational AX — Task

## Tasks
1. Check the state of fulfilment of the seven items: guaranteeing human dignity, humans' ultimate accountability, explainability, protection of personal and organizational information, prevention of bias and discrimination, safety and reliability, and building an AI governance system.
2. Identify the path by which organizational AX heads toward failure when each item is not complied with, and assess the level of risk.
3. Document and institutionalize the AI governance system as the operating constitution of organizational AX.
4. Set priorities by distinguishing the necessary conditions from the additional conditions (the factors that enhance competitiveness).

## Deliverables
- Results of the compliance check on the seven necessary conditions
- A risk assessment report on non-compliance
- A document of the AI governance operating constitution
