# Human Meaning Integrator — Task

2. Interpret and put in writing the meaning that AI results have for organizational members and stakeholders.
3. Deliver the interpreted meaning to the decision-making line and coordinate so that it is reflected in subsequent discussions.

- Records of the connection between AI results and the organizational context
