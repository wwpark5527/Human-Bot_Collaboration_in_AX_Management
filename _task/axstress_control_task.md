# Control Stress — Task

## Tasks
1. Grasp the contexts in which the question 'who actually controls?' is raised in decision-making situations.
2. Distinguish that control stress originates not from a matter of trust but from the ambiguity of the locus of control itself.
3. Establish principles and procedures for clearly redefining members' scope of control.
4. Monitor the deterioration path that, if left unresolved, leads to governance collapse.

## Deliverables
- A report on the identification of control stress and the analysis of decision-making contexts
- A document of principles for redefining the scope of control
- An early-warning system for governance collapse
