# Identity Stress — Task

## Tasks
1. Grasp the situations and contexts in which members are confronted with the question 'why am I needed?'.
2. Distinguish that identity stress is not simple work overload but a fundamental question about the reason for existence.
3. Design programs that support members in redefining their own role and value.
4. Monitor the deterioration path that, if left unresolved, leads to a crisis of existential value, and intervene early.

## Deliverables
- A report on the identification of identity stress and the analysis of its context
- A support program for redefining role and value
- An early-warning and intervention system for the crisis of existential value
