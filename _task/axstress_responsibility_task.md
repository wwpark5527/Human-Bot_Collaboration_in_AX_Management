# Responsibility Stress — Task

## Tasks
1. Grasp the contexts in which the question 'who takes responsibility?' remains unresolved in situations where an outcome has occurred.
2. Distinguish that responsibility stress originates not in the problem of the locus of control (control stress) but in the unclearness of outcome attribution itself.
3. Establish principles and procedures for clearly redefining the scope of members' responsibility.
4. Monitor the deterioration path of responsibility avoidance and declining performance that appears when it is left unresolved.

## Deliverables
- A report on the identification of responsibility stress and analysis of the outcome-attribution context
- A document of principles for redefining the scope of responsibility
- An early-warning system for responsibility avoidance and declining performance
