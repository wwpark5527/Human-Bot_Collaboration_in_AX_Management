# Trust Stress — Task

## Tasks
1. Grasp the situations in which the question 'can I trust the other party?' is raised in the interaction between humans and bots.
2. Distinguish that trust stress is not a problem of the individual member's identity but a problem at the level of the relationship (interaction).
3. Establish principles for designing trust-based cooperation (explainability, transparency, etc.).
4. Monitor the deterioration path that, if left unresolved, leads to a collapse of human-AI trust.

## Deliverables
- A report on the identification of trust stress and the analysis of interaction contexts
- A document of principles for the design of trust-based cooperation
- An early-warning system for the collapse of human-AI trust
