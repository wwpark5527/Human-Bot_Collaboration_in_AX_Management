# Misalignment — Task

## Tasks
1. Inspect the bot's training data, rules, command system, and governance policies for bias, incompleteness, conflict, and ambiguity.
2. Observe signs of increased unpredictability, emergent behavior, and policy violation in the bot's behavior.
3. Establish measures per cause of misalignment: re-examining data, supplementing rules, organizing the command system, and clarifying governance.
4. After applying the realignment measures, re-verify the predictability of the bot's behavior and its policy compliance.

## Deliverables
- An inspection report on the causes of misalignment (data bias, rule incompleteness, command conflict, governance ambiguity)
- Records of observations of emergent behavior and policy violation
- A document of the realignment measures and re-verification results
