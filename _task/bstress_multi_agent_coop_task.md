# Multi-agent cooperation stress — Task

## Tasks
1. Observe signs of coordination failure in the process of cooperation among agents within the multi-agent system.
2. Inspect whether communication overload occurs among agents.
3. Confirm authority ambiguity — which agent has priority, and who decides when judgments conflict.
4. Establish rules of priority and a system of decision rights, and redesign the structure of coordination among agents.

## Deliverables
- Records of observations of coordination failure and communication overload among agents
- A list of authority ambiguities (points where priority and decision rights are unclear)
- A document of the rules on agent priority and decision rights
