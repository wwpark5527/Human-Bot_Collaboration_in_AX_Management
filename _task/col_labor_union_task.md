# Labor-union-type resistance — Task

## Tasks
1. Grasp the trends of collective demands and resistance to AI adoption in industries and job families where labor unions are organized.
2. Confirm whether the four features — demanding limits on automation, mandating human employment, restricting the scope of AI use, and requiring mandatory retraining — appear.
3. Prepare a negotiation agenda that includes prohibiting the unauthorized training on human creations, restricting AI replacement, and protecting digital-reproduction rights.
4. Adjust the points of contention through labor-management negotiation channels before they escalate into a large-scale 'AX labor-rights struggle'.

## Deliverables
- A report grasping the trends of labor-union-type resistance by unionized industry and job family
- A negotiation agenda itemized by demand: limits on automation, mandatory employment, restriction of the scope of use, and retraining
- Labor-management negotiation materials including protection of creations, restriction of AI replacement, and digital-reproduction rights
