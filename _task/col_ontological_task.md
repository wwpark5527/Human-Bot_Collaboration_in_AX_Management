# Ontological resistance — Task

## Tasks
1. Confirm whether the ground of the resistance is a question about the meaning of human existence itself rather than jobs or institutions.
2. Grasp early the signs of deterioration such as cynicism, organizational disengagement, an anti-technology identity, human supremacism, and AI hatred.
3. Convey accurately within the organization the essence of the resistance as a 'defense of the meaning of human existence'.
4. Reconstruct the response messages with an augmentation narrative (role redesign, expansion of human value, collaboration) rather than a replacement narrative.

## Deliverables
- A report identifying the source of ontological resistance
- An early-warning system for signs of deterioration into cynicism and disengagement
- Communication materials for re-establishing the value of human existence based on the augmentation narrative
