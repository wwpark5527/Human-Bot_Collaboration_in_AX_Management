# Professional-group resistance — Task

## Tasks
1. Grasp the collective concerns about and resistance to AI adoption by highly skilled professional domain, such as the medical, education, and legal fields.
2. Confirm whether the subject of the resistance is a highly skilled profession and whether unclear allocation of responsibility, infringement of expertise, and devaluation of professional worth are at issue.
3. Establish concrete principles addressing the core concerns of each domain, such as medical judgment authority, the meaning of educational evaluation, and the locus of legal liability.
4. Position AI as an assisting tool for the professional and communicate clearly that the final authority of judgment rests with the professional.

## Deliverables
- A report grasping professional-group resistance by domain: the medical, education, and legal fields
- Response principles by issue: allocation of responsibility, infringement of expertise, and devaluation of professional worth
- Materials positioning AI as an assisting tool that state explicitly the professional's final authority of judgment
