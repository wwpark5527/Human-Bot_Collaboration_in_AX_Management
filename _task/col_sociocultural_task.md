# Sociocultural resistance — Task

## Tasks
1. Grasp the resistance by distinguishing whether it is of the 'formation of an anti-AI culture' type, such as human-first-ism, the Slow AI movement, and the emphasis on Human-made, or of the 'distrust of the technological elite' type based on distrust of a few big tech firms.
2. Track the diffusion pattern of cultural counter-movements such as the 'AI-free art, human-written, and human-creation certification' movement in light of the case of the organic movement.
3. Compile the concrete issues of distrust of the technological elite, such as critiques of AI monopoly, of data colonialism, and of algorithmic power.
4. Establish principles of transparency and explainability that respond to the perception of control by a few big tech firms such as OpenAI, Google, and Meta.

## Deliverables
- A report determining the type: formation of an anti-AI culture / distrust of the technological elite
- Materials tracking the diffusion of cultural counter-movements (human-creation certification, etc.)
- A principles document responding to critiques of AI monopoly, data colonialism, and algorithmic power
