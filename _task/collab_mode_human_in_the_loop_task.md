# Human-in-the-loop (human-approval-centered) — Task

## Tasks
1. Confirm whether the collaboration configuration in which the AI proposes results and the human gives final approval holds, and identify it as the Human-in-the-loop type.
2. Design an approval gate so that every AI deliverable passes through the human's approval procedure.
3. Guarantee that the human is positioned 'inside' the loop, and control the process so that no unapproved result is finalized.
4. Deploy human approvers equipped with review and approval capability at the level analogized to a master's candidate.

## Deliverables
- The result of the Human-in-the-loop type judgment
- A design document for the approval gate, and logs of the human approval history
