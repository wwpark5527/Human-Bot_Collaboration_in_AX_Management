# Human-on-the-loop (AI-autonomous + human oversight) — Task

## Tasks
1. Confirm whether the configuration in which the AI performs most of the work autonomously and the human monitors and intervenes holds, and identify it as the Human-on-the-loop type.
2. Design the oversight system so that human intervention takes the form of after-the-fact, continuous monitoring rather than prior approval.
3. Build an alerting and escalation system through which the human can intervene promptly when an anomaly is detected in the AI's execution results.
4. Assign human overseers with oversight capability at the level analogized to a postdoctoral researcher.

## Deliverables
- The Human-on-the-loop type judgment result
- A design document for the continuous monitoring and intervention system, and human oversight logs on the results of autonomous AI execution
