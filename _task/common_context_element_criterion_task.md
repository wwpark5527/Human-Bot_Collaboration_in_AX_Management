# Criterion — Task

## Tasks
1. Collect the standards of judgment for what counts as a good result in each area of work.
2. Consolidate the collected standards of judgment into one standard unified across the whole organization.
3. Register the unified standard of judgment, together with the purpose and language of the common context, so that AI can share it.
4. Inspect cases of criterion inconsistency in which different standards are applied to the same request.

## Deliverables
- The results of collecting standards of judgment by area of work
- A document of the organization's unified standard of judgment
- The results of the criterion-inconsistency inspection
