# AH-H communication — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify, among the participants in the communication, the augmented human (A: human + AI + common context) and the non-augmented human (B: human alone).
2. Disclose to the counterpart whether AI intervened.
3. Present the content generated by AI and the human's judgment as distinct.
4. Notify whether the counterpart's utterances are recorded and limit the scope of analysis.
5. Have a human re-confirm important agreements.
6. Specify the party bearing final responsibility.

## Deliverables
- Asymmetric communication and the six fairness requirements that follow from it (disclosing whether AI intervened, distinguishing AI-generated content from human judgment, notifying whether the counterpart's utterances are recorded, limiting the scope of analysis of the counterpart's utterances, human re-confirmation of important agreements, specifying the party bearing final responsibility).
