# Emotional complementation — Task

## Tasks
1. Divide the task of emotional support for members into the parts requiring empathy, trust, and psychological stability and the parts requiring immediate response and continuous feedback.
2. Assign immediate response, fatigue-free support, and continuous feedback (for example, 24-hour always-on support) to the bot (AI coach).
3. Assign the provision of empathy, trust, and psychological stability (for example, the formation of psychological safety) to the human leader.
4. Document the collaboration procedure by which the AI coach's always-on support and the human leader's provision of psychological safety are linked.
