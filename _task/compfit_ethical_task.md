# Ethical complementation — Task

## Tasks
1. Distinguish ethically sensitive work into the parts requiring the consistent application of rules and the parts requiring judgment of the legitimacy of the rules.
2. Assign the consistent application of rules (for example, determination according to fixed criteria) to the bot.
3. Assign the legitimacy of the rules themselves, value judgment, social responsibility, and context-based adjustment to the human, and place the authority for final approval with the human.
4. Design the procedure so that the bot produces rule-application results but cannot arbitrarily change or judge the legitimacy of those rules themselves.

## Deliverables
- An ethical work allocation table (rule application: bot vs. judgment of rule legitimacy: human)
- A governance procedure document placing the final authority to judge rule legitimacy with the human
