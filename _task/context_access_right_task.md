# Context Capital Access Right (Context Access Right) — Task

## Tasks
1. Survey separately the current state of formal access, such as AI accounts and software usage licenses, and the current state of actual access to context capital, such as purpose, standards, materials, and approval structure.
2. Diagnose whether context capital is monopolized by particular individuals or departments (on the criteria of sense, language ability, information access, rank, and departmental authority).
3. If context capital monopolization is confirmed, disclose and standardize the relevant purpose, standards, materials, and approval structure in a form accessible to the whole organization.
4. Design the procedures (onboarding, authority assignment) for granting the context capital access right to new and existing members.
5. Examine whether the ability to use AI has spread organizationally after the context capital access right was granted.

## Deliverables
- A diagnostic report on formal vs. actual access, the results of the context capital monopolization diagnosis, disclosed and standardized context capital materials, a procedure document for granting the context capital access right, and a diffusion review report.
