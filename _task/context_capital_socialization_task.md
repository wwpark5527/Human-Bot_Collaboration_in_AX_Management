# Socialization of Context Capital — Task

## Tasks
1. Survey in whom the context needed for AI use within the organization (purpose, standards, language, materials, format, verification standards, approval standards) is concentrated.
2. Identify the points at which context capital is monopolized by a few individuals or a particular department.
3. Establish a plan to convert the monopolized context capital into a common production base (a common context) that the whole organization can access.
4. Grant members access rights to the converted context capital and train them in how to use it.
5. In connection with the responsible operating system, manage the continuity of the socialization of context capital.

## Deliverables
- A diagnostic report on the points of context capital monopolization, a plan for conversion into a common context, and records of the granting of access rights and of training.
