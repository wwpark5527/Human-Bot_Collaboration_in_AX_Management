# Context Justice — Task

## Tasks
1. Diagnose whether the current access authority over the organization's purpose, standards, materials, and verification system is concentrated in a select few.
2. Design and distribute the procedure by which members can give context (purpose, standards, materials) to AI.
3. Establish the procedure and criteria by which members can verify AI output results.
4. Establish the procedure by which members can contest AI judgments (an appeal channel and a review process).
5. Regularly examine the actual exercise of the above three authorities (the right to give context, the right to verify, and the right of appeal).

## Deliverables
- A diagnostic report on the monopolization of access authority, a context-giving procedure document, a results verification procedure document, an appeal process document, and an authority-exercise review report.
