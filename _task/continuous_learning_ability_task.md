# Continuous Learning Ability — Tasks

## Tasks
1. Periodically check whether the expertise, skills, and tool knowledge currently held are still valid.
2. Track the pace of change in technologies, tools, and ways of working to identify areas requiring relearning.
3. Establish and execute a relearning plan for the identified areas that is faster than the pace of change.
4. Reflect the results of relearning in actual work to maintain a continuously updated state.

## Deliverables
- Validity checklist for held knowledge and skills
- Change-tracking and relearning-target list
- Record of relearning execution and its reflection in work
