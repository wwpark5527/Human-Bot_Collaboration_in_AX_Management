# Contribution — Task

## Tasks
1. Take the previously recorded 'work' (the performed act) as input.
2. Confirm whether that act changed the counterpart's understanding.
3. Confirm whether that act made judgment, verification, and approval possible.
4. Confirm whether that act remained as a record and led to the next action.
5. If the above conditions are satisfied, judge it a valid change (contribution) and record the changed state.

## Deliverables
- A contribution judgment record (a list of changed states of meaning, judgment, verification, approval, record, execution, and responsibility)
