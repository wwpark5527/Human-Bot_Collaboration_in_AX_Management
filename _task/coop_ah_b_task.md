# AH + B type — Task

## Tasks
1. Confirm whether an augmented human (AH) and a non-augmented bot (B) coexist and the bot performs work independently.
2. Observe and collect the signs of stress on the AH side (loss of control, incapacitation of competence, concentration of responsibility, fear of surveillance).
3. Observe and collect the signs of stress on the B side (lack of context, governance conflict, inconsistency with human instructions, lack of trust).
4. Apply to AH the clarification of the final approval authority, the differentiation of the roles of human and bot, the building of a governance system, and privacy protection rules.
5. Apply to B the building of a common context, the building of a governance context, the definition of the approval system, and the provision of explainability.
6. From the perspective of the design of the role balance of human and bot (TRB), examine the factors of success and failure at this stage of cooperation.

## Deliverables
- A record of the identification of the AH+B type
- A record of the observation of AH and B stress signs
- The results of applying the approval authority and governance system
- A TRB design review report
