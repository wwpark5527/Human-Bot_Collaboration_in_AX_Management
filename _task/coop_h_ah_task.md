# H + AH type — Task

## Tasks
1. Confirm whether the cooperating parties are a human (H) and an augmented human (AH) and whether a gap in augmentation level exists.
2. Observe and collect the signs of stress on the H side (relative inferiority, alienation, unfair evaluation).
3. Observe and collect the signs of stress on the AH side (continuous high-performance pressure, AI dependency, isolation in human relationships, identity confusion).
4. Apply to H the emphasis on complementary roles instead of comparison and the strengthening of the capabilities distinctive to humans (empathy, ethics, integrative judgment).
5. Apply to AH AI dependency monitoring, collaborative leadership training, and the establishment of a human orchestrator identity.
6. At the organizational level, review and apply the ban on human-only evaluation, an evaluation system that takes the augmentation level into account, and Pairing collaboration design (H↔AH).

## Deliverables
- A record of the identification of the H+AH type
- A record of the observation of H and AH stress signs
- The results of applying the individual-level and organization-level countermeasures
