# H + B type — Task

## Tasks
1. Confirm whether the cooperative relationship is the vertical relationship of human-led plus bot-assisted work (the H+B type).
2. Observe and collect the signs of stress on the human (H) side (loss of control, role erosion, imbalance of responsibility, learning pressure).
3. Observe and collect the signs of stress on the bot (B) side (lack of Context, overload from exceptional situations, inconsistency in human feedback).
4. Apply to the H side the adoption of explainable AI, maintaining Human-in-the-loop, maintaining augmentation rather than replacement, and training in AI comprehension.
5. Apply to the B side building a common context, an exception escalation system, and a feedback learning structure.
6. After application, examine whether the human's sense of control has been recovered and the bot's understanding of context has improved.

## Deliverables
- A record of the identification of the H+B type
- A record of the observation of human (H) and bot (B) stress signs
- The results of applying the countermeasures for each stress
- A review report on the improvement of the sense of control and of context
