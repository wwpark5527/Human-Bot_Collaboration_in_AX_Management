# Critical Thinking — Tasks

## Tasks
1. Do not accept the results produced by AI as they are, but treat them as objects requiring verification.
2. Search for counterexamples to the AI outputs and confirm whether there are parts that are plausible but wrong.
3. Check the logical flow of the AI outputs and examine whether there are leaps or contradictions.
4. Synthesize verification, counterexample search, and logic checking to assess and record the reliability of the AI outputs.

## Deliverables
- A checklist for verifying AI outputs
- Records of counterexample search and logic checking
- A reliability assessment report on AI outputs
