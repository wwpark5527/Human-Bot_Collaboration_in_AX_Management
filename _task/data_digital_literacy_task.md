# Data and Digital Literacy — Tasks

## Tasks
1. Grasp the types and structures of the data handled in one's assigned work and carry out interpretation practice.
2. Learn the stages and tools of the digital workflow to which the work has moved.
3. Establish and apply procedures for verifying the results produced by AI (collation against originals, logic checks, and so on).
4. Periodically check the level of one's data and digital literacy and supplement the areas that are lacking.

## Deliverables
- Records of data interpretation practice and checks
- A document organizing one's understanding of the digital workflow
- Procedures for verifying AI outputs and records of their application
