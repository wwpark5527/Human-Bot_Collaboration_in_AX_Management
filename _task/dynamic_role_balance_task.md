# Dynamic role balance — Task

## Tasks
1. Classify and detect the situation the organization faces as crisis, innovation, operations, and so on.
2. Decide, for each situation, the combination of human roles to be strengthened and AI support elements (AI risk agent, Creative AI, Workflow bot, and so on).
3. Reallocate the decided role combination in real time and reflect it in operations.

## Deliverables
- Records of dynamic role reallocation by situation
- A report on the status of the transition to a role ecosystem
