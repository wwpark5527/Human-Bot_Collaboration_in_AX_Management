# Efficiency-centered AX — Task

## Tasks
1. Confirm whether the organization's AX goals and KPIs are set solely around productivity, cost reduction, and speed.
2. Check whether there is a reduction of human roles (AI going beyond assistance to replace judgment and roles).
3. Check whether AI-competence gaps (gaps in productivity and compensation between those who use AI well and those who do not) are widening.
4. Check whether algorithmic management is being abused for work assignment, evaluation, surveillance, and discipline.
5. Check whether there is a weakening of judgment authority (loss of the authority to verify, correct, or reject AI results).
6. Check whether there is an absence of the right to object (difficulty in demanding explanation and re-examination of AI evaluations and recommendations).
7. Check whether there is performance concentration (the productivity gains from AI being concentrated among owners, senior managers, and the highly skilled).

## Deliverables
- An efficiency-centered AX risk diagnostic checklist (6 items), and the results of applying the efficiency-centered AX vs. inclusive-transformation AX comparison table.
