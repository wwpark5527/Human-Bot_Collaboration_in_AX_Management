# Extension of E (Sustainability of AI Infrastructure) — Task

## Tasks
1. Measure the electricity consumption arising from the organization's AI training, inference, and data-center operations and analyze the impact on the power grid and energy costs.
2. Measure the status of data-center cooling and local water-resource use and check the possibility of conflicts over local communities and water resources.
3. Calculate carbon emissions according to electricity use and the energy mix, and manage the share of renewable energy used.
4. Identify the semiconductor and mineral risks related to AI chip production and the supply chains for key minerals.
5. Manage the replacement cycles of servers, chips, and equipment and the volume of e-waste generated.
6. Assess the impact that data-center siting, power grids, cooling water, and land use have on the local environment.
7. Integrate the above items into the existing E-axis (environment, energy, carbon, resources, pollution) management system and report them.

## Deliverables
- Management data by item for electricity, water, carbon, semiconductors/minerals, e-waste, and local environmental impact, and an integrated E-axis report.
