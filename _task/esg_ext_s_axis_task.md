# Extension of S (AI Capability Equality and Human Dignity) — Task

## Tasks
1. Diagnose in which direction — replacement, complementation, redeployment, or upskilling — AI changes workers' roles.
2. Prepare an explainability and procedural fairness system through which humans can understand and appeal AI outcomes.
3. Where AI is used for evaluation and placement, prepare an algorithmic management system and safeguards for workers' rights.
4. Check whether the capability to use AI (education, retraining, accessibility) is provided equally to all members.
5. Check whether AI reinforces discrimination and bias through data bias.
6. Design safeguards for the right of judgment so that human oversight and responsibility are maintained in the final judgment.
7. Institutionalize and grant members the eight rights of AI access right, right to learn, right to use, right of judgment, right to explanation, right of appeal, right to transition, and right to benefit sharing.

## Deliverables
- A diagnosis of changes in workers' roles, explainability/appeal procedures, an algorithmic management system, the results of the check on equal provision of capability, a bias check report, safeguards for the right of judgment, and a document institutionalizing the eight rights.
