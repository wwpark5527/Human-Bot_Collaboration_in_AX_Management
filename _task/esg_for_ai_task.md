# ESG for AI — Task

## Tasks
1. Investigate cases of negative effects (misuse, bias, infringement, etc.) caused by AI use within the organization.
2. Identify the points where AI use induces ethical problems (human rights, the right of judgment, fairness, etc.).
3. Establish criteria for controlling AI use from the ESG standpoint (approval, verification, audit procedures).
4. Review the applicability of related frameworks such as the responsible AI (RAI) evaluation model.
5. Integrate the AI control criteria into the organization's ESG governance system.

## Deliverables
An investigation report on AI side effects and ethical risks, AI control criteria and procedure documents from the ESG standpoint, and an RAI application plan.
