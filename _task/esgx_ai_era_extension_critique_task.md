# ESG AI-era extension theory — Task

## Tasks
1. Organize the scope of responsibility reflected by the organization's current ESG system (industrial-era criteria).
2. Identify the problems newly emerging within the organization due to the introduction of AI (AI stratification, algorithmic management, infringement of human judgment authority, etc.).
3. Judge whether the existing ESG system captures these new problems.
4. Redefine the problems it fails to capture into an extended ESG scope broadened to the AI operating environment.
5. Present to the organization the grounds and direction for adopting an extended ESG (AI inclusive-transformation ESG).

## Deliverables
A gap analysis report between the existing ESG and the problems of the AI era, and a grounds document for adopting an extended ESG.
