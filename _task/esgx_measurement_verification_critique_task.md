# Measurement-verification-strengthening theory — Task

## Tasks
1. Compare the score calculation criteria of the multiple ESG rating agencies the organization uses or is exposed to.
2. Analyze the cause of score discrepancies between rating agencies (the non-unification of criteria).
3. Check whether the organization's ESG data disclosure practice selectively discloses only favorable indicators.
4. Review the feasibility of adopting unified evaluation and disclosure criteria.
5. Prepare measures for strengthening data verification procedures (third-party verification and the like).

## Deliverables
An analysis report on score discrepancies between rating agencies, a checklist for bias in data disclosure, and a plan for unified criteria and strengthened verification.
