# Stakeholder-expansion theory — Task

## Tasks
1. Check whether the organization's current ESG evaluation and disclosure criteria are limited to financial materiality (investor-centric).
2. Investigate how far non-financial issues such as climate change and human-rights issues are reflected in the evaluation.
3. Identify the points of divergence between the financial-risk perspective and the impact perspective.
4. Investigate regulatory and standard-setting trends toward impact materiality, such as the EU CSRD.
5. Propose a direction for ESG redesign that expands from an investor focus to a variety of stakeholders.

## Deliverables
A gap analysis report on financial versus impact materiality in the ESG evaluation criteria, and a proposal for the direction of stakeholder expansion.
