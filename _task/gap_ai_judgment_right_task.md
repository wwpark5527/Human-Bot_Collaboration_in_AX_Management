# AI Right-to-Judge Gap — Task

## Tasks
1. Grasp the scope of the decisions and results AI produces.
2. Stipulate who holds the authority to approve, revise, or reject these results.
3. Check the cases in which humans without authority remain only as objects of AI decisions.
4. Review whether the distribution of the right of judgment is fair and adjust it if necessary.

## Deliverables
- Rules for distributing the AI right of judgment, and approval, revision, and rejection procedures.
