# AI Outcome-Distribution Gap — Task

## Tasks
1. Measure the change in productivity before and after AI adoption.
2. Analyze which stratum within the organization (executives, managers, practitioners) the productivity gains accrue to.
3. Determine whether there is concentration at the top and exclusion at the bottom.
4. Establish and apply benefit sharing and redistribution measures (compensation, promotion, education opportunities, etc.).
5. Periodically re-check changes in the distribution structure.

## Deliverables
- An analysis of where the AI productivity gains accrue, and benefit sharing and redistribution measures.
