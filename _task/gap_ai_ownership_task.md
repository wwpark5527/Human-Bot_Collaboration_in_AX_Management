# AI Ownership Gap — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify the owning and operating actors of the AI models, data, and platforms the organization uses.
2. Grasp the flow of the revenue arising from them (token fees, subscription fees, knowledge-capitalization profits).
3. Analyze whether the owning actors of the revenue are skewed toward big tech, the organization, or the members.
4. When productivity profits are skewed, review redistribution or sharing measures.

## Deliverables
- An analysis of the revenue structure of AI models, data, and platforms, and review materials on profit distribution.
