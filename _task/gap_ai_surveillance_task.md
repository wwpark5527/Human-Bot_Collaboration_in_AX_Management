# AI Surveillance Gap — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify the actors within the organization who use AI as a management and surveillance tool (managers, system operators).
2. Identify the range of members who become the object of work assignment, evaluation, surveillance, compensation, and discipline by AI.
3. Document the areas to which algorithmic management is applied (work assignment, performance evaluation, surveillance, schedule management, compensation, discipline, productivity measurement).
4. Notify the members under management of the surveillance criteria and prepare an appeal procedure.
5. Periodically check whether the authority asymmetry between the managing actors and their objects is excessive.

## Deliverables
- A document on the scope of applied AI management and surveillance, appeal procedures, and records of authority asymmetry checks.
