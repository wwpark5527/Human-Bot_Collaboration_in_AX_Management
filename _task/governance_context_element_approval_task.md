# Approval — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify the types among the output artifacts AI produces that require human approval.
2. For each type, define the necessary conditions for human review and approval (who approves, when, and by what criteria).
3. Check the defined approval criteria by mapping them to ISO/IEC 42001 (operational control procedures), NIST (Manage), and the EU AI Act (human oversight, Article 14).
4. Register the approval system as the approval component of the governance context.

## Deliverables
- A list of the types of output artifacts requiring human approval
- An international-standard mapping checklist (ISO/IEC 42001, NIST, EU AI Act)
- A document on human intervention and the final judgment criteria
