# Authority — Task

## Tasks
1. Survey, for each local context in the organization (customer service, education, legal, marketing, development, management reporting, and so on), the list of materials and tools AI needs to access.
2. Define and document the scope of materials and tools accessible per context.
3. Check the defined access scope by mapping it to ISO/IEC 42001 (role/access control), NIST AI RMF (Govern), and the EU AI Act (human oversight, Article 14).
4. Register the management system for access and execution scope as the authority component of the governance context.

## Deliverables
- Definition document of the scope of AI-accessible materials and tools per context
- International standards mapping checklist (ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act)
- Management system document for access and execution scope
