# Record — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify the history items (prompts, materials, results, revisions, approvals) that must be kept in the course of AI operation.
2. Define, per item, the location and the way in which the record is kept.
3. Check the defined record system by mapping it to ISO/IEC 42001 (documentation requirements), NIST (Govern/Measure), and the EU AI Act (automatic logging and record-keeping, Article 12).
4. Register the record system as the record component of the governance context.

## Deliverables
- Definition document of the record location and method per history item
- International standards mapping checklist (ISO/IEC 42001, NIST, EU AI Act)
- System document for securing traceability and auditability
