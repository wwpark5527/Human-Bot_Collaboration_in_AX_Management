# Validation — Task

## Tasks
1. Make a list of the types of results AI produces.
2. Define, per type, the checklist and quality criteria to be confirmed (factuality, logic, quality, policy conformance).
3. Check the defined criteria by mapping them to ISO/IEC 42001 (performance evaluation and monitoring), NIST (Measure), and the EU AI Act (accuracy and robustness, Article 15).
4. Register the validation system as the validation component of the governance context.

## Deliverables
- Validation checklist per result type
- International standards mapping checklist (ISO/IEC 42001, NIST, EU AI Act)
- Confirmation results for factuality, logic, quality, and policy conformance
