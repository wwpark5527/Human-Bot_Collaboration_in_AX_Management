# Group Cohesiveness — Task

## Tasks
1. Fix the group or team to be measured.
2. Apply a tool that measures the degree of psychological attraction among members to like, cohere, and help one another.
3. Organize the measurement results as the teamwork indicator for the human-bond side.
4. Place them alongside the TRB (task-activation side) measurement results so that they can be contrasted.
5. Combine the two axes (human bonding versus task activation) to grasp the overall picture of the teamwork of the department or team.

## Deliverables
- Group cohesiveness measurement value (degree of psychological attraction among members)
- Teamwork indicator for the human-bond side
- Comparison material for contrast with TRB
