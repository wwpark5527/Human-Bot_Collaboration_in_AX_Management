# Measurement — Task

## Tasks
1. Confirm the object of observation (human or bot).
2. If human, collect emotional and behavioral indicators (for example, affective state, behavioral change).
3. If a bot, collect the indicators of Latency, Accuracy, and Stability.
4. Check consistency by comparing the collected indicators against the preceding judgments of essence, root cause, result, recovery mode, and risk.

## Deliverables
- A record of indicator collection per agent (human: emotional and behavioral indicators, bot: response speed, accuracy, stability)
- The result of the consistency check between the indicators and the preceding judgments
