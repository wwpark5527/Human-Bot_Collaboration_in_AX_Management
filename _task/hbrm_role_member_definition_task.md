# Member Definition — Task

1. Stipulate the scope of organizational members by extending it from humans (H) to humans and bots (H, AH, B, AB).
2. Document the definition and characteristics of each of H (non-augmented human), AH (augmented human), B (ordinary bot), and AB (augmented bot).
3. Decide the position the four types occupy within the organization (on a role basis, not a job basis).
4. Stipulate, at a first level, the role and responsibility boundaries each type is to carry out (detailed role design continues into the subsequent HBRM role).

## Deliverables

- Rules on the position within the organization by type
- Draft of the role and responsibility scope by type
