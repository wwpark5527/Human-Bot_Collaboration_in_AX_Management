# Role-Balance Management — Task

## Tasks
1. Review the current state of role allocation in the three dimensions human↔human, human↔bot, and bot↔bot.
2. Diagnose imbalance signs such as excess of technical roles, excess of automation roles, lack of verification roles, lack of coordination roles, and unclear responsibility roles.
3. Establish adjustment measures for each diagnosed concentration, deficiency, and conflict.
4. Document the adjustment results and connect them to the subsequent trust-management stage.

## Deliverables
- Role-balance diagnosis table for the three dimensions (human-human/human-bot/bot-bot)
- Adjustment measure proposals by imbalance type
- Role-balance management document
