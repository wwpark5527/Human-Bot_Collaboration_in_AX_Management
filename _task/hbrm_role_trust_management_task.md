# Trust Management — Task

## Tasks
1. Grasp the human-bot collaboration points on the basis of the role structure adjusted in the role-balance management stage.
2. Identify the points where there is a risk of overtrust in a bot's results (uncritical acceptance).
3. Identify the points where there is a risk of excessive distrust in a bot's results (impeded collaboration).
4. Establish and document collaboration standards that prevent both overtrust and distrust.

## Deliverables
- Overtrust-prevention standard (for example, a result verification procedure)
- Distrust-mitigation standard (for example, a procedure for presenting grounds for trust)
- Trust management collaboration standards document
