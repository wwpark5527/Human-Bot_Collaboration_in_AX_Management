# Baek (魄) — Tasks

## Tasks
1. Identify where in the organization's work the functions of baek — body, senses, labor, physical execution — are located.
2. Among those functions, distinguish the parts that can be performed by proxy through robots, automation devices, and mechanical execution from the parts that humans must perform directly.
3. Design operating procedures so that human rest, safety, and dignity are not violated even for the functions of baek delegated to automation devices.
4. Periodically examine whether there are signs of "baeksan," in which the human body and labor are subordinated to the rhythms and rules of the automation system.

## Deliverables
- A list identifying the functions of baek (body, physical execution) and the scope within which automation can perform them by proxy
- A procedure document for protecting human safety, rest, and dignity with respect to delegation to automation devices
- A checklist for examining signs of baeksan (the systematization of body and labor)
