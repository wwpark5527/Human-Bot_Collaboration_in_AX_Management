# Hon (魂) — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify where in the organization's work the functions of Hon corresponding to thought, memory, judgment, creation, and decision-making lie.
2. Distinguish, among those functions, the part that can be performed on the human's behalf by AI's analysis, reasoning, language generation, and judgment assistance from the part the human must carry out directly.
3. Design the procedure so that, even for the functions of Hon delegated to AI, the final judgment and verification are carried out by the human.
4. Periodically check whether there are signs of 'honbi', in which the human accepts AI's output as it is without verification.

## Deliverables
- List identifying the functions of Hon (cognition) and the scope that AI can perform on the human's behalf
- Human verification procedure document for AI judgment assistance
- Checklist for reviewing signs of honbi (the loss of the sovereignty of judgment)
