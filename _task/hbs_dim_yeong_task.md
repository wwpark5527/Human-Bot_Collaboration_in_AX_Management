# Yeong (靈) — Tasks

## Tasks
1. Identify which of the organization's decisions involve dignity, morality, responsibility, and agency, that is, which belong to the domain of yeong.
2. Design the approval and sign-off system so that those decisions are not delegated to AI or robots but remain under the final right of judgment of humans.
3. Insert into every important decision a procedure of questioning, verifying, and taking responsibility for oneself rather than accepting the answer AI produced as it is.
4. Examine whether signs of lost yeong (acceptance without questioning, trust without verification, evasion of responsibility) exist within the organization.

## Deliverables
- A list of decisions in the domain of yeong (dignity, morality, responsibility, agency) and a designation table of the holders of the final right of judgment
- A procedure document for the human judgment of accepting/rejecting/revising/taking responsibility for AI outputs
- A checklist for examining signs of the loss of yeong (giving up questioning, acceptance without verification, evasion of responsibility)
