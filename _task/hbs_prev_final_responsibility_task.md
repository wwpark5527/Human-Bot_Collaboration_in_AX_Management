# Ultimate responsibility must remain with humans and the organization — Tasks

## Tasks
1. Classify the types of decisions within the organization and identify the items that qualify as "important decisions."
2. For each important decision, explicitly designate the holder of the final right of judgment and the subject of responsibility (a human or an organizational unit).
3. Even when the results of AI's advice and analysis are fed into the decision process, make it procedural that the approval and signature stage always passes through the designated human or organization.
4. Periodically audit whether the subject of responsibility is in substance being transferred to AI or automation systems.

## Deliverables
- A list of important decision types and a designation table of the holders of the final right of judgment and the subjects of responsibility
- A procedure document for final human and organizational approval when AI advice is reflected
- A checklist for auditing whether the subject of responsibility has been transferred
