# Technology adoption must be designed together with governance — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify in advance, together with the functional scope of the AI or automation system scheduled for adoption, the authority and decision-making influence that system may exert.
2. Specify in the adoption plan the six governance elements of authority (who approves what), responsibility (who is responsible when a problem occurs), approval (the approval procedure before adoption and change), recording (recording the grounds of judgment, the data, and the approver), auditing (the ex-post review procedure), and risk review (the prior risk assessment).
3. Block, at the pre-adoption review stage, cases in which functions alone are deployed with the governance design omitted.
4. Periodically check, even after adoption, whether the governance elements actually work.

## Deliverables
- AI adoption governance design document including the six elements of authority, responsibility, approval, recording, auditing, and risk review
- Pre-adoption review checklist confirming whether function and governance were designed simultaneously
- Post-adoption governance operation review report
