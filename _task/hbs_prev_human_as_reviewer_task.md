# Humans must be reviewers of AI output, not consumers — Task

## Tasks
1. Define a three-step review procedure of 'interpretation → verification → adjustment' at every work step that receives AI output.
2. Identify the work in which there is a practice of adopting AI output as it is without verification, and insert a review procedure.
3. Train members in the knowledge and capabilities needed to verify AI output, so that they can carry out the role of judge and verifier.
4. Record the history of the human's interpretation, verification, and adjustment of AI output, and thereby track whether the review actually took place.

## Deliverables
- Standard three-step review procedure document for the interpretation, verification, and adjustment of AI output
- List identifying and improving uncritical adoption practices
- Training materials for AI output verification capability and a review history record form
