# Automation must not reduce humans to components — Task

## Tasks
1. Assess in advance the impact on human rest, safety, dignity, and right of judgment in the work areas where automation and robot adoption is planned.
2. Manage human protection indicators (rest time, safety accident rate, reports of dignity infringement, whether the right of judgment is guaranteed) in parallel with efficiency indicators from the design stage onward.
3. Review with priority the areas that risk subordinating human behavior to the system's rhythm, such as platform labor, surveillance systems, and production algorithms.
4. If signs of the componentization of the human (baeksan) are found, review the automation design again and correct it.

## Deliverables
- Pre-adoption human impact assessment for automation (rest, safety, dignity, right of judgment)
- Dashboard managing efficiency indicators and human protection indicators in parallel
- Report on the review and correction of signs of baeksan (the systematization of body and labor)
