# Autonomy — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify whether the bot's candidate actions infringe upon the human right of choice.
2. Identify whether the bot's candidate actions contain elements that manipulate or deceive humans.
3. Exclude actions for which infringement of the right of choice or manipulation or deception has been confirmed, and record the grounds for exclusion.
4. Judge the respect-for-autonomy criterion in an integrated way together with the other three elements of the technical definition of respect for humans (non-harm, fairness, accountability).

## Deliverables
- Results of the identification of infringement of the right of choice, manipulation, and deception
- List of excluded actions and the grounds for exclusion
- Record of the integrated judgment of the four elements
