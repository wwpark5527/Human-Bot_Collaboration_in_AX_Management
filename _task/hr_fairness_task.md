# Fairness — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify whether a bot's actions or results give rise to discrimination against a particular human group.
2. Exclude the actions and results in which discrimination is confirmed, and record the grounds for exclusion.
3. Review and correct the bias factors inherent in the output process.
4. Judge the fairness criterion in an integrated way together with the other three elements of the technical definition of human respect (non-harm, respect for autonomy, accountability).

## Deliverables
- Results of identifying whether discrimination occurs
- List of excluded and corrected actions and results with their grounds
- Record of the integrated judgment of the four elements
