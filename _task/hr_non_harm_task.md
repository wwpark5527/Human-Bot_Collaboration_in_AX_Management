# Non-harm — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify whether the bot's candidate actions have the potential to do physical harm to humans.
2. Identify whether the bot's candidate actions have the potential to do mental harm to humans.
3. Exclude actions for which the potential for harm has been confirmed, and record the grounds for exclusion.
4. Judge the non-harm criterion in an integrated way together with the other three elements of the technical definition of respect for humans (respect for autonomy, fairness, accountability).

## Deliverables
- Results of the identification of the potential for physical and mental harm
- List of excluded actions and the grounds for exclusion
- Record of the integrated judgment of the four elements
