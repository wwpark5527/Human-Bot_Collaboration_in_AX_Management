# Stage 4 (Verification Layer) — Task

## Tasks
1. Before executing the bot's action, verify in advance whether the expected result could harm humans.
2. After executing the bot's action, verify after the fact whether the actual result harmed humans.
3. Verify whether the bot's decision-making process followed the established rules (the requirements of Stages 1 to 3).
4. Record and preserve the verification results as evidentiary material for whether respect was observed.

## Deliverables
- Pre-verification results (an evaluation of the possibility of expected harm)
- Post-verification results (whether actual harm occurred, whether the rules were complied with)
- Records proving whether respect was observed
