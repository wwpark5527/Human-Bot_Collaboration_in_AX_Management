# Stress from distrust or overtrust of AI — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify, respectively, the signs of AI distrust ("what if it is wrong? what about hallucination? what about manipulated data?") and AI overtrust ("the AI recommended it, so it must be right") in members' utterances.
2. Observe whether the wavering back and forth between the two extremes itself operates as continuous stress.
3. Monitor the cases of a weakening of judgment and responsibility avoidance that appear in the state of overtrust.
4. Establish procedures and criteria for establishing a stable, evidence-based level of trust.

## Deliverables
- Observation records of the signs of AI distrust and overtrust
- A list of cases of a weakening of judgment and responsibility avoidance due to overtrust
- Guidelines for establishing an evidence-based level of trust in AI
