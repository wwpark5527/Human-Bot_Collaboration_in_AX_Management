# Cognitive Overload — Task

## Tasks
1. Grasp the list of objects that the human must supervise, orchestrate, and verify as a meta-manager (multiple AI agents, real-time data, automated workflows, verification of AI recommendations, governance compliance).
2. Observe the timing and intensity at which decision fatigue, attention fragmentation, and continuous monitoring burden appear.
3. Redesign the number and scope of the objects of simultaneous management to a manageable level.
4. Prepare tools and procedures that alleviate the cognitive burden of the meta-manager role.

## Deliverables
- A list of objects of simultaneous management and a diagnostic report on the level of burden
- Observation records of decision fatigue and attention fragmentation
- A document on procedures for alleviating the meta-manager's cognitive burden
