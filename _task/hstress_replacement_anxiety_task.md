# Replacement Anxiety — Task

## Tasks
1. Grasp threat perceptions in members' utterances and behavior, such as "is my expertise becoming inferior to AI", "am I being reduced to a supporting role", "is AI taking away promotion opportunities".
2. Confirm whether replacement anxiety appears more strongly among knowledge workers.
3. Monitor whether traditional job insecurity develops into existential anxiety and an identity crisis.
4. Establish measures that reconfirm and strengthen distinctively human capabilities.

## Deliverables
- An observation report on the signs of replacement anxiety
- An analysis of the level of threat perception by knowledge-worker group
- A document on measures for reconfirming and strengthening distinctively human capabilities
