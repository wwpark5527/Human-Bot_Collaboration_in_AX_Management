# Role Ambiguity — Task

## Tasks
1. Grasp the points at which "who is responsible for what?" is unclear in human-bot collaboration situations.
2. Explicitly stipulate whether strategy formulation is the human's share, whether the bot goes as far as decision-making, and who the ultimate responsible party is.
3. Establish response principles for situations in which human judgment and AI recommendation conflict, and for the problem of damage to human self-esteem when the AI is more accurate.
4. Monitor the deterioration path that leads to the problem of attributing responsibility when a failure occurs.

## Deliverables
- A human-bot division-of-roles specification (distinguishing strategy / decision-making / ultimate responsible party)
- Guidelines for responding to judgment-recommendation conflict
- A document on the procedure for attributing responsibility in case of failure
