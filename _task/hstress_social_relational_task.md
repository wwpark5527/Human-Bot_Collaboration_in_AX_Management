# Social and Relational Stress — Task

## Tasks
1. Observe, in human-bot collaboration situations, whether psychological distance from bots, deterioration of human-to-human relationships, social isolation, and increased emotional labor occur.
2. Identify the work phases in which a lack of emotional empathy appears in customer service, AI supervision, acceptance of AI-generated feedback, and collaboration with bots.
3. Monitor signs of a weakening of the human need to belong.
4. Establish relational support structures and measures to supplement emotional empathy.

## Deliverables
- An observation report on stress in the human-bot collaborative relationship
- A list of signs of a weakened need to belong
- A document of measures for relational support and empathy supplementation
