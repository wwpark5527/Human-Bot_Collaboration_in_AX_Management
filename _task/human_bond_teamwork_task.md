# Teamwork in the Human-Bond Aspect — Task

## Tasks
1. Fix the department/team to be measured.
2. Apply a group cohesiveness measurement instrument to collect the degree of psychological attraction among members.
3. Organize the measurement results as the teamwork indicator for the human-bond aspect.
4. Analyze the relationship with the perceived performance of the department as recognized by members.
5. Combine with the teamwork indicator of the task-activation aspect (TRB) to diagnose departmental teamwork as a whole.

## Deliverables
- Human-bond measurement values based on group cohesiveness
- Analytical material on the effect on perceived performance
- Comparative material for combination with TRB (a comprehensive diagnosis of the two aspects of teamwork)
