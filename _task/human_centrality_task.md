# Human Centrality — Task

## Tasks
1. For every AI- or AX-related decision, state explicitly the criterion 'does this harm the survival and development of humans,' and reflect in the procedure that the subject of judgment is the human.
2. Establish a governance procedure that rejects applications of AI and AX judged to harm the survival and development of humans.
3. Guard against and educate about the zero-sum thinking that 'humans always come first and bots must be sacrificed,' so that it does not take root within the organization.
4. Design the management system so that, taking human centrality as the basic premise, the three core spirits of augmentation, respect for and use of diversity, and pursuit of complementary fit follow on.

## Deliverables
- A document of the criteria for judging the impact on human survival and development and of the rejection procedure
- Educational material for preventing zero-sum thinking, and a design document for the management system leading on to the three core spirits
