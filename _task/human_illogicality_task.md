# Human Illogicality — Task

1. Identify the emotional, relational, symbolic, identity-based, and habitual factors of the human stakeholders the bot interacts with.
2. Review in advance design proposals that are efficient but may be disliked by humans, and evaluate their acceptability.
3. Grasp the points that fail to earn trust even when logically valid, and prepare measures for building trust.
4. Establish communication and management measures for the change points where emotional resistance is expected.
