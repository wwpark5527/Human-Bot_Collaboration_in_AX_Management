# True AX Organization / Hybrid Organization (AH+AB) — Task

## Tasks
1. Diagnose whether the humans and bots who are organizational members have been augmented into AH and AB respectively.
2. Embed the common context, the governance context, and AI governance in the bots so as to convert the bots from mere execution tools into subjects of joint judgment (AB).
3. Design a structure in which the roles of humans and bots are divided and shared centered on roles rather than jobs.
4. Accelerate the transition into an AH + AB organization by using a proper operating system (OS) for organizational AX.
5. Convert HRM into HBRM (Human Bot Resource Management) and build a management approach that recruits, develops, evaluates, and retires humans and bots together.

## Deliverables
- A diagnostic report on the transition to a hybrid organization (the status of AH·AB augmentation)
- A role-based collaboration design document, and a plan for introducing the organizational AX OS and HBRM
