# Inclusive / Just AI Transition ESG — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify within the organization the stratum that owns, uses, and directs AI and the stratum that is evaluated, replaced, managed, and excluded by AI.
2. Design a system that equitably guarantees the right of access to AI and the right to learn to organizational members.
3. Design the procedures of AI use, verification, and approval so that humans' right of judgment is reflected in them.
4. Build a system of AI-related recording and attribution of responsibility.
5. Establish a benefit-sharing system that shares the outcomes of AI use with organizational members.
6. Explicitly incorporate the whole of the above into the company's ESG responsibility system.

## Deliverables
A system guaranteeing the right of AI access and the right to learn, a procedural manual for AI use, verification, and approval, criteria for AI recording and attribution of responsibility, a benefit-sharing system, and an integration proposal for Inclusive AI Transition ESG.
