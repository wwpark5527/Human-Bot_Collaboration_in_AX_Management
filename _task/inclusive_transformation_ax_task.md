# Inclusive Transformation AX — Task

## Tasks
1. Diagnose whether the organization's current AX is designed solely around efficiency, productivity, and cost reduction.
2. Check the level of protection of each of humans' role, right to judge, right to learn, right to transition, right to appeal, and right to share in performance.
3. Evaluate the six risks of efficiency-centered AX (role reduction/widening gaps/algorithmic management/weakened right of judgment/absence of appeal/concentration of outcomes) in the organizational context.
4. Redesign the AX transition plan so as to reflect the perspective of responsibility and inclusiveness.
5. Connect the redesigned framework to the S·G execution structures of ESG and reflect it in operations.

## Deliverables
- An AX efficiency-inclusiveness diagnostic report, a checklist for the protection of the six rights, a redesigned Inclusive Transformation AX roadmap, and a document on the ESG-linked execution structure.
