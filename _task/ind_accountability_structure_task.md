# Accountability Structure — Task

The tasks performed in order to realize the objectives are all a matter of **producing one ratio and inspecting whether that ratio is trustworthy**. Designing or improving an accountability structure is not a task of this indicator — this indicator only measures whether what was designed was actually designated.

## Tasks

1. **Fix the denominator.** Define the unit of AI outputs the organization produced and used in the measurement period, and count their total number. First fix the unit definition (whether it is one document, one deployment, or one decision) in a document. Since the designation rate becomes incomparable if the unit changes, a change of definition is permitted only at a period boundary, and the fact of the change is recorded.
2. **Aggregate the numerator.** Among the outputs belonging to the denominator, count the cases in which a final responsible party is designated by an **individual identifier** and left in the records. Cases recording only a department name, team name, or job title are excluded from the numerator, and the number of excluded cases is recorded separately.
3. **Classify the time of designation.** Divide each designated case into prior designation and retroactive after-the-fact designation on the basis of the time the output was produced. Cases that cannot be classified because time information is missing are treated as after-the-fact designation.
4. **Decompose by department.** Calculate the designation rate at the department level, and state the department with the minimum and its figure.
5. **Verify the actual existence of authority by sampling.** Draw a sample from among the designated responsible parties and confirm whether that person actually holds the authority to demand access to, modification of, or suspension of the output in question. Cases with a name but no authority are reclassified as undesignated and removed from the numerator.
6. **Compare with the immediately preceding cycle.** Place the designation rate, the prior-designation ratio, and the per-department minimum side by side with the values of the immediately preceding cycle and report the trend alongside.
7. **Judge and record.** Decide PASS/FAIL by checking against the four success criteria conditions, and if FAIL, state the unsatisfied conditions and the department in question.

## Deliverables

- The final-responsible-party designation rate figure (one company-wide value) — the value this indicator must produce.
- A table of per-department designation rates and a statement of the department with the minimum.
- The prior-designation ratio and the number of retroactive after-the-fact designations.
- The number of cases excluded as not individually identified (only a department or job title recorded).
- The authority sample verification result and the number of reclassifications resulting from it.
- The trend against the immediately preceding cycle and the PASS/FAIL judgment record.
