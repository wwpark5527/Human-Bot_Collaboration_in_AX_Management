# AI Education — Task

## Tasks
1. Define the scope of AI-literacy education — state explicitly which courses fall under literacy education and what the conditions for recognizing completion are (finishing the course, passing the assessment, submitting a practical deliverable, and so on).
2. Calculate the **AI-literacy education completion rate** — compute the proportion of people who completed it relative to the target population according to the defined standard.
3. Calculate **job-specific reskilling hours** — aggregate the hours invested in retraining at the job level. Decide in advance whether to record reskilling (retraining for job transition) and upskilling (deepening the current job) separately.
4. Confirm the distribution across jobs — check whether reskilling hours are skewed toward particular jobs and whether they were actually invested in the jobs judged to be heavily affected at step 4, labor transition.
5. Report the completion rate and the reskilling hours together — since presenting only the completion rate allows the figure to be filled with formal attendance, state the invested hours alongside.
6. Fix a measurement cycle and accumulate a time series on the same standard.

## Deliverables
The education completion-rate figure and job-specific reskilling hours — the education scope and the completion judgment standard, the company-wide completion rate, job-specific reskilling hours, the distribution across jobs and the result of checking it against the jobs affected by labor transition, the measurement cycle and the time-series record.
