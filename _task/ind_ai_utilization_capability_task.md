# AI Utilization Capability — Task

## Tasks
1. Define the standard for recognizing work-improvement cases — state explicitly what will be recognized as an "AI-utilization work improvement". Take as the standard whether before and after the improvement can be compared, whether the contribution of AI can be identified, and whether the same approach is reproducible.
2. Collect and tally **AI-utilization work-improvement cases** — record the department and job together so they can be used for the later distribution calculation.
3. Fix in advance the way productivity gain is expressed — decide whether the gain will be shown in hours saved, throughput, rework rate, or a quality indicator, and do not change it throughout the measurement cycle.
4. Calculate the **productivity-gain distribution** — decompose it by department and by job to see where the gains took hold. Presenting only the company-wide average value does not satisfy this task.
5. Check against the preceding two indicators — confirm whether utilization performance actually appears in the groups where access was opened (AI accessibility) and education was carried out (AI education), and mark separately the range in which access and education existed but utilization performance did not.
6. Fix a measurement cycle and accumulate a time series on the same standard.

## Deliverables
The tally of work-improvement cases and the productivity-gain distribution — the case recognition standard, the list and number of cases, the way productivity gain is expressed, the gain distribution by department and by job, the result of checking against the accessibility and education indicators, the measurement cycle and the time-series record.
