# Benefit Sharing — Task

The tasks performed in order to realize the objectives are a matter of **producing one ratio and verifying, alongside it, that ratio's denominator and the distribution of benefit**. Distributing the benefit itself is not a task of this indicator — this indicator only measures whether distribution occurred.

## Tasks

1. **Compute the denominator and document its basis.** Convert the AI productivity gains confirmed in the measurement period into an amount of money. The three of computation method (what was regarded as a gain), reference point in time (what it was compared against), and covered work (how far it was included) must be left in a document. A denominator without a basis invalidates every ratio placed on top of it.
2. **Aggregate the numerator divided into three channels.** Of the gains, aggregate separately the amount paid as compensation, the amount invested in education, and the amount executed as welfare. Fix the three values separately first, then sum them.
3. **Record out-of-channel reinvestment separately.** Reinvestment not attributed to members, such as strengthening organizational capability, facilities, and financial retention, is excluded from the numerator, and its amount is left as a separate item. Excluding it does not mean leaving it out of sight.
4. **Calculate the reinvestment rate.** Reinvestment rate = (compensation + education + welfare) ÷ gains × 100.
5. **Calculate the distribution of benefit.** Produce the number of people who actually received reinvestment and their proportion relative to all members, and the distribution of amounts by grade and by employment type. Confirm explicitly whether non-regular, contract, and dispatched personnel were omitted from the distribution.
6. **Attach the time series of the denominator.** Place side by side the values of the gains themselves for the immediately preceding period and the same period of the previous year. If the reinvestment rate rose while the gains fell, expose that this may be not an improvement but a shrinking of the denominator.
7. **Judge and record.** Decide PASS/FAIL by checking against the four success criteria conditions, and if FAIL, state the unsatisfied conditions and their figures.

## Deliverables

- **The reinvestment-rate figure** — the value this indicator must produce.
- The amounts for each of the three channels of compensation, education, and welfare and their respective composition ratios.
- The amount of the gains (the denominator) and the document of computation method, reference point in time, and covered work.
- The time series of the gains (immediately preceding period, same period of the previous year).
- A separate record of the out-of-channel reinvestment amount (the portion excluded from the numerator).
- The proportion of beneficiaries and the distribution table by grade and by employment type.
- The PASS/FAIL judgment and the record of unsatisfied conditions.
