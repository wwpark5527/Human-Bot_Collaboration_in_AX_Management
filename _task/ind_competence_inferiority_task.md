# Competence-Inferiority Type — Task

## Tasks
1. Monitor the withdrawal reactions of members in organizations and job groups where differences in the speed of generative AI utilization appear distinctly.
2. Check whether the four characteristics of learning fatigue, digital inferiority, self-deprecation arising from differences in AI usage ability, and the sense of technological alienation among middle-aged and older members appear.
3. Grasp the degree to which the perception that "only young employees will survive" and the pressure felt toward colleagues who submit AI-based reports have spread.
4. Alleviate the self-deprecation caused by the competence gap through stepwise AI learning support for all members, including middle-aged and older members.

## Deliverables
- A monitoring report on competence-inferiority reactions by organization and job group
- Response measures for each item of learning fatigue, digital inferiority, self-deprecation, and the sense of technological alienation
- Materials for a stepwise AI learning support program including middle-aged and older members
