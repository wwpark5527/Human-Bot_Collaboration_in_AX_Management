# Control-Loss Type — Task

## Tasks
1. Monitor the reactions of members in professional domains where automated decision-making such as AI diagnostic assistance, AI hiring recommendations, and AI risk models is introduced.
2. Check whether the four characteristics of manager resistance, defense of expert authority, algorithmic distrust, and refusal of AI recommendations appear.
3. Design and communicate clear governance (the final right of approval, the auxiliary position of AI) regarding "who is the final judge?"
4. Position the introduction of AI as a tool that complements the human right of judgment so that expertise and authority are not infringed.

## Deliverables
- A monitoring report on control-loss reactions by professional domain
- Response measures for each item of manager resistance, defense of authority, algorithmic distrust, and refusal of recommendations
- AI-human decision-making governance materials that specify the locus of the final right of judgment
