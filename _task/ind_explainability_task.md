# Explainability — Task

## Tasks
1. Define the scope of the "AI decisions" subject to explanation — state explicitly to which decisions the duty of explanation attaches (assessment, recommendation, allocation, screening, and so on), and make clear that this becomes the denominator of the provision rate.
2. Fix the minimum requirements of an explanation — prescribe what must be included for something to be recognized as an explanation (the grounds of the decision, the data used, the criteria applied, the route of appeal). If the requirements are not fixed, the provision rate becomes the distribution rate of boilerplate phrases.
3. Calculate the **AI-decision explanation-provision rate** — compute the proportion of cases in which an explanation meeting the minimum requirements was actually delivered, relative to the decisions subject to explanation.
4. Fix the method of measuring comprehension — do not substitute a satisfaction survey. Use a method that confirms actual understanding, such as having people restate the grounds of the decision or answer confirmation questions.
5. Measure **user comprehension** — apply the method of 4 to the people who received explanations, and record alongside the response rate and the composition of the sample.
6. Report the provision rate and comprehension together — mark separately the ranges in which the provision rate is high and comprehension is low. These ranges are the state of formal explanation this indicator seeks to catch.
7. Decompose comprehension by target group — confirm whether comprehension diverges by job, grade, or experience of using AI.
8. Fix a measurement cycle and accumulate a time series on the same standard.

## Deliverables
The measured values of the explanation-provision rate and user comprehension — the scope of the decisions subject to explanation, the minimum requirements of an explanation, the explanation-provision rate, the method and questions for measuring comprehension, user comprehension with the response rate and sample composition, the decomposition of comprehension by group, the ranges of divergence between provision rate and comprehension, the measurement cycle and the time-series record.
