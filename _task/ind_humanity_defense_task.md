# Humanity-Defense Type — Task

## Tasks
1. Grasp the reactions to generative AI of job groups where human uniqueness is a core value, such as artists, professors, and counseling and education practitioners.
2. Check whether the four characteristics of defending human creativity, emphasizing human relationality, asserting the superiority of emotion, ethics, and intuition, and the reaction that "AI does not understand humans" appear.
3. Establish principles for using AI in a complementary way while protecting human uniqueness.
4. Clearly communicate the boundary between the role of AI and the uniquely human domain in the areas of creation, education, and counseling.

## Deliverables
- A report grasping humanity-defense reactions by job group centered on human uniqueness
- Response measures for each item of creativity, relationality, emotion-ethics-intuition, and the perception of non-understanding
- Principle materials specifying the boundary between the uniquely human domain and the domain complemented by AI
