# Labor Transition — Task

## Tasks
1. Define the unit of the "AI adoption case" to be measured — fix what will be counted as one adoption case (by tool, by work process, by organizational unit), and state explicitly what the point of adoption is taken to be (contract date, release date, date actual use began).
2. Judge for each adoption case **whether a job impact assessment was conducted before AI adoption** — confirm from source materials whether the writing date of the assessment document precedes the point of adoption. An assessment written after adoption is tallied as not conducted.
3. Present whether it was conducted in aggregate form — report alongside the number of cases in which an advance impact assessment was conducted relative to all adoption cases, so that a single yes/no does not represent the whole organization.
4. Define the denominator of transition reassignment — take as the denominator the **people judged** in the impact assessment to have their jobs substantively changed. Do not use the whole workforce as the denominator, since that makes the reassignment rate appear higher than it actually is.
5. Calculate the **transition-reassignment rate** — compute the proportion of the denominator population whose reassignment to a new role was actually completed, and mark separately the ranges of reassignment in progress, reassignment incomplete, and departure.
6. Report whether it was conducted and the reassignment rate together — since the range in which assessment was done but did not lead to reassignment is the state this indicator seeks to catch, do not read the two values separately.
7. Fix a measurement cycle and accumulate a time series on the same standard.

## Deliverables
Whether the impact assessment was conducted and the transition-reassignment rate — the unit of adoption cases and the definition of the point of adoption, the judgment of advance conduct per adoption case and the evidence of timing, the number of cases conducted relative to all adoption cases, the definition of the reassignment-rate denominator, the transition-reassignment rate and the incomplete and departure ranges, the measurement cycle and the time-series record.
