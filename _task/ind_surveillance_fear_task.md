# Surveillance-Fear Type — Task

## Tasks
1. Monitor the reactions of members in organizations and job groups where productivity-tracking AI tools, keyboard and mouse tracking systems, and AI meeting-analysis systems are introduced.
2. Check whether the four characteristics of anxiety over behavior monitoring, concern about privacy infringement, fear of AI evaluation, and stress from work logs appear.
3. Clearly disclose to members the purpose, collection scope, and manner of use of datafication, logging, and tracking.
4. Establish privacy protection principles so that work logs and behavior data are not unfairly used in individual evaluation.

## Deliverables
- A monitoring report on surveillance-fear reactions by organization and job group where data-based management tools are introduced
- Response measures for each item of behavior monitoring, privacy, AI evaluation, and work logs
- Transparency disclosure materials specifying the purpose, scope, and manner of use of data collection, and privacy protection principles
