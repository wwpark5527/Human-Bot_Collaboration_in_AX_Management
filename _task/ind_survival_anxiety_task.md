# Survival-Anxiety Type — Task

## Tasks
1. Monitor reactions to AI adoption targeting repetitive knowledge-work job groups such as call centers, office workers (subject to document automation), and developers.
2. Confirm whether the four features of fear of restructuring, concern over role reduction, concern over reduced promotion opportunities, and anxiety over wage decline appear.
3. Provide concrete information on employment stability and retraining opportunities that can mitigate the perception that "AI will replace me in the end."
4. Respond early at the individual and job-group level so that the survival-anxiety-type reaction does not spread into collective backlash (such as union-type backlash).

## Deliverables
- A monitoring report on survival-anxiety-type reactions by repetitive knowledge-work job group
- Response measures for each of the restructuring, role-reduction, promotion, and wage anxiety items
- Communication materials on employment stability and retraining opportunities
