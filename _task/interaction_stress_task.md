# Relational (Interaction) Stress — Task

## Tasks
1. Collect signals of tension at the relational dimension, separately from individual-actor stress, in the field of human-bot collaboration.
2. Confirm whether there is loss of a sense of control (a human feeling unable to control the bot).
3. Confirm whether there is misreading of intent (the bot misinterpreting human intent).
4. Confirm whether there is an imbalance of responsibility and authority (an imbalance between human responsibility and AI authority).
5. Confirm whether there is a collapse of trust (the collapse of human-AI trust), and connect the confirmed signs to a response at the organizational level (governance inspection, resistance management).

## Deliverables
- An inspection record of the signs of relational stress (loss of a sense of control / misreading of intent / imbalance of responsibility and authority / collapse of trust)
- The result of connecting to a response at the organizational level (governance inspection, resistance management)
