# Interplace — Task

## Tasks
1. Distribute the self-perception inventory (SPI) to the individual to be measured and collect their own responses.
2. Distribute the observer assessment (OA) to observers (4 or more) and collect objective responses.
3. Collect job-related data through the job requirement exercise (JRE) and the job observer assessment (JOA).
4. Enter the collected data (on personnel and jobs) into the Interplace system.
5. Process the data in real time and output 14 kinds of report (in the language the client wants).
6. Use the output results in a manner suited to practical purposes such as team building, appraisal, counseling, placement, manager training, and career development.

## Deliverables
- Per-individual TR type and level diagnostic reports (14 kinds)
- Analysis results for person-person fit and person-job fit
- Practical materials for team building, appraisal, counseling, placement, training, and career development
