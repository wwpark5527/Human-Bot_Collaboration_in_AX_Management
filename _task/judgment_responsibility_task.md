# Judgment Responsibility — Task

## Tasks
1. Review whether the result AI generated is correct and whether it is suitable for our organization.
2. Judge whether the result may be released externally.
3. Judge whether the result may be reflected in decision-making.
4. Judge whether further review or supervisor approval / legal / security review is required, and initiate the procedure when needed.

## Deliverables
- Record of the suitability judgment on the AI result
- Judgment statement on releasability to the outside and reflection in decision-making
- Determination result on whether further approval or review is required
