# Just Transition — Task

## Tasks
1. Review the just-transition principle of climate transition (fairness and inclusiveness of the transition process, no one left behind).
2. Apply that principle to the AI transition context and derive the proposition that must be upheld in AI transition (protection of humans' roles, income, judgment rights, learning rights, and dignity).
3. Connect the derived proposition to the AI Inclusive-Transition ESG (Just AI Transition ESG) frame as its source principle.
4. Check whether the just-transition principle has been reflected in the organization's AI transition plan.

## Deliverables
The proposition applying the just-transition principle to AI transition, and the supporting grounds document.
