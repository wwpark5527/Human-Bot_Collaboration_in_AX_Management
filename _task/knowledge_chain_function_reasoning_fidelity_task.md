# Reasoning-Chain Fidelity Measurement — Task

## Tasks
1. Confirm the conclusion to be evaluated and the reasoning process (the intermediate steps) leading to it.
2. Check whether each intermediate step is valid.
3. Confirm whether each step leads logically to the next step.
4. Confirm whether the last step actually supports the final answer.
5. Confirm whether each step is connected to evidence, and produce the result of the process-validity evaluation.

## Deliverables
- The check result on the validity of the intermediate steps
- The confirmation result on the connection between steps
- The confirmation result on the connection to evidence
- The result of the process-validity evaluation (a judgment on the reasoning path, not the result)
