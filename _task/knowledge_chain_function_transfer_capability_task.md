# Transferability Measurement — Task

## Tasks
1. Prepare a new situation (sentence/example/domain/counterexample) different from the originally learned sentence, example, and domain.
2. Confirm whether understanding is maintained even when the sentence changes.
3. Confirm whether the principle is maintained even when the example changes.
4. Confirm whether the same structure is applied even when the domain changes.
5. Confirm whether the concept is revised when a counterexample is presented, and judge the level of transferability.

## Deliverables
- The check result on whether understanding is maintained when the sentence changes
- The check result on whether the principle is maintained when the example changes
- The check result on whether the structure is applied when the domain changes
- The check result on the response to a counterexample (concept revision)
- A judgment on the level of transferability (whether the meaning structure is maintained into a new situation)
