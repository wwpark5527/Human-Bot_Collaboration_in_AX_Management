# Korean Government (Human-Centered AI Ethics Standards) — Task

## Tasks
1. State the 3 basic principles (the principle of human dignity, the principle of the public good of society, the principle of the purposiveness of technology) explicitly in the organizational AX policy.
2. Concretize the 10 core requirements (guaranteeing human rights, protecting privacy, respecting diversity, prohibition of infringement (harmlessness), fairness, safety, accountability, transparency, data management, explainability) into item-by-item practical guidelines.
3. Check whether the AI system infringes on human autonomy.
4. Evaluate whether the use of AI contributes to the welfare of humankind.

## Deliverables
- A policy document reflecting the 3 basic principles
- Practical guidelines for the 10 core requirements
- The result of the autonomy-infringement check
- The result of the evaluation of the contribution to the welfare of humankind
