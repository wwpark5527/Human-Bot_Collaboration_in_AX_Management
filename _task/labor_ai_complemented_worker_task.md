# AI-Complemented Worker — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify the workers and the work whose productivity and decision-making ability improve through the use of AI.
2. Compare the performance-demand standards (processing time, output volume, scope of decision-making) before and after the introduction of AI.
3. Check whether the increase in performance demand leads to an excessive burden on the worker.
4. Establish a plan for improving compensation and working conditions commensurate with the increment in productivity.

## Deliverables
- Measurement data on the AI complementation effect, an analysis report on the change in performance demand, and a plan for improving compensation and treatment.
