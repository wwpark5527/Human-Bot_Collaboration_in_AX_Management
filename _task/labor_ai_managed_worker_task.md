# AI-managed worker — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify the workers and work areas whose work is assigned, evaluated, and monitored by algorithms.
2. Document the judgment standards of the assignment, evaluation, and monitoring algorithms being applied.
3. Check whether workers can actually understand these standards (confirmation of explainability).
4. When opaque or unfair standards are found, improve them and re-notify workers.

## Deliverables
- A document of the algorithmic assignment, evaluation, and monitoring standards, a record of the explainability check, and a history of standard improvements.
