# Capability layer — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify, among the reactions of members subject to the AX transition, the cases that converge on the question "Am I falling behind?"
2. Observe in those members and job groups the emotional signs of inferiority and helplessness (comparison with colleagues who use AI well, avoidance of learning, self-deprecating remarks, and so on).
3. Provide tailored learning and coaching programs that reduce the gap in the capability to use AI and digital tools.
4. Combine support for capability improvement with recognition of performance, before the capability-layer reaction transfers to the relationship and existence layers.

## Deliverables
- A checklist for identifying capability-layer reactions (based on the core question and representative emotions)
- A learning program for closing the AI and digital capability gap
- An execution record of the capability-layer response (learning support, recognition of competence)
