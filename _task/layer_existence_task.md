# Existence layer — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify, among the reactions of members subject to the AX transition, the cases that converge on the question "Is the meaning of being human diminishing?"
2. Observe in those members and job groups the emotional signs of identity confusion (doubt about one's reason for being, fundamental helplessness, philosophical and existential remarks, and so on).
3. Show through concrete cases that uniquely human values (judgment, responsibility, empathy, the conferring of meaning, and so on) are maintained and strengthened even after the AX organizational transition.
4. Carry out human-centered identity reaffirmation activities before the existence-layer reaction spreads into fundamental resistance across the organization.

## Deliverables
- A checklist for identifying existence-layer reactions (based on the core question and representative emotion)
- Communication materials reaffirming uniquely human value and roles
- An execution record of the existence-layer response (identity reaffirmation)
