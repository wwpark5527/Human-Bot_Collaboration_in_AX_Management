# Reasonable interpretation — Task

## Tasks
1. Review the external evidence organized at the verifiable fact layer (literature from international organizations and research institutes).
2. Infer from those facts the possibility that gaps in access, competence, decision authority, and performance distribution arise.
3. Verify that the inference process is derived from the confirmed facts without any leap.
4. Organize the result of the inference into a problem diagnosis and hand it over to the proposed concept layer.

## Deliverables
A diagnostic report on the possibility of gaps arising, based on the confirmed facts (the reasonable interpretation layer, problem diagnosis).
