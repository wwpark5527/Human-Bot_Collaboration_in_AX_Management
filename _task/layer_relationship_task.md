# Relationship layer — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify, among the reactions of members subject to the AX transition, the cases that converge on the question "Is my position in the organization weakening?"
2. Observe in that member or job group the emotional signs of alienation and defensiveness (reduced participation in meetings, avoidance of collaboration, defensive and guarded attitudes, and so on).
3. Clearly explain how the member's position and network are restructured in the role and decision-making structure after the AX transition.
4. Carry out measures to rebuild the network and reconfirm the role before the relationship-layer reaction transfers to the existence layer.

## Deliverables
- A checklist for identifying relationship-layer reactions (based on the core question and representative emotions)
- Guidance material on the restructuring of roles and relationships after the AX transition
- A record of executing relationship-layer responses (network rebuilding and role reconfirmation)
