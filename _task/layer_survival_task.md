# Survival layer — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify, among the reactions of members subject to the AX transition, the cases that converge on the question "Is my job disappearing?"
2. Observe in that member or job group the emotional signs of anxiety and fear (an increase in inquiries about leaving, a decline in work engagement, resistant remarks, and so on).
3. Share information related to job continuation (the transition plan, redeployment principles, whether there will be workforce reduction) clearly and in a timely manner to lower uncertainty.
4. Carry out individual or collective responses (retraining, role redesign, and so on) before the survival-layer reaction transfers to the capability, relationship, or existence layer.

## Deliverables
- A checklist for identifying survival-layer reactions (based on the core question and representative emotions)
- A communication plan and materials related to job continuation
- A record of executing survival-layer responses (retraining and redeployment)
