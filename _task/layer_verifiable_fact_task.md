# Verifiable fact — Task

## Tasks
1. Collect the literature of international organizations and research institutions related to AI gaps, labor transition, algorithmic management, and human-rights risks.
2. Select from the collected literature only the externally verifiable facts.
3. Separate the selected facts from the author's interpretations, proposals, and operating model, and organize them into the "verifiable fact" layer.
4. Present the organized facts as the external evidence of the inclusive AI-transition ESG argument.

## Deliverables
A list of external evidence based on the literature of international organizations and research institutions (the verifiable fact layer).
