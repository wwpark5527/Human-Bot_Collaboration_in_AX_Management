# Least-preferred Role — Task

## Tasks
1. Confirm the team-role candidates that have not been classified as natural roles or potential roles.
2. Test whether the role is manifested even when the person makes maximum effort.
3. Confirm whether there is no possibility of manifestation even when others' support is applied.
4. If it remains impossible even when both conditions (maximum effort plus others' support) are applied, confirm it as a least-preferred role.
5. Register the given TR blank as an item that cannot be resolved by managing internal members.
6. Review and propose either abandoning the TR or recruiting or replacing personnel who hold that TR as a natural role.

## Deliverables
- A list of least-preferred roles per individual
- A list of TR blanks that cannot be resolved by internal management
- Supporting material for the decision to abandon a TR or to recruit or replace
