# Meaning/cognitive distance — Task

## Tasks
1. Observe and record, through the knowledge chain, the current conceptual structure of the person (or AI) being evaluated.
2. Define the target knowledge structure and compare its difference from the current conceptual structure.
3. Measure each of the eight factors: conceptual structure, level of mastery, belief structure, completeness of premise relations, fidelity of the reasoning chain, reliability of evidence, transferability, and epistemic maturity.
4. Combine the eight factors and produce the meaning/cognitive distance value.
5. Compare the distance values before and after AI intervention and compute the amount of distance reduction.

## Deliverables
- Measurement results for each of the eight factors
- The knowledge-chain-based meaning/cognitive distance value (the combined result of the eight factors)
- The amount of distance reduction before and after AI intervention
