# Meaning design capability — Task

## Tasks
1. Grasp the meaning elements, large and small, related to the organization and its work (vision, identity, community meaning, and so on).
2. Analyze the relations among the grasped meaning elements and draw the structure of their connections.
3. On the basis of the connected meaning system, organize the meaning-level foundation needed for problem framing.
4. Share the organized meaning system with the members of the organization and form a common understanding.

## Deliverables
- A list of grasped meaning elements (vision, identity, community meaning)
- A structural diagram of the relations among meanings
- A meaning system document that supports problem framing
