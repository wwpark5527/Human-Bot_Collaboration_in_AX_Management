# Team/Natural Role — Task

## Tasks
1. Observe the behavior types an individual actually manifests when working alongside others.
2. Confirm whether they are manifested easily and naturally without others' help or the person's own conscious effort.
3. Classify the team roles that meet the above condition as natural roles (team roles).
4. Confirm the list of natural roles per individual (commonly 2–3).
5. Gather the natural roles of all team members and compute how many of the 9 TR cells are filled.
6. Confirm whether all 9 cells are filled and pass this to the judgment of whether TRB is established or not.

## Deliverables
- A list of natural roles (team roles) per individual
- A status table of the 9 TR cells filled at the team level
- Input data for judging whether TRB is established or not, and the teamwork level score
