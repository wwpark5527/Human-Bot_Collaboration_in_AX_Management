# Observer assessment (OA: observer assessment) — Task

## Tasks
1. Select four or more observers from among the people around the person being measured who know them well.
2. Distribute the observer assessment (OA) to each observer.
3. Instruct them to mark, from the 45 positive adjectives and 27 negative adjectives, the items relevant to the person being assessed (a checklist method; unlike self-diagnosis, it is not a method of responding to items).
4. Gather the responses of four or more observers and organize them into the Interplace input format.
5. Pass on the gathered OA responses so that they can be combined with the self-perception inventory (SPI) responses.

## Deliverables
- Raw checklist data per observer (four or more)
- Aggregated observer response data
- Input data for combination with the SPI
