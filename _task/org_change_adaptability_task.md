# Organizational-change adaptability — Tasks

## Tasks
1. Continuously monitor the areas in which change occurs within the organization, such as roles, authority, collaboration, and evaluation.
2. For the detected changes, examine courses of adaptation rather than reactions of refusal.
3. Reflect the courses of adaptation in one's actual ways of working and attitudes.
4. Check whether the results of adaptation to change have contributed to maintaining one's position as a valuable presence within the organization.

## Deliverables
- Records of organizational change monitoring
- Courses of adaptation to change and records of their execution
- A report checking one's position within the organization
