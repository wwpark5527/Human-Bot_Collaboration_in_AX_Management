# Organizational AX — Task

## Tasks
1. Diagnose, in distinction from each other, the state of the spread of personal AX within the organization and the state of progress of organizational AX.
2. Decide the level of decision-making in which AI will participate, the locus of accountability (human/AI), changes in roles between departments, data access authority, and responsibility in the event of AI error.
3. Connect work, data, structure, authority, evaluation, culture, and governance across the whole enterprise, rather than adoption confined to some departments (partial optimization).
4. Execute the redesign plan for the decision-making structure, communication structure, power structure, and collaboration structure and spread it enterprise-wide.

## Deliverables
- Personal AX / organizational AX diagnosis report
- List of organizational redesign decisions (criteria for participation in decision-making, responsibility, roles, authority)
- Enterprise-wide connection roadmap (work, data, structure, authority, evaluation, culture, governance)
- Redesign plan for the organizational operating system
