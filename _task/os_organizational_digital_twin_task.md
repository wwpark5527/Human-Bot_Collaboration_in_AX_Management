# Organizational digital twin(ODT) — Task

## Tasks
1. Build a system that dynamically reproduces in digital space the organization's structure, roles, workflows, decision-making, collaboration, performance, and culture data.
2. Implement real-time and near-real-time simulation, prediction, and optimization functions on the basis of the reproduced organizational model.
3. Operate the system so that organizational experiments (what-if analyses and the like) can be conducted on the ODT rather than in reality.

## Deliverables
- A digital twin model of the organizational structure and workflows
- A report on the results of simulation, prediction, and optimization
