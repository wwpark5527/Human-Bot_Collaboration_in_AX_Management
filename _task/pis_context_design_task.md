# Context design — Task

## Tasks
1. Grasp the organization's purposes and criteria.
2. Translate human purposes and criteria into execution conditions that AI can understand.
3. Mediate these execution conditions between the knowledge chain and the world model.
4. Review whether the results of AI execution agree with the organization's purposes and criteria.

## Deliverables
- AI execution conditions (purpose and criteria), and an operating structure that connects technical discussion to human work and organizational operation.
