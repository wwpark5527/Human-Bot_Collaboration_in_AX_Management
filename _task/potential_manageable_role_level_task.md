# Potential/Manageable Role — Task

## Tasks
1. Confirm the candidate team roles that the individual does not exercise naturally.
2. Confirm whether that role can be exercised through the person's conscious effort and self-management.
3. Confirm whether the environment is one in which colleagues can understand and accept that behavior.
4. When both conditions are met, classify it as a potential/manageable role.
5. Reflect the potential roles in the calculation of the team's maximum potential teamwork level.
6. Establish a promotion plan that combines colleagues' acceptance and encouragement with the person's own effort, and pursue the transition to a natural role.

## Deliverables
- A list of potential/manageable roles for each individual
- Materials for calculating the maximum potential teamwork level at the team level
- The candidates and plan for promotion to natural roles
