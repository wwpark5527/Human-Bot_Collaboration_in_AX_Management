# Right to AI appeal — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify, among the tasks in which AI evaluation, recommendation, or decision intervenes, those for which an appeal may be needed.
2. Design the intake channel and procedure for reconsideration requests.
3. Receive and review reconsideration requests and decide whether correction or remedy is warranted.
4. Record and manage the reconsideration requests and their handling results.

## Deliverables
A document of the reconsideration request procedure, records of reconsideration requests and their handling, and the results of correction and remedy measures.
