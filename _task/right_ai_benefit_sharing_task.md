# Right to AI benefit-sharing — Task

## Tasks
1. Compare the productivity indicators before and after the introduction of AI and calculate the productivity gains attributable to AI.
2. Analyze the current accrual structure of the calculated gains and confirm whether they are concentrated among owners.
3. Establish the distribution criteria for the gains (the proportions of reinvestment in compensation, education, welfare, and organizational capability).
4. Reflect the distribution criteria in the compensation system and in the education and welfare budget, and execute them.
5. Measure the reinvestment rate regularly and check its distribution across departments and job groups, and correct any skew.

## Deliverables
The results of calculating the productivity gains, an analysis of the accrual structure of the gains, the distribution criteria and reinvestment principles, the execution records of reinvestment in compensation, education, and welfare, and a measurement report on the reinvestment rate.
