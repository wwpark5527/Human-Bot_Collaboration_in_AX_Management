# Right to AI explanation — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify the tasks in which the AI result affects the party.
2. Document the AI evaluation criteria for each such task and organize them in an understandable form.
3. Establish a procedure for providing the party with an explanation of the AI decision.
4. Record the history of explanation provision and confirm the degree of understanding.

## Deliverables
An explanatory document of the AI results and evaluation criteria, the explanation-provision procedure, and records of the history of explanation provision.
