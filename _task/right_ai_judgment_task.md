# Right to AI judgment — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify, among the tasks in which AI outputs intervene, the important judgment tasks that require human approval.
2. Define, for each such task, the purpose, meaning, and criteria that humans must provide.
3. Establish the human approval procedure and the system for retaining the final decision-making authority.
4. Record and manage the list of tasks for which human approval has been given and the judgment criteria applied.

## Deliverables
A list of tasks that require human approval, a document of the judgment criteria for each task, and records of the operation of the approval procedure.
