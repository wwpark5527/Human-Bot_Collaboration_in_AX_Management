# Right to AI learning — Task

## Tasks
1. Diagnose the AI literacy level required for each job and the current capability gap.
2. Design and provide a basic AI literacy education course.
3. Operate reskilling programs for personnel whose jobs are expected to change.
4. Provide upskilling programs for personnel already using AI.
5. Measure course completion rates and the degree of capability improvement.

## Deliverables
AI literacy education courses, results of operating reskilling/upskilling programs, and a measurement report on the capability gap and completion rates.
