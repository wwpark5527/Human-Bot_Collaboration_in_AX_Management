# Right to AI transition — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify the personnel and areas where job change is expected due to the introduction of AI.
2. Establish a role redesign plan suited to the changing job.
3. Design and provide a transition training program.
4. Carry out transition placement into the redesigned role and check the state of adaptation.

## Deliverables
A list of personnel expected to undergo job change, a role redesign plan, and the transition training program and transition placement results.
