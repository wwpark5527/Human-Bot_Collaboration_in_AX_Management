# ① Respect for (protection of) human safety and life — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify and evaluate in advance whether a bot's actions have the potential to harm a human.
2. Design and verify the duty logic that detects and intervenes for a human in danger.
3. Design the action, reward, and verification structures so that only actions that do not violate humans are selected.
4. Confirm that there is no priority conflict with the second law (obedience to humans) and the third law (self-preservation).

## Deliverables
- Results of the advance identification and evaluation of harm potential
- Verification results for the danger-intervention duty logic
- Design document for the human-centered action, reward, and verification structures
