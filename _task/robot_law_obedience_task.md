# ② Obedience to Humans (Recognition of Dignity and Hierarchy) — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify the order received from a human and interpret its content clearly.
2. Determine in advance whether carrying out that order conflicts with the First Law (respect for human safety and life).
3. Execute the logic that carries out orders which do not conflict with the First Law and refuses orders that do conflict.
4. Maintain the priority among the principles so that the Third Law (self-preservation) does not take precedence over the First and Second Laws.

## Deliverables
- Record of order reception and interpretation
- Result of the determination of whether the First Law is violated
- Decision to carry out or refuse the order, and its grounds
