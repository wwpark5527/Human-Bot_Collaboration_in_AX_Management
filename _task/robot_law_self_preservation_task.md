# ③ The Robot's Own Self-Preservation (Maintenance of Existence) — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify the threat or damage situation the bot faces and judge whether it is a situation requiring self-preservation.
2. Determine in advance whether the self-preservation action in question conflicts with the First Law (respect for human safety and life) and the Second Law (obedience to humans).
3. Execute only self-preservation actions that do not conflict with the two higher principles, and suppress actions that do conflict.
4. Confirm that there is no priority conflict between the First and Second Laws and the self-preservation action.

## Deliverables
- Record of the identification of the threat or damage situation
- Result of the determination of whether the First and Second Laws are violated
- Decision to execute or suppress the self-preservation action, and its grounds
