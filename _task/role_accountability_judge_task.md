# Accountability judge — Task

## Tasks
1. Judge whether the result will be used for internal reference only.
2. Judge whether it may be delivered to a customer.
3. Judge whether it may be reflected in decision-making.
4. Confirm whether a superior's approval is required and whether legal/security review is required.
5. Decide whether a record must be kept, and clarify the locus of accountability if a problem arises.

## Deliverables
- A determination statement on the scope of use of the AI result
- A determination of whether approval or review is required
- A record specifying the locus of accountability
