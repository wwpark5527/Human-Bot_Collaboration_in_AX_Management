# Social Cooperation Norm Learning — Task

## Tasks
1. Investigate the influence that the organization's power, trust, tacit knowledge, politics, culture, face, and emotion factors exert on decision-making and communication.
2. Define situation-specific norms about when the AI should speak and when it should stay silent.
3. Define the AI's mode of explanation and the criteria for selecting whom to persuade.
4. Identify expressions that may be felt as threatening and reflect expression norms that avoid them in the learning data.

## Deliverables
- Organizational cooperation norm definition document
- Situation-specific speech/silence criteria
- Explanation and persuasion guide
- List of threatening expressions to avoid
