# Pursuing Complementary Fit — Task

## Tasks
1. Do not merely divide the same work between humans and bots; grasp the essential strengths of each and differentiate the roles.
2. Design cognitive complementarity (the human's intuition, creativity, and imagination + the bot's calculation, search, and memory) — for example, in a strategy meeting the bot handles data simulation while the human handles the interpretation of strategic meaning.
3. Design emotional complementarity (the human's empathy, trust, and psychological stability + the bot's immediate response and tireless support) — for example, the AI coach's 24-hour support + the human leader's provision of psychological safety.
4. Design behavioral complementarity (the human's unstructured and exceptional response + the bot's repetitive and standardized action) and ethical complementarity (the bot's rule-based consistency + the human's value judgment and social responsibility).

## Deliverables
- A role-differentiation design document for each of the four major complementarity types: cognitive, emotional, behavioral, and ethical
- A plan for maximizing organizational performance based on complementary fit
