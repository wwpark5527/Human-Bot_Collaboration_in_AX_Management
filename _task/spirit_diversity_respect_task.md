# Respecting and Leveraging Diversity — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify the object of diversity within the organization by extending it beyond the interior of humanity (generation, background, etc.) to the essential differences between humans and bots.
2. Accurately distinguish the attributes of being different and diverging, and inspect and improve so that stratification by superiority and inferiority does not occur.
3. Design, centered on role diversity, measures that maximize the existence and utility value of humans and bots respectively.
4. Build a system that reinterprets diversity not as a 'factor of conflict' but as an 'augmentation asset' and uses it for creative management and performance improvement.

## Deliverables
- Principles for respecting human-bot diversity and a checklist for preventing stratification
- Measures for leveraging role diversity, and a performance report on turning diversity into an augmentation asset
