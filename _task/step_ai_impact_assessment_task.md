# 1. AI Impact Assessment — Task

## Tasks
1. Specify the tasks, organizations, and scope targeted for AI introduction and settle the object of the impact assessment.
2. Assess the impact in the area of labor — job changes, replacement risk, workload redistribution.
3. Assess the impact in the area of judgment — which judgments are transferred to AI and which judgments remain with humans.
4. Assess the impact in the area of authority — the movement of access, approval, and decision authority.
5. Assess the impact in the area of customers — customer touchpoints, quality of response, and the consequences for customers.
6. Assess the impact in the area of human rights — the risks of surveillance, discrimination, and exclusion.
7. Synthesize the assessment results of the five areas into an AI impact assessment result document and hand it over to the subsequent steps.

## Deliverables
The AI impact assessment result — the scope definition document, the impact analysis for each of the 5 areas of labor, judgment, authority, customers, and human rights, the risk classification and directions of response, and the items handed over to the subsequent steps.
