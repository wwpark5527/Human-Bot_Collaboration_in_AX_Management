# 6. Appeal Procedure — Task

## Tasks
1. Referring to the list of tasks requiring human approval settled in step 5, human approval criteria, determine the scope of the AI results (evaluations, recommendations, decisions, output artifacts) that are subject to appeal.
2. Create the path for **explanation requests** — designate the channel through which the party concerned can demand the grounds and judgment criteria of the AI result, together with the responding party and the response deadline.
3. Create the path for **re-review requests** — define the method of intake, the requirements for intake, the party performing the re-review (including whether it is to be separated from the original judge), and the processing deadline.
4. Determine the method of processing **corrections and remedies** — state how the result is corrected and how the resulting disadvantage is restored when the re-review result upholds the objection.
5. Determine the points at which the history from the intake of an appeal to the closure of its processing remains, and connect them to the subsequent step (audit record).
6. Put in place protective provisions so that no disadvantage arises on the ground that an appeal was raised.

## Deliverables
The appeal procedure and the results of processing re-reviews, corrections, and remedies — the scope subject to appeal, the channels for explanation and re-review requests with their parties and deadlines, the method of processing corrections and remedies, the points at which intake and processing history is recorded, and the provisions protecting the party who raises an appeal.
