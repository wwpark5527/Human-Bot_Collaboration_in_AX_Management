# 7. Audit Record — Task

## Tasks
1. Define each of the five kinds of preservation objects concretely — **prompts** (the input given to AI), **materials** (the sources and data used as grounds), **results** (the AI output artifacts), **corrections** (the changes a human made and the reasons for them), **approval history** (who approved what and when).
2. Designate the point at which the record of each kind is generated and the method of collection — design it so that records remain automatically in the workflow rather than depending on manual entry.
3. Set the identifiers and linking rules so that, for a single output artifact, the five kinds of records are connected to one another and **reconstructed as a single history**.
4. Determine the retention period, storage location, access rights, and method of tamper prevention.
5. Incorporate the approval history of step 5, human approval criteria, and the intake and processing history of step 6, appeal procedure, into this record system.
6. Determine the method of calculating the record preservation rate so that it can be confirmed whether preservation is actually taking place.

## Deliverables
The preserved audit record — the definitions of the five kinds of records, the collection points and methods for each kind, the rules linking history at the output-artifact level, the retention period, storage location, access rights, and method of tamper prevention, and the method of calculating the record preservation rate.
