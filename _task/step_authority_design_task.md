# 3. Authority Design — Task

## Tasks
1. Secure a list of the **AI** resources the organization operates — identify them at the level of models, agents, and services.
2. Secure a list of the **data** resources the organization holds and classify their sensitivity.
3. Secure a list of the **tools** the organization uses — including the tools AI can invoke and their scope of impact.
4. Referring to the per-task context capital built in step 2, map which tasks and roles require each resource.
5. Set, for each resource, the actors that can access it (roles, jobs, organizational units).
6. Document the results of the setting as the access-authority design.
7. Determine the procedures for granting and revoking authority so that authority is updated upon personnel transfers and job changes.

## Deliverables
The access-authority design — the lists of AI, data, and tool resources with their sensitivity classification, the resource-task-role mapping table, the details of the accessing actors set for each resource, and the procedures for granting and revoking authority.
