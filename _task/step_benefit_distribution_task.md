# 8. Benefit Distribution — Task

## Tasks
1. On the basis of the AI work history made confirmable by step 7, audit record, identify the productivity gains originating from the introduction of AI and determine the method of calculation.
2. Make each of the four reinvestment targets concrete — **education** (investment in literacy and reskilling), **compensation** (reflection in member compensation), **welfare** (improvement of working conditions and welfare), **organizational capability** (the accumulation of organization-level capability such as context capital, tools, and infrastructure).
3. Determine the mode of distribution and the distribution ratio for each target, and state the grounds for them.
4. Designate the method of securing funds, the executing party, and the execution cycle so that the distribution does not stop at a declaration but is carried out.
5. Put in place a checking procedure that confirms whether the distribution results are skewed toward particular job groups or strata.
6. Determine the method of calculating the reinvestment record, and connect it so that the basis needed for the calculation is secured from the record system of step 7.

## Deliverables
The reinvestment record — the method of identifying and calculating the productivity gains, the mode and ratio of distribution for education, compensation, welfare, and organizational capability, the funds and the executing party and cycle, the skew-checking procedure, and the method of calculating the reinvestment record together with the execution results.
