# 5. Human Approval Criteria — Task

## Tasks
1. Referring to the roles reconfigured in step 4, labor transition, enumerate by type the outputs and decisions in which AI is involved.
2. Determine for each item whether human intervention is needed — take as criteria the size of the impact, whether it can be reversed, external legal effect, and relevance to human rights and safety.
3. Organize the items determined to require human intervention into a **list of tasks requiring human approval**.
4. Write the **approval criteria** for each item — state what is checked and on what grounds it is approved or rejected.
5. Designate the approving party and the moment of approval for each item (immediately after production, before external dispatch, etc.).
6. Determine the points at which approval history remains so that omissions of approval can be detected, and connect them to the subsequent step (audit record).

## Deliverables
The list of tasks requiring human approval and the approval criteria — the list of types of AI-involved outputs and decisions, the determination results on whether human intervention is needed together with their grounds, the approval criteria for each item, and the table designating the approving party and the moment of approval.
