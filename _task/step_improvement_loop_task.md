# 9. Improvement Loop — Task

## Tasks
1. Settle the collection paths for errors and feedback — take as input the correction history left in step 7, audit record, the re-review and remedy processing results of step 6, appeal procedure, the approval omissions of step 5, and the distribution skew-checking results of step 8.
2. Categorize the collected items — distinguish whether each is a one-off mistake, a defect in the criteria themselves, or a problem in the design of authority and procedures.
3. Determine the **portion to be reflected into the common context** — designate what among purpose, criteria, sources, roles, formats, and verification standards must be fixed so that the same error does not recur.
4. Determine the **portion to be reflected into the governance criteria** — designate what must be fixed among authority design, human approval criteria, appeal procedure, and audit record.
5. Settle the determination results as a revision of the criteria — determine the revising party, the revision cycle, and the way the revision history is recorded.
6. Hand the updated criteria over as the starting condition of the next cycle, and leave the items not reflected together with the reasons.

## Deliverables
The updated common context and governance criteria — the collection paths for errors and feedback and the categorization results, the portion reflected into the common context, the portion reflected into the governance criteria, the revising party, cycle, and history, and the items not reflected together with their reasons.
