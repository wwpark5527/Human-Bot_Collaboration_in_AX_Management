# 4. Labor Transition — Task

## Tasks
1. Conduct a job impact assessment at a point **before** the introduction of AI — identify which tasks of which jobs are transferred to AI and which tasks remain.
2. Specify, from the job impact assessment results, the affected jobs and the personnel concerned.
3. Calculate the capability gap needed for each affected job and design a reskilling plan.
4. Bundle the tasks remaining after transfer to AI with the newly arising tasks and reconfigure the roles.
5. Design a transition placement plan into the reconfigured roles.
6. Calculate the transition-placement rate to confirm whether the design led to actual placement, and supplement for the personnel not yet transitioned.

## Deliverables
The job impact assessment result, the reskilling plan, the reconfigured roles (including the revised job descriptions), and the transition placement plan together with the transition-placement rate measurement result.
