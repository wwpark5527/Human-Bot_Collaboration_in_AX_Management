# Supplementary Fit — Task

## Tasks
1. Examine and maintain the positive function by which existing homogeneity-forming activities such as unity events and staff dinners actually contribute to teamwork and departmental performance.
2. Diagnose whether there is a practice within the organization of taking supplementary fit (homogeneity) alone as the ground of organizational performance.
3. Explain to the organization's members the limit of supplementary fit (the possibility of excluding diversity), and guide them toward expanding awareness into respecting diversity and pursuing complementary fit.

## Deliverables
- The results of examining the positive function of homogeneity-based teamwork activities
- A diagnostic report on the practice of relying on supplementary fit
- Guidance materials for expanding awareness into respecting diversity and pursuing complementary fit
