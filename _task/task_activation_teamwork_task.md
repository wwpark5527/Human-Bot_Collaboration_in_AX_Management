# Task-Activation Aspect of Teamwork — Task

## Tasks
1. Fix the department/team to be measured.
2. Collect each individual member's TR (team role) analysis result through Interplace.
3. Gather the TR analysis results of all team members and compute team role balance (TRB).
4. Organize the TRB result as the indicator of the task-activation aspect of teamwork.
5. Combine it with the indicator of the human bonding aspect of teamwork (group cohesiveness) to diagnose the department's teamwork as a whole.

## Deliverables
- TRB-based task-activation measurement values
- Analysis material on the effect on actual performance
- Comparison material for combination with group cohesiveness (comprehensive diagnosis of the two aspects of teamwork)
