# Team Role Balance (TRB) — Task

## Tasks
1. Diagnose, for each of the 9 team roles, whether each team member falls under a natural role, a latent role, or a non-preferred role.
2. Taking the whole team, map and confirm whether all 9 team-role types exist at the natural-role level.
3. Identify the team roles that are missing or that remain at the non-preferred level.
4. Supplement the missing team roles by adjusting the team's composition or by supporting the exercise of roles.
5. Connect the achievement of TRB to the measurement of the level of teamwork, and connect teamwork to team performance to verify the causal relation.

## Deliverables
- Per-member diagnostic table of the 9 team roles as natural / latent / non-preferred
- Team-level determination result on whether TRB is satisfied
- Teamwork and team performance linkage report (TRB → Teamwork → Team Performance)
