# Team Role (TR) — Task

## Tasks
1. Confirm the member's functional role (official, job-related).
2. Observe and diagnose the behavior type (team role) the member informally exercises within the team.
3. Measure the team role on the criterion of behavior type rather than personality type.
4. Distinguish, per individual, the team roles that are exercised easily, that are latent, and that cannot be exercised.
5. Register the diagnostic results as the input unit for team role balance (TRB) determination.

## Deliverables
- Per-individual functional role versus team role comparison table
- Behavior-type-based team role diagnostic results (natural / latent / non-preferred distinction)
- Input data for TRB determination
