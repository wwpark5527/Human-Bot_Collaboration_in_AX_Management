# Communication Context — Task

## Tasks
1. Identify who the speaker is in the communication situation.
2. State explicitly the role and scope of the AI's intervention.
3. Distinguish and mark content written by the AI from the human's judgment.
4. Give notice of whether the counterpart's utterance is recorded and analyzed.
5. Set the scope within which the message may be shared.
6. Confirm the human approval procedure for important decisions.

## Deliverables
- The communication design of the augmented human-other relationship (the connection to the outside).
