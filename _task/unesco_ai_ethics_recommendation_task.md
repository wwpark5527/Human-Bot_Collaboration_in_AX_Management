# UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI (2021) — Task

## Tasks
1. Review the four values of the UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI (respect for human rights, freedoms, and dignity; the flourishing of the environment and ecosystems; diversity and inclusiveness; peaceful, just, and interconnected societies).
2. Map the ten principles (proportionality and do no harm, safety and security, fairness and non-discrimination, sustainability, privacy and data protection, human oversight and determination, transparency and explainability, responsibility and accountability, awareness and literacy, multi-stakeholder governance collaboration) onto organizational AX policy.
3. Establish an implementation plan for concrete policy action areas such as education, culture, gender equality, and governance.
4. Put in place a system for regularly checking and reporting the state of implementation.

## Deliverables
- Review results on the reflection of the four values
- List of policies mapped to the ten principles
- Implementation plan by policy action area
- Implementation reporting system
